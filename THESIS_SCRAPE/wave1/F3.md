## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **F3-1 (Stack, JPM 2026-04-08): A-mall HoS volumes "meaningfully higher than … originally anticipated" with rent not "meaningfully higher"**. It is the only management statement in the corpus that explicitly says some upcoming HoS will beat the $35M model, and it was given as the reason for the lower store count. It is still only a claim; it becomes a smoking gun if web agents tie it to a number: a mall REIT or landlord saying a Sears-box HoS lifted wing rents or traffic (H04), a county or city projection for the Cerritos, Tysons, Palm Beach Gardens or Barton Creek HoS (H03), or a sales-tax step-up (H07).
2. **F3-2 (MS 2025-12-03): "the sales numbers here are just out of this world" in $800-1,500 psf malls.** Same thread, five months earlier, phrased as a result already seen rather than an expectation.
3. **F3-3 (Katy/Baybrook, Texas "hole in the middle")**. This is a concrete, checkable pair of conversions in a state that publishes city sales-tax allocations monthly. It is the most testable lead for a hard number.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **Management itself says relocations sit in comp** (Gupta Q3FY24: "vast majority … relocations/remodels … stay in comp → little difference comp vs total sales growth"). This supports WF's argument that reported comp is flattered by relocations (BURIED, AGAINST).
- **Displaced boxes can be strong** (Ross Park, F3-4). The comp increment per HoS may be below UBS's $35M − $15M = $20M.
- **The store count is not an upside variance.** Management: 35 + ~14 (FY26) + ~18 (FY27) = ~67-68 by end-FY27, vs Bloomberg FY27E 68-69 (consensus slightly ABOVE management). Stack Dec-25 even said "~15 next yr and going forward". Any variance has to come from volume per store, not from more stores.
- **Occupancy and pre-opening drag:** Q1FY26 Gupta lists "occupancy headwind from portfolio repositioning" as a FY26 GM headwind (BURIED). Pre-opening guide ~$90M FY26 vs $65-75M FY25. With ~18 HoS for 2027 (vs 14 in FY26), FY27 pre-opening is likely ≥ FY26 (INFERENCE: 18 × $3.5M = $63M of HoS pre-open alone, before FH).
- **HoS traffic is never disclosed** ("we don't break out", Q2FY25). Every per-store claim since the Nov-25 slides is qualitative.
- **Early comp evidence was thin:** only two HoS had a full year-2 comp at start-FY24 (F3-5a).
- **Experts:** E12's openings (10-15 HoS / 18-25 FH) and E14's +14-16% "from store expansion" are supplier hearsay (DUP). AlphaSense AI-interviewer prompts are leading.
- **Margin flow-through:** SG&A deleveraged in 6 of 7 quarters (DUP). Gupta's own Aug-25 claim of SG&A leverage "at a low single digits comp" has not held, which weakens T2-a/T2-b unless the labor redesign delivers.
