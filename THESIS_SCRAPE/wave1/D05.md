## Evidence against / caveats
- The SOTP framing gives no EPS variance and no Q3/Q4 catalyst. DKS management has never signalled a GameChanger monetization, spin or minority sale. Without a catalyst, SOTP value is not realized.
- The SportsEngine per-user value ($25-31 per athlete) implies GameChanger is worth only $250-310M on users, which undercuts the revenue-multiple case. Bloomberg's $400-500M was a sell-side process range. One earlier source put SportsEngine at $150-200M, and the actual price is undisclosed.
- The Primer already uses an SOTP in which FL ≈ −$1.1B at $137.34. Adding GameChanger value at a premium makes FL's implied value even more negative, which may be read as a bearish signal on FL rather than as upside.
- The OBBBA cash-tax benefit is already in reported FCF. Even with it, LTM FCF ($239M) is below the dividend (~$450M).
- The GOAT stake is impaired, GOAT reportedly has repeated layoffs, and there has been no round since 2021, so the risk of a further write-down is real.
- Owned real estate is already in EBIT (no rent), so a sale-leaseback would dilute EPS.
