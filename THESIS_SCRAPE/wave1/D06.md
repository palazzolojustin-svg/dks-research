# D06: ALT-DATA Q3 FY26 READ (Aug-Oct 2026): is the DKS core running ahead of the 1.69% 3Q26E comp consensus?
Agent D06, wave 1. Apify budget $4 (spent ~$0.04; Apify account then hit its monthly hard limit). Built 2026-10-07.
Bottom line: the alt data, once the Foot Locker contamination and panel biases are corrected, points to Q3 QTD DICK'S-business comp of roughly **+2% to +5% (central ~3-3.5%)** versus 1.69% consensus. That is a modest beat (+50 to +330bps), not a blowout, and it is a deceleration from Q2's +4.9%. Industry data (Census, Academy QTD, mall traffic, sporting-goods CPI) is clearly stronger than the DKS-specific panels. Main new contribution: the Bloomberg "Placer +1.1% Aug" datapoint that the digest treats as a possible organic proxy is in fact FL-contaminated (~9pt). On a clean basis DKS-banner visits fell ~8% in August.

Variance conversion used throughout (INFERENCE): Q3 FY26 DKS-segment sales ≈ $3.5B, so +100bps comp ≈ +$35M sales. At 25-35% flow-through that is $9-12M EBIT, or **≈ $0.07-0.10 EPS per 100bps in Q3** (27.46% tax, ~90M sh). If the run-rate persisted into FY27: $15,203M × 1% = $152M sales, $38-53M EBIT, **≈ $0.31-0.43/sh per 100bps** (2.3-3.2% of $13.36).

## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **D06-1 (Placer contamination).** It is the most defensible new piece and it changes how the Street's alt data should be read. The ~9pt FL step-up in the Bloomberg Placer series is visible at both the monthly and the weekly level. Once removed, DKS-banner traffic was about −8% in August. This is not a bull smoking gun. It is the honest calibration a PM would remember, and it defends the pitch against a bear citing "traffic down": comps ran 7.5-9.5pt above banner visits all year.
2. **D06-2 + D06-4 together (bias-corrected card data plus price).** Two independent mechanisms (BSM bias correction ≈ +4-7% core; CPI +5.3% making a 1.69% comp imply −3% units) both say 1.69% is too low. Of the two, this is the most "variance-creating".
3. **D06-3 (Academy QTD).** A peer reports Q3 opening at LSD positive after a negative Q2, helped by a tax-holiday shift that also helps DKS. It is cheap to cite and verifiable in the ASO 9/9 call.

## Evidence against / caveats
- DKS-specific panels are weak. On a clean basis the August banner visits were about −8% (D06-1). The late-Sep BSM had count −4.45%, customers −5.86% and ticket only +1.17% (digest), consistent with promo-led ticket erosion. Placer's estimated Q3 QTD core comp centres around ~2.3%, close to consensus.
- DKS now trails every industry gauge (Census +10%, ASO QTD LSD after −0.4%). The FY24 share-gain spread has vanished against Census (D06-5), unless Census is contaminated.
- Regional mix: the Northeast/Midwest lagged in Aug-26 retail traffic, and Placer's BTS outlook favoured the Sun Belt.
- Margin is the bigger Q3 risk than sales. Mgmt guided DKS GM pressure "most pronounced in Q3" and SG&A deleverage of ~50bps in Q3, so a sales beat may not convert to EPS.
- The calibrations rest on a few points (3-7 quarters) and on digitized chart values (C01A §9 ±0.2pp; weekly Placer ±0.5pp). All core-comp numbers above are INFERENCE.
