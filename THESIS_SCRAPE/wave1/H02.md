[H02-2] **The forward pipeline is shifting from relocations toward new-market stores, so each opening adds less comp (NEW, fresh calculation; AGAINST the size of the "comp floor").**
- **Method:** geocoded each pipeline site with OSM Nominatim and measured the haversine distance to every live legacy DICK'S Sporting Goods page.
- **Likely relocations:** 22 of 38 geocoded HoS sites sit within 1.5 miles of an existing DSG store. Removing Orland Park (H03: the existing store stays) and Christiana (format unknown) leaves about 20 of 37 (~54%) as same-center or adjacent relocations.
  - Frisco 0.1 mi (#421 next door), King of Prussia 0.3 (#1110), Tigard 0.2 (#342), Rockaway 0.2 (#196), Sarasota 0.4 (#1348), Poughkeepsie 0.0 (#16), Broomfield 0.1 (#423), Springfield VA 0.1 (#1141), Sioux Falls 0.1 (#1124), Short Pump 0.1 (#128), Mentor 0.1 (#1118), Corpus Christi 0.3 (#1113), Cerritos 0.1 (#1195), Franklin 0.6 (#285), Novi 0.8 (#432), Peabody 0.8 (Danvers #435), San Jose 1.0 (#1409; the relocation is confirmed by SGB), Cherry Hill 1.5 (#260; relocation confirmed by 42 Freeway).
  - Boardman and Maple Grove were geocoded to town centroids, so their distances are unreliable.
- **Incremental sites:** about 16. Three are first-in-market, with no DSG within 60+ miles: **Springfield MO (nearest DSG 76 mi), Lubbock TX (105 mi), Lowell AR (65 mi).** The rest are infill 3-13 mi from a DSG: Tysons 6.8, Palm Beach Gardens 13.0, Raleigh 6.0, Hollywood FL 6.8, St. Louis Galleria 5.9, Barton Creek 8.5, Sacramento 10.4, Braintree 7.9, San Diego 3.8, Costa Mesa 3.7, Fresno 2.8, Holyoke 2.6, Eugene 1.7.
- **Comparison:** H01 counted 13 relocations among the 16 FY25 HoS openings (~81%). The forward relocation share of ~54% is lower.
- Sources: `raw\H02_census.csv`; script `scripts\H02_census_distance.py`. Accessed 2026-10-07.
- Tags: NEW | confidence M (distance is only a proxy for "relocation", and two geocodes are centroids) | AGAINST (magnitude of comp floor) / SUPPORTS (total growth) | thesis #1.
- **Variance (INFERENCE):**
  - Each relocation HoS adds about $20M of comp sales (UBS: $35M vs ~$15M displaced) ≈ 13bps of FY27E segment revenue on a full-year basis.
  - An FY27 cohort of about 19 openings at 54% relocations ≈ 10 comping relocations, vs 13 in FY25. Three fewer × 13bps × ~0.5-year weighting ≈ **−20bps of in-year FY27 comp lift vs an FY25-like mix** (−40bps annualized).
  - New-market stores are non-comp for their first year. They still add about $35M each of total revenue: 3 first-in-market sites ≈ $105M ≈ 0.7% of FY27E segment revenue once open, mostly in FY27-28.
  - Read-across to Wells Fargo: its FY27E relocation contribution of 2.7pts (up from 2.2) looks hard to reach from HoS alone. That would make WF's "underlying comp" residual look better, and the reported comp floor look worse. FH relocations also feed WF's figure, so this read is not conclusive.

## Evidence against / caveats
- No evidence of HoS count upside vs consensus for FY26, FY27 or FY28. Consensus (49/68-69/86) sits at or slightly above the company plan (14 + 18 → 67) and matches the named pipeline. FYE26 is exactly 49 with zero slack.
- The forward relocation share is lower, so the comp contribution per opening falls (H02-2). WF's bear case (relocations inflate comp) still holds for the ~54% of the pipeline that is relocations.
- Some 2027 sites are already slipping (Cerritos 2028, Peabody Feb → fall 2027, Fresno into 2028).
- At least one HoS was downsized to FH (Ann Arbor), and the count may include a 72K box (Niles).
- Distance is a proxy for relocation. Boardman and Maple Grove geocoded to town centroids, Joliet failed to geocode (existing #1239 is in Joliet, likely a relocation), and Orland Park's existing store is said to stay.
- "Undated" FY27-likely sites (Rockaway, Sarasota, Lubbock, Poughkeepsie, Hollywood, Broomfield, Braintree) come from local press or mall posts without opening dates. They could easily be FY28.
- The SGB back-half list included "Glendale, AZ", which already opened in Oct-2025. This is likely an SGB error, so treat SGB lists with care.
