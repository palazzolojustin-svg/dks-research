1. **[F5-1] "Consensus is more bearish than the bear."** Using Wells Fargo's own decomposition (2.7% FY27E relocation lift + 0.3% GameChanger), Bloomberg's 1Q27E/2Q27E DKS comps of 2.41%/2.24% imply underlying DSG comp of about **−0.6% to −0.8%**.
