## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **International Plaza HoS: $12.3M in its first three months (SEC-filed CMBS term sheet, Oct-2025).** It is the only hard, third-party, per-store HoS sales number in the public record. It annualizes to ~$40-44M vs DKS's $35M year-1 model, in a filing nobody on the sell side cites. It is easy to verify (URL above) and memorable.
2. **Freehold: ">800,000 customers in ~9 months", "about 18% of mall traffic", "strongest opening out of 35".** These are on-record landlord numbers. Against Macerich's own Placer benchmark of ~935K visits per average HoS, Freehold runs ~+14%. Pair it with #1: two stores, two independent data types, both above model.
3. **Landlord underwriting at the company's numbers, with landlord money behind it.** Washington Square's sponsor "estimates [HoS] to generate sales of $35 million annually" and is putting in $21M. Empire Mall paid $100/SF TI. Northshore is $1 ground rent. This shows the returns math is shared and funded by third parties.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Tampa and Freehold are A/A+ malls with grand-opening effects. The $12.3M covers Oct-Dec, which includes holiday, so my seasonal annualization (28-31%) may still flatter it. One quarter is not a run-rate.
- Relocations replace above-average stores: Washington Square at $18.6M leaves only ~$16.4M incremental at $35M. That backs the WF "underlying comp" decomposition (relocations sit in comp) rather than refuting it. The A-mall 2027 pipeline (Tysons, Cerritos, Washington Square) is mostly relocations from big stores.
- Landlord-named pipeline is consistent with, not above, consensus (no unit-count upside).
- HoS deals are "not cheap" for landlords (Hsieh). TI and landlord money could tighten if mall REIT capital costs rise. Some structures carry short or contingent terms (Newport sublease with Sears lease exp. 10/2027 and a go-dark right; Viewmont exp. 2028).
- Scheels is a larger destination competitor that landlords actively court ($150M+ per box).
- Legacy-store panel (thin): store sales were flat to up 2021-2024 (Empire +14% over 3 years, Cape Cod psf +14% 2021-24 but flat since 2023, Mission Viejo -1% 2022→2023). Too small to rebut "underlying comp ~1.2%" either way.
- Simon's Brea "+1,000-1,500bp" uplift is for the whole redevelopment, not HoS alone.
