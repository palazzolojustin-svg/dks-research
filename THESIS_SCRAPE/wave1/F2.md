## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **F2-1 + F2-2 together: consensus 3Q26-2Q27 core comps imply an underlying comp at or below zero. Every quarter observed since FY25 ran +0.5% to +3.7% (average +2.8%), and space is not the driver: sales per sq ft rose +4.2% in 1H26 on +1.7% sq ft.** A PM will remember "consensus is below even the bear's own math". It is a calculation, not new external data, so it is the frame. The smoking gun would be dated store-level openings for 2H26 (H02) plus traffic data (H01) showing the HoS uplift in Q3. F5-1 reached the same conclusion independently, which strengthens it.
2. **F2-3: landlord-funded share of capex rose from 9.5% to 21.4% in two years; allowances per opening rose from ~$3.5M to ~$8.1M.** It is hard, filed data, and nobody (digest, primers, brokers) has framed it this way. It shows landlords competing for HoS/FH. It is better as a returns point than an EPS point.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Management's own guide also implies a near-zero underlying comp: FY comp +2.5-4.0% after 1H +5.4% implies 2H ≈ +1.3% at the midpoint (DUP, digest/primer). Consensus simply sits on the guide, and the variance depends on the known "core guide is always beaten" pattern (FY24, FY25 beaten). The format-floor math explains *why* the guide is conservative.
- 1H26 comps were flattered by the World Cup (company: 1H higher "primarily due to the FIFA World Cup") and the Knicks run. FY27 laps both. Q4FY25 underlying was only ~+0.5% (against a +6.6% compare, with a January storm), so near-zero quarters do happen.
- Model risk in F2-1: per-store increments are not disclosed. The $20M HoS uplift uses UBS's ~$15M displaced-store figure; "very high-performing" displaced boxes (Ross Park, F1-R7) would lower it. My FY25 base-case contribution (~1.6pt) sits below Wells' 2.3pt, so the high case (H $25M) is the Wells-consistent one, and it makes consensus-implied underlying more negative (−1.1% in Q3).
- The Q3FY26 format tailwind dips as the 13-store Q3FY25 cohort anniversaries. A Q3 core-comp beat is not assured from formats alone.
- UBS: later units' CoC ~20% (F2-4). Street pipeline cut once (F2-5). Sales gross of cannibalization (F2-8). SG&A per sq ft nearly doubled (F2-9). Ticket 70-100% of comp, transactions −1.3% in Q4FY25 (DUP).
- Landlord allowances (F2-3) may partly reflect timing (allowances received in 1H for stores opening in 2H) and deal structure (more leased vs purchased real estate). Purchased HoS sites get no allowance, so the mix shift could be structural rather than landlord competition.
