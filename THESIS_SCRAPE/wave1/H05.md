# H05: Reviews & engagement as a sales proxy (Google Maps review velocity, HoS vs legacy DSG)
Agent H05, wave 1, 2026-10-07. Apify budget $8; spent ~$5.07 (ledger reconciled). Apify then refused further runs ("Monthly usage hard limit exceeded", an account-level cap).

**Bottom line:** Google review velocity does NOT show HoS beating the company's own unit economics. It roughly matches them for new and converted stores (~2.0-2.15x a legacy DSG store, against the ~2.3x implied by $35M vs ~$15M) and it runs below them for mature HoS (~1.4-1.7x, fading vs legacy y/y). There is also a weak cannibalisation signal. Net: AGAINST "HoS out-earns the model", NEUTRAL-to-supportive on "conversions roughly double a store" (the comp-floor mechanic). The 2026 chain-wide review surge is a review-solicitation artefact (Target shows the same, Old Navy does not), so Google reviews cannot be used as a Q3 traffic read.

Method (all findings): Apify `compass/crawler-google-places` (Google Maps), accessed 2026-10-07. 44 HoS listings (all found by a US-wide "DICK'S House of Sport" search) + 36 legacy DSG listings in the same metros (30 usable) + 4 old DSG listings sitting at HoS addresses + 9 non-DKS retail listings. All dated Google reviews since 2025-01-01 (non-DKS: since 2025-04-01): 7,130 DKS reviews + 1,106 non-DKS. Velocity = reviews per listing per month; "vs legacy" = divided by the mean legacy-listing count in the same calendar months, which removes platform-wide and seasonal effects. HoS opening months are INFERENCE from step-changes in each listing's series, cross-checked with H07 TX permit first-sales dates (Dallas 2025-09-10, Live Oak 2025-10-15, Arlington 2026-06-17), Jersey City 2025-09-18 and Crabtree Sep-2026 (H04). Novelty: grep of digest/primers/KNOWN_BRIEF/wave1 for review/Yelp/velocity found nothing → all items below are NEW (fresh web data + fresh calculation).
Data: raw/H05_reviews.csv (one row per review: date, stars, text length, first 300 chars; no reviewer names), raw/H05_monthly_matrix.csv, raw/H05_store_velocity.csv, raw/H05_event_study.csv, raw/H05_legacy_monthly_mean.csv, raw/H05_nondks_reviews.csv, raw/H05_hos_locations.csv, raw/H05_controls.csv, raw/H05_gmaps_hos_run1.csv, raw/H05_apify_dumps/ (full Apify JSON). Scripts: scripts/H05_extract_reviews.py, H05_analyze.py, H05_groups.py, H05_event.py, H05_cannibal.py, H05_nondks.py, H05_store_pages.py.

## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **H05-1 + H05-2 together (conversion ≈2.0x, new site ≈2.15x legacy).** This is the first store-level, cross-sectional, dated proxy for "HoS vs the store it replaced" in the folder. It is hard and novel, but it points to "in line or slightly below company math", not upside. Use it defensively: the ~2x step-up is real and repeatable across 9 conversions, so a 15-20 store a year opening program mechanically delivers ~110-130bps of comp. That covers most of the 1.6-2.4% consensus core comp, which supports the "low bar" framing even if per-store productivity is not above plan.
2. **H05-3 (mature HoS fade -10.8% vs legacy y/y)** is the most memorable item, but it cuts AGAINST. A PM should know it before a bear raises it.
None of these is a long-side smoking gun on per-store sales upside.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Reviews are a proxy for visits, not dollars. HoS sell bigger tickets (golf, footwear), and many visitors use free experiences (climbing, cages), which can raise or lower reviews per $. H01 cites Placer.ai (2024-03) saying the 12 HoS got "5-6x visits per location vs the chain". Our 1.4-2.3x review ratio is far below that, so either review propensity per visit is much lower at HoS or Placer's chain base includes small formats. Treat the ratios as directional.
- The legacy controls are NOT a random chain sample: they are metro stores near HoS (Boston, NYC/NJ, Miami, Pittsburgh, Raleigh, OKC/Tulsa, Tampa, Dallas, Phoenix, Columbus), so probably above-average volume. That would understate HoS ratios. Field House stores could not be separated: Google has no "Field House" listings (a US-wide search returned 0), so a few controls may be FH remodels.
- Opening months are inferred. For in-place conversions the pre-period may include construction disruption, which inflates the uplift. Post windows for 5 of 9 conversions are only 1-3 months and include opening novelty.
- The scraper returned no data for 2 controls (Wesley Chapel FL, Pembroke Pines FL), which were excluded from the event study. H05_groups.py's raw LEG summary still includes them as zeros. Use H05_event.py figures.
- The 2026 solicitation surge raises all levels. Ratios assume the program rolled out evenly across HoS and legacy stores, which is consistent with the identical content shift in both groups.
