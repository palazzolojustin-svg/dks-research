## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **[F1-1] The consensus-implied ex-format comp (~0% for FY27 vs 3.4% trailing).** Built from consensus's own store path, so it cannot be dismissed as "your store count is different". It turns the Wells Fargo bear case around: even if relocations add ~2 pts, consensus is pricing the rest of the business for a cliff from ~3.4% to ~0% that has never happened in the 8-quarter record (lowest quarter 0.65%, against a +6.4% lap). It is fresh, quantified and reproducible (script), and a PM would remember it.
2. **[F1-2] Future-store leases signed: $352M → $756M (+115%).** An audited, hard number showing the pipeline is contracted. Pair it with the BBG HoS path 49/68/86 to argue the count is low-risk or beatable.
3. **[F1-3] Landlords paying more: H1 FY26 allowances +83% y/y (13% → 17% of gross capex).** Hard-number evidence for "mall developers love House of Sport" and for falling net capex per store, and consensus under-models it in 2H FY26.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **Chain foot traffic was negative before the deal (BURIED).** Bloomberg ALTD Placer DKS visits y/y averaged −4.0% from Oct-23 to Aug-25 (e.g., Oct-24 −9.5, Feb-25 −13.3, Jun-25 −9.4), while comps ran +4-5%. BSM transaction count was −3.5% (DUP). Reported comp transactions were only +0.3% in FY25 and −1.3% in Q4 FY25 (DUP). This is consistent with WF: comps are ticket- and relocation-driven. (C01A › ALTD §9d)
- **The $35M HoS headline is gross.** The Nov-2025 slide footnote reads: "Returns incl remodel, lease & purchased deal structures; sales gross before cannibalization" (BURIED; F4 also has it).
- **Deliberate self-cannibalization.** Ross Park HoS opened less than a mile from a "very high-performing, 50,000 box" ("we purposely disrupted that") (Gupta, Barclays 2024-12-03; BURIED). So the $20M net uplift in F1-1 may overstate the true chain-level increment.
- **Relocations stay in the comp base** (Gupta, 2024-11-26; BURIED). This confirms WF's adjustment is mechanically right. The debate is only about the size of the ex-format remainder (F1-1).
- **The mature-store sample is small.** "At the beginning of this year [2024], we had only two stores that had comped fully in the second year" (Gupta, MS 2024-12-03; BURIED).
- **Consensus already sits mid-guide.** The FY26 guide (2.5-4%, held 2026-08-25) implies 2H FY26 comp of ~−0.4% to +2.6%, so consensus at 1.6-1.7% is not below the guide. F1-1 is about FY27.
- Other caveats:
  - Cohort assumptions (quarterly opening timing, FH uplift, relocation share) drive the level in F1-1 but not the direction.
  - The FYE25 $756M lease figure includes some FL.
  - The $1B of new 6.2%/6.9% notes (+~$66M/yr coupon) is not in consensus interest (DUP; an EPS headwind).
