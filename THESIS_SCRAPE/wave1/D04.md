# D04: BRAND ALLOCATION & VENDOR DYNAMICS (wave 1, thesis #2 discovery)
Agent D04 | 2026-10-07 | scope: concrete evidence that brands are steering scarce or premium product to DKS (exclusives, new brands, distribution choices, brands cutting other doors, vendor terms, MAP).
Variance conventions: FY27E DKS segment revenue $15,203M, segment OI $1,647M, 89.09M shares, 27.46% tax, so $1M of segment OI = $0.0081/share and 10bps of comp ≈ $15.2M of sales.

## Bottom line
I found no standalone thesis #2 in brand allocation that is both new and big enough to move FY27 EPS by more than a few cents. The strongest new evidence is about house-of-brands ACCESS, and it lands in House of Sport. In 2026 a run of premium or scarce brands chose HoS as their first or only US big-box door: Arc'teryx (first-ever DKS entry, 15 HoS, Fall/Winter 2026), Vuori (12 HoS, May 2026), Jordan Strength (DKS is the only third-party retailer, from 2026-10-13) and Gymshark (12 HoS, Oct 2025). This is best used as SUPPORT FOR THESIS #1: HoS productivity has a brand-access mechanism, not just square footage. It should not be pitched as a separate thesis. One quantitative datapoint, ASICS US wholesale ≈50% DKS, shows how dominant DKS already is as the scaled outlet for performance-running brands. It is a single 2025 expert view.

## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **Arc'teryx → 15 HoS (D04-1).** It is new, dated, comes from a public-company CFO on an earnings call, and is the cleanest proof that the most selective premium brands now treat HoS as a "premium" door. A PM will remember "Arc'teryx chose House of Sport". Use it under thesis #1 as the brand-access reason HoS can beat the $35M model. It is weak on dollars.
2. **ASICS ≈50% of US wholesale through DKS (D04-2).** It is the only hard share-of-brand number I found. It shows DKS is the scaled gatekeeper for performance running, the category Nike, ASICS, HOKA and On are all leaning into. It is weaker because it comes from one 2025 expert.
3. (Honourable mention) **Nike FQ1 FY27 quote (D04-4).** Nike names DKS first among channels where performance sells through, while cutting the retro supply FL depends on. It is useful for the "core is insulated" argument.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Arc'teryx is explicitly "test and learn" in 15 doors. It could stall.
- Salomon, Amer's sneaker-culture brand, chose Foot Locker (44 doors), JD and Nordstrom for US wholesale, not DKS (Amer Q2 2026 call).
- New Balance 9060 (a lifestyle franchise) was selling at 20% off ($128 vs $160) at DKS (Gear Patrol, 2026-09-22). That fits management's "promotional through Q4" warning.
- HOKA ran up to 30% off Bondi 9 and Clifton 10 on its own DTC site (nj.com, Sep-2026). Brands discounting DTC are the Q2 cut driver.
- Nike is pushing its Family Footwear channel ($65-85 core footwear), which is more Nike in a cheaper competing channel. Jordan already has 55 Academy shops and is at Famous Footwear (known).
- Scheels: a 300,000 sq ft store in Littleton CO approved with a 30-year tax incentive (Denver Post, 2026-09-16). It is a premium experiential competitor expanding into DKS/HoS trade areas (for D01).
- The Pokémon drawing at 140-167% over MSRP drew press criticism ("scalper territory"). There is brand-trust risk with the young collector demographic.
- The ASICS ≈50% figure comes from one 2025 expert. The ASICS revenue inputs in my variance math are assumptions.
- The DKS segment did not show an exclusives-driven gross-margin beat: Q2 FY26 core GM +80bps was attributed to DMN and GameChanger, and FY26 guided GM "decline slightly".
