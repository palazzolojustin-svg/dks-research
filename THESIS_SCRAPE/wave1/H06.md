## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **H06-1 + H06-2: "DKS re-posted ~15,400 requisitions in four weeks (mid-Jun to mid-Jul 2026), ~77% of 2025's full-year requisition volume, converting every DSG/GGG store to the Teammate / Specialist / Team Captain structure."** Hard, dated, reproducible from public Workday and Wayback data. It proves Built to Win is fully implemented (not a pilot) and pins the cutover to the start of Q3 FY26, which no one in the corpus has dated.
2. **H06-3: Team Captain pay floor ≈ ASM floor and +36% over the old Lead floor.** This is the number a PM will press on: the labor reset is a role-mix and hours story, not a pay-cut story. Use it to show we have stress-tested thesis #2.
3. **H06-4: a hiring-implied HoS count of 48 vs consensus 49 at FYE26.** It is a useful honesty check for thesis #1 (the store-count piece of the comp floor is in line, not ahead).

## Evidence against / caveats
- H06-3: posted ranges are pay-transparency ranges, not actual wages. State mix differs between Team Captain and Lead samples. The old-Lead comparator is mostly HoS/Golf Galaxy roles (DSG pre-model Lead pay was not posted in archived sitemaps).
- The requisition counter covers the whole Workday tenant (stores, DCs, corporate; no Foot Locker reqs appear on this DSG site, but whether FL shares the tenant's counter was NOT verified). The Jun-Jul spike is administrative re-posting, not net new heads. Total open postings are not a demand signal YoY because of the structural break.
- Hiring-implied HoS count (48) could understate: a store opening in January 2027 could start hourly hiring in Nov-Dec 2026.
- HoS staffing and pay premiums (H06-7) are a cost the bull case must carry. The WF model (~168 employees per HoS) is already known.
- Nothing in the postings states store sales volumes or headcount targets (searched descriptions for "million", "volume", "sq ft", "teammates" and similar: none).
