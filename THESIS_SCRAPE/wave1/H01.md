## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **H01-1, the Braintree zoning exhibit.** It is DKS's own internal Advan analysis, unpublished in investor materials, and it sits in a public town record. A PM will remember "+14% mall traffic in year 1 and no decay in years 2-3, while comparable malls fell 7%", and "HoS draws 2X the visits and +20% the dwell of a DICK'S". It is hard, dated and third-party-measured (Advan, Green Street), and it directly answers "is HoS traffic a one-off relocation bump?"
2. **H01-3, the chain-definition bias.** The bear's alt-data point ("DICK'S traffic is negative") rests on a Placer series that excludes the HoS boxes the traffic moved into. Re-weighted, the DKS-banner family's traffic was positive in Q1-26. This is novel and quantifiable, but the decomposition needs one more check: Bloomberg's brand filter (see Leads).
3. **H01-2, the visit-to-sales gap.** HoS draws 2-6x the visits of a legacy store on ~2.3x the sales. That is monetization headroom, and a story a PM can explain in a sentence. Confidence is lower on the size.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **Third-party traffic panels are negative into Q3 FY26.** Advan has DKS negative through Jul-Aug 2026 with ticket "likely down", while Academy is positive. Bloomberg Placer is ~−1% after the FL lap. If the market reads these naively, they will weigh on sentiment before the ~Nov-24/25 print.
- **Legacy same-store traffic was genuinely negative for most of 2025.** Placer DSG same-store visits were −4.5% (Feb-Jul-25) and −4.6% (Dec-25). This partly supports Wells Fargo's "underlying traffic negative" bear case for non-HoS boxes. The improvement to +1.2% (~Feb-26) and −1.6% per location (Q1-26) is mixed.
- **The DKS deck is a leasing pitch with selection bias.** It uses 4 early, high-quality mall HoS (Victor, Knoxville, Minnetonka, Johnson City), a broker-chosen comp set, COVID-adjusted timeframes, and landlord quotes that are partly DICK'S-led interviews. Mall-level traffic is not store comp.
- **Visit multiples above sales multiples imply lower sales per visit at HoS.** HoS traffic is partly non-buying experiential traffic (climbing, skating, birthday parties).
- **DKS's Corpus Christi projection of 2M+ visits/yr (H03) looks high against Placer.** Top HoS stores in Placer's free tool run ~78-110K visits/month (~0.9-1.3M/yr; Dec-24 peaks ~170-183K). Macerich's Freehold figure is ~0.8M drawn into the center. Different methodologies (door counters vs device panel) likely explain it, but treat 2M as company-optimistic.
- **The Bloomberg-vs-public Placer gap may be partial Foot Locker coverage rather than HoS.** It is unresolved. The step-up began w/e 21-Sep-25, two weeks after the FL close.
