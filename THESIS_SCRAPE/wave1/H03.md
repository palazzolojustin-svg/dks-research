## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **H03-1 Corpus Christi council record** of DKS's own projection: $20M -> $40M revenue and 626K -> 2M+ visits from a relocation. It is DKS's number, in a public government document dated Sep-2026, and it is the only per-store before/after sales and traffic figure for a relocation outside company slides. PM line: "DKS told a city council that relocating one $20M store into an HoS doubles sales and triples traffic." It answers the bear point that the comp is only relocations and underlying traffic is weak: the relocations ARE the traffic engine, at 3x.
2. **H03-2 Joliet Exhibit I**: DKS's own approved line-item HoS budget ($38.0M), with taxpayers reimbursing up to $37M and DKS taking title. Hard, specific and memorable ("the city is paying for the climbing wall"). It reframes HoS returns: the company's 25% cash-on-cash is an average that includes subsidized, rent-free boxes worth far more.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Corpus Christi: $40M is a projection for 2028 in a document drafted by the mall/city to justify an incentive, so it carries promotional bias. Revenue per visit falls ~37% ($31.9 -> ~$20), so many visits are low-spend (field, cages, mall cross-shopping). The +$20M increment equals, and does not exceed, the UBS framework.
- Joliet: the money flows to DKS's developer, not to DKS. The DHOS purchase price in the 2025-11-12 PSA is not public, so DKS's true net outlay is inferred. The subsidy is conditional (co-tenant leases, opening by 2028, $1M/yr decay), so it is not repeatable everywhere. Norman shows councils can say no.
- Ground-up HoS costs (~$38M gross, $317/sf) run well above the ~$28M average. Without subsidy, new-build HoS returns would sit below the company average.
- Several flagship sites open in 2028-2029, not 2027, so there is no evidence of a pipeline above consensus.
- Relocations close the old store (Cedar Rapids, Cerritos). The comp benefit is net of the old store's sales, as already modelled by UBS/WF.
- Cedar Rapids staffing of ~200 vs 110 planned suggests labor intensity can run above plan in year 1.
