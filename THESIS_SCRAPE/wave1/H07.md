# H07: SALES-TAX & LOCAL ECONOMIC DATA (smoking-gun hunt), wave 1
Agent: H07 | 2026-10-07 | Thesis #1 (HoS/FH)
Method in one line: use government sales-tax data, which is quantitative and published on a fixed schedule, to measure how much category sales in a city or county actually rose when a House of Sport (HoS) opened. Two clean sources were found: New York county x NAICS 4511/4591 taxable sales (quarterly, 2013 to 2026Q1) and Texas city x sector taxable sales and permit records (quarterly to 2026Q1, permits live to 2026-10-03).
Novelty: no sales-tax, taxable-sales or QCEW data appears anywhere in the digest, primers, KNOWN_BRIEF or other wave-1 files (grep done 2026-10-07). Every computed series below is NEW.

## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **Victor/Ontario County (H07-1).** This is government tax-return data, not a model or a survey. It shows the first HoS doubled its county's sporting-goods taxable sales (~$34M/yr → ~$68M/yr), ranked #1 of 50 upstate counties for growth, and held for 5 years. The net county gain (+$19-28M/yr) matches or exceeds the +$20M per relocation the bulls assume, and it is a lower bound for DKS. A PM would remember "the county's whole sporting-goods market doubled the quarter the store opened, and it never came back down." Caveat: it is n=1 and the flagship store, and the later NY stores (H07-2) were weaker. Present it honestly, alongside H07-2.
2. **Live Oak TX (H07-3).** This is the only clean read on a 2025-vintage, net-new HoS. It independently confirms ~$30-37M omni year-1 sales from tax returns. It supports "the unit economics are real", not "consensus is too low".
3. None of these is a clean upside smoking gun on its own. The combined NY panel (+$26M, +$7M, ~0) points to **wide dispersion around the company's average**: strong where the HoS is the only big-format sporting store in a growing trade area (Victor), weak in shrinking or multi-door markets (Broome, Albany).

## Evidence against / caveats
- H07-2 (Johnson City +$5-9M, Latham ~0) is direct evidence that some relocations add far less than +$20M. A 3-store NY average of ~+$12M would imply ~100-115bps of HoS comp contribution, not 150-170bps.
- County/city category data measures *market* change, not DKS sales. Share gains from in-county specialty competitors (same NAICS) are invisible, which understates DKS's gain. Share pulled from general merchandisers or online (different NAICS) shows up, which inflates the measured "category" gain. Hobby and music stores share the category.
- Victor opened at the 2021 pandemic peak in sporting goods. The controls absorb the statewide boom, but the flagship status and novelty may not be repeatable.
- Texas: city-level Retail Trade is the finest public industry cut. NAICS 451110/459110 is not published by city, so the Live Oak read has wide error bands.
- 2-into-1 consolidations (Baybrook, Polaris, F&S conversions) lower the per-opening comp increment vs the simple relocation maths.
- NY 2023-2026 data is still status "P" (preliminary) and gets revised.
