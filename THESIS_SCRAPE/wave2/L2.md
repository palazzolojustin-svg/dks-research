## Top findings (ranked)

[L2-1] **What incumbents were actually paid contradicts the posted-band read (L3-1/L3-2, H06-3) that Built to Win re-priced store leadership upward.**

Posted bands say otherwise, but the actual offers made on 2026-06-22 were mostly cuts or flat pay. ASMs were demoted into the new Team Captain layer at a lower salary. Lead→Captain conversions got cents-per-hour raises.

I hand-coded 41 self-reported outcomes; 4 are hearsay and excluded from the statistics below.

Hourly outcomes (n=31):
- 17 cuts: median **−$3.00/hr**, mean −$3.58.
- 6 flat.
- 8 raises: median **+$0.24/hr**.
- Across all 37 non-hearsay outcomes: 59% cuts, 16% flat, 24% raises.

**Lead/key-carrier → Team Captain (n=9):** −$3.00, 0, +$0.10, +$0.20 (Ohio, "less than $24"), +$0.20, +$0.23, +$0.25, +$0.50, +$1.00. Median **+$0.20/hr**. Users' summaries:

[L2-4] **The actual mechanics of the redesign, which no filing or release discloses.**

New roles (from posts):
- Team Captains: Apparel/Footwear, Softlines, Hardlines/Team, On-field Ops, Off-field Ops, End Zone & Loyalty (front end). Captains are "salaried non-exempt": paid a guaranteed 38 hours, must fill shortfalls with PTO, overtime above 40h is "highly frowned upon", and one district is "on a strict notice… not to go over 40" (1sknw4f, 1ubpnfn, 1vs7h5a, 1wd3zhm).
- Specialists: footwear, apparel, sports & outdoor, golf, running, on/off-field ops.
- Advisors (ex-CSS) and Teammates.

Eliminated: hourly Leads (department and key-carrier), PSL, FFL, Omni lead, FE lead, and the store AA (→ remote district role).

Staffing by volume tier:
- "sub 10 mill stores" get fewer specialists, e.g., "only getting 1 ops specialist" (1sknw4f).
- A GGG store gets "2 specialist, 3 captain, 1 ASM".
- BTW "stops at 28 million stores". HoS (e.g., Ross Park, Katy, Dallas) are NOT restructured (1wtqi8s, 1ug3jjl). This matches H06's finding that HoS kept old titles.

Placement process:
- Online "AI" assessment for leads and ASMs, initially "70% weighing factor and SMs decision… 30%"; later reported as SM/DM/HR choice (1ucukuk).
- Offers came in Workday at 9pm with 48 hours to accept and "no counters" (1ucclqu, 1udwc04).
- Severance went only to those offered lower pay, plus AAs regardless (05-07 memo, 1t6n0qg). Reported amounts: **$17K** (20+ years) and **$4K** (~15 years) (1ucclqu).

Timeline:
- Plan leaked on Reddit ~mid-March 2026.
- Store-level announcement Mon **2026-04-13**, a day before the IR release.
- Assessments in the last week of April; district placement meetings in late May.
- Offers 06-22; the planned 1-month transition was "axed".

## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **L2-1, realized pay vs posted bands.** Wave 2's posting analysis (L3) said Built to Win added a salaried Captain layer at ASM pay, costing +$51-77M. Dozens of the affected employees describe the opposite:
   - ASMs demoted to Captain at a median −$11K/yr ($62K → $50.5K);
   - key-carrier leads re-titled Captain for a median +$0.20/hr;
   - 59% of reported outcomes are pay cuts (median −$3/hr);
   - the store AA role eliminated nationwide.

   This is new, dated, specific and memorable: "the Street hasn't credited a dollar of Built to Win savings, and the people who lived it say it was a pay-grade reset across ~734 stores". It needs the honest caveat that the sample is biased.
2. **L2-3, the hours squeeze from July.** Specialists sit at ≤28h vs 32h promised, a Captain req went unfilled for lack of hours, and "didn't add any labor hours". Combined with the cutover dates, Q3 FY26 (reported ~Nov 24-25; 10-Q early Dec) is the first full quarter. It sets up a falsifiable call: DSG personnel deleverage narrows sharply from +43bp.
3. **L2-5, the store-manager wave rumoured for November** is optional upside with low confidence.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **Selection bias:** r/DicksSportingGoods skews toward aggrieved hourly staff. Positive accounts exist but are rarer: "In our store it's been super successful… created structure" (1upgabt); "the restructure really needed to happen… some stores with a lead who works 3 days a week" (1ug3jjl); "it's coming together… now we have all 6 [captains]" (1wtqi8s). The magnitude data (L2-1) is self-reported and partly hearsay (flagged).
- **Captain overtime, training hours, vacancies and turnover** offset part of the savings in FY26, though most of this is one-off. Captains reportedly often work 40-48h.
- **The rate effect is two-sided.** L3 shows new-hire ASM bands +20-32%. Wherever ASMs are re-hired at the new band, cost rises. L3-4's ASM-posting freeze (−40%) suggests few are being hired.
- **Service/comp risk (thesis #1 link):** truck and floor-set backlogs, mass departures of 10-25-year veterans, a pulse-survey collapse and heavy Scorecard+/credit-card pitching pressure. A weak holiday execution would be blamed on BTW.
- **HoS excluded:** the most labor-intensive boxes (~168 staff) are untouched for now, and HoS is growing.
- **Legal/headline risk:** many threads discuss lawsuits (pay-transparency, NLRA, age, since >40s can revoke a severance within 7 days) and unionizing (UFCW/RWDSU/IWW links). No evidence of filings.
- Benefits changes (life insurance, maternity) are disputed and immaterial.
