## VERDICT (the Track P question)
**Peers show that category prices are rising about 4-7%, so DKS ticket will probably beat the +1.0-1.25% consensus. In every peer that discloses a split, though, the higher ticket comes with falling transactions or units, and the deepest-discount footwear channels have negative AUR. Peer evidence supports "ticket > +1.25%" (likely +2.5-3.5% in 3Q26). It does NOT support "comp beat from price", because the transaction offset eats most of it. As a standalone thesis #2, price/ticket is worth only about +$0.10-0.20/sh (INFERENCE below). It is better used as a supporting exhibit for the comp argument than as its own pitch point.** Confidence M.

## Variance translation (INFERENCE, stated assumptions)
- Consensus DKS core: 3Q26E comp 1.69% (ticket +1.25 / txn +1.5); 4Q26E 1.59% (+1.0 / +1.57). 2H FY26 consensus DKS segment revenue ≈ $7,581M (D03).
- Base case from peers: DKS ticket +2.5-3.0% in 3Q26 and +2.0% in 4Q26 (category price ≈+4-5% per ASO/Circana; footwear ≤+1-2 per Nike/SHOE/JD; DKS's own ticket floor was +3.6% in Q2 FY26 but includes mix), against transactions ≈0.5-1.0pt below consensus, using the peer elasticity of roughly 1pt of transactions lost per 1pt of ticket gained above trend at ASO, damped for DKS's higher-income customer.
- Net 2H comp ≈ +0.6-1.0pt vs consensus → $45-76M of revenue × ~20% incremental margin = $9-15M OI → **≈ +$0.07-0.12/sh** for FY26 ($1M pre-tax = $0.00814/sh). FY27 1H (consensus ticket +1%): a similar +0.5pt net = $38M × 20% = $7.6M ≈ +$0.06/sh. **Total ≈ +$0.10-0.20/sh.** This is too small and too uncertain to be thesis #2 on its own, and it overlaps the known "low bar for core comp" point.
- Bull (units hold like DKS's own history, txn ≥ +0.5): ticket +3% → comp +3.5% vs 1.69 → +$0.22-0.25/sh for 2H (matches D03-1). Peer evidence does not support units holding.

## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **P5-1, the ASO ticket/transaction series.** A clean 10-quarter, filing-sourced exhibit. The direct sporting goods comp shows ticket going from +0.5% to +4.9% as tariff inflation arrived, so the category price tailwind is real and ~5%. It also shows Academy's ticket overtaking DKS's in Q2 FY26, which says DKS held price (consistent with the known "investing in price" Q2 comment). A PM would remember "ASO ticket +4.9%, consensus DKS ticket +1.25%", but the honest second half of the slide (ASO transactions −5.3%) neutralizes most of the comp upside.
2. **P5-3, Circana junior team sports ASP +7% / units −3%.** Hard third-party category data from a DKS-dominant category (team sports and baseball), showing price-led dollar growth.
3. **P5-2, the ASO Q2 call: tariff refunds reinvested in lower prices and a more promotional Q4.** Not a smoking gun for the long. It is the best evidence that 4Q26 ticket upside is capped.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Units/transactions fall wherever ticket rises (ASO −5.3%, DSW ≈−8%, SHOE units −4%, Circana units −3% in team sports and down in footwear). The ticket-vs-consensus gap overstates the comp gap.
- Footwear AUR is weak: Nike NA footwear ASP −1pt (Jun-Aug 26, "higher discounts", P1-a), Shoe Station implied AUR ≈−3.2%, JD price investments and promotional Finish Line. Footwear is DKS's largest category.
- IEEPA tariff refunds (SCOTUS Feb-2026, refunds Apr-Jul 2026) are being passed back to consumers through price rollbacks/promotions (ASO; Beige Book Dallas/Chicago). This removes the tariff-pricing driver into 4Q26/1H27, and annualization of 2025 price increases starts ~2H26 (SHOE: prior-year pre-tariff price increases).
- Known card data (digest): BSM DKS ticket only +1.17% in w/e 27-Sep-26, close to consensus +1.25%. It is a single week, but it is the only DKS-specific Q3 ticket datapoint and it does not show a beat.
- DKS's ticket stopped tracking above the category in Q2 FY26 (−1.3pt vs ASO). Management chose to invest in price (Q2 call, known). If that persists, DKS ticket ≈ category minus 1pt ≈ +3-4%, still above consensus; but transactions are where the comp is decided.
- The ASO call details are from a secondary summary (globeandmail/TipRanks-style), not a verbatim transcript.
- Circana's footwear H1 release does not give the ASP % itself (only "ASP up, units down"); the circana.com page now 404s (raw\P5_pages\www_circana_com_*.txt).
