# L6: WARN notices & layoffs (DICK'S Sporting Goods / Golf Galaxy / Going, Going, Gone! / DKS-owned Foot Locker HQ), 2025-2026
Agent: L6 | Wave 2 | Run 2026-10-07 | Thesis #2 (FY27 store-cost reset / labor discipline)

**Bottom line:** No WARN notice was filed for any DICK'S-family entity anywhere in the US in 2025-2026. I checked 43 states plus NJ, TX, NY and NC directly. The only notice since the 2020 COVID furloughs is a pre-acquisition WSS (Foot Locker) DC in Houston. That makes the null result informative in one state. New Jersey has, since 2023, aggregated terminations statewide, and other retailers (Macy's, Saks) filed "Statewide" NJ notices for multi-store cuts in 2026. DICK'S filed none. So Built to Win cannot have involved roughly 50 or more outright terminations in New Jersey, which caps company-wide terminations at about 1,500, or ~2 per store (INFERENCE). The redesign was therefore mostly re-grading and reassignment ("demoted" roles, ">3,200 leadership opportunities"), not a layoff. Any FY27 savings will come gradually through pay grades, hours and attrition, not as one visible headcount step. Elsewhere WARN is structurally blind to store-role changes (per-site thresholds of 25-50+), so absence is not evidence of no savings.

## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **L6-2, the NJ statewide-aggregation null.** It is a hard, checkable, legally grounded constraint on what Built to Win was: a reassignment and re-grading of store roles, not a mass termination. Peers Macy's and Saks did trigger NJ statewide notices in 2026, which gives a clean contrast.
   - Bear-case use: it rebuts "they just fired managers and savings will be a one-time step".
   - Bull-case use: savings, if any, come through pay grades and hours, which flow gradually into FY27.
   - Limitation: this is evidence about the form of the program, not its size. It is not a smoking gun for above-consensus leverage on its own.
2. **L6-1, a nationwide WARN null across ~60K notices.** It cleanly removes the "hidden HQ/DC restructuring" possibility for the DICK'S segment.

There is no true smoking gun from this avenue. WARN cannot see per-store changes of 2-5 roles.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **WARN is structurally blind to store-role redesigns.** Federal WARN needs 50+ employment losses at one site (and ≥33% of staff, unless 500+). CA needs 50+ at a 75+-employee establishment; NY needs 25+ (≥33%) or 250+; IL 25+ (≥33%). A DICK'S store employs ~40-80 people (2020 CA furlough notices list 25-81 per store, mean 41), so removing 2-5 leader roles per store never triggers a notice. Demotions with continued employment are not "employment losses" anywhere. Absence of notices therefore says nothing about the size of savings, only about their form (L6-2).
- **The "reassignment, not termination" read cuts against a quick FY27 step-down in personnel $.** If leaders were re-graded into the >3,200 new Lead/Specialist roles, savings depend on pay differentials and attrition and are partly offset by the new roles. This echoes WA2/WA3's 2023 "largely offset by strategic talent investments" warning.
- The NJ bound assumes terminations fell inside a 30/90-day window and were spread evenly. A rolling, staggered rollout could have kept NJ under 50 even with more terminations company-wide, so the ~1,500 cap is soft.
- NY notices for Apr-Dec 2025 were not retrievable (the dashboard holds only 2026; the Wayback CDX returned 503). That is low risk, since the redesign is a 2026 event.
- MN, NH, NV, AR, WV and WY are not in the BLN archive. MN DEED returned SSL errors. These hold ~35 DSG stores (~4.5%).
- WSS and Foot Locker store closures (51 WSS closures by Q1 FY26, 113 total closures in 1H FY26) are an excluded topic and were not pursued.
