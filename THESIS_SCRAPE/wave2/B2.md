Agent B2 | 2026-10-07 | Goal: estimate combined DICK'S-business visits (DSG + HoS + FH; Foot Locker excluded) y/y by month from Jan-25 to Sep-26; separate legacy-store visits from HoS visits; measure the visit-to-comp gap; and say what it implies for Q3 FY26 (Aug-Oct 2026) legacy comp against the 1.69% consensus.
Builds on: H01-3 and H01-5 (public Placer prints; the HoS-exclusion hypothesis), D06-1 (FL-contamination calibration of the Bloomberg Placer series).

**Method breakthrough (NEW).** Every Placer.ai Anchor chart from 2024 to May-2026 is an Infogram embed. - DICK'S-banner visits and same-store visits;
- Academy;
- the Mall Index;
- a 2023-24 HoS-vs-DSG visits-per-location chart.

## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **B2-3 + B2-1 combined: "Consensus needs DICK'S legacy traffic to get worse in Sep-Oct than in August."**
   - The alt data the bears cite excludes every HoS box: Bloomberg equals the DSG banner to within 0.1-0.6pp in all pre-deal months.
   - Legacy same-store traffic had recovered to positive in 4 of 7 months from Oct-25 to Apr-26.
   - Consensus 1.69% implies about −6% legacy traffic for all of Q3.
   - It is concrete, built from Placer's own exact numbers, and falsifiable at the Nov-24/25 print.
2. **B2-2: "DICK'S banner-family traffic is up, not down."** Combined DSG + HoS visits were positive for 4 straight quarters from Q3FY25 to Q2FY26 (+1.2 to +5.7%). Comp transactions (+0.5 to +1.3%) sit right on that line. The "visits −5%, comps +5%" puzzle is mostly a chain-definition artifact.
3. **B2-4: legacy DSG stores beat Academy's same-store traffic by 1.6-3.9pp.** A clean share-gain signal that turns the "Academy beats Dick's" headline around.

## Evidence against / caveats
- August 2026 legacy traffic was weak: est. −5.6% same-store, −6.7% banner. The gap to the open-air market widened to about −13pp (B2-6). Advan agrees on Jul-Aug.
- The comp-to-visits gap (7.8pp average) includes ticket. Ticket is decelerating: BSM ticket was +3.5% in Aug and +1.2% in late Sep, and Advan says "average ticket likely down". A 1-2pp narrower gap would lower every implied comp above by 1-2pp, bringing the central Q3 case close to consensus.
- May-Aug 2026 DSG values are Bloomberg-derived. The add-on's composition is unresolved (B2-1), so ±1pp.
- The combined DSG + HoS series is a model (the multiple m and the timing of HoS openings are assumed). No public Placer HoS-chain series for 2025-26 was found.
- Only two post-lap weeks exist, and weekly Placer data is noisy (±4pp week to week). The late-September improvement is fragile.
- The legacy same-store visits gap to comp was only 4.8pp in Q4FY25. In a promotional quarter like Q3FY26 ("GM pressure most pronounced in Q3"), the gap could be low.
