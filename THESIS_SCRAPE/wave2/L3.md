Agent L3 | 2026-10-07 | builds on wave-1 H06 (Workday census 2026-10-07; posting surge; Team Captain pay; old Lead 316→83). Everything below is new relative to H06 unless marked.
Base for variance: FY27E DSG revenue $15,203M; 10bp = $15.2M = $0.124/sh; $1M pre-tax = $0.00814/sh. Built to Win scope = DICK'S + GGG stores ≈ 734 (DSG 630 + FH 52 + GGG 52 at 2026-08-01).

## Top findings (ranked)

[L3-1] **The "before" pay grid, recovered, shows Built to Win added a salaried layer and re-graded the ASM job upward. Posted pay bands alone say it costs money; any savings must come from headcount and hours, which postings cannot show (AGAINST a pure savings story, and it reframes thesis #2).**

I recovered 1,053 pre-cutover DICK'S postings: 530 Wayback-archived Workday job JSONs plus 523 archived old-careers-site job pages. Of these, 730 are store postings with a posted "Targeted Pay Range" (428 on 2025 requisitions, 300 on Jan-Jun 2026 requisitions created before the Jun-15 cutover). HoS, Golf Galaxy and Public Lands titles are excluded so the comparison is DICK'S-only. I compared them with the live 2026-10-07 census for DSG/GGG location types. Role descriptions confirm the structure:
- New ASMs "Directly manage[] a team of Captains (Supervisors)" and "approve[] Captain (Supervisor) staffing plans".
- Team Captains "When sole leader in building, act as Head Coach and perform general store oversight including opening and closing procedures". They also build schedules "while still meeting payroll and financial goals" and own hiring and reviews for their direct reports.
- The 2025 ASM Softlines posting described "managing the day-to-day operational aspects of their store department". The 2025 Lead posting "assists the store leadership team with general supervision".

Reading: Built to Win pushed manager-on-duty, scheduling and hiring down to a new salaried Captain layer paid at the old ASM entry rate. It turned ASMs into managers of managers at a +20% band. Old Leads mostly kept their pay under the Specialist title.

[L3-2] **INFERENCE: payroll per legacy store before vs after (the number the coordinator asked for). The leadership re-grade adds about +$15K to +$105K per store (burdened), or 7-51bp of FY27E DSG revenue (−$0.09 to −$0.63/sh), before any hours savings. Break-even needs 14-99 fewer part-time hours per store per week (≈1-7% of a ~1,500-hour store week).**

Script scripts\L3_store_payroll_inference.py → raw\L3_store_payroll_scenarios.csv. Pay is band midpoints from L3-1. Assumptions:
- 38h × 52 for full-time roles;
- burden 1.25 for full time (taxes plus self-insured health) and 1.10 for part time;
- 3 ASMs per store before (Softlines / Hardlines / Operations were the dominant 2024-25 ASM titles);
- 4.36 Team Captains per store (">3,200 new leadership opportunities" ÷ 734 stores);
- legacy-store payroll ~$1.7M.

| Scenario (per store) | Burdened Δ/store | % of store payroll | Fleet $M | bp FY27E DSG rev | $/sh | PT hours/week to break even |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A ASM layer kept 3→3; all Team Captains are ex-Leads | +$104.7K | +6.2% | +$76.9M | −51bp | −$0.63 | 99 |
| B 0.5 ASM per store re-slotted to Team Captain | +$86.9K | +5.1% | +$63.8M | −42bp | −$0.52 | 82 |
| C 1 ASM per store re-slotted to Team Captain (fits the "demoted" snippet) | +$69.1K | +4.1% | +$50.7M | −33bp | −$0.41 | 65 |
| D 1 ASM per store exits (severance); Team Captains are ex-Leads | +$14.7K | +0.9% | +$10.8M | −7bp | −$0.09 | 14 |

Offsets the postings cannot observe:
- each full-time Lead/Specialist role removed per store = **$54K/store, $39.9M fleet = 26bp = $0.33/sh**;
- each 10 fewer part-time hours per store per week = **$10.6K/store, $7.8M = 5bp = $0.06/sh**.

So thesis #2 works only if Built to Win cut store hours or full-time roles, not through pay. The new ASM titles map almost one-for-one onto the old ones (Sales Footwear & Apparel ≈ Softlines; Sales Sport & Outdoor ≈ Hardlines; Operations ≈ Operations). That argues against a large ASM cut, so A-C are more likely than D on the leadership layer. The bull case therefore rests on Captain-run scheduling plus AI forecasting cutting Teammate hours by roughly 5-7% (≈65-99 hours a week). The Q3/Q4 FY26 segment personnel line, and the FY26 10-K full-time/part-time headcount, are the tests.

## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **L3-1 (the ASM re-grade, with a built-in control).** DICK'S ASM posted bands jumped from $50,000-$76,000 to $60,000-$84,000 after Built to Win, while House of Sport ASMs (outside the program) stayed at $50,000-$76,000. A new salaried Team Captain layer was inserted at the old ASM floor ($49,500), and old Leads kept their exact pay as "Specialists".
   - It is hard, dated and reproducible from public archives.
   - A PM would remember it, but it is a two-edged sword. It shows Built to Win re-priced leadership up, so the thesis must be argued on hours and span of control, not wage savings. Presenting it pre-empts the obvious bear question.
2. **L3-2 (the break-even math).** "Built to Win adds ~$50-77M of leadership pay unless Captain-run scheduling cuts ~65-99 Teammate hours per store per week (5-7%)." It turns an unquantified program into a falsifiable KPI for the Q3/Q4 personnel line.
3. **L3-3 (frozen front-line bands)** is the supporting piece: posted pay for ~70% of store hours has not moved since mid-2025 except where minimum wages forced it.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Posted "Targeted Pay Ranges" are legally required hiring ranges, not actual pay. Incumbents can sit anywhere in the band, and merit raises are invisible.
- The pre sample is archive-capture-biased toward pay-transparency states and high-traffic URLs.
- Role counts per store (3 ASMs; 4.36 Captains; Lead counts) are assumptions. The 3,200 "leadership opportunities" may include Specialists, which would lower Captains per store and the cost estimate.
- The store-payroll base ($1.7M) is assumed (DICK'S personnel ~$2.0B ÷ ~890 stores = $2.25M including non-store).
- Team Captain pay quoted "based on a 38 hour work week" may be hourly-equivalent non-exempt pay, so overtime is possible. Full-time Captains and Specialists also carry health-plan cost (burden assumed 1.25).
- The ASM vacancy drop (L3-4) may be temporary absorption, not a smaller layer.
- The new ASM titles map almost one-for-one onto the old ones, which argues against an ASM cut.
- Old part-time techs → full-time Specialists at +21% is an additional cost not included in the L3-2 scenarios.
- Wayback refused connections intermittently. All fetch scripts are resumable.
