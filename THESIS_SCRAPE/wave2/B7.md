## Evidence against / caveats
- **FY27 variance is small in base (+$0.05) and negative in bear (−$0.38 to −$0.71).** If HoS increments are small ($10M) and cannibalisation high, consensus FY27 comps of 3.19%/3.23% (2H27) are not conservative: the bear-parameter implied legacy comp is +3.3%.
- **There are reasons the legacy fleet could genuinely run near zero in 2H26:**
  - The footwear promotional reset: >30% of Nike's assortment discounted across 14 partners, with promos "at least through Q4".
  - D06's FL-cleaned Placer DSG visits were ~−7.9% in August.
  - Management's own 2H guide implies only ~+1.3% total comp.
  - DKS trailed Census sporting goods by ~5pt in Q2FY26 (D06-5).
  - The 2H26 variance therefore needs the guide-beat pattern to repeat.
- **Sales beats may not convert to EPS.** Management guided Q3 SG&A deleverage of ~50bp and DKS gross-margin pressure "most pronounced in Q3" (D06). At the 10.8% segment margin rather than 20% flow-through, the 2H26 base falls to +$0.09.
- **One-off sizes are assumptions.** The World Cup benefit in 1H26 (and its FY27 lap) and the GGG/FL clearance benefit (F5-7) are not disclosed. Doubling the base lap (−0.4pt → −0.8pt FY27 average) lifts FY27 implied legacy to ~+2.1% and turns the FY27 base variance to about −$0.04.
- **Increment evidence is mixed.** H04 adds two A-mall stores above the model (Tampa, Freehold), but both are opening-period and A/A+-mall reads. Washington Square shows A-mall relocations replace $18-21M stores; the FY27 pipeline is A-mall-heavy (Tysons, Washington Square, KoP, Cerritos in FY28). The Freehold visits read conflicts with H05's 2.02x review read. Two-into-one consolidations (Baybrook, Polaris, Katy) lower increments (H07-4).
- **Consensus inputs are thin.** The consensus rows have few contributors, and the BBG sq-ft row conflicts with the store-count row (F4).
- **Model simplifications.** The model uses DKS-segment sales as the comp base; comp sales are a subset, so the dollar variance is slightly overstated. The comp definition changed three times (+110bp cumulative per WF), so back-test quarters before FY25 are not strictly like-for-like.
