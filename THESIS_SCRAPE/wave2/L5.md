## Evidence against / caveats
- L5-1 is assumption-heavy: the wage rate, the elasticity of store hours to volume (0.3), format staffing (WF's 168/65 heads) and the store share of personnel (0.8) all move the answer. The backtest error is ~±12bp. The direction (FY27 drift of −30 to −45bp on consensus revenue) holds across the plausible grid.
- My bottom-up FY27 OI ($1,542M base) uses consensus GM +7bp. A GM recovery after the Q3 FY26 promo trough could add +20-40bp (+$30-60M), which is the main reason consensus may still be right without large labor savings.
- The BLS sporting-goods series is NSA, preliminary for Aug-26, and ~1/5 DKS. Its hours and AHE jumps may partly reflect DKS's own transition (re-posting, training overlap).
- Bonus pool size (L5-5) is an upper-bound inference from accrued-payroll swings.
- H06-3: Team Captain pay is +36% over the old Lead floor, so Built to Win raises the rate per role. Savings must come from fewer hours and ASMs.
- Duplicates I deliberately did not re-report: the 2023 Business Optimization payback (WA1-2/WA2-1/WA3-2), the FY22-FY25 headcount series (WA1-1), personnel per store/sq ft (WA6-6), the World Cup roll-off resizing (WA6-2), retail AHE (D02-5), healthcare +8.2% (D02-6), the Fort Worth DC lap (D02-8/WA6-3).
