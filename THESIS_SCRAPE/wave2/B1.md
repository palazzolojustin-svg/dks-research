## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **B1-2, DKS's own floor-plan math: an HoS gets ~13% more selling space than the store it replaces but is underwritten at ~2x the sales.** It is a hard internal company document in a public record, and it reframes the HoS comp lift as productivity, not square footage. Strongest when paired with the memo's Ontario County / Tampa sales reads.
2. **B1-1, the Novi staffing plan with wage rates.** It is the only DKS-filed role and pay structure for an HoS (166 heads, $17.33/hr, $88.5K salaried, 4 ASMs, 3 directors). It is a smoking gun for the honest version of thesis #2: part of the personnel deleverage is structural HoS mix.
3. (Weaker) B1-3: Advan's 1.33x visits vs ~2x sales. Useful to defuse traffic-panel bear prints.

## Evidence against / caveats
- B1-1: HoS labor per $ of sales looks higher than legacy (heads 3-5x vs sales ~2x), so each opening is a small structural personnel headwind (~6-8bp in FY27, INFERENCE). The plan predates Built to Win and may have been re-staffed; the hours per PT head are assumed.
- B1-2: the sales forecast field is blank; the legacy #432 comparison is for space only. Whether #432 closes is unconfirmed.
- B1-3: Advan's 1.33x undercuts the "2X / 3X traffic" claims and the memo's Corpus Christi "tripled traffic" line. Panels disagree by a factor of 2-4.
- B1-4: Gaithersburg's early review-velocity uplift was weak (0.92x at ~3 months, H05). Cheap conversions may also deliver smaller lifts.
- The Braintree deck was not found anywhere else. It appears to be a one-off exhibit from the applicant's local counsel, so it cannot be replicated with a bigger or newer sample from public records.
