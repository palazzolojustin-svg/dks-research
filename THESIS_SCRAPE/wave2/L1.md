## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **L1-1 (proxy wage series → volume decomposition).** It is hard SEC data that nobody has used, and it gives a clean exhibit: "DKS held labor volume ~flat (+0.5-0.9%/yr) in FY24-FY25 while sales grew 5%. The 1H FY26 +44bp personnel deleverage is a ~5-6% hours spike during a severance-backed role redesign. Hours spikes reverse; consensus credits ~22bp of leverage, and a half give-back alone is ~30bp." Its weakness is the wage proxy (one median employee). Pair it with BLS retail AHE (D02) to show the rate assumption is conservative.
2. **L1-3 (Best Buy precedent under the same executive).** Best Buy booked a $48M "U.S. retail operating model" severance charge, ≈12bp of sales against DKS's ≈14bp, and domestic SG&A dollars came in flat on +2.1% revenue (−40bp rate). A PM will remember "same playbook, same guy, last time it bought ~40bp." Confirm Sliva's 2019 role before presenting it as his program.
3. **L1-2 (seasonal-hiring test).** This is the first 2026 public datapoint, due any day (prior years: 09/26-10/16). DICK'S-only ≤9,600 is the cleanest near-term proof point.

## Evidence against / caveats
- The DKS median FT wage rose +4.6% in FY25 (vs +3.1% in FY24), above the D02 retail AHE slowdown. The rate side may not slow as fast as BLS suggests. Built to Win's Team Captain/Specialist roles may also lift the average rate (H06: Team Captain posted at a $49.5-63.5K median range vs the FY25 median FT teammate at $46.5K).
- FY25 DICK'S-only seasonal hiring rose ~20% (L1-2). Labor intensity rose into the HoS wave, and 2027 still has ~14-20 HoS/FH openings.
- The Best Buy analog is confounded: incentive comp in FY20 and COVID in FY21. Sliva's role in the 2019 program is unconfirmed.
- The 1H FY26 volume estimate depends on the wage assumption: at a +6% wage rate, volume would be only ~+3%.
- Healthcare: DKS is self-insured for health with stop-loss (10-K FY25 l.2524), and "higher teammate healthcare costs" sit in the FY26 SG&A outlook. Claims volatility can persist into FY27.
- 2026 exec pay increases and the CEO STIP target rise (L1-4) are optics, not EPS.
- No direct savings evidence exists anywhere public (L1-6).
