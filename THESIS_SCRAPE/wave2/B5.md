## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **[B5-1] the 34-event QCEW panel with placebo.** Government payroll data, every HoS county since 2021, a placebo that gives ~−11 vs +140 jobs, and 71% of openings beating the placebo's 95th percentile. It turns the Victor anecdote into a national result: a typical HoS adds ~100-110 net local jobs ≈ $21-24M of net local sales, and it holds through year 2. It is the cleanest rebuttal to "HoS increments are smaller than UBS thinks" (H07-2), and it can be refreshed when the 2026Q2 QCEW file is released (~early Dec 2026, before or around the Q3 print).
2. **[B5-2] Saratoga solved = Bass Pro, with Latham/Albany unaffected.** Memorable because it removes a false positive and shows an HoS market absorbing a large-format outdoor big-box entry (size not verified) with no visible damage.

## Evidence against / caveats
- County totals net out DKS's gains against competitors' losses (good for the lower-bound argument) but also include unrelated openings and closures (Tulsa/Scheels, Dallas, Harris, Maricopa removed; others may remain).
- The jobs → sales conversion relies on two calibrations ($210-220K/job). Johnson City implies ~$70K/job (124 jobs vs +$8.6M NY taxable), so per-event dollar estimates have wide bands. NY taxable sales also omit exempt clothing/footwear under $110 in exempting jurisdictions, which affects footwear-heavy HoS more than Bass Pro.
- Fade [B5-3] appears only in small counties; the full balanced panel rises from year 1 to year 2 (+12% mean). Do not put the labour-fade margin upside in the base case.
- QCEW is month-3 headcount (part-time and full-time counted equally); the March and December snapshots are distorted by seasonal hiring.
- Year-1 effects for the 2025Q4 cohort (9 events) rest on only 2 quarters.
- FH [B5-6] shows nothing in aggregate.
