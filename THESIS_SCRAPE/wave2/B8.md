[B8-1] **The CMBS store panel says the gap between DICK'S reported comp and its older (non-relocated) stores' sales growth was about 1 point a year in FY22-FY24, not the ~2.4-2.7 points Wells Fargo subtracts.**
- Method: 11 clean year-over-year pairs from 7 legacy boxes, FY22-FY24 (excluding Cape Cod's ramp years, Concord Mills' opening year and Simon's flat St Johns estimate). Each pair's growth minus DKS's reported comp for that fiscal year:
  - FY22 (reported comp −2.0%, outside knowledge from the FY22 10-K): Empire 0.0% (gap +2.0), Brandon −9.1% (−7.1), Oxford Galleria II −3.3% (−1.3).
  - FY23 (comp +2.6%, W01): Empire +9.3% (+6.7), Mission Viejo −1.0% (−3.6), Hamburg Pavilion −3.3% (−5.9), Washington Square −0.5% (−3.1).
  - FY24 (comp +5.2%, W01): Empire +4.3% (−0.9), Cape Cod +0.8% (−4.4), Mission Viejo +9.8% (+4.6, TTM 9/2024 vs CY23), Hamburg +11.3% (+6.1, T-12 ~2024 vs 2023).
  - Mean gap −0.6pt, median −1.3pt, standard deviation ~4.8pt, so standard error ~1.45pt (n=11).
- By year, sales-weighted: FY22 −5.0% vs comp −2.0%; FY23 +0.5% vs +2.6% (median −0.7%); FY24 +6.6% vs +5.2% using all pairs, or +2.3% using only the two exact-fiscal-year pairs (Empire, Cape Cod).

## Evidence against / caveats
- **Thin and stale.** Only 11 clean FY22-24 pairs and 0 FY25 pairs. The latest y/y read is FY24 (lease year to Jan-2025). The panel says nothing about 2H26.
- **Exact-period FY24 pairs are weak.** Using only exact fiscal-year pairs, FY24 legacy growth was +2.3% (Empire +4.3%, Cape Cod +0.8%) vs reported comp +5.2%. That 2.9pt gap is consistent with WF's decomposition. The supportive reading depends on including the two TTM-shifted pairs (Mission Viejo, Hamburg).
- **FY23 also looks WF-like.** Weighted +0.5% (median −0.7%) vs reported +2.6%, a 2.1pt gap.
- **Mall-tier and survivorship bias.** These malls were being refinanced, so they are surviving, mostly A/B centers. Boxes in failing malls are absent.
- **Mixed definitions.**
  - Store-only vs omni: CMBS sales exclude eCom, which is ~20%± of DKS sales [outside knowledge].
  - "Sales" are tenant-reported gross sales for percentage-rent or co-tenancy purposes. Some are borrower estimates: Simon's St Johns figure is flat $20.1M for 3 years, so it was excluded.
  - Lease-year vs calendar-year periods vary.
- **Selection: relocating stores.** Two of the seven growth-pair stores (Empire, Washington Square) are being replaced by HoS. Landlords may disclose them precisely because a relocation is coming.
- **Weak boxes exist.** Westroads ($119/sf), Galleria at Sunset ($176/sf), Green Acres ($173/sf at 29.4% occupancy cost), Oxford Galleria II ($6.2M). Part of the fleet is low-productivity, which is consistent with a legacy base that may not comp.
- Reported comps for FY19-FY22 (3.0/9.9/11.3/−2.0%) are outside knowledge from DKS 10-Ks. They are not in WORKING_NOTES except FY23 +2.6% and FY24 +5.2% (W01/W05).
