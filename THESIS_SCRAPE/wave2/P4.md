## Verdict (Track P question: will 3Q26/4Q26/1H27 ticket run well above the consensus +1.0-1.25%?)
- **As a standalone thesis #2: NO, and keep it out of the memo as a separate pillar.** (1) There is no real consensus ticket to beat [P4-1]. The +1% rows are filler, and the only live number is comp, which thesis #1 already argues against. Any ticket upside is therefore the same comp variance counted again. (2) The mechanism the thesis needs (category CPI flowing through to DKS ticket) does not exist in the data [P4-3]: r = −0.19, and DKS ticket was below CPI in the last inflation episode (FY22-23). (3) Management says ticket is mix and units per transaction, not price [P4-4], and is cutting price through Q4 FY26 [P4-5]. (4) Category elasticity of −1.35 [P4-6] means pass-through costs units.
- **What survives (INFERENCE):** ticket momentum from mix and premiumization (newness, HoS/FH, UGG/Birkenstock, trading cards) is a reason the consensus comp of 1.6-1.7% looks too low. Consensus requires ticket to drop to ~+0.8-1.1% from +3.6%, which would be the largest one-quarter deceleration outside COVID since FY12 [P4-7]. A realistic 2H26 ticket of +1.8-2.5% is worth +$0.09-0.18/sh at 20% flow-through and belongs inside thesis #1's comp bridge as a cross-check. 3Q26 is the quarter most exposed to downside, because promotions are deepest there by management's own account.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Everything in [P4-3] to [P4-6] cuts against the inflation-pass-through thesis.
- [P4-7] is SUPPORTIVE but assumes transactions hold. The Q4 FY25 txn was −1.3%, and the BSM w/e 27-Sep-26 shows customers −5.9% and ticket +1.2% (digest l.463, DUP), which is consistent with a weak 3Q26 ticket. Promotions are deepest in 3Q26, which pulls ticket down directly.
- The 2-yr-stack method for 4Q26 inflates transactions (it laps −1.3%). I used the 8-quarter txn mean (+0.6%) for the EPS figures instead.
- The pre-FY17 history is on a DICK'S-stores basis, FY17-FY22 is consolidated and FY23+ is the DICK'S Business. Q4s for FY12-FY22 are derived (±0.5pt).
- The CPI basket weights are my assumption. The WF elasticity is category-level (BEA, includes ammo).
- The comp definition changed 3 times (+110bp cumulative per WF, DUP). Relocations lift comp, and probably ticket too (HoS mix).
