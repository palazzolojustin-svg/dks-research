- How to read it (INFERENCE):
  - Basis mismatch: the company's $35M is year-1 **omni** sales. CMBS tenant sales are store-reported gross sales, which exclude most eCom. DKS eCom is about 16% of consolidated sales (C06 R5).
  - Store-only equivalent of the model: $35M × 0.80-0.84 ≈ $28-29.4M. Ridgedale's $31.2M is therefore **+6% to +11% above model on a store basis**, in its third year.
  - Per square foot: $271 store vs the model's ~$292 omni ($35M / 120K SF).
  - Uplift: if the replaced #409 was an average $13.9M store, the increment is ≈ +$17M store-only, or ≈ +$20M omni-equivalent. That is in line with UBS's +$20M.
- Variance (INFERENCE): together with Tampa (H04-1, ~$40-44M store-reported, annualised in year 1), the two hard HoS reads average **~$36-38M store-reported ≈ $43-46M omni-equivalent**. Even taking the conservative reading (+$3M per store vs the $35M model) on the ~24 FY26-FY27 cohort store-years in FY27: +$72M ≈ +47bp of FY27E DKS revenue; at 20% four-wall that is +$14.4M EBIT ≈ **+$0.12/sh**. The bear reading (raw $31.2M vs $35M, −$3.8M per store) gives ≈ −$0.15/sh.

## Smoking-gun candidates
1. **B9-1, Ridgedale $31.2M (CY2024, SEC-filed).** It is the only full-year, third-party sales number for a single HoS. Read with the omni-vs-store basis adjustment, it shows a three-year-old HoS still at or above the store-equivalent of the company's year-1 model. With Tampa, it gives a two-store hard-data range of $31-44M store-reported. It is honest and checkable, and it cuts both ways depending on basis, which is why the PM will remember it.
2. **B9-3 + H04, measured replaced-store base.** Three CMBS legacy stores being replaced by an HoS (Washington Square $18.6M, Empire $12.2M, Brandon $14.3M) average $15.0M. That validates the denominator in UBS's +$20M per-relocation arithmetic with hard filed numbers.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **Raw read:** Ridgedale $31.2M is below $35M. If CMBS tenant sales include BOPIS/ship-from-store (not knowable from the filing), the basis adjustment shrinks and Ridgedale is a −11% miss in year 3. The company says HoS comps are positive in years 2-4, which implies Ridgedale's year 1 (Jun-22 to May-23) was below $31M.
- **Ridgedale is a weaker mall for DKS:** $547/SF in-line ex-Apple vs $1,539 at International Plaza. Two stores are not a distribution.
- **No mall halo at Ridgedale (B9-2):** this undercuts the leasing-deck halo statistics that the memo uses as supporting color.
- **Tampa:** a three-month figure that includes the grand opening and holiday; on a store basis it also replaced a nearby store (Westshore), so its increment is smaller than its level.
- **Still undisclosed:** no CMBS discloses FH sales, nor any HoS sales for Freehold, Jersey City, Empire, Northshore or Washington Square (all NAV or not yet open).
