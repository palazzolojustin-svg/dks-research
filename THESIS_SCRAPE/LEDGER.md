## Thesis #1: HoS / FH comp floor and returns

### Core variance (A): consensus implies ~zero or negative underlying comp for the legacy fleet
- Four independent calculations (F1, F2, F4, F5; F6 too) agree. Strip the HoS/FH format lift (≈120-250bp per quarter, from consensus's own store path and UBS's ~+$20M per relocated HoS) out of consensus DKS comp (3Q26E 1.69 / 4Q26E 1.59 / 1Q27E 2.41 / 2Q27E 2.24%) and the legacy fleet is left at about −0.9% to +0.4%.
  - F1: −0.49/−0.34/+0.25/−0.14%. F5, using WF's own 2.7% relocation lift: −0.8/−0.9/−0.6/−0.8%. F2, built opening by opening: −0.6/−0.4/+0.4/+0.2%. F4: −0.4 to +0.4%.
  - Same method on actual quarters: ~+2.8-3.4% underlying (F1 trailing 3.37%; F2 avg +2.8%; UBS ex-HoS ~3.3%). Even WF, the bear, models +1.1-1.2%.
  - Variance: if underlying comp is merely +1.2% (WF bear), FY27 EPS is ~+$0.24-0.46 above consensus; at +2.8-3% it is ~+$0.8-0.96 (F1/F2/F4/F5; 100bp of DKS comp ≈ $152M sales).
  - Caveats: management's 2H26 guide implies only ~+1.3% underlying; the ex-HoS figure depends on the +$20M uplift and an 84% relocation share; transactions ≤ +1.6% for 9 quarters.

## Thesis #2: FY27 core store-cost reset (Built to Win + FY26 one-offs)

### Core facts (A)
- "Built to Win" store operating model: press release 2026-04-14; $15.3M Q2 charge (~$21M FY26) booked in Corporate & other, NOT in segment personnel, so the 1H26 personnel deleverage is running cost (WA2). Savings never sized; "Built to Win" has 0 hits in the corpus. (D07)
- Rollout: postings went ~2,100 (Jun 15) → 9,760 (Jul 11) → 3,238 (Sep 2), and the requisition counter rose ~15,400 in 4 weeks. So Q3 FY26 is the first full quarter on the new model. New structure in every DICK'S/GGG store: part-time Teammates, full-time Specialists ($18 floor vs $15), 332 open Team Captain roles (FT, 38 hrs). Old hourly Lead postings fell from 316 to 83. HoS and Golf Galaxy keep the old titles. (H06)
- Personnel is the deleveraging line. DICK'S personnel % of sales: FY22 13.22, FY23 14.16, FY24 13.91, FY25 13.98; 1H26 +44bp vs a −54bp segment margin decline. Personnel per store +9.3%/+8.6% and per sq ft +8.2%/+7.2% in Q1/Q2 on a flat store count; $44.7M above a sales-proportional path over the last 4 quarters (≈29bp, ≈$0.36/sh). (D07, F6, WA6)
- Headcount, not pay, drove FY25. DICK'S year-end heads: 52.8K / 55.5K / 56.1K / ~59.8K (FY22-25), so FY25 heads +6.6% on +1.6% space and +5.0% sales, with pay per head only +0.6-1.6%. HoS/FH explain ~1,700 of ~3,700 added heads, leaving ~1,600-2,000 (≈$55-68M, 40-48bp) addressable. (WA1)
- Precedent: FY23 business optimization ($84.8M charge incl. $26.7M severance) → FY24 DICK'S personnel $ only +1.7% (+3.6% 53-wk adj.) on a 5.2% comp; personnel −17-25bp; savings ≈0.87-1.28x the severance; leverage showed up 2-3 quarters after the cut. Applied to Built to Win: $18-27M ≈ 12-17bp ≈ +$0.15-0.22/sh gross. (WA2, WA1, WA3)
- Retail wage growth slowed to +2.9-3.1% y/y (Jul-Sep 2026) from +4.0-4.6%. (D02)
- FY27 one-off roll-off: ~$15-20M of non-recurring SG&A (World Cup marketing; D07's $34M overstated it because ~$15M is logistics in COGS) ≈ +$0.12-0.16/sh. (WA6, D07)

### Variance (still being finalized by L5)
- Gross bull: personnel back to FY25's 13.98% ≈ +42bp ≈ +$0.52/sh. But consensus already has DSG margin +29bp (~22bp of SG&A), which implies ~2.5% fewer hours per sq ft (WA3). Net residual upside ≈ +$0.15-0.35/sh (D07, WA1, WA3). L5 will reconcile.

### Against (X)
- Team Captain pay ($49.5-63.5K) starts at the ASM level, ~36% above the old Lead floor, so savings must come from hours and fewer ASMs; up to −$0.26/sh if all 3,200 are upgraded leads. (H06)
- 2023 precedent: management said savings would be "largely offset by strategic talent investments", and total adj SG&A still deleveraged 48bp in FY24. Non-personnel SG&A rose +73bp FY24 and +34bp FY25. (WA2, WA1, WA3)
- Personnel includes HQ, field and GameChanger payroll (not touched by Built to Win). FY25 was a low-bonus base; a bonus rebuild could take back 20-40bp in FY27, while a Q3 FY26 bonus true-down could flatter the Q3 ratio. (WA6, WA1, WA3)
- HoS staffing intensity (~168 heads vs ~65 FH); healthcare +8.2% 2027; fuel and freight are FY27 COGS headwinds (diesel +19% y/y in Q1 FY27; freight 3.5x pre-war). (F5, D02)
