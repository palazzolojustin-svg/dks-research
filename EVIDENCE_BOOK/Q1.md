## Bottom line
1. **The 2-yr framing overstates the case.**
   - Consensus 4Q26E: comp 1.59%, 2-yr 4.69% additive (BBG row 4.74%), the lowest 2-yr since the stimulus-lap quarters of 1H FY23. It sits in the 14th percentile of post-COVID quarters and below all 12 quarters since Q3 FY23 (range 6.0-10.5%).
   - Against the full ex-COVID record (FY13 onward) it is in the 62nd percentile, and in the 50th percentile of ex-COVID Q4s (median 5.6%). The FY15-FY19 Q4 2-yr stacks were 0.9 / 2.5 / 3.0 / −4.2 / 3.1.
   - On a **3-yr basis, Q4 is not an outlier.** The consensus 11.29% matches Q2 FY25 (11.3%, mgmt 11.5%) and is about 1.2pt below Q4 FY25 (12.5%).
   - The low 2-yr figure is mostly the arithmetic of lapping a soft +3.1% Q4 FY25 (on +6.6%). Per a post-print CFO follow-up note (C06R2 l.438), weather in that quarter was "net immaterial", so the soft compare cannot be dismissed as weather.
2. **"Complete collapse in ticket" holds only if transactions are ≥ 0.** Ticket needed to hit consensus Q4 comp, by transaction assumption:

   | Q4 transactions | Ticket needed | How common a drop of that size is |
   |---|---|---|
   | +0.45% (trailing-4Q average) | 1.1% | 2.5pt below the Q2 FY26 3.6% over 2 quarters; 2 of 46 history, 0 of 29 reported-only |
   | 2-yr transaction stack held at its trailing 1.2% (implies Q4 txn +2.5%) | −0.9% | 0 of 42 same-quarter-LY drops |
   | −1.3% (Q4 FY25 repeats) | 2.9% | an ordinary 0.7pt slowdown |

   Consensus puts the ticket break in **Q3**. At trailing transactions, Q3 needs ticket of 1.2% (−2.4pt q/q). Only 1 of 48 ex-COVID quarter pairs fell that much, and none of 31 reported-only pairs.
3. **The HoS argument works through legacy comp, not through the size of the format lift.**
   - Q4 format lift: 0.69pt at $6M per relocation, 1.15 at $12M, 1.45 at $16M, 1.75 at $20M and 1.94 at $22.5M (all with FH at $2M; FH at $3M adds 0.12).
   - Implied legacy comp in consensus: +0.90 / +0.44 / +0.14 / −0.16 / −0.35%.
   - The same engine gives a trailing-8Q legacy comp of 3.35-4.44%. The required slowdown is 3.5-3.7pt in every uplift case.
   - Higher uplift assumptions make the consensus look *more* conservative, not less. The Street already uses large format lifts (UBS 150-170bp, WF 2.2-2.3pt), so "Street under-models HoS" is not supported. "Street implies a ~0% legacy fleet" is.
   - The Q4 lift is *smaller* than Q3's (1.45 vs 1.68 at $16M) because the record 13-HoS Q3 FY25 cohort ages out of its 12-month window during Aug-Oct 2026.
4. **Consensus Q4 (1.59%) already sits ABOVE management's guide midpoint.**
   - The FY +2.5-4.0% guide implies 2H −0.2% / +1.2% / +2.7%, and Q4 of −1.8% / +0.9% / +3.5% if Q3 prints the consensus 1.69%.
   - The variance case therefore rests on the beat pattern. Initial FY comp guides vs actuals: FY23 0-2% vs 2.4%; FY24 1-2% vs 5.2%; FY25 1-3% vs 4.5%. Implied Q4 FY25 of "slightly neg to +2%" came in at 3.1%.
5. **Variance, Q4 FY26** (vs 1.59%; comp base FY25 Q4 $4,051M; consensus DSG revenue $4,144.38M; consensus segment OM 10.58%):

## 2. Bear checks
- **Promotions / price-match deflating ticket [V quotes, I effect]:**
  - The Aug-26 guide cut cites an "increasingly aggressive promotional environment … at least through the fourth quarter". DKS chose to price-match ("investment"). DKS GM to "decline slightly", pressure "most pronounced Q3". IEEPA refunds were reinvested in price. Stack: "Q3 is going to be a bit more difficult than Q4" (digest s.3b; C02B l.325).
  - WF puts sporting-goods price elasticity at −1.35 (P4-6).
  - Card data, w/e 27-Sep-26 (BSM, post-FL-lap): spend −3.3%, customers −5.9%, ticket +1.2% (digest l.463). Ticket is clearly decelerating in Q3. The question is the transaction line.
  - I therefore treat txn −0.5% × ticket +2.5% (comp ~2.0%) as a realistic low case.
- **GGG FL-clearance lift lapping [V qualitative, size A]:**
  - In Q4 FY25 / 1H26, FL excess inventory was cleared through Going Going Gone! (Stack, C02B l.92/118). WF calls this a "one-time benefit to DSG comp — investors poke at this" (C06R5 l.252).
  - The size is undisclosed. If it is 25-50bp, the clean Q4 FY25 base was ~2.6-2.85%, and Q4 FY26 carries the same lap headwind. That lowers every 2-yr-hold scenario by 0.25-0.5pt (about −$0.02 to −$0.03 at 20%).
- **World Cup pull-forward [V qualitative]:**
  - 1H26 comps were "higher (WC timing)", and Q2 +4.9% included "strong World Cup".
  - JPM: Aug-26 card data showed a "post-WC lull". JPM's earlier 1H/2H core split was +4.2% / +2.5% (C06R2 l.451, as cited by B7; not re-read).
  - Mostly a Q3 issue (soccer hardgoods/jerseys bought early); a small Q4 issue. Note also that the trailing legacy figures in T8 are NOT ex-World Cup. B7's WC-adjusted engine gives 1Q26/2Q26 legacy ~0.3/1.0pt lower.
- **BTS shift [V]:** management said the back-to-school shift "cosmetically" hurt Q2/Aug sales and expected it to turn (C06R5 l.835). That is a Q3 timing item, not a Q4 one.
- **Management's own 2H guide [V arithmetic]:**
  - +2.5-4.0% FY on a 1H of 6.0% / 4.9% implies 2H −0.23% / +1.23% / +2.68% and Q4 −1.76% / +0.86% / +3.47% (if Q3 = 1.69%). Consensus Q4 is above the midpoint.
  - Bull counter: every FY guide since FY23 was beaten by 1.4-3.7pt at the midpoint.
- **Contributor mixing [I, untested lead]:**
  - Q4 FY26 is the quarter FL enters the comp base. If any contributor fed a consolidated comp, which carries FL's guided PF comp of −2% to 0%, into the DICK'S row, the Q4 mean would be dragged down.
  - Ask a terminal user for the # of estimates and high/low on "Same Store Sales" for 4Q26E.

## 3. What would prove the thesis right or wrong
- **Q3 print (~2026-11-24/25):**
  - A ticket ≥ +2.5% with transactions ≥ 0 makes the Q4 consensus (needs ticket ~1.1-1.6% at txn ≥ 0) untenable. Expect upward revisions.
  - A Q3 ticket ≤ 1.5% with negative transactions validates consensus.
- **Q4 commentary:** whether management speaks to Q4 on a 3-yr basis (~11-12%) or a 2-yr basis.
