- Q3 FY24, 2024-11-26, Q from McShane (GS) on same-SKU inflation vs mix. Gupta: ticket drivers are "mix, the differentiated product, and the pricing opportunity"; differentiated product is "not exposed to promo pressure → better margin recovery and higher ticket." (C02A › DKS_Q3-FY2024_Earnings-Call_2024-11-26.md)
- Q3 FY24 call, Yih (Barclays) framing: DKS wins with "full-price selling/AUR-led comp" while value players win on traffic. Hobart: 3 straight quarters >4%; national plus vertical brands. (same)

Hobart: no "knee-jerk promos"; DKS decides "how many weeks/how deep to participate in a MAP break." (C03A › conferences/DKS_Barclays-Conference_2024-12-03.md)

Gupta: "almost 1% of our comp came from the increase in transaction, and the remaining came from the overall basket increase"; basket drivers are "innovation/newness affinity, more units per transaction from in-store selling, product mix." (C02A › DKS_Q2-FY2025_Earnings-Call_2025-08-28.md)

### T1.3 Guidance history and implied 2H/Q4 comps (conservatism track record)
- FY24 (C02A › Q3 FY24 call, 2024-11-26): comp guide raised from +2.5-3.5% to +3.6-4.2% with YTD at +4.7%. [d] Assuming Q4 ≈28% of the year, implied Q4 was ≈+0.8% to +2.9%. Actual Q4 FY24 was +6.4%/+6.6%. Mgmt: "appropriately cautious"; holiday had 5 fewer days.
- FY25 path (C02A/C02B): Mar-25 +1-3% → May-25 +1-3% ("closer to high end through Q3") → Aug-25 +2-3.5% → Nov-25 +3.5-4% → actual +4.5%.
  - Q3 FY25 (2025-11-25), Lasser: "Q4 implies flat to slightly positive DKS comp — why?" Hobart: "the high end of our previous guidance now is the low end"; "really bullish on the holiday"; "We don't ever guide to the best possible outcome." Actual Q4 FY25 was +3.1%.
  - Q2 FY25, Horvers: implied 2H "sharp deceleration." Hobart: "We never guide to the highest possible outcome"; lapping 2H comps "50 basis points stronger than the first half." Actual 2H FY25 was 5.7% / 3.1%.
- FY26 path (C02B):
  - Mar-26: +2-4% ("midpoint = 7.5% two-yr stack"; "slightly higher comps in 1H, largely World Cup timing").
  - May-26: raised low end to +2.5-4%.
  - Aug-26: unchanged at +2.5-4% ("our sales expectations remain unchanged"), but op margin cut to 10.6-10.9% from 11.0-11.4%.
  - GS Sep-26, Hobart: "we kept our comp guidance the same ... we never guide to the best, first possible outcome, but we feel really good about our guidance."
- [d] Implied 2H FY26 comp from the guide. 1H DKS sales ≈ $3.37B (Q1: $5.16B less $1.79B FL) + $3.85B (Q2: $5.59B less $1.74B FL) = $7.22B. Sales-weighted 1H comp ≈ (3.37×6.0 + 3.85×4.9)/7.22 ≈ 5.4%. FY DKS sales guide $14.5-14.7B → 2H ≈ $7.38B. Implied 2H comp ≈ -0.35% (low end 2.5%) / +1.1% (midpoint 3.25%) / +2.6% (high end 4.0%). Consensus 3Q 1.69% / 4Q 1.59% sits just above the guide midpoint. Approximation: comp base ≠ total sales, and weights are by total sales.
- Q2 FY26 cadence statements (C02B › Q2-FY2026):
  - Stack: "Q3 is going to be a bit more difficult than Q4."
  - Hobart: DKS margin and SG&A investment "more aggressive in Q3 than Q4."
  - Gupta: SG&A deleverage ~50bps in Q3 at midpoint; "leverage in Q4 as lapping prior investments."
  - FY DKS GM to "decline slightly," with pressure "most pronounced Q3."

### T1.6 Items that cut AGAINST T1 (Q4 2-yr stack too low)
1. Transactions were negative in Q4 FY25 (-1.3%) and Q3 FY24 (-0.6%). Q4 is the quarter where traffic has been weakest. Horvers flagged ticket getting "harder as the year progresses."
2. Part of FY25 ticket came from tariff-driven "selective"/"sporadic" price increases (Q2 FY25: Hobart, Gupta, Kernan Q). Those are lapped in 2H26. Hobart's "really not due to inflation" is a management claim.
3. 2H26 price investment and price-matching on legacy footwear/apparel, "at least through the fourth quarter" (Stack, Q2 FY26; GS Sep-26).
4. Mgmt held the FY guide at 2.5-4% despite a ~5.4% 1H. The midpoint implies ~1.1% 2H [d], which is close to consensus. Consensus is not below the guide midpoint.
5. Q4 FY25's year-1 conversion step-up from 13 Q3-FY25 HoS is lapped. 2H26 has fewer HoS openings than 2H25.
6. HoS LT target cut from 75-100 to ~67 by 2027. FY26 FH plan cut from ~22 to 20.
7. Q2 FY26 comp included "strong results from the World Cup"; the 1H/2H gap is partly World Cup timing (Mar-26 guide).
8. Macro: Stack/Gupta cite fuel, healthcare and geopolitical pressure; "consumer across the country is under pressure" (Hobart, GS Sep-26).
9. Lifestyle legacy silhouettes "slowed and slowed relatively quickly" (Stack, Q2 FY26). "I think we have the hangover right now" (Stack, Q2 FY26, Horvers Q). Hobart says the issue is "lifestyle legacy silhouettes," not performance.
10. Definitional risk: FL enters the comp base in Q4 FY26 ("14th full month," Gilch, every call since Q3 FY25). These files do not say whether a DKS-only comp will still be disclosed in Q4 FY26. Check that BBG "DICK'S comp" 4Q26E is DKS-only.
