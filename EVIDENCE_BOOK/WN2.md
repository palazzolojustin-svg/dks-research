**2-yr and 3-yr stacks [d]** (current comp plus the LY comp as shown in the same 10-Q):
- Q1 FY25 9.8 | Q2 FY25 9.5 | Q3 FY25 10.0 | Q1 FY26 **10.5** | Q2 FY26 **9.9** | 26w FY26 10.1.

- Ticket has been the main driver in **every 10-Q quarter since Q2 FY24** (7 quarters: Q2-Q3 FY24, Q1-Q3 FY25, Q1-Q2 FY26; Q4s are not in 10-Qs): ticket +3.5 / +4.8 / +3.7 / +4.1 / +4.4 / +5.5 / +3.6, against transactions of -0.6% to +1.3%. Before that (FY22 to Q3 FY23), transactions drove the comp and ticket ran -1.0% to +2.8%.
- **Implied 2H FY26 DICK'S comp [inf]:** FY26 DICK'S comp guide is +2.5% to +4.0%. It was unchanged at the Q2 10-Q (2026-09-03), after the low end was raised at Q1 ("we have raised the low end of our full year comparable sales outlook"). 1H is +5.4%. Weighting 1H at about 49% of the comp base (1H FY25 DICK'S sales $6,821M; 2H FY25 ≈ Q3 $3,237M + Q4 ~$3.9B, and the Q4 figure is an assumption from outside these files), the implied 2H comp is about **-0.3% to +2.7%**. Against the 2H FY25 base (Q3 FY25 +5.7%; Q4 FY25 is not in the 10-Qs), the implied 2H 2-yr stack is about 5.4 to 8.4 [inf]. That compares with 9.5 to 10.5 in each of the last five quarters. If transactions hold their ~+0.9% run rate, the low end implies ticket of about **-1%** [inf].
- Management's own framing of the 2H decel: "We expect higher comparable sales in the first half of 2026, primarily due to the FIFA World Cup" (W04 › FY2026-Q1, repeated in Q2). In Q2 it added: "we are taking a more cautious view of the balance of fiscal 2026 due to the marketplace conditions we are seeing today" (W04 › FY2026-Q2).

**Comp methodology (relevant to the HoS conversion lift):**
- "relocations included from original open date"; store enters comp in 14th full month; closed stores excluded. From FY2022 the method was revised to include relocated stores (W03 › FY2022-Q3; definition repeated in W04 › FY2025-Q1).
- So HoS and Field House stores that are relocations or conversions of existing DICK'S stores stay in the comp base and their sales lift flows into the comp from day one. Brand-new HoS stores enter after 14 months. [inf] With ~two-thirds to ~75% of openings being relocations or remodels, most conversion lift is captured in the comp, mostly as higher ticket (bigger box, more premium brands, more categories) rather than new transactions.
- From FY2024 the comp includes GameChanger revenue (W03 › FY2024-Q1). From FY2025 it includes Warehouse Sale (GGG) locations from their 14th month (W04 › FY2025-Q1). Digital = all eCom, store-fulfilled omni, GameChanger subscriptions and DICK'S Media Network revenue (W04 › FY2025-Q1). [inf] Fast-growing high-margin GameChanger and DMN revenue sits inside comp and ticket, which helps the ticket line.
- FL enters the comp base in **Q4 FY2026** (14th full month after the 2025-09-08 close) (W04 › FY2025-Q3, FY2026-Q1).

## 8. Things that CUT AGAINST the theses

**T1 (Q4 comp too low / HoS lift under-modeled):**
- Management itself guides 2H decel ("higher comparable sales in the first half of 2026, primarily due to the FIFA World Cup"). Q1 FY26 hardlines +11.2% shows a one-off World Cup and trading-cards boost that will not recur in 2H.
- Ticket decelerated from +5.5% (Q1 FY26) to +3.6% (Q2 FY26).
- Q2 FY26 lists **declines in golf, outdoor equipment, accessories**.
- Mgmt is "taking a more cautious view of the balance of fiscal 2026" and expects GM pressure "most pronounced in the third quarter of 2026" amid "increasingly aggressive promotional environment". Promotions could compress ticket (lower AUR) even with good units.
- Q3 FY24's comp had transactions -0.6% and ticket +4.8%. Traffic has been weak for 2+ years; ticket has carried the comp.
- The HoS lift is partly already captured: relocations comp from original open date. And HoS conversions were heavily front-loaded into Q3 FY25 (13 in one quarter). In Q3 FY26 these lap their first year, so the incremental conversion contribution to the 2H FY26 comp is smaller than in 2H FY25 [inf]. FY26 has only ~14 HoS openings vs 16 in FY25, and Field House was cut ~22 → ~20.
- The comp includes GameChanger and DMN, so "ticket" is not purely store AUR.

**T2 (owned-brand mix and margin):**
- No 10-Q attributes margin to vertical brands. The "plans to provide our vertical brands with improved space in-store, increased marketing and expansion into additional product categories" FLS was **dropped after Q3 FY24**.
- DICK'S merch margin expansion faded through FY25 (+37 → +18 → +5bps). Q1 FY26 DICK'S GP was -36bps on "sales mix". Q2 FY26's +137bps is mostly IEEPA plus DMN/GameChanger, not product mix.
- FY26 guide: "full year gross margin to decline slightly".

**T3 (EU exit):**
- No held-for-sale, exit or strategic-alternatives language in any 10-Q.
- Europe has 559 stores (-4.4% since close, slower than NA -6.5%). Sq ft is flat. 12 international stores were relocated/remodeled in 1H FY26 (investment, not exit).
- Fast Break was rolled out to ">250 stores globally", i.e. including international.
- The new FLS frames international as "expand ... market share ... including through licensed or franchise arrangements" (a conversion to licensing is possible, but nothing says exit).

**Supports T3 (for balance):**
- International comp about -10%.
- The Q2 FY26 guide cut explicitly blames "continued challenges in Foot Locker's international operations".
- Foreign losses sit under a $288.5M valuation allowance and push the ETR to ~28%.
- Licensed doors are growing while owned international shrinks.
- FL-International is a separate operating segment.
- "cash repatriation" was added to the FLS.
- ~$234M of headroom remains under the "up to $750M" charge envelope.
