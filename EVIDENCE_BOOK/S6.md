## 1. Bottom line for T2 (INFERENCE, built from everything below)
1. **Mix acceleration: supported on assortment/placement/investment, not yet on sales or imports.** The best evidence is online assortment share (X11/R12 CI excludes zero), new-SKU share, the reorg/hiring, and a step-change in paid marketing (TikTok, TV, billboard, ScoreCard+ credit). Imports, management commentary, consumer surveys and history do not support acceleration. Honest framing: base +30-50bps/yr (vs consensus flat), not +100bps.
2. **Margin effect of mix itself is small.** Δmix × premium: +0.5pt × 800bps ≈ +4bps of DICK'S Business GM per year [d]; bull +1pt ≈ +8bps; ~+20-25bps cumulative by FY28E in the bull case (X13). M1: ~+$0.16 EPS FY27 even in the bull case.
3. **The bigger margin swing is the PREMIUM itself, and the markdown data puts it at risk.** Owned products are marked down ~2x as often as national (47-54% vs 25-28% on sale), and the gap widened y/y (owned +12.3pp, CI +4.8 to +20.4; national +1.5pp). If the displayed-price deterioration carried fully into realized margin, the owned premium would shrink by roughly 150-200bps, which on a 13% mix is ~-20-25bps of total GM [d, see section 4]. That is 5x larger than a year's mix benefit. **But** like-for-like realized prices held better for owned (-0.3%) than national (-2.5%), because owned "list" prices were raised +7.3%. So the deeper "% off" is partly reference-price inflation, not lost margin. Net: ambiguous; the premium is not obviously eroding on a realized basis, but CALIA/Walter Hagen/Alpine clearance is real.
4. **Pitchable version of T2:** "Owned brands are getting more shelf, more new SKUs, more media and a dedicated org; the national-brand price umbrella widened (Nike/adidas list up, more national promo); Maxfli/VRST/DSG hold realized price. The Street holds owned mix flat, so even +40-50bps/yr is unmodelled. The GM effect is a few bps a year on its own, so present it as margin resilience (the premium holds while national brands promote), not an EPS driver." Avoid "owned = full price".

### 4B. Quantification [d] (INFERENCE; illustrative)
Key point: the 700-900bps premium is management's realized GM premium, so the LEVEL of owned markdowns is already inside it. Only the CHANGE matters.
- **Scenario 1, displayed discounts flow fully into realized margin (bear for the premium).** Owned realized price factor (1 - avg disc) 0.819 -> 0.781 = -4.6%; national 0.927 -> 0.916 = -1.2%. Assume owned merchandise GM ~44% and national ~36% (illustrative, premium ~800bps; DKS GM includes occupancy so true merch margins are higher, which softens the effect). With cost fixed: owned GM 1 - 0.56/0.954 = 41.3% (-270bps); national 1 - 0.64/0.988 = 35.2% (-80bps). Premium compresses ~190bps (800 -> ~610bps). Impact on DICK'S Business GM from owned alone: 12.8% × -270bps ≈ **-35bps**; relative to national ≈ 12.8% × -190bps ≈ **-24bps**.
- **Scenario 2, like-for-like realized prices (base).** LFL offer owned -0.3% vs national -2.5%. Owned GM 1 - 0.56/0.997 ≈ -17bps on its own rate; national 1 - 0.64/0.975 ≈ -165bps. The premium WIDENS by ~150bps; national brands are the margin problem, not owned. Owned-relative-to-national effect ≈ +19bps [12.8% × 150bps].
- **Pure mix effect (both scenarios):** +0.4-0.5pt mix/yr × 700-900bps = +3-4.5bps/yr; bull +1pt ≈ +8bps (X13). M1: ~+$0.16 EPS FY27 in bull case.
- **Tariff on owned entries (R8-4):** ~$20M/yr lower duty in FY26 vs FY25 ≈ +110bps on owned GM rate ≈ +14bps DICK'S Business GM (INFERENCE), already partly in the Street's "tariff relief".
- **Takeaway:** the sign of the owned-margin effect depends on whether the CC "% off" reflects lost margin (composition shift to clearance, CALIA/WH/Alpine) or reference-price inflation (owned list +7.3% LFL with realized flat). The LFL realized data (the cleaner comparison) supports the premium holding or widening; the composition data says clearance volume of owned product rose. Both effects (roughly ±20-35bps) are an order of magnitude larger than the annual mix benefit (+3-8bps). So the margin argument for T2 should be framed as "premium resilience vs national-brand promo", conditioned on CALIA clearance normalising; the Q3 FY26 CC crawl (Oct/Nov) and the clearance bot are the tests.

### 4C. Caveats on the markdown evidence
- Online only; first 48 Featured results; displayed swatch can be a clearance colour; ScoreCard/coupons/in-cart and store shelf not captured (X11).
- High-low reference pricing: owned "list" behaves like a compare-at price; 20% of owned always on sale. "% on sale" is a poor margin proxy (X11 caveat).
- Clearance bots/Slickdeals rank by % off vs "was" price; no 2025 base for the bots; bot logic unknown (X08-6).
- Composition: bigger owned assortment (+25% listings, R12) mechanically adds end-of-season clearance; the CALIA reset (denim/lifestyle) likely clears legacy styles (X08 INFERENCE).
- Overbuild risk: Aug-Sep intake 2.30x run-rate into a "highly promotional" Q4 (R8-3) could create more owned markdowns.
