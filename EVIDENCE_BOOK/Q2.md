# Q2: Is DKS's owned-brand gain DKS-specific, is it still running, and what is it worth in GM?
Thesis tested: "mix of DKS owned will grow faster than modeled and this will be margin accretive."
Run 2026-10-07. All pulls were free, public Bazaarvoice (BV) front-door API calls (no login, no Apify, polite threading). Scripts and outputs are in `EVIDENCE_BOOK\Q2\`. Inputs were read from `PB_SCRAPE\raw` and `PB_SCRAPE\scripts`; nothing under PB_SCRAPE was modified.

## Bottom line
1. **The peer control supports the thesis: the gain is DKS-specific, not an industry private-label tide.**
   - At Academy (ASO), the closest US peer, private-label share of apparel reviews *fell* over the same windows:
     - Apr-Aug: 64.0% (2024) → 56.2% (2025) → 54.8% (2026).
     - Over the same windows DKS rose 40.3 → 43.9 → 46.0%.
   - Difference-in-differences (DiD), Apr-Aug 2025→2026: **+3.5pp (z 5.5)**. Using post-purchase-email reviews only, it is +7.5pp.
   - ASO's own filings point the same way. Reported private/exclusive mix was ~23% in FY24 and ~22% in FY25 and Q2 FY26.
   - **Caveat:** part of ASO's decline is its own national-brand expansion (Nike share of ASO apparel reviews 8.4% → 14.9%; Jordan added in 2025), so ASO is not a perfectly neutral control.
   - The conservative read is that DKS's raw y/y gain (+2.1pp) is the DKS-specific gain. The peer shows no positive industry trend to subtract.
   - In the cleanest recent months (May-Jul 2026), ASO was flat y/y and the DiD fell to roughly DKS's own +1.5 to +2pp.
2. **The refresh adds little new data.** DKS's post-purchase review email (PIE) has **not** restarted. There has been no batch on any of the 8 Tuesdays after 2026-08-11 (Tuesday PIE counts 0-5, vs 317-591 on the same Tuesdays in 2025).
   - The Q3 FY26-to-date read is therefore still the Aug 1-17 like-for-like window: apparel owned share 44.2 → 46.0% (+1.8pp; men's +5.3pp, kids +3.8pp, women's −3.2pp).
   - Over the same window ASO was flat (50.7 → 50.6%), so the Q3-to-date DiD is +1.9pp.
   - Organic-only reviews over Aug 1-Oct 6 slipped at both retailers: DKS 56.9 → 53.3%, ASO 65.2 → 61.3%.
3. **The margin effect is real but small.**
   - At the evidence-implied pace (~+0.45pt a year of penetration) and a markdown-adjusted 600bps premium: **+3.6bps GM in FY27E, +5.4bps in FY28E** vs a flat-mix consensus.
   - That is **+$0.04 EPS in FY27E and +$0.07 in FY28E** (0.3-0.5% of consensus).
   - Mix alone covers about **26%** of consensus's +7bps FY26E→FY27E DSG GM step-up.
   - Bull case (+1pt a year, footwear −1pt a year, 800bps): +14bps / +$0.17 in FY27E, +21bps / +$0.27 in FY28E. This is the only scenario that covers all of the +7bps.
   - **Best framing: a GM cushion (~6-21bps of total GM by FY28 ≈ 18-70bps of margin on national-footwear sales), not an EPS driver.**

## 1. Peer control: ASO vs DKS (measured data)
**Method (identical on both sides)**
- Native (non-syndicated) reviews on men's, women's, boys' and girls' apparel products in each retailer's BV catalog.
- Owned share = owned-brand reviews ÷ all-brand reviews, same basket and months, pooled.
- ASO owned brands are identified by brand name from USPTO marks owned by Academy, Ltd.: Magellan, BCG, Freely, R.O.W., O'Rageous, Brazos, Game Winner, H2OX, Austin Trading, Brava, Mosaic and others.
- ASO data: A1 pull, 15,694 products, complete through 2026-10-07. DKS data: R1.

Apparel owned share, % (n reviews):

| Window / source | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DKS Apr-Aug PIE+organic | 33.7 | 40.3 | 43.9 | **46.0** (25,123) |
| ASO Apr-Aug all ex-sampling | 60.4 | 64.0 | 56.2 | **54.8** (25,127) |
| ASO Apr-Aug email only | 59.7 | 63.0 | 60.0 | 55.1 (12,165) |
| DKS Feb-Jul PIE+organic | 34.1 | 38.7 | 44.3 | 44.8 |
| ASO Feb-Jul all ex-sampling | 59.6 | 59.3 | 58.7 | 54.7 |

DiD (DKS change minus ASO change), pp:

| Pairing | Apr-Aug 25→26 | Apr-Aug 24→26 | Feb-Jul 25→26 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broad (DKS PIE+ORG vs ASO ex-sampling) | **+3.5** (SE 0.6) | +14.9 | +4.4 |
| Email-prompted (DKS PIE vs ASO email) | +7.5 | +14.4 | +1.6 |
| Email+SMS (DKS PIE vs ASO email+SMS) | +3.5 | +14.7 | +4.5 |
| Organic (DKS ORG vs ASO ORG; ASO n ~300-500) | +11.5 (SE 4.0) | +14.6 | +9.0 |
| Men's broad | +2.4 (ASO +0.1) | +15.0 | +1.4 |
| Kids broad | +6.1 | +17.0 | +9.8 |
| Women's broad | +1.9 (DKS −0.7, ASO −2.6) | +12.3 | +5.2 |

**How to read it**
- ASO's 2024→2025 drop coincides with its post-purchase SMS program launch in 2025. SMS reviews skew national, and review volume roughly quadrupled. So use **2025→2026** (both years have SMS) or the **email-only** series for the two-year view.
- **Monthly y/y DiD for 2026** (Feb→Aug): +3.4, +0.9, +11.6, +3.5, +0.6, +1.5, +5.6pp; mean +3.9.
  - ASO was roughly flat y/y in May-Jul (−1.4 / +1.2 / +0.4).
  - In those months the DiD ≈ DKS's own gain (+1.8 to +2.1).
- **Men's.** ASO is flat, so DKS men's +2.5pp is entirely DKS-specific. This is the VRST/DSG story vs Nike.
- **Organic.** Organic owned share fell at **both** retailers in 2026 (DKS 56.8 → 50.1; ASO 78.1 → 59.9). That weakens R1's "organic decline = against" point: it looks like a reviewer-pool shift common to both, not DKS-specific owned-brand weakness. ASO's organic n is small.
- **The method validates on the peer.** ASO's review-share direction matched its reported private-label % in both years: up FY23→FY24 (~20 → ~23%; reviews 60.4 → 64.0) and down FY24→FY25 (~23 → ~22%). It also caught ASO's "better" brands growing, matching the CEO's "double-digit" Freely/R.O.W. comment (Q1 FY26 call): Freely 2.8 → 3.4 → 3.9%, R.O.W. 0.7 → 1.2 → 1.4%.
- **Limitations**
  - Basket coverage differs by brand. Licensed/fan Nike and UA apparel sits outside the apparel trees: only 51% of apparel-named Nike products at ASO fall in the basket, vs 94% for BCG. This affects levels more than trends.
  - Review share ≠ sales share.
  - The DiD does **not** change the review→sales conversion (β 0.29-0.54 was calibrated on DKS's raw series against the 10-K).

## 3. Margin bridge (assumptions → model; all INFERENCE)
**Frame**
- Consensus has no mix line, so it implicitly holds penetration flat.
- Base FY25 penetration of DICK'S non-footwear merchandise is 19.05%. Owned mix of DICK'S sales = penetration × (1 − footwear 30.33% − "Other" 3.4%) = 12.62%.
- Δmix × premium = GM bps on DICK'S-segment revenue (BBG $14,753M / $15,203M / $15,742M for FY26E / FY27E / FY28E).
- EPS = ΔGP × (1 − 27.46%) ÷ 89.09M shares.
- FY27 figures are cumulative from FY25. Excluding the FY26 gain (if consensus FY26E already embeds it) roughly halves the FY27 figures.

**Markdown-adjusted premium** (from X11's Common Crawl dicks.com listings, Aug+Sep 2025 vs 2026; GM recomputed on realized price at fixed cost; illustrative owned/national merchandise margins 44% / 36%)

| Method | Owned realized price | National realized price | Change in premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pooled average discount (owned 18.1 → 21.9%, national 7.3 → 8.4%) | −4.6% | −1.2% | **−196bps** |
| Matched-category average discount (19.7 → 24.9% vs 8.5 → 9.6%) | −6.5% | −1.2% | **−310bps** |
| Like-for-like products, realized offer | −0.3% | −2.5% | **+147bps** (owned list prices +7.3% vs +2.3%) |

- **Premium tiers used:** management 700/800/900; **markdown-adjusted central 600** (800 − ~200); **stress 400** (700 − ~300).
- The like-for-like result says part of the markdown "evidence" is inflation of the compare-at reference price, not realized-price erosion. That is why central is 600, not 500.

**Evidence-implied penetration pace**
- Review route: relative review-share change × β 0.29-0.54 × 19.05% penetration.
  - Apr-Aug +4.8% → +0.27 to +0.49pt a year.
  - R1's Q2 FY26 index +4.3% → +0.24 to +0.44.
  - R1's early-Q3 index +6.4% → +0.35 to +0.66.
  - (If β is misapplied to the +2.1pp *level* change, it gives +0.6 to +1.2pt. That is not how β was calibrated.)
- Listing route: R12 owned share of listings +0.94pt, taken 1:1 as an **upper bound**.
- **Evidence range: +0.25 to +0.94pt a year; central ~+0.45.**

**Footwear drift** (+0.5/yr penetration at 600bps)
- Footwear −0.5pt a year: FY27 +5.2bps; FY28 +7.8bps, +$0.10.
- Footwear −1pt a year: FY27 +6.4bps; FY28 +9.7bps, +$0.12.
- Footwear +0.5pt a year: FY27 +2.8bps.
- Bull case (+1/yr, footwear −1/yr, 800bps): FY27 Δmix +1.75pt, **+14.0bps, +$0.17**, y/y +7.1bps (101% of the +7bps); FY28 +21.2bps, **+$0.27 (1.8%)**.

**Break-even** to cover all of consensus's +7bps FY27 step-up from mix alone, at flat footwear:
- +1.76pt a year of penetration at 600bps;
- +1.32pt a year at 800bps.
- Both are above any post-FY18 pace (M1-6).

**Cushion**
- GM bps ÷ footwear share (30.3%) = margin points on national-footwear sales the mix offsets.
- Central FY28: 5.4bps of total GM ≈ **18bps** of footwear margin.
- +0.5/yr at 800bps: ≈ 26bps. Bull: ≈ 70bps.
- Against JPM's ~90bps Q3 FY26 promo pressure (from M1), the central case offsets ~6% in FY28. The bull case offsets ~25%.
