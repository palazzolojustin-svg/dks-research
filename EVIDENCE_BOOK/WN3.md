## 4. Vertical / owned-brand wording in the press releases (Thesis 2)
- No vertical-brand sales figure, penetration %, or margin uplift appears in any press release, 2022 through 2026. Press-release evidence for Thesis 2 is wording only.
- Stack quote, Q1 FY24 (2024-05-29 EX-99.1, checked in source): "The product pipeline from our key brand partners and our vertical brand portfolio has never been better."
- How the vertical-brand risk wording in the forward-looking statements evolved (checked in source):
  - 2022-11 and 2023-03: "risks relating to vertical brands and new retail concepts".
  - 2024-05 and 2025-03: "our investments in vertical brand offerings and new specialty concept stores"; "our vertical brands growth strategy" (2025-03-11).
  - 2025-05 to 2025-11, around the FL deal: "the combined company's vertical brand strategy and plans". That is, the vertical strategy now explicitly covers Foot Locker too.
  - 2026-03-12 and 2026-05-27: "risks associated with our vertical brand offerings, including brand strategy and marketing, improved space in-store, expanding product categories..."
  - **2026-08-25 adds "competition, innovation" and "third party liability and proprietary rights"** to that list. INFERENCE: wording about more in-store space and new categories suggests vertical brands are expanding. The added "competition" wording also arrives in the same release that cut segment margin over a promotional marketplace.
- Margin backdrop (the press releases never attribute it to vertical brands):
  - DICK'S Business gross margin [d from segment tables]: Q2 FY26 37.85% vs 37.06%; Q1 FY26 36.34% vs 36.70%; FY25 36.33% vs 35.90%.
  - Q2 FY26 consolidated gross profit includes $59.0M of IEEPA tariff refunds, of which $38.1M relates to prior-year costs. That $38.1M sits in corporate & other, so the DICK'S-segment gross profit may still include about $20.9M of current-year refund [d / INFERENCE]. This flatters the Q2 DICK'S gross-margin step-up; treat it as a caveat.
  - Q1 FY26 DICK'S gross margin fell 36 bps. CUTS AGAINST any clean mix-driven margin trend.
- Long-term incentive plan metrics: FY23-24 plan used "adjusted merchandise margin"; FY25-26 plan uses "external merchandise margin %" plus eCommerce growth (W05A > 2023-03-23 8-K; W05B > 2025-03-27 8-K). INFERENCE: "external" may exclude internal or vertical effects; its definition is not given.

## 6. Other context
- FL drag on the FY26 guide: FL segment cut to $(80)M to $(40)M from +$110M to +$150M; FL PF comp cut to -2.0% to 0.0% from +1.5% to +3.0%. Stack: FL has "greater exposure to legacy footwear silhouettes and greater dependence on footwear launch and retro product" (W05C > 2026-08-25).

- Inventory: DICK'S Business inventory +6% year on year at 2026-08-01 vs H1 sales +6.0% [d]. +3% at Q1 FY26; +1% at FYE25. Inventory is now growing as fast as sales. Watch item for promotions and margin.

## 7. Summary of what cuts against the theses
1. H2 FY26 guide implies roughly a 0% to 2.7% comp. Management attributes this to a promotional footwear/apparel market and price investment, and the DICK'S segment profit guide was cut by about $60M to $80M.
2. Fewer HoS conversions in H2 FY26 (about 8) than in H2 FY25 (13), and the Q3 FY25 cohort starts lapping its lift.
3. The comp series is flattered by method changes (relocations, GameChanger, Warehouse Sale), so it is not a pure like-for-like measure.
4. No quantified vertical-brand data in any press release. The forward-looking statements added vertical-brand "competition" risk in Aug 2026. Q1 FY26 DICK'S gross margin fell 36 bps. The Q2 FY26 gross margin may include part of the tariff refund.
5. Every FL international statement (new president, "expand its market share in international markets", constant-currency comps improving to about -2% / -3%) points to turnaround, not exit. Europe store closures are modest (-26, -4%).
