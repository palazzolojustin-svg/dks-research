# S7: Evidence book for T2, "owned-brand mix grows faster than modeled, and it adds to margin"
Compiled 2026-10-07 from PB_SCRAPE (STATE.md; wave2 M1, N1-N4, E1, R1, R3, R4, R5, R7, R8, R9 [interim], R12, R20; wave3 A1 [partial]). Raw files and scripts were spot-checked under PB_SCRAPE\raw\. Nothing under SOURCE, CORE_NOTES, WORKING_NOTES, THESIS_SCRAPE or PB_SCRAPE was edited.

**Conventions**
- **"DSG GM"** means the DICK'S Sporting Goods *segment* gross margin (Bloomberg ~36.2-36.3%, FY26E-FY28E). It does not mean the DSG brand.
- **Penetration** = owned (vertical) $ ÷ DICK'S non-footwear merchandise (hardlines + apparel). **Mix** = owned $ ÷ DICK'S-segment sales.
- **Fiscal years:** DKS FY26 = year ending Jan-2027. Q3 FY26 = Aug-Oct 2026.
- **Grades:** A = large sample, controlled, reproducible. B = solid but indirect or short window. C = qualitative or weak proxy.
- **INFERENCE** labels my own arithmetic or interpretation.

## 0. Bottom line for the pitch
1. **"Faster than modeled" is supported on direction.** Consensus implicitly holds owned mix flat at ~12.8-13%. Every independent 2026 scrape series that measures owned *presence* or *demand* inflected up in 2026, after being flat in FY24-FY25:
   - listings: R12 +0.94pt, 95% CI excluding zero;
   - review share: R1 apparel 40.3 → 43.9 → 46.0%;
   - new-style share: R5 5.7 → 7.9 → 8.1%;
   - SKU creation: R5 UPC clock +25-30%;
   - homepage full-price features: E1, 3.5x;
   - TV weight: R4, VRST.

   R7 also removes the "licensed inflation" objection. Licensed lines are a small and shrinking share of "vertical", so owned-only grew faster than the headline.
2. **The size the evidence supports is modest.** Calibrated to sales, the scrape data points to **+0.2 to +0.65pt a year of penetration** (R1 review elasticity; R12 listings at β < 1). That is consistent with M1's **+0.5pt/yr base case** (FY27E mix ~13.3-13.7%, **+4 to +8bps of GM, +$0.05-0.09 EPS**). It is not consistent with +1pt/yr yet.
3. **The margin-accretive leg is real per unit, but the 2026 data cuts both ways.**
   - For: owned growth is apparel-led and moving to full price (E1, R9 DSG full-price share up, list prices up). National brands are the ones flooding markdowns (R12-5: markdown listings +143%). Lower tariff cost on direct imports (R8-4) and licence-to-owned conversion (R7-4) add a little.
   - Against: CALIA and owned clearance are getting deeper (R9 CALIA full-price 47 → 28%, R12 clearance-apparel owned share 13.5 → 16.3%, E1 70-91% event days).
   - Net: accretive at the gross premium on core mix, with a haircut on incremental mix.
4. **+20 / +30 / +50bps of DSG GM is out of reach from mix alone on any scrape reading** (section 3).
   - +20bps by FY27 needs about **+1.9pt a year of penetration for two years**, roughly 2.7x the FY23-FY25 pace. The only reading that gets close is R12 listings translated 1:1 into sales, which gives about +16bps.
   - +30bps and +50bps would need the 2017 vendor-cull analog or bigger.
   - **Framing:** owned brands are a GM floor and resilience lever. JPM's ~90bps promotional pressure in Q3 FY26 dwarfs the mix effect. They are not an EPS engine. The cleaner variant is *direction plus durability*, not magnitude.

## 1. Model frame (M1): assumptions, scenario grid, and why the EPS effect is small
**[M1-1] Reconciled history.** Penetration bands come from intersecting the 10-K "$X billion" and "~X%" disclosures:

| Year | Penetration band |
|---|---|
| FY16 | 11.9-13.1% |
| FY17 | 14.7-15.8% |
| FY18 | 17.3-18.6% |
| FY20 | 18.3-19.5% |
| FY22 | 18.2-19.1% |
| **FY23** | **17.56-17.85% (trough)** |
| FY24 | 18.0-18.8% |
| **FY25** | **18.7-19.6% (mid 19.05%)** |

- FY23 → FY25 is +0.8 to +2.0pt (real). FY24 → FY25 is inside rounding noise.
- FY25 only matches the FY20 and FY22 highs. FY18-FY25 drift was ~+0.17pt a year.
- Owned $ CAGR FY23-FY25 was +5.1% (band 3.4-6.8%), against DICK'S sales +4.2% and non-footwear merchandise +1.1%.
- FY25 strips Foot Locker at 84% footwear. At 80% footwear the FY25 band is 18.9-19.8%; at 88% it is 18.4-19.3%.
- Method: primary 10-K HTML, interval arithmetic (`scripts\M1_mix_model.py`). Grade A for history, B for FY25.

**[M1-3] Denominator dilution.**
- "Other" revenue (GameChanger, DMN, services) rose +0.4 to +1.0pt of DICK'S sales y/y every quarter: Q3 FY25 3.12%, Q4 2.59%, Q1 FY26 3.65%, Q2 FY26 3.09%.
- Flat penetration therefore gives **12.63% mix, not 12.76%**. Consensus-flat needs penetration of **19.25%** (+0.2pt) just to hold.
- Implication: the 10-K will keep printing "~13%" even if owned brands win share. Ask IR for owned dollars.

**[M1-4] Footwear lever.** DICK'S footwear share was still rising y/y in 1H FY26 (+0.70pt at 84% FL footwear), but Q2 FY26 slowed to +0.23pt.
- Each −1pt of footwear share adds ~0.19pt of owned mix (≈ +1.5bps GM).
- The Q3 FY26 10-Q (early Dec 2026) is the test.
- N3-1 peer data supports footwear softening: ASO footwear −1.0% in Q2 FY26, JD North America LFL −5/−6%.

**[M1-5] Scenario grid.** Assumptions:
- Bloomberg DICK'S revenue $14,753 / 15,203 / 15,742M (FY26-FY28E).
- "Other" at 3.4%; footwear base 30.33%; 800bp premium; 100% drop-through.
- Tax 27.5%; diluted shares 89.95 / 89.09 / 89.67M.
- Consensus EPS FY27 $13.36, FY28 $15.03.

| Penetration × footwear | FY27E mix | Δmix | GM bps | EBIT $M | EPS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| flat × flat | 12.63% | −0.13 | −1.0 | −1.6 | −$0.01 |
| +0.5/yr × fw flat | 13.29% | +0.53 | +4.3 | +6.5 | +$0.05 |
| **+0.5/yr × fw −1/yr (base)** | **13.69%** | **+0.93** | **+7.5** | **+11.4** | **+$0.09 (0.7%)** |
| +1/yr × fw flat | 13.95% | +1.20 | +9.6 | +14.5 | +$0.12 |
| **+1/yr × fw −1/yr (bull)** | **14.37%** | **+1.62** | **+12.9** | **+19.7** | **+$0.16 (1.2%)** |
| FY28E bull | 15.28% | +2.52 | +20.2 | +31.7 | +$0.26 (1.7%) |

- Implied FY26 owned $ growth: consensus-flat +4.6%; +0.5pt case +5.8%; +1pt case +8.5%; bull +10.1%.
- **Why the EPS effect is small:**
  - each 1pt of mix × 8pt premium = 8bps = ~$12M EBIT = ~$0.10 EPS;
  - a 5% EPS move (~$82M) needs ~6.7pt of extra mix;
  - DICK'S bps are on segment revenue, so on consolidated revenue they are ~1/3 smaller;
  - F6's haircut (500-700bp on incremental mix) cuts the figures by ~25%.

**[M1-6] Pace benchmarks.**
- FY16 → FY18: +5.4pt in two years. This was the vendor cull, and it included licensed Reebok, so it overstates what owned brands can do.
- FY18 → FY25: ~0.17pt a year.
- FY23 → FY25: ~0.7pt a year (0.4-1.0).
- Floor from management: Hobart said on the Q1 FY26 call (2026-05-27) that vertical brands were "outperforming the chain". At FL 84% that implies **≥ +0.5pt y/y penetration in Q1 FY26** (≥ +1.2pt at FL 80%; ~flat at FL 88%).
- The Q2 FY26 call had zero owned-brand mentions.

**Grade for the M1 frame:** A (arithmetic), B (inputs). It is the denominator for everything below.

### 2B. "Margin accretive" (rate per unit of mix)
1. **Premium (management, KNOWN):** 700-900bp vs national. The FY25 10-K changed the comparison to "higher margins than equivalent third-party products" (N1-6).
2. **Owned growth is apparel-led and hardgoods are shrinking (R8-5, R12, R5).**
   - Imports: owned apparel kg Apr-Sep 2026 **2.95M, +34% vs 2024** (ex-consolidator +3%).
   - Imports: owned hardgoods value Q1-Q3 **−43% vs 2024** (outdoor −82% in Q2).
   - Listings: apparel, fitness and accessories up; golf and team flat.
   - INFERENCE: mix *within* owned is moving toward higher-GM apparel, so the rate gain exceeds the $ mix gain. Grade B-.
3. **Full-price push, not clearance-led, in the core (E1, R9).**
   - Homepage owned features 3.5x while owned discount phrases are flat.
   - **DSG full-price share 47 → 56% with list $25 → $30.**
   - VRST list $65 → $78. Maxfli list $27.99 → $39.99, never marked down vs Pro V1 (X11).
   - Grade B.
4. **National brands are the ones discounting (R12-5, N3).**
   - /f/markdowns total listings 42,659 → 103,768 (+143%); owned markdown listings +15%; owned share of markdowns 3.7 → 1.8%.
   - JPM (Aug-26): discounts "broad-based".
   - INFERENCE: wider owned value gap at full price, which supports share gain without owned price cuts. Grade B.
5. **Tariff relief on direct imports (R8-4, INFERENCE).**
   - Implied IEEPA duty on DKS's own entries ~$48M FY25 (validated against the $57M refund).
   - FY26 Section 301/122 at 10-12.5% ≈ $25-30M a year, so an **~$20M a year owned-brand cost tailwind (~13bps on DICK'S revenue)**.
   - The Street books this generically as "tariff relief". Grade C+ (unit-value assumptions).
6. **Licence-to-owned (R7-4):** Monarch replaces Prince with no royalty (<$30M category; tiny). Grade C.
7. **Peer rule (N3-4, ASO 10-K):** a PL unit shift "positive impact on our gross margin rate but an adverse impact on our total net sales". The revenue haircut is offset at DKS because comps are ticket-led and owned is moving up price.
8. **Incentives (N4-2):** the External Merchandise Margin LTIP (FY25-26). Stack's target was raised from $5.0M to $17.5M. Owned mix is the most controllable lever, but vendor support counts too. Grade C.
9. **Derived DICK'S merch & services margin (N4-1):**
   - Q4 FY25 **+73bps** (UBS credited "vertical brand performance" for 4Q25 GM).
   - Q1 FY26 −29bps ("sales mix").

## 3. Required penetration for +20 / +30 / +50bps of DSG GM (INFERENCE, M1 framework)
**Mechanics**
- ΔGM (bps of DICK'S revenue) = Δmix (pt of DICK'S sales) × premium (pt).
- Δpenetration = Δmix ÷ (non-footwear merchandise share of DICK'S sales) = Δmix ÷ 0.663 (FY27E: 1 − 30.33% footwear − 3.4% Other).
- EBIT = bps × $15,203M. EPS = EBIT × 0.725 ÷ 89.09M.

| GM target vs consensus | Δmix at 700 / 800 / 900bp | Δpenetration at 800bp | Per year if by FY27 (2 yrs from FY25) | Per year if by FY28 (3 yrs) | FY27 EBIT / EPS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **+20bps** | 2.86 / **2.50** / 2.22pt | **+3.8pt** (19.05 → ~22.8%) | **~1.9pt/yr** | ~1.3pt/yr | $30M / **$0.25 (1.9%)** |
| **+30bps** | 4.29 / **3.75** / 3.33pt | **+5.7pt** (→ ~24.7%) | **~2.8pt/yr** | ~1.9pt/yr | $46M / **$0.37 (2.8%)** |
| **+50bps** | 7.14 / **6.25** / 5.56pt | **+9.4pt** (→ ~28.5%) | **~4.7pt/yr** | ~3.1pt/yr | $76M / **$0.62 (4.6%)** |

- **Adjustments:** add ~0.2pt of penetration just to offset "Other" dilution. Footwear −1pt/yr contributes ~0.19pt mix a year (~+1.5bps/yr), so footwear −1/yr cuts the +20bps requirement by only ~0.3pt of penetration a year.
- **If achieved in a single year** (y/y GM step): +20bps needs +3.8pt of penetration in one year. That exceeds the FY16-FY18 vendor-cull pace (+2.7pt a year, partly licensed).

**Is any scrape evidence consistent with this?**

| R1 reviews at β 0.29-0.54 | +0.2 to +0.65pt/yr | ~+1 to +3.5bps/yr | Base case |
| R12 listings, relative +16% y/y, at β 0.3-0.55 | +0.9 to +1.7pt | ~+5 to +9bps | Upside case |

| R5 new-style share (+38% relative FY24 → FY25, +2% FY25 → FY26) | n/a | n/a | Says FY25 was the step and FY26 holds it. Not a +2pt/yr signal. |
| M1-6 management "outperforming" floor | ≥ +0.5pt (Q1 FY26) | n/a | Base case |

- **Conclusion:**
  - **+20bps is at the edge of the most aggressive scrape reading.**
  - **+30bps and +50bps have no support.** They would need a vendor exit or a Foot Locker owned-brand rollout. R1-9 shows zero DKS brands on FL/Champs today.
  - **Defensible variant:** FY27E mix ~13.7-14.4% vs ~12.8% consensus-implied, i.e. **+7 to +13bps, $0.09-0.16 EPS**, plus the ~$20M tariff tailwind on owned imports (R8-4, ~+13bps, partly in consensus already).
