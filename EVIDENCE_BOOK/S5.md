## 0. Bottom line for the pitch
- **T2 (owned mix grows faster than modeled) has real, independent, y/y alt-data support in Apr-Sep 2026**: purchase-triggered review share (X01/R1), listing share on matched pages (X03/R12/X02), best-seller slot share (X02, level only), full-price featured placement (X03, E1), and SKU creation (X01 style prefixes, R5). All point the same direction at +1.5 to +2.6pt of *share-of-units/listings* y/y in apparel.
- **The translation to sales mix is much smaller**: R1 calibrates to roughly +0.2 to +0.65pt of total sales mix y/y; the filing-based ex-footwear penetration rose ~+0.3-0.5pt/yr (Wells % basis) or ~+0.9pt/yr (mgmt $ basis, FL-assumption dependent). Supportable base case: **+0.5pt/yr of DICK'S Business mix vs consensus flat ~12.8-13%** (13.3% FY26E / 13.8% FY27E per F2 scenario).
- **The margin effect is small and partly offset**: +1pt mix × 700-900bps premium ≈ +7-9bps GM. +1.4pt by FY27 ≈ +11bps vs BBG DSG GM 36.24% FY26E / 36.31% FY27E. M1: ~+$0.16 EPS FY27 even in bull case. Owned markdown intensity is rising (JPM calendar 0→1→5-6 events; on-site 44.8% vs 23.9% marked down; clearance pages up), so incremental mix should carry a haircut premium (~500-700bps). **This is a margin-resilience argument, not an EPS driver.**
- **Biggest structural lever is the denominator**: footwear (0% owned) went 20.8% → ~30% of DICK'S sales FY21-FY25 and hid a flat-to-rising ex-footwear penetration of ~18-19%. A footwear stall (legacy "hangover" into 2027) lifts headline mix ~0.18-0.19pt per 1pt of footwear mix lost, mechanically.
- **Management has gone silent**: zero owned-brand mentions at the Q2 FY26 call and GS 2026-09-14; Q2 GM credited to DMN/GC/IEEPA refunds; deck wording softened "#1 or #2" → "top three vendor". Any mix upside is pure variant, with no management air cover.
- **T1 read-through**: owned brands carry LOWER list prices (women's apparel $58.77 vs $97.60 national; men's $60.76 vs $71.14) and higher markdown incidence, so faster owned mix is a mild **headwind to ticket/AUR**, not a support. Comps have been ticket-led (1Q26 ticket +5.5%, 2Q26 +3.6%). No dataset measures owned penetration inside HoS; only qualitative ("more spaces with our vertical brands in House of Sport", MS 2025-12-03).

## 4. Contradictions / corrections between agents
