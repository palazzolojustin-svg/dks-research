## 1. When does the promotional overhang end, relative to DKS Q4 (Nov-26 to Jan-27)?

**Bottom line from these files:** the promotional overhang is expected to last through most or all of DKS Q4. Nike's own deadline for clearing its marketplace is "end of December 2026", which falls in the middle of DKS Q4. JPM does not expect conditions to improve "materially over the next two quarters" (said 21 Aug, which takes us to about Feb-2027). DKS and JD both call NA promotional "through at least 4Q/Holiday". Some offsets point the other way: wholesale footwear channel inventory is already lean, and Nike is cutting how much it ships into wholesale in Sep-Nov 2026 (its FY2Q27).

## 4. Owned brands / private label (T2): what these files contain

- **No DKS vertical-brand data** (mix %, margin, growth) in these three files.

  - Private label ≈22% of FY25 merchandise sales (19 brands: Magellan Outdoors, BCG, Academy, O'rageous, Game Winner, Outdoor Gourmet, Freely, Redfield, H2OX, R.O.W.).
  - "~53% of customers bought a private brand FY25." National brands ≈78%; no brand >~12% of sales.
  - Source: [PA] › ASO summary §5.
  - Use as a ceiling/benchmark for a value-skewed full-line peer.
- **Price-gap backdrop that favors value/private label:**
  - 38% of consumers wait for sales; price/value is the #2 purchase attribute.
  - Lower-income DTC preference fell 9 pts to 32%; department/discount stores at 39%. BI: "broad wholesale and value-oriented retail partnerships remain critical to maintaining share among lower-income households."
  - Source: [N3] › BI survey; [N4] › §5.
- **National-brand premium is eroding in places (inference: supports private-label share gains on comfort/value):**
  - Nike resale prices fell for three straight months (Aug: Nike -3.7%, Jordan -5.9%, Adidas -8.1%, NB -5.0%).
  - Jordan "eroded scarcity."
  - Nike CFROI at a 30-year low of 6% (UBS HOLT).
  - Source: [N4] › table rows.
- **Footwear is "zero sum"** (Barclays): share moves between brands and retailers instead of the market growing. [N3]
- **Margin read:**
  - Nike GM 42.8%, with "higher sales returns and discounts with our wholesale partners."
  - FL division-profit history: FY24 merchandise margin markdown pressure "partially offset by vendor allowances, which improved our gross margin rate by 20 basis points." [PA] › FL Standalone §5a
  - **Inference:** in a heavy-markdown holiday, vendor support can protect national-brand margin, which narrows the private-label margin advantage.
- **Against T2:**
  - Comfort plus performance tech drive demand (running, Hoka, On, Vomero). These are national-brand innovation categories where private label rarely competes.
  - "Premium growth brands such as On and Hoka have essentially no presence at Famous Footwear" (UBS). Premium brand scarcity keeps traffic with national brands.

## 5. FL Europe / international and the DKS exit question (T3)

**Bottom line:** these files contain **no statement** from DKS, a broker or the press that DKS will sell or exit FL Europe.
