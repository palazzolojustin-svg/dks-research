L59: **Agreement to be Acquired by DICK’S**
L61: In a separate press release issued today, Foot
L62: Locker and DICK’S Sporting Goods announced that they have entered into a definitive merger agreement under which DICK’S will
L63: acquire Foot Locker in a transaction that implies an equity value of approximately $2.4 billion and an enterprise value of approximately
L64: $2.5 billion. Under the terms of the merger agreement, which has been unanimously approved by the boards of directors of DICK’S
L65: and Foot Locker, Foot Locker shareholders will elect to receive either (i) $24.00 in cash or (ii) 0.1168 shares of DICK’S common
L66: stock for each share of Foot Locker common stock. The election is not subject to a minimum or maximum amount of cash or stock consideration.
L68: The transaction is subject to Foot Locker shareholder
L69: approval and other customary closing conditions, including regulatory approvals, and is expected to close in the second half of 2025.
L71: Foot Locker plans to report full financial results
L72: for its first quarter ended May 3, 2025, before the U.S. markets open on Thursday, May 29, 2025. In light of the pending transaction with
L73: DICK’S, Foot Locker will not be holding its previously scheduled conference call to discuss its first quarter 2025 results and will
L74: not be providing or updating previously issued financial guidance.
