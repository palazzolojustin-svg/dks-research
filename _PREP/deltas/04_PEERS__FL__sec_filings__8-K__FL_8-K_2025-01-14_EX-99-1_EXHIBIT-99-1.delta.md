L20: **FOOT LOCKER, INC. ELECTS SONIA SYNGAL AND
L21: JOHN VENHUIZEN TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS**
L23: **NEW YORK, NY, Jan. 14, 2025** -- Foot Locker, Inc. (NYSE:
L24: FL) today announced that its Board of Directors has elected Sonia Syngal and John Venhuizen as directors of the Board, effective January 12,
L25: 2025. The Company also announced today that Guillermo G. Marmol will not stand for reelection at the Company’s 2025 Annual Meeting
L26: of Shareholders in accordance with the Company’s Director Retirement Age Policy within the Company’s Corporate Governance
L27: Guidelines.
L29: Ms. Syngal is an accomplished retail executive with two decades
L30: of industry experience spanning ecommerce sales, global supply chain, operations, brand strategy and marketing. She most recently served
L31: as President and Chief Executive Officer of Gap, Inc., including overseeing Old Navy, Gap, Banana Republic, and Athleta, after having
L32: served in other leadership roles within the organization, including Division President & CEO, Old Navy and Executive Vice President,
L33: Global Supply Chain. She also serves as a member of the Board of Directors of Tanger, Inc.
L35: Mr. Venhuizen has served for 12 years as President and Chief Executive
L36: Officer of Ace Hardware Corporation, the largest hardware cooperative and hardline distributor in the world, after having previously served
L37: as its President and Chief Operating Officer. During his 30-year tenure at Ace, he has held positions of increasing responsibility including
L38: leadership roles overseeing marketing, store operations, strategy, business development, supply chain, IT and international.
L40: “In line with our continued focus on regular refreshment to ensure
L41: we have the right mix of skills and experience on our Board, we are delighted to welcome Sonia and John as new independent directors,”
L42: said Dona D. Young, Non-Executive Chairman. “Both Sonia and John bring substantial leadership and retail industry expertise, and
L43: we are confident both will be valuable additions to our Board. We are pleased to benefit from their respective insights as we continue
L44: to oversee execution of the Company’s initiatives and focus on delivering shareholder value. On behalf of the entire Board, I
L45: would also like express our sincere gratitude to Gil for his significant contributions to Foot Locker, Inc. during his time as a
L46: director.”
L48: Mary Dillon, President and Chief Executive Officer of Foot Locker, Inc.
L49: added, “We are thrilled to welcome Sonia and John to our Board. We look forward to benefitting from their complementary experience
L50: and fresh perspectives as we continue to execute our Lace Up Plan, pursue operational excellence, revamp our digital experience, and strengthen
L51: customer engagement initiatives.”
L53: Mrs. Syngal commented, “I am pleased to join Foot Locker, Inc.’s
L54: Board and leverage my experience with multi-banner, international retail operations. I am eager to work with the rest of the Board and
L55: the Company’s leadership as the team executes on the vision to unlock the ‘inner sneakerhead’ in its customers and deliver
L56: for shareholders.”
L58: “I am passionate about retail and offering unique experiences
L59: to customers, and I am excited to bring my expertise to Foot Locker’s Board,” Mr. Venhuizen said. “I look forward
L60: to collaborating with my fellow directors and the leadership team to capture the Company’s many opportunities for growth and value
L61: creation.”
