L34: PITTSBURGH and NEW YORK,
L35: Aug. 26, 2025 /PRNewswire/ -- DICK'S Sporting Goods, Inc. ("DICK'S Sporting Goods") (NYSE: DKS), a leading U.S. based
L36: full-line omni-channel sporting goods retailer, and Foot Locker, Inc. ("Foot Locker") (NYSE: FL), a leading footwear and
L37: apparel retailer, today announced that the waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended
L38: (the "HSR Act"), expired at 11:59 p.m., Eastern Time on August 25, 2025 in connection with the previously announced acquisition
L39: of Foot Locker by DICK'S Sporting Goods (the "Merger").
L41: Following the expiration
L42: of the waiting period under the HSR Act, all required regulatory approvals to complete the Merger have been received. As was also previously
L43: disclosed, Foot Locker shareholders approved the Merger at the Foot Locker special meeting held on August 22, 2025. The Merger is
L44: expected to close on September 8, 2025, subject to the satisfaction of remaining customary closing conditions.
L47: and Foot Locker today also announced that the deadline for Foot Locker shareholders of record to elect the form of consideration that
L48: they wish to receive in connection with the Merger is 5:00 p.m., Eastern Time on August 29, 2025 (such deadline, as it may be extended,
L49: the "Election Deadline"). Participants in the Foot Locker 401(k) Plan and Foot Locker Puerto Rico Savings Plan (the "Plans")
L50: are subject to an earlier election deadline with respect to shares of Foot Locker common stock allocated to such participants under the
L51: Plans and should submit their elections for such shares by 5:00 p.m., Eastern Time on August 27, 2025.
L53: As further described
L54: in the election materials and in the parties' proxy statement/prospectus dated July 11, 2025, each Foot Locker shareholder may elect
L55: to receive, for each share of Foot Locker common stock held prior to the closing of the Merger, either (i) $24.00 in cash (the "cash
L56: consideration") or (ii) 0.1168 shares of DICK'S Sporting Goods common stock (the "stock consideration"). Foot Locker
L57: shareholders who fail to make a proper election by the Election Deadline will receive cash consideration for their shares of Foot Locker
L58: common stock. Foot Locker shareholders who otherwise would have received a fractional share of DICK'S Sporting Goods common stock upon
L59: an election for stock consideration will receive cash in lieu of such fractional share.
L61: Foot Locker shareholders
L62: of record wishing to make an election must deliver properly completed election materials to Equiniti Trust Company, LLC by the Election
L63: Deadline. Additional information about the election, deadlines and contacts can be found in materials sent to Foot Locker shareholders
L64: beginning on July 23, 2025.
L66: Foot Locker shareholders
L67: with questions regarding the election materials or the election process should contact D.F. King & Co., Inc., the information
L68: agent for the election, at (888) 541-9895 or their bank, broker or other nominee, as applicable, as soon as possible.
L70: A more detailed description
L71: of the Merger, the election process and the merger consideration is contained in the proxy statement/prospectus. Foot Locker shareholders
L72: are urged to read the proxy statement/prospectus carefully and in its entirety. Copies of the proxy statement/prospectus may be obtained
L73: free of charge by following the instructions below under the section entitled "Additional Information about the Merger and Where
