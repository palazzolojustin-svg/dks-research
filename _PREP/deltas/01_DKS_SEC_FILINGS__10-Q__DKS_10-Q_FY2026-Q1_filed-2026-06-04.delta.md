L515: Tariff Policy Changes
L517: On February 20, 2026, the United States (“U.S.”) Supreme Court issued a ruling that tariffs imposed under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (“IEEPA”) on goods imported into the U.S. were unauthorized. Following this ruling, and effective on April 20, 2026, the U.S. Customs and Border Protection launched a platform for importers of record to begin submitting IEEPA tariff refund requests.
L530: The Company has applied a gain contingency model in accordance with Accounting Standards Codification (“ASC”) 450-30, “Gain Contingencies” to account for potential recoveries of previously paid IEEPA tariffs. Under this model, a gain contingency is not recognized in the financial statements until the gain is realized or realizable. We did not receive any refund payments during the 13 weeks ended May 2, 2026, and accordingly, given the uncertainty regarding the amount or timing of collection, we will record the refunds as they are received.
