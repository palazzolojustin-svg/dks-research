### Risk factors (merger-specific substance)
- Fixed exchange ratio, no adjustment for price; cash fixed at $24.00 (subject only to extraordinary-transaction adjustment). Stock electors bear DKS price drop between election and receipt; cash electors forgo upside.
- Conditions may not be met; parties agreed to reasonable best efforts; outside date 2026-05-15, extendable to 2026-08-15 and again to 2026-11-15 in certain circumstances. Waiver of conditions could hurt holders.
- Regulatory approvals (HSR + certain other jurisdictions) may impose requirements/limitations/costs/restrictions on combined co; reasonable best efforts obligation may require complying with conditions; could delay or cause abandonment; could materially limit combined co revenues. DKS pays $95.5M reverse fee if clearances not obtained before outside date or denied.
- FL directors/officers have differing interests.
- No-shop + $59.5M fee may deter competing bidders or lower competing price.
- Interim operating covenants on FL (ordinary course; restrictions waivable with DKS consent not unreasonably withheld) and some DKS restrictions; customer/supplier/partner disruption; key-employee retention risk; may impair DKS ability to realize expected synergies.
- Significant non-recurring costs (legal, advisory, accounting, consulting, severance/employment, filing/regulatory fees, integration planning, retention); elimination of duplicative costs expected to offset over time but net benefit may not be near-term or at all.
- If not completed: FL stock decline, customer/supplier/employee reactions, unrecoverable costs, FL remains subject to strategic plan execution risk, "structural risks inherent in Foot Locker's business model," and risk FL stock might not trade at/above merger consideration value; litigation risk.
- Pro forma info preliminary; final PPA may differ materially.
- Change-in-control provisions in certain FL agreements may be triggered; counterparties may terminate, seek damages, require fees or renegotiate.
- Lawsuits/injunction risk (no-injunction is a closing condition).
- DKS post-close risks: dilution from stock issuance may pressure DKS price; former FL holder selling. Financing: DKS intends to finance portion via cash-on-hand, revolving borrowings and other new debt; no financing condition.
- Synergy/integration: "DICK'S Sporting Goods expects to operate Foot Locker as a standalone business unit within its portfolio and maintain the Foot Locker brands and expects to grow the business by applying operational expertise." DKS "expects significant cost synergies." Challenges listed: preserving FL relationships; "operating multiple banners, a differentiated store concept and successfully addressing the challenges facing Foot Locker's business"; integrating financial forecasting/controls/reporting cycles; consolidating corporate/IT/finance/admin infrastructure; coordinating sales & marketing; "coordinating operations in countries in which DICK'S Sporting Goods has not previously operated"; integrating employees/HR systems/benefits/morale/retention. Expanded size & geographic scope adds complexity/cost.
- Ownership: at absolute maximum (all FL holders elect stock), based on FL and DKS shares outstanding at 2025-07-07, former FL holders would own ~16.5% of outstanding DKS common stock; actual may be materially less.
- DKS Class B common stock held by Exec Chairman Edward W. Stack, relatives and family trusts controls majority of combined voting power; will control outcome of any stockholder vote post-merger; interests may differ.
- Indebtedness: DKS aggregate indebtedness as of 2025-05-03 ~$7.38B (incl. ~$3.08B operating leases); pro forma as of 2025-05-03 after merger + anticipated incurrence/assumption of debt as much as ~$13.03B (incl. ~$5.47B operating leases) assuming all FL holders take cash. Consequences: economic vulnerability, interest-rate risk "due to its variable rate term facilities," reduced flexibility, competitive disadvantage vs less-levered peers, harder future borrowing, cash flow dedicated to debt service. DKS "is focused on maintaining investment grade ratings" but may be unable; downgrade could hit debt trading price, increase term facility interest expense, raise refinancing cost, limit new debt. Some merger debt may bear variable rates.
- Other risks: per DKS 10-K FYE 2025-02-01 and FL 10-K FYE 2025-02-01 (incorporated).
