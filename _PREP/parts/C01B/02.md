### 1. How DKS makes money
- Largest US sporting-goods retailer (~$14.1B FY25 DICK'S Business sales; ~9% share of ~$140B US "core" TAM per Mar-2026 investor deck). Nike ~25% of core purchases; total co 31% FY2025 incl ~5 mo FL (FY25 10-K). ~13% owned vertical brands (DSG, CALIA, VRST, Maxfli…). ~890 large-format stores fulfil most online orders. Profit from (i) merch margin on full-price allocated/"high-heat" nat'l brand + vertical brands, (ii) leverage of largely fixed occupancy/labor, (iii) small high-margin alt profit pools (GameChanger SaaS, DICK'S Media Network). FL segment: mall/urban sneaker specialist, ~2,212 owned stores in 20+ countries, low-20s/high-20s GM, currently loses money.
