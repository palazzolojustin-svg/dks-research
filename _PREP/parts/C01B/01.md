Full chronology:
- Pre-Sep 2022 (before Mary Dillon FL CEO 9/1/22): DKS/FL execs "discussed from time to time the potential industrial logic of a combination"; no offers, no MNPI, no price.
- Mar 2023: FL announces "Lace-Up Plan" (tech, loyalty, digital, store refreshes, re-imagined concepts, core banners) w/ "aspirational long-term sales goals and EBIT margin targets."
- Jan 2, 2024: Ed Stack (DKS Exec Chair) messages Dillon re meeting (industry trends, strategic merit).
- Jan 4, 2024 (Chicago area): Dillon + Frank Bracken (FL) meet Ed Stack, Lauren Hobart (DKS CEO), Michael Stack (DKS VP Strategy/Corp Dev; since May 2025 SVP Footwear & Golf Galaxy). Interest: scale, global expansion; DKS "envisioned operating the Foot Locker banner independently from DICK'S Sporting Goods' other banners." Open to all-cash/all-stock/mix.
- Jan 11, 2024: FL board (Evercore, Skadden present): FL "could create more value ... by continuing to focus on the execution of the Lace-Up Plan"; declines. Dillon informs Stack Jan 12.
- Feb 26, 2024: Stack: DKS board concluded further conversation "was warranted." Feb 29: written letter in 2 wks; DKS doing public diligence.
- Mar 6, 2024: FL Q4 2023 results, FY2024 gdnc w/ 2-yr delay in Lace-Up targets; FL $34.31 (3/5) → $24.24 (3/6).
- Mar 12, 2024: DKS letter assumes FL not interested given mkt reaction; price "would need to reflect the risk and work required to execute the Lace-Up Plan."
- Apr 11, 2024 (Paris vendor mtgs): Stack tells Dillon DKS "was exploring other potential strategic options"; requests mtg w/ Dona Young (FL Chair).
- Apr 23, 2024: JD Sports agrees to acquire Hibbett; Stack asks to speak. Apr 25: agree to meet May 1.
- May 1, 2024 (Chicago): Young, Dillon meet Stack, Hobart. DKS open to all-cash or election cash vs cash/stock mix. FL: stock "did not appropriately reflect the value of successfully executing the Lace-Up Plan"; not right time.
- May 10, 2024: DKS board met; engaging fin advisor, legal, consultant; written proposal after DKS 5/29 and FL 5/30 earnings calls.
- May 20, 2024 FL board: reviews "the complexities and risks to achieving the Lace-Up Plan, the differences between Wall Street analyst estimates and the targets in the Lace-Up Plan, and recent investor feedback ... regarding the timing and achievability"; structural risk: "relies heavily on a single product category, with merchandise sourced from a limited number of brand partners"; sensitivities.
- Jun 7, 2024: DKS letter $31–$34 (20–31% prem to $25.88), cash or mix; price may adjust post-diligence; wants to speak w/ "a brand partner that DICK'S Sporting Goods considered to be important to the success of Foot Locker"; exclusivity; response by 6/21. Same day FL signs Evercore engagement.
- Jun 10, 2024 FL board: mgmt's latest forecasts "and two illustrative sensitivity cases"; two-phase diligence (limited first; more only if DKS raises price); authorizes NDA.
- Jun 14–Jul 25, 2024 NDA: FL proposed 24-mo standstill; DKS exclusivity + 3-mo (6/17); Skadden 18 mo no exclusivity (6/18); DKS refused >3 mo (6/19), "very serious"; FL floated 12 mo (6/20); 6/21 mtg cancelled. Jul 12: DKS drops exclusivity, holds standstill, "prepared to make its proposal to acquire Foot Locker public" if FL won't engage. Jul 15: Stack 4 mo max. Jul 18: FL agrees 4 mo. Jul 25, 2024: NDA signed, 4-mo standstill, no "don't ask, don't waive."
- Jul 26, 2024 (Palm Beach): mgmt mtg w/ Goldman, Evercore; FL presents Lace-Up, aggregated fin model thru FY2028, tech, real estate, new formats.
- Aug 28, 2024: FL Q2 2024; stock $32.81→$29.45.
- Sep 5, 2024: DKS $37.00, cash or mix; response by 9/16.
- Sep 15, 2024 FL board: Skadden fiduciary duties; Evercore prelim valuation + pro forma cap structure; alternatives: Lace-Up execution, JVs, divestitures, acquisitions, operational transformation, cap structure/return changes, capital mkts txns, minority investments (incl private investors), licensing, other strategic/financial buyers. Prefers all-cash; DKS should pay more as DKS "would retain the benefits of potential synergies in an all-cash transaction"; FL "was undervalued by the market ... because the market was not taking into account the Lace-Up Plan." Instructs $41.00 counter.
- Sep 16, 2024: Dillon conveys $41; Stack: DKS "may not be willing to pay $41.00." Sep 18: DKS stays $37 pending Lace-Up diligence.
- Sep 23, 2024 FL board: Dillon on "the recently announced CEO succession at an important Foot Locker brand partner" and impact on FL results. No further diligence "until DICK'S Sporting Goods put its best foot forward on price"; Evercore tells Goldman (9/25).
- Sep 27, 2024: Stack $40.00 "best and final," 60 days diligence from 10/7; response by 9/30.
- Sep 29, 2024 FL board: CEO succession and "performance issues at an important Foot Locker brand partner"; CFO Baughn: "weaker than expected operating performance in September, particularly in Foot Locker's international business." Evercore compares $37 vs $40. Decides not to contact other bidders "in light of the risk of a public disclosure of such outreach and the low likelihood that other strategic acquirers would have strategic interest in, or the financial capacity required to complete, an acquisition of Foot Locker." Authorizes diligence at $40.
- Oct–early Nov 2024: "Extensive due diligence" (DKS, advisors, consultants), two all-day NY mgmt mtgs, online data room, "including a focus on its execution of the Lace-Up Plan."
- Oct 31, 2024: WLRK initial draft merger agmt. Nov 1 FL board: Q3 likely low end of gdnc; "continued weakness at an important Foot Locker brand partner." Skadden flags: no antitrust commitment, no RTF, expense reimbursement on no-vote. Nov 6: Skadden draft (lower term fee, enhanced antitrust efforts, RTF, no expense reimbursement).
- Nov 15, 2024: DKS terminates. Stack: diligence "revealed that Foot Locker required a much more significant turnaround effort than it had anticipated"; DKS "had not been able to validate Foot Locker management's financial case that is core to successful execution of the Lace-Up Plan and was the basis for the $40.00 per share proposal." Nov 18/26: FL confidential info destroyed.
- Dec 4, 2024: FL Q3 2024; FY2024 sales & EPS gdnc lowered; stock $24.17→$22.02.
- Feb 18, 2025 FL board: Lace-Up progress; inflation, consumer sentiment, "lack of innovation in athletic apparel and footwear categories and lagging international consumer confidence."
- Mar 5, 2025: FL Q4 2024 + 2025 outlook: "ongoing pressure on margin recovery and another year of significant investment." "The outlook for 2025 was meaningfully lower than financial information shared with DICK'S Sporting Goods during its due diligence review in October and November of 2024."
- Mar 13-14, 2025: DKS letter $24.00: FL falling stock & analyst reports "reflected a lack of confidence in Foot Locker's ability to achieve the Lace-Up Plan on a stand-alone basis," consistent w/ Nov conclusions; DKS "had the expertise, brand partner relationships and credibility to restore Foot Locker to its rightful position"; $24 "reflected the significant work, effort and investment that would be required"; limited confirmatory diligence; response by 3/20; "time was of the essence."
- Mar 16, 2025: Stack: won't transact at pre-Nov price; "significant investment would be required by DICK'S Sporting Goods to meaningfully improve the state of the Foot Locker business."
- Mar 19, 2025 FL board: requests analysis of LT plan assumptions (consumer sentiment, "delays in the repositioning of an important Foot Locker brand partner," macro, structural risks), alternatives (divestitures, partnerships, op transformation, cap structure, cap mkts, minority investments, licensing), other acquirers. Mar 21: Dillon asks for time; Stack: absent engagement DKS "would consider making public its proposal."
- Mar 23, 2025 FL board: reviews margin, brand-partner sales, banner/geography growth assumptions; headwinds "appeared to be worse than Foot Locker management had expected" at 2/18. Instructs LT forecast of "the most likely base case in the current environment through fiscal year 2028" w/ lower margin expansion, lower brand-partner sales, slower intl growth.
- Mar 25, 2025 FL board: mgmt presents "management case" (EBIT margin, brand-partner sales, "a re-allocation of capital investment from Foot Locker's international businesses to Foot Locker's more profitable domestic banners"). "Wall Street analyst estimates for Foot Locker were lower than the management case." Deemed "a reasonable estimate"; Evercore to use.
- Apr 1, 2025 FL board: Evercore prelim valuation of $24 (trading comps, precedents, DCF, all-cash). Weighs delaying for tariff visibility vs DKS going public; value of cash/stock election. Authorizes $32 counter.
- Apr 2-3, 2025: tariffs; FL -15.4% ($14.72→$12.45); Dillon, Young, advisors delay conveying $32.
- Apr 4, 2025: Dillon: "not the right time." Stack: DKS "did not intend to lower its offer price of $24.00," open cash/stock/mix; DKS "had acquired Foot Locker shares through open market purchases, and ... currently owned approximately 4.5% of Foot Locker's common stock." Feedback by 4/8.
- Apr 7, 2025: Evercore/Goldman call on election; Goldman: "minimal diligence."
- Apr 8, 2025 FL board: Evercore reviews combo metrics, implied exchange ratios, "potential sources of synergies," stock-election value "under varying assumptions with respect to synergies and trading multiples." Uncapped election valued (participate in retail recovery & synergies; DKS has "a broader portfolio of product offerings, less brand partner concentration and a wider customer base"). Counter $27.00 uncapped, fixed ratio (conveyed 4/9).
- Apr 16, 2025: Stack $25.50 "best and final," stock capped 50%; maybe flexibility on structure, later little confidence DKS board would modify.
- Apr 17-18, 2025 FL board: accepts $25.50; proposes 80% cap, election shortly pre-close, better regulatory risk allocation, fee to FL if no regulatory approval.
- Apr 19-24, 2025: advisor calls on election mechanics, regulatory efforts, fees. Apr 21: Goldman reaffirms $25.50, 80% cap, election at shareholder mtg, RTF, expense reimbursement on no-vote. Apr 24: DKS revised — election "in the days immediately prior to closing."
- Apr 25, 2025: Dillon note: agreed $25.50, fixed ratio, 80% cap, election pre-close, RTF 4%, term fee 2.75%, 1% expense reimbursement on no-vote. Apr 26: WLRK draft. Apr 29: FL data room opened. Apr 30: Skadden draft; FL posts prelim Q1 2025 info.
- May 1, 2025: Stack: DKS mgmt "very concerned by Foot Locker's lower than anticipated financial performance in the first quarter which indicated that Foot Locker's business was declining more rapidly than DICK'S Sporting Goods had expected."
- May 2, 2025 (NY diligence): DKS asks FL to accelerate prelim Q1 to coincide w/ announcement.
- May 6, 2025: Stack lowers to $22.00 citing Q1 and "the achievability of second half 2025 targets"; no cap on stock election.
- May 7, 2025: Bracken meets CEO of "Company X" at industry event (ongoing talks re separate business txn); Company X "would not be interested in acquiring Foot Locker" but "would potentially be interested in a minority investment with board designation rights and exploring a global partnership"; never followed up w/ Dillon. Same day WLRK draft at $22/no cap; NDA made mutual for FL reverse diligence.
- May 8, 2025 FL board: Q1 + prelim FY2025: "negative consumer trends, the continued underperformance of an important Foot Locker brand partner, weak apparel sales and international results that were significantly below the management case." Considers "continued pressure through fiscal year 2026 on an important Foot Locker brand partner," tariffs, "the increasing likelihood that Foot Locker would significantly underperform the management case for the year." Company X "vague and uncertain," worth less than DKS deal.
- May 10, 2025 FL board: Evercore prelim valuation of $22 (mgmt case); reviews "additional scenario" and "for information purposes only" Evercore DCF on it. Company X "highly dilutive." Counter $24.00, no cap, term fee 2.5% (from 2.75%), no expense reimbursement; "move quickly."
- May 11, 2025: Stack accepts all; announce pre-open 5/15.
- May 12, 2025: FL reverse diligence call on DKS incl DKS anticipated Q1. May 11-14 final agmt negotiated.
- May 14, 2025 FL board: Human Capital Cttee approves retention plan for critical employees; Audit Cttee approves prelim Q1 PR. Evercore: 80.3% prem to 5/13 close, 98.6% to 1-mo VWAP, 6.4x TTM EBITDA; oral + written fairness opinion. Evercore analysis "did not consider the potential value to shareholders from electing to receive DICK'S Sporting Goods common stock ... or the potential value of the election itself." Skadden reviews regulatory covenant "which included commitments by DICK'S Sporting Goods to divest assets of Foot Locker generating revenues of up to $100 million." Unanimous; 2.5% fee "sufficiently low so as to not preclude any offers to acquire Foot Locker at a higher price."
- May 15, 2025: announced pre-mkt; both release prelim Q1.
- Other bidders: none contacted; only "Company X" (unnamed) — declined acquisition, floated minority investment/global partnership rejected as dilutive/vague. Board twice (9/29/24 and Reasons section) declined market check (leak risk, low strategic interest/capacity). DKS repeatedly said prepared to go public.
