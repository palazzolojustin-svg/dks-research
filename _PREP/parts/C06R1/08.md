### Our call
- FL results vs strong core DKS results highlight ongoing debate: bulls LT-oriented, focused on value creation via execution; bears on NT heavy lifting and questions on current lifestyle trends.
- Mgmt callback takes: (1) 4Q FL GM guide (1000)-(1500) bps is GAAP and includes obsolescence & write-downs; (2) intent to exit 4Q with clean FL inventories but work could extend into 1H26; (3) 4Q FL op inc guide "slightly negative" is pro forma, excludes charges; (4) building FL leadership via combo of industry experts & operational experts; (5) BTS perhaps more promotional y/y than expected, but holiday promo environment seen similar to last yr.
- Core DKS again solid: 7 straight qtrs SSS 4%+; ecomm again outpaced overall biz. Core comp +5.7% (vs St +3.7%); DKS-only EPS beat St by +$0.09 (+3.5%). Core GM looks like a miss at 36% vs St 36.4% (in line w/ mgmt expectations): (1) more promotional BTS, (2) product shift to hardgoods in golf, (3) some mix shift.
- FL "inconclusive": only stub period reported (9/08 close through Oct qtr close); BTS excluded. PF SSS decelerated to (4.7%) from (2%); International (10%), NA (2.6%). 4Q25 FL comps guided down (HSD) to (MSD)%. Reported PF FL GM ~23% vs 29.7% in 3Q24; WF believes guide implies 4Q core [FL] GM down (500) bps (vs GAAP -1000 to -1500 bps).
- Bull case read: discipline, merch execution & product access were key FL issues DKS cited — exactly what DKS brings. DKS argues FL comps hurt not only by lifestyle shift from basketball to running but also (perhaps more) by assortment prior FL mgmt bought, compounded by stale presentation. Bulls see NT fixes bridging to BTS '26 inflection.
- Bear case read: DKS announced $500-750M restructuring charges and 1000-1500 bps 4Q GM pressure to "clean the garage." Mgmt: no real inflection in PF sales trends before 2H26 (1H26 inventory — poor quality — already bought), excl. store/inventory rationalization. Most updates below expectations; highlight NT bear arguments. Fashion trends in lifestyle = key unsettled MT-LT question.
