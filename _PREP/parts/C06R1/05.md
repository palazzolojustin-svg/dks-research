### Tour
- 9/18/25 grand-opening tour of HoS Jersey City; DKS attendees: Lauren Hobart (President & CEO), Navdeep Gupta (EVP & CFO), Nate Gilch (Sr Dir IR), Elly Boyer (Sr Mgr IR), plus store/tech/ops leadership.
- Location: Newport Centre (Simon mall), Jersey City, 6.5 miles from Manhattan; one of 4 anchors and only non-department-store anchor (others Macy's, Kohl's, JC Penney). Draws from metro NYC and greater NY/NJ region.
- Store ~83,000 sq ft (vs avg 120,000 sq ft prototype); full-size-equivalent Golf Galaxy, premium House of Cleats, full-service premium footwear deck, in-store services, multi-sport hitting cage, rock climbing wall — "linger longer" experiential model.
- HoS = core DKS primary growth engine over next several years (with Next-Gen Field Houses); proof of share dominance. Leadership aimed to "build the type of sporting goods retailer that would put their legacy stores out of business." Barclays: DKS has created something not previously existing in market.
- Three standouts: (1) investment in premium service (level, enthusiasm, knowledge of teammates); (2) breadth of brands/products/price points — shopper has little to no need to shop elsewhere; (3) fun/excitement — shopping as event & experience.

### Service as competitive advantage
- Service levels meaningfully above existing sporting goods retail. Teammate training/education/growth central to HoS & Next-Gen Field House. While peers cut store payroll, DKS invests in (1) service & professional-skills teammates, (2) store payroll coverage, (3) skills & product training. DKS views as elevating experience & competitive advantage few can afford to replicate (sporting goods and broader retail). Others play defense; DKS invests capital & SG&A to play offense and take share.

### "Revolutionary – not evolutionary" stage
- Investor concern on larger boxes: more sales/EBIT $ but potentially weaker sales productivity & four-wall margins. Reasons for confidence: (1) no other sporting goods retailer has better purpose-built concept leading w/ product & service; (2) larger formats combine several "stores within the store," expanding audience; (3) investments drive new customer transactions via cross-shopping (new category/product discovery); (4) strong Teammate-to-Athlete engagement creates "linger longer" environment and in-person interactions.
