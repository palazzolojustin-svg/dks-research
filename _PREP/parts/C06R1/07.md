### PM take
- FL performance drives higher uncertainty, as expected; cleaning up inventory key first step, esp. since accretion mostly fundamentally driven.
- Core DKS comp +5.7% = "a rare acceleration in our coverage vs. 2Q"; but 2nd straight qtr of lackluster flowthrough (core DKS op profit down modestly again). FL "touching all the (negative) bases": comp miss, significant GM compression, massive clearance expected 4Q.
- 2026 deal accretion (vs updated 2025 core DKS EPS guide $14.25-14.55) predicated largely on FL fundamental improvement (vs JPM expectation of substantial buyback offsetting 10MM share dilution) — clean inventory, better merchandising; full assortment touched by BTS 2026 (2H weighted).
- Mgmt "resoundingly affirmed" confidence in FL turnaround; brands fully on board (NKE 59% of FL merch in 2024); Chairman/"founder" Ed Stack's relationships = key "what you need to believe in" factor. JPM still concerned footwear cycle slowing and global store portfolio effect raises bar for stock.

### Key takeaways
1. FL turnaround = better product + clean inventory; still accretive FY26 but no big ASR. FL closed Sep 8; 3Q includes 8 wks post-acquisition. Mgmt: FL strayed from "Retail 101"; didn't react fast enough when largest vendor NKE (59% of 2024 FL merch vs 71% in 2019) pivoted to DTC → too much stale inventory. FL comp -4.7% vs JPM -3%e. Still expects accretion to 2026 EPS vs $14.25-14.55 2025 base; built on better product & cleaner inventory (upgraded mgmt incl. hiring former CEO of Aldi to run international), modest lift from closing underperforming stores, $100-125MM synergies over time ("very" achievable). JPM had modeled 4.5MM repo in 4Q25 vs ~10MM dilution; mgmt says far more fundamentally driven, repo opportunistic. Tax rate modest headwind to 2026 accretion. LT goal: FL op margin back to DKS-pre-COVID-era 9-10% (vs 2.5% in 2024) [text: "DKS pre-COVID op margin of 9-10% as the goal (vs. 2.5% in 2024)"]. 2026 BTS expected turning point for FL; DKS full control of inventory buys in 3Q26.
2. Core DKS FY guide raised; 4Q bracketed; goal to exit 2025 with clean FL inventories via aggressive clearance. Core comp +3.5-4.0% (vs +2.0-3.5%); EPS $14.25-14.55 (vs $13.90-14.50). Implied 4Q comp slightly negative to +2%, EPS $3.72-4.02, brackets Street +1.7% / $3.90 (Consensus Metrix). FL GM down ~500 bps in 3Q (partial qtr; clearance & promo). 4Q: non-GAAP FL GM headwind 1,000-1,500 bps disposing inventory (markdowns, clearance channels, order cancellations); none relates to cancelled 2026 orders. FL comps down M-HSD in 4Q; ex these & one-time impacts from cleaning out unproductive assets, 4Q FL OI slightly negative. Consolidated: only 29% 4Q tax rate and 91MM shares for FY25.
3. Core comp +5.7% vs JPM Street-high +5.0%, Street +3.4%, buyside +5-5.5%; ticket +4.4% (modest tariff price increases), traffic +1.3%; growth across all key categories, esp. golf (equipment & apparel outperformed). Differentiated assortment → robust BTS, strong holiday positioning. Expanding into trading cards & collectibles (favorable response). FL comp -4.7% vs JPM -3% & buyside -3-4%; NA -2.6%, International -10.2%. Close Sep 8 excluded "profitable BTS season". JPM models core comps 4Q/FY25/FY26 +3.5%/+4.6%/+2.7%; FL -6.5%/-4.1%/-0.4%.
4. Core DKS GM +~30 bps y/y: merch margin +5 bps (net of mix, similar promos y/y, advertising, GameChanger), partly offset by tariffs & unfavorable business mix (higher licensing penetration). Consolidated GM -~260 bps y/y, entirely FL (-520 bps y/y) as markdowns began late 3Q. Core GM to expand FY & 4Q; 2H > 1H (alternate profit pools, similar promo backdrop y/y, 30-bp storm donation lap in 4Q; JPM models core 4Q GM +60 bps). Combined GM 2025/2026 34.0%/33.4%; core 36.3%/36.7%; FL 25.0%/26.2%.
5. Core SG&A deleveraged ~30 bps (digital, in-store, marketing); consolidated ~70 bps (FL deleverage). Core FY25 SG&A deleverage offsets GM expansion → op margin 11.1% (+10 bps y/y).
6. Core inventory +2% y/y (vs +7% 2Q, +12% 1Q), composition positioned to fuel momentum; FL inventory +5% y/y. GameChanger & DMN ongoing strength (new features/partnerships/content in GameChanger). 13 HoS opened in 3Q (most ever in a qtr) → 35 total; 6 Field House opened 3Q completing planned 15 for yr → 42 total.
