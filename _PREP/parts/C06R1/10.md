### Summary
- Core DICK'S beat on sales & EPS: comp +5.7% (cons +3.5%): ticket +4.4%, transactions +1.3%. Strong start to holiday continued into 4Q25, assuming slight q/q moderation; core comp guide raised for FY25.
- FL: comp -4.7%; 4Q25 gdnc: comp down mid- to high-single digits; GM down 1,000-1,500 bps. Barclays: outsized 4Q headwinds = "necessary pain-points" to quickly clear unproductive inventory via markdowns and jobber channel; aim to be clean for 2026. Likely deeper NT promotions, but positive LT view on better promo levels across total sporting goods landscape post-merger (FL more disciplined under DKS).
- Reiterate OW; combined entity benefits: (1) improved bargaining power w/ emerging & existing brand partners; (2) secular health/wellness trends & expanding athletics across ages/demographics; (3) experiential formats incl. HoS and Golf Galaxy → own the category.
- Stock after 11/25/25 BMO release: +0.2% (SPX +0.9%).
- Tariffs: DKS effectively absorbed tariff costs in merch margin; merch margin +5 bps despite tariffs & slightly unfavorable product mix; consistent w/ prior mgmt view that 2H25 tariff impact modest. Updated gdnc reflects all tariffs in effect; mgmt confident navigating macro. Prior tariff commentary & QuikCalc (Fig 8) =same as SRC 2025-09-05 Barclays (AUC +15.8%, pass-through price +6.9%, $952.5mn tariff, 686 bps; base cons as of 4/1/25).
- Quarter: core adj EPS $2.78 vs cons $2.71 (higher sales & GM). PF comparable sales (total co) +1.7%.
- Core FY25 gdnc: adj EPS $14.25-14.55 (prior $13.90-14.50); net sales $13.95-14.0bn (prior $13.75-13.95bn); comp +3.5% to +4.0% (prior +2.0% to +3.5%); OM 11.1% at midpoint / +10bps at high end (unch); GM expand (unch); SG&A deleverage offsetting (unch).
- FL 4Q25 gdnc: GM down 1,000-1,500 bps vs FL reported same period LY; PF 4Q comp -MSD to -HSD; OI ex one-time items slightly negative. Inventory-clearing actions + other merger/integration costs → future pre-tax charge $500mn-700mn (as printed; other brokers cite $500-750M).
- Estimates: FY25 adj EPS $14.45→$12.94 (non-GAAP FY25 includes FL stub 3Q25); FY26 $15.49→$14.41 (FL still in turnaround, majority of synergies unrealized); FY27 $16.41→$16.15 (some y/y margin expansion from FL synergies).
