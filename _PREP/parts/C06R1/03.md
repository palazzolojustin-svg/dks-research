### Key themes
1. Can DKS fix FL? May 15 announced FL acquisition at $24/sh (~$2.3B). 5 tenets: domestic→global operator; broader customer sets via multiple concepts; strengthen key supplier relationships; broader omni reach; synergies $100-125mm (mostly COGS; businesses run separately). WF: historical synergies closer to 2%, or $160mm on ~$8b FL revs → slight upside optionality. DKS at announcement: accretive within 12 months of close; medium-term cost synergies via procurement & direct sourcing (COGS). On 2Q25 call less direct on synergy timing & core FL performance: "there are lots of moving pieces", "more work to be done."
2. Executing at high level: GameChanger & Dicks Media Network digital interaction; new formats mostly remodels of existing locations — closer to hardcore athletes, differentiated experience, better ("exciting") real estate, access to better product from large global and small "boutique" suppliers. Core DSG share gains secure premium/unique product; SSS upside funds reinvestment in stores (training, technology). "it really does come down to the blocking and tackling". If sport participation rebound continues, DKS prime beneficiary.
3. Youth sports participation cuts both ways: youth participation up y/y boys & girls but mixed over medium/LT. NSGA: HS sport participation record high 2023-2024; SFIA: participation highest since 2015; other studies: boys from 50% range to 40% range 2013-2023; girls 56%→50% 2017-2023. Bull: further retracement to levels of several yrs ago; bear: participation fluctuating but biased down LT.
- Bull/bear tug of war: Bull — FL results/synergies "better than feared"; DKS bought FL at near-trough levels on potentially trough profitability; excellent, more consistent base execution; sport participation retracement. Bear — FL becomes "albatross" given myriad issues; margin outlook less visible after multiple yrs post-COVID growth; footwear innovation/newness pushed out; cumulative tariff macro headwinds pressure discretionary spend.
