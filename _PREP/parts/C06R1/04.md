### Summary
- 2Q25 beat on sales, GM, SG&A, EPS. Comp +5.0% (cons +3.1%): ticket +4.1%, transactions +0.9% → "reaffirming our view of DKS's dominant positioning". 6th straight qtr of healthy MSD comp; growth balanced across all key categories. Adj EPS $4.38 vs cons $4.30.
- FY25 EPS gdnc raised to $13.90-14.50 (from $13.80-14.40) on 2Q upside & better 2H visibility. Offsets: higher projected tax rate weighed on EPS guide by $0.19; GM language pulled from "~75bps of GM expansion" to just "expansion". DKS to invest in core from position of strength (digital, stores, marketing).
- Combined DKS-FL (close expected Sep 8) benefits: (1) strengthened relationships/bargaining power with key brand partners; (2) complementary real estate — DKS large-format, suburban-skewing vs FL smaller-format, urban-center; (3) cost synergies $100mn-125mn, EPS accretion expected FY26 (reiterated on 2Q call); (4) foothold in international markets.
- Stock after 8/28/25 print: -4.8% (SPX +0.3%).
- Quarter detail: sales $3.65bn (cons $3.61bn); SG&A rate 24.0% (cons 24.3%) — investments in digital/stores/marketing meaningfully increased, deleverage y/y; GM 37.1% (cons 37.0%) on slight merch margin expansion.
- FY25 gdnc vs cons: adj EPS $13.90-14.50 (cons $14.36); net sales $13.75-13.95bn (prior $13.6-13.9bn; cons $13.93bn); comp +2.0% to +3.5% (prior +1% to +3%; cons +2.9%); OM 11.1% at midpoint / +10bps at high end (unch; cons 11.0%); GM to expand (prior +75bps at midpoint; cons +60bps); SG&A deleverage offsetting GM expansion.

- Estimates: FY25 adj EPS $14.42→$14.45 (2Q beat); FY26 $15.49 and FY27 $16.41 unch — slightly reduced GM estimates (higher tariff rates; implied GM guide reduction) offset by faster sales growth.

### Tariffs
- Minimal tariff impact 2Q25; some 2H25 impact in gdnc. Brand partners already pushing price increases; no corresponding demand slowdown on those items. Vertical brands ("direct imports") 13% of FY24 rev; country mix not disclosed; negligible Mexico/Canada exposure; significantly diversified away from China. Remaining ~87% exposed indirectly via pass-through costs; mgmt: many suppliers already diversified; DKS well positioned to push back on cost increases given status as choice retailer (bargaining power w/ emerging & existing brands).
- QuikCalc model now uses global tariff rates effective 8/7/25 (prior: 30% China, 10% RoW ex Mexico/Canada). For DKS: total average unit cost +15.8%; pass-through price increase needed 6.9%.
