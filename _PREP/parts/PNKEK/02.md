### Item 1A Risk factors — FY25 differences
- FLS list: "risks related to an increased focus on sport and rebalancing of our channel mix" (FY26 added "product and" channel mix and "growth initiatives", "categories and geographies").
- Competition: FY25 cited "ongoing rapid changes in technology (including marketing and advertising technology) and artificial intelligence"; FY26 expanded to AI use by consumers/retailers/platforms and need to leverage AI-enabled tech.
- Climate/ESG: FY25 longer ESG section (stakeholder diverse expectations; GHG/carbon cost regs); HQ "World Headquarters" in seismic zone.
- Pandemic risk list (FY25) included disruptions to DCs/contract mfrs causing higher inventory or shortages; wholesale customer bankruptcies.
- Endorsers FY25: no mention of athlete personal brands/athlete-led ventures (added FY26).
- Demand forecasting FY25: lacked FY26 additions (regional demand variability, retailer shelf-space decisions, declining cultural relevance).
- NIKE Direct FY25: no AI-enabled/agentic shopping language (added FY26).
- IT FY25: "as we integrate AI into our operations, there may be increased cybersecurity and privacy risks" (shorter).
- Retailer consolidation FY25: market shares "may increase" (FY26: "have increased and may continue to increase") [DKS-relevant wording change].
- Real estate FY25: lacked FY26 sentence on store traffic declines, digital shift, local safety concerns, format/region profitability.
- Tax FY25: Pillar Two 15% effective FY2025; "Other countries are also actively considering changes"; no OECD Jan 2026 side-by-side mention.
- Swoosh, LLC ~78% of Class A as of 2025-06-30 (~16% of Class B if converted) (vs ~79% at 2026-06-30).
- Credit rating FY25: "in July 2025, our rating was downgraded by Standard & Poor's"; "Any negative change to our credit rating will increase borrowing costs for our existing facilities" (FY26: downgraded by both S&P and Moody's in 2025; "may increase").
- Securities class action & derivative suits re stock drop — present in both.
- Cyber FY25: VP Corporate Information Security, Risk & Compliance (VP, CIS) → CIO → CTO (FY26: VP Foundational Technology & Services → CTO). FY25 did not state past incidents (FY26 adds "we have experienced cybersecurity incidents in the past").
