### Takeaways
1. Hoka +19% Q3 vs cons +11%; DTC +19% (positive US and overseas) from +8% in Q2; first time in 4 qtrs DTC outgrew wholesale; wholesale +18%. Particularly strong European wholesale demand, averaging 90% sell-through rates. Q4 Hoka gdnc +13%-14%; could be conservative (easiest compare of yr).
2. Hoka distribution potential: only in ~half of targeted sporting goods doors in US; ~40% penetrated in Europe. Athletic-specialty: ~25% of doors US, <20% Europe. Run specialty fairly saturated in US; ~80% penetrated in Europe.
3. UGG +5% Q3 vs cons flat; similar DTC and wholesale growth; DTC notable after Q2 -10%. Q4 planned flat, could be conservative.
4. GM -50bps vs cons -290bps (lower tariff and markdown impacts). Q4 gdnc -200bps GM may be conservative (lean inventories, brand momentum). Q4 gdnc implies SG&A growth accelerates to ~15% (vs +4% Q3) — possible EPS upside source.
5. Buyback: nearly $350M in Q3 (all-time quarterly high); at least another $200M planned Q4 → >$1B for yr. >$2B cash, no debt, nearly $1B FCF.
