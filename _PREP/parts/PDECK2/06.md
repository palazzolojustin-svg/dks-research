### Call
- "Solid 3Q beat (incl the inflection of UGG DTC) driving stock up, but bears likely remain focused on a lack of US growth, core GM pressure, and growing competitive headwinds in the athletic space."
- Bulls satisfied: substantial beat, Hoka & UGG above estimates, better-than-expected tariff mitigation via strong pricing power. Bear concerns: 1) lack of US growth — +DD in FY25 → -LSD in 1H26 → muted 3Q; 2) no core (non-tariff) GM upside, promos still up y/y; 3) notable UGG wholesale slowdown despite 4Q→3Q shipment timing shift.
- Hoka rev +18% vs Street +11%; DTC accelerated to +19% from +8% in 2Q on similar compares; mgmt: return to healthy US growth w/ meaningful improvement in online customer acquisition; membership program lifting revenue per transaction, customer units, multi-category purchasing.
- UGG +5% vs Street ~flat; wholesale material decel to +4% from +17% in 2Q despite 4Q→3Q shipment shift and improved partner in-stocks; DTC returned to growth (+5%) on slightly tougher compares — growth in aggregate membership, email subscribers, retained consumers; interest in launches.
- Channel mix: mgmt targets more balanced mix; 1) LT target of improving DTC over several years (membership program changes); 2) balancing wholesale demand by fulfilling a little earlier and addressing late-season demand with DTC (implemented this yr, to be maintained).
- Outlook: 4Q rev plan below Street (UGG shipment timing; Hoka +13-14%, UGG ~flat). Implied 4Q GM 54.2% vs Street 54.0%; ~200bps 4Q tariff GM headwind; FY26 net tariff impact $25M (from $55-75M); core GM still expected to delever ~50bps.

### Thesis / debate
- EW: stock re-rated lower after 2H25 slowdown; Hoka needs meaningful re-acceleration to recapture above-avg multiple of 2022-2024; competitors investing more heavily in running (where Hoka concentrated) and copying Hoka's silhouette.
- Base $110: 16x FY27 — reasonable given slowing growth, declining margins; in-line view: relative top-line strength both brands + best-in-class profitability ("a rare ~20% margin brand") offset by slowing demand. Bull $125: Hoka sustains momentum w/ technical launches, fresh sartorial viewpoint for lifestyle; UGG +MSD or higher ("pull model" intact); GM gains sticky, mix sustains margins >20%. Bear $90: pricing/margin normalization as brands revert to normal growth; Hoka slows as competition ramps on technical running and aesthetics; margins retrench to 18%.
- Hoka history: acquired 10+ yrs ago at $3-5M sales; ~$200M+ pre-COVID → $2B+ FY25 (+50% CAGR over several yrs); Top 3 share in specialty run channel; expanded to casual joggers and lifestyle. Mgmt sees "billions" opportunity; but FY26 slowdown — US slowed on tough compares/competition, DTC no longer growth vehicle, more reliance on non-US.
- Growth decel: 3Q25 +24% (from +30-35%) → 1Q26 +20% → 2Q26 +11%; outlook +LDD for 2H26; culprit US DTC slowdown, crowded road/trail running; bears cite launch schedules focused on core runner vs casual customer (now much bigger part of mix). Intl solid but masked by US softness.
- Margin: Hoka margin 5% → 25%+ drove DECK margin 12% → 24% FY25, EPS ~$1.00 → $6.00+. FY26 guided margin pressure (GM & SG&A); not guiding EPS up y/y for first time since Hoka launched; tariffs ~$85M (mitigated FY26/2H26). Debate: has Hoka matured vs reversible via product and channel mix (leaning wholesale recently).
- Bull/bear tug of war — Bull: Hoka regains magic, growth back >20% on easier compares; 2H26/1H27 margin headwinds tariff-related/one-time, channel mix less of headwind; UGG healthy (innovated beyond classics, inventory mgmt); valuation better risk/reward. Bear: Hoka plateaued, competition pushes growth down; Hoka margins normalizing as brand matures and moves deeper into wholesale; UGG over-earning, hard to sustain profitability (channel mix).
- FY26E: EPS $6.80 (+7%); CC sales +7%; GM -~80bps to 57.0%; SG&A +10% $ to 34.5%; OM -~110bps to 22.5%. FY27E: EPS $6.98 (+3%); CC sales +4%; GM -~90bps to 56.2%; SG&A +6% to 35.1%; OM -~150bps to 21.0%.
- Upside risks: Hoka 20%+ growth; UGG faster; aggressive buybacks. Downside: slowing Hoka; margin reversion; choppy global macro. Catalysts: earnings; competitor and retail partner earnings.
