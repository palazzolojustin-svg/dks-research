### Call
- Raise 4Q/1Q/FY27 EPS above Street. "While we have been concerned on HOKA's trajectory since the start of 2025, today we give DECK credit as the brand appears to have stabilized product-flow/margin/DTC demand (driving NT confidence higher)."
- Worst negative catalysts likely behind: lapping difficult LY channel dynamics, rebalanced 2026 launch calendar, incrementally less negative narrative on resurgence of Nike Running. WF checks: HOKA growth remains strong post holiday; narrative shift possible if DTC stays robust with healthy brand margins (HOKA GMs +20bps in 3Q).
- FY26 HOKA headwinds: 1) optically difficult channel dynamics (slowing DTC + higher wholesale sell-in); 2) launch calendar missteps; 3) Nike's resurgence in US running. FY27: more balanced channel/geo (intl still higher); new launch cadence → cleaner transition, fewer inventory pressures. HOKA DTC +DD (and positive US) begun 3Q to sustain 4Q and FY27.
- "Is All Growth Valued Equal?": +MSD-HSD growth business w/ above-industry OM, growth mainly wholesale/intl expansion; not rewarded for "lower-quality growth (global wholesale fill-in)"; if DTC acceleration continues, upside to multiple from ~14x.
- Model changes: higher HOKA & UGG sales, higher GM, more SG&A leverage. 4Q HOKA DTC raised to +15% (from +10%) → HOKA revs +15% (above guide); 4Q EPS $0.86 (from $0.72) above Street; 1Q27/FY27 EPS $0.96/$7.45 (from $0.92/$6.98) above Street.
- "Remain EW, but See Fewer Headwinds": leaned cautious past 12+ months, now a bit more positive; DTC upside + fewer headwinds into FY27; expects fewer/smaller beats given industry-leading OM (22%+); durability of growth in both channels and in US critical.

- 2. NEW Expanding UGG TAM: less seasonal, male customer; sneakers, all-weather boots, more apparel; progress on "365 initiative"; Lowmel franchise expanding to lifestyle sneaker (doubling growth in 3Q26). UGG grew mid-teens FY24/FY25, HSD FY26; LT algorithm MSD.
- 3. Sustaining growth harder: 2026 headwinds = difficult channel comps (tougher DTC comps, more wholesale fill-in), resurgent Nike Run in US, 1H26-weighted launch calendar of new models against higher inventory of old models. Growth slowed to +11% in 2Q26, HOKA accelerated to +19% in 3Q26. Bears: launches focused on core runner vs casual customer. Intl solid, masked by US softness; stock not rewarded for lower-quality growth (more wholesale sell-in intl).

NEW Margins well above industry: OM near 23% FY26E; debate whether margins peaked and growth requires more investment amid competition ("marginal revenue has a higher marginal cost"); near-term benefits: tariff reversal, managing costs down and pricing up new models; excess margins may face continued LT pressure.
