### Thesis / tone
- "Back on Track; Reiterate OW." Beat on all fronts: +7.1% sales, sequential GM expansion despite tariffs, meaningful SG&A leverage. DECK recovered from earlier-year missteps (channel and inventory mgmt); disciplined marketplace mgmt. Both HOKA and UGG DTC growth outpaced wholesale; HOKA US DTC returned to growth (Barclays est positive MSD). Mgmt: strong holiday execution (innovation, elevated consumer experience, brand activations/marketing) → higher full-price selling, growth across all channels/regions. Product innovation key to topline/margin.
- Full 3Q beat not flowed to FY ("brand manufacturers tend to operate in 'half years'"): 4Q slower sales, UGG flat (orders shifted 4Q→3Q), GM -200bps on tariffs, SG&A deleverage on investment.
- Confidence drivers: 1) rising wholesale demand; 2) strong innovation pipeline across categories at UGG & HOKA; 3) domestic + intl build-out; 4) continued full-price selling; 5) multiple levers for global share gains.
- Why OW: 1) Hoka growth runway; 2) improving wholesale inventory backdrop supportive of replenishment, Hoka as traffic-driving full-price brand; 3) margin expansion from channel and geography mix; 4) highest ROIC in footwear, still expanding.
- Proprietary PRISM curve: DECK in "HUNT" phase (strategically offensive; mgmt taking share on own brand strategy; beating sales/margin expectations, earnings growing faster than sales; Region C = max stock appreciation; can last multiple years).
- Risks: slowing HOKA growth; premium valuation; SG&A investments offsetting flow-through; stock needs significant earnings upside vs multiple expansion.

### Tariffs
- 3Q GM better on lower-than-expected tariff impact (inventory flow timing, lower-tariff inventory in pipeline). 4Q GM = largest FY26 tariff hit: 200bps headwind entirely from tariffs as full 20% tariff burden flows through. FY26 unmitigated tariff headwind lowered to $110mn (from $150mn); net tariff impact to $25mn (from implied $55-75mn); reasons: better price-action benefits, favorable timing of inventory sold. Barclays expects tariff pressure in FY1Q27, FY2Q27, lesser FY3Q27.
- Prior (2Q) commentary: unmitigated ~$150mm with $75-95mm mitigation (down from ~$185mm unmitigated guided in 1Q); 2Q26 net headwind delayed by pulling forward inventory before higher rates (unique to 2Q). Mitigation: select, strategic, staggered price increases; partial cost-sharing with factory partners. Sourcing: <5% of footwear from China (some not routed to US); majority SE Asia, Vietnam ~80%.
