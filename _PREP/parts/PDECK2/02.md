### Top questions for mgmt (substance)
1. UGG US DTC traffic soft; 2Q26 DTC sales -10.4% y/y. Mgmt 2Q quote: "recent consumer trends [are] indicating a heightened preference for multi-brand shopping experiences". When does UGG DTC return to sustainable growth; total US UGG growth potential after 2 yrs very solid growth?
2. Underlying growth of UGG core classic (hero: Tasman franchise, Classic Boot, Classic Mini/Ultramini, Disquette, Scuffette slippers); innovation within Golden Collection and Lowmel (4-season brand); core-classic innovation for F/W 2026/7?
3. Impact of pulled-fwd EMEA wholesale shipments from third-party warehouse transition (cited on past 3 calls); EU wholesale inventory capacity into FY27?
4. Tariffs: $150M unmitigated FY26 headwind, partly offset by ~$75-95M mitigation; offset in FY27? incremental mitigation beyond $75-95M?
5. Hoka CY26 innovation in lifestyle, trail, road; max-cushion vs low-profile mix; mgmt 2Q26 cited Solimar, Transport (lower-profile tooling), Speedgoat 2 (lower profile) resonating; trend to lower profile implications for Bondi; third iconic style beyond Bondi and Clifton?
6. Higher marketplace promo in athletic footwear (large public peers promotional in US); will DECK promote more; full-price positioning; choppy Hoka & UGG US trends — confidence brands stay premium?
7. Apparel innovation at Hoka/UGG; apparel % of brand sales?
8. Hoka APAC underlying growth; APAC % of Hoka; timeline for APAC to reach ~10% of Hoka global sales; contribution to DECK LT target intl = 50% of total sales; Hoka Europe underlying growth/LT opportunity.
9. Capital allocation: continue $500-600M/yr buybacks? dividend? cash needed for WC?
10. New LT targets? None since Feb-2024. UGG healthy LT growth still +MSD%? Hoka healthy rate? Tariffs/promos change view of healthy EBIT margin +high-teens%? Analyst day?
11. AI initiatives (CX, supply chain, digital marketing, DTC traffic); financial impact/timing.
