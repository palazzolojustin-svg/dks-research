### Thesis
- "We see a very good opportunity to buy shares in a growth company we believe is significantly undervalued by the market." High-quality beat & raise; expects DECK to keep positively surprising NTM as Hoka and UGG grow better than expected → market realizes +HSD%/+LDD% 5-yr sales/EPS CAGRs → FY2 P/E to 20x from 15x.
- Differentiated view 1: Hoka low-to-mid-teens rev CAGR vs market M-HSD% (market cites competition caught up; +MSD% DTC growth 4Q25-2Q26). Hoka DTC growth popped to 19% in 3Q26; FY26 Hoka gdnc raised to mid-teens; drivers through FY27+: underpenetrated geographies, more doors in existing geographies, new categories.
- Differentiated view 2: UGG +M-HSD% vs market flat to +LSD% (perception reliant on few matured styles); new styles Lowmel sneaker, Minimel resonating; on track M-HSD% FY26 despite tariffs and tough compares.
- Thesis map: global footwear 3-4% CAGR skewed to sports; athleisure M-HSD% annual (Euromonitor: sports footwear +8%). Hoka DTC back to +DD%: FY26 US DTC weighed by Bondi 9/Clifton 10 transition choppiness + tariff-related US price increases; FY27 Hoka DTC +LDD% (US +MSD%, intl >+20% led by APAC) via training, lifestyle, apparel, retail footprint. EBIT margin to ~23% by FY30E (DTC/digital mix, SG&A leverage as Hoka scales, partly offset by tariffs). Hoka drives ~13% 5-yr consolidated top-line CAGR. UBS quant: growth stocks outperformed in Softlines past 25 yrs. UBS Evidence Lab Google search/social media support bull view. Reverse DCF: market prices +MSD% 5-yr EPS CAGR vs UBS ~9.5%.

### Valuation & scenarios (5-yr EPS algorithm base FY25)
- Multiples analysis (peers WWW, LULU, Moncler, Asics): DECK in line w/ peers on P/E, P/Sales, FCF yield; PT within range of all 3; 20x within DECK historical range; DECK trades below industry average.
- UPSIDE $233: ~16.5% EPS CAGR FY25-30E; UGG LDD% (China intl expansion, post European marketplace reset, all-season styles w/ younger consumers) + faster Hoka DTC rollout → +~430 bps annual sales growth vs base; OM ~25.5% by FY30E; ~23x P/E.
- BASE $161: ~9.5% EPS CAGR; topline strong FY26E then ~9%/yr; ~20 bps annual OM contraction stabilizing ~23% by FY30E; ~2.5% annual share count reduction; Hoka rebounds in US DTC, evolves to lifestyle; UGG ~6% CAGR; tariffs pressure GM NTM, GM expands beyond 1HFY27 (DTC mix, Hoka profitability); 20x FY28E $8.05.
- DOWNSIDE $49: -0.5% EPS CAGR; US consumer softens significantly CY26 → FY26E rev ~+5.5%, +MSD% thereafter; negative channel mix (wholesale/distributors); UGG loses share as comfort-fashion trend shifts, launches fail; Hoka share gains slow as trend shifts to lower-profile silhouettes (hurts Clifton, Bondi) + competition in road running; promos; SG&A deleverage; -110 bps OM/yr; ~9x P/E.
