## SRC 04_PEERS/FL/sec_filings/8-K/FL_8-K_2024-03-06_EX-99-1_EXHIBIT-99-1.md | 2024-03-06 | FL Ex 99.1 press release (contacts Kate Fitzsimons IR; Dana Yacyk Corp Comms) | Q4 FY2023 (14 wks ended 2024-02-03) & FY2023 (53 wks); FY2024 outlook
### Headlines
- Total sales +2.0%; comp -0.7%; Foot Locker + Kids Foot Locker North America comp +5.2%; EPS loss $4.13, non-GAAP EPS $0.38; inventory -8.2% y/y; anticipates return to positive comp and EBIT margin expansion in 2024; 2024 non-GAAP EPS $1.50-$1.70 incl non-recurring $0.10 charge; update on timing of Lace Up targets.
- "We are pleased to report fourth quarter results ahead of our expectations, including meaningfully accelerated sales trends relative to the third quarter, earnings per share that exceeded our guidance range, and improvements across multiple KPIs... we built significant momentum through the holiday season, driven by full-price selling in addition to compelling promotions. We also proactively reinvested in markdowns to end the year with leaner inventory levels compared to our expectations." —Mary Dillon, CEO. Also: evolving into "a modern, omnichannel retailer for 'all things sneakers'"; progress on brand partnerships, customer engagement, real estate, digital; successful NBA All-Star 2024 activation; 2024 leaning into investments in digital, store experience, loyalty, brand-building; Foot Locker brand 50th anniversary later in 2024.
### Q4 2023
- Sales incl 53rd week $2,380M vs $2,334M (+2.0%); ex-FX +1.5%. Comp -0.7%: 210 bps impact from Champs repositioning, consumer softness, changing vendor mix; FL + KFL NA combined comp +5.2%.
- GM -350 bps: primarily higher markdowns, partly offset by occupancy leverage. SG&A rate +10 bps: cost savings more than offset by inflation, front-line wages, technology.
- Net loss $389M vs NI $19M; non-GAAP NI $36M vs $92M. Diluted EPS $(4.13) vs $0.20; incl $0.12 contribution from 53rd week; non-GAAP EPS $0.38 vs $0.97.
- Non-GAAP excludes non-cash $478M minority-investment charge (impairment assessment) and $75M pension partial settlement charge (acceleration of previously deferred losses; transferred ~$109M of U.S. Qualified pension plan registered assets and liabilities to an insurer via group annuity contract).
- Cash $297M; total debt $447M. Inventory $1,509M, -8.2% y/y (ex-FX -7.8%).
- Stores: Q4 opened 29, remodeled/relocated 66, closed 113. 2,523 stores in 26 countries at 2024-02-03; 202 licensed stores in Middle East and Asia.
### FY2024 outlook & Lace Up timing (CFO Mike Baughn)
- "We maintain conviction in the longer-term earnings potential that our Lace Up plan will generate and reiterate the 8.5-9% EBIT margin target communicated at our March 2023 Investor Day. Given our lower starting point exiting 2023, we expect a two-year delay in achieving that goal and now see reaching that target by 2028." —Mike Baughn, EVP & CFO.
- "2024 will serve as a cash rebuilding year, and we, therefore, are not resuming a dividend at this time... including a return to quarterly dividends and share repurchases over time." —Baughn.
- FY2024 = 52 wks ending 2025-02-01. EPS gdnc includes ~$0.10 non-recurring Q2'24 charge from rollout of enhanced FLX loyalty program to rest of North America (loyalty points converted into additional customer benefits).
