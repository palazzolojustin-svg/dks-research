## SRC 04_PEERS/FL/sec_filings/8-K/FL_8-K_2024-05-30_EX-99-1_EXHIBIT-99-1.md | 2024-05-30 | FL Ex 99.1 press release (contacts Kate Fitzsimons IR; Dana Yacyk) | Q1 FY2024 (13 wks ended 2024-05-04)
### Headlines
- Total sales -2.8%; comp -1.8%; global Foot Locker + Kids Foot Locker comp +1.1%; EPS $0.09, non-GAAP EPS $0.22; inventory -5.6% y/y; reaffirms first-half and full-year 2024 non-GAAP EPS outlook.
- "We had a solid start to the year, which demonstrates that our Lace Up Plan is working. We delivered comparable sales results and gross margin in line with our expectations, while earnings per share outperformed due to disciplined expense management and some favorable shifts in expense timing. Importantly, we are well-positioned with fresh assortments as we approach the summer and Back-to-School seasons, and we are pleased to be reaffirming our full-year outlook." —Mary Dillon, CEO.
- Dillon also: strengthening brand partnerships, digital/loyalty investment, basketball & sneaker culture; store refreshes; new retail concept opened in Wayne, New Jersey, with four more locations opening this year; reimagined FLX Rewards program launching in U.S. in Q2 to "further strengthen our demand flywheel."
### Q1 2024
- Sales $1,874M vs $1,927M (-2.8%); ex-FX -2.4%. Comp -1.8% incl 220 bps impact from continued Champs repositioning; global FL + KFL comp +1.1%.
- GM -120 bps: markdowns sequentially moderating vs Q4'23, partially offset by occupancy leverage. SG&A rate +220 bps: technology and brand-building investment, inflation; partially offset by cost savings, expense discipline, and shift of expense timing from Q1 into Q2.
- NI $8M vs $36M; non-GAAP NI $21M vs $66M. EPS $0.09 vs $0.38; non-GAAP $0.22 vs $0.70.
- Cash $282M; total debt $446M. Inventory $1.7B, -5.6% y/y (ex-FX -4.9%).
- Stores: opened 4, closed 37; remodeled/relocated 16; updated 13 to current design standards. 2,490 stores in 26 countries at 2024-05-04; 206 franchised (Middle East, Asia). No dividends paid in Q1'24 (vs $38M Q1'23).
