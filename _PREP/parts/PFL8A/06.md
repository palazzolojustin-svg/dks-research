## SRC 04_PEERS/FL/sec_filings/8-K/FL_8-K_2023-03-20_EX-99-1_PRESS-RELEASE-DATED-MARCH-20-2023.md | 2023-03-20 | FL Ex 99.1 press release (contact Robert Higginbotham, Interim CFO / SVP IR & FP&A) | Q4 & FY2022 (FYE 2023-01-28)
### Headlines
- Q4 total sales -0.3%; comp +4.2%; Q4 EPS $0.20, non-GAAP EPS $0.97; launching "Lace Up" strategy w/ updated financial targets; "Reset year" 2023 non-GAAP EPS $3.35-$3.65; beyond 2023 strategies to drive low- to mid-twenties adj EPS growth. Investor Day 8:30am ET same day.
- "Our team delivered a great finish to the year with strong fourth quarter results that capitalized on resilient Holiday demand and a compelling assortment and inventory position from our brand partners" —Mary Dillon, President & CEO. "We are entering 2023 with a focus on resetting the business – simplifying our operations and investing in our core banners and capabilities to position the Company for growth in 2024 and beyond." 2024 = 50th anniversary of Foot Locker brand; "Lace Up" plan "designed to set us up for success for the next 50 years."
### Q4 2022
- Comp +4.2% driven by increased traffic and improved access to high-quality inventory; broad-based strength across brands and regions.
- Sales $2,334M vs $2,341M Q4'21 (-0.3%); ex-FX +3.6%.
- GM -290 bps y/y, mainly higher markdowns on increased promotional activity across industry. SG&A rate -10 bps y/y (cost optimization savings offset by inflation).
- NI $19M vs $103M; non-GAAP NI $92M vs $148M. EPS $0.20 vs $1.02; non-GAAP EPS $0.97 vs $1.46.
### Balance sheet / capital return
- Cash $536M; debt $452M; net cash $84M vs $347M LY. Inventory $1.6B, +29.8% y/y (as of 2023-01-28).
- Q4 dividend $0.40/sh; FY2022 buyback 4.1M shares for $129M; dividends paid $150M. Declared $0.40/sh payable 2023-04-28, record 2023-04-14.
### Stores
- Q4: opened 21, remodeled/relocated 45, closed 101. At 2023-01-28: 2,714 stores in 29 countries (N. America, Europe, Asia, Australia, NZ) + 159 franchised stores in Middle East and Asia.
### Asia business model change (announced 2023-03-20)
- Close stores and ecommerce in Hong Kong and Macau; convert owned stores/ecommerce in Singapore and Malaysia to license model; continue operating stores in South Korea; pursue regional growth via license partners. MAP Active (Indonesia's leading lifestyle retailer; already FL partner in Indonesia and Philippines) takes over Singapore and Malaysia store/ecommerce ops, to grow there and new regional markets.

### Investor Day "Lace Up" plan
- Strategic imperatives: Expand Sneaker Culture (more sneaker occasions, more choice, greater distinction); Power Up the Portfolio (more banner distinction incl re-launching Foot Locker brand; transform real estate: new formats, shift off-mall, close underperforming stores); Deepen Our Relationship with Customers (reset loyalty program; enhanced analytics); Be Best-in-Class Omni (online experience across full shopping journey).
- LT targets FY2024–FY2026 (annual growth from 2023 on 52-wk basis): total sales growth 5% to 6%; comp 3% to 4%; sq ft growth approximately 5%; adj EBIT margin 8.5% to 9% by 2026; adj EPS growth low- to mid-twenties.

### Non-GAAP policy change
- From Q1'22 FL excludes all gains/losses on minority investments from non-GAAP (previously only certain amounts). Amounts not excluded in 2021: Q2'21 $17M ($12M AT, $0.12/sh); Q3'21 $27M ($20M AT, $0.19/sh); Q4'21 $27M ($20M, $0.21/sh); FY21 income $71M ($52M AT, $0.50/sh), primarily Retailors, Ltd. investment; pre-2021 not significant. Non-GAAP excludes (i) minority investments, (ii) impairments/other charges, (iii) nonrecurring/unusual tax matters. Constant currency = both years at prior-year avg FX rates. Non-GAAP consistent with exec comp determination; tax effect at marginal rate per item.
