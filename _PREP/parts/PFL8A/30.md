## SRC 04_PEERS/FL/sec_filings/8-K/FL_8-K_2024-08-28_EX-99-1_EXHIBIT-99-1.md | 2024-08-28 | FL Ex 99.1 press release (contacts Kate Fitzsimons IR; Dana Yacyk) | Q2 FY2024 (13 wks ended 2024-08-03) & YTD
### Headlines
- Returned to topline growth: total sales +1.9%, comp +2.6%; GM +50 bps y/y; EPS loss $0.13, non-GAAP loss $0.05; inventory -10.0% y/y; reaffirms FY2024 non-GAAP EPS outlook; further streamlining international ops and optimizing corporate office footprint.
- "The Lace Up Plan is working, as evidenced by our return to positive total and comparable sales growth as well as gross margin expansion in the second quarter. Our top line trends strengthened as we moved through the quarter, including a solid start to Back-to-School. We were also particularly pleased to deliver stabilization in our Champs Sports banner. As planned, we relaunched our enhanced FLX Rewards Program in the United States during the quarter and have been encouraged by initial results." —Mary Dillon, CEO.
- Dillon: leveraging brand partnerships, store refreshes and new concept doors, digital and loyalty; simplifying to focus on core banners/markets; streamlining Asia and Europe while expanding licensing; relocating HQ to St. Petersburg, Florida in 2025 for collaboration and expense discipline; "position the Company for its next 50 years of profitable growth."
### Q2 2024
- Sales $1,896M vs $1,861M (+1.9%); ex-FX +2.5%. Non-recurring FLX Rewards rollout charge reduced sales by $11M.
- Comp +2.6%, led by global Foot Locker + Kids Foot Locker comp +5.2%.
- GM +50 bps incl 40 bps drag from FLX charge; ex-FLX GM +90 bps, led by reduced markdowns and occupancy leverage. SG&A rate +130 bps: technology investments, brand-building, inflation; partly offset by cost savings and expense discipline.
- Net loss $12M vs $5M; non-GAAP net loss $4M vs NI $4M. EPS $(0.13) vs $(0.05); non-GAAP $(0.05) vs $0.04; both incl $0.09/sh negative impact from FLX charge.
- Cash $291M; total debt $445M. Inventory $1.6B, -10.0% y/y (ex-FX -9.2%).
- Stores: opened 5, closed 31; remodeled/relocated 14; refreshed 67 to current design standards. 2,464 stores in 26 countries at 2024-08-03; 213 licensed (Middle East, Asia).
### International updates
- Closing stores and ecommerce in South Korea; closing stores and ecommerce in Denmark, Norway, Sweden; agreements with Fourlis Holdings S.A. (Fourlis Group; SE Europe retail/licensing operator) to transfer store and ecommerce ops in Greece and store ops in Romania. Combined: closure/transfer of ~30 stores of FL's 140 APAC and 629 Europe stores; complete by mid-2025. Fourlis also licensed for future SE Europe expansion; FL + Fourlis see opportunity for >100 stores in region over next several years.
### HQ relocation / tech hub
- Global HQ to move to St. Petersburg, Florida in late 2025; only limited NYC presence going forward; intent: build on St. Petersburg presence, cross-banner collaboration, reduce costs.
- Global Technology Services (GTS) Hub opening in Dallas, Texas in September (2024), led by CTO Adrian Butler.
