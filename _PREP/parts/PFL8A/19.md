## SRC 04_PEERS/FL/sec_filings/8-K/FL_8-K_2023-11-29_EX-99-1_EXHIBIT-99-1.md | 2023-11-29 | FL Ex 99.1 press release (contacts Kate Fitzsimons IR; Olivia Mata) | Q3 FY2023 (13 wks ended 2023-10-28) & YTD
### Headlines
- Total sales -8.6%; comp -8.0%; EPS $0.30 and non-GAAP EPS $0.30; narrowing 2023 guidance; investing in basketball (NBA partnership, Home Court); entering India with license partners.
- "We delivered third quarter results that were ahead of our expectations as strong execution and early progress against our Lace Up plan improved conversion trends across channels. Looking forward, we are updating our outlook to reflect the momentum we have in our strategic initiatives into the fourth quarter, which includes strong results over the Thanksgiving week period, against the backdrop of ongoing consumer uncertainty. As such, we are narrowing our 2023 outlook and still expect to end the year with inventory levels flat to down slightly, as compared with the prior year." —Mary Dillon, CEO. Also: NBA partnership + Home Court to "cement Foot Locker's leadership at the center of basketball and sneaker culture"; "rounding out our reset year and building on our progress in 2024 and beyond."
### Q3 2023
- Sales $1,986M vs $2,173M (-8.6%); ex-FX -10.0%. Comp -8.0%: consumer softness, changing vendor mix, 3% negative impact from Champs Sports repositioning.
- GM -470 bps: higher markdowns, occupancy deleverage, higher shrink. SG&A rate +100 bps: cost savings more than offset by deleverage, inflation, front-line wages and technology investment.
- NI $28M vs $96M; non-GAAP NI $28M vs $121M. EPS $0.30 vs $1.01; non-GAAP $0.30 vs $1.27.
- Cash $187M; debt $449M. Inventory $1.9B (+10.5% y/y) incl ~6.0% impact from strategic pull-forward of inventory into Q3 for holiday; ex-FX +9.9%.
- Q3 dividend $0.40/sh = $38M; dividends paused thereafter (as previously announced). No Q3 buybacks.
### Basketball / India
- 2023-11-16: multi-year NBA partnership, FL official league marketing partner in US (relationship dating to 1999); NBA All-Star activation; on-court virtual signage in national broadcasts; NBA social; extended to FLX loyalty. Early Nov global platform "The Heart of Sneakers" with Kevin Durant (Nike), Anthony Edwards (adidas), LaMelo Ball (Puma), Steph Curry (Under Armour). Home Court multi-branded basketball concept rolling out in select US stores.
- India entry 2024 via long-term licensing: Metro Brands Limited (MBL; one of India's largest footwear/accessories specialty retailers) exclusive rights to own/operate FL stores in India; Nykaa Fashion (>17.6M monthly visitors) exclusive ecommerce partner (FL India website + FL-branded shop on Nykaa).
### Stores
- Q3: opened 22, remodeled/relocated 36, closed 14. 2,607 stores in 26 countries at 2023-10-28; 190 franchised (Middle East, Asia).
