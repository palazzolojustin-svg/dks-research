### 1. Business (Item 1, lines 285-626)
- Overview verbatim (l.289): "DICK'S Sporting Goods, Inc. ... is a leading global sports retailer offering an extensive assortment of authentic, high-quality sports equipment, apparel, footwear and accessories. Our banners include DICK'S Sporting Goods, Golf Galaxy, Public Lands and Going Going Gone! stores in addition to the experiential retail concepts DICK'S House of Sport and Golf Galaxy Performance Center which are all located across the United States. Additionally, as owner and operator of Foot Locker, which includes Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs Sports, WSS and atmos banners, we serve the global sneaker community across North America, Europe, Asia and Australia, plus a licensed store presence in Europe, the Middle East and Asia. We also own and operate GameChanger, a youth sports mobile platform for live streaming, scheduling, communications and scorekeeping."
- Founded 1948 Binghamton NY by Richard "Dick" Stack; Edward W. Stack Pres/CEO 1984; renamed DICK'S Sporting Goods, Inc. Apr 1999 (l.291).
- 2 reportable segments from FY2025: DICK'S (DSG, Golf Galaxy, Going Going Gone!, Public Lands, GameChanger) and Foot Locker (FL, Kids FL, Champs, WSS, atmos) (l.293). Employees="teammates"; customers="athletes" (DKS)/"sneakerheads" (FL); FL store employees="stripers"/"blue shirts" (Champs). HQ 345 Court Street, Coraopolis, PA 15108.
- vs FY2024 10-K: FY2024 = single-segment US "omni-channel sporting goods retailer"; FY2025 adds "global", FL banners, 2 segments.

#### 1.2 Store concepts (l.325-329, 413-427)
- DSG store: "unites several sports specialty stores under one roof and typically contains the following specialty shops: Team Sports, Athletic Apparel, Outdoor, Golf, Fitness and Footwear" ("store-within-a-store") (l.417).
- House of Sport (HoS): "provide highly experiential destinations for our athletes. These innovative formats are built around experience, service, community and product" (l.327); HitTrax batting cages, Trackman golf sims, premium full-service footwear decks, House of Cleats.
- Field House: "we are incorporating House of Sport learnings into our most typical 50,000 square foot DICK'S store, which we are calling our DICK'S Field House concept" (l.327).
- Golf Galaxy Performance Center: redesigned GG launched 2021, "equipped with Trackman golf technology and include an elevated staffing and service model".
- Going Going Gone! (GGG): clearance/value; "improve our clearance optimization through the consolidation of clearance inventory for omni-channel selling opportunities to better serve our value athletes" (l.417).
- Public Lands: outdoor; 3 stores at FYE; 1 closure planned for conversion to Field House early FY2026 (l.1392).
- FL Business: "a portfolio of specialty athletic footwear and apparel banners, including Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs Sports, WSS and atmos" + licensing w/ 3rd-party operators Middle East/Europe/Asia → royalty income (l.425).
- Fast Break (new FY2025): 11-store FL pilot "which includes enhanced in-store presentation and a more focused assortment"; +10 stores (Los Angeles) early 2026; goal "to have approximately 250 locations ahead of the back-to-school season" 2026 (l.399, 1247, 1605).

#### 1.7 ScoreCard / DMN (l.387-389)

Approximately 8 million of the athletes in our loyalty program are part of our ScoreCard Gold tier ... These ScoreCard Gold members account for over 50% of total sales for the DICK'S Business."
- DMN: retail media network monetizing 1P data (in-store/online displays, video, social, email, search, GC app).
