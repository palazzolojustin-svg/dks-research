### 2. Deal announcement 2025-05-15
#### 2.1 Investor presentation (Ex 99.3) — no slide text in EDGAR (≥12 pages); reconstructed from call slide references
- Disclaimer slide (Gilch). Slide 4: Stack "$140 billion" US TAM, >3,200 stores, "$300 billion global sports retail market". Slide 5: FL overview "a global footwear and athletic apparel retailer"; banners; ~2,400 stores across 26 countries end-2024; licensed ME/Europe/Asia; >400 refreshes; digital 18%; 2024 rev $8B (joint PR says "20 countries", call says "26 countries"). Slide 6: five benefits. Slide 7: US real estate "highly complementary"; intl expansion first time (Europe, ME, APAC); "$300 billion global total addressable market for sports retail." Slide 8: sneakerheads/lifestyle (FL) + performance (DKS). Slide 9: brand partner logos. Slide 10: DKS profitable growth history; FL digital modernization & refreshes; HoS and FL "reimagined concept store". Slide 11: efficiencies "primarily through procurement and direct sourcing." Terms slide (Gupta): $24/sh; cash or 0.1168; no min/max; equity ~$2.4B; EV $2.5B; $100-125M cost synergies medium term; EPS-accretive first full FY post-close ex one-time; committed to IG ratings; unanimous boards; close 2H25.
- Slide cited by Yih: "the 25% that you have of merchandise purchases and the 59% of Foot Locker's merchandise purchases" (single-vendor concentration DKS ~25%, FL 59%; vendor not named in transcript). Other slide numbers not in document.
#### 2.2 Joint PR (Ex 99.2) exact
- Headline "DICK'S Sporting Goods to Acquire Foot Locker to Create a Global Leader in the Sports Retail Industry". "This transaction implies an equity value of approximately $2.4 billion and an enterprise value of approximately $2.5 billion."
- FL: "approximately 2,400 retail stores across 20 countries in North America, Europe, Asia, Australia and New Zealand, and a licensed store presence in Europe, the Middle East and Asia. In 2024, Foot Locker achieved net worldwide sales of $8 billion. DICK'S expects to operate Foot Locker as a standalone business unit within its portfolio and maintain the Foot Locker brands."
- Consideration "(i) $24.00 in cash or (ii) 0.1168 shares ... not subject to a minimum or maximum".
- "Based on the closing price of Foot Locker common stock on 5/14/2025, the $24.00 per-share consideration represents a premium of approximately 66% to Foot Locker's 60-trading day volume weighted average price. The total consideration represents an acquisition multiple of approximately 6.1x fiscal 2024 adjusted EBITDA."
- "DICK'S intends to finance the acquisition through a combination of cash-on-hand and new debt."
- "accretive to EPS in the first full fiscal year post-close (excludes transaction and other one-time costs to achieve synergies) and to deliver between $100 to $125 million in cost synergies in the medium-term achieved through procurement and direct sourcing efficiencies." Close "expected to close in the second half of 2025", subject to FL vote + regulatory.
- Advisors: Goldman Sachs (DKS FA; "provided fully committed bridge financing"); Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz (DKS legal); Evercore (FL FA); Skadden, Arps, Slate, Meagher & Flom (FL legal).
- 5 rationale headings verbatim: "Create a global platform within the growing sports retail industry."; "Serve a broader set of consumers across differentiated concepts."; "Strengthen relationships with brand partners through global reach."; "Invest in future growth through an industry-leading omnichannel experience."; "Unlock operational efficiencies that create shareholder value."
- Quotes: Stack ("We have long admired the cultural significance and brand equity that Foot Locker and its dedicated Stripers have built…"); Hobart ("…we'll create a new global platform…"); Mary Dillon, FL CEO ("…offers the choice of significant and immediate cash value or the opportunity to invest in the combined company…"). DKS boilerplate: "more than 850 DICK'S Sporting Goods, Golf Galaxy, Public Lands and Going Going Gone! stores".
#### 2.3 Prelim Q1 FY2025 PR (Ex 99.1): qtr ended 2025-05-03 comps +4.5%; diluted EPS $3.24; non-GAAP $3.37 (adds back $0.13 "Investment losses … non-cash losses from non-operating investment in equity securities"). Q1 call 2025-05-28 8:00 ET.
#### 2.4 Merger Agreement (Item 1.01)
- Parties DKS; RJS Sub LLC (NY LLC, wholly owned); Foot Locker, Inc. (NY corp). Merger Sub merges into FL; FL survives as wholly owned sub.
- Election period "not less than twenty business days ending on the date that is five business days prior to DICK'S good faith estimate of the Closing date."
- Equity awards: employee RSUs/PSUs → DKS time-based RSUs on stock consideration (perf goals deemed achieved per award agmt); non-employee director RSUs/deferred units vest, cash out at cash consideration; options cashed out for spread over $24.00 (OTM cancelled for no consideration).
- Conditions: 2/3 of outstanding FL common; HSR + "the antitrust laws of certain other jurisdictions"; no Restraint; S-4 effective; reps/covenants; no FL MAE.
- Financing: commitment letter 2025-05-15 w/ Goldman Sachs Bank USA "up to $2.4 billion of senior bridge term loans" for cash consideration, repay certain FL debt, fees.
- Outside date 2026-05-15 "subject to two automatic extensions of three months each if on such date all of the closing conditions except those relating to regulatory approvals have been satisfied or waived"; 30-day cure.
- Fees: FL pays $59,500,000 on change of recommendation/Superior Proposal; DKS pays FL $95,500,000 "if the Merger Agreement is terminated in connection with a failure to obtain the requisite antitrust approvals." Ex 2.1 schedules omitted.

#### 2.6 Q1 FY25 call excerpts 2025-05-28 (FL-related)
- Stack: DKS "positions us to participate in a $300 billion global sports retail market, and expands our reach to over 3,200 stores worldwide"; "We understand that there's really a group of people out there, shareholders that would really have preferred we just continue to do what we're doing … We don't think that's right long term"; "we don't make investments or decisions for a quarter or two. We make these decisions and investments for a lifetime."

- Buybacks "Nearly 300 million in the first quarter" (analyst); Gupta "there are certain restrictions as we are in the phase of the S4 filing." Gilch: Q2 FY25 results tentatively 2025-09-03. Integration: "we are going to put a small group of people working for Ed to work with the Foot Locker team … The gross margin improvement that we know is available".
- May 28 PR financing: "through a combination of cash-on-hand, revolving borrowings and other new debt, to the degree Foot Locker shareholders do not elect to receive their consideration entirely in shares of the Company's common stock."
