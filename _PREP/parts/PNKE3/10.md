### Recent event reaction (10/01/26): "Nike's Weaker Results, Softer Outlook Risk to Turnaround"
- NA continues to improve, but weakness elsewhere + much softer FY27 outlook "suggest the turnaround may have stalled." FY27 sales expected down high-single-digit % after 1Q rev missed expectations, hurt by mid-20% c-n decline in Greater China. Pace (new program building on existing cost-savings plan) to generate $2.5b savings through 2031.
### Thesis (10/01/26)
- Turnaround will take much longer: performance gains offset by resets in sportswear, Jordan, Greater China → sales pressured into FY28. Running, global football, basketball showing momentum; restoring scarcity in Jordan, tightening sportswear supply, cleaning China digital marketplace take time. Pace should improve speed/lower costs; sharper assortments, stronger wholesale execution support healthier, more profitable marketplace. New five-year outlook (investor day) to add color.
### Key topics summary
- Near-term choppy: CY2026 sales may decline LSD-MSD (China weakness, sportswear, inventory cleanup offset running, wholesale, new product). FY27 margin recovery more achievable than sales rebound (less discounting, supply-chain savings, full-price mix offset tariffs). China reset critical.
- Rebalancing wholesale: more balanced go-to-market, reach consumers where they shop; digital still focus, e-com could recover as excess clears; "Strengthening partnerships with more differentiated wholesale retailers is an added catalyst."
- Women's: largest women's athletic brand; dominates sports bras; new leggings/footwear. Endorsements: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander moved to Nike Basketball from Converse; World Cup storytelling, national-team kits, more football retail locations.
