### Thesis / framing
- 1Q27 less about headline rev/EPS, more about evidence "Sport Offense" strategy translating into cleaner marketplace inventories, improved wholesale productivity, China stabilization. Mgmt guided 1Q27 rev down LSD-MSD w/ GM inflecting positive for first time in turnaround. NKE following classic "margins before sales" recovery; path back to sales growth "will not be linear"; expect OM drivers below top line and muted top-line recovery.
- 5 key debates: 1) meaningful China improvement, 2) durable running momentum, 3) Sportswear progress, 4) continued wholesale recovery, 5) credible path to margin expansion. Positive signals → confidence in FY27-28 earnings recovery; if not, "stuck" narrative persists while competitors gain share.
- Lifestyle athletic footwear slowdown = product-cycle problem (lack of innovation/urgency), not demand/macro. DKS-READ: "DKS described a lifestyle industry lack of newness impacting Foot Locker, driven by slowing legacy/retro lifestyle product, weak launches, elevated inventories, and an increasingly promotional marketplace." "Importantly, performance running remains healthy at core Dick's Sporting Goods, which tells us this is not a demand problem but a newness problem and that the footwear market, much like apparel, is becoming increasingly 'zero sum.'" DKS mgmt explicitly cited slowing legacy lifestyle silhouettes, fewer launches, retro fatigue, MAP breaks from inventory buildup. At NKE Sportswear remains weak point while performance running inflects positive. OM may be near bottom but investors unlikely to underwrite turnaround until successful new lifestyle franchise launches.
- Greater China largest rev downside: FY4Q26 rev -12% reported (-17% CC), EBIT -20%; no clear near-term inflection; reset deeper and longer; PRISM "TRAP" phase. Mgmt gdnc: subdued China; overall sales down LSD-MSD from FY4Q26 to FY1H27; continued Sportswear pressure; uneven global demand.
- China reset: from Jan-2027 NKE moves China digital sales to official flagship destinations (Tmall, JD.com, Douyin, plus Nike.com.cn and Nike App), away from partner-operated stores. Topsports and Pou Sheng confirmed complete termination of their China online NKE sales → near-term top-line pressure (lost wholesale); change begins CY27, so no direct FY1H27 impact. LT: less fragmentation, inventory/promo control back to NKE, elevated brand image.
- Valuation now reflects turnaround/restructuring story vs historical premium growth. Key debate: margins worse before better vs return to growth w/ double-digit OM. Negative sentiment on 1) structural China pressure, 2) competitive share loss in running and lifestyle. "we are at the apex of uncertainty with little investor appetite to underwrite NKE's resurgence." If recovers, very attractive multi-yr risk/reward.
- Recovery tenets: 1) NA early wholesale stabilization drives DTC recovery; 2) China stabilization sufficient for stock to work; 3) sport-led innovation beyond running halos brand equity; 4) margins at/near trough, margin improvement before sales recovery.
- Estimates: more GM visibility, less sales confidence; raised GM, lowered sales. Adj EPS FY27/28/29 $1.57/$1.94/$2.37 (from $1.55/$1.90/$2.24) vs BBG cons $1.68/$2.12/$2.48. Multiple cut on slower sportswear innovation progress + macro; 22x still premium to current NTM P/E 19.9x (near-trough GM).
- Why OW: 1) regional/channel reset actions materializing, 2) innovation potential for sustainable growth, 3) cost-savings efficiencies. Upside $53 = 22x NTM P/E on CY28 $2.39 (dissipating competition, NA DTC acceleration, intl recovery). Downside $31 = 16x on CY28 $1.95 (heightened competition, rising input costs, macro).
- PT risks: China softness persists; macro/geopolitical; inability to maintain innovation; demand uncertainty/competition intensifies; reset actions take longer.

### 5 debates: bull vs bear evidence
1. Greater China (most important swing factor): FY4Q26 rev -17% CC, EBIT -20%; mgmt: deeper/longer reset, no clear inflection; inventory clean-up, reduced promos, store refresh underway, limited traction; question cyclical vs structural share loss to domestic champions. Bull: CC declines moderate meaningfully vs 4Q26; sequential sell-through/inventory improvement; better traffic, premium positioning. Bear: double-digit declines persist; markdown reliance; further wholesale shipment cuts.
2. Running (strongest proof point): >20% growth multiple qtrs; share gains NA and Europe; Vomero, Pegasus, Structure, post-recovery Mind. Bull: running growth >15%; more share gains; positive Mind, Peg Premium, pipeline. Bear: sharp decel; innovation concentrated in few products; competition from ONON, HOKA, Salomon, Altra.
3. Sportswear (biggest drag): rightsizing AF1, AJ1, Dunk inventories. DKS-READ: DKS recently stated Foot Locker experiencing persistent weakness in legacy lifestyle; promo pressure across retro/lifestyle elevated. Bull: AF1/AJ1/Dunk normalization largely complete; Air Max, lifestyle running silhouettes, women's gain traction; full-price sell-through improves. Bear: more liquidation; elevated markdowns; lifestyle demand weak despite cleaner inventory.
4. Wholesale recovery vs clean-marketplace discipline: NKE rebuilt wholesale relationships; NA wholesale back to growth; marketplace promotional in lifestyle. Bull: positive spring/summer order book commentary; wholesale beats; clean inventory/healthy partner economics. Bear: sell-through fails to support order books; cancellations/delayed shipments; more markdown support.
5. GM inflection: 1Q27 guided slightly positive GM despite tariffs; mgmt said 1Q27 could be first qtr recovery actions outweigh headwinds; watch mix/productivity vs temporary. Bull: GM beats gdnc; lower promo; mix to performance/innovation. Bear: GM miss despite easy compares; more markdowns; China/Sportswear offset.
### PRISM curve
- Phases: Reset/"TRAP" (sales & margins pressured, defense) → Rebase/"ESCAPE" (merch margins rebase, offense) → Regrow/"HUNT" (product/marketing/brand drive sales accel + full-price GM expansion; profits/OM "explode"). NKE executing 2 competing turnarounds: North America entering "HUNT" (beating sales/margin expectations; earnings > sales growth; multi-yr); Greater China early "TRAP" (stiff competition all categories, fleeting pricing power on core franchises, share erosion → operating deleverage).
### 4 catalysts
1. Positive IM Spread inflection: FY4Q26 IM spread (sales growth vs avg inventory growth) -86 bps vs +207 bps prior qtr (first negative qtr); GMROI worsened 8 consecutive qtrs; OMROI improved 1 qtr.
2. Positive GM inflection (forecast FY1H27): full-price selling from channel clean-up + innovative brand-heat product; look for margins/earnings bottom and sustained beat-and-raise.
3. Return to positive accelerating sales growth (forecast FY1H28): NKE guided 1Q27 rev down LSD-MSD with sequential decel 1Q→2Q27 (China weakness, higher EMEA promos, NA wholesale shipment timing, Sportswear/Jordan weakness, geopolitical/macro). Less fixed-cost deleverage as growth improves; opex deleverage fluctuates by qtr.
4. Brand recovery / "Win Now" (FY1H28+): CEO Elliott Hill accelerating newness, innovation at scale, marketing; five fields of play, three countries, five cities; early wholesale partner feedback positive (green shoots).

### Valuation framework
