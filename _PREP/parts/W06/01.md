### Key note terms
- Ranking: senior unsecured unsubordinated; pari with RCF + Existing Senior Notes; effectively sub to secured debt; structurally sub to sub obligations.
- Optional redemption: before Applicable Par Call Date (X months before maturity; blanks) at greater of (1) PV of Remaining Scheduled Payments (to par call date) at Treasury Rate + [blank] bps (each series) semi-annual 30/360, (2) 100% principal; + accrued. On/after par call: 100% + accrued. Treasury Rate via H.15 constant maturities (interpolation; fallback to UST security bid/ask at 11:00am on 2nd biz day prior). Notice 10–60 days; may be conditional (e.g., equity offering/financing); partial redemption pro rata/by lot; none of $2,000 or less redeemed in part.
- Change of Control Triggering Event (CoC + Below IG Rating Event by both Moody's and S&P within Relevant Period: from public notice until 60 days after CoC, extended while on watch): offer to repurchase at 101% + accrued; notice within 30 days; payment date 30–60 days after notice; third-party offer satisfies; waivable by majority of series; if ≥90% tendered, company may redeem remainder at 101% (10–60 days' notice, within 30 days after payment date). CoC feature result of negotiations with underwriters; "no present intention" to engage in CoC transaction.
- CoC definition: sale of all/substantially all assets to non-subsidiary person; or any person (other than Company/subs, Permitted Person, employee benefit plans) becomes beneficial owner >50% voting power; exception where DKS becomes wholly owned sub of holdco and pre-transaction holders hold majority / no >50% holder.
- Permitted Person = Edward W. Stack and his Affiliated Parties (entities he controls voting, trusts for him/Immediate Family Members (relation not more remote than first cousin), trustees/estates, Immediate Family Members); and any 13(d) group in which those persons own ≥50% of group's voting power.
- Investment Grade Rating = ≥Baa3 (Moody's) and ≥BBB- (S&P).
- Limitation on liens: no debt secured by Voting Stock of any Significant Subsidiary unless notes equally secured; exceptions: (1) liens on stock of entity when it becomes Significant Sub, (2) intercompany debt, (3) existing at issue date, (4) basket ≤ greater of 15% of Consolidated Net Tangible Assets and $1,950,000,000, (5) refinancings (no principal increase beyond accrued interest/fees/premiums).
- CNTA = total assets − net goodwill & intangibles − total current liabilities, latest filed quarter; pro forma for acquisitions/dispositions >$500,000,000 since balance sheet date.
- Significant Subsidiary = sub with total assets >10% of consolidated; excludes subs not doing substantial business/fixed assets in US, foreign-organized subs, holdcos of foreign subs, subs majority-owned by foreign subs.
- Merger covenant: successor must be US entity assuming obligations, no default; exempt: reincorporation merger, intra-group asset transfers.
- No covenants on: sale/leasebacks, financial ratios/net worth, repurchasing/prepaying other debt, investments, dividends/stock repurchases; no limit on debt incurrence.
- Reports: file w/ trustee within 15 days after SEC filing deadline; EDGAR filing suffices.
- Events of Default: interest default 30 days; principal/premium default; covenant breach 90 days after notice from trustee or ≥25% holders; bankruptcy/insolvency. Acceleration by trustee or >25% holders; majority can waive/rescind. Holder suits need >25% request + indemnity + 90-day trustee inaction.
- Satisfaction & discharge, legal/covenant defeasance (standard; deposit of cash/non-callable USGs; Make-whole Deficit / Applicable Deficit may be deposited by redemption date; legal defeasance needs IRS ruling or change in tax law since Issue Date).
- Modification: majority of series; unanimous-affected for rate/maturity/principal/currency/premium/waiver mechanics changes. Can add guarantors, issue Additional Notes w/o consent.
