### 2029 goals (10/01/26)
- "2029 Goals Make Sense": high-teens sales growth should extend through 2029 (low share, rising awareness, geographic whitespace). Faster EMEA + APAC growth broadens regional mix; running, sneaker and apparel drive most of >CHF2bn planned incremental sales. Scale + automation lift adj EBITDA margin to at least 22%; stronger cash gen → self-funding; first $1bn share-buyback authorization.
- Core categories: target high-teens CAGR through 2029 relies mainly on established businesses. Running, sneaker/lifestyle, apparel → >75% of absolute sales increase through 2029; apparel targeted to triple; running remains largest area of R&D. Training, tennis, outdoor, kids add growth; football and golf contribute only modestly to 3-yr plan. Limited soccer drops start 2027; standard cleats + performance all-day products launch 2028 → newer sports = upside/awareness, not required to reach at least CHF5.6bn 2029 sales.
- Channel: DTC targeted 50% of sales by 2029 from ~45% in 2026 (stores + e-com); DKS-READ: wholesale "remains a major growth engine through deeper penetration and more shelf space with existing partners."
- Regions: APAC fastest-growing, led by China, on track to exceed 10% of sales, expanding premium retail. EMEA high-teens to low-20s annual growth next 3 yrs mainly from closing penetration gaps in existing markets; share in southern/eastern Europe, Benelux, Scandinavia only ~half that in DACH and UK. Cloudtilt rapid growth; On emerging as Gen Z lifestyle brand in Europe. Americas solid contributor despite larger base.
- Awareness: expansion into soccer and golf + apparel enlarge TAM. Global brand awareness from ~12% in 2023 to >30%; mgmt target >50%. 1.3% share of global sportswear.
- Margin: adj EBITDA margin +>200 bps to ≥22% by 2029 via SG&A leverage + automation. GM expected to remain ≥65% (premium pricing, full-price selling); mgmt to reinvest underlying efficiencies into product innovation/quality rather than further GM expansion. Distribution cost % sales on track for 10% in 2026 vs 13.4% in 2023; target ≥ another 100 bps improvement as Atlanta automation ramps and two legacy hubs consolidate into one automated facility. Marketing more efficient on larger base; G&A benefits from built capabilities + automation; selling expense rises with stores but retail profitability above co avg. Adj EBITDA to grow >20% annually through 2029 without GM upside; scale/execution not pricing/mix are key drivers.
- Cash: cash more than doubled to ~CHF1.2bn from CHF500m in 2023; net cash maintained. Priority organic investment incl ~100 additional stores through 2029 + tech/infrastructure. Strong EBITDA-to-FCF conversion; first share repurchase authorization up to $1bn through 2029 while maintaining net cash.
