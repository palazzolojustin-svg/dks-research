### Post-2Q26 outlook (08/11/26)
- Sales growth may slow in 3Q before improving 4Q as co deliberately reins in wholesale sell-in, esp Americas, to protect full-price positioning and clear channel ahead of major running-product refresh. 2Q FX-neutral sales +21.6%; DTC +34.3%; wholesale slowed to 12.7% amid heavy promotions across brands and softer sell-through in some everyday-running franchises in Americas. Broader refresh of all everyday-running franchises over next 14 months; BI watching US wholesale for evidence softness transitory.
- GM +390 bps to 65.4% in 2Q (DTC mix, full-price, op efficiencies, despite higher US tariffs) — key offset.
