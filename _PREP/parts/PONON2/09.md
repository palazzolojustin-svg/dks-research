### Our take
- 2Q26 below expectations on softer wholesale sell-in and Americas. Sales +22% ex-FX (JPMe and BBG cons +24%); DTC +34% ex-FX (JPMe +30%, cons +29%); wholesale +13% (JPMe and cons +20%). On had cautioned 2Q sell-in softer (conservative sell-in ahead of key 3Q product launches) but "the scale of the deceleration is still likely to disappoint." Regions ex-FX: Americas +13% (JPMe +15%), EMEA +21% (JPMe +20%), APAC +55% (JPMe +65%).
- GM 65.4% (JPMe 64.8%, cons 63.9%) but topline miss → gross profit 3% below JPMe. Adj EBITDA 3% below cons, 10% below JPMe.
- Outlook: FY sales gdnc slightly lowered to "low 20s" ex-FX (vs +23% / "at least" +23% prior); GM gdnc "at least 65%" (vs "at least 64.5%"); adj EBITDA margin gdnc maintained "19.5% to 20.0%". Investor discussions: topline trajectory most important element of equity story; expected shares to react negatively.
- Noteworthy: 1) Q2 sales +22% ex-FX/+13% reported to CHF850m vs JPMe +24%/CHF885m, BBG cons CHF881m; 2) DTC +34% ex-FX vs JPMe +30%, wholesale +13% vs +20%; 3) APAC +55% (JPMe +65%), EMEA +21% (JPMe +20%), Americas +13% (JPMe +15%); 4) GM +385 bps y/y to 65.4% despite "fully absorbing higher U.S. import tariffs" and excluding any tariff refunds; 5) adj EBITDA +23.5% to CHF168m (JPMe CHF188m), margin +160 bps y/y to 19.8% (JPMe 21.2%); 6) inventory +31% y/y and +13% vs Dec'25.
- FY26 gdnc: net sales "low-20%" ex-FX (vs "at least" +23% prior), absolute CHF3.47bn-3.56bn (vs CHF3.51bn prior; BBG cons CHF3.56bn); DTC to "strongly" outperform wholesale in H2; GM "at least" 65.0% (vs ≥64.5% prior; cons 64.4%), up ≥220 bps y/y; adj EBITDA margin 19.5-20.0% reiterated (cons 20.0%) → midpoint adj EBITDA ~CHF694m (BBG cons CHF708m).
- Likely cons changes: LSD downgrades to FY adj EBITDA.
- Valuation: 15.8x P/E 2y fwd, slightly above sector avg 15.5x (adidas, Nike, lulu, Asics, Deckers); c.40% discount to own last-2-yr avg 27.1x.

### Thesis / valuation / risks
- Premium Swiss sports brand founded 2010; footwear >95% of sales; industry w/ barriers to entry, health + casualisation tailwinds; balance of performance roots (credibility) and lifestyle (growth); early in growth life-cycle.
- PT: 3-stage DCF; 15% medium-term growth / 4% terminal growth; 15.5% medium-term and terminal EBIT margin; WACC 10.3%.
- Risks: competition (Nike, adidas, Hoka, Brooks); DKS-READ: "The Nike comeback, in particular, may delay On's expansion into the wholesale space."; Trump tariffs → US inflation hurting demand; multi-channel complexity (c.59% wholesale, c.41% retail); fashion risk (similar look/feel of shoes); elevated inventory → promo/markdown risk to margins/brand; discretionary spend; unusual leadership (co-CEOs, multiple founders on board); ambassador dependence (Roger Federer co-entrepreneur, Zendaya, athletes).
- JPM rating/PT history (date, rating, price, PT): 13-Mar-24 OW 30.64/34; 15-Apr-24 OW 32.87/36; 15-May-24 OW 36.30/40; 17-Jun-24 OW 42.00/47; 26-Sep-24 OW 50.20/55; 13-Nov-24 OW 52.62/58; 02-Dec-24 OW 58.33/66; 12-Aug-25 OW 45.72/71; 13-Nov-25 NR 41.51/--; 02-Jul-26 OW 35.56/51.
