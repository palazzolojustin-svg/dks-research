### Recent event reaction (08/11/26)
- 2Q sales trailed cons on slower wholesale growth amid greater sell-in discipline ahead of key 2H launches; underlying brand momentum strong. FY sales gdnc maintained at low-20% FX-neutral growth; GM view raised to at least 65% from 64% [sic; other BI docs say 64.5% prior]; full-price + distribution discipline supports industry-leading profitability despite tariffs.

### Thesis (08/05/26)
- Early stages of penetrating athletic shoe/apparel markets; room to build awareness/share expanding beyond running. Growth from running innovation, wider apparel reach, DTC mix; new stores + accelerating intl demand esp APAC. Higher DTC mix, new-product pipeline, scale economies drive share + margin.

### 2Q26 preview (08/05/26; consensus)
- Sales +17.8% (DTC +22.9%, wholesale +14.9%); APAC +47.2%, Americas +10.2%, EMEA +16.4%; footwear +16.5%, apparel +47.1%; GM +~236 bps on higher DTC mix + full-price sell-through; adj EBITDA margin +150 bps (op leverage, premium pricing offset retail investment); adj EPS (Class A) CHF 0.33. Catalysts: global launch Lightspray Cloudmonster 3; first co-created footwear+apparel collection with Zendaya.

### 2026 targets (03/31/26)
- On set to reach target CHF 3.55bn sales by end-2026 (doubling revenue) despite FX. Runway for low-20% annual growth given 2.9% share of global sports footwear market, smaller share of sportswear. Drivers: DTC, disciplined retail expansion, wholesale demand, underpenetrated markets (China), innovation. Scenario analysis: low-20% growth could moderate as scale increases; DTC penetration over 40%; disciplined wholesale → more balanced/resilient model; investment in innovation, retail, infrastructure supports premium/full-price.

### Share / awareness (05/13/26)
- Expansion into tennis, training, outdoor, lifestyle + apparel enlarges TAM. On has 1.3% share in global sportswear (footwear+apparel); brand awareness high-single to low-double digits in key countries, far below peers. Engagement via events, wholesale to reach untapped communities, athletes.
- Rose to No. 8 sports-footwear "retailer" in 2025 [sic; later BI doc says No. 7], widening gap vs Deckers' Hoka (1.4% share).
- China biggest opportunity: middle class, sports interest; new stores/larger formats in Mainland China + major digital platforms. Since first store 2019, China sales growing triple-digit; aspiration China 10% of sales beyond 2026. APAC = 17% of sales. Americas remains largest region; EMEA most mature but underpenetrated in France, Spain, Italy.

### DTC / stores (03/10-03/17/26)
- Plan to at least triple store footprint by 2026 + e-com → DTC 31% CAGR over 3 yrs (BI scenario); store count triples to 90, online still majority of DTC. Wholesale growth to moderate but solid: scenario 22% CAGR. Controlling wholesale maintains premium brand equity, converts momentum to DTC; store proximity → "digital halo effect".
- Mgmt: after opening a store, e-com traffic can be 3x higher and wholesale partners in market see uplift. Accelerating openings to 20-25/yr could push retail store penetration to 10% LT, driven by new retail formats beyond flagships. E-com to outpace overall growth; distribution capacity expansion key.
- DKS-READ (03/13/26): wholesale growth more controlled; most major strategic accounts secured in core markets; growth from adding shelf space and share within key partners "such as Dick's, JD and Foot Locker"; products in just 40-50% of key accounts' locations → runway for mid-single-digit door expansion annually; same-store gains + improved assortments drive majority of wholesale growth in mid-to-high teens.

### Sports / product (03/13-03/17/26)
- Pivot footwear→sportswear brand; tennis, training, lifestyle widen TAM; running remains key driver. Nike, Adidas, Puma racing for fastest marathon shoe but struggled to gain loyalty among everyday runners; On became top-3 running brand in most main markets and with key retail partners over past 5 yrs; major franchise refreshes this yr. US sports footwear share 6.3% vs 1.2% in 2020.
- Tennis: Roger Federer partnership credibility; focused high-impact partners Iga Swiatek, Ben Shelton (head-to-toe on-court); multiyear Zendaya partnership; court styles translating into lifestyle demand esp Roger franchise.
- Apparel: entered 2019; global sports-apparel market $245.1bn, >$300bn by 2030 (Euromonitor); apparel sales CHF 170m in 2025; 2026 apparel a core pillar (training, tennis), goal apparel penetration double digits over time; apparel ~20-30% of revenue in stores.

### Peers (03/31/26)
- Sales growth may keep outpacing peers; selective wholesale distribution, premium pricing, DTC → GM above most cohorts; op margin improving even as marketing ramps. Surpassed Brooks, Under Armour, Hoka as 7th-largest sports-footwear brand globally.
