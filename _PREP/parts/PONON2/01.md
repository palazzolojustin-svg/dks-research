### 2Q26 modeling detail (UBS)
- Sales: 2Q26 net sales +18.3% y/y (+23.3% ex-FX) to CHF 885M; DTC to outpace wholesale. DTC +22.6% y/y (+28% ex-FX) to CHF 378M; wholesale +15.1% (+20% ex-FX) to CHF 507M. ~510 bps FX headwind to 2Q growth at spot (better than ~600 bps previously modeled; old = FX rates at last print). FY26: net sales +19.5% y/y (+23.1% ex-FX) to CHF 3.60B; co FY26 gdnc +23% ex-FX = reported CHF 3.51B.
- Innovation 2Q: several lifestyle footwear launches incl Cloudnova Moon in collaboration with Zendaya (Fig 3 product launches).
- DTC drivers: US brand awareness eclipsed 30% for first time in 1Q; marketing % sales +~200 bps y/y to 13.1% in 1Q; expect awareness gains → DTC traffic; new stores at healthy clip; 2Q26 DTC laps +54% growth prior year.
- Wholesale: mgmt actions to keep wholesale inventory clean ahead of major 2027 launches → more conservative shipment cadence balance of year despite healthy end demand → potential deceleration in remaining quarters. DKS-READ: on 1Q call ONON said products available at ~50% of doors operated by key wholesale partners incl Dick's Sporting Goods, Foot Locker, JD Sports; UBS expects gradual expansion to more doors + more shelf space in existing partner locations; more penetration at run specialty via curated performance running launches.
- Run specialty: 1Q call said run specialty orderbooks up over 25% y/y; initiatives: expanding co-branded events, prioritizing product allocations for these partners; only ~5% of On's US running footwear sold in run specialty.
- GM: 2Q26 GM +250 bps y/y to 64.0%. 1Q26 GM 64.2% (+~430 bps y/y): supply chain efficiency +250 bps, stronger full-price selling +150 bps, favorable FX +100 bps, offset tariffs -100 bps; co considers full-price selling and cost efficiencies sustainable through FY26 and beyond; also DTC share expansion + FX tailwinds. Current product tariffed at 10% vs 20% rate FY26 outlook contemplates → potential GM upside. FY26 GM +~170 bps y/y to 64.5%.
- SG&A: 2Q26 SG&A CHF 424M, +18.8% y/y; 47.9% of sales, ~25 bps deleverage; CHF appreciation headwind to G&A (significant CHF-denominated cost base); operational improvements lower distribution expenses. 2Q adj EBITDA CHF 176M, 19.9% margin, ~175 bps expansion. FY26 SG&A 48.6% of sales (+~30 bps y/y); FY26 adj EBITDA margin +~105 bps to 19.9%.
- 2Q26 EPS forecast CHF 0.35, 1c above cons (co gives no quarterly EPS gdnc).

### Guidance outlook (UBS expectation)
- Expect FY26 ex-FX sales gdnc raised 50-100 bps from +23%; strong DTC 2Q26; "fairly pronounced deceleration in 2H26 wholesale sales growth" expected from inventory management ahead of 2027 innovation cycle. Implied FY26 reported growth outlook +16.5% includes ~650 bps FX headwind; FX moved fairly significantly positive esp 2H26 → 100 bps ex-FX raise could mean slightly higher implied reported raise.
- Expect reiteration of FY26 adj EBITDA margin gdnc 19.5-20.0%. Co raised FY26 GM outlook 150 bps in 1Q despite tariffs, emerging freight headwinds, reinvestment (supply chain efficiencies, scale, strong DTC share). Co assumes 150 bps FY26 GM drag from tariffs assuming 20% incremental Vietnam tariff; currently paying 10%; most recent policy suggests 12.5% tariff on US imports from Vietnam → possible GM upside; but co may stay cautious; sustained elevated oil prices may add cost pressure; FX pressure on SG&A + reinvestment could limit EBITDA flow-through.

### Valuation / scenarios
- PT $83 = ~33x CHF 2.10 FY28 EPSe, FX CHF/USD 1.22 (from 1.25; PT cut entirely FX). $83 puts ONON in line w/ peers on P/E and P/Sales (Fig 14; peers ADS-DE, PUM-DE, DECK, LULU, NKE, AS, BIRK, CHWY, ABNB). ~33x within 3-yr hist avg (Fig 15); FY2 P/E above Softline peer avg (Fig 16). DCF (secondary) also $83 (Fig 17). 29% 5-yr EPS CAGR forecast.
- Upside $102: ~33% 5-yr EPS CAGR FY25-30E; fully mitigates US tariffs; much more than running brand (tennis, apparel); stays 'cool', grows lifestyle w/o losing performance credibility; ~35x on CHF 2.40 FY28E upside EPS (FX 1.22).
- Base $83: ~29% EPS CAGR; some US tariff pressure FY25-FY26; share gains in running footwear; growth in Europe, North America, China; ~18% annual sales growth; mix shift to own web/stores → GM expansion; fixed-cost leverage → ~17% EBIT margin FY30E; ~33x on CHF 2.10.
- Downside $19: ~16% EPS CAGR; unable to mitigate tariffs; sportswear demand slows (inflation not transitory), macro, competition stymie growth/share; supply chain limits sales; costs pressure margins; ~11x on CHF 1.40 FY28E.
- Thesis map: global athletic wear industry +M-HSD% annually multi-year (7% avg in 10 yrs pre-pandemic; COVID amplified health/wellness + casual dress; sportswear only 25% of total apparel+footwear). ONON can reach ~2% LT market share (= Puma FY21 sales); rare brand able to become global multi-sport multi-category (like Nike, Adidas, Puma). High-teens EBIT margin LT: premium/innovation not price; DTC/eCom-led; apparel accretive; Nike/Lulu similar margin outlooks. Market sees On as running shoe brand. 5-yr sales/adj EBITDA/EPS CAGR 18%/20%/29%; justifies 33x FY2 P/E. UBS EL 2022 Global Athletic Wear survey: sportswear demand strong; global On awareness low; those aware increasingly favorable. Reverse DCF: market prices mid-teens sales growth (vs UBS ~17% 5-yr) and EBIT margin ~12-13% (vs ~17% FY30E).
- Risks (valuation/risk statement): third-party manufacturing/supply chain disruption; third-party retailers' influence over end-market sales; fashion cycles; ability to keep rapid innovation; slowing China; global competition; low visibility on consumer spending. Company description: founded Sep 28 2012, Zurich; FY22/21/20 net sales CHF1,222.1m/724.6m/425.3m (68.7%, 70.4% growth); as of Mar 21 2023 19 stores, >60 countries.
