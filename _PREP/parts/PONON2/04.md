## SRC 04_PEERS/ONON/2026-08-06_BloombergIntel_ONON_On_Can_Top_Hoka_in_Apparel_Market_Share_as_Retail_Strategy_Grows.md | 2026-08-06 | Bloomberg Intelligence; Abigail Gilmartin (file ticker focus DECK) | On vs Hoka apparel opportunity to 2030
- Headline: On could surpass Hoka in global apparel share over next decade, reaching 0.3% of a market Euromonitor projects at $304bn by 2030; On current 0.1% vs Hoka 0.2% (BI calc). Drivers: product innovation, premium retail, brand-building; head-to-toe assortments; elevated flagship experience; US + China. BI: apparel sales ~36% CAGR through 2030, >4x to nearly CHF800m.
- Market: global sports apparel ~$247bn today → nearly $304bn by 2030 (Euromonitor); drivers athletic participation, health/wellness spend, performance apparel worn more of daily life. Top 10 sports-apparel brands = just 32% of global sales (less concentrated than footwear) → room for premium entrants.
- Unlike Deckers' Hoka (apparel tied to footwear franchise), On building apparel as stand-alone growth engine. Broadening beyond running into training, tennis, lifestyle; more occasions, cross-sell, repeat purchase; focus on women's apparel, technical innovation, premium design; lifestyle campaigns w/ Zendaya and FKA Twigs; co-created collections + fashion collabs.
- US: ~45% of global sports apparel market; On ~0.1% share (Euromonitor), Hoka 0.2%; BI believes On better positioned to surpass Hoka via premium flagships + awareness. Owned retail central (control merchandising, storytelling, experience vs wholesale). US store counts: On 14, Hoka 17. Hoka stores footwear-centric; On merchandises apparel as core, head-to-toe, resembling Lululemon and Alo Yoga → higher attachment. Path to US apparel share ~0.1% → ~0.3% by 2030 = >CHF200m incremental apparel sales over next 5 yrs.
- Pricing: On apparel at premium end; core women's categories align with or exceed Lululemon and Alo Yoga, meaningfully above Hoka; premium most pronounced in technical categories (leggings, outerwear, performance bottoms). (Avg $ MSRP exhibit = image.)
- China: just over one-fifth of global sports apparel market; ~5.8% CAGR through 2030 (Euromonitor). Opportunity from premium retail footprint + awareness among higher-end consumers (vs US = low share). 38 owned stores in Greater China (largest owned retail market); significant share of new flagship openings; larger formats in Chengdu, Shenzhen, Shanghai. Several flagships >20% of sales from apparel, well above co avg; China to contribute disproportionate share of apparel growth through 2030.
- DKS-READ: none explicit (owned-retail-led apparel strategy, wholesale not the apparel vehicle).
