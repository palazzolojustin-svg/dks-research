### MD&A
- Strategy: pillars athlete experience, differentiated product, brand engagement, teammate experience; vertical brands; repositioning store portfolio toward House of Sport, Golf Galaxy Performance Centers, DICK'S Field House as key omni-channel growth driver. "We believe there is strength and momentum in the sports industry in the United States and expect this trend to continue in the near term, with continued excitement around women's sports, the 2026 World Cup and the 2028 Olympics." Plans investments in digital and in-store to grow share, key categories, accelerate eCommerce.
- FL deal per MD&A: FL FY2024 sales $8B; banners Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs Sports, WSS, atmos; ~2,400 stores in 20 countries (N. America, Europe, Asia, Australia, NZ) + licensed stores in Europe, Middle East, Asia. Expected "$100 to $125 million in cost synergies in the medium-term achieved through procurement and direct sourcing efficiencies." Plans to operate FL as standalone business unit, maintain FL brands.
- Macro: elevated rates, inflation, potential trade/tariff changes could impact pricing, discretionary spend, promotions.
- Guidance: "we have reaffirmed our full year outlook for 2025 and continue to expect comparable sales growth for the year to be in the range of 1% to 3% and earnings per diluted share to be in the range of $13.80 to $14.40, which does not include acquisition-related costs, investment losses or results from the recently announced plan to acquire Foot Locker."
- FY2025 trends: expect GM expansion driven by assortment quality; SG&A to deleverage in FY2025 on strategic digital, in-store and marketing investments; "We expect this deleverage to moderate in the second half of the fiscal year."
- Comp definition: store enters comp in 14th full month; relocations comp from original open date; closed stores excluded; digital revenue = all eCom incl store-fulfilled omni, GameChanger subscriptions, DICK'S Media Network revenue. Lower CFO typical in Q1/Q3 (inventory ahead of peaks), higher Q2/Q4. Critical policies: inventory obsolescence, shrink, goodwill/intangibles, long-lived asset impairment; no changes.
- Exec summary: sales +5.2% to $3.17B; comp +4.5% (vs +5.3% LY); NI $264.3M / $3.24 diluted vs $275.3M / $3.30; NI includes non-cash FL equity loss $10.3M net of tax ($0.13/diluted sh). Repurchased 1.4M sh for $298.7M under $2.0B 2021 program; new 5-yr $3.0B program authorized.
- Comp methodology change: from FY2025 Warehouse Sale locations included in comp from 14th full month; prior year revised (8-K 2025-03-11, Ex 99.2).

### Risk factors (new: FL transaction)
- Closing conditions: FL two-thirds shareholder vote, HSR expiry, other antitrust clearances; regulators may impose conditions/costs/restrictions.
- If not completed: costs still payable; DKS may owe $95.5M termination fee to FL in certain circumstances; stock price impact.
- Business relationship disruption; dilution from stock consideration; financing not a closing condition; may not obtain debt on favorable terms.
- Integration: operate FL as standalone unit; challenges incl preserving FL relationships, multiple banners/differentiated concept and "successfully addressing the challenges facing Foot Locker's business", integrating forecasting/controls/IT/finance, operating in countries DKS hasn't operated, HR/retention; expanded size/geographic scope; significant non-recurring costs (advisory, severance, filing fees); interim operating covenants under merger agreement; transaction litigation risk; post-close stock driven by different factors, FL holders may sell.
- FLS list additions: DICK'S Media Network, GameChanger, tariffs, AI/ML in eCom regulation, House of Sport/Field House/GG Performance Center growth & capex.
