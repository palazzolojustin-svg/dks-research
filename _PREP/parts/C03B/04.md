### Proposal 4 presentation (pre-recorded, Curtis Hill, Senior Advisor, Free Enterprise Project, National Center for Public Policy Research)
- Requests report (reasonable cost, omitting proprietary info) on whether DKS transgender-policy decisions are informed by (1) definition of women as "adult human females based on biological reality" and (2) assessment of risks to females and women's/girls' sports and private spaces.
- Claims: DKS champions women (girls' sports, female leadership programs, youth philanthropy), aligns w/ Title IX, but partners with groups prioritizing "males identifying as females"; activist rating systems give DKS high marks; DKS training, transition guidelines and benefits embed that view → "clear contradiction". Cites biological male athletic advantages not fully removed by hormone suppression; athletes Laney Olmstead, Chelsea Mitchell. Alleged risks to DKS: lawsuits from displaced female athletes, reputational harm, lost customer goodwill from those who want DKS "to sell sporting goods, not ideology". Says board's "monitors risks in the ordinary course of business" response "not enough"; proposal "does not dictate policy".
