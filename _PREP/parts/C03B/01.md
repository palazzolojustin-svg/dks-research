## SRC 02_DKS_TRANSCRIPTS/conferences/DKS_MorganStanley-Conference_2025-12-03.md | 2025-12-03 10:15 AM ET | Morgan Stanley Global Consumer & Retail Conference, fireside (FactSet corrected transcript) | Ed Stack (Exec Chair), Lauren Hobart (Pres/CEO), Navdeep Gupta (EVP/CFO); host Simeon Gutman (MS hardline/broadline/food retail analyst) | post-Q3 FY2025, holiday 2025

### Host framing (Gutman, MS)
- DKS hasn't retained all post-COVID EBIT margin but "one of the biggest transformation stories post-COVID"; amplified changes in megatrend category; "going big with stores" when retail shrinking; "gone small" w/ Foot Locker (FL) acq. MS "mixed on it" due to questions not understood, now "getting more clarity"; "we think it actually has among the best asymmetry in all of retail."

### Transformation / House of Sport (HoS) (Stack)
- "we do virtually nothing the same" vs ~2016-2017: products, merchandising, marketing, store presentation, distribution, e-com.
- HoS concept started ~10 yrs ago; HQ basement ~25,000 sq ft mock-up space to build displays pre-store. First version built, judged "not different enough", scrapped. Restarted ~7 yrs ago as "ecosystem of the future" = digital + community + service + product components. North Star: "build the concept that will kill DICK'S Sporting Goods."
- Nike on first HoS: "best expression of sport any place in the world"; question was scalability (flagship that loses money/breakeven?); DKS: "definitely scalable."
- First HoS opened 2022; now 35, "extremely productive"; economics (sales, EBITDA margins) previously laid out. Opened 15 this yr (2025), ~15 next yr (2026) "and going forward."
- Access to iconic real estate unavailable 5 yrs ago: "the mall at Palm Beach Gardens", Cerritos Mall (LA), Tysons Corner (DC); sales in high-performing malls "just out of this world"; malls at "$800 to $1,200, $1,500 a square foot".
- HoS tweaks: collectibles/trading cards (Collectors Clubhouse; credits Michael Rubin/Fanatics for reinvigorating industry, popular with 12-17 yr olds); Gymshark in 12 HoS stores; more space for vertical brands; several HoS elements taken down to traditional "Field House" concept, "worked extremely well". "knock on wood, things are pretty good in House of Sport and DICK'S".
- Gupta: matured HoS stores continue to perform well on top and bottom line; consumer responding to "newness and innovation" presented visually.

### Strategic pillars (Hobart)
- 4 pillars: differentiated products (footwear investment ~10 yrs ago enabled sit-and-fit, opened new category, led to HoS); athlete (customer) experience (e-com + in-store); teammate experience/culture; brand ("sports change lives"; charitable; kids play). Vision: "best sports company in the world" (not best sporting goods retailer).

### Foot Locker: rationale & turnaround
- Stack rationale: "footwear is the engine that pulls the train"; deeper strategic involvement w/ key brands; global footprint never had before; FL consumer more urban. HoS in Manhattan: can't imagine it; rents too high; "we're not ever going to build these stores that it's okay to lose $5 million, we're going to chalk it off to marketing."
- Most misunderstood aspect: "how simple this turnaround is"; "back to retail 101"; prior FL team lacked right product/access, right place, right store; distribution "really difficult". "the solution is simple, the work is hard."
- Mgmt: Ann Freeman, President FL North America, longtime Nike exec, first job in Finish Line store ("real sneakerhead"); new Nike team ("[ph] Elliott and TP (00:10:33) and the group") respects her, wants FL to win. "all the brands that we've talked to want Foot Locker to succeed"; brands need "stable, growing, viable, predictable Foot Locker".
- Timing: "cleaning out the garage" (per last earnings call); fresh start 2026. Q1/Q2 2026 product bought by previous mgmt (most no longer there); hope better in Q1/Q2 but first DKS-built assortment is back-to-school (BTS) 2026 → "real step change in back-to-school."
- Hobart: DKS strengths transferring: art/science of merchandising, visual presentation/storytelling, brand building/traffic, long strategic brand relationships (now bigger global partner), operational excellence. Stack spending "a ton of time" w/ FL team; Stack spends every other week in FL NY offices.
- FL GM erosion (Gutman: followed brand proliferation across other channels — Stack: "Absolutely"): biggest FL profit deterioration was margin rate, "500 to 600 basis points of margin erosion" (however calculated); "we can reverse that trend", won't quantify.
- 11-store test: planned during diligence, started ~day 10 post-close; all merch removed, walls re-laid out; old FL wall "a run-on sentence"; now franchises highlighted (e.g., Nike AF1, New Balance 9060, adidas Campus); "roughly 30% less SKUs"; sales: "we're really enthusiastic"; apparel re-added (FL had mostly exited apparel over last several yrs), assortment "cobbled together", apparel "much better"; "real green shoots". Hobart: test "totally small and short amount of time, but we're thrilled".
- Hobart: FL team quality lets DKS team stay focused on DKS flywheel — "absolute prerequisites of doing this deal."
- International/EMEA: keeping it (not divesting); pre-COVID intl was very profitable, "really got forgotten"; prior team changed reporting (EMEA once ran independently, then reported to US counterparts who didn't visit stores); capex stripped, no investment in stores/people, wrong people (changed). Hired Matthew Barnes (ex-CEO ALDI UK) — European team running Europe. NA ahead of EMEA in turnaround, EMEA "not terribly far behind".
- Gupta: brand partnerships global → global-level conversations on access/allocation/buying; intl brands say "so much of low-hanging fruit in just buying the product better."
- Q4 FY25 FL markdowns: "expect our margin rates to be 1,000 to 1,500 basis points below what Foot Locker was last year" (clearing old/unsold product); FL comps "down mid-single digits to high-single digits"; foundation for fresh start 2026; avoid "death by 1,000 cuts" — don't want charges in Q4 then Q1 then Q2; "we think we've captured it"; caveat "You never know you're not going to have any setbacks."

### Holiday / consumer / guidance
- No intra-quarter comment. 7 consecutive qtrs comp >4%; Q3 comp 5.7%, 2-yr stack 10%. "The intersection of sport and culture has never been higher." Just raised Q4/FY guidance; "very enthusiastic about the holiday season."
- Gupta: promo landscape expected "relatively similar to what it was last year"; raised comp expectation and margin expectations; expect margins to expand y/y in Q4 vs Q3; still expect FY expanded merch margin and GM.
- Hobart: Q3 strength broad-based: footwear, apparel, team sports (BTS), golf "incredible resurgence"; Collectors Clubhouse trading cards "whole new business"; World Cup balls "flying off the shelves"; licensed "doing fantastic".

### GameChanger (GC) / DKS Media Network / youth sports
- 9M unique users; #1 baseball/softball scoring & stat app w/ live-stream video. GC athletes who are DKS customers = best customers. GC "most unique asset" in Media Network: youth-sports eyeballs "can't be acquired anywhere else".
- Dedicated mgmt team led by Samir (GC CEO). Youth sports tech industry $40B, growing fast. GC = SaaS platform, "$100 million of revenue last year" (Gupta misspoke $100B then corrected), "very profitable"; 30%-40% growth "very reasonable expectation" from new sports: basketball, volleyball, flag football launched. $100M includes media (media "very early stages").
- GC/ScoreCard members most engaged/loyal; DKS Media Network + GC in "middle stages" (not very early). Avg GC user ~45 minutes on platform.
- Stack: DKS led last funding round of Unrivaled (hosts youth tournaments/events); youth sports ecosystem investments.
- Focus on FL/HoS does not dilute GC focus ("Absolutely not" —Hobart).

### Brands
- On, Free People Movement, Gymshark (DKS first US retailer; "off the charts"; in 12 HoS); Nike: running construct around 9box, Pegasus, Structure, Vomero "out of this world", Nike gained mkt share; "a lot of opportunity ahead with Nike." Fanatics relationship: licensed/jerseys, collectibles, cards.

### AI / agentic commerce (Hobart)
- 3 buckets: teammate efficiency/tools; athlete experience (recommendations, smarter teammates); new growth (GC uses AI, e.g., auto-scoring/reports from filmed basketball games). "early innings"; experiments in labor mgmt planning, ML, regional relevancy, sizing curves; "making a big bet next year" changing how marketing is produced; highly personalized content investment.
- Advantages in agentic world: differentiated assortment; speed of distribution — 800-plus stores near most customers; "almost 90% of our products come out of our stores" (ship-from-store); stores "accelerating and accelerating" on Black Friday.
- Agent not named (joke "It's Lauren"/"It's Ed"). Stack: FL has AI embedded in merchandising system — product/category analytics, "very productive tool".

### Margins / P&L construct (Gupta)
- Gutman: margins step-changed to low double-digits post-COVID. Gupta: construct unchanged — drive top line and bottom-line improvement; GC, DKS Media Network, ML investments yield margin expansion; GC/DMN "early innings", future margin drivers.
- 2026: opening new distribution center (investment); "plenty of flexibility in our cost structure from an SG&A" to deliver bottom-line improvement despite investments; expansion mix will be combination of GM and SG&A.
