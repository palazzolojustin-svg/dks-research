## SRC 02_DKS_TRANSCRIPTS/conferences/DKS_Goldman-Conference_2026-09-14.md | 2026-09-14 11:30 AM ET | Goldman Sachs Global Consumer and Retail Conference, fireside (FactSet corrected transcript) | Ed Stack (Exec Chair; Chair/CEO 1984–Jan 2021), Lauren Hobart (Pres/CEO; joined 2011 as SVP/CMO, Pres 2017, CEO 2021), Navdeep Gupta (EVP/CFO; joined 2017 as SVP Finance/CAO, CFO 2021); host Kate McShane (GS) | post-Q2 FY2026 guidance cut, 2H26 outlook

### Athletic cycle (Stack)
- "the idea that the athletic cycle is over, is overdone"; DKS footwear "really very good"; specialty channel "a bit more challenged".
- FL (and some specialty players) more dependent on high-launch retro shoes than DKS; that business slowed; DKS pivoted quickly to emerged brands On, Hoka; FL "was not able to or didn't do that". Broadening: Saucony, Salomon, ASICS "coming back", Timberland, UGGs, Birkenstock → more alternatives vs a handful of dominant brands, "very good for us".
- Legacy silhouettes are the issue for some brands but pipeline strong: Nike Mind shoe "terrific" (supply-constrained, manufacturing ramp); basketball around Ja and Caitlin Clark shoes. "a bit of a dip in the industry, but it's far from a real long-term problem"; On/Hoka "doing extremely well"; "way over done".
- "The margin pressure is real" — legacy silhouettes slowed, brands discounted, "the discounting got pretty aggressive"; DKS chose to "stay with the market from a price standpoint" (didn't want store/FL associates hearing "$25 less expensive on such and such a site"). Promo framed as "investment" to retain consumer for Christmas, spring cleats etc.; avoid high-price perception; "lifetime investment". "we do think it's going to continue through the fourth quarter"; "we had a mulligan... what we did in the second quarter, we'd do it all over again exactly the same way".

### Legacy silhouettes / FL Q1 vs Q2
- McShane: FL US comp "about 6%" in Q1 FY26. Stack: Q1 helped by launch/Jordan Retro product; Q2 "a disappointment in that product" across DKS, FL, direct competitors and brands. Legacy silhouettes trending down, "really slowed" somewhere in Q2. But Nike new materials/embellishments on AF1/Dunks "couldn't keep them in stock": traditional triple white/triple black/varsity-color AF1 "slowed significantly"; triple white AF1 in patent leather, AF1 in black "can't keep those in stock". Duration unknown; more such product would change scenario. Discounting aggressive in Q2, "we expect that to continue through the balance of the year."

### Guidance cut (Q2 FY26)
- Market surprised by cut to DKS core margins and FL comp & margins.
- Hobart (DKS): "we kept our comp guidance the same"; reflected promo from legacy footwear (affects DKS too) + "conservatism toward fuel costs and health care costs, which we have been experiencing all year"; "we never guide to the best, first possible outcome, but we feel really good about our guidance."
- [Transcript attributes to McShane but content is mgmt, likely Stack:] no FL "contagion" to DKS; no category comp disclosure, but DKS footwear comps "really quite good, you'd be pretty pleased"; DKS ahead on legacy-silhouette transition but had to stay competitive on price; footwear shift into Birkenstock, Uggs, Timberland. "Five shoes" thesis for HS athlete: sport shoe (cleats/basketball), running, training (e.g., HYROX; not a running shoe), recovery (Mind shoe "extremely hot"), lifestyle/school shoe; DKS+FL "best positioned".
- FL comp cut reasons (Stack): retro launch difficulty "well chronicled", not anticipated in original gdnc; "Q1 was pretty good, Q2 was pretty difficult"; EMEA "very difficult" — "there was not a war going on in the Middle East" at original gdnc, "really meaningful impact on Europe"; legacy silhouette pivot "taking longer than we thought"; industry "overcapacity" in legacy silhouettes vs "under-capacity" in new hot shoes; manufacturing constraints limit supply.
- Supply vs demand: "Don't see this as a demand issue whatsoever"; hot: On Cloudtilt, Nike Mind, Nike 9box running (Pegasus, Structure, Vomero); DKS non-footwear: bat launches, Vuori (number of stores), Gymshark, Free People Movement → "extremely well-positioned".
- McShane context: FL new assortment flowing; ad campaign reached consumers only ~5-6 weeks ago; 250 stores remodeled to Fast Break, ~350 by year-end (FY26). Stack: GC would call underestimating question "very dangerous"; guidance reflects where business is today.

### Clearance / ecosystem
- Stack: discounting legacy alongside new full-price product "not that difficult" — consumer knows what's new (Mind, Nike running, Cloudtilt, new adidas running); legacy silhouettes discounted, "out of favor right now".
- Hobart: FL sees trends ahead of DKS → DKS can taper buys on slowing franchises; Going Going Gone! (GGG) used for clearance; teams cooperating.
- Stack: DKS has longer tail on franchises; FL consumer "more fashion-conscious, faster"; e.g., "New Balance is a bit more difficult at Foot Locker. It's rocking in DICK'S." Visibility to "entire ecosystem of athletic footwear": atmos (Tokyo; owned via FL) = "Tier 0 retailer, very similar to Kith", gets brand seeding product (anecdote w/ Bjørn in Germany brand rooms: FL won't get a shoe; atmos will) → ignite in FL street/fashion doors + DKS HoS → scale in DKS & traditional FL → end-of-life in GGG. "nobody else has got that kind of visibility".

### FL asset / capital / options
- Not taking assets from DKS; "$1 billion on the balance sheet right now"; Fast Break "pretty low capital-intensive"; FL won't need significant capital.
- Brand anecdote: CEO of very important brand (big DKS vendor, little FL business) said previously "we didn't really trust Foot Locker"; now "fully invested and supportive"; "a couple of different brands" similar. "believe me, this is not easy with Foot Locker"; transition to other brands/allocation "taking longer than we anticipated"; expected "more legacy brands" to be supportive quicker — "that hasn't happened".
- "we are creating a menu that all of you would expect us to create based on the environment"; "no preconceived ideas of what has to be done"; not "damn the torpedoes"; owned FL "a year and a week"; assessing what's working / could work / won't work; "we'll give you more details as we kind of make some final decisions." (signals possible restructuring/closures options).
- Hobart: FL not a distraction; helpful to DKS (joint brand negotiations, access, allocation). Gupta: $100M-$125M synergy is "collective company synergy"; negotiate as "DICK'S Inc." benefiting both; "1-plus-1 definitely is accretive"; "going to take a little bit of time".

### SG&A / flow-through (Gupta)
- McShane: flow-through "has not been awesome" — Gupta: "That's fair."
- Don't view SG&A in isolation: Q2 FY26 "80 basis points of gross margin expansion" driven by DKS Media Network + GameChanger; their investment sits in SG&A, benefit in GM/comp ("geography shift"). Prioritization of investments (assets, tech, platform enhancements). "it's been running hard for some time now, it's not lost on us and we are consciously focused on that"; synergy negotiations to help SG&A intensity.

### House of Sport / Field House
- Hobart: HoS ~100,000-125,000 sq ft, climbing wall, field; "now in our fourth year"; margins/ROI "quite good", comp good; "even in year two, year three, year four, we're comping the comp". Inspired new 50,000 sq ft model (Field House, "our real core"). Brand incubator: Hoka came via HoS; On originally via Public Lands and HoS; FP Movement; Gymshark and Vuori "just coming in"; collab spaces; brands then spread across DKS banner.
- Gupta: Field Houses "doing fantastic", economic returns great; future DKS fleet = HoS or Field House. HoS test-and-learn: DKS Media Network tested in HoS now rolling; Collectors Clubhouse started in HoS → moves to Field House.
- Stack: mall developers want HoS to replace vacant Sears/Penney's/other dept stores; drives mall traffic, sales, GLA; access to A-mall real estate (Palm Beach Gardens Mall FL, Barton Creek Austin, Cerritos LA, Tysons DC); sales "phenomenal"; will continue investing.

### Fast Break prototype
- "we're never in the final stage" but "a pretty good representation right now". Initial plan 30% SKU cut; "there's more editing to be done": within the 20% of sales tail ("80/20 rule", directional) still too many SKUs; reinvest in working colors/franchises, deeper sizes, in-stock. Trying additional fixtures and more apparel → "will help drive the margin rate".

### GS four standard questions
- Income cohorts / 2H26: Hobart — consumer across country "under pressure", same in EMEA; DKS consumer "holding up very, very well" (sport, outdoors, health & wellness; World Cup showed sport+culture); no trade down best→better→good; growth across all income demographics; FL consumer "may be a little bit more under pressure" but responds to newness. Gupta: EMEA different cadence, pressure "much more in the EMEA segment compared to the US", contemplated in FY gdnc.
- Pricing 2H vs 1H: Stack — "balance of the year will be similar to the second quarter"; look quarter-to-quarter.
- 2027 margin headwinds/tailwinds: Gupta — no 2027 gdnc; balance right long-term investments (lean into HoS etc.); "clear focus and collective work... on productivity"; will monitor promo/pricing environment.
- AI 2027: Hobart — teammate efficiency (remove friction); athlete opportunity; "power of our opinion" = ScoreCard first-party data + teammate sport/product knowledge → consumer-facing "Coach by DICK'S" now embedded in DKS mobile app; "biggest input for next year"; "AI is still requiring investment"; "not doing for tech for tech's sake."
