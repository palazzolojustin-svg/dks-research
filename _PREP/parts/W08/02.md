### Teammate letter "A Message from Ed and Lauren" (distributed to DKS employees 2025-05-15)
- Headline: "DICK'S to acquire Foot Locker, Inc., expanding our reach in the global sports retail industry!"
- Definitive merger agreement to acquire FL, "a global leader in footwear and athletic apparel"; "This acquisition will redefine our business"; links to PR + brief video from Ed and Lauren.
- Timing: "we do not anticipate this deal to close until the second half of this year."
- Structure post-close: "Foot Locker will continue to operate as a separate entity, reporting directly into Ed." Until close FL independent. "Even after closing, it's important to know that it will be business as usual for all DICK'S teammates, and our strategic priorities remain the same. There are no expected changes to our operations, our team, or our strategic priorities."
- FL profile: ~2,400 retail stores in 20 countries (North America, Europe, Asia, Australia, New Zealand) + licensed store presence in Europe, Middle East, Asia; "a leader in sneaker culture for sneakerheads around the world"; similar foundational values, passion for sport.
- "We will invest in and grow Foot Locker's business to position the combined company for long-term success and profitable growth."
- Remainder: FLS/additional info/participants boilerplate (=same as 01).
