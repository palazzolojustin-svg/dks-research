### Prepared excerpts
- Gilch: Form S-4 to be filed; tentatively Q2 2025 results to be published Sep 3, 2025.
- Stack: "very strong start to the year with another quarter over a 4% comp"; FL "a truly exciting and transformational moment"; global leader serving "more types of athletes, consumers and communities"; $300B global sports retail market; >3,200 stores worldwide; ops expertise to "unlock the next chapter of growth for Foot Locker"; more important partner to brands across geographies/channels/banners; "accretive to DICK'S EPS in the first full fiscal year post close"; "clear path to unlocking meaningful cost synergies over the medium-term."
### Q&A excerpts
- Q [Brian (Nagel)]: DKS stock still down since announcement—what is market missing? A: some shareholders "would really have preferred we just continue to do what we're doing"; business strong (HoS, Field House under control); "We don't think that's right long term"; strengthens brand relationships via global presence; serves market DKS stores can't; operational efficiency to raise FL profitability; $100–125M synergies medium term; accretive in first full FY after close; compete for global sport/culture share "not just abdicate it to other retailers"; "we don't make investments or decisions for a quarter or two. We make these decisions and investments for a lifetime"; "it's up to us to prove to the street"; DKS momentum "we do not expect to be interrupted."
- Q [Simeon (Gutman)]: Nike—inventory clean-up, distribution strategy, innovation? A: Nike "very important strategic partner"; innovating on longer-term trends/pipelines; Nike "continues to perform really, really well for us"; heard of distribution changes; Nike "very good at segmenting their products"; "We expect minimal overlap with some of the new distribution"; innovation: running, lifestyle apparel, women's basketball.
- Q [Robby (Ohmes)]: share taken from FL historically? share from digital/omni; Amazon getting better Nike allocations; FTC timing? A: share gains from core product categories—apparel, footwear, team sports, some outdoor; "In terms of the FTC approval on the merger, we anticipate that would be somewhere in the second half of this year."
- Q [unidentified]: low divestiture threshold (~$100M—if forced to divest more, DKS could walk)? A: deal rationale is serving consumer DKS can't today; large divestitures inconsistent with strategy; "that's why we've got that $100 million number there."
- Q [Michael (Lasser)]: is DKS comp driven by temporary factors (HoS, unique footwear allocation)? A: growth over many quarters from 4 core strategies: (1) access to differentiated product (brand relationships; HoS/Field House enable newer/emerging brands, brand storytelling); (2) elevating athlete experience (teammates; reinventing concepts w/ HoS and "Foot Locker" [as transcribed]); (3) team & culture ("best team in sports"); (4) brand investment & Sports Matter program. "I don't at all think that our growth has been driven by unique and temporary factors."
- Q [same]: DKS already gets best allocations—can FL contribution get better? A: as global business, will partner with global brands on longer-term global product innovation; "a real win for our relationships with our brand partners."
- Q [John (Kernan)]: biggest FL operational/financial opportunity; FL op margin depressed vs history—which line items? A: early stages; extensive diligence; FL "Lace Up plan" strong aspects—reinventing stores, digital experience, marketing; DKS team stays focused on DKS; "small group of people working for Ed" to work w/ FL team "to really unlock all of that. The gross margin improvement that we know is available"; Ed's ops excellence, brand relationships, real estate development relationships; "execute the heck out of this and really drive that gro[ss] margin improvement that will drive profitability."
- Q [Lejuez]: % DKS stores in same centers as FL; % mall; customer overlap; % Nike SKUs shared w/ FL? A: won't answer most until close; "about 30% of our stores are in malls"; acquiring a different customer; access to US urban locations unavailable to large-format stores; hoped incremental to customer base. Revenue synergies? "we'll share much more detailed points when the transaction is closed."
- Q: buybacks—nearly $300M [stated "300 million"] in Q1—on hold pending close? A: "continue to be nimble and flexible"; "certain restrictions as we are in the phase of the S4 filing"; evaluate for balance of year.
