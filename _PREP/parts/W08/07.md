### Merger Agreement (dated 2025-05-15; DKS, RJS Sub LLC [NY LLC, wholly owned], Foot Locker, Inc. [NY corp])
- Merger Sub merges into FL; FL survives as wholly owned DKS sub.
- Consideration per FL common share ($0.01 par; excl. shares owned by DKS/FL/subs), at holder election, no interest: (a) $24.00 cash ("Cash Merger Consideration") or (b) 0.1168 DKS shares ("Exchange Ratio"; DKS $0.01 par). Election period ≥20 business days, ending 5 business days before DKS good-faith estimated Closing date unless agreed otherwise.
- Equity awards: (i) FL employee time-based RSUs and all PSUs → DKS time-based RSUs based on Stock Merger Consideration (performance goals deemed achieved at levels per award agreement/plan), same terms otherwise; (ii) FL non-employee director RSUs (incl. deferred units) fully vest, converted to cash at Cash Merger Consideration; (iii) FL options (vested or not) cancelled for cash = shares × (Cash consideration − exercise price); out-of-the-money options cancelled for no consideration.
- Customary reps/warranties/covenants; FL customary non-solicitation; FL board required to recommend adoption (subject to customary exceptions).
- Closing conditions: (i) adoption by holders of two-thirds of outstanding FL common stock; (ii) HSR waiting period expiration/termination + antitrust clearance in certain other jurisdictions; (iii) no Restraint (injunction/law); (iv) Form S-4 effectiveness; (v) accuracy of reps (MAE standard generally); (vi) performance of obligations in all material respects. DKS obligation also conditioned on no continuing FL Material Adverse Effect since signing.
- Financing: commitment letter dated 2025-05-15 w/ Goldman Sachs Bank USA — up to $2.4B senior bridge term loans to fund all/part of Cash Merger Consideration, repay certain FL/subsidiary indebtedness, pay fees/expenses.
- Termination: mutual consent; either party if not closed by May 15, 2026 (Outside Date), w/ two automatic 3-month extensions if only regulatory conditions unsatisfied; final non-appealable Restraint; uncured material breach (cure period earlier of 30 days after notice or business day before Outside Date). DKS may terminate if FL board changes recommendation, FL materially breaches non-solicit/proxy filing/shareholder-meeting obligations, or Shareholder Approval not obtained at meeting. FL may terminate to enter Superior Proposal after required procedures.
- Termination fees: FL pays DKS $59,500,000 (e.g., recommendation change or Superior Proposal); DKS pays FL $95,500,000 if terminated for failure to obtain requisite antitrust approvals.
- Exhibits: 2.1 Merger Agreement (schedules omitted per Item 601(a)(5)); 104 cover XBRL.
