### Top 10 questions for mgmt
1. Sneaker fashion moving away from "fashion athletic"; Nike Sportswear + Jordan Streetwear ≈ roughly 60% of NKE rev — sustainable % and how long to get there?
2. How high is inventory (balance sheet and in field) vs where it should be? DKS-READ: "Many retailers have commented that Nike inventory levels are high and this is leading to deep discounting"; how deep must discounting go to clear excess.
3. How long to clear excess inventory; how long deep discounting lasts; how much overhang constrains newness.
4. How many low-end wholesale doors will Nike add? Investors sense big push into many lower-quality wholesale doors; concern erodes premium image; may be driven by short-term revenue need; will Nike enter Decathlon? (DKS-READ: channel/door expansion risk to premium wholesale partners.)
5. Long-term Jordan strategy: Jordan taken down market into channels like US family footwear channel in a big way; risk of being seen as "mass" brand.
6. China: July — reimagining Greater China marketplace end to end starting with digital; with some exceptions across licensee partners, partner-operated online storefronts will transition out of selling Nike product; impact on sales & margins?
7. Early demand signals from new product: Pegasus Plus 2 (road running), "Ja 4" (basketball), Hybrid RN (training), Air Jordan 41, Caitlin 1 (not all on sale yet); depth/quality of innovation pipeline.
8. Supply chain: IT upgrades caused delivery-schedule disruptions in first part of CY2026; upgrades complete? Service levels normal?
9. What is mgmt most excited about — green shoots? Many data points in last 90 days suggest trends deteriorated.
10. Investor day at HQ November 16-17, 2026: FY27 gdnc (if not on 1Q call) and LT targets; drivers & confidence.
