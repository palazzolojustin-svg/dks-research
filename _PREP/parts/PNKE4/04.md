### React (10/01/26)
- Nike Greater China rev -26% [sic; other sources say -22%] and forecast of continued sales deterioration through FY2027 -> mixed near-term backdrop for China sportswear. Nike cutting unprofitable digital distribution, reducing discounting, refreshing aged physical storefronts -> could rationalize promo intensity over time. Benefits Anta & Li Ning (healthier pricing, less Nike-led inventory clearance) but raises their execution bar: must localize, sharpen positioning, upgrade retail, sustain innovation in running/performance as Nike steps up investment there.

### Anta thesis (08/26/26)
- Poised to gain China share even if sentiment lackluster into 2027; high-end Descente, Kolon Sport; Arc'teryx, Salomon, Wilson via Amer Sports stake; planned Puma stake purchase (pending regulators) adds premium asset. Core Anta brand priced ~half of Nike/Adidas.
- Post-1H: Anta better placed than Li Ning through 2027; 1H OM beat cons (Fila efficiency, mix to more profitable labels); ad-to-sales stable despite Milano-Cortina spend; 1H rev +4.8%; reaffirmed LSD retail sales growth FY gdnc (March gdnc). Li Ning trimmed rev & net-margin outlook; Li Ning must defend volume by raising expenses vs Anta using supply chain/channel optimization. Medium-term catalysts Maia Active, Jack Wolfskin, Puma (3-5 yrs). Puma stake closing this year would limit FCF.
- Overseas: Anta brand SE Asia stores 500 in 2025, another 500 planned through 2028; expand Kolon/Descente range; cons ~9% annual rev growth through 2028 (08/30/26).

### Nike China (03/31/26, 05/19/26, 09/02/26)
- Nike guided steeper-than-expected 20% decline FQ4 (May) Greater China rev despite 3Q beat (April 1 gdnc); contradicts expected sequential narrowing. Reset (cut sell-in, fewer discounts, clean digital) -> profitability can bottom before revenue; recovery slower & more product-dependent than cons. Running, tennis, golf, ACG sales in China rose -> pressure on Anta, Puma, Li Ning, Amer through 2026.
- Shang Zhi Zhen: Nike Jan-Feb online sales (Tmall, JD.com, Douyin, Kuaishou) -16% y/y, trailing multi-sport peers; Nike Jan-Feb online markdown 12%, lowest among multi-sport brands (Adidas, Anta, Fila, Li Ning); narrower discounts weakened value proposition. Anta LSD retail target for namesake brand; Li Ning implied HSD sell-through -> fragmented market.
- Pou Sheng flat sales, narrower discounting through Feb; attributed to lower inventory burden after partner buybacks last year (Nike likely among them).
- ACG: one store opened Feb, second slated May 2026; outdoor: online growth of six major outdoor brands slowed to 12% through Feb from ~35% in 2025. Anta pushing Descente, Kolon Sport, Jack Wolfskin sales to surpass namesake brand & Fila in 2026.
- Data methodology: Shang Zhi Zhen monthly online transactions since 2023; 14 sportswear brands; 4 platforms (Tmall, JD.com, Douyin, Kuaishou); includes brand flagships and distributors Topsports and Yue Yuen's Pou Sheng; Douyin since 2026 updated methodology.
- 1Q (CY26) retail sales: Adidas +17% Greater China, Fila low-teens; Anta brand & Li Ning trailed. Adidas benefits from 2023-25 China reset (running, athleisure; China-designed product). April online: Li Ning surpassed Adidas but deepest discount; Nike weakest (09/02/26).
- April online discounts (Tmall/JD/Douyin): Lululemon 6%, Nike 5%, Maia Active 9%, Adidas 14%. Lululemon Texas PFAS probe little China impact; April online receipts outgrew multisport peers.
- Outdoor (thru April): six tracked outdoor labels outpaced Nike/Adidas, led by The North Face and Kolon Sport; Kolon min 5% discount vs TNF 13%; Descente 3rd, Arc'teryx 5th (prioritizing offline). Anta stake in Amer 39.4% (05/19/26); elsewhere 41.7% (04/20/26) and 37.8% (08/11/26).
- Yue Yuen/Shenzhou: Nike reset lowered Nike China rev seven quarters through Feb 2026; Yue Yuen manufacturing sales decline accelerated to 5.5% in 1Q from 4Q 4.6%; April 21 profit warning cited cautious customer ordering & "weakening consumer momentum." Shenzhou can lean on Uniqlo.
- China clothing/footwear/accessories retail sales +9.3% 1Q; slipped to 7% in March.

### Anta-Puma (04/27/26)
- EUR1.5B Puma investment; 29.06% stake; cost based on Puma 2027 rev/EBITDA cons -> below-industry EV/sales, EV/EBITDA below Nike, Amer, On. Unconditional only in 4Q -> avoid most of Puma guided EUR50-150M EBIT loss in 2026. Puma China = 7% of global sales; DTC ~70% of Puma China sales 2025; cons Puma Greater China growth 8% in 2027; wholesale partners likely to withhold new store openings due to DTC pivot. Puma unlikely to raise margins beyond pre-pandemic in next nine months.
- Anta FCF ~16B yuan 2025; net cash ~32B yuan (>75% bank fixed deposits); 50% payout supports >7B yuan dividends 2026; self-funded 12.4B yuan payment absorbs at least 75% of annual operating cash inflow; may cut payout below 3-yr avg ~50% (2019 cut to 30.7% funding Amer deal). Approvals >6 months.
- Hyrox: Puma exclusive shoes/apparel through 2030; ~650,000 participants 2024-25, expected >1.3M this season, from 175,000 in 2023.
