### Thesis / scenarios
- Thesis: increasingly competitive environment; still correcting strategic missteps of FY20-FY24.
- Bull: rebased rev could return to growth FY27; margin recovery steeper (~100bps/yr) -> FY28 EPS ~$2.25, stock ~$55.
- Base: FY27 sales down LSD-MSD, EBIT margin -150bps largely from lapping tariff refunds in FY26 -> FY27 EPS $1.64; FY28 modest rev growth, ~50bps EBIT margin expansion -> EPS $1.80. Trades low-20s P/E on FY27 $1.64.
- Bear: rev recovery longer, more FY27 declines, flattish margins; FY28 earnings power $1.50 x mid-teens P/E -> ~$20.

### North America fundamentals
- Bloomberg ALTD "Observed Sales" (US DTC credit-card): historically 85% correlation of y/y change w/ NKE NA DTC growth. After FQ4 decel, no improvement in Q1: both quarters -17% y/y. Magnitude unreliable (actual NA DTC Q4 -6% vs ALTD -17%) but directionally accurate. Street forecasts NA DTC improving q/q to almost flattish y/y (vs Q4 -6%) -> risk of disappointment. QTD (2 wks ALTD) -16% y/y, no meaningful change.
- Jordan retro resale: pre-COVID & immediate post-pandemic 90%-100% of retro Jordans commanded resale premiums (GOAT/StockX); prior to Q4 ~50%-60%; 4Q26 28%; 1Q27 26% (brutal July, every retro release soft) -> could signal Jordan needs more rationalization.
- Nike.com sale SKU count y/y (weekly weekend count): early CY flattish (+/- few pts); FQ3 (ended Feb'26) -1% y/y; Q4 +40% y/y; Q1 +63% y/y. Headline promos bigger Jul/Aug: extra 25% off BTS vs Aug 2025 "extra 20% off" -> slow BTS start needing bigger discounts.
- Wholesale partners: DKS-READ: DKS reported Foot Locker segment comp decline of almost 4% (vs slight increase in Q1); lowered FY Foot Locker comp gdnc to flat-to-down-2% from original up-1%-to-3%; lowered FY EBIT margin gdnc ~100bps (incl 50bps cut at core Dick's banner, larger cut at Foot Locker), attributed to highly promotional environment, GM expected to decline at both segments. JD Sports Q2 NA LFL almost -7% vs Q1 <1% decline; cut FY PBT gdnc by GBP50M (~6% at midpoint).
- Promo pressure = too much NKE inventory in channel: late-CY25 big ship into NA wholesale; 4 consecutive qtrs DD NA sell-in growth (+14% y/y LTM; +14% in FY26); Nov 2025 qtr largest domestic wholesale sell-in in company history. LTM shipped $1.5B more wholesale product y/y ≈ ~$3B incremental at retail; retailer comps slowed shortly after -> insufficient demand vs supply -> excess promo at retailers.
- Wholesale discount tracking (14 retailers; sporting goods, sneaker specialty, run/outdoor specialty, family footwear): FQ1 >30% of Nike assortment discounted vs 25% in 1Q26. Foot Locker & JD saw decrease in % items on sale, yet called out slowing sales & more promo; very promotional BTS messaging on sites (not Nike-only) -> infer smaller % on sale but deeper discounts to clear old inv; breadth of discounting at FL/JD could pick up in months ahead if inv builds.
- Instagram followers: Nike -3% y/y, tied worst in coverage; On +28%, Hoka +7%.

### China
- 8 straight negative qtrs, low visibility. Late July NKE notified Topsports and YY Sports (owned by Pou Sheng Intl.) that Nike product may only be sold in stores beginning CY2027 (online limited to Nike site & select marketplaces e.g. T-Mall) for pricing integrity.
- Topsports: Nike online ~22% of FY2025 sales; rev 25.7B RMB (~$3.6B) -> ~$785M retail, just shy of $400M wholesale-equivalent. Pou Sheng: Nike online ~15% of CY25 rev; rev 17.1B RMB (~$2.4B) -> ~$355M retail, ~$180M wholesale-equivalent. Total $550-$600M lost wholesale rev ≈ almost 10% of NKE Greater China rev; ~1-pt headwind to NKE sales CY2027. Presents incremental risk over next 12-18 months.
- Pou Sheng/YY monthly sales: -10% during NKE FQ1 (Jun-Aug) vs -3% in each of prior 3 qtrs. Topsports May qtr sales -11% y/y (FQ2 not yet reported). Recent -DD declines at both.

### Upside drivers / risks
- Upside: new CEO Elliott Hill could reinvigorate brand more than expected; reduced supply of over-distributed styles -> less markdowns, higher GM; green shoots (Vomero, P-6000, Signature Basketball) gain momentum.
- Risks: longer for Hill to effect change (long product lead times); China exposure 15% of rev; weaning consumers off discounts harder -> lid on margins or hurts rev.
