## SRC 02_DKS_TRANSCRIPTS/earnings_calls/DKS_Q2-FY2025_Earnings-Call_2025-08-28.md | 2025-08-28 | Earnings call (FactSet corrected transcript) / Ed Stack (Exec Chairman), Lauren Hobart (Pres/CEO), Navdeep Gupta (EVP/CFO), Nate Gilch (Sr Dir IR) | Q2 FY2025 (13 wks ended ~2025-08-02)

Davidson), Christopher Horvers (JPM), Joe Feldman (Telsey), John Kernan (TD Cowen), Joseph Civello (Truist), Paul Lejuez (Citi), Justin Kleber (Baird), Eric Cohen (Gordon Haskett)
- IR: Q3 FY2025 results tentatively 2025-11-25.

### Stack — prepared
- Q2 comp +5%; "momentum continues to build"; "convergence of sport and culture has never been stronger"; DKS "uniquely positioned."
- Foot Locker: FL shareholders approved; all regulatory approvals received; close anticipated 2025-09-08. Creates "global leader in the sports retail industry," broader consumer set, stronger brand partnerships, "meaningfully expand our total addressable market." FL plans detail on Q3 call.

### Hobart — prepared
- Raising FY outlook: comp +2% to +3.5%; EPS $13.90–$14.50.
- Q2 comp +5%, growth in avg ticket and transactions; on top of +4.5% LY and +2% in 2023; continued share gains from online-only and omnichannel retailers. GM +>30bps; non-GAAP EPS $4.38.
- Real estate: opened 1 HoS in Q2; expect 13 in Q3 = highest HoS openings in a single quarter; ~16 HoS in 2025 → ~35 at YE. Field House: 4 in Q2; 6 more in Q3; ~15 total in 2025 (vs ~16 said on Q1 call) → ~42 at YE. Driving "powerful financial results."
- Key categories: in Q2 more athletes purchased, more frequently, spent more per trip vs LY; strong brand partner product pipeline.
- E-com ("multibillion-dollar, highly profitable") again grew faster than company overall; app drives launch culture/sell-through. Stores building athlete-centric service and selling culture.
- Harnessing athlete data; GameChanger, DMN long-term growth. Enthusiastic about DICK'S and Golf Galaxy growth and FL opportunities; confident in deal's strategic/financial benefits.

### Gupta — Q2 results
- Net sales +5% to $3.65B; comp +5%; 2-yr stack 9.5%, 3-yr stack 11.5%; avg ticket +4.1%, transactions +0.9%; broad-based strength across key categories.
- Gross profit $1.35B, 37.06% of sales, +33bps y/y: higher merch margin + occupancy leverage on higher sales. (Q&A: merch margin +18bps.)
- Non-GAAP SG&A +9.9% to $864M, deleveraged 105bps vs LY non-GAAP — expected; strategic investments digital, in-store, marketing.
- Pre-opening $12.3M, +$3.4M y/y.
- Non-GAAP OI $475M, 13.02% vs $480.5M, 13.83% Q2 2024. Non-GAAP EBT $472.6M, 12.96% vs $482.3M, 13.89% LY.
- Non-GAAP EPS $4.38 vs $4.37 LY. GAAP EPS $4.71: incl non-cash gains on non-operating investment in FL stock, and merger/integration and financing costs re pending FL acquisition.
- Cash & equivalents ~$1.2B; no borrowings on new $2B unsecured credit facility [was $1.6B]. Inventory +7.1% y/y; well-positioned entering Q3.
- Q2 net capex $213M; dividends $96M. [No buyback mentioned for Q2.]

### Q&A
- Q [Nagel, Oppenheimer]: FL just reported much weaker results — revitalization plan/timing? (Stack's line dropped; he was in Europe visiting FL stores.) A [Hobart]: "tremendous opportunity"; good for consumers, employees, vendors, shareholders; increasingly optimistic after time at HQs and in stores with "Stripers" (FL store staff); team "really, really wants to win." Plan: work with brand partners (excited about turnaround, sharing strong insights); invest in stores; invest in marketing; core merchandising opportunities; apparel opportunities; new assortment of products. Details on Q3 call.
- Q [Nagel]: tariff mitigation; demand impact of price increases? A [Hobart]: raised top/bottom-line gdnc including all tariff impacts seen; Q2 GM expanded; "We've seen some sporadic price increases, but they are surgical and not across the board"; consumer responding well (5% comp); increasing guidance "and our gross margin for the back half."
- Q [Gutman, MS]: 2H comp assumptions — consumer slowing? A [Hobart]: "We are not seeing any signs of slowdown with the consumer"; growth across all key segments: footwear, apparel, team sports, golf; innovation/newness energizing consumer; technicity in hardlines and softlines — bat launches, apparel, technical running, new running constructs; took 2H comp assumptions up. A [Gupta; transcript mislabels as Gutman]: macro "continues to remain dynamic" → range gdnc; inventory well positioned for 2H.
- Q [Gutman]: Q2 merch margin, GM expansion level; medium-term SG&A leverage threshold. A [Gupta]: Q2 GM +33bps, merch margin +18bps; drivers: assortment quality, favorable mix from core categories, early benefits from GameChanger and DMN; same 3 drivers for 2H FY GM expansion. Not sharing LT rubric; "we believe that we can deliver a kind of a leverage on the SG&A at a low single digits comp," balanced vs margin outlook and SG&A investments (GC, DMN, personalization, RFID).
- Q [Yih, Barclays]: athleisure/perf footwear pressured YTD, wholesale outperforming brand DTC, FL makes DKS bigger for Nike — balance of power shifting? Comp from price increases? Spring pricing? A [Hobart]: growth across footwear, apparel, team sports, golf; with FL, "even more important brand to our wholesale partners" — part of thesis (LT insight sharing, trend identification, product development); "I don't know why I would say the balance of power would shift"; relationships very strong; high-heat footwear, bats, innovation. A [Gupta]: "almost 1% of our comp came from the increase in transaction, and the remaining came from the overall basket increase"; basket drivers: innovation/newness affinity, more units per transaction from in-store selling, product mix. Won't break out Q3/Q4; spring/2026 plan in progress with national and vertical brands.
- Q [Ohmes, BofA]: online vs store shift; BTS; promotions? A [Hobart]: strength across board; e-com outperforming but "incredibly strong growth in all of our channels" — "not a shift"; BTS feels "terrific," much of it falls in Q3 (footwear, apparel, backpacks, lunchboxes); "promotional activity hasn't been an enormous factor for us"; surgical; no trade-down best→better→good; growth across all income demographics.
- Q [Lasser, UBS]: stock under pressure on GM language change from explicit +75bps to "up" — realistic GM and why less? A [Gupta]: still expect FY GM expansion; balancing puts/takes: tariffs, keeping inventory "really vibrant" (driving 6 straight quarters >4% comp), dynamic pricing/promo landscape; balanced vs SG&A; at high end still op margin +10bps (consistent with prior). GM drivers: GC, DMN, "predominantly" differentiated assortment.
- Q [Lasser]: lower stock price → more shares for FL deal; still accretive in first year? A [Gupta]: FL shareholder election (stock vs cash) on the 29th [Aug] determines consideration mix → level of accretion; post-Sept 8 detailed evaluation; confident in $100M–$125M synergies; outlook on Q3 call.
- Q [Baker, D.A. Davidson]: GC stats. A [Hobart]: Q2 7.4M unique active users; on average 5.5M monthly active users in app, +16% y/y [as stated]; >$100M revenue last year; on track for almost 50% revenue growth [FY2025]. DKS-GC closer: Bat Lab live experience rating new equipment with top athletes; sharing data for personalization; GC huge piece of DMN (live sports, engaged parents/grandparents). A [Gupta]: Q1 = first-ever brand advertising for GameChanger (brand awareness opportunity).
- Q [Baker]: FL synergy change/revenue synergies; required divestitures? A [Hobart]: too soon; "not changing the synergy numbers" $100M–$125M; more on Q3. [Divestitures not directly answered.]
- Q [Horvers, JPM]: ~11M shares issuable; still EPS-accretive with full share conversion or only OI-dollar accretive? A [Gupta]: multiple moving pieces: share count, synergy expectation and timing, core business performance; "continue to remain confident that this would be accretive," level depends on consideration mix; deeper outlook on Q3 and Q4 calls.
- Q [Horvers]: implied 2H comp = sharp deceleration — caution? A [Hobart]: balancing momentum with "appropriate caution" on uncertain macro; tariffs included; lapping 2H comps "50 basis points stronger than the first half"; "We never guide to the highest possible outcome." A [Gupta]: Q4 FY2024 comp was 6.4%, contemplated; 2H outlook raised vs prior.
- Q [Feldman, Telsey]: where innovation is. A [Hobart]: consumers leaning into innovation/technical performance: Nike running construct "doing really well"; hardlines; "tremendous excitement in the license category"; small trading card tests doing very well; sport and culture intertwined.
- Q [Feldman]: AI/computer vision/ML. A [Hobart]: SG&A investment in marketing and technical tools: digital (e-com, GC), marketing stack personalization, RFID teammate tools to find products and ship faster; AI embedded: AI-enabled "supercharged search" on e-com, teammate scheduling, product and merch assortment planning; "early innings with AI"; drives productivity, frees teammates for sales/service.
- Q [Kernan, TD Cowen]: footwear cycle; consumer absorbing price increases. A [Hobart]: footwear "very, very strong"; "some selective price increases"; DKS and brands surgical on minor increases to offset tariffs; no consumer issue with small price increases at 5% comp; "incredible demand for footwear."
- Q [Kernan]: tariff COGS/GM impact 2H and 1H26. A [Gupta]: "minimal impact from tariffs in Q2"; small impact in 2H contemplated in outlook; FY2026 later; active work with manufacturers, national brands, pricing/promo.
- Q [Civello, Truist]: HoS/Field House traffic vs chain; ticket vs transactions outlook. A [Gupta]: don't break out; enthusiastic on HoS/Field House performance on traffic and basket; resonating with brand partners; won't guide ticket/transactions; comp growth consistently from both.
- Q [Civello]: retail media scaling 2025/2026. A [Hobart]: DMN increasingly powerful via data/reporting automation; endemic and non-endemic partners excited; "early innings"; not broken out for 2025 or 2026; LT growth, profitability, margin and revenue driver; unique access to youth/live sports via GC. A [Gupta]: DMN + HoS/Field House intersection: activate endemic and non-endemic brands in-store; GC + stores + online.
- Q [Lejuez, Citi]: private brand performance; tariff cost increases on direct imports and national brands; maintain margin % or profit $? A [Hobart]: vertical brands doing very well, esp. flagship apparel DSG, CALIA, VRST — meeting need not covered elsewhere, incremental; vertical brands 700–900bps higher margin than average national brand; DKS is #1 or #2 vendor to itself in key categories: accessories, athletic apparel, fitness, golf, team sports. A [Gupta]: "a little bit of an increase in prices" seen on both vertical and national brands; surgical, flexible pricing balancing top-line momentum vs profitability; contemplated in 2H outlook. [Margin % vs $ not directly answered.]
- Q [Kleber, Baird]: vertical brands into FL stores? A [Hobart]: too soon, not closed; "a lot of merchandising opportunities," apparel opportunities at FL; vertical brands "way too soon to tell."
- Q [Kleber]: raise = 1H beat flow-through only? A [Hobart]: took up 2H assumptions modestly. A [Gupta]: flowed through Q2 beat vs internal expectations and modestly raised 2H comp expectations.
- Q [Cohen, Gordon Haskett]: post-FL footwear concentration risk? A [Hobart]: "Footwear is the engine that pulls the train"; "the outfit starts with the footwear"; key for performance and sport lifestyle; serving different consumers at DKS and FL banners.
- Q [Cohen]: HoS productivity across market sizes as openings accelerate? A [Gupta]: very happy; "even some of the smaller markets are able to support the House of Sport locations very, very productively" → expands HoS opportunity over next few years.
- Close [Hobart]: welcome to new Foot Locker teammates; start after Sept 8.
