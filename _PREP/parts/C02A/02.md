- IR: Foot Locker (FL) deal — S-4 registration statement to be filed; DKS/FL directors may be deemed proxy solicitation participants. Q2 FY2025 results tentatively 2025-09-03. Q1 results had been previewed ~2 weeks earlier (with FL announcement).

### Stack — Foot Locker acquisition (announced 2025-05-15)
- "very strong start to the year with another quarter over a 4% comp." FL deal = "truly exciting and transformational moment."
- Rationale: sports/culture convergence; admired FL brand and its sneaker-culture community; create "global leader in the sports retail industry" serving more athletes/consumers/communities; participate in $300B global sports retail market; >3,200 stores worldwide combined; apply DKS operational expertise to unlock FL's next chapter of growth; more important partner to leading sports brands (larger, connected platform across geographies/channels/banners).
- Expected accretive to DKS EPS in first full fiscal year post close; "clear path to unlocking meaningful cost synergies over the medium term."

### Hobart — prepared
- Q1 comp +4.5%; 5th straight quarter >4% comp. Growth in both avg ticket and transactions: more athletes purchased, more frequently, spent more per trip. GM +>40bps on higher merch margin; non-GAAP EPS $3.37, ahead of LY.
- Macro: "increasingly complex macroeconomic environment, one shaped by shifting trade policies and a more cautious consumer mindset"; operating from position of strength. Acquired >20M new athletes over past 3 years.
- Reaffirmed FY2025 gdnc, incl expected impact of all tariffs currently in effect: comp +1% to +3% (midpoint ≈ nearly 10% 3-yr comp stack); EPS $13.80–$14.40.
- Three growth areas: (1) repositioning real estate/store portfolio; (2) strong growth in key categories; (3) accelerating e-commerce.
- HoS: opened 2 in Q1 + 1 early May; ~16 total in 2025. Field House: 4 in Q1 + 2 a few weeks ago; ~16 total in 2025 [note: Q3 FY24 call said ~20 Field House for 2025]. Response "incredibly positive."
- Categories: access to top-tier national and emerging brand product + premium in-store/digital experience → robust demand, strong sell-through on launches.
- E-commerce: "multibillion dollar, highly profitable"; opportunity to gain share from online-only and omnichannel retailers; investing aggressively in technology and marketing to drive consideration of dicks.com. Q1 e-com growth strong, again outpaced total company growth. In-app capabilities key to launch success; biggest diamond sport launches ever in Q1.
- GameChanger and DICK'S Media Network (DMN): both delivering strong, profitable growth as they scale. GC Q1: >6.5M unique active users; avg ~2.2M daily active users, nearly +28% y/y.

### Gupta — Q1 results
- Net sales +5.2% to $3.17B. Comp +4.5% (gaining share from online-only and omnichannel retailers); 2-yr stack 9.8%, 3-yr stack 13.4%. Avg ticket +3.7%, transactions +0.8%. Vertical brands led by DSG, CALIA, VRST resonating.
- Gross profit $1.17B, 36.7% of sales, +41bps y/y on higher merch margin.
- Non-GAAP SG&A +7% to $791.2M, deleveraged 42bps vs LY non-GAAP — expected; strategic investments digital, in-store, marketing; partly offset by lower incentive comp vs PY.
- Pre-opening $13.4M, -$7.7M y/y, in line.
- Non-GAAP OI $360.4M, 11.35% of sales vs $334.5M, 11.08% Q1 2024.
- Non-GAAP other income (mainly interest income) $13.3M, -$8.3M y/y (lower cash, lower rate environment).
- Non-GAAP EBT $361.6M, 11.39% vs $342.4M, 11.34% Q1 2024.
- Tax rate ~24% vs 19.6% LY (+~440bps): PY benefited from higher deductions on greater employee equity award exercises, ~+$0.19 to Q1 2024 EPS vs current year.
- Non-GAAP EPS $3.37, +2.1% vs $3.30 LY. GAAP EPS $3.24, incl non-cash losses on non-operating investment in Foot Locker stock.
- Cash & equivalents ~$1B; no borrowings on $1.6B unsecured credit facility. Inventory +12% y/y; "well positioned"; deliberate investment in key items/categories fueling sales.
- Q1 net capex $242M; dividends $100M; repurchased 1.4M shares for $298.7M at avg $218.65.

### Q&A
- Q [Nagel, Oppenheimer]: stock down since FL announcement — what is market missing? A [Stack]: some shareholders would prefer DKS "just continued to do what we're doing" (HoS, Field House under control); "We don't think that's right long-term for the business." FL: strengthens brand relationships via global presence; serves market segment DKS stores can't; bring operational efficiency, raise FL profitability; $100M–$125M synergies over medium term; accretive in first full fiscal year after close. Sport/culture intersecting globally; won't "abdicate" share to other retailers. "we don't make investments or decisions for a quarter or two. We make these decisions and investments for a lifetime"; up to DKS to prove it; DKS momentum not expected to be interrupted.
- Q [Nagel]: tariff plans with brand partners. A [Hobart]: 5 consecutive quarters >4% comp; consumer held up well; prioritizing activities, healthy lifestyle, team sports, running, walking, outdoors with kids. Q1 "no trade down from best to better to better to good"; growth across all income demographics; growth in ticket and transactions. All known tariffs factored in; affirming top/bottom-line gdnc and 75bps GM improvement; "incredibly dynamic pricing ability."
- Q [Gutman, MS]: 5 straight +4% comps but gdnc 1–3% — why? A [Hobart]: consumer "incredibly strong"; lapping higher comps in back half is a factor; business resilient, people prioritizing these categories with available income; expect that through year.
- Q [Gutman]: Nike assessment — marketplace inventory cleanup, distribution, innovation. A [Hobart]: very important strategic partner; working on longer-term consumer trends/product pipelines; Nike "continues to perform really, really well for us." Re distribution changes: Nike good at segmenting; "We expect minimal overlap with some of the new distribution"; excited about innovation: running construct, lifestyle apparel, women's basketball.
- Q [Yih, Barclays]: price increases needed at 30% China/10% elsewhere ~low-to-mid single digit; own brands vs brand-driven. A [Hobart]: with known tariffs can manage; pricing assessed down to item/SKU level based on demand and profitability; "very advanced pricing capability," real-time decisions; GM +75bps at midpoint.
- Q [Yih]: inventory pull-forward, when tariff costs hit P&L, units vs dollars. A [Gupta]: Q1 inventory +12% = differentiated inventory driving top line; focused investment end of Q4 bringing spring products earlier "worked really well" → outsized comp. Inventory growth expected to moderate even with anticipated tariff headwinds, as lapping 2H24 investments.
- Q [Ohmes, BofA]: share taken from FL historically; shift in share sources; Amazon getting better Nike allocation; FTC timing. A [Hobart]: US TAM $140B; DKS only 8% market share; gaining share from digital and omnichannel channels; brands can present whole brand head-to-toe incl gear/equipment; rooted in sport. A [Gupta]: share gains in core categories: apparel, footwear, team sports, some outdoor. FTC approval anticipated in 2H 2025. A [Hobart] re Amazon: Nike and all brands segment well; "We expect minimal overlap."
- Q [Baker, D.A. Davidson]: affiliate investment in Unrivaled Sports; impact on GC; in gdnc? A [Hobart]: GC >$100M revenue last year, growing to $150M [FY2025], highly profitable SaaS; data across athlete journey (sign-up, play, fans/parents watching scores/stats) — "incredibly rich database"; GC = live sports media platform feeding DMN. Unrivaled: on-the-ground youth sport experiences e.g., Cooperstown All Star Village; hosts 600,000 youth athletes, 2M families/yr. A [Gupta]: youth sports opportunity beyond game day; equity investment/partnership with Unrivaled + GC → holistic ecosystem; $40B TAM growing well. [Gdnc inclusion not directly answered.]
- Q [Baker]: Nike tried Amazon before and pulled back — why different? A [Hobart]: won't speak for Nike; Nike has effort "to clean up the marketplace," driver of current moves.
- Q [McShane, GS]: category performance and monthly cadence. A [Hobart]: growth in footwear, apparel, team sports; February cold and wet early, improved; strength in each month.
- Q [Horvers, JPM]: tariffed items received/priced yet? A [Gupta]: no tariff impact in Q1; working with brand partners on cadence/flow; timing contemplated in gdnc; still confident in 75bps GM expansion.
- Q [Horvers]: FL merger agreement divestiture threshold ~$100M (can walk if forced to divest more) — why low? A [Stack]: deal's purpose is to serve a consumer DKS can't serve today; large divestitures inconsistent with strategy → $100M threshold.
- Q [Feldman, Telsey]: Golf Galaxy/golf trends. A [Hobart]: golf important, compelling LT growth; 2024 rounds played at all-time high; reinventing with Golf Galaxy Performance Center (GGPC: lessons, fittings, immersive): 27 GGPC going to 35 this year. DKS is its own #1 vendor in golf via vertical brands; shout-out to Ben Griffin playing Maxfli ball, doing well in two PGA TOUR events.
- Q [Feldman]: GC user / DKS shopper crossover. A [Hobart]: crossover users among "absolute best shoppers," highly engaged, gold (ScoreCard Gold) members; driving GC sign-up among DKS shoppers and DKS purchase options for GC users; DMN enables in-game advertising on live sport platform, highly targeted; "early innings." A [Gupta]: Q1 Bat Lab content series: 20 high school/collegiate players tested 12 BBCOR bats using GC app, qualitative + quantitative scoring, data published on DKS website.
- Q [Lasser, UBS]: perception comp driven by temporary factors (HoS, unique footwear allocation)? A [Hobart]: growth ongoing many quarters from LT strategies; four core strategies: (1) access to differentiated product across the board (HoS/Field House bring newer product, emerging brands, brand storytelling); (2) elevating athlete experience (teammates; reinventing concepts with HoS "and Foot Locker"); (3) team/culture — "best team in sports"; (4) brand investment & Sports Matter program. "I don't at all think that our growth has been driven by unique and temporary factors."
- Q [Lasser]: how much better can footwear allocations get? A [Hobart]: "it can always get better"; premium full-service footwear decks now 90% of stores, will continue. A [Gupta]: head-to-toe look, team sports and accessories = differentiation for athletes and brand partners → drives differentiated allocation. A [Hobart]: growth/newness/launches across all business incl diamond sports, trading cards.
- Q [Lasser]: (clarified) can FL contribution to allocations get much better? A [Hobart]: as global business, partnering with global brands on longer-term global product innovation; "real win for our relationships with our brand partners."
- Q [Kernan, TD Cowen]: biggest operational/financial opportunity in FL; op margin depressed vs history. A [Hobart]: early stages but extensive due diligence; FL's Lace Up plan has strong aspects (reinventing stores, digital experience, marketing) to keep driving; DKS team stays fully focused on DKS; "small group of people working for Ed" with FL team to unlock "gross margin improvement that we know is available"; Stack brings retail/operational expertise, brand and real estate development relationships.
- Q [Kernan]: square footage growth (~35 HoS, low-40s Field House by YE; sq ft ~+5% y/y in Q1). A [Gupta]: HoS LT 75–100; ~35 HoS by end FY2025, just over 40 Field House; sq ft growth ~2% or slightly north depending on new stores; anticipate ~20 HoS openings in 2026; all new stores and DICK'S relocations will open as Field House (reimagined DICK'S 50k). Low-single-digit sq ft growth "generally in that range."
- Q [Lejuez, Citi]: FL overlap — co-located centers, mall %, customer overlap, Nike SKU overlap. A [Hobart]: too early, will share after close; ~30% of DKS stores in malls; FL brings different customer, US urban locations DKS large format can't access; hoped incremental to customer base. Q: revenue synergies? A [Gupta]: details after close.
- Q [Kleber, Baird]: GM from +40bps Q1 to +75bps FY despite occupancy deleverage from comp moderation? A [Gupta]: Q1 +41bps from merch margin: differentiated product, pricing/promo optimization, vertical brands which carry 700–900bps higher margin [Q3 FY24 call said 600–800bps]; same 3 drivers for rest of year plus 2 new drivers — GameChanger and DICK'S Media Network.
- Q [Kleber]: ~$300M buybacks in Q1 — on hold during acquisition? A [Gupta]: "nimble and flexible"; certain restrictions during S-4 filing phase; will evaluate for balance of year.
- Q [Matuszewski, Jefferies]: trimming assortment to offset tariffs? A [Hobart]: no — not a stated strategy; full range opening price point to best performance; optimize inventory to athlete needs.
- Q [Matuszewski]: GC free vs paid mix. A [Gupta]: strong engagement across free and paid; DMN monetizes free users; team upsells/cross-sells across active database; confident in "40% to 50% growth that we have been driving on a top line basis" on GC [Q3 FY24 call said 30–40% CAGR].
