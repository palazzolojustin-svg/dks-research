### Key Topic: Hoka (02/24-25/26)
- Crown jewel; 1.1% share of $174B global sports-footwear market [note: $179B in Focus Idea section]; DTC + wholesale share gains + diversification beyond running -> mid-to-high teens annual sales growth through FY27.
- On track to >$2.5B brand in FY26. Gains more balanced wholesale/DTC this yr: selective wholesale door additions, franchise refreshes skew near-term to wholesale. Intl: China + Europe largest opportunities (wholesale + new stores).
- Market $174B, Euromonitor may reach $223B by 2030. Europe = majority of Hoka intl sales; China greatest opportunity (recent focus); marketing activations.
- US: top-10 sports-footwear brand in 2023 (topping Asics and Brooks, tied Under Armour for 10th); 7th largest in 2025 (Euromonitor); only 3.6% US share; room via store footprint, lifestyle.
- DTC leads LT, wholesale skews near term: wholesale could outpace DTC this yr (sell-in, incremental store additions, reorders as sell-through > shipments); DTC could reach 50% of brand sales; e-com majority of DTC; select retail stores in key markets drive awareness/online.
- Beyond running: biggest near-term = hiking, lifestyle, apparel; trail/hike/lower-profile lifestyle silhouettes, more versatile Mafate + Speedgoat formats; apparel -> broader wholesale footprint + shelf space with premium marketplace discipline; selective collaborations/fashion releases.
- Running core: everyday runners via franchise innovation; recently gained share in US road-running >$140; returned to #1 position in run specialty; double-digit sell-through growth + rising reorders intl; cleaner marketplace/launch cadence vs intensifying max-cushion competition.

### Key Topic: UGG (02/25-27/26)
- FY26 at least HSD growth; potential to top cons +6.9%; men's catalyst.
- LT sustainable MSD-HSD growth; FY26 moderates from LY +13%; "365 strategy" reduces seasonality; sneakers, sandals, transitional, men's; wholesale benefits from improved in-stocks + disciplined allocation; DTC to reaccelerate via loyalty/CRM.
- Spangenberg (ex-Nike chief merchant), president since mid-2022: product diversification, franchise evolution, tight marketplace mgmt creating scarcity -> high full-price sell-through; brand heat all-time high this yr.
- Quote (Dave Powers, CEO, 1Q23 call Jul 28 2022) on Spangenberg: "she is an exceptional merchant, first and foremost" (3 yrs in China at Nike; merchandising/storytelling).
- Simplified assortment: reduced style count, inventory concentrated behind core franchises; clogs, sandals, sneakers; trend styles Quill ballet sneaker, Ellis loafer.
- Men's: ~1/3 of UGG customer base; men's outpacing women's recent qtrs; all-gender Lowmel sneaker; men's weather hybrid collection; UGG only 1.4% of US men's footwear market, less abroad.

### Key Topic: Margins (04/15-20/26)
- FY26 GM/OM ease modestly (tariffs, freight, wholesale shift); EBIT margin >21% preserved via full-price, phased price increases, opex discipline; DTC (esp. Hoka) = margin lever.
