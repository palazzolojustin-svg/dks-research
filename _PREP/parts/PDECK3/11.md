### Thesis
- "We see a very good opportunity to buy shares in a growth company we believe is significantly undervalued by the market." Solid 4Q26 raises conviction. Expect DECK to positively surprise over NTM via Hoka + UGG rev; market to realize +HSD% sales / +LDD% EPS 5-yr CAGRs -> FY2 P/E to 17x from 14x.
- Differs from market (1): growth potential. Market thinks Hoka has little growth; co introduced new LT Hoka rev guide = +LDD% 4-yr CAGR; Hoka in large growing global athletic footwear industry with modest share. Buybacks $1.08B in FY26 (7% of share count) vs $567M FY25; "We believe $1B+ is the new annual buyback rate." UBS models 9%/12.5% 5-yr sales/EPS CAGRs.
- (2) brand equity: market likely views 2H-weighted FY27 guide negatively (brands losing momentum); UBS disagrees: 4Q26 GM +85bps y/y despite tariffs + tough compares; GM +740bps over last 3 yrs; inventory end-4Q26 -2% y/y despite +5% 1Q27 sales guide + tariffs -> both brands healthy.
- Reverse DCF: market pricing +HSD-LDD% 5-yr EPS CAGR vs UBS ~12.5%. Thesis map otherwise =same as SRC 04 except: Hoka US DTC choppiness (Bondi 9/Clifton 10 transition, tariff price increases) "weighed" on FY26 (past tense); EBIT margins ~22% range through FY30E (prior 22-23%).
