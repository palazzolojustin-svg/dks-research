### Item 7.01 – comp sales method change (eff. FY2025)
- Comp definition: growth vs PY period for recurring revenue sources: same-store sales + digital revenue (eCommerce, omni-channel txns fulfilled by stores, GameChanger subscriptions, DICK'S media network).
- Pre-FY25: Warehouse Sale stores expected temporary → excluded from comps. Now determined operating longer than expected → from FY25 included in comps beginning store's 14th full month of operations (same as other stores); also now included in disclosed store counts + sq ft.
- Ex 99.2 = FY24 quarterly/FY comps recast on new method; to be posted at investors.dicks.com and used as historical comps going forward.
