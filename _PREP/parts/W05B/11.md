## SRC 01_DKS_SEC_FILINGS/8-K/exhibits/DKS_8-K_2025-05-28_EX-99-1_EX-99-1.md | 2025-05-28 | 8-K Ex 99.1 Q1 FY2025 earnings press release (Ed Stack Exec Chairman; Lauren Hobart Pres & CEO) | Q1 FY25 (13 wks ended 2025-05-03) vs Q1 FY24 (13 wks ended 2024-05-04)
### Headlines
- Record Q1 sales; comp +4.5%; EBT margin 11.0%, non-GAAP EBT margin 11.4%; reaffirmed FY25 comp + EPS outlook (A).
- Diluted EPS $3.24; non-GAAP $3.37; vs $3.30 PY (no PY non-GAAP adj).
- Q1 opened 2 new House of Sport + 4 new DICK'S Field House locations.
- (A) Outlook excludes acquisition-related costs, investment losses, and Foot Locker results.
### Quotes
- "Earlier this month, we announced our plans to acquire Foot Locker, a move that represents a truly exciting and transformational moment for DICK'S. For many years we've admired Foot Locker's brand and the powerful community they've built in sneaker culture. By bringing our two great brands together, we see the opportunity to create a global leader in the sports retail industry by serving a broader set of athletes." —Ed Stack
- "Our Q1 comps increased 4.5% driven by growth in both average ticket and in transactions and this was our fifth straight quarter with comps over 4.0%. Our first quarter gross margin expanded and we delivered non-GAAP EPS ahead of the prior year. We are reaffirming our 2025 outlook, which reflects our strong start to the year and confidence in our strategies and operational strength while still acknowledging the dynamic macroeconomic environment." —Lauren Hobart
