## SRC 01_DKS_SEC_FILINGS/8-K/DKS_8-K_2025-05-15_Foot-Locker-merger-agreement-entered.md | 2025-05-15 | 8-K Item 1.01 Merger Agreement w/ Foot Locker (Rule 425); signed Navdeep Gupta EVP-CFO | deal terms
- Acc# 0001193125-25-120951. Parties: DKS (DE), RJS Sub LLC (NY LLC, wholly owned DKS sub, Merger Sub), Foot Locker, Inc. (NY corp). Merger Sub merges into FL; FL survives as wholly owned DKS sub.
### Consideration
- Each FL common share ($0.01 par; excl shares held by DKS/FL/subs) converts, at holder's ELECTION, into (a) $24.00 cash ("Cash Merger Consideration") or (b) 0.1168 DKS common shares ("Exchange Ratio"/Stock Merger Consideration), no interest. Election period ≥20 business days ending 5 business days before DKS good-faith estimate of Closing date (unless agreed otherwise).
- Equity awards: (i) FL employee time-based RSUs + all PSUs → DKS time-based RSUs based on Stock Merger Consideration (PSU goals deemed achieved per award agreement/plan), same terms otherwise; (ii) FL non-employee director RSUs (incl deferred units) fully vest → cash at Cash Merger Consideration; (iii) FL options (vested or not) cancelled → cash = shares × (Cash Merger Consideration − exercise price); out-of-the-money cancelled for nothing.
- Customary reps/warranties/covenants; FL customary non-solicit; FL board required to recommend adoption (subject to exceptions).
### Closing conditions
- (i) Adoption by holders of two-thirds of outstanding FL common; (ii) HSR waiting period expiry/termination + antitrust clearance in certain other jurisdictions; (iii) no Restraint (injunction/law); (iv) DKS Form S-4 effective; (v) accuracy of reps (MAE standard generally); (vi) material performance of obligations. DKS obligation also conditioned on no continuing FL Material Adverse Effect since signing.
### Financing
- Commitment letter 2025-05-15 w/ Goldman Sachs Bank USA: up to $2.4B senior bridge term loans to fund all/portion of Cash Merger Consideration, repay certain FL/sub indebtedness, pay fees/expenses; subject to customary conditions.
### Termination
- Mutual consent; either party if: (i) no Closing by 2026-05-15 ("Outside Date"), with two automatic 3-month extensions if all conditions except regulatory satisfied/waived; (ii) final non-appealable Restraint; (iii) uncured material breach by other party (cure period earlier of 30 days after notice and business day before Outside Date; terminating party not in material breach).
- DKS may terminate if FL board changes recommendation, FL materially breaches non-solicit/proxy filing/shareholder-meeting obligations, or FL shareholder approval not obtained at a meeting where vote taken.
- FL may terminate to enter definitive agreement for a Superior Proposal after specified procedures.
- Termination fees: FL pays DKS $59,500,000 in specified circumstances (incl FL board recommendation change, Superior Proposal entry). DKS pays FL $95,500,000 (reverse fee) if terminated for failure to obtain requisite antitrust approvals.
