## SRC 01_DKS_SEC_FILINGS/8-K/exhibits/DKS_8-K_2025-05-15_EX-99-2_EX-99-2.md | 2025-05-15 | 8-K Ex 99.2 Joint DKS/Foot Locker press release (Ed Stack, Lauren Hobart, Mary Dillon FL CEO) | FL acquisition announcement
### Headline terms
- DKS to acquire Foot Locker (NYSE: FL) → "global leader in the sports retail industry". Implied equity value ~$2.4B; EV ~$2.5B.
- FL holders elect $24.00 cash or 0.1168 DKS sh per FL sh; no min/max on cash or stock. Merger agreement unanimously approved by both boards.
- $24.00 = ~66% premium to FL 60-trading-day VWAP (based on FL close 5/14/2025). Total consideration ~6.1x FY2024 adjusted EBITDA.
- Financing: cash on hand + new debt. Goldman Sachs = DKS financial advisor + fully committed bridge financing; Wachtell Lipton = DKS legal; Evercore = FL financial advisor; Skadden = FL legal.
- Subject to FL shareholder approval + customary conditions incl regulatory; expected close 2H 2025. Deal call 9:00am ET 2025-05-15.
- Expected accretive to DKS EPS in first full fiscal year post-close (excl transaction and other one-time costs to achieve synergies); $100–125M cost synergies in medium term via procurement + direct sourcing efficiencies.
### Foot Locker profile
- Brands: Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs Sports, WSS, atmos. ~2,400 retail stores in 20 countries (North America, Europe, Asia, Australia, NZ) + licensed stores in Europe, Middle East, Asia. 2024 net worldwide sales $8B.
- DKS to operate FL as standalone business unit within portfolio and maintain FL brands.
### Strategic rationale (as stated)
- Global platform in growing sports retail industry; expands DKS addressable market; new U.S. locations via FL complementary real estate + first-time international presence; strong long-term industry tailwinds.
- Broader consumer set: performance-focused athletes to sneakerheads; builds on DICK'S House of Sport and FL "Reimagined" concept store learnings for immersive retail.
- Stronger partner for key brands; multiple platforms for established + emerging brands, global visibility.
- Invest in FL brand; growth via differentiated store concepts + robust digital.
- Operational efficiencies (synergies/accretion above).
