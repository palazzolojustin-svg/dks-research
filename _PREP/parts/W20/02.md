## SRC 05_SECTOR_MACRO/2026-02-23_BloombergIntel_SECTOR_Sneakerinas_En_Pointe_to_Reshape_2026_Footwear_Market_Share.md | 2026-02-23 (page printed 10/05/2026; some sections dated 02/19/26 and 07/23/26) | Bloomberg Intelligence, Abigail Gilmartin (BI Industry Analyst), Poonam Goyal (BI Sr Industry Analyst); contributing Sydney Goodman | Footwear 2026 trends
- Thesis: shift to slimmer, fashion-forward sneaker silhouettes in 2026 benefits large lifestyle incumbents (esp Adidas, Puma); opportunity for Nike to rebuild share if innovation gains traction; On, Hoka, Salomon rightly prioritize technical credibility over trend participation.
- 1. Nike/Adidas/Puma can regain share as preferences shift to aesthetic, mixed-use styles (slimmer profiles, sneakerinas, clogs) — scale, distribution, lifestyle authority; in selective spending backdrop helps demand & full-price sell-through. On/Hoka stay focused on technical performance, measured lifestyle franchises, limited releases for heat.
- 2. Chart "US Footwear Market Share by Brand (%)" (values not extracted). Lifestyle rotation may aid incumbents, test niche brands.
- 3. Adidas share gains: built on Samba & Gazelle with on-trend Tokyo & Taekwondo; Puma Speedcat resonating (slim, low-profile). Nike experimenting with ballet-inspired "sneakerina" concepts, slimmer profiles, new Mind franchise (sensory engagement); slower in lifestyle — no truly scaled on-trend lifestyle franchise since Air Force 1 — but inroads in core performance.
- 4. Athletic brands expanding into recovery clogs, sandals, mules → more competition with Ugg, Birkenstock, Crocs; comfort brands must keep clear comfort leadership, not just awareness. Ugg shows marketing+comfort+fashion works but needs continued differentiation. Birkenstock stronghold in sandals; closed-toe momentum may need innovation in materials/silhouettes/narrative.
- 5. Niche brands: test fashion silhouettes (lower-profile hybrids) but prioritize adjacent expansion over permanent lifestyle pivot; Hoka & On use capsule drops/design partnerships; both capitalized on outdoor/hike trends. Salomon: technical trail DNA → organic fashion adoption esp urban; led gorpcore; if trail styling fades, must reinforce technical authority to convert new customers to repeat.
- 6. BI survey: casual/lifestyle sneakers top planned next footwear purchase (chart "Planned Upcoming Footwear Purchases"; values not extracted).
- 7. (dated 07/23/26) Nike: renewed innovation underpins LT rev; FY2027 trends may stay mixed (China, sportswear pressure). Win Now → stronger pipeline esp US: Pegasus Premium, Vomero 18, Pegasus 42, Nike Mind; global sports engagement, momentum in women's & wholesale, NikeSKIMS partnership. BI sees $200M upside to FY2027 cons $45.7B, down from $600M prior, as China reset takes longer. Risks: economic/geopolitical.
- 8. (dated 02/19/26) Deckers: innovation pipeline (Hoka franchise updates; Ugg year-round & men's) can boost FY2026 rev ≥10% vs cons HSD. Ugg outside core (sandals, clogs, lifestyle sneakers) → reported YTD sales +8%, in line with BI, ahead of cons LSD; more 4Q momentum could push nearly +8% for yr. Hoka YTD +16%, supporting BI high-teens brand growth thesis vs cons low-to-mid teens (newness, shoe updates, added distribution abroad).
