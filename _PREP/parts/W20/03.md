### Sector themes
- 4Q25 trends delivered upside thus far; geopolitical issues weighing on sentiment; most cos issuing prudently conservative gdnc accounting for tariffs, macro volatility, potential oil spikes.
- Key 4Q25 themes: 1) resilient consumer demand; 2) better-than-expected tariff impact on margins; 3) lower freight, cotton, polyester, sourcing costs; 4) clean, disciplined inventory. Risks: rising oil prices, consumer slowing from geopolitical risk; surging gas prices incremental headwind. Stock selection critical; cos with sales acceleration + pricing power outperform as tariff headwinds subside CY2H26. Many guides still factor IEEPA tariff rates → upside potential 2H26 if current global 15% rate holds.
- Positive 2026 view, 5 pts: 1) inventory discipline better than expected; 2) promos under control; 3) healthier US macro (Fed cuts, One Big Beautiful Bill stimulus) offset by geopolitical tensions; 4) tariff headwinds ease CY2H26 as effective rates fall, price increases likely sticky; 5) spending supported by strong macro & higher-end HH spending.
- "price inflation necessarily drives sales growth" —Barclays; retailers expected more optimistic on tariff headwind as relief to effective rates likely following SCOTUS ruling.
- Related notes: 4Q25 Preview & Promo Tracker (04 Feb 2026, "Better" promos at TPR); ICR Takeaways: Holiday meets expectations (15 Jan 2026); 2026 Outlook: Upgrade Industry to Positive; narrative shift from demand destruction to margin expansion (06 Jan 2026).
