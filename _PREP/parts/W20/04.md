## SRC 05_SECTOR_MACRO/2026-07-28_BloombergIntel_SECTOR_China-s_Weak_1H_Retail_Sales_Put_Full_Year_Forecasts_at_Risk.md | 2026-07-28 (sections dated 07/15-10/02/26) | Bloomberg Intelligence, Catherine Lim (Sr), Jason Zhu; contrib Chang Shu, Eric Zhu (Economics), BI China Consumer Team | China consumption 2026
- Headline: China 1H26 retail-sales growth 1.3% makes BI FY est 1.7% and cons lowered 2.1% hard; needs sharp 2H accel. Slack June credit data → 2H downside if PBOC support fails. Signs of opportunistic gold buying; persistent fall in online services spending could hit Meituan, Trip.com.
- 1. Subsidized consumer-goods categories 1H retail sales down nearly 7% (2Q -11% vs 1Q -2%); ex-autos down 8% in June vs -6% Jan-May → subsidy fatigue, weak demand. Autos, appliances, furniture, construction items collapsed (big-ticket hostage to subsidies & housing). Cultural/office appliances/communications stronger June y/y, driven by ~62.5B yuan incentives from third tranche of ultra-long special bonds (funding-driven, not demand); subsidy rates/caps unchanged vs Jan-May and 2025. "the subsidy lift belies fragile consumption" —BI. June jewelry sales -3% vs -15% Apr-May as bullion fell below 32,000 yuan/troy oz → gold demand shifting defensive→opportunistic.
- 2. Luxury in mainland China should improve through 2026: Richemont DD Greater China growth Apr-Jun (led HK & Macau) vs 3% for FY ended Mar 2026, jewelry-driven; LVMH strong 1Q leather goods (new Dior), Tiffany & Bulgari improved from weak 2025 bases; Kering jewelry (Boucheron) strong amid overall 1Q decline; Prada strong 1Q (Prada & Miu Miu growing; Greater China above-avg vs other regions, Apr 30); Moncler robust DD growth Chinese customers home & abroad 1Q (Apr 22); LVMH mainland 1Q stronger than overseas (Apr 13); Burberry Greater China SSS +10% 1Q (May 14); Richemont fiscal 1Q (Jul 15).
- 3. China 2026 clothing/shoes growth likely to trail non-subsidized goods; selective shoppers; winners = innovation & performance-driven. Nike, Adidas, Anta, Puma, Li Ning need pivot to sports-led innovation/performance franchises to defend share vs fast-growing Arc'teryx and Salomon (Amer Sports). Lululemon and Anta's Maia Active well-positioned in specialist yoga/training 2026. (Chart "Sales Growth by Brand (YoY%)" values not extracted.)
- 4. (10/02/26) BI Economics: "drip-feed" targeted stimulus → stabilization not sprint; GDP 3Q 4.4%, 4Q 4.5% after 1H 4.7%; FY 4.5%, just clears low end of 4.5%-5% target; similar pace aimed for 2027.
- 5. BI China retail-sales scenario model: 2026 +1.7% vs cons +2.1%; 1H +1.3% → risk toward low end of sub-2%. Newly subsidized categories won't spur spending w/o income/employment confidence; exception communications equipment (phones, tablets, AI wearables, trade-in eligible) +16.5% June, +14% 1H = pull-forward. Forecasts exclude potential PBOC 10bp rate cut & 25bp RRR cut (Bloomberg Economics expects by end-2026); easing alone unlikely to revive spending w/o fiscal.
- 6. Likely more cuts to China retail-sales forecasts (07/15/26; chart only).
- 7. Jewelry/gold can outperform other non-subsidized discretionary only if bullion stable; volatility → selective, timing-conscious buying, not structural loss. Branded luxury demand to rise 2026 after 2 yrs contraction; pricier sportswear (Arc'teryx, Salomon, Lululemon) benefit from specialized athletic gear demand; cosmetics need innovation & online engagement.
- 8. June cosmetics retail sales +12.6% y/y (fastest in 20 months) vs overall +1%; 1H +6.3%; BI FY forecast +2.7% after >5% last yr; Hainan duty-free recovery supports global premium brands.
- 9. Platforms (Alibaba, Meituan, JD.com) unlikely to scale back incentives in 2026; pushing on-demand convenience, expedited delivery, agentic apps. June online physical goods +3.6% y/y; online services -1.5% — first decline on record — after subsidies pulled from high base; services volume to decelerate 2H; on-demand services usage drops immediately when vouchers fade → risk Meituan (instant retail/food delivery), Trip.com (promo-reliant travel booking).
