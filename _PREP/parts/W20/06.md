## SRC 05_SECTOR_MACRO/2026-07-29_DeutscheBank_AS_Apparel_Footwear_Textiles_Global_Sporting_Goods_Chan.md | report 2026-07-22 (distributed 22/07/2026; file date 07-29) | Deutsche Bank, Krisztina Katai (analyst), Nick Breckenridge, Jessica Taylor | Global sporting goods channel checks (US, EMEA, China), 2Q26 & July
- Title: "Global Sporting Goods Channel Checks: Athletic Slows, Sandals Show". Contacts: US Contact #1, US Contact #2 (retailers), EMEA Contact #1, China Contact #1.
- Thesis: bifurcated athletic/outdoor market, "winners vs. losers"; brands w/ newness, innovation, clear product segmentation take share. Increased conviction AS (Arc'teryx, Salomon; Buy, TP $52); Salomon praised across all regions, China contact noted Q/Q accel in 2Q; "yesterday's -5% pullback" in AS = compelling entry, "one of the cleanest growth stories in our coverage". Incremental concern NKE internationally (Spring 2027 order books written down); US contacts see improving momentum in key running models.
- Macro themes: higher energy costs, macro uncertainty, weather disruptions pressure demand; innovation = clearest share driver. Heard: 1) continued strength in sandals & seasonal footwear globally; 2) meaningful World Cup-driven demand boost in Europe; 3) consumers willing to pay for differentiated product; 4) notable slowdown in US run-specialty demand since April; 5) accelerating promo activity in China; 6) increasing retailer selectivity on inventory commitments & shelf space.

### 2Q26 & July sales trends
- US #1: Q2 choppy — strong Apr & Jun, weak May. July started down DD (tougher compares, weather, higher gas prices, potential Prime Day disruption, pull-forward from strong spring). Consumers asking for fall product earlier than expected.
- US #2: Q2 finished -0.3%, June weakest month; July improved modestly despite difficult compare; two key upcoming launches to support month.
- EMEA #1: demand pressured by cautious consumers & energy-cost inflation; World Cup drove significant uplift; watching sustainability through 3Q.
- China #1: macro pressure & extreme weather; strong heat-sensitive categories offset by slower running & trail.

### Consumer behavior
- US #1: gas prices frequently discussed in stores; shift to sandals, comfort footwear, men's dress; customers already searching fall assortments.
- US #2: footwear price inflation beginning to affect purchasing; consumers sensitive to refresh cycles, reward frequent newness.
- EMEA #1: highly price-conscious esp online; respond to innovation, storytelling, event-driven purchases.
- China #1: cautious; value & promos over brand messaging; quality/innovation/functionality > trend marketing.

### Order books
- US #1: reducing future commitments to ONON; tightening where demand weakened; supporting core athletic into fall.
- US #2: ONON primary source of order revisions, repeatedly reducing; Spring 2027 plans growth-oriented for NKE, Asics, Brooks, Hoka.
- EMEA #1: NKE order books reduced further; open-to-buy redirected to Adidas and Hoka lifestyle; growing order-book cancellations across market.

### Inventory & promo
- US #1: inventory relatively controlled at brands & retailers, limiting ability to raise promos materially; instead reduced vendor marketing support, modest expense controls.
- US #2: inventory somewhat elevated vs expectations; revision privileges help; holiday discounting could rise if 3Q sales don't improve.
- EMEA #1: promo intensity elevated, esp NKE & lifestyle footwear; excess NKE inventory significant overhang.
- China #1: promos up materially; retailer shifted to monthly promotions (vs special periods only); consumers discount-oriented.

### Brand takeaways
- Nike (NKE; Hold, TP $45): polarized. US contacts constructive — US #2 cited Nike standout 2Q performer (Pegasus, Vomero, Structure; improved inventory availability, strong turns, attractive wholesale economics); US channel seeing clear share gains & improving wholesale productivity. EMEA: persistent markdowns, excess inventory, weak momentum; China expects worse before better despite clearance; both international contacts question execution & positioning; further shelf-space loss into Spring 2027. DKS-READ: US wholesale partners seeing NKE running momentum/turns improve.
- Adidas (ADS GY; DB Adam Cochrane; Buy, TP €210): positive all regions; EMEA balanced portfolio, innovation pipeline, share gains, running momentum compared to prior major successes; China DD growth (execution, World Cup, "China-for-China"); key beneficiary of incremental shelf space & OTB at several retailers.
- Puma (PUM GY; Cochrane; Hold, TP €25): uniformly cautious; EMEA turnaround w/ low expectations; recent Anta investment could provide strategic support; running constructive, lifestyle & positioning challenged.
- Asics (7936 JT; not covered): consistently positive; EMEA household-name winner on running demand; China contact: never produced a down quarter in his business, reliable DD growth; US #2 strong recent performance, momentum into future order books; performance credibility + lifestyle acceptance.
- New Balance (private): positive overall; EMEA highly constructive (brand evolution, comfort positioning); both US contacts noted operational disruptions & softer recent sales, but brand health intact; launches/execution could support rebound.
- Arc'teryx (AS; Buy, TP $52): uniformly positive; China strong despite winter bias; increasingly competitive in trail running (athlete recruitment).
- Salomon (AS; Buy, TP $52): most uniformly favored; EMEA key innovation leader across outdoor/performance/lifestyle (prior EMEA call similar, disruptive innovation); China strong growth, considering store expansion; US #1 more cautious (limited access to flagship product, weaker run-specialty relevance); gaining share globally.
- On (ONON; not covered): all experts agree momentum deteriorated; both US contacts: meaningful sales declines, weak sell-through, poor reception to recent launches, shrinking order books, shelf-space concerns; EMEA questions performance authenticity & strategic focus; China more constructive (Tier 1 city growth, strong full-price selling); increasing skepticism whether innovation stabilizes after prolonged core running slowdown.
- Hoka (DECK; not covered): mixed but constructive; US contacts acknowledge recent softness/category underperformance, upcoming launches & core franchises as catalysts; EMEA positive (renewed growth, lifestyle opportunity); China cautious (weaker trail positioning, global & local competition); healthy brand, moderated growth expectations.
