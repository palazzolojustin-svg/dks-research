### Thesis (07/15/26)
- DECK can sustain HSD annual sales growth through FY2030: HOKA expansion + UGG evolving to year-round lifestyle brand. HOKA early-stage intl, room to become multibillion-dollar running brand; UGG LT opportunity in product diversity, men's, seasonal mix. Freight, tariffs, input costs may pressure near-term margins; full-price selling + disciplined marketplace mgmt keep OM low-20% range over time.

### Post-1Q27 earnings outlook (07/23/26)
- 2Q27 sales could rise ~5% per gdnc; HOKA + UGG strength tempered by planned intl wholesale shipment timing shifts + wind-down of smaller brands; upside hinges on stronger back-to-school demand (DKS-READ: BTS demand as swing factor for footwear sell-in/sell-through).
- HOKA +7.7% in 1Q27 on robust DTC; similar rate 2Q, accelerating 2H as delayed intl shipments + broader launches help. UGG MSD in 2Q per gdnc (sneakers, sandals, men's, apparel).
- GM +60bps to 56.4% (full-price selling, fav mix, better closeout mgmt) despite ~150bps tariffs. Raised FY GM and EPS gdnc; lifted tariff assumption to 12.5% from 10%.
- 1Q27 highlights: sales +5.7% (HOKA +7.7%, UGG +4.9%); wholesale +2.2%; DTC +13% (accelerated); comparable DTC net sales +6.8%; international +8.4%, domestic +3.2%; GM +60bps to 56.4% (full-price selling, freight savings, fav FX, product mix, offset tariffs); diluted EPS $0.94 topped cons; maintained FY27 sales gdnc +7-8%.

### HOKA
- Can sustain mid-teen expansion several yrs (intl, adjacent categories, DTC). 1.1% share of $179bn sports-footwear mkt (another section: $174bn; Euromonitor $223bn by 2030). Core franchises Clifton, Bondi, Arahi; trail + racing innovation; disciplined wholesale expansion.
- More strategic launch cadence (less overlap, cleaner inventory) re-accelerated FY26 growth; gdnc raised from low-to-mid-teens; cons moved to 15% from LDD at initiation.
- On track for >$2.5bn brand FY26; mid-to-high-teens annual growth through FY27 (scenario analysis); more balanced wholesale/DTC; selective wholesale door additions skew near-term growth to wholesale. China + Europe largest intl opportunities (Europe majority of intl sales; China greatest opportunity, recent focus).
- US: top-10 sports-footwear brand in 2023 (passing Asics, Brooks; tied Under Armour at #10); #7 in 2025 (Euromonitor); only 3.6% US share. DKS-READ: HOKA climbing US athletic footwear share ranks (relevant to DKS vendor mix).
- DTC key LT; near-term wholesale may outpace DTC (sell-in, store additions, reorders as sell-through outpaces shipments); DTC could reach 50% of brand sales; e-com majority of DTC; select stores in key markets.
- Category expansion: hiking, lifestyle, apparel nearest-term; trail/hike/lower-profile lifestyle; Mafate, Speedgoat formats; apparel helps broaden wholesale footprint + shelf space; collabs validate lifestyle.
- Running: gained share in US road-running >$140; returned to top position in run specialty, DD sell-through growth, rising intl reorders; competition intensifying around max cushioning.
- DTC re-accel (02/19/26): DTC pressure structural not demand: earlier wholesale shipments + expanded doors (esp UGG) shifted traffic to partners; HOKA model transitions disrupted digital; HOKA US DTC inflected positively (cleaner inventory, tighter launch spacing, enhanced membership program). UGG softer DTC = stronger wholesale in-stocks + multibrand shopping preference, not fatigue; DTC could modestly outperform wholesale 4Q. DKS-READ: consumers shifting to multibrand (wholesale) doors for UGG/HOKA supports wholesale partners.

### UGG
- FY26 at least HSD vs cons +6.9% (02/27/26); FY26 growth may moderate from LY +13%; LT sustainable MSD-HSD via 365 strategy; sneakers, sandals, transitional styles, men's.
- President Anne Spangenberg (ex-Nike chief merchant; role since mid-2022): diversification, franchise evolution, scarcity-driven full-price sell-through; brand heat all-time high. CEO Dave Powers quote (1Q23 call, Jul 28, 2022) praising her merchandising, 3 yrs in China.
- First male-focused campaign in >5 yrs featuring Post Malone (previously long collab w/ Crocs). Men's + apparel key to doubling UGG sales LT. Men ~1/3 of customer base; men's outpacing women's; Lowmel (all-gender), weather hybrid collection. UGG 1.4% US men's footwear share.
- Shoulder-season Lowmel, Tasman drove 1H26 sales +12% (cons 7%). Colder 4Q could beat flat gdnc -> HSD for year.
- Simplified assortment, fewer styles, inventory behind core franchises; trend styles Quill ballet sneaker, Ellis loafer.

### Margins
- FY26 GM/OM may ease (tariffs, freight, wholesale shift); full-price, phased price increases, opex discipline keep EBIT margin >21%; DTC (esp HOKA) key lever (04/20/26).
- Since FY2023 GM +>700bps (UGG gains, HOKA mix, channel/inv mgmt); UGG GM +11pts, surpassing HOKA in 2025 (higher ASP, mix, full price).
- EBIT margin down modestly from record 23.6% FY25; opex +8.2% YTD (brand-building, retail expansion, EMEA logistics). UGG EBIT margin +~10pts since FY23 to 39.6% in 2025.
- 1H: both brands 20%+ wholesale growth, muted DTC; more balanced 3Q; mix headwind FY26; LT goal 50/50 channel mix.
- Tariffs: <5% sourced from China; rest SE Asia mainly Vietnam; premium pricing limits elasticity; scale/full-price give leverage w/ wholesale partners + manufacturers.
