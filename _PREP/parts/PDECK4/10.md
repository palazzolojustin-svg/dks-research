### Thesis
- Growth company significantly undervalued; solid 1Q27 raises conviction; expects positive NTM surprises from HOKA + UGG revenue; market to realize DECK grows sales/EPS at +HSD/+LDD 5-yr CAGRs.
- Takeaway 1: market undervalues HOKA product diversification (TAM expansion, share gains). Clifton 11 + Clifton Pro resonating; Clifton Pro attracting younger male consumer. HOKA assortment previously locked in $140-$160 across mainly 2 platforms; now selling $115-$300 across multiple platforms -> gaining shelf space. Robust pipeline rest of 2026 and CY27. DKS-READ: HOKA wider price ladder ($115-$300) + more platforms = more shelf space at wholesale partners.
- Takeaway 2: GM beat (~200bps vs Street) + inventory control: 1Q27 ending inventory -5% y/y while 2Q27 sales guided ~+5%.
- Near-term stock may lag: 2Q27 gdnc perceived slightly underwhelming; gdnc assumes HOKA growth accelerates through year (skepticism). DECK quarterly EPS beat cons >95% of time since 2021. 2H-weighted HOKA guide = wholesale delivery timing shift vs LY. HOKA acceleration should be positive catalyst (market likes sales growth acceleration driving upward EPS revisions).

### 1Q27 results
- EPS $0.94, $0.07 above cons (and ~7c above UBSe). Rev +5.7% y/y to $1.020B (~20bps ahead of cons growth). HOKA +7.7%; UGG +4.9%.
- GM +~60bps y/y to 56.4% vs Street 54.3% (+200bps): full-price selling + fav mix +110bps, improved closeout mgmt +60bps, FX +40bps, vs ~150bps tariff pressure. Mgmt: closeout tailwind largely unique to 1Q (volume of franchise transitions/closeouts).
- SG&A $ +12.7% to $420M; 41.2% rate; ~255bps deleverage y/y; ~95bps more than Street. OM -~190bps y/y to 15.2%, ~115bps above Street. Inventory -4.9% y/y to $808M.
- HOKA 1Q: +7.7% (DTC +13%, wholesale +3%). DTC broad-based: intl (Europe, China, Japan) + positive US momentum. Product drivers: Clifton, Bondi; Speedgoat 7; Mach 7; Mafate Speed 2; Skyward; early positive Clifton Pro + Clifton 11. Wholesale pressured by logistics timing: LY EMEA warehouse transition pulled wholesale/distributor orders into 1H; this year shipments pushed to 2H.
- UGG 1Q: +4.9% (DTC +6%, wholesale +5%). Mgmt: consumers may buy in more event-driven pattern; no cancellations in UGG order book; brand more versatile/diversified across categories, geos, consumers. DKS-READ: event-driven consumer purchasing pattern noted by mgmt.
