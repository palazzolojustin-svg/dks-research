### 4Q thoughts
- 1) sales beat w/ in-line HOKA + UGG upside; GM strength partly offset by SG&A deleverage on pull-forward of investments; 2) guide usually conservative but implies flattish EBIT on contracting margin, EPS offset by repurchases; 3) lack of domestic growth keeps flags raised; business driven by wholesale expansion. Results "okay, but are of low quality".
- Downgrade rationale (recent): 1) more attractive relative opportunities in apparel; 2) cracks from domestic decel at HOKA; 3) risk margins peaked, likely down in increasingly competitive backdrop; 4) little valuation support. 1Q guide does little vs difficult wholesale compares; sees less upside through FY27 though DECK commits to algo.

### HOKA
- 4Q +14.5% vs guide 13-14% (in line); DTC +18%, wholesale +13%. 1Q outlook HSD on tough W/S compare below expectations.
- Building beyond core Bondi/Clifton: 6 models ~$100M each.
- Brand awareness US 60% vs 50% LY; intl 40% vs 30%.
- W/S expansion at sporting goods/specialty athletic (DKS-READ: HOKA wholesale expansion into sporting goods channel). DTC expansion 20-25 stores/yr.
- History: acquired 10+ yrs ago at $3-5M sales; from just over $200M pre-COVID to $2B+ FY25 (~+50% CAGR); Top 3 share in specialty run; moved from authentic runner to casual joggers + lifestyle. Growth slowed FY26 as US slowed (tough compares, competition), DTC no longer growth vehicle, more reliance on intl. FY27 expected more balanced channel/geo mix, intl still outpacing domestic.
- WF industry checks: opportunity/risk for HOKA to embrace lifestyle customer, not done enough; needs more product beyond super-cushioned platform run shoe, more robust launch calendar (vs 1 new model/yr). Competition has replicated HOKA; needs to break away on core shoe.
- Growth choppy: +11% 2Q26, +19% 3Q26, +15% 4Q26. Bears: release schedule focused on core runner vs casual customer (now bigger mix). Headwinds 2026: tougher DTC comps, more wholesale fill-in, resurgent Nike Run in US, 1H26-weighted launch calendar vs higher inventory of old models.
- Nike risk: Nike grew significantly in NA specialty run through 2025 as HOKA shifted to wholesale fill-in w/ decelerating DTC ("more than coincidental"); Nike now correcting overseas, reclaiming share, rationalizing marketplace -> possibly more negative promotional environment for longer. DKS-READ: Nike Run resurgence/specialty run share shifts.

### UGG
- 4Q +9% vs guide flat (outperformed); DTC +8%, W/S +10%; 1Q outlook in line w/ FY algo MSD.
- New products working: half of brand's FY growth from Lowmel + Golden collection; men's >20% of growth in year; channel growth more balanced through year.
- TAM expansion: less seasonal, male customer, sneakers, all-weather boots, apparel, 365 initiative, Lowmel to lifestyle sneaker. UGG grew mid-teens FY24/FY25, HSD FY26; LT algo MSD.

### FY27 guide / LT algo
- FY27 +HSD top line, 160bps OM contraction -> flattish EBIT. Inclusion of buybacks in EPS gdnc is a change (FY24, FY25 gdnc excluded buybacks).
- 2028-2030 framework: HOKA +LDD, UGG +MSD (in line w/ historic view); OM 20+% leaves door open for further contraction.
- Margins: FY26 OM ~23.1% well above industry; FY27 guide 21.5% (-160bps) has conservatism, but risk/reward skewed to downside; near-term benefits possible (tariff reversal, cost mgmt, pricing up new models); excess margins may face LT pressure from reinvestment ("marginal revenue has a higher marginal cost").

### Bull vs bear (buy-side)
- Bull: HOKA recaptures magic via technical investment, growth back above +15-20%; 2H26/1H27 margin headwinds largely tariff-related one-time, channel mix less a headwind; UGG healthy, innovated beyond classics, manages inv; valuation better risk/reward.
- Bear: HOKA plateaued, competition reverts growth down; HOKA margins normalizing, fall over multi-yrs as brand matures + shifts deeper into wholesale vs DTC; UGG over-earning, hard to sustain profitability (channel mix).
