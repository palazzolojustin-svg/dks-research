### Thesis / scenarios
- Thesis: one of highest-quality companies in coverage; multi-yr track record; two of strongest brands (HOKA, UGG); strong mgmt; fortress balance sheet.
- Bull: new products + wholesale channel fill -> HOKA re-accel to 15%-20% next few yrs; UGG MSD-HSD on intl momentum + domestic launches; margins low-20s -> FY28 EPS ~$9.50, price ~$190 (20x P/E on FY27E EPS, as written).
- Base: FY27 EPS at high end of gdnc; +DD EPS growth FY28 -> EPS power $8.00-8.50 FY28 (Mar'28); PT $138 = 16x-17x FY28 EPS ~$8.30.
- Bear: difficult compares + tough macro -> UGG flat-line, HOKA single-digit (mainly new distribution); tariffs + normalized discounting push margins to high-teens; FY28 EPS $7.00, price ~$90 (low-teens P/E on FY27E EPS).
- Upside drivers: HOKA awareness + retailer shelf space faster; UGG continued successful new products; full-price selling maintained; faster buybacks.
- Risks: highly competitive athletic footwear mkt; HOKA could run out of new distribution channels; UGG stall if new products fail; margin pressure if growth slows and more discounting/markdowns needed.
