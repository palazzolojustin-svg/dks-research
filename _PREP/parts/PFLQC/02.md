### New risk factors (DKS transaction)
- Pendency: customers, vendors, suppliers, landlords may delay/defer decisions or seek alternatives; merger-agreement covenants restrict actions/contracts with vendors, suppliers, landlords without DKS approval; employee attraction/retention/productivity risk from role uncertainty; management diversion and integration-planning burden; agreement may discourage higher competing bids.

If not closed: stock may fall to extent price assumes completion; loss of investor confidence, litigation, partner relationship damage, key-personnel loss; negative publicity; disruptions may continue; unrecoverable significant transaction costs.
- Regulators may impose conditions/remedies, delay closing; shareholder meeting may occur before regulatory approvals known; FL and DKS may agree to remedies without further shareholder vote even if adverse to FL, DKS or combined company.
- Fwd-looking factors add: merger termination rights, litigation re Transaction, regulatory/shareholder approval timing, reputational risk, management distraction.
