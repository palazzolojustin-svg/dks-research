### Our call / 1Q thoughts
- "We see more good than bad in the 1Q print: FL US is comping positive, the fast-break initiative is driving tangible results that give credibility to the BTS positive inflection, and margin pressure should alleviate at core DSG through the year."
- Most important takeaway: strength in FL US comps; Fast Break stores sustained improvement (comping double digit) with another 150 to go before BTS, all before new merch assortment arrives in 2H
- Disappointing flow-through on strong DSG comp viewed as 1) transitory given GM tailwinds through year, 2) not a surprise given marketing reinvestment

### Mgmt callback takes
1. Expect DSG to lever SG&A in 2H; 2. DSG flow-through a function of increased marketing investment; 3. Merch margin to expand sequentially; Media Network and GameChanger acceleration drive GM higher; 4. DSG comp broad-based (not just trading cards); 5. FL Fast Break improvement more weighted to footwear; 6. FL Europe earlier in improvement process, initiatives should accelerate from here

### FL / Fast Break
- FL turn "remains most critical to the DKS story" with significant 2H positive inflection in comp and margin at FL
- Proof points: FL US comp 6.4%; Fast Break stores comping double digits (100 of planned 250 completed) despite 30% SKU reduction; "If DKS can drive FL result before new fall merchandise arrives, we have increasing confidence that it should improve with better product."
- Deal history: May 15 [2025] DKS announced FL acquisition at $24/share (~$2.3B); 5 tenets: 1) domestic -> global operator; 2) serve broader customer sets via multiple concepts; 3) strengthen key supplier relationships; 4) broader omnichannel reach; 5) synergies $100-$125mm (mostly COGS). Mgmt to run businesses separately; WF notes historical synergies closer to 2% = $150mm on ~$7.5b FL revs -> slight upside optionality. DKS repeated deal accretive in FY26; medium-term cost synergies via procurement and direct sourcing (COGS). As of 1Q26 DKS remains committed to $100M-$125M cost synergies, portion in 2026 outlook; 2H26 improvement from Fast Break (re-merchandising, clearing out 30% of non-performing SKUs) rolled to larger portion of fleet

### Core DSG flow-through
- 6% core comp (5.5% ticket, 0.5% transactions) but core OM -66 bps: GM contraction from freight, fixed-cost deleverage (new DC opened), mix (trading cards); higher SG&A on previously communicated ramped investments ahead of World Cup
- 2Q = low point for flow-through (more marketing investment); mgmt confident in sequential improvement through 2H
- Reinvestment: retail growth initiatives (House of Sport, Field House); adjacent margin-enhancing businesses (vertical brands, Dick's Media Network, GameChanger app); traffic via loyalty program and category adjacencies (collectibles, trading cards); 1Q headwinds transitory, "prudently manages margin for LT growth"

### Thesis themes
- EW: core strong but "near-term visibility remains opaque as the Foot Locker is integrated"
- Executing at high level: 15 straight qtrs of LSD/MSD SSS as of 1Q26; ecomm outpacing business; GameChanger & Dick's Media Network; Field House from 26 to 51, House of Sport from 19 to 35 (mostly remodels of existing locations; closer to hardcore athletes, better real estate, access to better product from global and boutique suppliers); share gains secure premium/unique product; SSS upside reinvested in training/technology; "it really does come down to the blocking and tackling"; prime beneficiary if sport participation rebound continues
- Youth sports participation cuts both ways: up y/y for boys and girls; NSGA: HS participation record high 2023-2024; SFIA: participation highest since 2015; other studies: boys from 50% range to 40% range (2013-2023), girls 56% -> 50% (2017-2023); bears: participation fluctuating but biased down LT
- Bull case: FL results/synergies "better than feared"; DKS may have bought FL near trough levels on trough profitability; excellent, more consistent base execution; commanding share with optionality from Dick's Media Network and GameChanger
- Bear case: FL becomes "an albatross"; margin outlook less visible after multiple years of post-COVID growth; footwear innovation/newness pushed out

### Scenarios
- Base $220: ~13x FY27 EPS $16.50
- Upside $240: ~14x FY27 EPS $17.00; drivers: sport participation up (more girls in youth sports); GM better from premium product, channel shift, digital engagement; revenue upside from newer FieldHouse and House of Sport; FL results/synergies better than feared
- Downside $180: ~11x FY27 EPS $16.00; drivers: FL fix harder, distracting from core; cumulative retail tariff impact weighs on consumer (trade-down/delayed purchases); price increases lower than planned; SG&A spend continues apace offsetting GM gains
- Catalysts: credit card and traffic data updates; 2H FL performance on re-merchandised stores
- Upside risks: comps back to +MSD; sticky merch margins; FL acquisition/synergies drive EPS upside and expand international TAM. Downside: comps decelerate/turn negative; EBIT margin gains reverse; FL long-term dilutive or slower turn
