### Narrative
- Adj EPS beat cons; GM and OM matched; net sales beat
- Core DICK'S comp +6.0% (ticket +5.5%, transactions +0.5%); FL comp +0.6%, "FL's first positive comp since 4Q24" (Barclays wording), US FL +6.4%; 'Fast Break' stores (~100 total) comps up double digits; pro forma consolidated comp +4.1%
- FL FY comp guide +1.5% to +3% (from +1% to +3%); FL OI to be "meaningfully" back-half weighted
- Fast Break now ~100 FL stores with "meaningful" merch margin improvement; plan ~250 locations by BTS; light capex/refresh; main driver = tightened merchandising/curation
- Consolidated margins pressured 1H26 on investment timing; mgmt expects expansion 2H26 as synergies materialize and FL improves
- More confident on visibility into FL turnaround and accretion in 2H26 given Fast Break track record
- Combined DKS-FL benefits: 1) improved bargaining power with emerging and existing brands; 2) secular health/wellness/athletics trends across ages and demographics; 3) experiential formats (House of Sport, Golf Galaxy, Fast Break) "enabling DKS to own the sports category"
- Demand drivers: hot brands strengthening incl. FP Movement and On Apparel expansion; new brand intros in select stores: Vuori, Gymshark, Helly Hansen, Salomon
- Tariffs not addressed on call; on 4Q25 call mgmt said tariffs not a material driver of 4Q25 or FY26 gdnc; mgmt acknowledged dynamic macro/geopolitical environment, no updated tariff commentary
- Quarter: adj EPS $2.90 vs cons $2.83; revenue $5.16bn vs cons $5.05bn; GM 33.4% matched cons, -328 bps y/y on FL mix; OpEx rate 26.1% matched cons
- FY26 gdnc: adj EPS $13.50-14.50 unchanged (cons $14.48); net sales $22.1-22.4bn unchanged (cons $22.3bn); core comp +2.5-4.0% (from +2.0-4.0%); FL comp +1.5-3.0% (from +1.0-3.0%); EBIT $1.71-1.83bn (from $1.68-1.81bn; cons $1.77bn)
- Inventory (core DICK'S only until FL acquisition anniversary): 3 consecutive qtrs positive sales-to-inventory growth; IM Spread 377 bps in FY1Q26 (from 228 bps prior qtr); GMROI improved 3 consecutive qtrs; OMROI worsened for 1 qtr
- Estimates: FY26/27/28 adj EPS $14.48 / $16.48 / $18.03 unchanged (lower GM assumptions offset by better SG&A leverage)
- PRISM curve: DKS in "HUNT" phase (strategically offensive; mgmt in control of taking share; retailers usually beating sales/margin expectations, earnings may grow faster than sales; Region C = max potential stock appreciation; may last multiple years)
