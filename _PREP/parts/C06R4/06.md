### Our call
- "Upgrade to OW. While NT trends appear squishy at best (we model 2Q below Street), at current levels we are buyers of the risk/reward and are firm believers in the developing multi-year story - led by recovery at FL."
- 3 keys: 1) multi-year FL margin upside ("from 1-2%, we see a return to 7-8% margins on the table") via better allocation/merchandising; 2) DSG execution, "early-innings of building a compelling fly-wheel (high ROI investment cycle)"; 3) DKS "best way to play Nike's eventual product turnaround (where checks point to bullish early reads on Spring 2027)"
- Potential FY28 EPS power $20.00+ (vs Street $18.69)
- "The New Star of US Sport": core DSG category leader with structurally stronger productivity, margins, returns; +MSD sales growth on +LSD underlying comp (ex-relocations and GameChanger); HoS and Field House remodels earn ~30% / ~40% 4-wall ROIC, add +200-275 bps to comp; GameChanger, paid loyalty, DICK's Media Network = under-appreciated early-innings flywheel cementing "gatekeeper for athletic brands in the US"
- Cleaner Nike play: for investors believing in Nike recovery but uncomfortable with Nike international resets; industry checks: NA retailer demand "incrementally more positive on innovation and product hitting for Spring 2027"; DKS most concentrated benefit "at just ~12x P/E"
- 2Q "Squishy at Best, Bad at Worst": NT results weak vs prior plan; laterals cite deteriorating sell-through and highly promotional environment; WF 2Q below Street almost entirely due to FL (WF FL comp -3.5% vs Street +0.4%) -> EPS $3.72 vs Street $3.77; B2S outlook, FL inflection timing and 2H profit levers more critical than 2Q print
- What bulls need to hear: 1) B2S trends at FL (comp/margin from ~250 Fast Break doors); 2) DSG trajectory post World Cup (esp. footwear, traffic vs ticket) and flow-through vs investments; 3) 2H profit levers if B2S demand falls short ("can they hold 'E'?"); if DKS holds earnings through slowing trends, bulls can re-engage at depressed multiples
- Stock recently retrenched on concerns business slowing (esp. footwear) after World Cup; pullback = opportunity for longer-duration investors wanting NA athletic exposure without single-brand risk
- Key contention points: 1) FL skepticism (store-base rationalization, lower-income consumer pressure; durability of Fast Break into 2H26 as Nike cites deteriorating wholesale sell-through and lifestyle athletic footwear weakness); 2) core DSG harder to dissect (evolving strategy, changed reporting methodology, investments in unproven initiatives holding back flow-through); 3) combined-entity risk (failed scaled US retail M&A history, international complexity, higher footwear and Nike concentration)

### Scenarios
- Base $240: ~14.5x FY27 EPS $16.50
- Upside $285: ~16x FY27 EPS ~$18.00; drivers: Field House/HoS remodels/conversions generate higher leverage/flow-through; core DSG sustains +MSD comps; FL inflection hits as planned, margin story builds; new concepts/GameChanger/paid loyalty/DMN drive profit
- Downside $150: ~10x FY27 EPS ~$15.00; drivers: core DSG slows further, margins lower (lack scale on investment spend); FL inflection pushed out further, lack of profit becomes structural concern; spending accelerates without needed ROI
- Catalysts: credit card & traffic data; 2H FL performance on re-merchandised stores
- Downside risks to PT: comps decelerate/turn negative at core DSG; store-initiative investments offset sales/merch margin gains limiting flow-through; FL long-term dilutive or slower turn
- Company description now lists banners: House of Sport, Field House, Going, Going, Gone!, Golf Galaxy, Foot Locker

### Top 3 reasons positive
1. FL recovery: strategic rationale sound (DSG omni/merch/brand relationships address FL weaknesses; Fast Break +DD comps with improving merch margin); Nike's reduced wholesale allocation at FL = ~1,000 bps sales impact and ~490 bps OM impact (over half the deterioration) — "clearly reversing"; Nike NA trajectory at least improving from trough despite deteriorating sell-through commentary from April; FL OM return to >7% (still below pre-COVID) could drive DKS FY28 EPS north of $20.00 (Street $18.69)
2. Core DSG: +LSD underlying comp + HoS/FH relocations/renovations -> +LSD/+MSD sales growth with ticket and traffic; omni-channel in-housed since 2017 unmatched; brand partnerships shifted power to DSG (partner of choice while brands in attractive, full-price lifecycle); store productivity positive (occupancy leverage, SG&A reinvestment); margins comfortably 11%-12%. "Fun Fact": DKS capex guide ~$1B for DSG banner nearly 5x ASO's $200M-$240M guide for this year, >300 bps higher as % of segment sales
3. Flywheel: store concepts (HoS, Field House, Going Going Gone!, Golf Galaxy); GameChanger direct access to highest-spending youth-sports customers; growing loyalty + recurring subscription potential; DICK's Media Network (highly profitable ad revenue)

### FL acquisition / recovery
- Announced May 2025: $24/share (~100% premium to trailing 30-day VWAP), $2.5B EV (~6.4x trailing EV/EBITDA); accretive to EPS in first full FY post-close (FY26); $100-125M medium-term cost synergies. Rationale: 1) global platform (DSG had no international; JD bought Hibbett 2024); 2) broader consumers (FL urban, younger male sneaker enthusiast); 3) stronger brand relationships; 4) omni-channel/"Retail 101" sharing; 5) operational efficiencies (procurement, direct sourcing; larger buyer). Closed September 2025; 92.6% of FL shareholders elected DKS shares instead of cash
- Recovery "fairly binary": merchandising/vendor problem (quick fix) vs structural (costly multi-year drag, distraction); WF: truth in between, more likely FL improves meaningfully off multi-decade-low earnings; "right bet at current levels (just ~12x P/E today)"; range of outcomes skewed to upside since current estimates embed FL at ~half historic margins
- 3Q25 call (Nov): "Clean Out The Garage" inventory purge + 11-store Fast Break pilot. 4Q25 call (Mar 2026): Fast Break strong positive comps, expanding to 250 locations (primarily US) by BTS; expecting fewer FL closures than originally anticipated. FY26 net capex guide $1.4B incl ~$400M FL (>5% of segment sales) vs FL 20-yr avg ~3% of sales. By 1Q26 ~100 Fast Break stores comping +DD with merch margin improvement
- Nike importance: >half of FL business; Nike penetration 56% (2007) -> 75% (2020) -> 59% (2024 last disclosed) -> ~57% est 2025; next top-4 brands 16% (2020) -> 26% (2024) -> ~28% est 2025. In 19 yrs since 2007 FL comped positive 11 yrs, only 3 while Nike declining. FL GM highly related to Nike penetration; regressions: FL comp = 0.7239 x Nike growth + 0.007; FL GM bps change = 0.0002x + 0.0237
