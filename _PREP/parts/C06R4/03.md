### Thesis / tone
- "Expecting the burden of proof to quickly shift to the bears with 1Q providing strong support for the bull case."
- All expectations proved true, but qtr "blemished by no upward EPS revision on tax clipping $0.14 out of EPS in 1Q and $0.25 from the updated guide for the year (so the low end of sales and op profit were raised but no EPS benefit)"; disappointing core DKS GM (50 bps below Street; down 36 bps y/y)
- Generally negative view on current credit card data, "no doubt impacted by weather in the Northeast, especially on golf which peaks in mix in 2Q/June"; stock traded down modestly
- "we believe the core bull case was affirmed and we are a warm weekend away (and a meeting with mgmt) from the bears flipping to bulls", esp. with World Cup ahead and FL's turn to positive comps/op profit "with little change to its assortment, other than having clean inventory"
- Chairman Ed Stack's merchandising touch (50-yr vendor relationships) to show across FL stores for BTS, with media brand relaunch, and the "clean out the garage" FL clearance lap in 4Q
- Expected "change in trajectory" in core DKS GM in 2Q as tariffed inventory largely sold through; better 2H sales leverage in core (another LT sticking point)
- Thesis: DKS executing at high level; positioned for wellness/sports trends, 250th Anniversary, 2026 World Cup, 2028 Olympics; core strength via House of Sport (HoS), leading assortment enabling full-price selling, alternative profit pools; FL turnaround off to good start (remodels, clean inventory, BTS assortment, improved vendor relations); optimistic on accelerating results 2H and into 2027
- Refers to detailed highlights from April conference meetings with mgmt incl. Ed Stack

### Call/follow-up takeaways
1. 2H-weighted profitability gdnc intact for core & FL. FY26 consolidated: sales $22.1-22.4B; OI $1.71-1.83B; EPS $13.50-14.50; assumes current tariff rates (~10%) persist. Core DKS OM 11.0-11.4% ("specifically the high end of the range" per JPM text); FL 1.4-1.9%. Consolidated capex guide lowered to $1.4B (from $1.5B) on HoS and other core productivity/efficiencies; $1.0B core DKS + $0.4B FL (vast majority of FL capex to Fast Break remodels and storebase enhancements)
2. Core DKS comp +6.0% vs JPM +4.8% (Street-high) and +3.5% Consensus Metrix; ticket +5.5%, traffic +0.5%. Drivers: strong response to newness/innovation; all consumer cohorts healthy (esp. younger athletes), no trade-down; broad-based across apparel, hardlines, footwear. 1H-weighted comps (World Cup timing + prudence) but profit 2H-weighted; low end of +2.5-4.0% guide raised. JPM models core 2Q comp +5.0%, FY26 +3.9%
3. FL positive comp +0.6% — first since 4Q20 — vs Street -1.3%, JPM +1.0%; FL US banner +6.4%, North America +1.4%, international -1.7%; attributed to clean inventories (suggesting AUR-driven) and Fast Break lift (+DD SSS). FY26 FL comp guide +1.5-3.0% (low end raised), SSS 2H-weighted as DKS touches full FL assortment by BTS. JPM models FL 2Q +1.0%, FY26 +2.4%
4. GM: core DKS 36.3%, -36 bps y/y, vs Street 36.8%; drivers: mix (collectibles/trading cards: higher AUR, lower margin rate), tariff costs (pre-SCOTUS inventory receipts), supply chain (fuel up y/y; Fort Worth DC ["Forth Worth"] opened tail end of qtr). FL GM +~100 bps y/y to 27.9%: occupancy leverage from store closures + merch margin expansion on cleaner inventory (4Q "clean out the garage"); "meaningful" improvement at ~100 Fast Break locations; GM excludes buying synergies (coming 2H). GM & merch margin expansion expected FY26 for both. DKS tailwinds: abating tariffs, moderated SC costs, 2H buying synergies, accelerating Media Network and GameChanger; FL: elevated clearance (esp. 4Q) lap, occupancy leverage, higher heat/margin product 2H post-BTS. 2H gdnc excludes fuel headwinds but also excludes tariff refunds and recapture of shared tariff pain from vendors (DKS not importer of record for most sales). Last year DKS originally expected GM +~75 bps, cut at 2Q on tariffs; ended +40 bps (JPM view)
5. SG&A: core DKS SG&A % deleveraged ~30 bps y/y, $ +7.6% (digital, store investments); FL SG&A % deleveraged ~30 bps, $ +0.9%; consolidated deleveraged ~90 bps, ~86% of $ increase from FL consolidation. Core FY26 SG&A deleverage peaks 2Q (World Cup advertising), leverage 2H on lapping investments; FL SG&A $ build slightly from 1Q, step up in 3Q (brand relaunch advertising). FL historically spends 2%+ of sales on advertising; DKS close to 4.0%. Core DKS 1H OM down y/y; 2Q decline > 1Q's 70 bps decline, on World Cup advertising and HoS pre-opening doubling to ~$28MM; mgmt "appeared comfortable" with core OM decline ~120 bps y/y in 2Q. Pre-open peaks 3Q (on way to $90MM FY); SG&A leverage 2H on lapping investments + synergy savings
