- Title "Down but No Knockout Stage"; "1Q results were solid; we continue to see a positive catalyst path for DKS shares ahead."

### Thesis
- 1Q "more confirmatory for the bull case than the bear case." Sector: anything but pristine results met with skepticism; DKS no exception, overshadowing favorable signs: outsized DKS comp, inflection at FL US business, guidance boost keeping improving profitability prospect in place.
- Debate: solid sales but "flow-through was underwhelming"; some say continues even if sales robust, others say sales slow -> limits flow-through. UBS: factors holding back 1Q flow-through dissipate over next few qtrs: early-stage FL integration/transformation; new core DC weighed on profitability; incremental lower-margin collectible sales (but shows merchandising agility). "there are just not many retailers in the U.S. that have as much influence over the profit pool of the category they operate in as Dick's."
- Dick's momentum strong into 2Q: 6.0% 1Q comp broad-based, consistent through qtr (vs more volatile cadence at other retailers); strength across categories + income demographics; +1.5mm new customers to file; mostly ticket (5.5%), transactions still positive; World Cup boosts 2Q; similar financial benefit from House of Sport stores.
- Core GM can improve: bears focus on ~35bps core GM decline; contributors (new DC, lapping tariff costs) largely transitory; collectibles mix headwind but shows DKS at intersection of sports + culture; retail media network + vertical brand penetration tailwinds; no flag of promotional environment deteriorating.
- FL transformation progressing: core US Foot Locker banner +6.4% (NA +1.4%), positive operating profit ("probably a faster improvement than many expected"); Fast Break stores DD comp growth. UBS updated: Fast Break could drive 120bps to FL comp this year (vs 60bps prior). Improvement largely better merchandising, store execution, leadership -> more upside as new product + better brand relationships arrive; DKS outlook embracing inflection during/after BTS "looks more credible today."

### Bull vs bear post-print
- Bulls: core 6% comp above market expectation of 5% (elusive across retail); would have beaten EPS + raised gdnc absent higher tax rate; FL comp positive + profit contribution faster than anticipated; significant synergies; potential $400m EBIT from FL a few years out vs $110-150m this year; combined $20 EPS a few years out; attractive multiple.
- Bears: gdnc back-half weighted in very uncertain macro; top-line risk as World Cup boost dissipates after 2Q; weaker-than-expected 1Q DKS GM adds margin risk for rest of year -> 2H profitability could be worse; "too many moving pieces"; FL structurally tough turnaround, barely positive comp, long way to meaningful benefit; weigh on intermediate-term multiple; $15 EPS power more realistic in out-year (minimal FL contribution).

### Updated guidance (UBS breakdown)
- Consolidated EPS range reiterated; most building blocks moved favorably except tax rate -> expect cons EPS to stay ~$14.30 (level pre-event).
- DKS comp gdnc raised low end +2.0-4.0% -> +2.5-4.0%; midpoint implies ~2.5% comp rest of year vs +6.0% 1Q; bulls see expectations well managed (UBS agrees). Comps front-half weighted; 2Q largest World Cup beneficiary (good momentum already).
- FL comp gdnc raised low end to +1.5-3.0% (from +1.0-3.0%); midpoint implies ~3% rest of year vs +0.6% 1Q. Significant operational changes yet to hit P&L: Fast Break ~100 stores -> ~250 by BTS, comping DD%; BTS = first season with newly bought merchandise (DKS didn't buy current FL assortment); back-weighted; UBS model has FL comp accelerating next few qtrs. ~100 FL closures still reasonable base case this year.
- Synergies: still $100-125m over medium term (procurement + direct sourcing at combined scale); no exact figure for this year; expect support to profitability later this year as FL assortment turns.
- DKS OM gdnc raised at high end to 11.0-11.4% (prior 11.0-11.2%; UBSe 11.3%; cons 11.1%) = up to ~30bps expansion at high end (from 10bps). Mgmt: 1H pressure, 2H expansion; 2Q most pressured (World Cup marketing timing + heavier HoS pre-opening; "tens of millions" plus normal SG&A growth). UBS: pre-opening doubles to ~$28m in 2Q from $14m in 1Q; core SG&A to leverage in 2H. DKS GM expected to expand every quarter for rest of year incl 2Q (DC ramp, rolling higher tariff costs, retail media, GameChanger, owned brands).
- FL OM gdnc raised at low end to $110M-150M (prior $100M-150M) = ~1.4-1.9% OM (prior ~1.3-1.9%; UBSe 2.0%; cons 1.8%). Buying synergies not yet in GM -> upside as new merch mix scales into BTS + holiday; back-half weighted.
- EPS non-GAAP gdnc reiterated $13.50-14.50 (UBSe $14.00; cons $14.27). Better fundamentals offset by FY tax rate ~27% (up ~150bps from ~25.5%) from foreign earnings mix + purchase accounting adjustments; mgmt quantified EPS drag ~$0.25 for full year.

### 1Q26 results detail
- DKS comp +6.0% vs UBSe +4.0%, cons +3.4%, buy-side bar ~+5.0%; ticket +5.5%, transactions +0.5%; strength across footwear, apparel, hardlines (team sports, licensed, trading cards, golf called out). 2Y stack 10.5%, 3Y stack 15.8%; meaningful share gains; ~1.5mm new athletes added to database; some World Cup benefit late in qtr but comp consistent across qtr.
- No evidence of trade-down; healthy across all income demographics; none within good/better/best framework.
- FL global comp +0.6% vs cons -1.4% (buy-side bar flattish); first positive FL comp since 4Q'24. NA +1.4%, US Foot Locker banner +6.4%; International -1.7% (Europe a qtr or two behind US on Fast Break rollout + mgmt changes). FL op margin 1%, +70bps y/y, +130bps q/q.
- Fast Break: DD comps in 1Q w/ meaningful merch margin improvement, driven by streamlined presentation (~30% SKU reduction, key style/color/story focus) not new product; first full set of buys hits shelves at BTS (targeted inflection). Footprint +~90 stores in qtr to ~100; goal ~250 by BTS. UBS Fig 1 (chart): 120bp FL comp lift achievable (about double pre-event view).
- Private label outperforming chain: DSG, CALIA, VRST, Maxfly; private label GM ~700-900bps above DKS company average; used as margin lever + to fill assortment white space.
- Core DKS GM -~40bps y/y to 36.3% (UBSe & cons 36.7%): supply chain headwinds from late-1Q opening of Fort Worth DC (6th DC), elevated fuel costs; mix pressure from fast-growing trading cards/collectibles (below-avg margin but drives traffic + younger consumer). Mgmt still expects FY DKS GM expansion (access/allocation gains, private brand penetration, pricing discipline, DMN + GameChanger growth). Consolidated GM 33.4% (-37bps y/y) on FL mix. FL GM 27.9% (UBSe 30.4%; cons 27.3%) reflects lingering clearance, no buy-in synergies yet.
