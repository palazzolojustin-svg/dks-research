### DKS 1Q26 preview
- "Increasing core DKS comp to +4.8% with FL at +1%; EPS above Street led by FL op profit." Based on JPM data + channel work: core DKS comp +4.8% (from +3.4%), FL +1% vs Consensus Metrix +3.4% and -1.4%.

- Optimistic on top/bottom line: footwear momentum continuing (brand laterals Figs 6-9), ongoing sports/healthy living/casualization engagement, "a stimulated consumer," clean inventory (FL most noteworthy y/y). Mid-to-high end consumer healthy + World Cup ahead -> strong results likely extrapolated, esp. FL: improved BTS merchandising, higher heat product, better in-stocks, Fast Break remodels (Figs 17-18, NYC Fast Break location photos), easy topline/margin compares "portend potentially much higher results than the current consensus going forward."
- April JPM conference meetings w/ DKS mgmt incl. Chairman Ed Stack: category confidence high; ease of FL turnaround; high visibility to better product allocations with vendors "fully behind fixing" FL. -> DKS placed on Positive Catalyst Watch.

### DKS investment thesis / valuation (Neutral, PT $240)
- DKS executing at high level, strongly improved vendor relationships, benefiting from casualization + healthy living. Worry: footwear cycle slowing; any core DKS softness makes FL improvement more important in 2026; process may take time given lower-income US consumer exposure + global retail footprint -> maintain Neutral.
- Dec-26 PT $240 (from $228) = ~15x 2027 EPS (in range of historical FY1 valuation) = ~8x EV/EBITDA.
- Valuation matrix: EPS 2025 $13.27 / 2026E $14.43 / 2027E $16.22; P/E 16.7x / 15.4x / 13.7x (avg 10.2x; 3-yr low 9.0x; 3-yr peak 17.0x); EBITDA $2,004.9 / $2,465.3 / $2,726.2mn; EV/EBITDA 10.3x / 8.4x / 7.6x (3-yr avg 7.6x); at PT: P/E 18.1x / 16.6x / 14.8x, EV/EBITDA 10.5x / 9.2x / 8.4x; upside 8%.
- Downside risks: higher unemployment, housing woes, weakening demand; LT competitive pressure from DTC + online; highly promotional environment; FL increases exposure to malls + lower-income consumers + "potentially unwieldy international presence." Upside risks: conservative ests; positive comps on difficult laps -> re-rate; FL buying + SG&A synergies, TAM expansion, operational efficiencies, improved brand relationships.
