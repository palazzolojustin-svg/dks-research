### Thesis / quarter
- Adj EPS matched cons, sales beat offset by GM miss. Core DICK'S comp +3.1% ("healthy (albeit moderating)"), growth across categories; FL comp -3.4%; FL gdnc sequential improvement through FY26, FY26 FL comp +1% to +3%.
- FL "clean out the garage" inventory clean-up finished; next phase = store fleet refresh "Fast Break". Pilot in 11 stores drove "very strong positive comps, actually meaningfully exceeding the DICK'S business" (mgmt); scaling to ~250 locations by back-to-school. Light capex/refresh investment; main driver tightened merchandising/curated offering.
- Consolidated margins pressured 1H26 (investment timing); mgmt expects expansion 2H26 as synergies materialize + FL improves.
- Barclays: positive on core DICK'S taking share; "more confident on the visibility into the Foot Locker turnaround and accretion in 3Q26" after inventory reset + early Fast Break success. "FL ready to progress towards 3Q26 inflection."
- Combined DKS-FL benefits: 1) improved bargaining power w/ emerging + existing brand partners; 2) secular health/wellness, athletics across ages/demographics; 3) experiential formats (House of Sport, Golf Galaxy) enabling DKS to "own the category".
- Tariffs: mgmt cited dynamic macro/geopolitical env, no updated tariff commentary; gdnc embeds tariff rates before the Supreme Court ruling; tariffs not cited as material driver of 4Q25 or FY26 gdnc. Prior (3Q25) commentary: absorbed tariff costs within merch margin, still +5bps merch margin expansion despite tariffs + slightly unfavorable mix; consistent w/ prior view 2H25 tariff impact modest; updated 3Q25 gdnc reflected all tariffs in effect.
- 4Q25: adj EPS $3.45 = cons; rev $6.2bn vs cons $5.5bn (cons likely did not fully reflect DKS+FL combined). DICK'S comp +3.1% = avg ticket +4.4%, transactions -1.3%. FL comp -3.4%. Pro forma consolidated comp +0.8%. GM 31.9% vs cons 33.5%, miss driven entirely by FL mix (FL inventory liquidation); underlying core DICK'S GM +67bps y/y. OpEx 24.9% of sales vs cons 25.9%.
- FY26 gdnc (consolidated): adj EPS $13.50-14.50 (cons $14.83); net sales $22.1-22.4bn (cons $21.7bn); DICK'S comp +2.0% to +4.0%; FL comp +1.0% to +3.0%; EBIT $1.68-1.81bn (cons $1.76bn).
- Inventory (Barclays metrics core DICK'S only until FL anniversary): 2 consecutive qtrs positive sales-to-inventory growth; IM Spread 228bps 4Q25 (vs 153bps 3Q25); GMROI improved 2 consecutive qtrs; OMROI improved 1 qtr.
- PRISM curve: DKS in "HUNT" phase (strategically offensive; mgmt on offense, taking share, beating sales/margin expectations, earnings may grow faster than sales; Region C = max potential stock appreciation; may last multiple years). Promo score figures (Fig 4-7) charts only, no values extracted.

### Estimates / valuation
- FY26 adj EPS $14.48 (from $14.41); FY27 $16.48 (from $16.15); initiating FY28 $18.03. PT $264 = 16x (from 15x) FY27E/CY27 adj EPS $16.48; multiple +1 turn on increased confidence in FL turnaround.

- Why OW: 1) sustainable positive B&M comps; 2) elevated product offering targeting enthusiast -> higher AUR, fewer promos; 3) increased private label; 4) share gains.
- Risks: consumer discretionary income weakness; inflationary costs; non-exclusive branded product in competing channels; brands going DTC + regional competitors; comp deceleration.
