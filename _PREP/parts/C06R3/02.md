### Our Call / 4Q thoughts
- "Momentum on core DSG continues with solid SSS, non-comp opportunities, and optionality on ancillary profit drivers - but the key debate remains on if solving for product will drive the turnaround at FL."
- FL turnaround front and center; mgmt doubled down w/ 2026 rollout of "Fast Break" remodels w/ re-merchandised assortments post inventory cleanup. Key bull question: can better product/merchandising alone drive 2H26 FL inflection, or take longer, more investment, drag on strong core DSG.
- Mgmt callback takes: 1) confident FL turnaround gaining traction, path to positive (1-3%) comps + margin expansion; 2) core DKS comps weighted 1H vs 2H (2Q higher than 1Q); FL improves sequentially, weighted to positive 2H26; 3) DC opening in April = GM headwind offsetting merch margin expansion; 4) $100-125M synergies split GM and SG&A starting in 2H; core DKS benefits too; 5) Fast Break rollout driving improvement via better allocation + merchandising.
- Core: positive comps + merch margin expansion; non-comp opportunities House of Sport, Field House, Golf Galaxy; additional profit drivers GameChanger, DICK'S Media Network (DMN), vertical brands; elevated experiences entrench DKS as vendor partner of choice.
- FL: inventory "cleanest it has ever been" after 4Q ("clean out the garage"). Fast Break to >250 locations by BTS 2026 globally (~11% of total stores); early tests turning around previously unprofitable locations -> confidence to close fewer stores than previously expected.
- Where we shake out: 2H FL inflection predicated on better inventory arriving + better retail fundamentals; rejuvenated key vendor relationships + marginal Nike NA improvements should benefit trough-level FL earnings; remain EW given risk FL needs more investment/takes longer.

### Investment thesis themes
- EW: core DSG strong but near-term visibility "opaque as the Foot Locker is integrated."
- 1) Can DKS fix FL? May 15 [2025] DKS announced FL acquisition at $24/sh, ~$2.3B. 5 tenets: domestic->global operator; broader customer sets via multiple concepts; strengthen key supplier relationships; broader omnichannel reach; synergies $100-125mm (mostly COGS; businesses run separately). WF: historical synergies closer to 2%, or $150mm on ~$7.5b FL rev -> slight upside optionality. DKS repeated deal accretive in FY26, medium-term cost synergies via procurement + direct sourcing (COGS). 2Q25 call less direct on synergy timing + core FL performance ("there are lots of moving pieces," "more work to be done"); 3Q25 call repeated accretion. As of 4Q25 committed to $100M-125M cost synergies, portion in 2026 outlook; 2H26 improvement from Fast Break (essentially re-merchandising, clearing out 30% of non-performing SKUs) rolled into larger portion of FL fleet.
- 2) Executing at high level: 14 straight qtrs LSD/MSD SSS as of 4Q25; ecomm outpacing business; GameChanger + DMN; Field House from 26 to 51, House of Sport from 19 to 35 (mostly remodels of existing locations); closer to hardcore athletes, better real estate, access to better product from large global + "boutique" suppliers; share gains secure premium/unique product; SSS upside reinvested in training + technology. If sport participation rebound continues, DKS prime beneficiary.
- 3) Youth sports participation cuts both ways: up y/y boys + girls but mixed medium/long term. NSGA: HS sport participation record high 2023-2024; SFIA: participation highest since 2015; other studies: boys from 50% range to 40% range 2013-2023, girls 56% to 50% 2017-2023. Further retracement to earlier levels = bull argument; bears: participation fluctuating but biased down LT.
- Bull case: FL results/synergies "better than feared"; FL acquired at near-trough levels on potentially trough profitability; excellent, more consistent base execution; sport participation retracement upside. Bear case: FL becomes "an albatross"; margin outlook less visible after multi-yr post-COVID growth; footwear innovation/newness pushed out; cumulative tariff macro headwinds pressure discretionary spend.

Upside $230 = ~14x FY27 EPS $16.50 (sport participation up incl. more girls; GM upside from premium product, channel shift, digital engagement; FieldHouse/House of Sport rev upside; FL "better than feared"). Downside $170 = ~11x FY27 EPS $15.50 (FL fix harder, distracts core; cumulative tariffs drive trade-down/delayed purchases; price increases lower than planned; SG&A spend offsets GM gains).

- Catalysts: credit card and traffic data updates.
