- Title "We See A Cup More than Half Full"; "DKS 1Q should provide further evidence of consistency."

### Thesis / setup
- Market typically braced for signs DKS trends will dim; "The burden of proof with this stock is constantly on the bulls." Expect print to provide support -> favorable response; near-term path filled w/ positive catalysts.
- 1Q core Dick's comp ~4.0% vs cons 3.3%; market (buy-side) bar slightly higher ~5% ("we think this is realistic"). Implies share gains + fair share of increased tax refunds. "Brewing case" that GLP-1 adoption supporting athletic footwear/apparel sales (healthier lifestyles).
- House of Sport: ~150bps of comp contribution in 1Q alone; significant runway. Less tangible benefits: engagement w/ brands, landlords, local communities. Risk: unit economics may diminish as more opened. Co pro forma HoS model: $35mm sales, $7mm 4-wall EBITDA, 25% cash-on-cash returns; even ~20% returns on later locations add considerable value.
- Core profitability 1Q: flat GM offset by 20bps SG&A deleverage (new DC pressures GM; front-loaded marketing spend). Expect 2H recovery: moderating investment spend, GameChanger growth, some GM relief from tariff situation.
- FL: model 1Q comp -0.5% vs cons -1.3%, market bar ~flattish; co previously said actions wouldn't drive meaningful turn until BTS, so early progress = better footing. FL 1Q OI $11mm (probably conservative) assumes 30.4% GM (highest in last few years).
- Fast Break: 21-store pilot comps likely MSD-HSD %; ramping to 250 stores by BTS; UBS: could add ~200[bps] comp contribution to FL by 2027e; DKS could accelerate to nearly 350+ locations this year.
- Expect FY gdnc reiterated (sales $22.1-22.4bn, non-GAAP EPS $13.50-14.50); chance 1Q beat lifts EPS midpoint by $0.05-0.10 -> well received as base case is simple reiteration, esp. given rising energy + freight costs perceived as profit headwind.
- Catalysts: World Cup (kicks off June 11 in North America; licensed merch tailwind; longer-term US soccer interest); FL acceleration into BTS; easy FL profit compares lapping 4Q inventory disposition. "We think the risk is much more tilted to the upside than the downside."

### Core DKS comp / HoS analysis (Fig 1 chart, values in text)
- Last 8 qtrs core comp ~5% (avg ~4.9%). Assumes HoS ~$35mm annual sales vs ~$15mm avg for displaced store. 35 HoS open as of FY'25 YE -> ~$1.3bn annualized sales. Comp ex-HoS avg ~3.3% over 8 qtrs -> HoS ~155bps avg contribution (~30% of comp).
- 2026: HoS ~170bps comp contribution avg; if overall comp decelerates to ~3.0% (gdnc midpoint), ~60% of comp from HoS; if ~5% held, HoS ~30% (in line w/ history). 35 HoS across ~640 "legacy" DKS stores = runway; ~15-20 openings/yr sets ~2.0-2.5% comp floor (assuming rest of business flat).
- Elevated oil prices likely impact consumer spending; co believes it is less discretionary than credited (many products seen as necessities); its consumer held up well through tougher macro.
- "All in, we expect core DKS comp sales in 1Q and in FY'26E of 3.0%" [sic; elsewhere 4.0% 1Q and 3.2% FY]. Co: comp slightly front-weighted due to World Cup, 2Q likely to outperform.

### FL / Fast Break
- Pivot from significant closures of unprofitable FL stores to fleet optimization = positive. 21-store pilot in 4Q: comps meaningfully exceeded DKS business (3.1%) + strong GM improvement; from clearer storytelling, better presentation, removing ~30% of unproductive styles on shoe wall. Confidence to keep more stores open vs original closure plan = key reason FY2026 FL profitability guided lower than originally expected.
- Rollout back-half weighted: 250 by BTS; gradual through 1Q/2Q; another wave pre-holiday; marketing deliberately held back until right product in stores. FL comp + EBIT back-half weighted, consistent w/ 1-3% pro forma comp and $100-150mm operating profit gdnc.
- UBS framework: Fast Break adds ~10bps to FL comp in 1Q; ~5% comp lift yr 1 for converted stores (haircut to pilot), moderating yrs 2-4; ~60bps FL comp in 2026E, ~210bps in 2027E (Fig 2).

### Margins
- Core DKS 2026 EBIT margin guided ~flat at 11.1%, up to ~10bps expansion at high end; benefits from premium merch, private label penetration, high-margin streams (GameChanger, DMN); offset by Fort Worth DC ramp in 1Q + HoS rollout. 1Q most pressured qtr; core profitability down 1H, up 2H; marketing front-weighted (March Madness, World Cup).
- FL: no positive OI in 1H'26 expected; 2H GM improvement -> FY'26 op margin 1.8% (~270bps improvement vs FY'25); timing of DKS-sourced product hitting stores + lapping 4Q'25 clearance. Synergies (procurement + direct sourcing) additional tailwind; portion of $100-125mm in FY2026 (2H-weighted), balance medium term.

### 1Q26 UBS model vs cons
- Combined comp 0.8% (4Q'25) -> 2.6% 1Q'26e (cons 2.2%); core DKS ~+90bps seq accel to 4.0%; FL ~290bps seq improvement -3.4% -> -0.5%.
- GM +~70bps to 34.5% (cons 33.3%, ~50bps contraction); FL GM +~170bps to 30.4% (cons ~150bps contraction to 27.2%) on better merchandising.
- SG&A % +~60bps to 26.8% (cons 25.8%) on new DC + D&A from HoS openings. EBIT margin 7.4% (cons 7.1%). EPS $3.03 vs cons $2.86.

### FY26 UBS
- Core DKS comp 3.2% (gdnc 2-4%; cons 3.3%); 2Q World Cup boost. FL store comp 2.0% (cons 1.8%) vs -4.2% FY'25. Sales $22.2bn (cons $22.3bn).
- DKS segment: flat GM 36.4% (cons 36.4%); flat EBIT margin 11.1% (cons 11.1%); new DC in April + HoS D&A offset premium/private label.
- FL: return to profitability 2026; GM +~290bps to 29.2% (cons 28.0%); EBIT margin +~265bps to 1.8% (cons 1.7%).
- EPS $14.00 vs cons $14.25. DKS "typically conservative with their guide".
