- Sales $1,788M vs $1,874M (-4.6%; ex-FX -4.5%)
- Comp -2.6%: NA -0.5%; international -8.5% led by softness in Foot Locker Europe
- GM -40 bps: merch margin -10 bps; occupancy +30 bps deleverage

- Cash $343M; total debt $445M; inventory $1,665M (+0.4% y/y; ex-FX -0.7%)
- Q1 stores: opened 9, closed 56 incl all stores in South Korea, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Greece, Romania; remodeled/relocated 11; refreshed 69. 2025-05-03: 2,363 stores in 20 countries + 236 licensed stores (Middle East, Europe, Asia), licensed incl Greece & Romania business sold to license partner April 2025
- DKS merger (announced 2025-05-15) pending; no conf call; no guidance provided or updated
