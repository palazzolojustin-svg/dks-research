### Deal terms
- DKS to acquire FL; implied equity value ~$2.4B, EV ~$2.5B; unanimously approved by both boards
- FL holders elect $24.00 cash or 0.1168 DKS shares per FL share; no min/max on cash or stock
- $24.00 = ~66% premium to FL 60-trading-day VWAP (based on FL close 2025-05-14); total consideration ~6.1x FY2024 adjusted EBITDA
- DKS financing: cash-on-hand + new debt; Goldman Sachs financial advisor to DKS w/ fully committed bridge financing; Wachtell Lipton legal (DKS); Evercore financial advisor (FL); Skadden legal (FL)
- Subject to FL shareholder approval, regulatory approvals, customary conditions; expected close 2H 2025
- DKS expects EPS accretive in first full fiscal year post-close (ex transaction and one-time costs to achieve synergies); $100 to $125M cost synergies in medium term via procurement and direct sourcing efficiencies
- DKS to operate FL as standalone business unit and maintain FL brands
### FL profile per release
- ~2,400 stores across 20 countries (NA, Europe, Asia, Australia, NZ) + licensed stores (Europe, Middle East, Asia); 2024 net worldwide sales $8B; banners Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs Sports, WSS, atmos
### Strategic rationale (DKS)
- Global platform in growing sports retail; expands DKS addressable market; new U.S. locations via FL complementary real estate and international for first time
- Serve broader consumer set (performance athletes to sneakerheads); build on DKS House of Sport and FL Reimagined Concept learnings
- Stronger partner for brands via global reach/multiple platforms
- Invest in FL brand growth via differentiated store concepts and digital
- Operational efficiencies (accretion, synergies above)
