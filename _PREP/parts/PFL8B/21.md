### Supplemental disclosures — Background of the Merger
- 2024-05-20 FL board (w/ Evercore, Skadden): Dillon updated on interactions w/ DKS senior mgmt; board reviewed complexities/risks to Lace-Up Plan, gap between Wall Street estimates and Lace-Up targets, investor feedback on timing/achievability. NEW: investor-meeting topics included higher inflation and consumer sentiment effects, brand partner allocations and product mix, promotional activity level, cost-savings opportunities, technology platform initiatives, store refresh progress. Board discussed structural risks: heavy reliance on single product category, merchandise from limited number of brand partners, sales highly dependent on consumer enthusiasm for brand partners' new/innovative product; uncertainty of achieving plan targets on timeline; reviewed sensitivities
- 2025-05-10 board (video): Evercore preliminary valuation using management case (approved for use 2025-03-25) incl comparison of $22.00/share purchase price [DKS's price at that time] to trading multiples of FL/selected cos, precedent specialty retail multiples, DCF. Board considered challenges to mgmt case (structural risks, macro) and that Wall Street estimates were below mgmt case. Reviewed mgmt "additional scenario" (assumptions reviewed at 2025-03-23 and 2025-03-25 board meetings) w/ lower revenue growth and lower EBIT margin than mgmt case, based on slower recovery of an important FL brand partner and lower comp growth; Evercore ran DCF on it for information only
- Board considered stock election value (DKS price fluctuation), cash election certainty, no cap/proration; stock electors benefit from DKS model: broader product portfolio, less brand partner concentration, wider customer base, less dependent on single category
- Alternatives reviewed: continue Lace-Up Plan; JVs/divestitures; operational transformation; capital structure/capital return changes; capital market transactions; minority investments (incl private investors); licensing partnerships; other strategic/financial buyers. NEW: board discussed parties other than DKS and Evercore's views on several athletic retail companies and financial buyers' return expectations; concluded alternative strategic or financial buyer had low likelihood of greater value than DKS; discussed execution risk, time/capital needed, shareholder reaction for alternatives
- "Company X" potential transaction (terms conveyed to Mr. Bracken 2025-05-07): investment by Company X deemed highly dilutive and could tie FL's hands re future partnerships/alternatives
- Counterproposal decisions: reduce termination fee from 2.75% to 2.5% of transaction value; reject DKS request for expense reimbursement if FL holders don't approve; desire to close negotiations quickly given quarter's weaker-than-expected results and potential stock price pressure after Q1 release; counter at $24.00/share with no cap on DKS stock for stock electors; message that board wanted to move quickly to an announced transaction
- 2025-05-14 board: Human Capital Committee approved retention plan for critical employees; Audit Committee approved prelim Q1 release; Skadden reviewed NY-law fiduciary duties. Evercore: $24.00 = 80.3% premium to FL price as of 2025-05-13; 98.6% premium to 1-month VWAP; 6.4x FL TTM EBITDA at end of Q1 FY2025. Evercore methods: trading comps, precedent transactions, DCF on mgmt case; analysis did NOT value stock election (cash certainty, unrestricted election). Evercore oral opinion (confirmed in writing, Annex B) that $24.00 fair from financial POV to FL holders (other than Cancelled/Converted Shares) as of 2025-05-14. DCF on additional scenario info-only
- Skadden reviewed final terms: cash/stock election mechanics, closing conditions, termination rights, regulatory efforts covenants incl DKS commitment to divest FL assets generating revenues of up to $100 million and to give prior notice/seek approval for post-closing actions, if required by antitrust regulators
- Board rationale: though supportive of standalone plan, merger consideration more favorable given risks: "negative trends in consumer sentiment, challenging international market conditions, the underperformance and delayed turnaround of an important Foot Locker brand partner and uncertainties with respect to tariffs" + structural single-category/limited-brand-partner risk; certainty of closing and regulatory risk allocation; 2.5% termination fee viewed as low enough not to preclude higher offers; no alternatives likely to create greater value; holders no longer bear execution risk of mgmt case. Board unanimously approved, recommended adoption
- Evercore relationship disclosure (NEW): letter covered IB fees from FL in prior 3 yrs; no fees from DKS in prior 3 yrs; neither Evercore nor core team members owned direct equity in FL or DKS; board found no conflicts
