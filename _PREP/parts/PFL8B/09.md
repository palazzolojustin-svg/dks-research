### Reimagined concept stores
- Go-forward store expression for Foot Locker brand; pillars: customer-centric design; elevated brand storytelling; immersive/communal experiences; enhanced technology tools; supportive Striper experience
- Ended 2024 w/ 8 Reimagined locations (NA, Europe, APAC; various sizes); plan ~80 Reimagined doors in 2025, primarily conversions/relocations of existing locations; "Based upon 2025 performance relative to our hurdle rate, we would expect to maintain or accelerate the pace of rollout of the concept over the next few years."
- Unit economics: ~$4 to ~$5M year-1 sales; ~20% year-1 EBITDA margin; avg ~6,500 gross sq ft; avg capex/door ~$1.0-1.2M; payback ~2 yrs; net inventory ~$0.53MM; cash-on-cash returns ~50% (pre-tax; based on pro-forma deal structure; actuals may vary)
