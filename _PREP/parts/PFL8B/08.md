### Headline
- Total sales -5.8% y/y; comp +2.6%; global FL+KFL comp +3.6%; GM +300 bps; GAAP EPS cont ops $0.57, non-GAAP EPS $0.86; 160 store refreshes in Q4, >400 for year; issues FY2025 outlook
- "We delivered fourth quarter results above our previously revised expectations... Our return to positive comparable sales growth, gross margin expansion, and positive free cash flow in fiscal 2024 serve as proof points that our Lace Up Plan is working." —Mary Dillon, President & CEO (2024 progress: Reimagined doors, store refresh program, digital/mobile, FLX Rewards, brand-building campaigns/partnerships)
- "While we expect consumer and category promotional pressures to remain uncertain into 2025, especially within the first half, our Lace Up Plan strategies continue to resonate with our customers and brand partners." —Dillon; NBA All-Star 2025 = one of largest basketball activations in company history; priorities: customer-facing investments, controlled inventories, expense discipline; "confident that our strategies and actions will enable us to achieve our growth expectations in 2025"
### Q4 results
- Sales $2,243M vs $2,380M (-5.8%); 2023 incl 53rd week = $98M sales; ex-FX -4.6%
- Comp +2.6%; global FL+KFL +3.6%; Champs second consecutive quarter of positive comp, +1.8%
- GM +300 bps, led by sequentially improved merch margin recapture vs Q3'24 despite elevated promotions; occupancy flat % of sales
- SG&A rate improved 10 bps: cost optimization savings, expense discipline, lower incentive comp, partially offset by tech and brand-building investments
- NI cont ops $55M vs net loss $389M; non-GAAP NI cont ops $82M vs $36M; EPS cont ops $0.57 vs ($4.13); non-GAAP EPS $0.86 vs $0.38
- Cash $401M; total debt $446M; inventory $1,525M (+1.1% y/y; ex-FX +2.5%)
- Q4 stores: opened 7, closed 47; remodeled/relocated 21; refreshed 160. 2025-02-01: 2,410 stores in 26 countries + 224 licensed stores (Middle East, Europe, Asia)
