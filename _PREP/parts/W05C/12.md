### Headline
- Title: "DICK'S Sporting Goods, Inc. Reports Third Quarter Results; Raises 2025 Outlook for the DICK'S Business"; 5.7% comp for DICK'S Business; raises FY25 comp & EPS gdnc for DICK'S Business; completed FL acquisition "to Become a Global Leader in the Sports Retail Industry".
- GAAP EPS $0.86; non-GAAP EPS $2.07; non-GAAP EPS DICK'S Business $2.78 vs GAAP & non-GAAP $2.75 PY qtr.
- Opened 13 new House of Sport + 6 new DICK'S Field House in Q3.
- FY25 DICK'S Business comp gdnc raised to 3.5%-4.0% (from 2.0%-3.5%); EPS gdnc to $14.25-14.55 (from $13.90-14.50).
- Initiated review of unproductive assets; with merger & integration costs expected future pre-tax charges $500-750M.
- Definitions: (A) "DICK'S Business" = DICK'S Sporting Goods, Golf Galaxy, Going Going Gone!, Public Lands banners + GameChanger; "Foot Locker Business" = Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs Sports, WSS, atmos. (B) DICK'S Business outlook excludes FL results, investment gains, M&I costs. (C) DICK'S Business EPS excludes dilution of 9.6M shs issued for FL.
### Quotes
- "We delivered another strong quarter with DICK'S Business comps of 5.7%, and we continue to operate from a position of strength. At Foot Locker, we've assembled a world-class management team and are taking decisive actions to "clean out the garage" by clearing unproductive inventory, closing underperforming stores and laying the foundation for a fresh start in 2026. Reflecting these strong results and our continued confidence, we are again raising our full-year 2025 outlook for the DICK'S Business. ### Foot Locker acquisition
- Acquired 2025-09-08 all FL shares per 2025-05-15 agreement. Total consideration $2.5B: $2.1B share consideration (9.6M DKS shs issued), $223.0M cash, $111.6M pre-existing DKS equity ownership in FL. Current period includes FL post-close only (except pro forma comps).
- Review of unproductive assets: clearing unproductive inventory, closing underperforming stores, right-sizing assets not aligned with go-forward vision for FL Business; with additional M&I costs -> future pre-tax charges $500-750M.
### 2025 Outlook
- DICK'S Business FY2025 (excl FL results, investment gains, M&I costs): EPS $14.25-14.55 (~81M dil shs, excl 9.6M FL-issued shs; ETR ~24%; incl all tariffs currently in effect); net sales $13.95B-14.0B; comp +3.5% to +4.0%; capex ~ $1.2B gross / ~ $1.0B net.
- Foot Locker Business Q4 2025 commentary: strategic actions on unproductive assets (inventory optimization, closure of underperforming stores) to lay groundwork for success starting 2026. "Q4 2025 gross margin for the Foot Locker Business will be down between 1,000 to 1,500 basis points as compared to Foot Locker's reported results in the same period last year with pro-forma comp sales being down mid- to high-single digits." Excluding one-time costs of these actions, expects Q4 2025 FL operating profit "to be slightly negative".
- Consolidated Q4 2025: ETR ~29%; wtd avg dil shs ~91M (incl 9.6M FL-issued shs).
