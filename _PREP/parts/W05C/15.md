### Headline
- Title: "DICK'S Sporting Goods, Inc. Reports Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Results; Delivers Record-Setting Fourth Quarter Sales for the DICK'S Business"; Q4 DICK'S Business comp +3.1%; "Guides to Growth in Sales and Profitability in 2026".
- FY25 DICK'S Business comp +4.5% (avg ticket + transactions growth).
- FY25 GAAP EPS $9.97; non-GAAP EPS $13.20; non-GAAP DICK'S Business EPS $14.58 vs GAAP & non-GAAP $14.05 PY.
- Opened 16 House of Sport + 15 DICK'S Field House in 2025; plans ~14 additional HoS and ~22 additional Field House in 2026.
- FY26: DICK'S Business comp +2.0% to +4.0%; Foot Locker Business pro forma comp +1.0% to +3.0%; consolidated op income $1.71-1.83B (non-GAAP $1.68-1.81B); consolidated EPS $13.70-14.70 (non-GAAP $13.50-14.50).

- (A) Segment definitions as before; FL not in quarterly comps until Q4 FY2026 and full-year comps in FY2027. (B) Non-GAAP EPS excludes FL investment gains, acquisition-related costs and an asset impairment charge; DICK'S Business EPS excludes 9.6M FL-issued shs.
