- Matthew Barnes appointed President of Foot Locker International effective 2025-12-03; leads FL international ops: "driving strategic growth, accelerating business momentum and executing targeted turnaround strategies".
- Oversees FL stores, eCommerce, digital in Europe, Asia, Australia + licensed stores in Europe, Middle East, Asia. Reports to Ed Stack (Exec Chairman, leads FL business). Peer: Ann Freeman (ex-Nike) leads FL North America.
- Background: nearly three decades across UK, Europe, US, China, Australia; Aldi (started as Area Manager; became Group CEO & Member of International (Executive) Board of Aldi Süd); UK CEO at Tesco. BA (Hons) law & politics, University of Liverpool.
- "We are thrilled to welcome Matthew Barnes ... as we bring the business back to where it belongs at the top of our industry and position it for its next phase of global growth... Matthew's proven tenure working with prominent global brands to transform and grow their businesses makes him the ideal leader to elevate Foot Locker's international platform." —Ed Stack
- Hobart: Barnes "highly accomplished global retail executive" with "longstanding familiarity with the international markets where Foot Locker operates".
- Barnes: honored to join at "pivotal and exciting time for the sports retail industry"; personal passion for sports; aims to bring innovative experiences and new products to consumers worldwide.
