## SRC 01_DKS_SEC_FILINGS/8-K/exhibits/DKS_8-K_2025-09-08_EX-99-1_EXHIBIT-99-1.md | 2025-09-08 08:00 ET | Ex 99.1 press release (PRNewswire, Pittsburgh) "DICK'S Sporting Goods Completes Acquisition of Foot Locker" | FL deal close
- DKS completed acquisition of Foot Locker; positioned "to become a global leader in the sports retail industry at the intersection of sport and culture".
- Combined: >3,200 stores + e-commerce/digital across 20 countries (North America, Europe, Asia, Australia) + licensed stores in Europe, Middle East, Asia; broader reach/visibility strengthens key brand partner relationships.
- Continues operating FL brands: Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs Sports, WSS, atmos.
- Leadership: Exec Chairman Ed Stack leads global FL businesses with two new presidents (North America, International) "to enable acceleration of business momentum and targeted turnaround strategies". Ann Freeman (longtime former Nike exec) = President, Foot Locker North America. President FL International to be appointed.
- FL North America team: Tony Aversa SVP & GM Foot Locker and Kids Foot Locker NA (30-yr FL veteran; most recently GM Champs Sports); Denise Karkos SVP & GM Champs Sports (from DKS, was SVP Chief eCommerce Officer); George Jenkins SVP Store Ops & Customer Experience FL NA (30 yrs FL stores; was VP Customer Experience NA); Peter Scaturro SVP CFO FL NA (was SVP Strategic Planning & Growth FL); Steve Miller SVP COO FL NA (former DKS sr exec, led Hardlines Merchandising, before that Strategy, eCommerce & Analytics); Brett O'Brien SVP CMO FL NA (from PepsiCo, most recently Chief Sports Officer); Michael Keinath SVP Chief People Officer FL NA (from DKS, talent/org design/culture).
- Synergies: "between $100 million to $125 million in cost synergies in the medium term, primarily through procurement and direct sourcing efficiencies"; expects transaction "accretive to EPS in Fiscal Year 2026 (excluding transaction and other one-time costs to achieve synergies)".
- "We are very enthusiastic about the future of Foot Locker... The world class team we have assembled is committed to returning Foot Locker to its rightful place in our industry. We are committed to investing in and growing Foot Locker through its strong culture, led by the Stripers, and creating a more powerful experience for consumers." —Ed Stack, Exec Chairman
- "Bringing together the strengths of both companies will help us return Foot Locker to growth while continuing to fuel DICK'S momentum. As a combined company, DICK'S and Foot Locker will create a global platform that will redefine the sports retail industry and unlock value..." —Lauren Hobart, President & CEO
- Ann Freeman: thrilled to lead FL NA; opportunity to build on FL heritage, serve footwear consumers "from athletes to sneaker enthusiasts"; aims to accelerate growth, enrich brand partnerships.
- Advisors: Goldman Sachs (financial), Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz (legal) for DKS.
