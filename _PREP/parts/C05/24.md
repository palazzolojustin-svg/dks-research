### Strategy (slides 8-20)
- "entering one of the most compelling multi-year periods for sport": 2026 World Cup mostly on US soil, 2028 LA Olympics, Ryder Cup US 2029.
- Four pillars: Athlete Experience, Differentiated Product, Brand Engagement, Teammate Experience.
- House of Sport (pillars experience/service/community/product): ended FY25 with 35, plan ~14 more in FY26; "delivering strong financial performance". Quote Jack Hsieh (Macerich CEO, Q1-24 call 4/30/2024): toured Ross Park Mall (Pittsburgh) & Eastview Mall (Rochester) stores; "love to do eight to 10, if not more… one of the best concepts I've seen."
- DICK'S Field House (next-gen ~50K sq ft store with HoS learnings): ended FY25 with 42, plan ~22 more in FY26. Ed Stack (Q2-23 release 8/22/2023): "We haven't seen growth opportunities like these since we went public in the early 2000s."
- Golf Galaxy: ended FY25 with 113 locations incl. 33 Performance Centers; plan ~15 more Performance Centers FY26; 2025 US rounds played record high (Golf Datatech/NGF).
- Omni-channel: ~70% of FY25 sales from omni-channel athletes (+>1,000 bps since FY19); omni athletes spend 2x+ single-channel; 800+ stores; 90%+ of sales enabled by stores; 80%+ of online orders fulfilled by stores (ship-from-store, curbside, BOPIS); RFID. Options: in person, 1-hour in-store/curbside pickup, same-day delivery.
- eCommerce: multibillion-dollar business with profitability in line with overall DKS Business; "opportunity to significantly expand our online share"; will "aggressively invest in technology and marketing"; app at center (GameChanger Bat Lab, personalized NFL journeys, redesigned homepage, launch reservations, loyalty & MOVE, holiday shops).
- GameChanger FY25: nearly $150M sales; nearly 10M games covered; ~10M unique active users annually; over 1M teams created; over 4M avg MAU; over 6M hours livestreamed; ~40% revenue CAGR since 2017; expect continued strong revenue growth 2026.
- DICK'S Media Network: nearly 20B impressions; highly engaged ~30M audience; ScoreCard: ~30M athletes = 75%+ of sales [owned]; ~8M offsite athletes ~50% of sales [garbled pairing]; GOLD members incl.; expected important long-term GM driver.
- National brands: ~80% of athletes purchased >1 brand (multi-brand experience).
- Vertical brands: ~13% of sales 2025; 700-900 bps higher margin than national brands; ~$1.8B combined sales 2025 → second largest vendor behind Nike; top-3 vendor in athletic apparel, team sports, golf, accessories, fitness, outdoor equipment; flagships incl. 2nd largest women's apparel brand (behind Nike), largest premium men's apparel brand [names not extracted].
- Teammates: ~86% recommend DKS as great place to work (+2,070 bps vs US retail avg; Medallia 2026); 50,000+ DKS Business teammates.

### Foot Locker (slides 21-23)
- Acquisition benefits: global platform; broader consumer set across concepts; strengthen brand partner relationships via global reach; invest in industry-leading omni experience; unlock operational efficiencies.
- Progress: world-class mgmt team incl. - "We remain confident in achieving the previously announced $100M to $125M in cost synergies over the medium-term"; "continue to anticipate an inflection point for both sales and profitability beginning with the 2026 back-to-school season".
