### FY2022 ARS (FYE 1/28/2023; filed 2023-05-05)
- Front: Belief ("sports have the power to change lives"); Common Purpose.
- 2022 highlights: record sales $12.4B (+41% vs 2019); market share gains "at an accelerating pace" in largest categories; acquired 7M new athletes; record ScoreCard Gold members (>40% of total sales); EBT margin >11% (more than double 2019); EPS $10.78 GAAP / $12.04 non-GAAP (>3x 2019).
- >50,000 teammates; Teammate Relief Fund ~$500K to >350 teammates in 2022; >80% rate DKS a fun place to work.
- 75th anniversary 2023: founded 1948 by Dick Stack (bait & tackle shop funded by $300 from his grandmother's cookie jar); 1984 Ed Stack with siblings bought business from father; Ed continues as Executive Chairman.
- Foundation: 2019 goal 1M kids by 2024 surpassed Feb 2022 (2 yrs early); new goal 2M youth athletes by 2024; 75 grants × $75,000 → >$5.6M to underfunded communities in 2023. Purpose Playbook: GHG -30% by 2030; pledge $300M annual spend with diverse suppliers by 2025.
- Capital: "Since 2021, we have returned nearly $2.4 billion"; 2023 quarterly dividend more than doubled → $4.00 annualized ("underscores our confidence in the structurally higher sales and earnings profile of our business").
- "Since 2017, we have methodically transformed virtually every aspect of our business"; positioned to extend leadership in "large and highly fragmented industry".
- Merchandising: differentiated/on-trend assortment; key brand partners value DKS rooted in sport & showcasing complete portfolios; new/emerging brands; "powerhouse vertical brands". In-store: training investment; HitTrax batting cages, TrackMan golf simulators, premium full-service footwear decks; stores enabled >90% of 2022 sales (in-store pickup, curbside). Most extensive youth sports dataset; personalized marketing; GameChanger "premier scoring and statistics mobile app for youth sports" in multibillion-dollar youth sports tech market.
- Specialty concepts Public Lands, Golf Galaxy Performance Center; recently acquired Moosejaw (outdoor).
- House of Sport (launched 2021): 3 locations, all exceeded initial expectations; ~20 more over next two years; 75-100 HoS nationwide by 2027.
- "as the largest U.S. sporting goods retailer, we are well-positioned to extend our lead and continue gaining share in a vibrant $140 billion industry."
- Back matter: transfer agent American Stock Transfer & Trust (Brooklyn); auditor Deloitte & Touche (Pittsburgh); holders of record 4/17/2023: 233 common / 15 Class B; annual meeting 6/14/2023 virtual. Board: Edward W. Stack (Exec Chairman), William J. Colombo (Vice Chairman), Mark J. Barrenechea, Emanuel Chirico, Anne Fink, Larry Fitzgerald Jr., Lauren R. Hobart, Sandeep Mathrani, Desiree Ralls-Morrison, Lawrence J. Schorr (Lead Director), Larry D. Stone. Execs: Stack, Hobart, Navdeep Gupta (EVP-CFO), Raymond Sliva (EVP-Stores), Vlad Rak (EVP-CTO), John E. Hayes III (SVP-GC), Julie Lodge-Jarrett (SVP-Chief People & Purpose).

### FY2023 ARS (FYE 2/3/2024; filed 2024-05-02)
- Front adds Values: Dedication • Optimism • Integrity • Authenticity.
- Four strategic pillars framed: athlete experience, differentiated product, marketing/brand, culture/teammates.
- 2023: record sales ~$13B; continued share gains in largest categories; nearly 7M new athletes, record active athlete database; comp +2.4% driven by transactions; EPS $12.18 GAAP / $12.91 non-GAAP. 55,000 teammates (stores, DCs, HQ, GameChanger).
- Stores: >800 forward points of distribution for omni fulfillment; enabled >90% of 2023 sales.
- House of Sport origin: Ed Stack challenged team to create "the store that if put across the street from a current DICK'S store could put that DICK'S store out of business". vs typical store, athletes travel farther, spend more time, visit more often; national brands give unique/expanded assortments; emerging brands see it as growth platform. "For a new House of Sport, in year one, we expect approximately $35 million in omnichannel sales and very strong profitability, with a target of approximately 20% EBITDA margins." 20 HoS by end 2024; 75-100 by end 2027.
- Next-gen 50K prototype (later Field House): 11 opened 2023; 16 more in 2024; "This one-two punch of House of Sport and our next generation 50K format is the future of our DICK'S stores."
- Golf Galaxy >100 locations incl. 14 Performance Centers; more PCs in 2024.
- Vertical brands VRST, DSG, CALIA "gaining meaningful traction"; "highly profitable portfolio". Brand: storytelling, data science, personalized marketing; GameChanger "premier scoring, streaming and statistics" app.
- Sports Matter + Foundation: >2M kids; >$190M committed; 75 × $75K grants in 2023; $2M grants in 2024 (Sports Matter 10-yr anniversary).
- Capital: returned $1B in 2023; Q1-24 dividend +10% → $4.40 annualized.
- 2024 outlook: "guiding to another strong year and expect to grow both our sales and earnings through positive comps, higher merchandise margin and productivity gains."
- Back matter: transfer agent Equiniti Trust (Ridgefield Park, NJ); holders 4/15/2024: 232 / 15 Class B; meeting 6/12/2024. Board adds Robert W. Eddy (BJ's Wholesale; director since 2023); Mathrani (Sycamore Partners); Schorr (Deputy Chairman, SURTECO North America). Officers: Lodge-Jarrett now EVP-Chief People & Purpose; Elizabeth H. Baran SVP-GC & Corporate Secretary (replacing Hayes).

### FY2024 ARS (FYE 2/1/2025; filed 2025-05-02)
- Front adds Vision: "To be the best sports company in the world."
- 2024: record sales $13.4B (+3.5%); EPS $14.05 (+15% vs 2023 GAAP, +9% vs 2023 non-GAAP); comp +5.2% (avg ticket and transactions); share ~+50 bps vs 2023 (priority categories); >25M active ScoreCard members ≈75% of sales; opened 7 HoS and 15 Field House in 2024; >50,000 teammates.
- Three growth areas for 2025: repositioning real estate/store portfolio; footwear; accelerating eCommerce.
- HoS experiences: multi-sport cages (baseball, softball, soccer, lacrosse), golf simulators, rock walls, fields, ice rinks; services glove steaming, lacrosse restringing, bike repair; community hub; "commanding unprecedented landlord interest, positioning us to be an anchor in some of the most successful retail centers". ~16 new HoS in 2025; 75-100 by YE2027. Field House: 26 at YE2024, ~18 more in 2025; committed to expanding across DKS network.
- Footwear: premium footwear decks added in ~90% of DKS locations over past decade; "significant opportunity to further grow"; strategic marketing, in-store/digital focus, partnering with elite brands, athletes, celebrities.
- eCommerce: "multibillion-dollar"; "significantly expand our online presence… aggressively pursuing"; app enhancements; in-store tech.
- GameChanger 2024: ~9M unique active users; >$100M revenue; targeting ~$150M revenue 2025. DICK'S Media Network: early stages, strong initial interest, potential long-term sales growth & GM expansion.
- Macro: "We move forward with these growth initiatives in a dynamic macroenvironment, and we will continue to balance our growth with appropriate caution."
- Capital: ~$2.2B returned over past three years; 2025 dividend +10% (11th consecutive increase); new 5-year buyback up to $3B; increased rate of business reinvestment.
- Industry: growth through 2030+ — women's sports, Soccer World Cup matches, 2028 LA Olympics, 2031 Rugby World Cup; "largest omnichannel sports retailer in the country, commanding nearly 9% of our $140 billion industry".
- Back matter: Equiniti; holders 4/14/2025: 223 / 17 Class B; meeting 6/11/2025 8:00am virtual. FLS paragraph covers three growth areas, HoS/FH, footwear via "elite brands, athletes and celebrities", GameChanger ~$150M target, DMN, dividend increase. Board/officers page unreadable (structure matches FY23 list).

### FY2025 ARS (FYE 1/31/2026; filed 2026-05-01) — signed as CEO, "DICK'S, Inc."
- "This past year was a defining chapter for DICK'S, Inc., marked by growth and significant transformation across our business." FL acquisition "transforming DICK'S into a new global sports retail platform". DKS and FL have distinct brands/strategies/near-term priorities but shared fundamentals (differentiated assortment, experiences, brand engagement, teammates).
- DKS Business 2025: record sales $14.11B (+5%); comp +4.5%; non-GAAP EPS $14.58 (+3.8%); >40 bps GM expansion; nearly +50 bps share (priority categories); ~30M active ScoreCard members, >75% of sales. Athletes "willing to invest in differentiated product".
- HoS & Field House expanded (no counts); "unprecedented interest from landlords". "Tremendous growth" in eCommerce and app (launches, exclusive access, MOVE). GameChanger nearly $150M sales 2025, ~40% sales CAGR since 2017. DMN nearly 20B impressions 2025.
- 2026 outlook: "we expect to deliver continued comparable sales growth, strategic expansion of our square footage, and strong profitability for the DICK'S Business. While we remain mindful of macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainty, we are confident in our strategies, the strength of our offering, and the powerful momentum of our business."
- Foot Locker: acquisition completed Sept 2025; "our excitement – and conviction – in the long-term opportunity at Foot Locker continues to grow"; combination gives scale, deeper brand relationships, new consumers, global footprint. Since Sept: world-class mgmt team; strong alignment/renewed engagement from brand partners; "clean out the garage" — "completed a successful inventory clean-up that positions the business well for fiscal 2026". 11-store "Fast Break" pilot (clearer storytelling, better presentation, more focused assortment); "Early results have been very encouraging"; expand to ~250 stores by back-to-school. "Back to school is also when we expect an inflection point in both sales and profitability for the Foot Locker Business." $100M-$125M cost synergies over medium term. "We are confident that Foot Locker will reemerge as a stronger, more resilient, and more dynamic business."
- Investment priorities: DKS — differentiated assortment, store growth, relocations & remodels, technology, supply chain; FL — optimize assortment, reenergize stores, compelling marketing, invest in teammates.
- Capital: ~$2.4B returned over past three years; 2026 dividend +3% → $5 annualized (12th consecutive increase). 100,000 teammates across all banners.
- Back matter: Equiniti (Mendota Heights, MN); holders 4/13/2026: 375 common / 19 Class B (up from 223/17, consistent with FL stock consideration); meeting 6/10/2026 virtual; FLS centered on FL acquisition benefits. Board (12): Stack, Colombo, Barrenechea (former OpenText CEO), Chirico, Eddy, Fink, Fitzgerald, Hobart, Mathrani (Managing Partner, Atlas Hill RE), Ralls-Morrison, Schorr, Stone (10-K: Stone retiring at 2026 meeting → board of 11). Officers: Stack, Hobart, Gupta, Lodge-Jarrett (EVP-Chief People, Purpose & Transformation), Sliva, Rak, Baran, Ann Freeman (President – Foot Locker North America), Matthew Barnes (President – Foot Locker International).
