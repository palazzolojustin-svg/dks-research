- Title: "Q1 Was Fine, But Not As Much Upside As The Street Is Used To".

### Main takeaway
- 1Q27 slightly better than gdnc but, given DECK's history of big beats, investors likely disappointed. Still: smallest qtr of year; both brands growing nicely ("even if the growth isn't as exceptional as it once was"); DTC solid; inventory/margin dynamics favorable; valuation compelling → positively inclined into key Fall/Winter season; PT cut on more conservative multiple.
- Rev +6% (gdnc +5%); EPS $0.94 vs gdnc $0.82-0.87. FY rev outlook reiterated +7-8%; FY EPS raised $0.05 to $7.35-7.50. Q2 gdnc below cons: rev +5%, EPS $1.73-1.78 (cons +8%, $1.90).

### Takeaways
- #1 HOKA +8% (in line w/ +HSD gdnc); DTC +17%, wholesale +3%. Strong domestic wholesale offset by timing of intl shipments (drove unusually strong +30% global wholesale growth in yr-ago qtr). HOKA Q2 +HSD again; timing shift benefits 2H → reacceleration; FY +LDD reiterated → implies 2H growth in the teens. New products: two Clifton additions (elevated Clifton Pro, Clifton 11).
- #2 UGG +5% (in line w/ +MSD); DTC +6%, wholesale +5%; growth domestic & intl. Strong adoption of newer categories (sneakers, sandals, fashion footwear) → GM mix benefit. FY MSD reiterated; Q2 MSD → implies 2H +MSD.
- #3 GM +60bps y/y vs gdnc for decline (cons -130bps); tailwinds channel mix, product mix, full-price sell-through, FX, better closeout mgmt; more than offset 150bps tariff headwind. Inventory -5% y/y → bodes well for GM upside in Fall/Winter amid conservative outlook. Gdnc implies GM ~-150bps y/y rest of year, "may prove conservative". Gdnc excludes IEEPA tariff refunds → further upside.

### Thesis / cases / valuation
- Thesis: one of highest-quality cos in coverage; multi-yr track record; two of strongest brands (HOKA, UGG); strong mgmt; fortress balance sheet.
- Base: FY27 EPS at high end of gdnc; FY28 +DD → EPS power $8.00-8.50 FY28 (Mar'28); PT $125 = 15x P/E on FY28 EPS ~$8.30.
- Bull: new products + wholesale channel fill → HOKA reaccelerates to 15%-20% next few yrs; UGG MSD-HSD (intl momentum, domestic launches); margins low-20s → FY28 EPS ~$9.50, share ~$170 ("18x P/E on FY27E EPS" per source).
- Bear: difficult compares + tough macro → UGG flat-lines; HOKA slows to single-digit (mainly new distribution); tariffs + normalized discounting push margins to high-teens → FY28 EPS $7.00, share ~$70 (10x FY28E).
- Upside drivers: HOKA awareness/shelf space faster; UGG continued new product success; full-price selling maintained; faster buybacks.
- Risks: HOKA in highly competitive athletic footwear mkt; HOKA could run out of new distribution channels; UGG stall if new products fail; margin pressure if growth slows and more discounting/markdowns needed.
