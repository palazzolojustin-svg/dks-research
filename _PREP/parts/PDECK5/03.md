### Thesis / scenarios
- UW thesis: stock re-rated lower after 2H25/FY26 slowdown; HOKA rev needs meaningful reacceleration to recapture above-avg multiple of 2022-2024; competitors again investing more heavily in running (where HOKA highly concentrated) and have copied HOKA's silhouette.
- Base $85 = 11x CY27 EPS (slowing growth, declining margin); below-cons view (slowing demand, structural margin deterioration as co invests to sustain top line in increasingly competitive category).
- Upside $115 = ~14x CY27 EPS ~$8.00: NA reacceleration & extending brand beyond specialty run; intl momentum w/ significant white space; competitive pressure eases protecting above-industry margins.
- Downside labeled $80 in chart; text: "Our $72 Bear Case reflects a 10x P/E multiple on CY27 EPS of ~$7.50" (internal inconsistency in source): NA sales slow further (HOKA & UGG, DTC & wholesale); intl pressured by competitors recapturing share; industry-leading margins pressured by needed investment. Chart axis $64-$138.
- Upside risks: HOKA accelerates above LDD algo; UGG faster than expected & product extension/men's works; aggressive buybacks continue.
- Catalysts: upcoming earnings; competitor and retail partner earnings.
- Key themes: (1) HOKA LT opportunity: acquired 10+ yrs ago at $3-5M sales; ~$200M+ pre-COVID → just over $2B+ FY25 (+50% CAGR); dominance in technical running; fashion via silhouette, colorways, larger foam heel; Top 3 share in specialty run channel; expanded beyond authentic runner to casual joggers and lifestyle consumer; still sees opportunity to add "billions"; growth slowed FY26 (US slowed on tough compares + competition; DTC no longer a growth vehicle; more reliance outside US — "neither detail favorable for investors"). FY27 more balanced channel/geography mix, intl still outpacing domestic (esp 2H). Industry checks: opportunity (and risk) to embrace lifestyle customer, not done enough; needs more product beyond super-cushioned platform run shoe & more robust launch calendar (away from 1x new model/yr); "competition now has replicated what made HOKA great"; brand must show product to break away on core shoe.
  (2) UGG TAM: less seasonal; male customer; sneakers, all-weather boots, more apparel; 365 initiative; Lowmel franchise expanding to lifestyle sneaker; UGG grew mid-teens FY24/FY25, HSD FY26; LT algo MSD.
  (3) Sustaining growth harder: durability concerns intensified 2026 — tougher DTC comps, more wholesale fill-in, resurgent Nike Run in US, 1H26-weighted launch calendar vs higher inventory of old models. Brand growth choppy: +11% as of 2Q26, HOKA +19% 3Q26, +15% 4Q, then "+8% in 4Q" (sic, = 1Q27). Bears: new release schedule focused on core runner, less on casual customer (now much bigger part of mix). Risk from Nike actions to recapture specialty run share: "more than coincidental that Nike saw significant growth in specialty run in North America throughout 2025 at the same time that HOKA began to focus on wholesale fill-in with decelerating DTC trends"; Nike now correcting overseas, reclaiming share, rationalizing marketplace → may create more negative promotional environment for longer.
  (4) Margins: OM ~23.1% FY26 well above industry; debate whether margins peaked / growth requires more investment ("marginal revenue has a higher marginal cost"); FY27 guide contraction to just over 21.5% has some conservatism but risk/reward weighted to downside; NT benefits: tariff reversal, cost mgmt, pricing up new models; LT excess margins pressured by reinvestment.
- Bull vs bear "tug of war": Bulls — HOKA recaptures magic via technical investment, growth back above +15-20%; margin headwinds largely tariff-related, channel mix less of a headwind; UGG healthy (innovated beyond classics, manages inventory); valuation better risk/reward. Bears — HOKA plateaued, competition reverts growth down; HOKA margins normalizing, likely fall over multi-yr as brand matures and moves deeper into wholesale vs DTC; UGG over-earning, hard to sustain profitability (channel mix).
- Outlook: FY27E EPS $7.45 (+6% y/y); cc sales +7%; GM ~-90bps to 56.8%; SG&A +9% $ to 35.2%; OM ~-150bps to 21.6%. FY28E EPS $7.65 (+3%); cc sales +4%; GM ~-40bps to 56.5%; SG&A +6% $ to 35.8%; OM ~-90bps to 20.7%.
