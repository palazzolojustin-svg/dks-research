### Summary / thesis
- FY1Q27 adj EPS $0.94 vs cons $0.87, driven by higher GM (strong full-price selling, more favorable mix). FY27 gdnc mostly reiterated; EPS raised $0.05 at m/p, m/p below cons. FY2Q guidance (source text says "FY2Q26", context = FY2Q27) sales & EPS m/p below cons. Growth expected to accelerate FY2H27.
- Brand momentum, healthy demand, encouraging newer franchises (Otzo Clog [UGG], Clifton Pro [HOKA]); mgmt reiterated confidence despite dynamic backdrop.
- Wholesale decelerated sequentially to +2.2% y/y — Barclays: timing noise from lapping LY early shipments required for European warehouse transition, not demand deterioration. Strong sell-through + innovation pipeline support 2H27 improvement as new facility ramps to full speed. 2H acceleration "a function of channel partner inventory turnover versus having 'safety stock' last year for the warehouse transition"; new product traction + retail partner inventory normalization = incremental driver alongside DTC execution.
- Tariffs remain headwind (assumed tariff rate raised to 12.5% from 10%); expect partial offset via sourcing diversification, pricing actions, productivity.
- OW reasons: 1) increased wholesale demand; 2) strong innovation pipeline in multiple categories at UGG & HOKA; 3) domestic & intl build opportunity; 4) full-price selling commitment; 5) multiple levers for global share gains at both brands.
- "Why OW" box: 1) continued HOKA runway; 2) improving wholesale inventory backdrop supportive of replenishment, likely benefiting HOKA as traffic-driving, full-priced brand; 3) expanding margins from channel & geography mix; 4) highest ROIC within footwear, still expanding.
