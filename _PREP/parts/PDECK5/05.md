### Innovation Day takeaways
- HOKA lifestyle pipeline: (1) Bondi CL — Bondi platform expansion for everyday wear, lower profile, less labeling, keeps Bondi cushioning; retail $175; launch Spring CY27. (2) Cielo 70 — low-profile trainer, aesthetic colors/materializations, traditional HOKA midsole; mgmt sees opportunity to expand into new colors/materials; retail $140; launch Spring CY27 into HOKA DTC + select premium lifestyle retailers. UBS liked both, esp Cielo 70. Lifestyle = major growth opportunity over NTM. "Our conversations with investors suggest most don't believe Hoka can successfully extend its assortment into the lifestyle category" → potential meaningful revenue upside surprise if products resonate.
- Performance franchises early CY27: Bondi 10 early Spring CY27, retail $180, mgmt views as potentially season's largest volume opportunity. Bondi Ultra X June 2027 at $300 — top of innovation pyramid, near-50mm stack, triple-density midsole, carbon plate. Innovative upgrades for Clifton, Mach, Tecton X, Mafate in CY27.
- UBS: HOKA innovation capability industry-leading, main reason for Buy; leveraging experience/scale for more sophisticated, robust, repeatable innovation process; will advance almost all key franchises over NTM; innovating with purpose (more durable, lighter, better performance); learned when consumer wants a style unchanged vs new. "only three athletic footwear brands globally that can compete with Hoka on innovation"; best consistent innovators become biggest share gainers.
- Distribution: HOKA controls distribution & inventory tightly → brand strength, pricing power. DECK mgmt noted entering **15 Academy Sports + Outdoors doors with a limited assortment and color offering** → UBS: well-thought-out plan to raise awareness/drive growth while keeping market segmented so as not to compromise premium pricing in higher-end channels.
