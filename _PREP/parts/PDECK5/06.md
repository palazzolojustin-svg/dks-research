### 1. Why DECK matters to DKS (DKS-READ, all facts from notes)
1. DKS-READ: UBS (Jay Sole) repeatedly names DKS as key domestic HOKA wholesale partner — 3Q26 preview (23-Jan-26), 3Q26 review (29-Jan-26), 4Q26 preview (14-May-26), 1Q27 preview (7-Jul-26): "We anticipate expanding distribution with key domestic wholesale partners like Foot Locker and Dick's Sporting Goods as well as key international wholesale partners should further drive solid wholesale sales growth for the Hoka brand." Jul-26 version adds "and sell-in to new wholesale partnerships like Academy Sports."
2. DKS-READ: HOKA under-penetrated in US sporting goods. Needham (30-Jan-26): "Hoka is only in about half the targeted sporting goods doors in the U.S., and only ~40% penetrated in Europe. In athletic-specialty, they're only in ~25% of doors in the U.S. and less than 20% of doors in Europe. And though run specialty is fairly saturated in the U.S., they're only ~80% penetrated in Europe." Barclays (9-Sep-26) whitespace in sporting goods/athletic specialty/intl; key partners underpenetrated esp athletic specialty. Wells (21-May-26): "W/S expansion at sporting goods/specialty athletic" as HOKA lever.
3. Wholesale large & faster than DTC through FY26 (BBG FA): FY26 wholesale $3,208mm (+12.3%) vs DTC $2,264mm (+6.3%); wholesale 58.6% of rev; HOKA FY26 wholesale $1,652mm (63.8% of HOKA); UGG FY26 wholesale $1,445mm (52.7% of UGG). Barclays FY27E (22-May-26): wholesale +7.0%, DTC +9.5% → mix rebalancing toward DTC.
4. Channel philosophy: UBS (7-Jul-26) quoting FY27 gdnc: "fuel DTC acquisition through differentiation and expand wholesale doors in a controlled manner." Barclays (9-Sep-26): door expansion "highly selective and guided by full-price sell-through metrics." UBS (10-Sep-26): entering 15 Academy Sports + Outdoors doors, limited assortment/color.
5. Wholesale sell-in timing noise affects retail partners' receipts: UGG wholesale +17% FY2Q26 as "wholesale partners took shipments earlier given inventory shortages last Holiday season" (Needham 24-Oct-25); $25M UGG wholesale deliveries pulled from FY3Q26 into 1H26 (UBS 23-Jan-26); FY4Q26 UGG wholesale orders pulled into FY3Q26 (Barclays 30-Jan-26); FY1Q27 HOKA wholesale only +3% (lapping EMEA warehouse transition, delayed APAC distributor shipments, reduced Clifton 10 shipments ahead of Clifton 11) (UBS 21-May-26; Needham 24-Jul-26).
6. Running competition: Wells (21-May-26, 23-Jul-26) "more than coincidental" Nike specialty-run NA growth in 2025 as HOKA focused on wholesale fill-in w/ decelerating DTC; UBS (23-Jan-26): "others are worried Hoka will be negatively impacted by a Nike comeback."
7. Tariffs/pricing: DECK sources <5% China, ~80% Vietnam (Barclays 24-Oct-25). FY26 unmitigated tariff est $185mm → $150mm → $110mm; net FY26 ~$25mn (Barclays 30-Jan-26). Mitigation: "select, strategic, and staggered price increases" and "partial cost sharing with factory partners" (2Q26 call via UBS 23-Jan-26). BI (07/21/25): DECK "scale and full-price sell-through could also provide leverage in negotiations with wholesale partners." FY27: paid ~$120mn IEEPA; if refunded "will factor in cost sharing arrangements with partners" (Barclays 22-May-26).
8. DKS-READ: Wells lists "retail partner earnings" as DECK catalyst in every note (Oct-25–Jul-26).
