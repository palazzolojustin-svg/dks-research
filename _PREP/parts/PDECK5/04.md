- Header: OW; industry POSITIVE; PT $133 (unch); price $82.60 (08-Sep-26); upside +61.0%. Presenters: Erinn Kohler (VP, IR, Corporate Planning & Business Analytics), Andrew (Andy) Poljak (Director, IR & Corporate Planning). Barclays "incrementally more positive" on UGG diversification into year-round product, HOKA innovation/product newness, and go-to-market strategy.

### What's incremental
- UGG 365 strategy working, reducing weather dependence: evolving into year-round lifestyle brand vs seasonal cold-weather; growth broadening across sneakers, sandals, clogs, apparel, men's; multiple franchises outside Classics driving relevance/engagement; aim to be less dependent on cold winters. Barclays: supports LT algo and confidence in durability of UGG +MSD outlook.
- HOKA innovation pipeline key driver: innovation, newness, differentiated tech drive demand; positive reception to newer franchises/extensions (Clifton 11, Clifton Pro); pipeline showcased should resonate broadly across cohorts + increasing new customer acquisition. Upcoming launches: Bondi X, premium-priced Bondi Ultra, additional lifestyle-oriented extensions; broaden addressable mkt. Barclays: HOKA growth "fundamentally product-driven, which is typically the healthiest and most sustainable form of brand expansion".
- Marketplace discipline differentiates DECK: inventory control, selective distribution, scarcity, full-price selling = core pillars. HOKA prioritizes productivity-driven growth over max NT volume; door expansion "highly selective and guided by full-price sell-through metrics" → brand heat, pricing power, sustainable share gains, brand equity, margin durability.
- HOKA runway across channels/geographies: U.S. specialty run relatively mature, but meaningful whitespace in sporting goods, athletic specialty, and international; "several key wholesale partners remain underpenetrated, particularly in athletic specialty"; intl expected to outgrow domestic over time (awareness, distribution, adoption). Barclays: brand early in global penetration.
- LT margin & capital allocation: confidence in low-20% OM framework through FY30 (premium pricing, GM durability, SG&A leverage with scale). Buybacks remain preferred use of excess capital alongside HOKA/UGG investment; limited appetite for M&A or increased leverage.

### What to do with the stock
- Valuation compelling, but market likely requires concrete evidence HOKA innovation pipeline can sustainably reaccelerate sales. DECK = high-quality growth compounder (two strong global brands, disciplined marketplace execution, long share-gain runway).
