### Our Call / exec summary (p1, p4)
- US sporting goods structurally changed over 2 decades: consolidated w/ differentiated strategies, shift to higher-margin softlines, omni-channel capabilities, winners critical to value chain with key athletic brands. Now $150-250B US market (depending on definition); market grows +LSD and consolidating (led by DKS); NT growth above historical norms.
- Demand drivers: daily sports/exercise participation 23% = all-time high; annual participation ~75%; inactivity fallen 7 consecutive yrs; youth participation recovered to 58% of kids 6-17 (from 49% in 2021); record 181M Americans (60%+ of pop age 6+) in outdoor rec 2024 (+3% YoY), fastest growth in higher-income and older cohorts.
- Mix shifted to softlines, brands, omni-channel; AMZN category share ~13% flat for several yrs / "better part of a decade". Moats: fitting, service, team-sports relationships, vendor partnerships, immediate availability.
- DKS now 76% softlines pro forma, nearly half business footwear; brand access = primary point of differentiation; omni-channel moved from cost center to moat.
- Macro: category PCE +5%/yr for 20 yrs, accelerated to roughly +12% in June; retail sales +7% 2026 YTD vs +3% LT avg.
- Core DSG = category leader (sustainable +MSD comps); GM ~700bps and OM ~600bps above pre-COVID; sales/sq ft up ~50% (nearly +50%); 11-12% OM viewed as healthy baseline/new baseline not peak. +MSD sales growth on +LSD underlying comp (ex-relocations & GameChanger); underlying comp +1.0-1.5% vs reported +4-5%. House of Sport (HoS) and Field House (FH) remodels earn 27% and 42% 4-wall ROIC respectively, at 120-125% of productivity of stores they replace; add +200-275bps to comp. Initiatives (GameChanger, paid loyalty, DICK'S Media Network) more strategically linked than mkt credits.
- FL swing factor: Nike allocation reset drove at least ~1,000bps sales pressure and ~490bps of ~880bps OM decline FY21-FY23; now reversing under DSG merchandising/vendor playbook. FL from ~1-2% OM today: path to recapture 300-400bps lost MM + 200-300bps leverage (on +LSD comp) → 7-8% FY28 OM (vs St ~5.0%). FL earned ~9% OM and 30%+ GM pre-COVID; cons embeds only 5% by FY28 — "we do not need a home run."
- ASO: EW; inexpensive (7-8x P/E) but unproven; "stuck in the middle" — later brand access, e-com 12% (vs DKS 15%+), store base skewed to lower-income/lower-participation states. Comps negative every yr since FY22; 1Q26 comp +2.9% entirely price (+4.5% ticket vs -1.5% transactions). Store sales/sq ft $252 vs $276 FY17; lease-adj ROIC down every yr since FY21 to 10.1%. Offsets: refined outside-in store strategy ~+100bps to comp; GM held 34-35%; retail media, loyalty, better brand allocation. Needs durable comp inflection.

### DKS summary (p5)
- Three reasons positive: (1) FL upside — DKS brings complementary ops/merch capabilities addressing FL historic weaknesses; re-established brand relationships/allocations that drove at least half of earnings deterioration over last 4 yrs; should clear low expectations for margin recovery; (2) core DSG category leader, +LSD/+MSD growth, defensible moat (omni-channel, merchandising, brand partnerships) → sustainable improvements in store productivity, margins, returns; (3) initiatives (unique store concepts, paid loyalty, GameChanger, DICK'S Media Network) work together driving value/engagement with highest-value customers, cementing partner-of-choice status in US.
- Key contention points/risks: (1) FL skepticism — structural need to rationalize store base, lower-income consumer pressure; NT durability of Fast Break into 2H26 esp. as Nike cites deteriorating wholesale sell-through and lifestyle athletic footwear weakness; (2) core DSG harder to dissect (strategy evolved, reporting methodologies changed, investments in unproven initiatives hold back flow-through); (3) combined entity risk — history of failed M&A in scaled US retail, international complexity, much higher concentration to footwear and Nike.
- Stock retrenched on concerns business slowing (esp. footwear) after World Cup in US; pullback = opportunity for longer-duration investors wanting NA athletic exposure without single-brand exposure.

### Top 3 reasons positive (p6)
1) FL recovery: gating item for investors. Strategic rationale sound; Fast Break comping +DD with improving merch margin; Nike wholesale allocation cut ~1,000bps sales impact, ~490bps OM (over half of deterioration) — clearly reversing; still hard to reconcile Nike NA return to healthy growth given deteriorating sell-through starting April this yr, but trajectory improving from trough; FL OM >7% (still below pre-COVID) → DKS EPS north of $20.00 in FY28 (Street $18.69), all else equal.
2) Core DSG: +LSD underlying comp + HoS/FH relocations/renovations → +LSD/+MSD sales growth w/ ticket and traffic; omni-channel from in-housing starting 2017 unmatched; brand partnership shifted power to DSG (partner of choice while brands in attractive full-price lifecycle phase); store productivity trending positively → occupancy leverage, reinvest in SG&A; margins comfortably 11-12%. Fun fact: DKS capex guide ~$1B for DSG banner ≈5x ASO's $200M-$240M guide this yr, 300bps+ higher as % of segment sales.
3) Initiatives flywheel: store concepts each distinct purpose (House of Sport, Field House, Going Going Gone, Golf Galaxy); GameChanger = direct access to youth-sports customers who spend most; growing loyalty program and potential recurring subscription revenue; DICK'S Media Network (highly profitable "advertising" rev). Early, all building to same goals.

### FL acquisition & recovery (p8-14)
- Deal announced May 2025: $24/sh (~100% premium to trailing 30-day VWAP), $2.5B EV (~6.4x trailing EV/EBITDA); expected accretive to EPS in first full FY post-close (FY26); $100M-$125M cost synergies medium term. Rationale: global platform (DSG never had intl; UK JD bought Hibbett 2024 for bigger US presence); broaden consumer set (FL more urban, younger male sneaker enthusiast); strengthen brand partner relationships; omni-channel + "Retail 101" to FL; operational efficiencies (procurement, direct sourcing, bigger buyer). Closed Sept 2025; 92.6% of FL holders elected DKS shares vs cash.
- Recovery "fairly binary": either merchandising/vendor problem (fixable quickly) or structural (multi-year, costly, distracts from DSG). WF: truth in between, more likely FL improves meaningfully off multi-decade low earnings; ~12x P/E today. Allocation problem from 2022-2023 largely resolved, but Nike negative DTC and deteriorating sell-through a big question mark; outcomes skewed to upside as ests embed FL at ~half historic margins.
- Timeline: 3Q25 call (Nov) "Clean Out The Garage" purge of unproductive inv + 11-store "Fast Break" pilot; 4Q25 call (Mar 2026) Fast Break strong positive comps, expanding to 250 locations (primarily US) by back-to-school, closing fewer stores than originally anticipated; FY26 net capex guide $1.4B incl ~$400M FL (>5% of segment sales) vs FL 20-yr history avg ~3% of sales; by 1Q26 ~100 Fast Break stores done, comping +DD with merch margin improvement.
- Nike at FL: 56% of business 2007 → 75% 2020 → 59% 2024 (last disclosed), WF est 57% 2025. Next 4 brands 16% (2020) → 26% (2024) → est 28% 2025. Since 2007 (19 yrs) FL comped positive 11 yrs, only 3 of those while Nike declining. FL GM highly related to Nike penetration.
