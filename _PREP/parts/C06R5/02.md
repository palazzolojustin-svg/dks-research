### Thesis / setup
- Debate "multi-layered, complex, and cautious"; skewed negative sentiment creates opportunity; unclear if 2Q clears confusion; DKS has several paths to shareholder value not reflected in shares.
- Athletic footwear backdrop: market skeptical; outsized decade-plus footwear cycle pausing — pairs-per-capita expansion, lack of new tech/silhouettes, softness in secondary market → "digestion period"; evident in recent results of several large footwear brands. If prolonged, DKS (disproportionate beneficiary) faces headwind; makes FL transformation harder. Any 2Q blemish scrutinized; fear '27 cons $16.40 revised lower.
- Stock at 12.4x '27 cons EPS; at 15x, current price embeds '27 EPS ~$13.50 — "This strikes us as too low."
- Lower multiple partly FL profitability fears, magnified by higher-frequency data showing step back in last few months: market view FL 2Q comp -2% to -4% (also cited as market expectation -3% to -4%) w/o much improvement so far in 3Q → BTS inflection expectation may be too optimistic. DKS $203 vs $209 day before FL announcement → implied FL value discounted. Value levers: prune FL store base and recapture sales through DKS locations, improve core locations, monetize ancillary assets.

### DKS/FL scenario framework (Fig 1 image; text)
1. DKS 3%-5% comp, stable/improving margins; FL flat-to-negative comp, OM stuck ~1%-2% → acquisition "missed opportunity"; valued on legacy; cons trends modestly higher; little multiple reward.
2. Minimum threshold to turn constructive: FL stabilizes, +LSD comp, modest margin improvement → signals playbook transferable, issues fixable not structural; FL stops being balance-sheet/execution risk; gradual estimate revisions, steady appreciation.
3. Narrative changes: FL credible turnaround, positive comps, margins approaching 3% → FL becomes source of earnings leverage (small improvements in productivity, merch margin, expense leverage → disproportionate gains); outperformance.
4. True bull: FL sustainable +MSD comps, OM >4% → rising estimates + multiple expansion; FL valued as growth platform; meaningful re-rating.
5. Principal risk: DKS core slows materially, margins compress, FL under pressure → estimates down, multiple compression.
- "DICK'S likely does not need a perfect outcome from Foot Locker to create shareholder value"; earnings leverage nonlinear as FL moves from stabilization to productivity.

### Core DKS 2Q preview
- Momentum broad-based across footwear, apparel, hardlines; customer acquisition, engagement, share gains. HoS deepens brand relationships, community engagement.
- 2Q core comp forecast 5.0% vs cons 4.7% and market expectation 5%. Modest OM pressure: SG&A and pre-opening deleverage from World Cup-related marketing and HoS openings. LT margin drivers: DC productivity, owned brands penetration, DMN and GameChanger growth, merchandising discipline.
- Expect FY'26 outlook reaffirmed, possibly flowing part of 2Q core beat, balanced by FL investment/uncertainty. Current DKS-segment gdnc: comp 2.5-4.0% (low end raised 50bps in 1Q); EBIT margin 11.0-11.4% (high end raised from 11.2% in 1Q).

### FL

### HoS comp analysis (Fig 2 image)
- Core DKS avg comp ~5.0% over last 8 qtrs. Assume HoS ~$35M annual sales vs ~$15M legacy store replaced. 36 HoS as of 1Q'26 → ~$1.3bn annualized revenue. Comp ex-HoS ~3.5% avg over 8 qtrs → HoS ~150bps avg (~30% of comp growth).
- Rest of 2026: HoS 150-200bps avg; at FY'26 comp 2.5% (low end) HoS ~70% of comp growth; at 4% (high end) ~45%. 36 HoS vs ~640 legacy stores → long runway; 15-20 openings/yr could support 2.0%-2.5% comp floor even if rest of business flat.

### DMN & GameChanger (Fig 4 image)
- DMN formally launched 4Q24; FY25 nearly 20 billion annual impressions (owned + off-site); leverages GameChanger youth sports data; ScoreCard ~30M athletes, ~75% of 2025 sales; enhanced ad measurement.
- GameChanger FY'25 rev ~$150mm (from ~$100mm FY'24); ~10mm unique active users (from ~9mm); 2025 growth ~50% vs 40% CAGR since 2017; DKS expects continued strong growth 2026. UBS: 40% growth at ~80% contribution margin → incremental ~$60mm gross profit FY'26 ≈ ~20bps GM benefit; potential 50+ bps GM annually as it scales.
