### PM's take (verbatim-heavy)
- "short the inventory build, own the clean up; we've entered stage 1." Imbalances when large brands lack innovation and rush to promote to keep channel inventories clean can be substantial, esp. for retailer dependent on one brand ("a la FL"). DKS comments "rhymed with 2Q17" when more extreme dynamic set in motion ("perhaps by the same player"): DKS merch margin down ~200bps in 3Q17 and ~100bps in 4Q17 (JPM math). DKS assortment expansion/brand diversification since → relatively resilient this cycle (est. 90bps promo pressure in 3Q), "but this significantly delays the FL margin story with our 2027 FL op profit cut by more than half to $103MMe." DKS remains bullish on core and broader footwear category; legacy silhouettes the headwind (Nike classics and Jordan where newness lacking; aspects of Adidas and New Balance).
- Tactically being short an inventory overhang typically fruitful until channel clears and headwind becomes easy lap next yr. Mgmt planning promo financial impact higher in 3Q; not expecting full resolution before 2027 (prudently assuming more promotional 4Q). NT caveat to bear case: sales trends/data should improve — mgmt acknowledged BTS shift cosmetically hurting sales, expected to turn; self-described upside case to guide = strong core DKS 2-yr stack in 2Q. Better footwear release calendar in 2H.
- "the stock is oversold, especially as the market fearlessly pushes the stock into the trough valuation range of 5-6x EV/EBITDA on our 2026 forecasts". Expect some recovery through BTS shift; next pivot 3Q earnings (full picture of channel clean-up). "Mark your calendar for stage 2. DKS could ultimately set up for a TGT-like move in 2027."
- Thesis: brands need DKS → better allocations; FL deal improves balance of power; DKS merchandising/marketing → FL sales & margin improvement (weighted to margin); stock recovery through BTS, "ultimately release once inventory cleans up, which we expect by early 2027"; alternative profit pools support core GM expansion.

### Key takeaways from call / mgmt follow-up
1. Guidance; FL turnaround pushed back a year. Core DKS: reiterated +2.5-4.0% SSS (confidence in HoS, GameChanger, newness/innovation); OM guide lowered to 10.6-10.9% (increased promotional pressure, higher fuel/healthcare costs). FL: now -2% to flat SSS (broader footwear market pressure, EMEA business); OM now -1.1% to -0.5% (due to increased promotions, "not so much other costs"). JPM FY27 FL op profit est now in line with low end of prior FY26 guide.
2. Core DKS 2Q comp +4.9% (Street/JPMe largely in line; market expectations ~+4.5%, low end +4.0%); outpaced industry by ~200bps; ticket +3.6%, traffic +1.3%. Mgmt: strong response to newness/innovation; all consumer cohorts healthy, no trade-down; broad-based across apparel, hardlines, footwear; World Cup results "strong"; license, team sports, running/cleats/outdoor footwear healthy. Still 1H-weighted comps (World Cup timing); FY guide maintained; 2H implied ~+1.3% at midpoint. JPM now core DKS 3Q +3.0%, FY26 +4.0%.
3. FL topline miss: comp -3.6% (after positive 1Q) vs Street +0.3%, JPM -2.0%e; in line w/ JPM work (negative L-MSD expectations). International -3.3%. Causes: consumers shifting away from legacy footwear/apparel franchises; launches meaningfully fewer than 1Q; launches didn't resonate → promotional marketplace starting with brands, spreading to specialty retail (FL more exposed: narrower assortment, higher legacy-brand exposure). JPM FL 3Q -2.0%, FY26 -1.2%.
4. GM "the toughest pill": core DKS 2Q GM 37.8%, +~80bps YoY, beat Street 37.1%: +50bps IEEPA [tariff] refunds ($19MM), DMN est +25bps (likely aided by World Cup/Adidas partnership), GameChanger est +~35bps; partly offset by promotions picking up at quarter end (esp athletic footwear/apparel) and ("to a lesser extent") higher fuel and supply chain costs. FL GM 25.7% faced larger promo headwind. Mgmt: core DKS FY26 GM down "slightly", 3Q most pressured; promo headwinds throughout 2H, less in 4Q on full-price launches — Caitlin Clark "Nike Caitlin 1" (10/1 launch), Air Jordan 11 "Space Jam" collab (12/12 launch), further Nike Mind newness (also aid FL). Lower guide includes fuel headwinds and higher SC costs. JPM core DKS 3Q GM 35.5% (down ~60bps YoY), FY26 36.3% (down ~10bps); FL 3Q 23.4%, FY26 26.4%.
5. SG&A: core DKS SG&A % sales peaked, deleveraged ~100bps YoY (dollars +9.8% YoY) — World Cup marketing, store/digital investments, higher health care. FL SG&A 27.5% of sales (promo headwinds, turnaround investments incl marketing and "Stripers" payroll). Core DKS FY SG&A deleverage still expected; 3Q deleverage 50bps (vs prior 3Q leverage guidance); 4Q leverage on annualized investments. FL SG&A $ step up in 3Q on BTS/brand relaunch advertising ("It Will Always Be Foot Locker" released on 10/7 [as printed; prior note said 8/7]). Pre-opening peaks in 3Q ($36MMe) toward ~$90MM FY. FY26 openings now 14 HoS and 20 Field House (vs 14 and 22 prior guide). Core DKS ad expense peaked 2Q (World Cup); FL ad peaks 3Q. JPM 3Q SG&A % sales: consolidated 27.2%, core DKS 26.3%, FL 29.0%; FY26 25.8%, 25.0%, 27.3%.
