### Setup & expectations
- Lowering sales/margin outlook; "Low bar with BTS timing in focus as market ponders the risk of a slower footwear cycle and lower-end consumer exposure (FL)."
- DKS SSS maintained: +5% 2Q (vs +4.5% JPM view of market expectations; Consensus Metrix +4.9%; low end of expectations +4%), +2.5% 2H (in line w/ cons); +2.5%e 3Q better than "the bar". Expectations came down over past month to ~+4.5%. 2Q momentum: World Cup, America 250, Knicks win (Figs 11-12: DKS White Plains location 6/21; Tri-State extended hours for Knicks gear), continued mid-to-high income consumer resilience.
- FL SSS lowered: 2Q -2% (from +1%e prior); 3Q/4Q +1.5%/+3% (from +3.8%/+4%; Consensus Metrix +3.7%/+4%, recent updates in line with new JPM). Market expects down L-MSD 2Q comps and FL "running much worse QTD" → JPM numbers likely relief. Reasons: lower-income consumer weakness (see WMT, DIY auto), potential post-World Cup lull, disappointing laterals (JD Sports, On Running). Still expect 2H inflection: Chairman Ed Stack's merchandising touch (50-yr vendor relationships) seen across FL stores for BTS; media brand relaunch "It Will Always Be Foot Locker" released 8/7; ~250 Fast Break locations by BTS (from 100 at end of 1Q; Figs 13-14 NYC Fast Break photos).
- Margins reduced for both: Street too low on DKS 2Q SG&A — GM sees "change of trajectory" but op margin down "in the 110-120 bps zipcode vs. Street's -70 bps"; FL 3Q SG&A too low given brand re-launch spending.
- Optimistic on 2H: supportive sporting goods/wellness backdrop, FL re-merchandising/ad campaign, GM drivers (clearance laps, synergies, alternative profit pools). "we continue to see 2H26 as the the potential 'own the hope' stock moment." Expectations plummeted: post-World Cup lull, later BTS suppressing QTD credit card data → spiraled concerns category at fault. DKS geographic exposure → LDW/BTS timing shift likely significant impact. Bull case: data headwind of lapping BTS weeks last yr with lower-volume weeks QTD shifts to benefit lasting into early Sept (similar to WMT messaging).

### Margin breakdown
- Core DKS 2Q GM 37.2%, +10bps YoY (Street 37.1%): "change in trajectory" as tariffed inventory largely sold through; alternate profit pools tailwinds. 2H: buying synergies for both brands; moderating supply chain tailwinds likely offset by persistently high fuel costs (not embedded in 1Q guide). Moderate uptick in promotions in July (Figs 7-8). 2Qe op margin 12.1% reflects peak World Cup ad spend; 2H expansion predicated on lapping investments and synergy savings.
- FL 2Q GM 27.0%, op margin -0.7%. 2H benefits: elevated clearance (esp 4Q) [lapping], occupancy leverage, higher heat/margin product after BTS assortment. 3Q Street too low on SG&A (brand relaunch, BTS assortment); advertising key to watch.

### Thesis / valuation / risks
- Thesis: DKS executing at high level; benefits from wellness/high sports engagement; only national sporting goods retailer, better/best emphasis → "brands need DKS and the FL acquisition only improves the balance of power"; FL turnaround faster than expected (lapping high clearance, better merchandising/assortments starting BTS); alternative profit pools → margin/earnings upside.
- Valuation table: EPS 2025 $13.27 / 2026E $14.04 / 2027E $17.00; P/E 13.8x / 13.1x / 10.8x; avg P/E 15.1x, 3-yr low 14.0x, 3-yr peak 17.1x; EBITDA $2,004.9 / $2,420.8 / $2,799.6M; EV/EBITDA 8.7x / 7.2x / 6.2x, 3-yr avg 8.4x; at PT $245: P/E 18.5x / 17.5x / 14.4x; EV/EBITDA 10.7x / 9.3x / 8.0x; upside 34%.
- Risks: higher unemployment, housing woes, weak consumer demand; LT competitive pressure from DTC and online; highly promotional; FL adds mall and lower-income exposure and potentially unwieldy international presence.
