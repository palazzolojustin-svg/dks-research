- Title: "The Footwear Hangover Hits DKS; Cut Numbers/PT Post Challenging 2Q Print"

### Our Call
- "While remaining OW, the 2Q print was as bad as it gets." Legacy footwear challenges deteriorated; all signs point to much more challenged athletic footwear backdrop into 2H (weighs on entire sub-sector)
- DKS signaled athletic footwear backdrop not improving, deteriorating further; legacy footwear "hangover" weighs on 2H margins for both banners (more FL vs DSG) in overly promotional category with flooded marketplace lacking core innovation
- "following their FY guide down, we now view a 2027 Bull Case EPS as below where the Bear Case sat prior"
- Other Shoe Drops Harder: material change in athletic footwear over Summer vs expectation of improvement by now; aggressive promotions to clear excess inventory, MAP breaks, some retailers cutting well below MAP; much worse than expected; negative laterals to brands, esp NKE (DKS-READ for peers); gives bears evidence FL takes longer to turn and flow-through can't be managed
- Core DSG "OK": comp +4.9% in line; core GMs expanded (ex refunds); promo backdrop requires participation, driving down FY margin outlook; 2H GMs planned down (3Q weighted) as DSG takes share; FY margin plan cut ~50bps; DSG also impacted by legacy footwear though more tethered to FL; positives: 1) comp guide maintained, 2) plans to show expense scale exiting FY
- FL Turn "Kicked" Out: comp -3.6% below plan → material 2H profit cut; weak launch calendar, launches that occurred didn't resonate, athletic inventories elevated, EMEA worsened; FL taking pain on clearance while continuing to invest; FL revs and EBIT both cut ~$200M (implies 100% flow-through on lost sales) → concern on margin guardrails
- 2H goes from inflection story to cleanup story: 2026 EPS gdnc $11.00-$12.00 = ~20% cut at mid pt; negative FL comps; GM pressure both banners from promos, most pronounced 3Q (implies downbeat back-to-school view); FL now expected to lose money; hard to see expansion next yr w/o improvement in underlying athletic footwear category

### Scenarios
- Base $185: ~13.5x FY27 EPS $13.70
- Bull $210: ~15x P/E on 2027 EPS ~$14.00 (above forecast); drivers: (1) athletic footwear stabilizes; (2) core DSG sustains +MSD comps; (3) clearance sets up FL inflection in 2027; (4) new store concepts, GameChanger, paid loyalty, DMN drive profit
- Bear $110: ~10x FY27 EPS ~$10.00; drivers: (1) core DSG slows further, margins lower as lacks scale on investment spend; (2) FL inflection pushed out again, lack of profit becomes structural concern; (3) spending accelerates without ROI
- Catalysts: credit card and traffic data updates; FL 2H actual performance and clearance into 2027
- Risks: comps decelerate/turn negative; EBIT margin gains reverse; FL acquisition long-term dilutive or slower turn

### Investment thesis (WF)
- OW: 1) upside from FL recovery applying DKS merchandising/vendor playbook, modest margin recovery embedded; 2) core DSG established leader at structurally higher productivity; 3) new stores, GameChanger, paid loyalty, Retail Media early innings of differentiated flywheel
- FL recovery: getting comfortable FL is fixable = gating item; strategic rationale sound (DSG omni/merch/brand relationships address FL weaknesses), evidenced by early Fast Break initiatives comping +DD w/ improving merch margin; Nike's reduction in wholesale allocation at FL ~1,000bps impact to sales, WF est ~490bps impact to OM (over half the deterioration) — "clearly reversing"; Nike NA healthy growth hard to reconcile given commentary on deteriorating sell-through starting April this year, but trajectory improving from trough; FL OM return to >7% (still below pre-COVID) could drive DKS EPS >$20.00 all else equal; but summer 2026 legacy footwear deterioration and another year of negative margins push out timeline and raise structural questions
- Core DSG: +LSD underlying comp plus House of Sport expansion and Field House relocations/renovations → +LSD/+MSD sales growth w/ ticket and traffic; omni capabilities in-housed since 2017 unmatched; brand partnerships shifted power to DSG (partner of choice while brands in full-price lifecycle); store productivity positive → occupancy leverage, reinvest in SG&A; margins comfortably LDD; DKS capex guide ~$1B for DSG banner ~5x the $200M-$240M ASO guided this year, >300bps higher as % segment sales
- Flywheel: store concepts (House of Sport, Field House, Going, Going, Gone!, Golf Galaxy); GameChanger direct access to youth sports customers who spend most; growing loyalty program + potential recurring subscription revenue; DMN positioned for more brand investment (highly profitable "advertising" revenue)
- Bull (buyside): FL acquired near trough on trough profitability, merch/vendor synergies on reset 2026 numbers; consistent execution in base biz at higher productivity; share position + optionality from DMN and GameChanger
- Bear (buyside): FL "albatross" amid footwear market turn for worse; margin outlook less visible given remodels in comp and high investment holding back leverage; footwear innovation/newness fails, business over-exposed to Nike turnaround
