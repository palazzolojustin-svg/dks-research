### Thesis
- 2Q consolidated adj EPS $3.53; FY EPS gdnc cut to $11.00-$12.00 from $13.50-$14.50; core franchise healthy: DICK'S banner comp 4.9%, share gains, transactions and avg ticket up, outperformed sporting goods industry by ~200 bps
- Reset tied primarily to excess legacy footwear inventory, heightened promo activity, and FL where expected OP $110-$150mm became projected loss $40-$80mm
- Buy maintained: shares can rise as investors gain confidence margin pressure = "temporary inventory and product-cycle adjustment rather than a permanent reduction in earnings power"
- Key debate = duration of footwear correction and FL losses, not core DICK'S health
- "one of the more consequential and nuanced quarters recently across retail"; earnings revision attributable to industry-wide inventory dislocation + near-term FL pressures, not deterioration of DICK'S franchise
- Mgmt maintained DICK'S segment FY comp 2.5%-4% → implies 2-3% same-store sales growth in 2H26 despite increasingly promotional env

### Demand / industry framing (mgmt via UBS)
- Company repeatedly emphasized consumers have not pulled back from athletic footwear as category; spending shifted from mature lifestyle franchises to newer performance-oriented products, innovation-driven launches, emerging brands; legacy franchise demand slowed → inventory accumulated across brands, wholesale, retailers → widespread promo
- DKS frames env as supply-side/product-cycle adjustment, not demand downturn; strength in running, performance footwear, team sports, outdoor, soccer, UGG, Birkenstock, several newer apparel concepts; consumers more selective on product, innovation, brand relevance
- Mgmt investing in price/competitiveness rather than protecting margin; called env a market-share opportunity ("maintaining value leadership during periods of industry stress often represents one of the highest-return investments available"); consistent w/ historical playbook (scale, balance sheet, vendor relationships, ops while weaker competitors pressured)
- Increasing investment in advertising and wages (FL) while sales declining; with promo intensity → FL swing from $110-150mm gain to -$40 to -$80mm loss; i.e. DKS expects $200mm sales swing → $200mm profit swing for FL, incl deeper investments not previously factored
- GM pressure "real and could persist through year-end"; mgmt confident once excess inventory cleared DKS emerges w/ stronger customer relationships, positioning, larger share; earnings "deferred rather than structurally impaired" (UBS view)

### Foot Locker
- Primary source of market concern and principal reason gdnc lowered; multiple simultaneous headwinds rather than single breakdown
- FL disproportionately exposed to most pressured categories: legacy athletic footwear silhouettes, launch-driven cycles (UBS interprets: Air Force 1s, Dunks, Jordan 1s, Sambas); launch productivity below expectations in qtr; EMEA materially more promotional and economically challenged than anticipated
- Mgmt: shortfall from sharp slowdown in legacy lifestyle footwear, weaker launch productivity, fewer launches in 2Q vs 1Q; challenge extends beyond Nike, tied to consumers' desire for newness; FL lacked sufficient exposure to categories/products driving demand; expects some improvement 4Q vs 3Q but promo pressure elevated, visibility "somewhat limited"
- Strategic rationale unchanged: unique global platform deepening brand partner relationships, expanding reach in athletic ecosystem; little sign mgmt conviction weakened
- EMEA expected to need longer recovery than North America; EMEA consumers cautious amid geopolitical tensions, elevated fuel costs → industry-wide excess inventory
- Store optionality: ~1,600 North American FL stores, ~$3.0mm annual sales/location; per 100 closures, 30% recapture → $90-$100mm to DKS stores, +60-80 bps to core growth, at 40%+ incremental margin ~+$0.30 EPS (underappreciated)

### Growth engines
- House of Sport, Field House, GameChanger, DICK'S Media Network (DMN) performing well, increasingly contributing to profitability; diversify earnings beyond retail; 2022-2024 investments paying off
- DMN reached profitability; mgmt: meaningful future growth/profit contributor
- GameChanger: mgmt reiterated 30%-40% CAGR; highly profitable, cash-generative, EBITDA accretive; UBS: "significant hidden asset"

### Outlook view (UBS)
- Footwear demand normalizing after years of strong lifestyle consumption; not a 1-2 qtr adjustment; cycle could remain challenged into 2027 as consumers work through elevated lifestyle penetration and brands reset pipelines/assortments/launch calendars; DKS needs other categories to offset
- Near-term estimates "almost certainly need to move lower" given FL and EMEA

### 2Q results detail
- Comp: DICK'S 4.9% vs market bar ~5.0%, pre-print UBSe 5.0%, cons 4.5%; FL -3.6% incl -3.3% international vs UBS 0.0%, cons -0.2%, bar -3% to -4%
- GM: FL GM 25.7% on promo pressure from legacy silhouette inventory; FL got $2mm tariff refunds in non-GAAP merch margin; DICK'S segment GM +~80 bps to 37.8% driven by DMN and GameChanger growth + $19mm tariff refund benefit
- OM: DICK'S segment 12.6%, -42 bps y/y (targeted pricing investments amid promo); FL op loss $31.90mm, OM -1.8%, -124 bps y/y (promo + turnaround investment)
- SG&A: FL integration added ~$477mm to consolidated SG&A in qtr; FL investments incl first major brand marketing campaign in >10 yrs and higher store associate payroll; DICK'S SG&A deleveraged on World Cup marketing and digital/in-store experience spend; macro/geopolitical pressure on fuel, supply chain, healthcare, other op costs
- Tariffs: ~$60mm refunds received in 2Q; ~$21mm in qtr results (merch sold 1H): $19mm DKS, $2mm FL; ~$38mm prior-year, excluded from non-GAAP EPS; vast majority now received, de minimis expected 2H; largely reinvested to stay price-competitive and offset fuel/supply chain/inflation
- Adj EPS $3.53 vs pre-print UBSe $3.60, cons $3.76

### Bull / bear post-print
- Bulls: bad news largely priced, shares <13x EPS and below levels at time of FL acquisition; revised gdnc de-risked/achievable; drivers: FL operational fixes, stronger launch pipeline, reduced lifestyle exposure over time, improving credit card data, back-to-school; many model FY'27 EPS ~$13.00
- Bears: FL lifestyle franchise issues (AF1, Jordans, Dunks, Sambas) won't dissipate next yr+; FL could become "black hole" absorbing earnings elsewhere; parallels to Family Dollar's impact on Dollar Tree; core DKS moderating; reminder of model volatility; market may assign permanently lower multiple; bears model ~$10.00 FY'27 EPS

### Updated FY26 guidance (per UBS)
- Promotionality = single largest swing factor between low and high end
- DKS comp 2.5%-4.0% reiterated; outpacing industry by nearly 200 bps
- FL comp -2.0% to 0% (was +1.5%-3.0%): promo market, excess legacy inventory, fewer launches than anticipated, longer EMEA recovery
- DKS OM 10.6%-10.9% (was 11.0%-11.4%; UBSe 11.1%); modest GM pressure from promo + higher fuel/supply chain; GM pressure most pronounced 3Q vs 4Q; FY SG&A deleverage, ~50 bps deleverage at midpoint in 3Q
- FL OP loss -$80mm to -$40mm (was profit $110-$150mm) → OM -1.1% to -0.5% (was ~1.4%-1.9%; UBSe -0.9%)
- Non-GAAP EPS $11.00-$12.00 (was $13.50-$14.50; UBSe $11.50); FY tax rate ~29% (up ~200 bps from ~27%) on foreign earnings mix + purchase accounting adj; EPS headwind ~$0.35
