### "Footwear Reset" thesis
- Reaction reflects significant reset of investor expectations for athletic footwear ecosystem; Dick's delivered robust +4.9% comp and held FY sales outlook, but industry-wide reset from slowing legacy lifestyle footwear, weak launch product, elevated inventory, increasingly promotional marketplace
- Performance categories incl running healthy; pressure concentrated in classic lifestyle silhouettes → issue extends beyond Nike to much of lifestyle footwear complex
- Largest surprise: magnitude of FL reset, $110mn-$150mn profit → $40mn-$80mn loss; reinforces concern footwear correction and FL turnaround take longer than anticipated
- Key debate shifted from share gains (Dick's continues to demonstrate) to duration of lifestyle footwear correction and timing of return to meaningful product innovation to reignite category growth and restore margin expansion
- "Despite lack of visibility, we believe 2H26 expectations are reset." Near term, lack of evidence of FL turnaround but sell-off reflects much negative sentiment; expect shares "largely range bound until there is greater clarity on innovation, which is slated to come from many of its branded partners during 2027"
- FL: in legacy silhouettes/fewer launches/retro, mgmt cited more promo env due to inventory buildup → MAP (minimum advertised price) breaks; FL expected to have weaker fall season despite earlier gdnc that forecast back-to-school as key turnaround period
- Dick's FY26 comp reiterated but expected to moderate from current levels in 2H26
- FY26 adj OI gdnc lowered $260mn at midpoint (-15%): Dick's -$70mn, FL -$190mn

### 2Q26 additions
- Dick's comp +4.9% = avg ticket +3.6% + transactions +1.3%
- Pro forma comp +2.1% vs cons +3.6%
- GM 34.1% beat (cons 33.9%) driven by $21mn tariff refund benefit; core Dick's GM improvement from DMN and GameChanger growth offset higher promotions, fuel and supply chain costs, and negative mix from FL acquisition
- OpEx 25.9% vs cons 25.5%: lower sales, FL acquisition impact, World Cup marketing, digital/in-store experience investment, higher teammate healthcare costs
- Cons as of 8:00 AM ET 08/25/2026

### Tariffs
- 2Q26 tariff refunds $59.0mn + $2.1mn related interest income; $57mn core Dick's, $2mn FL
- ~$21mn recognized in adj 2Q26 ($19mn Dick's merch margin, $2mn FL merch margin); remaining $38mn excluded (related to FY25 tariff expense); $2.1mn interest also excluded; $38mn + $2.1mn lifted GAAP pre-tax ~$40mn above adjusted
- Largely reinvested to stay price-competitive and offset fuel/supply chain/inflation; substantially all refunds now received, no material additional refund claims outstanding
- Tariffs not addressed on call; on 4Q25 call mgmt said tariffs not a material driver of 4Q25 or FY26 gdnc

### PRISM Curve (Barclays proprietary)
- Leading indicator of sales inflection based on proactive mgmt actions (purge inventory, cut costs, share best practices, invest in product/brand)
- Dick's in HUNT phase (strategically offensive; in control of taking share; Region C = max potential stock appreciation, hold/accumulate; may last multiple years)
- Foot Locker in TRAP phase (strategically defensive): fundamentals deteriorating, stiff competition in all categories, pricing power fleeting for core franchises, share erosion → operating deleverage; actions taken but not yet stabilized
- Figs 2-7 (PRISM charts, promo analysis/scores/cadence, Absolute Promo Tracker 2Q17-present; no data 1Q20-2Q20 due to COVID) image-only; no values extractable. Figs 8-9 (IM Spread 1Q08A-present; GMROI/OMROI trailing 4Q) axis labels only

### Estimates / PT change
- FY26/FY27/FY28 adj EPS $11.04/$12.28/$13.62 (from $14.48/$16.48/$18.03): lower GM and sales assumptions, greater SG&A deleverage
- Multiple cut 6 turns (17x→11x) on roll-forward to FY28 and elevated fundamental risk (esp FL) from highly promotional athletic footwear/apparel market; still above Street NTM multiple 8.3x given confidence core Dick's delivers consistent results and keeps gaining share
- Why OW: 1) sustainable positive brick-and-mortar comps; 2) elevated product offering for enthusiast → higher AUR, fewer promotions; 3) increased private label; 4) share gains in sporting goods retail
- Upside $165 = 11x NTM P/E on CY28 earnings power $14.98 (positive B&M comp, margin expansion, better-than-expected merger synergies); Downside $98 = 8x on CY28 $12.26 (negative comps, multiple and margin contraction, lower synergies)
- Risks: consumer discretionary income weakness; inflationary costs; non-exclusive branded product in competing channels; brands going DTC and regional competitors; comp deceleration
