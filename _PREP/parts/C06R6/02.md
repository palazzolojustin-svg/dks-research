### Summary
- 2Q26 missed on sales and EPS; core DICK'S comp +4.9%; Foot Locker comps turned negative -3.6%; FY26 gdnc cut for sales and EPS; FL sales/margin gdnc lowered; core DICK'S comp gdnc unchanged but margins now seen lower
- "Despite challenging footwear backdrop, core DICK'S proves the strength of the model; Foot Locker exposes the weakness of the category." Despite increasingly promotional footwear/apparel env, DICK'S delivered +4.9% comps w/ broad-based growth, higher transactions, share gains
- FL -3.6% comp and sales outlook cut underscore pressures on footwear-centric retailers tied to legacy silhouettes, fewer launches, retro product
- With FY26 comp reiterated for DICK'S banner, likely to moderate from current levels; FL expected to have weaker fall season
- FY26 adj OI lowered on expectations for increasingly challenging footwear industry ("as has been evidenced by the footwear brands in recent weeks")
- "We believe the long-term strength of DKS as the dominant player remains, but near-term industry trends will impact the Foot Locker turnaround."
