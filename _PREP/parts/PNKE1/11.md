- "Working Out 'The Lats' - Recent Fieldwork, Industry Access Takes & Sportswear Primer Points". Underweight (unch, since 04-Aug-26 at $42.64, PT $40); price $39.60 (28 Aug 26).
- Purpose: share takeaways by region from global channel work + lateral reports (DKS, JD Sports, Pou Sheng).
### I. China — promotional intensity rising ahead of Jan online reset
- Fieldwork: select Tier 1 online dealers incl YY Sports (owned by Pou Sheng) and Topsports already began inventory reduction to limit Nike exposure before Jan 1 2027 online termination; actions started in July; clearance focused on Nike lifestyle and Jordan, apparel + footwear (primarily footwear).
- Pou Sheng monthly sales: July -13.9% y/y; June -8.6%; May -2.7%; April +0.1%.
- Reiterates: $1B+ unmitigated annualized headwind (~20% of FY26-end Greater China rev); possible recapture on official Nike flagships depending on innovation pace, local marketing (price key driver on partner storefronts).
- Broad-based promotional China marketplace: Pou Sheng + Topsports = majority of sector share → "knock-on effect" to nearly all sportswear retailers + other brands reacting to depth of Nike discounts; sequential deceleration of industry sales into July/August (worse than partners' initial expectation of bottoming in Apr/May). Channel partners (e.g. Pou Sheng) reducing forward orderbooks with NKE; below-plan sell-through on recent launches in region. Wildcard: local brands + national brands (e.g. Adidas) aggressively pursuing share Nike donates online after Jan 1 2027 cutoff (outside Nike's own flagship sites).
### II. North America — elevated promotions through "at least" 4Q/Holiday
- "Trifecta" of NA demand constraints: JD Sports 8/20 2Q (May-Jul) trading update: NA LFL -6.8% (vs 1Q -0.6%, -620bps); mgmt: NA "reflects weaker core consumer sentiment, a slower quarter for high-heat footwear product, and deferred 'back-to-school' demand from July into the first half of August." Labor Day 9/7 this year vs 9/1 last year → school start dates pushed ~1 week. JD NA fascias: JD US, JD Canada, Hibbett, DTLR, Shoe Palace, Finish Line (standalone + .com) and Macy's Finish Line concessions; JD US & Canada outperformed (comp -1.1% in 2Q; more multi-brand, apparel-weighted); total -6.8% pressured by Hibbett, DTLR, Shoe Palace, Finish Line (dotcom + legacy stores being wound down, source of promotional activity); JPM believes Nike lifestyle footwear key contributor; estimates NKE >40% of JD total revenues.
- DKS-READ (DKS 8/25 2Q (May-Jul) EPS call, quoted by JPM): "As demand continued to shift during the quarter, inventory built up in parts of the industry, particularly within certain legacy footwear silhouettes and apparel franchises that simply aren't resonating the way they once did. The inventory built up across the industry supply chains and across the retail marketplace, which led to an increasingly aggressive promotional environment." —DKS mgmt. Impact across DKS and FL banners, greater at FL (higher exposure to legacy footwear silhouettes, greater dependence on launch & retro; NKE ~60% of FL revenue mix); core DKS more diversified brand/category mix.
- DKS-READ: DKS 2Q comps: FL -3.6% y/y (vs 1Q +0.6%, -420bps); core DKS +4.9% (vs 1Q +6.0%, -110bps).
- DKS-READ quote: "We're taking action to shift the mix toward in-demand brands, and while we expect the launch calendar to be more favorable in the back half of the year, the quality of those launches will be critical. We expect the broader promotional environment, particularly around legacy silhouettes, to remain challenging at least through the fourth quarter" —DKS mgmt (JPM: = at least through NKE F3Q27).
- Lifestyle footwear headwind only partly offset by performance: JD noted tougher compares (launch schedule shift 1Q→2Q prior year), ongoing softness in end-of-cycle footwear lines (JPM: lifestyle silhouettes), partly offset by performance running (e.g. NKE Vomero + other brands) and newer styles. DKS-READ quote: "I really do believe this is focused on the lifestyle legacy silhouettes that aren't doing well. The performance aspects of the category and then some of the newness that Ed mentioned continued to do really well" —DKS mgmt; Nike Vomero and Peg cited as strong performance running silhouettes. Nike Sportswear + Jordan Streetwear (all Jordan non-performance) = 60% of Nike total revenue.
### III. Europe — promotions greater than US
- DKS-READ: DKS cited "very aggressive" EMEA promotional environment on elevated industry inventory and "even more cautious" consumer (geopolitics, higher fuel): "The impact on EMEA is much greater than what's going on in the U.S. It's a more competitive market. It's a much more promotional market than the U.S. right now. Based on fuel costs, the consumer is much more cautious in EMEA." —DKS mgmt.
- JD Sports: Europe 2Q "continued to be impacted by a subdued consumer environment, amidst a promotional market"; UK "underlying performance remained challenged, particularly in footwear."
- Fieldwork: performance-led product outperforming lifestyle → extended clearance of legacy lifestyle inventory. Consumers increasingly shop multi-brand across sports (vs one brand across all sports pre-pandemic); specialist brands (performance road running, trail/hiking, tennis, padel, golf) outperforming "mega brands" (NKE) whose mix concentrated in lifestyle. (DKS-READ: multi-brand shopping behavior.)
### Model tie-back (from 8/4 downgrade)
- FY27 EPS $1.55 (cons $1.72); 1Q27 rev -3.9% (mgmt -LSD-MSD%, Street -2.8%); 2Q27 -5.9% (Street -4.4%) — multi-point headwind: (i) lap of higher prior-yr digital promotions esp EMEA, (ii) timing of NA wholesale shipments; 2H27 -4.6% (nearly 500bps below Street ~flat) vs 1H27 -4.9%; below-cons Greater China (online sales loss) and NA (store closures).
- Incremental downside not in model: laterals point to lifestyle sneaker underperformance into BTS in 1Q27 (Jun-Aug) esp NA + EMEA; into 2Q/2H27 knock-on inventory/promotional headwinds across NA, EMEA, Greater China.
