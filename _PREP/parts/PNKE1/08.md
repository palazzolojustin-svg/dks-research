- Method: monthly tracker of "high heat" launches = sell out completely in primary channels then trade at premium on resale (StockX, GOAT), excl low-volume niche launches; proxy for sneaker category demand.
### July 2026
- Only 7 high-heat launches vs 21 in July 2025 and below July 2024's "highly-disappointing" 8. Lack of meaningful innovation/"buzz" + less-than-ideal macro → "it seems clear to us that the sneaker market is challenged at the moment." [Figs 1-3: July high-heat launches; historical July data; July 2025 reference]
- Jordan (6 retro launches vs 8 retro in Jul 2025 + Jordan 40 debut): not a single July retro Jordan trading at resale premium (every one at discount) despite reduced supply → Jordan faces difficult demand environment despite significant supply pullback in recent years. July cohort: Jordan 4 Kids "Sweet Beet", Jordan 3 "True Blue", Jordan 4 Women's "Coconut Milk", Jordan 4 "Comic", Jordan 7 "Miro", Jordan 3 Cleats "True Blue" (images sourced Nike.com, Footlocker.com). [Figs 4-5]
- Dunk: 0 launches in July vs 2 in July 2025; NKE less enthusiastic about Dunk franchise than in many years → very big supply decline.
- Nike Mind (2 launches): recent success; pre/post-activity shoes with sole texturing to stimulate athlete's mind; every launch so far sold out quickly; July Mind 001 "Indigo Burst" slide and Mind 002 "Black and Indigo Burst" sneaker both sold out, trading at premiums. [Fig 6]
### August 2026 (back-to-school)
- 25 potential high-heat launches vs 20 high-heat in Aug 2025; tough start: 3 of 5 Jordan launches already released trade at resale discounts; few "must have" styles (no Mind, no Dunks) → could mean challenging back-to-school season overall. (DKS-READ: weak BTS sneaker heat; Footlocker.com a primary Jordan launch channel.) [Figs 7-9: Aug pipeline; historical Aug data; Aug 2025 reference]
- Dunk: none planned Aug → ~3 months without a Dunk launch (last June 1); 1 Dunk launch Aug 2025. No Mind launches planned Aug.
- Jordan: 8 launches planned Aug 2026 vs Aug 2025 9 retro + 2 high-heat modern (Jordan 40 colorway; women's "Trunner"); front-loaded (5 released); 3 of 5 slow, at discount—improvement vs July but "far cry from the Jordan brand's heyday". Aug pipeline: Jordan 13 "Flint", Jordan 13 "Wings", Jordan 1 "Love Letter", Kids Jordan 3 "Play Your Way", Jordan 6 "Oreo", Jordan 3 Women's "Sport Renaissance", Jordan 1 Low "Laser", Jordan 3 "Phantom and Palomino" (sources FootLocker.com, Nike.com).
