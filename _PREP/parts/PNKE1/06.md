### China digital marketplace change
- Jul 21 2026: Topsports and Pou Sheng separate press releases: Nike gave notice "that the Group's existing online sales of Nike's products in mainland China will terminate completely with effect from January 1, 2027." Nike essay by Greater China GM Cathy Sparks ("How We're Reimagining the Marketplace in Greater China to Better Serve Athletes").
- Why: China difficult since 2021 (Xinjiang controversy, rise of "guochao" domestic-brand preference); Anta, Li Ning took significant share at significant discount to Nike. Region once consistent 20%+ growth with segment OM approaching 40%; last two years sales declined low-teens, margins collapsed toward ~20%. Market increasingly fragmented, heavily promotional, primarily from digital; markdowns hurt brand perception, muddied journey; revs unproductive. WF checks: brand has become option only bought on discount (competition + lack of brand resonance—athletes not culturally/currently relevant—and lack of clear innovation). NKE wants more premium digital marketplace, stronger retail experience, more locally created product. "This is not about reducing access, it's about reducing fragmentation." WF: less like forcing channel shift (vs NA DTC push), more like resetting unproductive part of business.
- Open questions: (1) total rev/profit size for region (other smaller distributors also impacted; how much spend comes out to offset deleverage); (2) impact on retail store allocation (Topsports retail = 65%+ of its business); (3) partner behavior before Jan—clearing/ordering inventory, where unsold digital product goes; may deepen near-term promotions; does Nike pay for cleanup; (4) more restructuring to come (stores rebased lower?).

### Thesis (EW)
- (1) NA improving as classics headwind dissipates; international deteriorating, near-term drag; (2) limited catalysts until "Win Now" actions end by CY-end 2026 and "Sports Offense" impacts Spring 2027; (3) little upside to out-year earnings, little valuation support for muted/prolonged recovery.
- Key themes: (1) Elliot Hill (30+ yr veteran) rejoined as CEO late 2024: lead with sport/athletes, pivot away from lifestyle; sports-led org across 10 sports; demand creation now 10%+ of sales vs 7-8% prior 5 yrs, shift performance→brand marketing; rebuild integrated marketplace (wholesale) after post-COVID deprioritization, liquidate aged inventory; invest in specialty channels (running, football/soccer) via sales org (EKINs) for full-price selling; timeline taking significantly longer than anticipated. (2) NA: classics cleanup largest rev headwind last 12-24 mo (MSD-HSD total rev headwind), cumulative ~$4B rev cleared; region grew past 3 qtrs driven by outsized wholesale growth as brand returns to channel; key debate: can growth continue lapping significant wholesale sell-in given weakening sell-through commentary on 4Q26 call after step-down in demand mid-April (DKS-READ: NKE wholesale sell-in to US retailers lapping; sell-through softened mid-Apr 2026). (3) International: China (Anta, Li Ning better tech, lower price, China-centric shift; On, Hoka took share via trail/road running at expense of basketball); China OM down (1,700)bps over 5 yrs, (950+)bps over 3 yrs; ending 3P digital sales from 2027 → restructuring weighs through next year. EMEA inventory significantly elevated (+20% in 3Q26 vs c-c sales -7%; still elevated 4Q26); uncertain share recapture esp specialty channels that replaced Nike with competitors. (4) Limited catalysts; Nov 2026 analyst day may give clarity; "even if things go well, we see little upside above ~$2.00 in EPS Power in the out-years"; no catalyst for multiple to expand beyond current levels above peer group.
- Bull (buyside): favorable risk-reward, brand LT opportunities intact; Hill aggressively cleaning inventory; FY27 numbers reset, sentiment low; clean marketplace → growth via sport. Bear: choppy NT, geographies on separate timelines; China structurally challenged beyond 2027; strong competitors On, Hoka (US), local brands China, Salomon, Arc'Teryx; shift to trail/outdoor vs indoor sports; >20x NTM P/E elevated vs peers growing earnings; limited upside to earnings power esp given shift to more variable distribution model.
