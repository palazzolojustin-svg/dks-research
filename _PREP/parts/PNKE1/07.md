### I. "Win Now" financial impact extends into 2H27/FY28
- Mgmt access clarification: financial impact of Win Now decisions made through end CY2026 will linger into 2H27 and FY28. Win Now launched Dec '24 under CEO Hill (hired Oct '24): (i) refocus culture on Sport & growth, (ii) emotional connections/storytelling, (iii) accelerate innovative product across fields of play, (iv) elevate and grow marketplace, (v) win on the ground with localized connections; changes across leadership, structure (Sport Offense vs prior Gender construct), supply chain, marketplace.
- 7/15 10-K: "our reportable operating segments are at different stages of progress, and we expect to complete these actions by the end of December 2026. The timing of financial impacts has varied and will continue to vary by segment. North America has made the most progress against these actions, while Greater China and Converse will take more time." Also: "we expect negative impacts from Greater China and Converse to continue throughout fiscal 2027" (consistent w/ 6/30 4Q26 call).
- (1) Greater China online reset = $1B+ unmitigated rev headwind from Jan '27: 7/21 announcement—from Jan 2027 China digital anchored by official Nike flagship experiences on Tmall, JD.com, Douyin + Nike.com.cn + Nike App; partner-operated online storefronts transition out of selling Nike. Rationale: single elevated digital destination, clearer presentation, stronger storytelling, connected journeys. JPM math: $1B+ annualized (~20% of FY26-end Greater China rev) based on Topsports + Pou Sheng disclosures; potential mitigation via recapture on official flagships (magnitude depends on innovation pace + local marketing; price has been key driver on partner storefronts). Timing: begins Jan 1 2027 = mid-3Q27; fully anniversaried 3Q28. Profit headwind far less material (aligned w/ Pou Sheng lateral commentary; NKE 6/30: "we continue to believe that profitability will bottom before sales in this market"). [Fig 1: China partner lateral commentary]
- (2) N. America US store footprint consolidation = headwind through 1H28: July '26 closure announcements across CA, FL, GA, IL, KY, MD, MO, NJ, NC, TX. FY26 US retail fleet -7.7% y/y: 347 stores FY26-end vs 376 FY25 (18 Converse closures → 60; 10 Nike in-line closures → 75; 1 Nike factory closure → 212). Current US store directory: 252 Nike Brand stores (in-line + factory) vs 287 at FY26-end → 35 Nike closures since May '26-end; Converse locator 62 (vs 60). Total 314 US locations vs 347 = -10% y/y (-18% 2-yr stacked). Rev headwind to NA until closures annualize (Jul '27 / mid-1Q28). FY26 Direct rev -6%: digital -10%, stores -2% (comp -2% and -7.7% store count reduction). (DKS-READ: Nike shrinking own US store fleet/digital → more volume through wholesale partners.) [Fig 2: US store count -10% vs FY26-end]

### III. Six investor pivot points (likely Investor Day topics)
- #1 Category maturity: Euromonitor global sports apparel & footwear growth +4.5% FY25-28 vs +6.2% pre-pandemic; outlook cut ~120bps vs year-ago forecast. Sports A&F ~24% of total apparel & footwear spend vs ~16% in 2015 (+~800bps). Growth premium vs total A&F narrowed to ~+130bps (from +410bps pre-pandemic). North America largest driver of downgrade: $167B TAM now +3.0% in 2026/2027 (cut from +5.1% a year ago; vs +6.5% 2015-19); by 2028 NA sports vs total apparel spread ~30bps (vs ~460bps pre-pandemic); NA wallet share already ~35%. (DKS-READ: NA athletic category growth decelerating to ~3%.) [Fig 3]
- #2 "Ankle biters": Nike sports footwear share down ~300bps vs 2019 to ~19%; On/Hoka/Brooks cohort from collectively <10% in 2019 (many sub-1%) to ~17% today; Nike's >10-pt lead vs cohort compressed to ~200bps; question whether consumer behavior structurally shifted to lower loyalty, more multi-brand trial. (DKS-READ: multi-brand trial favors multibrand retailers.) [Fig 4]
- #3 China: Nike brand share down ~590bps vs 2019 to 12.9% (2025) in sports A&F; local brands (Li Ning, Anta, 361 Degrees, Xtep, others) gained >1,000bps since 2019. Nike China rev from ~1% FY25-28 CAGR to -16% FY25-28E CAGR on JPM model (digital rationalization). Not broad rejection of global brands: last 12 mo Amer Sports +44%, Coach (TPR) +40%, Ralph Lauren >30% in China. China ~25% of Nike segment profit pre-pandemic with >38% OM (FY19); today ~12% of segment profit, margins down >1,000bps to ~21.9% → ~50% reduction in EBIT $ vs peak. [Fig 5]
- #4 Portfolio/mix: Converse ~100bps avg headwind to total rev growth FY24-27E; Sportswear ~60% of Nike rev, forecast slowest-growing segment of TAM (from ~7% pre-pandemic to ~4.2% CAGR through 2030E) → more assortment/mix actions likely, re-tilt to performance. [Fig 6]
- #5 Marketing: demand creation ~10% of sales; mix focus: athlete contracts/top-of-funnel vs mid-funnel/influencer; "owning the athlete" vs "owning the community". Examples: FP Movement x Asics via localized micro-influencer; Adidas China viral "Urban Errands" meme converted to exposure within days. Effectiveness more dependent on agility, localization, mid-funnel. [Fig 7]
- #6 Double-digit OM timing: bridge from ~6.6% FY27 to 10%+ FY30; FY30 GPM 44.6% (mgmt cited "mid-40s"; FY19 44.7%); demand creation ~10% (10.4% FY30); op overhead 23.7% of sales FY30 (vs ~22.9% pre-pandemic) → annual leverage via cost savings + sales recapture; debate what's needed to return toward ~12% FY19 OM. [Fig 8]
### Thesis / valuation / risks
- Thesis: global athletic leader; initial greenshoots from Sport Offense in NA and running; international (EMEA, Greater China, APLA) still resetting marketplace; sell-through challenged globally → elongated timeline to rev inflection and double-digit OM; estimates below cons; NA DTC store closures + China online changes incremental risk to cons.
- Valuation: Dec '27 PT $40 = ~20.5x (also stated ~21x) CY28E EPS $1.97, linear + peer regression; discount to 27x pre-pandemic 3-yr avg.
- Risks (upside): economy/employment; stronger household spending; ~50% of rev outside US (weaker USD upside); SE Asia manufacturing exposure—tariff easing could improve product costs.
