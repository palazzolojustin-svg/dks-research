### FY27 (FYE May 2027)
- Sec 1 Post-1Q FY27 outlook (10/01/26) "Nike Revenue Reset Extends Into Fiscal 2028": reset keeps sales pressured into FY2028 as mgmt cuts supply in sportswear and Jordan and cleans up Greater China digital marketplace; performance portfolio keeps growing. FY2027 rev gdnc = high-single-digit decline; 2Q faces ~400bps added pressure from EMEA Cyber Week and North America wholesale comparisons (DKS-READ). GM pressured by discounts, mix, fixed-cost deleverage, higher input costs; partly offset by supply-chain savings, better full-price realization. China digital cleanup to take multiple seasons. New cost savings program "Pace" to lower cost base ~$2.5 billion, most savings FY2029-30; reinvestment could limit near-term profit upside.
  - 1Q FY27 results: FX-neutral sales -5%, reported -4%; FX-neutral NA +2%, EMEA -5%, Greater China -26%, APLA flat; Nike Brand digital -13%, Nike-owned stores -5%, wholesale -1%; GM +60bps (lower warehousing, logistics); GM pressure expected for year; SG&A -3% (lower wage, admin); EPS $0.48 beat cons; FY2027 sales could fall high-single digits.
- Sec 2 1Q FY27 preview (09/15/26) "Nike's China Drag, North America Gains Shape Reset": uneven recovery—NA growth partly offsets Greater China weakness, softer EMEA, APLA; newer launches and wholesale momentum may support demand; DTC pressure keeps reset gradual; GM can improve as supply-chain actions counter tariffs; sales must revert to gains for meaningful turnaround. Watch: China inflection, tariff mitigation, order-book visibility, innovation broadening beyond a handful of launches, Caitlin Clark signature shoe.
  - Cons: 1Q sales -3% (EMEA -4.3%, APLA -3%, Greater China -13.3%, NA +1.8%); footwear -3.2%, apparel -2%, equipment -1.5%; Bloomberg Second Measure data suggest sales falling; GM +28bps (supply-chain actions); cons EPS $0.45; sales trends could improve in FY2027.
