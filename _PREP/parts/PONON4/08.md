### Summary
- High-teens sales growth should extend through 2029 (low share, rising awareness, geographic whitespace); faster EMEA & APAC broaden regional mix; run, sneaker, apparel drive most of >CHF2bn planned incremental sales; scale & automation lift adj EBITDA margin to ≥22%; stronger cash generation → increasingly self-funding; first $1bn buyback authorization.

### 1. Core categories anchor 2029 plan
- High-teens CAGR "appears achievable" as relies mainly on established businesses. Run, sneaker/lifestyle, apparel >75% of absolute sales increase thru 2029; apparel targeted to triple; running = largest R&D investment. Training, tennis, outdoor, kids additional growth; football & golf modest in 3-yr plan. Limited soccer drops 2027; standard cleats & performance all-day products launch 2028 → newer sports = potential upside/awareness drivers, not required for ≥CHF5.6bn 2029.
- DTC 50% of sales by 2029 from ~45% in 2026 (store expansion, stronger e-com); wholesale remains major growth engine via deeper penetration and more shelf space with existing partners.

### 2. International diversifies mix
- APAC fastest, led by China (on track to exceed 10% of sales; expanding premium retail). EMEA high-teens to low-20s annual growth over 3 yrs mainly from closing penetration gaps in existing markets: share in southern & eastern Europe, Benelux, Scandinavia ~half that in DACH and UK.
- Cloudtilt's rapid growth and On's emergence as Gen Z lifestyle brand in Europe broaden demand beyond runners. Americas solid contributor despite larger base.

### 3. Awareness / share
- Global brand awareness ~12% in 2023 → >30%; target >50%; well below larger lifestyle sportswear peers.
- On just 1.3% share of global sportswear (footwear+apparel; apparel early). In footwear: rose to No. 7 sports-footwear [brand] in 2025, widening gap vs Deckers' Hoka (1.4% share).

### 4. Operating leverage → >22% adj EBITDA margin
- Adj EBITDA margin +>200bps to ≥22% by 2029 via SG&A leverage & automation; GM ≥65% (premium pricing, full-price selling); efficiencies reinvested in product innovation & quality, not further GM expansion.
- Distribution costs % sales on track for 10% in 2026, down from 13.4% in 2023; mgmt targets ≥100bps further improvement as Atlanta automation ramps and two legacy hubs consolidate into one automated facility. Marketing efficiency on larger base; G&A benefits from built capabilities & automation; selling expenses rise w/ stores, retail profitability above company avg.
- Adj EBITDA growth >20% annually thru 2029, faster than sales, w/o GM upside; scale & execution (not pricing/mix) key drivers.

### 5. Cash generation
- Cash more than doubled to ~CHF1.2bn from CHF500mn in 2023; net cash maintained. Organic priority: ~100 additional stores thru 2029, technology & infrastructure. Strong EBITDA-to-FCF conversion → first buyback up to $1bn thru 2029 while maintaining net cash.
- DKS-READ: none explicit (wholesale growth via deeper penetration/shelf space with existing partners).
