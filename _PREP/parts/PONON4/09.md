### 1. Why ONON matters to DKS (DKS-READ, all from notes)
1. On = key growth vendor inside Dick's and Foot Locker doors; relationship early. Barclays (12-May-26) OW pillar: "continued door growth into the key sporting goods retailers: Dick's Sporting Goods, Foot Locker (DKS), and JD Sports". BI (06/16/25, reprinted in 2026-02-20 file): "just 20% penetration among some of its key larger wholesale accounts -- Dick's, Foot Locker and JD"; planned wholesale door count +~5% to 11,300 in 2025. UBS (12-May-26, 29-Jul-26) relaying 1Q26 call: products "available at approximately 50% of the doors operated by its key wholesale partners, including Dick's Sporting Goods, Foot Locker, and JD Sports." BI (03/13/26): 40-50% of key accounts' locations, "mid-single-digit door expansion annually."
2. Investor Day (22-Sep-26) made sporting-goods channel explicit 2029 lever. JPM (22-Sep-26, Key take-aways note): "The brand aims to deepen relationships with key accounts such as Dick's Sporting Goods, Foot Locker, and JD, and expand into new doors and new verticals. By 2029, On expects to be in c.75% of doors at key partners across all regions vs 50% today." UBS/Barclays/Needham Investor Day wholesale points =same as SRC 05/06/07.
3. High-ASP, low-markdown vendor: JPM (22-Sep-26): "In the US, management noted that On has the highest ASP and the lowest discount levels at its wholesale partners." Needham =same as SRC 07 ($145 → $170 ASP over 5 yrs).
4. 2Q26 wholesale reset concentrated in Americas wholesale (the DKS channel): 2Q26 wholesale +12.7% cc (vs +25.1% 1Q26), Americas +13.0% cc; mgmt "deliberately reins in wholesale sell-in, particularly in the Americas, to protect full-price positioning" amid "heavy promotions across brands and softer sell-through in some everyday-running franchises in the Americas" (BI 08/11/26). UBS (12-Aug-26) est 2Q26 Americas wholesale +mid-to-high single digits ex-FX; Americas DTC low-to-mid 20s. JPM (12-Aug-26) models 3Q26 wholesale +7% and Americas +7% (the "low point").
5. Street links US promo backdrop to DKS/Foot Locker results: Needham 18-Sep quote =same as SRC 02 (DKS Foot Locker comp ~-4%, FL comp gdnc flat to -2% from +1-3%, DKS EBIT margin gdnc -~100bps incl -50bps core Dick's; JD NA LFL ~-7%; Nike ~$1.5bn incremental sell-in LTM, ~$3bn at retail).
6. Early US wholesale re-acceleration: Barclays 23-Sep "meaningful uptick" =same as SRC 06. UBS (12-Aug-26): Cloudsurfer 3 to run specialty in October w/ 3-month exclusivity before broader January launch; all everyday-running franchises refresh within ~14 months.
7. Run specialty small for On in US: UBS (29-Jul-26) "only ~5% of On's running footwear product in the US is sold in run specialty"; run specialty order books +>25% y/y (1Q26 call) → large sporting-goods/mall chains carry bulk of On US wholesale volume.
8. Bear-leaning voice ties doubt to DKS: Williams Trading 25-Sep =same as SRC 03 (DKS Hold $130 PT); Williams 16-Sep: Americas (>50% of rev) must be secured before new categories; Aug 6 checks: athletic-specialty sell-in up but sell-through not ("push" not "pull").
9. DTC 45.7% of sales in 2Q26, Q2 record (Ameriprise 17-Aug-26); target 50% by FY29 vs ~45% FY26; Bloomberg cons wholesale 54.5% of FY26E rev → 50.4% FY29E (Bloomberg FA 10/04/2026).
