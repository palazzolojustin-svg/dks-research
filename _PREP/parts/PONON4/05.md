### Thesis
- Investor day increases Buy conviction; focus on innovation, performance, athletes, sports, DTC, premium full-price → industry-leading growth, upside earnings surprises, stock outperformance; 29% 5-yr EPS CAGR. "We think the market sees On as mainly a running shoe brand and doesn't appreciate the brand's potential to address a much larger market."
- Summary highlights: premium playbook (innovation, athlete validation, curated distribution, brand discipline) now applied to tennis, football, golf; 600+ patents, CloudTec, Lightspray, extending into apparel; awareness >30% globally; ~8B earned media impressions from Kylian Mbappé partnership; disciplined channel expansion (larger stores, more wholesale penetration, personalization); high-teens growth led by running, lifestyle, apparel, all regions; margin expansion from SG&A leverage (automation, operating efficiencies) funding innovation & retail expansion.

### On Premium Playbook
- Enters only sports where it can bring meaningful innovation, cultural relevance, premium positioning; tennis = proof; football & golf attractive (large TAMs, tech differentiation, premiumization).
- Innovation = primary growth engine; clean-sheet product creation; CloudTec, Superfoams, Lightspray manufacturing → pricing power.
- Athlete validation: elite athlete feedback refines products pre-launch; example Helen Obiri (marathoner; shoes developed to address her stiff legs). Global awareness >30%.
- Premium over volume: plans to more than double store base by FY29 while managing wholesale distribution, inventory, pricing; On has demonstrated discipline as public co.

### Innovation & Design
- Repeatable loop: athlete insight → rapid prototyping & biomechanics testing → real-world feedback iteration; challenge industry assumptions; apply across footwear, apparel, future categories.
- >600 patents across CloudTec, CleanCloud, Lightspray; in-house foam engineering, new materials, production capabilities. Lightspray expected to be 10% of footwear volume over time.
- Unified design language, horizontal tech transfer across running, lifestyle, apparel, football, golf, training. Apparel material platforms: GuardTec, DryTec, ThermaTec, SenseTec, FormTec.
- AI: concept generation up from ~2-3 concepts per designer per season to ~30 (reduced admin workload); GenAI visualizes innovations in future ad campaigns.

### Community & Brand
- Curation over scale; earned adoption compounds better than paid; selective athlete sponsorships; disproportionate athlete success vs representation; culturally-focused campaigns (Zendaya collaboration).
- Three core growth engines: run (foundation; innovation-led share gains), lifestyle (cultural relevance via sneaker communities), apparel (wallet share, esp. women; dedicated performance, everyday essentials, premium activewear) → majority of future growth.
- Running assortment reorganized into 4 underfoot sensations: Support (Cloudrunner), Soft (Cloudsurfer), Energy (Cloudmonster), Fast (Cloudboom).
- Football: awareness beyond running; ~8B earned media impressions from Mbappé announcement; significant impact expected in South America, Africa; halo across sneaker, lifestyle, apparel, other performance.

### GTM & Channels
- Retail: larger stores = higher productivity & stronger premium expression; apparel incremental productivity lever; aims to more than double apparel productivity (still below footwear). Store base 83 today → 180 by FY29 (Fig 1). Stores payback 2 yrs; retail EBITDA margins above company overall. DTC target 50% of sales by FY29 (Fig 2: 50/50 DTC/wholesale).
- China: expects to reach LT China target 10% of sales with significantly fewer stores than originally planned (premium locations, relocations into stronger traffic corridors, full-price discipline); loyalty events; plans to relocate as many Chinese stores as it opens next year; luxury-level traffic helps secure premium locations.
- Wholesale: penetration across key wholesale partners ~20% → ~50-60% of doors FY23→FY26E; path to ~75% LT (Fig 3: targeted presence as % of total key account doors); ~75% cap reflects selectivity—only premium doors even within existing partners. Productivity via more shelf space, diversification into sneaker, football, golf, apparel, tailored retail concepts; expects strong sell-through, industry-leading pricing, low promo.- Omnichannel: previously product-focused vs loyalty/personalization; step change planned; omnichannel customers up to 4.5x higher LTV vs single-channel in some cases; personalization, loyalty programs, connecting retail/wholesale/digital.

### Financial Ambitions
- Broad-based growth; G&A leverage to drive larger portion of margin expansion than prior years.
- CHF125B premium sportswear TAM; ~75% of incremental rev next 3 yrs from run, lifestyle, apparel; football & golf modest incremental rev but halo.
- Regional targets: APAC mid-to-high-20s% growth; EMEA high-teens to low-20s%; Americas low-to-mid-teens%. APAC via disciplined retail, awareness, China execution; EMEA share opportunity outside strongest markets, awareness below US but gap shrinking; Americas via wholesale penetration, retail productivity, omnichannel.
- GM drivers: rising DTC penetration, sourcing/manufacturing efficiencies, inventory improvements, pricing discipline; offsets: product innovation investment, apparel (modest drag, subscale). 65%+ GM target assumes tariffs remain at current levels.
- EBITDA margin 22%+ primarily from SG&A leverage: full automation of Atlanta DC, another facility consolidation this year; marketing efficiency after years of upfront investment; automation/AI in G&A (finance, procurement); more than offset retail expansion & store selling expenses.
- Capital allocation: 1) organic reinvestment (retail rollout, innovation, infrastructure); 2) strong net-cash balance sheet; 3) excess capital returned via $1B buyback thru FY2029; all growth initiatives fully funded before returns.
- Plans to transition to USD reporting as early as 2027 (majority of rev & costs outside CHF).
