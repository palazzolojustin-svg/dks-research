### Thesis / valuation / risks
- Premium Swiss sports brand founded 2010; footwear >95% of sales; industry w/ high barriers to entry, health & casualisation tailwinds; balance of performance roots (credibility) and lifestyle (growth). Narrative shift from "early stage, high growth, premium brand" to "somewhat maturing running shoe brand that needs to find its next leg of growth". Mid/long-term runway: 1) apparel expansion, 2) international expansion notably Asia, 3) brand building → niche running brand to multi-category lifestyle brand.
- PT Dec-27 $41: 3-stage DCF (model forecasts + mid-term + fade to terminal); 4% terminal growth, 15.5% terminal EBIT margin, 10.3% WACC.
- Risks: competition (Nike, adidas, Hoka, Brooks); success of new launches (refresh everyday running franchises w/ new foams/tech; 2027+ growth hinges on reception); fashion risk (similar look/feel of shoes); discretionary spend; ambassadors (co-entrepreneurs Roger Federer, Zendaya; loss of Federer esp. would hurt).
