### 5 Key Takeaways
1. Next phase of premium growth via run, sneaker, apparel: >75% of absolute sales growth through FY29, each ~equal contribution. Run = foundation, largest R&D share, innovation transferred to sneaker (2nd pillar). Apparel (3rd pillar): target tripling business through FY29 (stronger female engagement, owned retail, toe-to-head assortments). Growth "multidimensional": training, tennis, golf, outdoor, young movers (kids). High-teens target not dependent on any single product/geography/channel.
2. Innovation cycle (SURREAL, LightSpray): every major running franchise gets next-gen update, substantially complete by mid-2027. Cloudsurfer 3 introduces SURREAL superfoam: 15% lighter, 20% softer, 15% more explosive than Helion (existing foam). Portfolio organized around 4 sensations: support, soft, bounce, fast. Early Cloudmonster 3 demand skewed meaningfully toward higher-priced Hyper and LightSpray versions (willingness to pay). LightSpray LT ~10% of footwear sales; early pilots in lifestyle.
3. Channels: DTC mix ~45% FY26 → 50% FY29 (mgmt: mix is "an outcome rather than a target"). Stores ~80 today → 180 by FY29; cash payback ~2 yrs; profitability comfortably above company adj EBITDA margin; larger stores more productive; apparel productivity to more than double through FY29. Wholesale remains central; to contribute more absolute incremental revenue than retail or e-commerce. Aggregate key-account door penetration ~50% today → ~75% by FY29. Existing-door growth from broader vertical representation, more shelf space, elevated presentation, deeper strategic-partner collaboration; shop-in-shop upgrades generated triple-digit sell-through growth in certain doors; omnichannel customers up to 4.5x LTV of single-channel. [DKS-READ: key-account door penetration expansion and shop-in-shop upgrades at wholesale partners.]
4. Regions through FY29: Americas low-to-mid teens; EMEA high-teens to low-20s; APAC mid-to-high 20s. EMEA may exceed investor expectations: share in Southern Europe, Eastern Europe, Benelux, Scandinavia ~half of penetration in Germany, Austria, Switzerland, UK. APAC fastest (retail expansion, relocations, premium China positioning); China to exceed 10% of sales before FY29; expects to complete original 2023 sales plan during FY26 with slightly more than half originally contemplated doors. Americas: more wholesale doors, higher productivity in existing accounts, retail expansion, broader run/sneaker/apparel assortments. Underpenetrated: Mexico, Middle East, Indonesia.
5. Margin via SG&A leverage, stable GM: LT gdnc assumes no material incremental GM expansion from record levels to reach adj EBITDA margin 19.5-20% FY26 → >22% FY29. DTC mix, sourcing scale, efficiencies support GM but reinvested in innovation, quality, apparel scaling. Distribution: ≥100bps leverage via Atlanta automation & supply-chain consolidation. Marketing efficiencies from elevated FY26 base; G&A leverage via automation, AI, indirect procurement. Selling expense rises with ~100 store additions; retail profitability comfortably above group margin. 65%+ GM = "a margin at work, not at rest."
