### Bull/base/bear
- Bull: 2H26 rev beat on rising brand awareness & DTC; FY26 rev low-to-mid 20s cFX; rev +15-20% next yr, mid-teens FY28 → stock >$45.
- Base: double-digit growth multiple yrs; rev +20-25% cFX FY26, +10-15% FY27 & FY28; ~100bps EBIT margin expansion combined FY27-28; PT $37 = 18x FY28E EPS CHF1.70 (~$2.08).
- Bear: brand "hitting a wall"; growth sub-20% FY26, single digits FY27; margins protected by slowing investment but no expansion beyond 2026 → ~$20.
- Company: global athletic footwear brand, HQ Switzerland, majority of rev from North America.

### Near-term challenges
- Q2 print: first-ever cFX rev gdnc cut in 5-yr public history, to low-20s growth (prior "at least 23%"); only other "cut" 2Q24 (reported rev lowered on FX, cFX reiterated). Shares down >30% since print vs SPX -2%; short interest >10% of float (vs <5% 5 months ago; more than doubled since April, Fig 1).
- Cut attributed to slower US wholesale; increasingly promotional athletic footwear; sell-through of some "everyday" styles slowed as consumers took advantage of reduced prices at other brands; aligns w/ cautious mall-based wholesale accounts.
- DKS-READ: DKS reported Foot Locker segment comp decline of almost 4% (vs slight increase Q1); lowered FY Foot Locker comp gdnc to flat-to-down-2% (orig +1% to +3%); lowered FY EBIT margin gdnc ~100bps (incl 50bps reduction at core Dick's banner, larger cut at Foot Locker), attributed to highly promotional environment, expected to cause GM decline at both segments.
- JD Sports Q2 North America LFL sales down almost 7% (vs Q1 <1% decline).
- Promo pressure largely from Nike channel inventory: late-CY25 NKE shipped heavily into NA wholesale; 4 consecutive qtrs double-digit NA sell-in growth (+14% y/y LTM); Nov-2025 qtr largest domestic wholesale sell-in in NKE history; LTM ~$1.5bn more wholesale product y/y ≈ ~$3bn incremental product at retail (grossed up). NKE lacks brand heat to support; retailers aggressively discounting Nike. adidas, New Balance also slowed → discounts for BTS; "crowded out" demand for smaller brands like On. Fig 3: Foot Locker (footlocker.com, Aug 2026) very promotional.

### Demand indicators intact
- Wholesale growth cut nearly in half (>25% Q1 → <13% Q2); DTC accelerated 29% Q1 → 34% Q2; mgmt cited accelerating NA DTC demand; gdnc cut entirely wholesale; DTC expected strong through YE. Needham: DTC more reflective of brand heat; restricting NA wholesale sell-in LT positive (hard to wean consumers off discounts, cf Nike, Under Armour); sacrificing near-term volume for pricing integrity.
- Instagram: 3.7mn followers, +28% vs Sep-2025 (2.9mn); only other covered brand w/ double-digit increase = DECK's UGG +14%. Supports DTC strength and possible wholesale re-inflection when market cleaner (perhaps after Holidays).
- Q2 GM up almost 400bps y/y to 65.4%, 2nd-highest qtrly GM ever (3Q25 65.7% incl 200bps one-off benefits). DTC mix almost 46% of sales vs 41% 2Q25 → <100bps of expansion; ~300bps underlying. Incremental US tariffs not quantified; on sell-side callback mgmt said impact almost as much as Q1's ~200bps. Normalizing tariffs (~200bps headwind) and mix (~100bps tailwind) → ~500bps y/y GM expansion.

### Upside drivers / risks

- Risks: highly competitive, fast-changing trends; failure to innovate; promo environment forcing discounting → margin degradation (margins among best in industry from full-price selling).
