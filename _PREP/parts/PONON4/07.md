- On track to exceed 2023 Investor Day targets (≥26% cFX rev CAGR, >60% GM, >18% adj EBITDA margin). FY29: ≥CHF5.6bn rev; GM ≥65%; adj EBITDA margin ≥22% (= adj EBITDA CAGR 20% through FY29). China 10% of total sales.

- Football & golf: Mbappé signing garnered 8bn earned media impressions; medium-to-longer-term, modest near-term financial impact; football limited product drops 2027, broader in-line product 2028; golf similar. Brand awareness currently 30%; target 50% by FY29.

Store payback ~2 yrs; retail-level profitability above company overall. DTC ~50% of rev by FY29 (vs ~42% in FY25).

On carries highest ASP and lowest discount rate of any brand in the industry across many markets incl U.S.
- Margins: FY26 GM gdnc ≥65%; FY29 GM similar (DTC mix & efficiencies offset by product investment & category mix; COGS savings reinvested in innovation). 22%+ adj EBITDA via SG&A leverage (lower distribution & G&A), partly offset by marketing and selling (store buildout).
