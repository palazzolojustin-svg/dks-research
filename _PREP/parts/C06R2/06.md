### Thesis / summary
- Complex 4Q setup w/ many boxes to check; "the company largely delivered." 4Q met expectations at DKS & FL: DKS comp 3.1% met 3% market bar; FL significant progress "cleaning out the garage." Comprehensive combined gdnc w/ segment detail should clear confusion on 2026 trajectory.
- Increased conviction in re-accelerating FL earnings growth: initial Fast Break 21-store pilot exceeded expectations in a few months. Created noise in FY26 guide: DKS retaining a higher number of unprofitable stores this year → pressures 2026 earnings and delays EPS accretion from acquisition until FY2027.
- Catalysts: World Cup, tax refunds; potential ahead-of-schedule FL upside as DKS executes playbook. Near-term FY26 pressure but longer-term earnings growth profile increased (UBS view) → risk/reward favorable, esp after pullback.

### FL early results
- DKS keeping more FL stores as it sees larger LT growth opportunity.
- Fast Break pilot store 4Q comps surpassed DKS standalone (3%+); co views as validation, sees pathway to greater FL improvement than initially anticipated.
- May cause FY26 EPS dilution vs DKS standalone 2025 EPS ($14.58); retaining more unprofitable stores pressures FY26 earnings.
- Some investors read change as sign FL integration harder than anticipated; UBS disagrees — pilot showed meaningful improvement in short period solely by streamlining merchandise presentation.
- Additional FL levers: (1) more premium merchandise from existing brand partners; (2) new brand partners like Hoka & On; (3) broader FL apparel selection.

### 4Q25 results
- Consolidated comp 0.8%: DKS 3.1%, FL -3.4% vs cons 2.1% DKS, -7.3% FL.
- DKS comp: ticket +4.4% (premium merchandise sales); traffic -1.3%, deteriorated from +1.3% in 3Q; 2-yr stacks flat q/q on comp (9.7%) and traffic (0.7%).
- GM: noise from FL clearance; FL GM ~-470 bps y/y vs gdnc ~-1,000-1,500 bps. DKS GM +~70 bps y/y to 35.6% (premium merchandise), in line w/ cons 35.6%.
- Adj SG&A: DKS 24.7% of rev vs cons 24.4% ("met"); FL 25.3% vs cons 23.0% (miss).
- EPS: consolidated $3.45 vs cons $2.79 (lower FL GM contraction + solid DKS execution).

### FY26 guidance (UBS read)
- "Although DKS typically guides conservatively, its 2026 guide signals confidence."
- DKS comp 2%-4% vs market 1%-3%; slightly 1H front-weighted; 2Q likely > 1Q on World Cup. FL comp 1%-3% vs expectations for flat at midpoint (Fast Break optimism).
- DKS margins: GM outlook more muted after several years of expansion; benefits from premium merchandise, private label penetration, high-margin revenue streams offset by new DC coming online in April and incremental D&A from HoS ramp → flat EBIT margin 11.1% FY26.
- FL margins: unprofitable in 2025; return to profitability 2026 via GM gains from lapping 4Q clearance & improved merch mix; OI ~ $125 million at midpoint = 1.6% EBIT margin.
- Adj EPS gdnc $13.50-$14.50 below ~$15.00 expectations; midpoint ~4% dilution vs DKS standalone FY25 adj EPS $14.58.
