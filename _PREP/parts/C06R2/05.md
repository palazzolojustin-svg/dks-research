### FY26 guidance
- Main debate: lower-than-anticipated FY26 adj EPS gdnc; midpoint $14.00 implies ~4% decline vs DKS stand-alone FY25 adj EPS $14.58; market expected ~$15.00 baseline. Consolidated FY26 OI gdnc in line w/ cons → "moving pieces"; call focus on particulars.
- Comp gdnc DKS 2.0%-4.0%, FL 1.0%-3.0% vs pre-print expectations DKS 1%-3%, FL -1% to 1% (combined 0-2%) → both ahead; suggests confidence; may ease concerns about deteriorating growth in DKS ex-HoS.
- Consolidated EBIT margin midpoint 7.8%: DKS 11.1%, FL 1.6% → similar DKS trending, modest GM improvement redeployed to SG&A growth investments. FL segment profit $125mm midpoint (return to positive OI).
- Net capex $1.5bn: 14 new HoS, 22 Dick's Field House locations.
- FY26 EPS gdnc includes -$1.43 adjustment for income from settlements to be received and $1.24 adjustments related to FL acquisition.
- UBS view: clean slate entering FY26; gdnc clear & comprehensive vs 3Q; comp outlook improved; adj EPS gdnc conservative; "positioned well for an upward revision cycle throughout FY 2026."
