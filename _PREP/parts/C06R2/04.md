### PM's take
- Better-than-feared: core comp +3.1% (tick above +2.5% previewed bar), core GM in line (vs miss concerns), FL non-GAAP op loss ~$6MM in line w/ slight-decline gdnc.
- Mgmt "took the clear tact of stoic confidence" on call & follow-up re core momentum, gdnc conservatism, FL opportunity (closing fewer stores; "Fast Break" test format results better than expected, rolling to ~250 locations in 2026).
- Like many retailers, prudent gdnc incl recent geopolitical uncertainty in range; pushed down 1H. Should geopolitical concerns fade, story could shine: clean-inventory FL (margin turnaround narrative could expand), momentum even before World Cup, FL topline turnaround 2H26.

### Key model takes (CFO & group follow-ups)
1. Weather net immaterial in 4Q; all months comped positive in core.
2. Recent geopolitical events added extra prudence to gdnc range. On traffic: DKS cited stable 2-yr trend, strength across all categories & consumer cohorts, typical prudence. FL comps to inflect 2H via ~250 Fast Break remodels (US + some Europe) and improved vendor allocations (brands aligned w/ DKS to improve FL core assortment). HoS year-2 comps "modestly positive."
3. Core merch margin "not down" in 4Q; mix, GameChanger, Media all modest contributors (GC seasonally highest spring & fall; Media ongoing benefit, not new y/y). Core FY26 GM flat to up slightly: HoS cost headwinds + new DC offset by GameChanger, Media, buying synergies (synergies start 2H26; FL GM up for year).
4. Pre-opening $90MM in 2026 (opening costs for 2026 & 2027 HoS); roughly even by half, concentrated 2Q/3Q.
5. Core op margin & OI down in 1H26; 2Q influenced by World Cup higher sales but also opportunistic advertising investments.
6. Capex likely at high end of $1.0-1.5B in 2027.

### Guidance detail
- Consolidated FY26: sales $22.1-22.4B, 2-4% SSS, OI $1.68-1.81B, EPS $13.50-14.50, no repo (91MM DSO), assumes tariff rates pre-Supreme Court decision.
- Core: 11.0-11.2% op mgn (Street 11.2% at high end); op mgn down 1H (OI $ down y/y), expanding 2H on timing of investments/synergy savings. FL: 1-3% comps, $100-150MM op profit, 2H-weighted as comps/GM improve into BTS. (Fig 5 core op mgn expansion drivers — image, no data.)

### Core DKS 4Q detail
- Comp +3.1% vs JPM +3.5%, Street +2.1%; ticket +4.4%, traffic -1.3% (+0.7% 2-yr stack). Consumer environment "relatively unchanged"; growth in all key categories (apparel, footwear, hardlines); gains across income cohorts; all months positive.
- FY26 core comp +2-4%, slightly 1H-weighted (World Cup) though profit 2H-weighted. JPM now: 1Q +3.4%, 1H +4.2%, 2H +2.5%, FY26 +3.3%.
- GM 35.6%, ~+70 bps y/y (Fig 1: +67 bps), in line w/ Street. Like TSCO/BBY/sporting goods vendors, DKS cited heightened holiday promotionality, offset by GameChanger & Dick's Media Network (GC seasonality less tailwind in 4Q).
- SG&A: core leveraged ~40 bps y/y, $ +2.5% (digital, in-store, marketing investments). Consolidated SG&A deleveraged 9 bps on FL.

### Foot Locker
- 4Q comp -3.4%, better than down M-HSD guide; vs cons -6.7%, JPMe -6.5%; co cited better topline from Fast Break; JPM expects inventory rationalization/markdowns contributed + renewed basketball strength cited by NKE & JD Sports.
- FL GM 25.2% (text; Fig 1 shows 25.0% actual), hit by 1,000-bp headwind to right-size inventory (in line; ~350-bp impact to consolidated GM).
- FY26 FL comp +1-3% from Fast Break stores and overall FL banner; positive inflection (topline & profit) around BTS (DKS expects to touch full FL assortment by BTS). JPM: 1Q -3.0%, FY26 +1.5%. FL GM to expand FY26 on 2H inventory buys.
- Fast Break: last qtr 11-store reset test, removed ~30% of unproductive styles, renovated formats to resemble DKS (clearer storytelling/presentation) → positive store comps "meaningfully higher than the DKS business" and "strong" GM improvement; test broad-based (urban, suburban, high & low volume, some Europe); added 10 more Fast Break stores in LA area ahead of NBA All-Star Game (mid-Feb). Plans 250 Fast Break locations (US & Europe, weighted domestic) by BTS; reduced initial FL closure list.
- JPM Times Square test-store visit: no clearance; elevated footwear & apparel assortments (esp apparel) resembling a Dick's; SKUs organized by gender and by sport (running, basketball). (Figs 3-4 photos.)
- JPM remains positive on margin opportunity; sees accelerating incremental margins.

### Etc.
- Reiterated $100-125MM medium-term synergies (procurement & direct sourcing); some realized 2026, back-half weighted; flow through COGS & SG&A.
- HoS: opened 16 in FY25; guiding 14 openings (text says "FY25", context FY26) and 18 in FY27 → 67 total at end-2027, slightly below initial 75-100 HoS by 2027 guide. 2026: 22 Field House locations, 15 Golf Galaxy performance centers.
- 6th DC on track to open this year: 800,000 sq ft in Fort Worth, TX, building on 50-store Texas presence, improving regional availability ("a negative for ASO").
