### FY26 guidance framing
- Market debating go-forward DKS/FL enterprise earnings for 2026, stand-alone Dick's momentum, FL trajectory/inventory health; clarity = positive for case.
- Data points "confusing" so far; last qtr co focused outlook on core business w/ parameters for FL segment but insisted FL acquisition accretive in 2026 → expect enterprise guide.
- Simple math: 3Q guided FY25 $14.25-$14.55 (DKS business only); midpoint $14.40 + 2-3% accretion in 2026 → ~$14.75 midpoint w/o core growth = UBS "floor"; vs cons $14.94. Accretion supported partly by $100mm-$125mm synergies primarily procurement & direct sourcing.
- Expected FY26 gdnc: stand-alone DKS comp 1-3% (conservative, like last year, despite strong 2025 trend/catalysts); FL comp -1% to +1%, negative 1H turning positive 2H (DKS said no FL pickup in 1H26); total co comp 0%-2%. Margins: modest GM expansion, flat EBIT margin (GM savings reinvested in growth). Expect update on cost synergy gdnc & sources. FL return to positive OI $100mm-$200m (after negative OI FY25). EPS: maintain FL accretion to FY26 EPS (ex one-time costs) vs FY25; UBS FY26E $14.97 (cons $14.94), ~4% y/y; guided range $14.75-$15.25 "within market expectations."

### 4Q key points / UBS 4Q model
- Core DKS comp focus: tough 4Q24 compare 6.4% comp; expectations started low but rose over last couple months; buyside bar ~3.0% DKS stand-alone (vs cons 2.1%).
- FL turnaround validation: need signal of progress clearing legacy FL inventory in 4Q; market would respond well to concrete path back to FL OI $100mm-$200mm → validates modest EPS accretion from FL in 2026 (ex one-time costs).
- Revenue: combined entity 4Q comp decel 1.7% (3Q) → 1.3% (cons 1.4%). DKS comp 5.7% 3Q → 2.0% 4Q (cons 2.1%); HoS openings slow to 3 in 4Q after 10 in 3Q. FL 4Q comp -7.0% vs -4.7% 3Q (cons -7.3%), DKS "clean the garage" in 4Q, significant markdowns, intl struggles persist.
- GM: stand-alone DKS ~+70 bps y/y (premium merch margin mix, scaling high-margin rev streams); FL liquidations offset; co guided FL GM contraction 1,000-1,500 bps; total GM -300 bps y/y (cons -262 bps).
- SG&A: DKS investing in tech, talent, marketing; declining FL sales; total ~10 bps deleverage (cons -22 bps). EBIT margin 8.3% LY → 5.1% (cons 5.9%).
- 4Q EPS $2.42 vs cons $2.79.

### House of Sports (HoS) comp analysis
- Core Dick's avg ~5% comp over last 8 qtrs.
- 35 HoS opened as of 3Q; UBS est ~$1.3bn annualized sales (assume $35mm/store); 38 HoS expected at YE2025; ~650 "legacy" DKS stores.
- Method: new HoS (~$35mm rev) vs avg store replaced (~$15mm).
- DKS comp ex-HoS averaged ~4% over last 4 qtrs → HoS only ~25% of comp growth; expect relationship to invert; at least 100-200 bps comp contribution from rollout ongoing; that assumes DKS comp decels to ~3.0% next year; if comp holds ~5%, HoS ~50% of growth.
- Views HoS concentration as validating bull case (virtuous cycle: experience → traffic → vendors allocate top product → destination; core to moat). ~15-20 new HoS/yr → ~2.0-2.5% comp floor (assuming rest of business at least flat); not all locations may fit.

### Footwear
- Through 1H25 ~70% of DKS growth from footwear (On, Hoka, Adidas). 1H25 DKS footwear +$232mm = 70% of sales growth; FL domestic rev -$89mm = ~39% of DKS footwear growth.
- Not all FL loss to DKS: ~50% of FL locations have DKS within 10-min drive; DKS <10% share of sporting goods. Conservative DKS capture of FL share donation 25%-50% → only ~10-20% of DKS footwear growth from FL share; FL share gains ~0.4%-0.7% of 1H25 DKS rev growth.
- Footwear concentration seen as advantage (recurring, repeat traffic, key for top vendors). Combined DKS+FL ~33% US footwear market share (UBS est); "top player in athletic footwear retail".
- Other levers: GameChanger & Dick's Media Network (~80% GM) → ~30 bps GM benefit 2024, 50 bps 2025, 60 bps 2026 (UBS est); data asset w/ Scorecard loyalty; new categories: premium non-athletic apparel (e.g., Faherty), in-store collectibles w/ Fanatics.
