## SRC 02_DKS_TRANSCRIPTS/conferences/DKS_MorganStanley-Conference_2024-12-03.md | 2024-12-03 | FactSet corrected transcript, Morgan Stanley Global Consumer & Retail Conference fireside; DKS: Hobart (CEO), Gupta (CFO); host Simeon Gutman (MS hardline/broadline/food retail) | post-Q3 FY2024 (same day as Barclays SRC 02)
- Host framing: DKS at conference ~9th yr in a row; "one of the few remnants of retaining almost all of their COVID gains"; durability = "raging debate on the stock".

### Consumer / 2025
- Hobart correction: vs 2019 sales "over 50%" higher, profits "I think, 4x". Consumer holding up multiple quarters; prioritizing healthy/active lifestyle, team sports, outdoors; increasingly choosing DKS.
- Strategic pillars recap: tech/e-com brought in-house to scale digital profitably; athlete experience; portfolio reinvention (HoS = best expression → Field House 50,000 sq ft, start of chain repositioning); differentiated product (premium full-service footwear decks unlocked hot lifestyle product requiring fit environment).
- Q3 comp +4.2%; three quarters of 4%+ comps.
- 2025: Gupta "too early"; "predicting even Q4 is not that easy"; watching macro/geopolitics. New athletes: 1.5M acquired in Q3; ~7M ("almost 7 million") YTD.
- Hobart: sport/culture outsized mutual influence; women's basketball, post-Paris Olympics, 2026 US World Cup, 2028 LA Olympics → trend continues.

### Share / categories
- "consistently gaining share" in core four: footwear, apparel, team sports, golf. Most share gain in footwear and apparel (Gupta); "We already have a very dominant market share in team sports" (Hobart). Golf healthy: rounds played rising; Golf Galaxy portfolio work. More opportunity in team sports + licensed.
- Pandemic pull-forward: golf was "pandemic winner" but rounds/participation still growing; golf sales still above 2019. Big-ticket "pandemic winner" items (e.g., fitness) small part of business; some future replacement cycle likely but not focus; "all of it is bigger than it was in 2019".

### Brands / footwear
- On & HOKA "amazing job" reinventing performance run; growth also from adidas Terrace collection (Samba, Campus, Gazelle), New Balance; excited about Nike's coming "running construct" and air technology; "not just a two brand story"; innovation may rotate; long-term potential.
- Nike = 24% of business, #1 strategic partner; 80% of shoppers multi-brand; much growth from Nike due to new access; DKS was Nike's #1 strategic partner even during Nike DTC focus. "90% of our stores have premium full service decks"; decks getting bigger; "We're not looking at it as cannibalistic." —Hobart. Analyst: Nike exposure grew y/y.
- Gupta: no longer anchoring footwear decks/apparel pads to one brand; athlete tells DKS what's relevant; vertical apparel gives flexibility.

### House of Sport
- Will finish FY24 at 19 HoS; additional 15 in FY25 → "call it about 35 doors by the end of 2025" on path to 75-100.
- HoS: 100,000+ sq ft; rock climbing, track & field; Co Lab space (brand partner story changed every few weeks); seasonal fashion show. Athletes drive longer (more travel time), spend more time in store. Emerging brands on-ramp: On came in via Public Lands into HoS; FP Movement via HoS. Mall traffic "goes up significantly" with HoS → access to more premium space; destination. Field House = "close cousin".
- Markets: first Victor/Rochester NY, second Knoxville; smaller markets "doing even better than you might expect"; small markets have demand served better than 50K/80K box. Still prioritize core markets: Boston Prudential Center, Ross Park (Pittsburgh) – existing boxes with significant outperformance.
- Success metrics: brand resonance (national + emerging; # brand partners visiting, inbound interest); landlord excitement → real estate flexibility (100 lease renewals/yr) incl Field House; community engagement (local sports teams, local college teams partnerships).
- Returns: year-one returns posted on IR website; at start of FY24 only 2 stores had fully comped in year 2, now more → more confidence beyond year 1; happy with ROI, comp contribution and bottom-line contribution. Analyst cited payback ~3 yrs HoS, ~2 yrs Field House (not confirmed by mgmt).

### Promotions / holiday
- LY Q4 more promotional than prior yr; this yr some retailers have "a little bit too much inventory" → planned increased promotionality; "We counted for that in our guidance, and still have a merch margin gain expected in Q4." —Hobart; surgical (when/where/how deep).
- Gupta: short holiday season, 5 fewer days vs LY, adding urgency/promos across retail; well prepared (service, team engagement).

### Inventory
- Q3 inventory +13%; targeted, not "peanut butter spread": earlier spring receipts for warm-weather southern stores (historically held too much winter product post-holiday); avoid being broken on key items pre-Christmas (e.g., Nike fleece key styles/colors/sizes); licensed opportunity with College Football Playoff expanding to 12 teams; footwear access. "strategic weapon and asset"; good product into 2025; liquidation via value chain.
- Gupta: Q2 inventory grew a bit faster than sales as conscious investment; 3rd straight quarter >4% comp; strong back-to-school. Despite inventory investment raised guidance 3rd quarter in a row: raised comp expectations (Q3 + Q4 confidence) and full-year merch margin/GP expectations. Value chain didn't exist in 2019; now 50 (Going, Going, Gone!) stores to liquidate mistakes, better clearance monetization, bring value-conscious athletes. FY25 expectations at next guidance; "really, really confident about our Q4 business and into 2025."

### Vertical brands
- 600-800 bps higher margin rate; collectively #2 vendor behind Nike; DKS is #1 vendor in golf, fitness, team sports (hardlines) via own brands (golf: Maxfli, Walter Hagen). DSG = opening price point all-family solution w/ quality/function/fashion; CALIA, VRST fill whitespace "to/from" lifestyle-sport vs hardcore on-field. Goal $2B; "well on our way".

### GameChanger
- Vision "best sports company in the world" → own best sports tech platform = GC; synonymous with youth sports; live video, AI highlight reels; data (e.g., 12-year-old's team/position) → retail media network for DKS + partners; "just scratching the surface".
- Gupta: GC industry TAM ~$30B-$40B; GC revenue $100M by end of FY24; grown ~30%-40%/yr since acquisition; expect that growth opportunity continues; very profitable. Dominant sport baseball/softball; built basketball over ~1yr+ with AI scoring detection, coach game review; similar volleyball tool. No advertising on GC today. "still evaluating our options" on broader $30-40B opportunity.
- Q: in 2025 collect ad revenue on GC and use GC data for personalization in DKS app? A: "Yes and yes" (Gupta); second carefully to protect engagement/NPS. Hobart: "start of a long-term journey… early innings".

### Margin
- Analyst: op margin in low teens; GM came in from peak due to promotions. Gupta: nothing holding back; investing in GC, "best data set in our sporting goods industry" for RMN, personalization, redefining loyalty infrastructure, plus HoS. Playbook FY24 → FY25 and beyond: drive top line, GM expansion, invest in SG&A, while improving bottom line.

### Balance sheet
- Conservative; pre-2019 aimed to end year with no debt; investment grade now, important given real estate investments. On levering to buy back: "plenty of flexibility" between cash on hand and other; "if there is a dislocation in the marketplace, we will definitely lean in and support the valuation of the company." —Gupta

### What changed vs pre-COVID
- Transformation began 2016-2017: e-com in-house, premium full-service footwear decks. Pre-COVID quarters +5%-6% comps; weeks before COVID +6%-7%; spun up curbside in COVID. Overhang "can you possibly comp the comp" now answered. 50,000 teammates; culture of recognition (district and regional president award winners; Texas visit next day).
