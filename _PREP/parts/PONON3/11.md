- BI: On can sustain roughly 20% annual sales gains over next 3 yrs vs consensus midteens; expanding businesses offset core maturation; apparel, owned retail, international underpenetrated; soccer (Mbappé) another sizable opportunity; strong margins/cash → capacity to invest; growth moderation may understate LT runway

### 1. ~20% growth ahead of Street
- ~20% annual over next LT planning period = low end of prior 20–25% ambition; ahead of cons midteens through 2030
- Slowdown reflects larger base, not lack of runway; 2023–2026 (BI calc) sales CAGR ~25% vs 26% target set at last investor day, despite recent US wholesale softness
- Upper end of 20–25% less likely; 20% target would imply meaningful upside to LT sales estimates, consistent with 2023 framework

### 2. Soccer
- ~$95B global soccer-sportswear market (IMARC); 0.5% capture → annual sales approaching $500M; footwear, apparel, lifestyle rolling out 2027
- Mbappé signed after nearly 20 yrs with Nike; role developing boots & apparel; Thierry Henry director of football; Sydney Schertenleib on women's side → dedicated vertical; LightSpray differentiation; Mbappé cultural reach into lifestyle/apparel
- Financial capacity: ended 2Q with >CHF1.2 billion net cash; 65.4% GM; 19.8% adj EBITDA margin; key question = return on spend vs entrenched Nike & Adidas

### 3. Apparel could quadruple
- Apparel ~36% annual growth through 2030 to nearly CHF800 million (BI); training, tennis, lifestyle, soccer, women's, technical innovation, fashion partnerships
- Global sports-apparel market could reach $304B by 2030 (Euromonitor); On share ~0.1%; US ~45% of global sports-apparel sales, On US share ~0.1%, could rise toward 0.3% by 2030 via premium stores & head-to-toe assortments

### 4. Retail & international
- On track to more than double store footprint since 2023 to ~86 locations vs Nike ~990 and Adidas 886 full-price sites; stores could reach 10% of sales over time
- Stores outside China on pace to quadruple to 40 in 2026 from 10 in 2023; China to 46 from 22; APAC ~20% of sales; diversifying beyond US running; "core US business matures"
