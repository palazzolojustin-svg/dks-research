### Thesis
- Premium Swiss brand founded 2010; footwear >95% of sales; industry w/ substantial barriers, health & casualisation tailwinds; balance performance (credibility) vs lifestyle (growth)
- Narrative shifting from "early stage, high growth, premium brand" to "somewhat maturing running shoe brand who needs to find its next leg of growth"; runway: apparel, international (Asia), brand building → multi-category lifestyle brand

### Risks
- Competition: Nike, adidas, Hoka, Brooks; success of new launches (everyday running refresh; 2027+ growth hinges on reception); fashion risk (similar look/feel across shoes); discretionary spending; ambassadors (Roger Federer, Zendaya co-entrepreneurs; loss esp Federer adverse)
