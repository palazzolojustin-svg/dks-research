### Overview
- Investor day: "premium growth" playbook – innovation pipeline, product strategy (running, lifestyle, apparel), first look at new categories (football, golf)
- 3Y net sales CAGR target high teens: ambitious; key debate feasibility/upside; JPM sees thoughtful target w/ clear roadmap by product & region
- Americas expected low-to-mid-teens CAGR 2026–29 (vs JPMe +8%) → re-acceleration from low point Q3-26 (JPMe +7%)
- Mgmt did not specify timing of US wholesale re-acceleration / H1 2027 expectations; flagged strong back-to-school sales, growth in lifestyle, good visibility of wholesale sell-through (weekly data down to franchise & color level) → wholesale growth under control
- EMEA target high teens to low 20s (JPMe +13% 3Y CAGR): share catch-up in Southern & Eastern Europe, Benelux, Scandinavia; APAC target mid-to-high 20s (more moderate vs JPMe +37%)
- 75%+ of additional sales from core (running, lifestyle, apparel); new sports contribution moderate; JPM: halo could be more meaningful – 8bn earned media reach from launch alone
- Q3 guidance slightly below expectations → growth Q4-weighted; credibility debate to persist near term; JPM "broadly more constructive" on growth into 2027+; remain OW

### Growth by product
- Confident scaling all verticals over 3 yrs: running (performance focus), lifestyle via sneakers, apparel driven by HER, new sports football & golf
- >75% of expected absolute net sales additions (c.CHF2bn) from core categories, broadly equal from running, lifestyle, apparel; apparel sales expected to triple by 2029
- Mgmt on new sports: "the TAM for dedicated products is substantial, but we will build it slowly and steadily with care for the decades to come. 2027 will start with limited drops and in 2028 we will start with inline portfolios for cleats and some performance all-day products."

### Growth by region (3 yrs)
- APAC fastest mid-to-high 20s; EMEA high teens to low 20s; Americas low-to-mid teens
- Americas: fastest growth in retail (many uncovered cities); "tangible opportunities" for same-store growth as shelf space expands across existing & new verticals; "meaningful potential" to grow door count with key wholesale partners + specialty accounts for new verticals
- EMEA: similar drivers + outperformance; Southern Europe, Eastern Europe, Benelux, Scandinavia (share c.50% of UK) picking up, closing gap vs UK
- APAC: elevate premium positioning via "consciously" expanding stores & upgrading locations

### Growth by channel
- DTC to grow faster than wholesale → c.50% of mix vs 45% today
- ~100 more stores by FY29, linear, in line w/ history → 180 retail stores vs 83 today; larger stores more efficient w/ higher sales productivity; footwear productivity already very high (above luxury & premium benchmarks); apparel productivity to double by 2029 but remain below footwear
- Wholesale growth to lag DTC but add more net sales than DTC in absolute terms over next 3 yrs
- Aims to deepen relationships with key accounts such as Dick's Sporting Goods, Foot Locker, JD; expand into new doors & new verticals; by 2029 in c.75% of doors at key partners across all regions vs 50% today
- Wholesale comp growth opportunity: product diversification, deeper partner collaboration, brand elevation
- US: On has highest ASP and lowest discount levels at its wholesale partners
- Mgmt: "channels can compound, not compete"; "channel split is an outcome. It's not a target."
- DKS-READ: ONON explicitly targets deeper relationship with Dick's Sporting Goods (plus Foot Locker, JD); door penetration at key partners 50% → c.75% by 2029; wholesale to add more absolute $ than DTC through 2029; new verticals (football, golf) to expand into specialty accounts; ONON claims highest ASP/lowest discounting at US wholesale partners; weekly sell-through visibility at franchise/color level; Americas wholesale trough Q3-26 per JPM

### Margins
- GM: tailwinds channel mix, sourcing/manufacturing efficiencies, better inventory mgmt; headwinds apparel mix, reinvestment in product quality → only very modest GM improvement over 3 yrs
- Meaningful adj EBITDA margin improvement by 2029: opex efficiencies, leverage incl marketing efficiencies, at least 100bps from warehouse automation ramp-up, G&A efficiencies from prior 3 yrs' capability investments; selling expense up w/ store roll-out but retail profitability (incl expansion costs) above group → DTC growth "is not hindering" margins

### Reporting change
- Moving from CHF to USD reporting as early as 2027 (majority of sales, product & operating costs outside CHF; significant translation impact; align w/ share price currency); exact timing & restated history not yet disclosed
