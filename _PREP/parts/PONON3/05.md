### Thesis
- Focus on innovation, performance, athletes, sports, DTC, premium full-price → industry-leading sales growth, upside earnings surprises
- 2Q26 pullback = buying opportunity; issues mostly short-term industry dynamics; new product launches likely catalyze 4Q26 sales growth inflection; bullish Sept analyst day → boost sentiment and ~17x FY2 P/E
- (1) Executing full-price strategy: softer sell-through in certain mature running franchises esp Americas wholesale amid elevated promotions (UAA cited similar dynamic on its recent call); ONON intentionally limiting sell-in vs discounting → near-term wholesale pressure; helps maintain industry-leading GM and supports FY27 innovation demand
- (2) Underlying demand robust: 2Q26 DTC +34% ex-FX despite lapping +54% in 2Q25; mgmt attributes partly to younger consumer acquisition; Cloudtilt franchise +190% y/y (lifestyle traction); retail partner feedback on upcoming launches incl Cloudsurfer 3 (new Super Foam) highly positive
- Thesis map: global athletic wear industry +M-HSD% annual multi-year (pre-pandemic decade avg 7%); sportswear only 25% of total apparel & footwear; ONON ≥2% LT market share (= Puma FY21 sales); LT EBIT margin mid-to-high teens (premium, DTC/e-com led, apparel accretive; Lululemon-like); 5-yr CAGRs sales 18%, adj EBITDA 20%, EPS 29%
- UBS Evidence Lab Global Athletic Wear survey: global sportswear demand strong; global On awareness low (room to grow); those aware increasingly favorable
- Reverse DCF: market pricing mid-teens sales growth (vs UBS ~18% 5-yr) and mid-teens EBIT margin (vs UBS high-teens FY30E)

### Three important points
1. Continued marketing investment: ~13.5% of sales in marketing this year (one of highest ratios in athletic wear); global brand awareness just 30%, expected to reach 60% over time; "The wrong answer is to pull short-term levers to keep up appearances with Wall St. ONON is doing the opposite."
2. Product diversification working: despite everyday running slower, still guiding >20% ex-FX growth → breadth of assortment (counters "one-shoe brand" bear thesis)
3. Big US wholesale share gain opportunity as Nike loses share: Elliott Hill became Nike CEO Oct 2024; by mid-2025 pushing wholesale partners to give Nike 2nd chance and take more inventory; given long lead times product landed at retail this year; UBS guess: not selling as well as expected, key retail partners using deep discounts to clear; brands often match promos; On declined ("race to the bottom"); retailers clear excess Nike goods, then restock but shift shelf space to brands with momentum & pricing power like On rather than Nike
- DKS-READ: UBS thesis that Nike's 2025 wholesale re-push landed at retail in 2026 and is being cleared with deep discounts by key retail partners (promotional US athletic footwear environment); ONON (and UAA) citing highly promotional Americas wholesale; UBS expects retailers to reallocate shelf space from Nike to momentum brands like On after clearance; Cloudsurfer 3 3-month run-specialty exclusive (Oct) before broad launch Jan
