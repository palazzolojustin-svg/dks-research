### What caused the downside
- FY rev gdnc cut from at least +23% y/y cc to low-20%, implying CHF3.47–3.56bn vs FactSet cons CHF3.56bn; midpoint 1.3% miss vs cons, low end 2.5% below
- View: rare gdnc downshift primarily "quality-of-growth decision, not a demand collapse"
- ONON preparing major refresh of everyday running franchises: Cloudsurfer 3, Cloudsurfer Max 2, Cloudflow – new foams, new technologies, Swiss-engineered precision fit; mgmt goal: clean runway so innovations "land well" in late 2026 and 2027
- Core issue: weaker sell-through in some everyday running franchises, esp Americas wholesale, in highly promotional marketplace; ONON intentionally held back sell-in to protect full-price integrity & keep inventories clean; mgmt: revised top-line gdnc "very much linked to the wholesale actions"; DTC strong & above expectations
- Hence GM gdnc raised from at least 64.5% to at least 65.0%; adj EBITDA margin kept 19.5%–20.0%; operational efficiencies helped
- 2Q GM 65.4%, +390bps y/y, beat ests by 2.2%, despite higher US import tariffs and before any tariff refund benefit
- Ameriprise: deliberate "reset the channel before the innovation wave" move, not broad demand reset

### Key takeaways
- 2Q adj EBITDA miss on softer sales (wholesale drag); DTC beat; margins beat
- Shares slid after FY rev gdnc lowered for subdued Americas wholesale growth in 2H
- ONON expects wholesale to re-accelerate in 2027 as innovations land & inventory normalizes
- Next catalyst: Investor Day in September (LT roadmap, growth ambitions)
- Premium positioning key differentiator in "otherwise discount-driven marketplace"

### Valuation / bull-bear
- Premium multiple warranted only if quarter seen as disciplined brand-management reset vs demand reset
- Bull: strong DTC, expanding GM, high brand awareness, younger consumer adoption, apparel growth, APAC momentum, credible tech-driven pipeline into 2027
- Bear: premium valuation leaves little room for missteps; may struggle if wholesale doesn't reaccelerate post channel reset or running launches fail to restore everyday-running confidence
- Post sell-off P/S 2.7x vs 5-yr avg P/S 7.2x

### Thesis
- Awareness up materially but far from saturated → long runway for new customers; existing customers deepen via higher baskets, repeat, multi-category; demand largely at full price → pricing power; disciplined distribution over volume
- Premium positioning → structurally higher GM via price/mix + scaling opex efficiencies; 2026 EBITDA gdnc implies incremental growth accretive; reinvestment funded by internal cash
- Apparel & accessories scaling faster than core, attractive margins via high DTC penetration; innovation (LightSpray manufacturing, materials/design) extends tech moat

### Risks / profile
- Supply chain, geopolitical, currency (reports CHF, US sales recognized in USD); sourcing from EMs → US tariff rise could lower spending; rapidly changing footwear industry; failure to innovate/expand; Vietnam labor law changes; margin compression in weaker retail; excess inventory from forecast error
- Profile: founded 2010, Zurich; tech CloudTec, CloudTec Phase, Helion superfoam, Speedboard; manufacturing Vietnam (90%), Indonesia (10%); channels wholesale w/ selected retailers, own stores, DTC digital; NYSE listed 2021
