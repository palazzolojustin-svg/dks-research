### Tom's Takeaways
1. Wholesale caution clouds DTC strength: DTC +34% in Q2, acceleration over prior 2 quarters, mgmt: momentum "highly encouraging"; wholesale only +13% in Q2 vs +25% in Q1. On Q1 call (5/12) co had alluded to slower 2H wholesale for clean marketplace ahead of early-2027 launches (new additions to CloudSurfer & CloudFlow franchises, further LightSpray scaling); appears it began reducing sell-in earlier than expected (Q2 slowdown) and at larger magnitude (Q3 more impacted than Q4). Mgmt: marketplace promotional, esp Americas → softer sell-outs in some everyday running franchises
2. EMEA, Asia solid: outside Americas strong wholesale sell-out rates; EMEA +21% Q2 (vs +26% Q1), outpacing regional peers; Asia +55% (vs +61% Q1), led by China & Japan
3. Margin outlook strong: GM +390bps y/y vs cons +300bps (full-price selling, DTC mix, lower freight), offsetting unquantified tariff headwind; FY26 GM now at least 65% (from prior 64.5%–65.0%), >220bps y/y growth, potential additional upside as tariff refunds (text: "contemplated in guidance")
- DKS-READ: ONON reducing sell-in to (US) wholesale earlier and more than planned, Q3 more impacted than Q4; promotional Americas marketplace w/ softer sell-out of everyday running; thesis cites ONON gaining shelf space "with the biggest and best sneaker retailers in the world"; early-2027 launches (CloudSurfer, CloudFlow, LightSpray)

### Thesis / scenarios
- Thesis: one of strongest fundamental names in coverage; runway via brand awareness & shelf space gains with biggest/best sneaker retailers
- Bull: 2H26 better on awareness & DTC → FY26 low-to-mid 20s cc; ~20% next year → stock could exceed $50
- Base: FY26 rev +20–25% cc, FY27 +15–20%; ~50bps adj EBITDA margin expansion/yr; PT $37 = 20x FY27E EPS CHF1.50 (~$1.85)
- Bear: brand "hitting a wall"; FY26 sub-20%, FY27 LDD; margins protected via slower investment but no expansion beyond 2026; FY26 adj EBITDA ~CHF100mn below forecast; stock to mid-$20s
- Description: HQ Switzerland, majority of revenues in North America
- Upside drivers: faster share/shelf space gains; Asia & apparel; full-price sell-through → margin beat
- Risks: highest valuation multiples in coverage (P/E, EV/EBITDA) → multiple contraction risk; highly competitive, fast-changing footwear; innovation failure; promotional retail could force discounting → margin degradation
