### Risk factors (FY2024 version — distinct content)
- Macro: incl pandemics/public health; conflicts in Ukraine & Middle East; consumer debt and rising cost of basic necessities.
- Competition: AI/ML innovation; real-time price comparison.
- Product costs: "tariffs (including tariffs recently enacted or that may be enacted in the future by the federal government or by other countries in response to U.S. tariffs)", strengthening USD; transport: fuel, Ukraine/Middle East conflicts impacting ocean trade routes; transport labor shortages; port congestion.
- Foreign sourcing: import duties/quotas, possible destination-based income tax on foreign suppliers, loss of "most favored nation" status; tensions w/ China, Russia, Middle East → sanctions/tariffs.
- Consumer: COVID-19 shifted demand toward athletic apparel, athleisure, active lifestyle — uncertain whether trends continue; supply chain challenges from Ukraine/Middle East.
- Vertical brands ~13% of overall sales, not available from other retailers.
- Reputation list incl: cyber/ransomware, data governance/privacy, product liability/recalls/boycotts, corporate responsibility, social media, "our policies related to the sale of firearms and accessories", public stances on controversial social/political issues, celebrity/athlete/influencer sponsorships, real estate decisions, foreign manufacturer labor/environment practices.
- Strategic initiatives risk incl strategies "to better resource for future growth and manage various cost categories" may need short-term spend/not achieve savings.
- Real estate risk (FY2024): performance depends on growing HoS, Field House, GGPC; repositioning existing portfolio incl consolidating stores in a market, lease renewals/restructurings, remodels, closing underperformers, repurposing real estate for specialty concepts; stores primarily in shopping centers/malls in regional areas; landlord/anchor tenant financial difficulty risk.
- Distribution risk: may need more reliance on stores, 3PL fulfillment centers, vendors if facility leases expire/terminate.
- Data: "from time-to-time, non-material data security issues"; no material breaches; state privacy laws & federal cyber disclosure rules.
- IT: power outages, infected updates, ransomware, DoS; hybrid remote work; gen-AI "still in their early stages".
- Labor: store managers, DC staff, tech/digital/real estate professionals; federal/state minimum wage; unionization risk.
- Key persons: Hobart; Stack "continues to oversee our merchandising group and key strategic growth initiatives"; "If Mr. Stack no longer serves a role in our business, our results could be materially adversely affected."
- Weather: hurricanes, tornadoes, storms, wildfires, floods, earthquakes could damage company/vendor facilities, staffing shortages.
- **Shrink (FY2024 wording): "We have experienced, and may continue to experience, elevated levels of inventory shrink relative to historical levels, which have adversely affected, and could continue to adversely affect, our business"**; high shrink at certain stores could cause impairments (FY2025: "generally stabilized in 2025").
- Stock: "We have in the past failed and may in the future fail to meet market expectations"; subject to securities class action and derivative lawsuits.
- Anti-takeover adds Delaware §203 (15% holder business combination restriction).
- Suppliers FY2024: ~1,400 vendors; Nike ~25%; vendors increasingly sell DTC or via broadened channels (department stores, family footwear stores, eCommerce companies); incentives (return privileges, volume allowances, co-op advertising) not guaranteed.
- Regulation: incl knives, food items, regulated products; eCommerce incl AI; historical firearms regs; pandemic store-closure risk.
- Firearms: no longer sold; litigation risk re ATF/state compliance, background checks, municipalities' suits.
- IP increasingly important for growing vertical brands. Tax: changes "may be more rapid under the federal government".
- ESG: federal executive orders/memoranda/investigations opposing private-sector DEI.
- Strategic transactions: generic (pre-FL). Capital: Revolving Credit Facility limits certain subs' unsecured debt; RCF & indenture (2032/2052 Notes only) limit secured debt & sale of substantially all assets. Senior Notes CoC put 101%.
