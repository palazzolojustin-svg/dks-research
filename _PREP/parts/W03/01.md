### MD&A
- Strategy context: over past 5 yrs improved assortment via key brand partner relationships (differentiated product) and vertical brands, enhanced store selling culture/service, experiential elements & tech, data science for pricing, digital marketing, personalization; consumers made lasting lifestyle changes toward health/fitness/sports/outdoor. Net sales +40.5% FY2021 vs FY2019; merch margin +626 bps same comparison. Pretax margin 4.7% FY2019 → 16.2% FY2021.
- Macro: uncertain—inflation, economic slowdown; supply chain disruption (factory closures, port congestion) caused apparel overages from late-arriving inventory and elevated container/transport costs "which began moderating during the current quarter"; may affect discretionary spending and promotional landscape "including the Company's actions to manage its apparel overage"; current-year outlook contemplates this.
- Inflation Reduction Act (enacted 2022-08-16): 15% min tax on adjusted financial statement income, 1% excise tax on net buybacks after 2022-12-31; not expected material to results, ETR, or liquidity.

Critical accounting policies: inventory valuation, business development allowances, goodwill/intangibles, long-lived asset impairment, self-insurance reserves, SBC.
