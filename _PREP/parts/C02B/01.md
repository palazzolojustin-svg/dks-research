### Admin (Gilch)
- Foot Locker (FL) enters comp calc beginning Q4 2026 = start of 14th full month post-acquisition.
- Q1 2026 results tentatively 2026-05-27.

### Stack prepared remarks
- DKS Business Q4: comps over 3%, double-digit non-GAAP EPS growth; market share gains.
- Owned FL ~6 months; "our excitement and our conviction in the long-term opportunity here continue to grow."
- "Fast Break" initiative (N. America test-and-learn) = evolution of 11-store pilot discussed last qtr. Q4: Fast Break stores drove "very strong positive comps, actually meaningfully exceeding the DICK'S Business, while also delivering strong gross margin improvement." Drivers: clearer storytelling, better presentation, more focused assortment — removed ~30% of styles on shoe wall that were unproductive; eliminated the "run-on sentence."
- Expanded Fast Break to additional 10 stores in LA before NBA All-Star Game; strong early perf. Plan to "rapidly scale Fast Break by back-to-school 2026."
- "Clean out the garage": inventory cleanup "now essentially complete"; drove Q4 profitability results as previously guided; leveraged DKS value chain to clear product; Q4 FL sales better than expected; FL inventory "now well-positioned"; "set up to play offense and deliver the inflection point we expect to see in this business, starting with back-to-school."
- FL global store fleet review: still assessing underperformers but "we anticipate our closure list is now much smaller than we initially estimated"; identified opportunities to reposition/improve profitability in "a meaningful number of stores," informed by Fast Break success.
- Brand partners leaning in; NBA All-Star activation w/ Nike, Jordan, Adidas et al.; sought-after launches w/ exceptional sell-throughs; NBA talent appearances, community experiences in LA; sales "meaningfully eclipsed last year's event."
- FY2026 FL gdnc: "we expect Foot Locker to deliver growth in comp sales of between 1% to 3% and operating income in the range of $100 million to $150 million. We continue to anticipate an inflection point for both sales and profitability, beginning with the back-to-school season."
- Combination: more scale, deeper brand relationships, access to new consumers, global footprint. >100,000 teammates globally.

### Hobart prepared remarks (DKS Business)
- 4 strategic pillars: omni-channel athlete experience; differentiated on-trend product assortment; deep engagement w/ DKS brand; teammates & culture.
- FY2025 DKS Business: record sales $14.1B; comps +4.5%, exceeded high end of expectations, driven by avg ticket & transactions growth; GM expansion; op margin 11.1%; non-GAAP EPS $14.58 (above high end of outlook) vs $14.05 2024.
- Q4 DKS Business: comps +3.1% on +6.6% LY; 2-yr stack "nearly 10%"; more athletes purchasing, spent more per trip; Q4 GM expansion accelerated sequentially; op margin 11%; non-GAAP EPS $4.05, both well ahead of LY.
- Sport/culture tailwinds: expanded college football playoffs, record women's sports interest, Team USA Winter Olympics; most 2026 World Cup matches on US soil June–July; 2028 LA Summer Olympics; Ryder Cup returns to US 2029 → "one of the most compelling multiyear periods for sport in this country's history."
- 2026 priorities: growth in key categories via national brand partners, new/emerging brands, vertical brands.
- House of Sport (HoS) & Field House (FH), 5 yrs into journey: FY2025 opened 16 new HoS → 35 total; 15 new FH → 42 total. Landlord interest "extremely strong." FY2026 plan: ~14 HoS, ~22 FH openings.
- Athlete-service "common purpose"; consistency across channels (search/reviews online, digital tools in store/app, teammates); evolving in-store service/selling culture (relationship building, training, tools) — "ongoing journey," feedback "incredibly encouraging."
- Athlete data; GameChanger and DICK'S Media Network (DMN) long-term opportunities.
- FY2026 DKS Business gdnc: comps 2%–4% (midpoint = 7.5% two-yr stack); op margin ~11.1% at midpoint; ~10 bps non-GAAP op margin expansion at high end. Consolidated FY2026 non-GAAP EPS $13.50–$14.50.

### Gupta: FY2025 results
- Consolidated net sales +28.1% to $17.22B; $3.11B contribution from partial-year FL; DKS Business comp +4.5% = avg ticket +4.2%, transactions +0.3%; 2-yr stack 9.7%, 3-yr stack 12.3%.
- Consolidated non-GAAP OI $1.52B, 8.81% of sales vs $1.5B, 11.14% LY. DKS Business OI $1.57B, 11.12% of sales (strong comps + GM expansion); FL partial-year op loss $52.2M.
- Consolidated non-GAAP EPS $13.20 (just over 20 weeks of FL; diluted shares 85.1M). DKS Business non-GAAP EPS $14.58 on 81.2M shares (excl. FL acquisition share dilution); exceeded high end of gdnc; +3.8% vs $14.05 LY.

### Q&A
- Q [Nagel, Oppenheimer]: DKS sales gdnc above Street — source of confidence? Why modest Q4 decel vs Q3?
  - A [Hobart]: Q4 +3.1% on +6.6%; 2-yr stack exceeded internal expectations, close to 10%; expanded GM & op margin. Growth across footwear, apparel, hardlines; "consumers are doing very, very well"; growth across all income demographics; "We haven't seen trade-down"; new/innovative/technical product resonating; March Madness, World Cup ahead. 2026 2-yr stack 7.5% (2–4% on top of 4.5%).
- Q [Yih, Barclays]: Clean-out-the-garage status; more work? How many Fast Break stores this yr? 
  - A [Stack]: lots of excess unproductive inventory; Fast Break took out ~30% of SKUs; teams in N. America, Europe, Asia executed; "that work is done. That's behind us." Cleared via store markdowns, through FL & Champs stores, and DKS value chain "Going Going Gone" — recovered higher cash than via a jobber. "This inventory at Foot Locker is probably cleaner than it has ever been"; bodes well for margins/sales; return to growth w/ 1–3% comp; "should have margin expansion here. We're confident of that."
- Q [Yih]: 2026 innovation pipeline (technical running, performance basketball) — iconic product from big partners vs niche brands?
  - A [Hobart]: growth across the board; strategic partners strong; running footwear innovation & Nike's new run construct doing very well; running broadly strong; signature basketball very strong (DKS & FL); women's sports — Sabrina, A'ja doing well, Caitlin coming; team sports driving buzz formerly driven by footwear launches; new brands via HoS → widening into FH → full DKS format; DKS is Gymshark's first US wholesale partner. Growth across strategic, emerging, vertical brands.
  - A [Stack]: Nike doing very well; leaning into World Cup w/ Adidas; partnered w/ Fanatics on collectibles/trading cards — collectible shops in all HoS going forward, some FH; new business, "will be accretive to our sales number."
- Q [Gutman, MS]: Expected stronger core 2026 margin (HoS penetration, GM gains) and more FL EBIT for accretion — fair?
  - A [Stack]: could have guided FL higher based on original projections; but Fast Break (original 11, +10 LA, a couple in Europe) shows underperforming stores (losing money or marginally profitable) can be made very profitable via Fast Break renovation → closing fewer stores than originally anticipated; closing them would have made FL more profitable in Q1/Q2; "we will get to probably 250 of these stores by back-to-school, which is a Herculean effort"; optimism on FL "even greater than it was originally."
  - A [Gupta]: gdnc balances confidence vs macro/geopolitical situation ("very dynamic"), factored in. Q4 DKS GM +67 bps all from merch margin in typically promotional qtr.
  - A [Stack]: Q4 "more promotional out there than we had anticipated"; team managed margin rates well.
- Q [Gutman]: 2026 1H/2H — explicit World Cup top-line impact? Are investments core-related / pre-World Cup?
  - A [Gupta]: DKS comps slightly higher 1H on World Cup; no explicit number but assumed in gdnc. Op margin declines 1H for two reasons: (1) investments to position business long term; (2) synergy benefits back-half weighted.
- Q [McShane, GS]: GameChanger & retail media initiatives; margin contribution this year?
  - A [Hobart]: GameChanger = market leader in multibillion-dollar sports tech space; "nearly 40% CAGR and strong profitability"; SaaS; embeds DKS in youth sports (parents, grandparents, kid stats/highlight reels). Unique live-sports inventory for DMN; appealing to brand partners & non-endemic advertisers. New features: HD video quality; coaches' tools just launched. DMN: tech team built automation to attribute sales to partners' investments.
  - A [Gupta]: GM drivers intact for 2026 & pipeline: product access, vertical brands, GameChanger, DMN; balanced in 2026 vs opening of sixth distribution center in early 2026 (contemplated in gdnc).
- Q [Horvers, JPM]: Learnings from 11-store test; city-center vs suburban mall mix; receptivity to HOKA/On running vs basketball w/ FL customer; commonality of "200 locations" targeted by BTS?
  - A [Stack]: 11-store test broad-based: urban, suburban, high-volume, lower-volume stores (lower-volume success = why fewer closures); LA 10 more urban. Common merch presentation theme across banners: removed unproductive inventory, ~30% of choices out, relaid wall w/ key product (e.g., Air Force 1 in color, New Balance launch) to communicate new/high-heat product; comps "extremely strong" → roll out to ~250 stores by BTS: cross-section of urban, suburban, mall; store-by-store; includes US + Europe (a couple in Europe, pleased). "If we weren't highly confident ... we wouldn't be rolling out 250 of them by back-to-school."
- Q [Horvers]: DKS traffic turned negative in Q4; ticket comparisons get harder; tariff inflation? Traffic outlook?
  - A [Hobart]: Q4 transactions positive on 2-yr stack; full-yr positive; tough compare. Basket/AUR strength = differentiated assortment, "It's really not due to inflation"; increasing access/allocation to great product. 2–4% on 4.5% → "we are not concerned about traffic or transactions."
- Q [Lejuez, Citi]: Synergies grow beyond medium term? Define medium term. FL used to earn $700M+ OI pre-2020 — path back?
  - A [Gupta]: reiterate $100M–$125M over medium term, very confident; six months in; will update if expectations change. Portion in 2026 included in gdnc; medium term "maybe a couple of years after that." $700M: "a little bit too early to be able to give a long-term outlook"; focus on 1–3% comp & returning business to profitability; longer-term outlook later.
- Q [Baker, D.A. Davidson]: FL profit pacing — negative in Q1/Q2 until BTS?
  - A [Gupta]: sales and profitability back-half weighted; real inflection when buys sourced the way DKS wants (BTS timeframe) and Fast Break stores in position (BTS); synergies kick in 2H 2026. (Did not directly confirm Q1/Q2 losses.)
- Q [Baker]: Agentic commerce impact/positioning?
  - A [Hobart]: exploring all AI incl. agentic. Two opportunities: (1) teammate efficiency — MarTech tech removing manual work; AI in store labor forecasting; new AI-enabled tool in app w/ custom recommendations; inventory mgmt & regional relevancy factored w/ AI; (2) agentic future — make DKS expertise/data/knowledge of sports available to consumers in "the new world." "More to come."
- Q [Civello, Truist]: Extend DMN to FL? Timeline?
  - A [Hobart]: "a little premature"; DKS maniacally focused on DKS, FL on FL; long-term opportunity.
- Q [Civello]: Financial impact by segment of using Going Going Gone for FL clean-out? In synergies?
  - A [Stack]: helps — removes old unproductive inventory, brings cash, cleans store to feature newer styles sold at "basically full price"; helps margins, sales, cash flow; done on DKS side, expect on FL side.
  - A [Gupta]: not in synergies; synergies = merchandising actions (primarily negotiations) + non-merch procurement negotiations.
- Q [Fernández, Telsey]: Q4 FL pro forma comp −3.4% vs gdnc "down mid to high single-digit" — why better? Regions (NA vs Europe)? Fast Break include Champs/Kids Foot Locker?
  - A [Stack]: beat due to "Stripers" (FL team) driving clean-out of old inventory → better sales; margin rate "right in line." NA vs Europe: not huge difference; US now a bit ahead of Europe because more Fast Break stores in US; Europe "not very far behind." Hired Matthew Barnes from Aldi to run Europe/international; he has made team changes; confident in turning international business around. (Champs/Kids FL part not directly answered.)
