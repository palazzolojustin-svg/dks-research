### Stack prepared remarks — what changed
- Q2 strong on sales, esp. DKS Business "nearly a 5% comp"; share gains; encouraged by World Cup investment response → positions DKS for rising soccer participation long term.
- "While the DICK'S Business delivered the sales and profitability we expected, as the quarter progressed, it became clear that inventory levels were building up across parts of the industry, leading to a much more promotional environment. This pressured our overall company earnings."
- Consumer preferences evolving toward newness, innovation, broader set of brands. Inventory built "particularly within certain legacy footwear silhouettes and apparel franchises that simply aren't resonating the way they once did," across industry supply chains & retail → "increasingly aggressive promotional environment."
- Response: remain competitively priced to protect leadership; "we view the pricing investments we're making today as an investment in the future strength of our business."
- Affected DKS & FL to different degrees. DKS: diversified model, broad category mix, balanced brand portfolio mitigated. Strong DKS areas: team sports, licensed; new emerging apparel brands strong engagement; running, cleats, outdoor footwear healthy. DKS gained share & strong sales despite margin pressure.
- FL hit harder: greater exposure to legacy footwear silhouettes; more dependent on launch & retro product; fewer launches in Q2 and launches "performed below industry and our expectations" → meaningful impact.
- Actions: shift mix toward in-demand brands; launch calendar expected more favorable 2H "the quality of those launches will be critical." Promotional environment esp. legacy silhouettes expected challenging "at least through the fourth quarter."
- FL EMEA "more challenging than expected"; always anticipated longer recovery than N. America; promo environment "very aggressive in EMEA," industry carrying too much inventory, consumer more cautious than expected due to geopolitical environment. EMEA softness + market dynamics "delayed the pace of improvement that we expected to see in the Foot Locker Business."
- Company-wide: macro & geopolitical concerns weighed on profitability; impacted fuel, supply chain, healthcare, other costs → revised balance-of-year expectations.
- "We believe the DICK'S Business remains strong and none of this changes our confidence in the long-term opportunity at Foot Locker. We're still early in the Foot Locker turnaround."
- Investing in FL brand: first major brand campaign in more than a decade, "incredibly well-received"; investing in Stripers.
- Fast Break: surpassed BTS goal of ~250 stores globally; continuing expansion ahead of holiday.
- Brand partners highly supportive; see DKS as "the most important player in the global athletic ecosystem." Combination DKS + FL + GameChanger best positioned. Continue investing in HoS, FH, GameChanger, FL turnaround; "deliberate investments in price to protect and grow our leadership position." "We've navigated environments like this before."

### Hobart prepared remarks (DKS Business)
- Q2 DKS total sales +5.6%; comp +4.9%; "our growth outpaced the broader industry by nearly 200 basis points"; ticket & transactions growth; broad-based across footwear, apparel, hardlines; DKS GM expanded.
- DMN & GameChanger strong returns, diversify earnings; together with tariff refunds recognized in qtr helped offset promotional pressure in athletic footwear & apparel. Deliberate price investment.
- World Cup: invested significantly in marketing, primarily via adidas partnership; "outstanding results"; expect World Cup to catalyze long-term US soccer participation, fan engagement, demand.
- Q2 opened 5 HoS, 8 FH. FY2026 now ~14 HoS and 20 FH total (FH was ~22 at Mar-26 gdnc). HoS experiences: Collectors Clubhouse, Lids partnership driving engagement/sales. Real estate: Cerritos, Tysons Corner, Palm Beach Gardens; "the future of sports retail."
- ScoreCard loyalty: ~30 million active athletes. Relaunched ScoreCard in qtr (greater value, flexibility, member benefits); introduced ScoreCard+ paid tier at $99/year for most engaged athletes; aims: engagement, purchase frequency, loyalty.
- GameChanger "exceptional results"; one of most comprehensive youth sports ecosystems; feeds DMN.
- Positive footwear and apparel comps at DKS in Q2, but industry worsened as qtr progressed; expectations adjusted for ongoing dynamics; "our sales expectations remain unchanged" for DKS.

### Q&A
- Q [Gutman, MS]: What changed vs 90 days ago when raising gdnc and optimistic?
  - A [Stack]: DKS comped 4.9%, DKS sales expectations not reduced; margin pressure as market became "much more promotional, primarily in the athletic footwear space." "What changed is a number of brands got very promotional on their sites, and those promotions spilled into the broader marketplace; and we expect that to, unfortunately, continue through the balance of the year." DKS broad category portfolio, far less footwear-reliant than FL. Took DKS EPS down "a little bit" (margin pressure, macro/geopolitical). Participating in promotions = "one of the best investments we can make" to keep share — promotions "going to subside at some point." FL: footwear = vast majority of FL; more reliant on slowed legacy silhouettes and launch product; Q2 launch product "disappointing in the marketplace, not just for us but for the entire marketplace." EMEA harder than realized: more competitive, much more promotional than US; consumer more cautious due to fuel costs; excess inventory → margin pressure; other European retailers echo. Brand partnerships as global partner "are going to pay huge dividends in the future"; "industry is going through a bit of a transition"; FL acquisition "absolutely the long-term right decision, although, right now, I'm sure some of you are kind of scratching your head."
- Q [Gutman]: Footwear fatigue/demand vs a supplier or two clearing old inventory? Same for athleisure apparel?
  - A [Stack]: several suppliers w/ inventory promoted on their sites, spilled over. "I don't think this is a demand issue." Consumers want new/innovative/different; legacy silhouettes/franchises "slowed and slowed relatively quickly." Working: Nike Mind shoe, Nike running construct, adidas women's print & pattern. "I think this is temporary"; "the pain that we're going to endure here from a promotional standpoint." Apparel: legacy items broadly distributed, slowed; new product strong — Nike Solo Fleece [audio gap], Gymshark, Free People Movement → why DKS sales gdnc not cut.
- Q [Horvers, JPM]: Casual/fashion vs running? Hangover after emergence of two huge brands (4 yrs), slowing innovation hitting DKS traffic?
  - A [Stack]: "I think we have the hangover right now" with legacy silhouettes; reset across Nike, adidas ("addy"), On, HOKA. Nike Mind shoe great; Nike redefined running silhouette — Vomero and Peg doing extremely well. Nike basketball pipeline exciting (Ja, Caitlin, A'ja); exciting NBA playoffs last yr / Knicks to give basketball "a bit of a pop," Nike best positioned. On, adidas, HOKA innovation coming. Casual: UGG and Birkenstock — greater access & allocation, terrific partnership. Pain "at least through the end of this year."
  - A [Hobart]: two-big-brands hangover "is not the issue" — running and all performance doing really well; issue focused on "lifestyle legacy silhouettes."
  - A [Stack]: brands innovating within legacy silhouettes (materials, print, pattern, color) showing some life, but "not enough of them in the marketplace to offset the traditional ones, not yet anyway."
- Q [Horvers]: FL 2H top line & margin cadence; Q3 vs Q4 depth?
  - A [Stack]: "Q3 is going to be a bit more difficult than Q4"; won't detail Q4. Brand exec quote: "I've never seen the specialty channel of distribution so promotional in my career"; specialty channel "extremely promotional"; seen at a competitor. Normalizes once inventory cleaned. "Footwear is the engine that pulls the train." Kids need ~five pairs: sport-specific, running/training (plus specialized weightlifting), recovery, lifestyle. "Every once in a while, an industry has to go through a little bit of pain to reset"; will come out stronger.
  - A [Hobart]: DKS margin & SG&A investment also more aggressive in Q3 than Q4.
- Q [McShane, GS]: Fast Break vs legacy FL store perf; assortment changes (started hitting August) response; YE Fast Break count?
  - A [Stack]: Fast Break "definitely outperformed the traditional legacy stores"; still carry some legacy silhouettes, "not perfect yet," need new assortment, coming. Continue investing. YE count "somewhere in the north of 300, 350 doors by the end of the year globally."
- Q [McShane]: Quantify cut from EMEA vs US?
  - A [Stack]: won't quantify; "EMEA is much more impactful than in the US"; more competitive, much more promotional.
- Q [Yih, Barclays]: Is it lifestyle athletic vs performance; shift to non-athletic (UGG, Birkenstock) — participate at scale? Brands cite new cycle for BTS next year — canceling orders? spring buys?
  - A [Stack]: athletic performance shoes "continuing to do very well"; lifestyle "a bit more of an issue"; brands adding lifestyle newness (Nike Mind; Nike patent leather; adidas print & pattern); "going to take a little bit of time." Shift toward "brown shoe" (UGG, Birkenstock) — "Those businesses for us are really on fire. They're up significantly"; evaluating a couple of other brands to add. DKS footwear expected to "comp positively in the back half of the year," with some margin pressure. Price investment to keep share: "we don't make investments for a quarter or two ... We really look to make investments for a lifetime." Orders: "modifying some orders here and there but nothing really out of the ordinary"; seeing some innovation now and into next yr.
- Q [Hutchinson, BofA]: FL margin self-help while comps weak in 2H?
  - A [Stack]: must be competitively priced; "we're not leading this margin erosion. We are participating in it."
  - A [Hobart]: bringing apparel into FL stores + allocations of new product help.
  - A [Stack]: brands provided additional higher-heat product sellable at full price to offset some margin.
- Q [Boruchow, Wells Fargo]: Early BTS signals at FL and DKS? FL guide-down: "$200 million revenue cut driving a $200 million profit cut" — FL GM/merch margin expectations 2H/Q3?
  - A [Stack]: drivers = sales cut (marketplace), margin change, plus marketing investment. FL had no out-of-home marketing plan "since roughly 2013"; prior mgmt "did nothing to market the business from a top-of-funnel standpoint"; new campaign "Colors" (journey of sneaker culture) — great response; "putting a sizable amount of money into marketing"; increased Stripers' payroll. "So that's where the profitability cut's coming from."
  - A [Stack]: no intra-quarter BTS commentary; BTS embedded in FY gdnc.
- Q [Lasser, UBS]: 2027 economics — DKS laps World Cup, Knicks win, tariff benefits; if conditions persist, market will extrapolate 2H DKS outlook?
  - A [Hobart]: Q2 4.9% comp includes World Cup, but ex-World Cup still growth across hardlines, apparel, footwear; no 2027 gdnc; exciting top-line initiatives; continued investment.
  - A [Gupta]: no 2027 gdnc; DMN & GameChanger becoming bigger merch-margin expansion drivers; merch margin expanded "almost about 150 basis points" (said "in Q3"; context = Q2) incl. tariff benefit (not quantified on call), ex-tariff still expansion from new capabilities offsetting footwear/apparel promos & higher fuel. Framework: GM expansion + appropriate investments continues into 2027.
- Q [Lasser]: Engineer greater FL profitability via fleet optimization or monetizing assets under FL umbrella? Conditions to shift from improving to rightsizing?
  - A [Stack]: "we're not going to kind of lay out specifics, but the answer to your question is simply yes."
  - A [Gupta]: reiterated $100M–$125M synergies — "bigger part of the rubric."
- Q [Lejuez, Citi]: SG&A management in tough market; 2H SG&A growth; rationale for big FL marketing spend when stores/assortment not ready?
  - A [Stack]: best time to invest in marketing while market in turmoil and others scale back → gain share; FL did "virtually no marketing other than digital marketing for so many years," brand atrophied; "perfect time."
  - A [Hobart]: long-term lens; DKS SG&A investments (tech, GameChanger, DMN) plus HoS/FH capex drive growth, profitability, GM — offset market pressures; "absolutely not considering slowing down."
  - A [Gupta]: DKS SG&A deleverages FY, highest pressure Q3 ~50 bps; SG&A to leverage in Q4 as lapping investments.
- Q [Drbul, BTIG]: Trading card business performance?
  - A [Stack]: "has been great"; Collectors Clubhouse in all HoS going forward, moving into a number of FH; "very big growth opportunity"; partnership w/ Michael Rubin's company (Fanatics) in trading cards & collectibles; will market trading cards more aggressively; aim to be "the main distribution points for these cards"; family activity.
