### Admin (Gilch)

### Stack prepared remarks
- >100,000 teammates. "Sport is one of the hottest categories in the country today"; evidence: rising pro team valuations, streaming/network investment, advertiser demand for live sports. 2026 World Cup, 2028 LA Olympics → "one of the most exciting multi-year periods for sport."
- Ecosystem: HoS/FH reshaping retail; GameChanger embedded in youth sports; Golf Galaxy leadership in category w/ strong participation & rising cultural relevance; FL reaches different consumer (sneaker culture, basketball, lifestyle). Brands partner to launch ideas, tell stories, scale concepts globally.
- "We're playing offense for the long-term, and it's widening the gap between us and the rest of the industry. Our vision is to build the best sports company in the world, and we're just getting started."
- DKS Business Q1 comp +6%.
- FL Q1: global FL Business "slightly positive comps and operating income, with merch margin improvement"; first qtr of positive comps for FL Business since Q4 2024. North America comp +1.4%; within it US Foot Locker banner comp +6.4%. FL banner = largest/most critical part, focused there first. "We have a clear plan and it's working."
- FL FY comp gdnc raised low end: 1.5%–3% (from 1%–3%).
- Store teammates ("Stripers" = FL, "Blue Shirts" = DKS) energized; authenticity in telling sneaker story.
- Fast Break = "capital light remodel initiative": expanded by ~90 stores in Q1 → ~100 total; Fast Break stores delivered double-digit comps in Q1 + meaningful merch margin improvement. Plan ~250 Fast Break stores by BTS across Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker and Champs globally; further expansion ahead of holiday. Elements: more focused shoe wall, improved storytelling, reintroduction of apparel w/ curated complementary offerings; typically completed in a few days; limited capital; "At its core, its retail 101, and when you execute it with discipline, it works."
- BTS = first season team had full control over buys; feel great about product; supported by "bold brand relaunch" to bring consumers back to FL brand. Fundamentals: supply chain improvements (moving product faster, to right stores), greater pricing discipline, real-time data.
- Brand partners fully engaged; DKS/FL is "their largest global partner."

### Hobart prepared remarks (DKS Business)
- Q1 DKS comp +6%, growth in avg ticket and transactions; on top of +4.5% LY and +5.3% in 2024; share gains.
- More athletes purchasing, more frequent purchases, spent more per trip; healthy consumer across income demographics, "no signs of trading down"; particularly strong engagement from younger athletes. Broad-based growth footwear, apparel, hardlines.
- DKS FY comp gdnc raised low end: 2.5%–4% (from 2%–4%). High end now ~30 bps non-GAAP op margin expansion (prior ~10 bps). Consolidated non-GAAP EPS maintained $13.50–$14.50.
- Brand partners: Nike, adidas, Fanatics; emerging brands "Bioré" (as transcribed) and Gymshark; access, allocation, marketing support.
- Q1 opened 1 HoS, 2 FH; on track for ~13 more HoS and ~20 more FH this year (=14/22 total plan [d]). Landlord interest extremely strong; iconic locations incl. Palm Beach Gardens, Cerritos, Tysons Corner; can be selective.
- In-store: elevated service & selling rooted in sport/product expertise; investing in site & app.
- Announced summer launch of "Coach by DICK'S," AI-powered digital agent; extends teammate expertise into personalized conversational experience across product, training, services.
- DMN "high-growth asset"; reaches athletes across HoS & digital.
- Opened Fort Worth distribution center (serves fast-growing Texas market).
- GameChanger: earlier this yr most comprehensive product update in its history — 1080p live streaming, automated game highlight reels, AI-powered coaching tools. Q1: ~50% of all games covered on platform streamed live (record). "In the last month alone, more games were streamed on GameChanger than have been played in the entire history of Major League Baseball."

### Q&A
- Q [Gutman, MS]: Drivers of DKS +6%; underlying vs one-time; comps from here?
  - A [Hobart]: "definitely not a result of a one-time factor"; broad-based footwear, apparel, hardlines; within hardlines team sports, licensed, trading cards, golf strong; long-term strategies (differentiated/elevated product, newness, technical innovation, HoS/FH repositioning "cascading through our entire business"). Sport/culture; World Cup; LA28. No trade-down "from best to better or better to good"; growth across all income demographics; added 1.5 million new athletes to database.
- Q [Gutman]: Expected stronger flow-through on 6% comp — unique to Q1/2026 (World Cup, HoS timing)? Whole yr w/o op leverage?
  - A [Hobart]: performing exactly as expected/guided: 1H higher comps, significant investments (World Cup; continuing in Q2) → lower 1H flow-through. Raised high end by 20 bps: from +10 bps to +30 bps, 11.4%. "We're absolutely expecting leverage for the full year"; comes in 2H due to investment timing.
- Q [Nagel, Oppenheimer]: State of FL turnaround; Fast Break progress; FL inventory health?
  - A [Stack]: "right on schedule." Cleaned out garage last yr; inventory "in terrific shape." Repaired relationships w/ key brands "somewhat disenchanted with Foot Locker" — now fully supportive, "really want and need Foot Locker to be a stable growing retailer." Rebuilt mgmt teams; remerchandised via Fast Break: reduced ~30% of SKU choices; key styles, colors, stories; Fast Break comped double-digits Q1. Augmented some assortment in Q1, helped. Inflection begins BTS = first time team bought entire assortment; first time back marketing w/ big FL brand relaunch. US FL locations comped over 6% in Q1. "Our plan is working."
- Q [Nagel]: Fast Break stores we visited are cleaner but no new product — legacy product? Sales driven just by better organization?
  - A [Stack]: "Right now, yes." Increased apparel business/presentation (not perfect yet); FL had significantly scaled back apparel; brought back; "cobbling together, because we didn't buy this assortment"; brands provided additional allocation to improve in-stock. First self-bought assortment = BTS → inflection.
- Q [McShane, GS]: Capex outlook lowered — why; DKS vs FL split; Fast Break capex by FL banner?
  - A [Gupta]: net capex DKS ~$1B, FL ~$400M for 2026; vast majority of FL $400M = store investments incl. Fast Break (capital-light per store but large store count → significant portion). ~$100M capex reduction predominantly in DKS Business, from multi-year productivity initiatives — also shows in op margin leverage expectation.
- Q [McShane]: FL Europe currently?
  - A [Stack]: Europe "a little bit behind" US, as expected; just implementing Fast Break in Europe — couple stores done, "pretty promising"; similar mgmt changes; Europe about where anticipated; expected to stay behind US through year-end, "but we do expect that we'll catch up."
- Q [Yih, Barclays]: Macro backdrop embedded in gdnc? Footwear trends performance vs lifestyle vs non-athletic?
  - A [Hobart]: balanced confidence w/ "appropriate level of caution" on macro/geopolitical → left top end of comp range unchanged at both DKS and FL. Controllables working. Footwear: performance running very strong; basketball incl. women's basketball doing well; some lifestyle footwear very strong, esp. retro run; training and recovery strong. "Feeling very bullish" on footwear.
- Q [Yih]: Specific DKS brand-relationship muscles ported to FL?
  - A [Stack]: FL gets different allocation, access to products it didn't have, brands' confidence restored — brands "had really lost confidence in Foot Locker," doubted it could present/protect brands; relationships repaired. Location, exclusives on styles and/or colors, storytelling around athletes/sneaker culture "very different"; "you'll see a lot of that start to come to life to even greater degree in Q1 of next year as we're beginning to build those assortments now."
- Q [Drbul, BTIG]: DKS segment GM decline this qtr?
  - A [Gupta]: ~35 bps y/y (see above): supply chain (fuel; sixth DC opened tail end Q1, fixed-cost headwind) + trading card mix (new customers, collectibles market, lower GM). Both in line w/ expectations; FY GM expected to expand.
- Q [Drbul]: Basketball at DKS vs FL?
  - A [Stack]: basketball had slowed, "coming back in a really big fashion," built around women's basketball (Sabrina, A'ja); boys and girls buying; excited at both banners.
- Q [Lasser, UBS]: DKS GM improvement over yr — offsets to collectibles pressure beyond supply chain easing?
  - A [Gupta]: puts/takes contemplated: fuel pressure assumed to persist near term; sixth DC; occupancy headwind from portfolio repositioning; trading card mix. Offsets: access & allocation (key driver); pricing team; vertical brands ("vertical brands carry 700 basis points to 900 basis points of higher margin rate"), growing penetration; DMN strong growth; GameChanger growth.
  - A [Hobart]: collectibles/trading cards "exciting, incremental"; lower margin than overall mix but thinking of them as pressure "is the wrong way to look at it" — incremental GM dollars, more frequent visits, younger audience, drives trips; math works to grow GM for FY.
- Q [Lasser]: Quantify collectibles/trading card contribution in Q1? A [Hobart]: "We do not."
- Q [Lasser]: HoS economics — comp growth in yrs 2–4 vs chain; ROI sustainable scaling to 75+ locations?
  - A [Hobart]: tangible + intangible benefits; comp store growth confirmed in years three and four; "they open fully and then continue to grow"; strong sales, profitability, ROI. Intangibles: athletes spend more time and "significantly higher" spend than typical athlete; on-ramp for new/emerging brands (just added "Bioré" last qtr, Gymshark) enabled by HoS head-to-toe brand presentation; landlords — HoS drives traffic/revitalizes centers/malls → access to bigger, more premium locations. FH = "50k prototype," "really a mini version of a House of Sport." Whole chain benefits from product access, experiential selling.
  - A [Gupta]: investing in DMN within HoS; visual/marketing teams create measurable, quantifiable brand moments and report metrics back to brands.
- Q [Lejuez, Citi]: Good/better/best performance; leverage balance sheet / more aggressive buyback?
  - A [Hobart]: no trade-down across good/better/best; won't give specifics; range from opening price point (DSG brand — attractive pricing, high function/fashion) to technical apparel & performance equipment (cleats etc.); every tier doing well.
  - A [Gupta]: strong balance sheet; bought $140M of shares in Q1 and still ended w/ $1B cash; plenty of flexibility; buybacks remain "opportunistic" for balance of year.
- Q [Lejuez]: Private label perf vs chain?
  - A [Hobart]: vertical brands DSG, CALIA, VRST strong; Maxfli "doing amazingly well"; outperforming chain and expanding GM; vertical brand avg 700–900 bps higher GM than average; fill white space.
- Q [Horvers, JPM]: Did Q1 benefit from tax stimulus (all income levels) such that 2–3-yr trend unsustainable?
  - A [Gupta]: confidence in core strategies balanced vs macro/geopolitical. "I don't know if I would call out that we saw any significant benefit from the stimulus checks as they were puts and takes" — stimulus checks vs higher gas prices within Q1; strong results across both banners anyway.
- Q [Horvers]: FL comp AUR vs transactions? Remnant clearance in FL GM? Buy-in synergies in GM yet?
  - A [Stack]: still some clearance in GM ("always going to be clearance in a retail business"); won't report FL transactions/traffic until it becomes comp in Q4; on schedule; BTS inflection w/ own buys + relaunch marketing campaign. No buy-in synergies in FL GM yet ("No, no.").
- Q [Fernández, Telsey]: Fast Break count for full yr beyond 250 at BTS; accelerate given double-digit comps? BTS merch changes by category?
  - A [Stack]: ~250 by BTS; program continues through holiday — more done at end of Q3/beginning Q4; not guiding count; deciding how much to disrupt holiday business. BTS merch: better women's assortment; better basketball; performance run and retro run w/ more storytelling; better apparel tied to shoe stories; key accessory items FL previously ran out of will be in stock.
- Q [Civello, Truist]: Incremental comp lift from GLP-1 usage (categories/sizing)?
  - A [Hobart]: no specific data/correlation; long-running lean-in to healthier active lifestyle (post-COVID), outdoor, team sports, golf.
- Q [Civello]: Promotional environment at DKS and FL?
  - A [Hobart]: not a major factor in Q1; surgical, advanced pricing capabilities; "nothing on the horizon that we're particularly concerned about."
