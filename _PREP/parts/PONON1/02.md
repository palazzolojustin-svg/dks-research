### Valuation/thesis
- Expect solid upward revisions to 2026/27 EPS -> rebound; P/S 3.1x vs 5-yr avg 7.8x; MSCI ACWI ex-US Cons Disc 1.01x; deserves premium to slow-growth footwear/athleisure peers.
- Thesis: brand awareness up materially but far from saturated; new customer acquisition + deeper engagement (basket, repeat, multi-category); demand captured largely at full price; disciplined distribution vs volume. Premium -> structurally higher GM via price/mix + scale efficiencies; 2026 EBITDA gdnc implies incremental growth accretive, reinvestment funded internally. Apparel/accessories scaling faster, attractive margins (high DTC penetration). Innovation (LightSpray, materials) = catalyst/moat.
