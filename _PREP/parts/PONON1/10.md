1. Asia fastest (+61% CC) vs EMEA +26%, Americas +18% (Needham figure); Asia >50% of CHF growth (highest seen); Korea triple-digit, China above region avg; Asia slowed from +85% in 4Q25 on larger base.

3. LightSpray: new Korea factory opened Feb 2026, production capacity 30x; selling "several hundred" pairs/day in DTC at $280-$330.
4. Apparel: footwear +24%, apparel +58%; apparel >10% of DTC sales first time; approaching 7% of consolidated sales; "toe-to-head" brand acceptance.
5. Wholesale to slow in 2H ahead of '27 launches: gdnc embeds slower 2H top line; mgmt wants clean wholesale inventories ahead of slew of new products early 2027, restraining 2H sell-in. Needham: wise given issues rival Hoka had amid 2025 product launches. (DKS-READ: lower ONON sell-in to wholesale partners incl. DKS in 2H26.)

### Thesis/cases
- Thesis: one of strongest fundamental names in coverage; runway via brand awareness + shelf space gains with biggest sneaker retailers.
- Bull (>$60): 30%+ growth, FY26 mid-high 20s CC -> FY26 rev ~500bps above forecast; FY27 adj EBITDA ≥20%.
- Base ($45): FY26 rev +20-25% CC, FY27 +15-20%; ~50bps adj EBITDA margin expansion/yr; PT = low-mid 20s P/E on FY27E EPS CHF 1.52 (~$1.95).
- Bear (mid-$20s): brand "hitting a wall"; 4Q25 low-mid 20s, FY26 sub-20% -> FY26 rev ≥5% below target; no margin expansion beyond 2024; FY26 adj EBITDA ~CHF100mn below forecast. (Bull/bear text stale, references 4Q25.)
- Upside drivers: faster share/wholesale shelf space gains; Asia + apparel runways; full-price sell-through -> margin beat.
- Risks: highest valuation multiples in Needham coverage (P/E, EV/EBITDA) -> multiple contraction; competitive, fast-changing athletic footwear; innovation failure; promotional environment could force discounting/margin degradation.
