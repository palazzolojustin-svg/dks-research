- Inventory +2% y/y despite solid topline growth.
- FY26 gdnc: net sales growth "at least" +23% ex-FX reiterated; absolute "at least" CHF 3.51bn vs CHF 3.44bn prior (BBG cons CHF 3.54bn). GM ≥64.5% (vs ≥63.0% prior; cons 63.1%), up ≥170bps y/y; adj EBITDA margin 19.5-20.0% (vs 18.5-19.0%; cons 18.9%) -> midpoint adj EBITDA ~CHF 679mn (cons CHF 666mn).
- Expect LSD% upgrades to estimates and positive share reaction.
