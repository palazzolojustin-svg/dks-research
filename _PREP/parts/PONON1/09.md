### Thesis map (UBS standing views)
- Athletic wear industry +M-HSD% annual multi-yr; pre-pandemic decade avg 7%; COVID amplified health/wellness + casual dress; sportswear only 25% of total apparel+footwear sales.
- ONON can reach ≥2% LT market share (if equals Puma's FY21 sales); rare brand able to become global multi-sport multi-category like Nike/Adidas/Puma.
- LT EBIT margin mid-to-high teens: premium price, innovation not price competition, DTC/e-com-led; apparel accretive over time; comparable to Lululemon.
- UBS Evidence Lab Global Athletic Wear survey: global sportswear demand strong; global On awareness low (room to grow); consumers who know On increasingly favorable.
- Reverse DCF: market prices mid-teens sales growth (vs UBS ~18% 5-yr) and low-teens EBIT margins (vs high-teens FY30E).

- DTC: US brand awareness eclipsed 30% for first time in 1Q; marketing % sales +~200bps y/y to 13.1% in 1Q; healthy store opening pace; 2Q26 DTC laps +54% prior-yr growth.

- GM: 2Q26E 64.0% (+250bps y/y). 1Q: full-price selling, supply chain efficiencies, favorable FX more than offset ~100bps tariff headwinds; co views full-price + cost efficiencies as sustainable through FY26 and beyond; DTC mix + FX also help; current product tariffed at 10% vs 20% in FY26 outlook -> upside from 2Q26. FY26 GM 64.5% (+~170bps).

- Downside $19: ~16% EPS CAGR; can't mitigate tariffs; demand slows (inflation not transitory); innovation limited traction esp performance running; macro + competition; supply chain issues; ~11x on CHF 1.40 FY28E.
