### Event reaction (10/01/26, post-1Q27 print)
- "While Nike's North America business continues to improve, weakness elsewhere and a much softer fiscal 2027 outlook suggest the turnaround may have stalled." FY27 sales now expected to decline high-single-digit % after FY1Q rev missed, hurt notably by mid-20% c-n decline in Greater China. New program "Pace" builds on existing cost-savings plan; $2.5bn savings through 2031.

### Thesis (09/15/26)
- Progress reducing unwanted domestic inventory → room for share gains from new products/partnerships. China, sportswear, Jordan underperforming, need sharper execution. CEO Elliott Hill "Win Now" matters most in China (innovation, sports, fresh inventory). Shorter lead times, tighter wholesale partnerships, strategic pricing, stronger digital engagement → faster demand response, less discounting. CY26 sales could fall low- to mid-single digits; GM begins expanding FY1Q.

### 1Q27 preview (09/15/26)
- Uneven recovery: NA growth partly offsets Greater China weakness, softer EMEA and APLA. Newer launches + wholesale momentum support demand; DTC pressure keeps reset gradual. GM can improve as supply-chain actions offset tariffs; sales need to revert to gains for meaningful turnaround. Watch: China inflection, tariff mitigation detail, order-book visibility, innovation broadening beyond handful of launches, Caitlin Clark signature shoe commentary.
- Cons FY1Q: sales -3%; EMEA -4.3%; APLA -3%; Greater China -13.3%; NA +1.8%. Footwear -3.2%; apparel -2%; equipment -1.5%. Bloomberg Second Measure data suggest sales falling. GM could expand 28 bps (supply-chain actions). Cons EPS 45c; sales trends could improve in FY27.

### Regions / channels (FY4Q26 = qtr ended May 2026)
- CY26 sales may fall low- to mid-single digit; FY27 margin recovery more achievable than sales rebound. FY27 sales may decline low-single-digit % per cons.
- Mix of Nike Brand sales: NA 45%, EMEA 28%, Greater China 13% (vs nearly 20% in 2021), APLA 14%.
- Greater China 4Q c-n rev -17%: Nike Direct -14%, Digital -25%, owned stores -9%, wholesale -19%. House of Innovation Shanghai sales up double digits. Sales rising high-single digits where Nike invests (must-win doors). Tmall, Shenzhen innovation center, new ERP, localized fits. Nike #1 footwear share in China, #3 apparel. China high-school bball league, Jordan The One tournament.
- GM 4Q 49.2%, +890 bps, aided by 900-bp IEEPA tariff-recovery benefit; ex-benefit 40.2%, -10 bps. Mgmt: revenue growth moderates over next 3 qtrs (tighter purchases, reduced sell-in); GM expansion starts FY1Q. Incremental tariff rates 10-15% partial offset. Tariffs drag; improving order books and one-time levy refunds may help.
- NA 4Q c-n +3%: wholesale +10%, Nike Digital -5%, owned stores -7%. Cons low-single-digit NA growth FY27. Women's apparel bigger opportunity (underrepresented vs men's).
- EMEA 4Q rev -6%: Direct -16%, Digital -24%, owned stores -9%, wholesale -1%. Running, global football, golf each double-digit growth. Off-price sales fell >50% after cut promotions; full-price realization +15 pp. Middle East disruption. Partners JD Sports, Sports Direct, Snipes.
- APLA 4Q rev -1%: DTC -3%, digital -8%, owned stores +2%, wholesale +1%. Running and global football double-digit; tennis, ACG high-single digit. Japan and LatAm returned to growth in FY3Q; APAC weaker. Digital partners Flipkart, Myntra, Zozotown; new digital partners in 5 SE Asian countries.
- Wholesale 4Q +1% global; NA +10%; China -19%; growing global order book spring/summer. 2027 wholesale may dip slightly after rising in 2026.
- DKS-READ: "an elevated women's fitness concept at Dick's Sporting Goods could draw more female shoppers" — part of Nike wholesale revamp alongside Foot Locker multibrand basketball concept HomeCourt. Wholesale share >half of NKE revenue; NA wholesale +10% in FY4Q26.
- Digital: digital sales -12% in 4Q; half of channel had been promotional, returning to premium; resumed Amazon.com after 6-yr absence. Sensor Tower: Nike app and SNKRS downloads/active users declined; June SNKRS strength aided by WC.
- Stores: refreshed >150 owned stores with sport-led experiences and >15,000 wholesale spaces globally; will rezone fleet, close misaligned doors. Jordan World of Flight; SNKRS in-store pickup.
