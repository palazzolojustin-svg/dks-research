### Thesis map
- Can Nike deliver +HSD% sales growth? Not next 2 yrs; maybe by FY30E. Will probably lose share as it resets; leaned too much into lifestyle/fashion, rebalancing to athletic performance takes years → may lose lifestyle share. Rev FY27 -3%, FY28 +3%, FY29 +4%.
- EBIT margin: hover ~HSD% long term; performance reset needs significant SG&A investment; mix shift to performance and wholesale → margin headwinds NTM; "There is no easy way for Nike's EBIT margin to rebound"; needs strong sales growth to releverage.
- Athleisure: +MSD% global CAGR; developing markets disproportionate.
- Neutral: major reset limits upward revisions, weighs on P/E; brand/balance sheet can ultimately return to +MSD% sales and 10% EBIT margin long term. ~3.5% 5-yr EPS CAGR FY26-FY31E; justifies 21x P/E; balanced skew. Evidence Lab: Nike #1 global mindshare, significant room to reduce promotions. Reverse DCF: market assumes L-MSD% annual sales growth post FY27E and ~10-40 bps annual EBIT margin expansion.

- Rev $11.19B, -4.5% y/y, -1.7% 2-yr; FX neutral. Performance momentum persists (Global Football / summer '26 World Cup). Mgmt reducing inventory buys → lower future sell-in; mgmt: 2H of 4Q26 saw weaker traffic and sell-through across direct and wholesale sell-through. Ongoing sportswear and Jordan pressure. EMEA: elevated promos, Middle East disruption, sportswear softness. Greater China: more aggressive sell-in management → pronounced topline hit.
- North America: -0.1% y/y reported; FX 20 bps headwind; $5.015B, 44.8% of rev. NA DTC pressured (innovation/brand heat, esp. lifestyle). Web traffic: US visits +37% avg Q1, Canada +31% (sequential improvement; 4Q US +9%, Canada -16%). US Google searches +31% y/y vs +61% in 1Q (decel). Secondary price premiums falling for Nike/Jordan; Converse highest price premium as of Aug 2026. DTC ~-MSD% as Nike pulls back core franchises esp. Dunks mainly in DTC.
- EMEA: -5.4% reported; FX ~70 bps headwind; $3.15B, 28.1%. Web traffic (UK, FR, DE, ES, IT) -9% avg (declines notably Germany, Spain; UK +4%) vs -13% in Q4. EU Google searches -4% avg Q1 (France +0.5%, Germany -8.2%, UK -5.0%), same as -4% in Q4. Evidence Lab Europe spend → EMEA DTC reported ~-LDD%. Losing wholesale share esp. lifestyle.
- EU specialty retail check (senior leader, large European specialty retailer): Nike likely losing share; turnaround slower than expected; challenges beyond typical product cycle; running gains insufficient to offset lifestyle weakness; losing share to adidas, Hoka, On, Salomon esp. younger consumers; execution concerns (innovation, marketing, supply chain); "Nike has become one of the most promotional brands in the market"; broader distribution into channels Nike historically avoided → exclusivity/brand concerns; limited evidence of near-term inflection.
- Greater China: -14.0% reported; FX ~550 bps tailwind; $1.30B, 11.6%. Tmall Nike -17% y/y Aug (rolling 3M) vs -1% in May; Tmall correlates more with DTC than wholesale. WeChat reads/post and Wows/post declining y/y, low end of peers. Nike keeps price premium on Dewu vs Li Ning, Anta.
- China athletic wear expert: Nike and Jordan weakest major global sportswear brands in China recently; trends deteriorated through summer, -DD% range, further decel into Sept MTD; largely idiosyncratic; less performance innovation y/y; reduced distributor participation in e-commerce, scaled back promo support, pricing integrity focus → near-term pressure; inventory somewhat elevated but improved Jul→Aug; recovery depends on innovation, cleaner inventory, revised distribution execution.

Dunks; channel mix headwind (wholesale > Direct); more value-channel sales to clear excess inventory (Greater China, Converse); product mix headwind toward performance esp. running.

### Guidance outlook (UBS expectations for 1Q27 call)
- 2Q27 implied EPS $0.31–$0.43 (midpoint 30% below Street $0.53).
- Sales: guide -5-7% y/y, FX ~neutral; managing lifecycle of top franchises esp. Dunks (inventory pullback); lapping elevated promo clearance under Hill's "Win Now"; wholesale growth from expanded distribution (esp. NA) partly offsets DTC declines; Direct pressured (fewer promos on Nike sites as Digital repositions full-price; lapping 2Q26 clearance; soft EMEA, China, Converse; classics reductions). -5-7% below -LSD-MSD% guide for balance of CY26 (4Q26-2Q27); market expects -MSD%.
- GM: flat to +30 bps y/y; lapping higher tariffs (incremental 15% post July 2026 vs IEEPA blended 19% in 1H26); mgmt expects y/y GM expansion beginning 1Q27; headwinds channel mix, product mix, higher product costs, freight/oil surcharges; Nike likes beatable forward guides → extra caution.
- SG&A: flat to +LSD% (marketing investment vs annualizing headcount cuts). Reuters: 775 US jobs cut Jan 2026 + Converse reductions; CNBC: 1,400 jobs cut Apr 2026 mainly tech. Tax low-20%s (Q1 guide low-20%; 2Q26 21%).
- FY27: mgmt previously said FY27 + LT guidance at Fall investor day (November 2026); UBS sees risk of 2H27 commentary now given deterioration and decision (company reports/CNBC) to cut >1,000 third-party digital distributors/online vendors in China from January 2027. Rationale: get bad news out before analyst day. Guidance likely disappoints: slower global DTC, Greater China wholesale distribution changes, higher marketplace inventory and promos, oil-related input costs; CFO transition → prudence. Investors ask whether new CFO David Denton announces new savings initiative; UBS expects any such at investor day, not 2Q report.
