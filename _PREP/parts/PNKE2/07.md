## SRC 04_PEERS/NKE/2026-09-22_BloombergIntel_NKE_Nike_Can_Keep_Winning_Despite_Headwinds_-11_Billion_of_Debt.md | 2026-09-22 | Bloomberg Intelligence credit drivers / Mike Campellone | NKE FY26 (FYE May 31 2026)
- Thesis: despite pressured FY26, robust credit metrics and liquidity let Nike withstand near-term headwinds, keep global sportswear lead. Progress clearing wholesale inventory, launching new products; lingering tariff costs, consumer uncertainty.

### Balance sheet / liquidity
- Debt well above pre-pandemic but metrics solid vs single-A retail peers. Cash + ST investments $9bn > $8bn funded debt at May 31 → funded-debt net cash before $3.1bn operating-lease liabilities. Two committed facilities totaling $3bn and $3bn CP program undrawn.
- Maturities: $1bn Nov 2026; $1bn Mar 2027; $1.5bn 2030. Not disclosed whether 2026-27 maturities repaid or refinanced.
- Peers: Adidas adj net leverage 1.6x at 2Q (target <2x); Ralph Lauren net leverage usually <1x; Target 1.4x end FY2Q26.
- Ratings A2/A+ (Moody's/S&P).

### Cash flow FY26
- OCF $2.87bn vs $3.70bn; working capital used $1.68bn incl. $684mn tariff receivable (substantially collected after year-end); A/R consumed $1.21bn. FCF ~$2.18bn after $684mn capex. Dividends $2.41bn; buybacks $123mn; none in 4Q. Dividend exceeded FCF; tariff collections + $9bn liquidity limit funding pressure. Return to large buybacks before 2026-27 maturities addressed would weaken flexibility.
- Knight family ownership ~20% (BI calc), largely Class A; significant reduction could shift financial policy.

### Market position / mix
- Global sportswear share 2025: Nike 14% vs Adidas 8.7%. Sports footwear: Nike 23% vs Adidas 12%; sports apparel 7.9% vs 6.3%.
- FY26 Nike Brand rev: NA ~45%, EMEA 28%, APLA 14%, Greater China 13%; wholesale ~61%, Nike Direct 39%. Wholesale +4% c-n; Direct -8% (Digital -12%, stores -4%). Performance categories gaining; mgmt expects Sportswear and Jordan Streetwear negative in FY27 before improving in 2H. "Brand scale preserves recovery capacity, and full-price sell through determines when it converts to earnings."
- Nike higher inventory turnover and lower cash-conversion cycle than Adidas. EMEA inventory up low double digits; Greater China inventory down double digits (reduced sell-in, promotions). FY27 cash conversion should improve on receivable collection.
- DKS-READ: none explicit; NKE wholesale (~61% of brand rev, +4% c-n FY26) is the channel DKS sits in.
