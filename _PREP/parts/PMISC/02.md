### Sector feedback
- Met broad set of investors over 2 weeks; sector sentiment cautious; consensus that slower Nike (Boss) recovery extends window for adidas + On share gains (solid brand momentum). Key pushback = duration: both trade at meaningful discounts on lack of conviction in sustaining strong growth in 2027/mid-term. JPM: multi-year growth stories (brand building, innovation, regional opps); H2 26/2027 margin uplift as FX + tariff headwinds fade and op leverage kicks in.
### adidas
- Pushback: investors expect strong Q2 (JPMe 14% Q2 cFX rev growth incl c.6 pts World Cup jersey demand; buyside "bar" slightly higher than JPMe). Concern: market looks through Q2 as "one-off", tougher 2027 comps; growth may slow from Q3 as footwear decelerates esp. if Terrace volume scaled down; which product engines beyond 2026, particularly if apparel not sustained driver; Investor Day expectations.
- JPM view: more than Q2 story; model 8%+ topline growth in H2 26 and FY27. Lifestyle: underappreciated (1) longer runway of current heroes, esp. Samba (search trends extended life span since peak April 2024), (2) broader portfolio of smaller growing franchises; expect Samba playbook extended to Stan Smith (refreshed designs, materials, collabs) into H2 26/FY27. Performance: running led, halo from sub-2 marathon successes; football, training, basketball opps. Regions: big upside outside Europe ("local for local"). World Cup 2026: broadened assortment beyond jerseys ("Lifestyle collection"/"Blokecore"/"Jerseys redefined" under "FIFA World Cup 26" pages on adidas sites in Europe + NA); upside = converting traffic into sustainable football-inspired lifestyle demand; "Blokecore" Google searches re-accelerated in 2026. Expect Q2 + Investor Day to reassure brand appeal / escape boom-bust.
### On
- Pushback: multiple can't re-rate while revenue growth decelerates (cite Lululemon, Hoka/Deckers de-rating after weaker NA growth). Q2 focus: sensitivity to topline softness after mgmt flagged slower wholesale sell-in in Q2 ahead of major Q3 product launches. Want clearer growth framework (categories, geographies, channels). Recurring fear: Sept Investor Day guides 3-yr sales CAGR below sell-side c.20%. Q whether margin targets pressured if On invests in new sports (e.g. football). "top-line miss with a profit beat" unlikely to change sentiment.
- JPM view: at 16x P/E deceleration concerns overdone; topline 20%+ over next 3 yrs driven by Asia (CAGR c.35%) and apparel (c.40%) vs fears of mid-teens or lower. Market underappreciates brand building/premium lifestyle ("feel-good" demand); Ralph Lauren analogy; On hired Alice Delahunt (ex-Ralph Lauren/Burberry) to lead global DTC. Margin upside under-discussed: JPM Q2 and FY26 adj EBITDA 9% and 7% above cons; GM near-term, op leverage medium-term.
### Puma
- Polarized. Bulls: multi-year reset/turnaround ("Could Puma be the next adidas?"), Greater China upside via Anta 29% stake; near-term catalyst = gdnc upgrade or smaller-than-feared EBIT loss. Bears: soft channel reads, limited visibility on newness driving topline inflection, execution risk.
- JPM: multi-year turnaround; wait for Anta (Qian Yao) strategy before modelling China upside; Amer (Boss) not comparable (niche premium vs mass-market brand needing rebuild); success not guaranteed. Q2 model -9% topline, -€61m EBIT. Neutral pending M&A updates/turnaround signs.
### JD Sports
- Investors: valuation attractive but limited near-term re-rating catalysts esp. if Nike recovery slower; positives strong FCF, buybacks, execution/productivity focus; challenging if macro soft + core customers pressured; exposure to Nike lifestyle franchises still in transition may constrain topline acceleration. DKS-READ: same Nike lifestyle-franchise transition headwind relevant to Foot Locker banner.
- JPM: =same as 01 JD view (self-help vs headwinds; Q1-27 soft, weaker than US peers; expectations reset; valuation undemanding; Neutral).
