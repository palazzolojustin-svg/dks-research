### 4. Store base / new-store economics
- 322 stores at Jan 31 2026 in 21 contiguous states; all leased except one owned; none mall-connected. 327 at Aug 1 2026.
- Size ~40,000-130,000 gross sq ft; avg ~70,000; ~85% selling; total ~21.9M sq ft (≈68k/store).
- Leases initial 15-20 yrs; operating lease obligations $2.37B at 1/31/26 ($266M in FY26).
- States: TX 117 (36%), GA 22, FL 21, LA 18, NC 18, AL 17, TN 15, OK 13, MO 12, SC/AR/MS/IN 10 each, KS 6, KY 6, VA 5, IL/WV/OH 3 each, PA 2, MD 1.
- Openings FY23 14; FY24 16; FY25 24 (Q1 5, Q2 3, Q3 11, Q4 5); FY26 YTD 5 (Q1 2, Q2 3); zero closures/relocations. FY2026 plan 20-25 new stores.
- New-store program relaunched 2022; 63 opened FY2021-FY2025 (68 through Q2 FY26); off-mall power centers/stand-alone. 39 stores open ≥12 months "averaged approximately $13 million in net sales per store, including e-commerce" LTM (44 stores at Q1 FY26, 47 at Q2 FY26, same ~$13M); year-one performance "partially affected by the season in which the new store opens and the brand awareness in the region".
- New-store contribution: FY25 24 stores $142.8M; Q3 FY25 $48.6M; Q1 FY26 21 stores $54.5M; Q2 FY26 $61.5M; H1 FY26 $108.9M.
- Chain avg sales/store ≈$19.5M (derived); new stores ~$13M ≈ 2/3. New-store capex ≈$5.0M/store (derived: $119.9M/24); 60% of FY26 capex budget ($200-240M) to new stores. Pre-opening ≈$0.6M/store.
- 3 DCs: Katy TX 1.4M sq ft; Twiggs County GA 1.6M; Cookeville TN 1.6M; each ~150 stores (≈450 capacity).
- Stores facilitate ~95% of sales incl BOPIS/ship-from-store; BOPIS counted in e-comm comps. Team members ~23,000 at 1/31/26 (45% FT/55% PT); ~22,000 Q1/Q2 FY26; ~24,000 Q3 FY25.
### 5. Merchandising/brands/sourcing
- National brands ≈78% of FY25 merch sales; private label (19 brands incl Magellan Outdoors, BCG, Academy, O'rageous, Game Winner, Outdoor Gourmet, Freely, Redfield, H2OX, R.O.W.) ≈22%; ~53% of customers bought a private brand FY25.
- No brand >~12% of sales; largest vendor ≈12% of inventory purchases; ~1,500 vendors. Key brands: Nike (incl Jordan Brand launched Q1 FY25, $7.5M SG&A), Under Armour, adidas, Winchester, Columbia, The North Face, Brooks, Skechers, Yeti, Stanley, Carhartt.
- Sourcing: China, Bangladesh, Vietnam, Cambodia, Brazil (incl much private label); Hong Kong sourcing office. Tariff mitigation: purchase timing/pull-forward, pricing/promo, country-of-origin diversification, vendor negotiations.
- Firearms ≈6% (licensed dealer; ammo, suppressors). Customer "active families", 2-3 visits/yr; myAcademy Rewards launched July 2024; Academy Credit Card.
- Seasonality: Q2 largest profit quarter (Memorial Day/Father's Day/July 4); BTS Q2/Q3; holiday + cold-weather Q4. 16 categories in 4 divisions: Outdoors = camping, fishing, hunting; S&R = fitness, team sports, recreation (patio, wheeled goods, trampolines), front end; Apparel = outdoor/seasonal, youth/swim, athletic, licensed; Footwear = casual/seasonal, work, youth, athletic, team sports.
