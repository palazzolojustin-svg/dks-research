### Thesis / summary
- Assesses growth outlook, execution risk, risk/return asymmetry using data, co discussions, own channel checks + expert calls. Expect slower Nike comeback (Nike covered by Matthew R. Boss; post Nike Q4 results that week) extends window for current share winners.
- Q1 momentum: adidas +14% topline, +16% EBIT; On +26% topline, +45% adj EBITDA. Expect continuation.
- 12-mo share moves: adidas -13%, On -32%; 2Y fwd P/E adidas 14x, On 16x; concern = "nearing the end of their high growth phase".
- Two camps: "Consistent-Delivery" (adidas, On: consistent beats, multiples de-rated; investors demand more beat-and-raise quarters) vs "Reset & Rebuild" (Puma, JD Sports: lower earnings bar after resets; multiples positively re-rated in recent months as investors regain confidence in shareholder-return potential). Consistent-Delivery underperformed Reset & Rebuild over last 12 mo (Fig 3, rebased 30-06-2025). Fig 4: Reset & Rebuild camp's 2Y fwd cons EPS bar lowered; similar/higher for Consistent-Delivery. Fig 5: Consistent-Delivery (notably On) lower P/E. Fig 6: Puma 2Y fwd P/E large swing (chart up to ~1000% rebased) as "E" expectation evolves.
- Brand heat solid for adidas + On per Google search trends, web traffic, social media engagement. Both have investor days in Sept 2026 (expect product innovation/newness focus; next leg of growth).
- Model 18% EPS CAGR adidas FY25-FY30; 32% adj EPS CAGR On. End-market growth 2-3% (as described by JD Sports) → "such growth profiles are top quality".
- Channel checks: consumer preference for performance + comfort remains; sneaker hype not returned; lifestyle running strength to stay. Google search/social/web activity broadly similar to 6 mo ago.
### H2 26 outlook
- JD Sports earlier in 2026 flagged "muted" market in 2026 with 0-1% overall demand growth for sporting goods & athleisure (consumers cautious/selective amid inflation). JPM expects H2 26 similar to H1; promotional environment (widely flagged by brands + retailers) continuing into Q3.
- Supports: global supply chain recovery, falling oil prices after Middle East progress, better consumer sentiment, ongoing World Cup. Slower Nike comeback extends window for adidas, On, Puma to gain/recover share; competition intense.
- Freight: spot rates (WCI Composite 40ft) more than doubled over last 6 mo after geopolitical disruptions; still 30% below July 2024 peak and 60% below 2021 peak; ME progress should ease rate inflation as brands negotiate new contracts toward mid-year.
- Softer USD (USD/EUR, USD/CHF) = GM tailwind into H2 26 and 2027; adidas flagged better FX hedges benefit margins into late 2026, more in 2027.
### Q2 preview (sector)
- Q2 challenging; promo pressure headwind partly offset by World Cup. Europe most affected by Middle East conflicts (channel checks + expert calls); declining traffic + slowing spend since March. PVH (Boss) cited "a big slowdown" in April after ME conflict, knock-on in Turkey (reduced tourism, macro), EMEA slowdown as inflation expectations rose.
- US: demand more resilient (rising equity market, re-accelerating labour market, higher tax refunds) offset by rising inflation; lower-income cohort more pressured by rising gas prices. DKS-READ: "This trend was echoed in recent reporting of the likes of DICK'S Sporting Goods and Academy Sports (both covered by Christopher Horvers)."
- China: retail sales data + Topsports/Pou Sheng reporting = weak overall demand, but growth varies widely by co; expect On + adidas strong momentum to continue in China, outperforming.
- Profitability: more visible promo pressure Q2 vs Q1 (varies by co); some FX GM tailwind from weaker USD; lower tariff pressure vs Q1. Most cos have not included tariff refunds in gdnc; may change → potential tailwind to FY25 P&L (Nike recently added tariff refunds in results & gdnc).

### adidas (OW, PT €230 Dec-27; price €180.00 01 Jul 26; analyst block Chiara Battistini; title "Broader, Healthier, Still Delivering, Remain OW")
- Key changes (FYE Dec) prev→cur: Rev 26E €26,654mn→26,946 (+1.1%); Rev 27E 28,976→29,334 (+1.2%); adj EPS 26E €9.73→9.89 (+1.6%); adj EBIT 26E 2,495→2,536 (+1.7%); adj EBIT 27E 2,967→2,989 (+0.8%).
- Broader/healthier growth drivers: (1) longer Samba life cycle, (2) broader newness across adidas Originals, (3) rapidly growing running, (4) multiple non-European growth opps. Q1: 22% DTC growth + 51% GM → healthy demand, disciplined pricing (rare amid promo environment).
- Q2: extra 6% boost from World Cup → mid-teens topline (JPMe +14%; model 14.2% cFX vs cons +12.4%); World Cup-related sales 6% of topline in Q2 vs 4% in Q1 (premium jersey sales via DTC). FY26 DD cFX growth (JPMe 11%, cons 10%); EBIT margin 9.4% FY26 (disciplined pricing/sell-in, eased FX + tariff, op leverage). "one of the few businesses in the sector that is still on track to deliver double digit topline growth and strong margin uplift."
- Samba lifespan longer than expected, already longer than Superstar/Stan Smith peaks a decade ago (2014-18 comparison, Google Trends). Newness (Samba Jane, adidas Japan, Superstar, Stan Smith, adistar, Adidas Taekwondo) to offset Terrace (ex-Samba) slowdown; scaling down Gazelle and Campus. Evo SL search interest now slightly surpassing Hoka Bondi + On Cloudmonster worldwide; Hyperboost (new) insignificant so far; US trend similar shape but Hoka still dominates.
- Regions: Europe 32% of mix; Asia + LatAm jointly >40% of net sales. Upside: locally empowered teams converting Nike weakness into durable share gains in Greater China, Japan/Korea, SE Asia, India, LatAm (more white space). HSD topline sustainable if momentum there holds even if Europe normalises + NA gradual recovery.
- China = validation of "Global Brand, Local Mindset"; e.g. viral "Urban Errand" campaign in China (translation mistake turned into marketing; case study by Qian Yao). Upside if replicated in North America.
- Valuation: EPS ests up over 12 mo while multiples fell; room to re-rate with more delivery; 14x P/E.
- World Cup: CEO Bjorn Gulden told WELT of "a business between one and 1.5 billion euros" (vs prior ~€1bn indication on calls); German jersey sold 3x as much as 4 years ago; football-inspired lifestyle promising. Mgmt: WC impact not fully "incremental" — about assortment for shelf space. Investor Q: tough 2027 comps; upside if football-lifestyle drives multi-year opps.
- Q2 regional model: LatAm +32%, Japan/S.Korea +20%, Greater China +20%, North America +15%, Europe +8% (WC offsets weaker macro demand). Q2 GM 51.3% (-40bps y/y; FX + tariff headwinds offset by DTC + apparel mix); Q2 EBIT €623m, 9.3% margin. FY26 sales +11% cFX to €26.9bn; EBIT €2.5bn, 2% ahead of cons.
- Alt data: SimilarWeb visits (>1 page) solid ex seasonal volatility (~9-19mn range); Reddit (Sprinklr) discussion volume broadly stable (~20-40k) with rising % positive reviews (Jan-23 to May-26).
