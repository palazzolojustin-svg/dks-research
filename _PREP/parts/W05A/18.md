- Bob Eddy = Chairman & CEO of BJ's Wholesale Club. BJ's: President & CEO since Apr 2021; Chairman since Jun 2023; BJ's board since 2021; joined BJ's 2007 as SVP Finance; EVP & CFO 2011; EVP, Chief Financial & Administrative Officer 2018. Prior: PwC audit & business advisory (retail/consumer products), Boston & San Francisco.
- Other roles: Board + Executive Committee of National Retail Federation; Board of Trustees Boston Children's Hospital; former director Troon Golf; College Advisory Board Babson College (alma mater).
- DKS Board expands to 12 directors.
- "Bob's decades of retail experience will be invaluable as DICK'S expands our newest store concepts, and as we continue to find ways to innovate and provide the best possible experience for our athletes." —Ed Stack, Executive Chairman
- "DICK'S is a true leader in its space, and I am pleased to join the Board as the company is reimagining retail and thinking of new ways to interact with its athletes... DICK'S has a unique opportunity to further shape the future of both retail and sports" —Bob Eddy
