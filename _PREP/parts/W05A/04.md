### Context
- 3.25% Convertible Senior Notes due 2025 ("2025 Notes"), original aggregate principal $575M; issued with convertible note hedge (call options) + warrants (call spread).
### Item 1.01 – partial unwind (2022-12-07)
- Note Hedge Partial Early Termination Agreement + Warrant Partial Early Termination Agreement with a "Hedge Counterparty" covering call options/warrants corresponding to portion of Exchanged Notes / underlying shares. Counterparty delivers DKS common shares, number based on VWAP over averaging period starting 2022-12-08. Forms filed as Ex 10.1, 10.2.
- Financial advisor on call-spread terminations and exchanges: HudsonWest LLC (independent equity derivatives/convertible advisory firm).
### Item 1.02 – complete unwind with another counterparty (2022-12-07)
- Note Hedge Early Termination Agreement + Warrant Early Termination Agreement with "Early Termination Hedge Counterparty"; counterparty delivers shares, VWAP-based over averaging period from 2022-12-08. No early termination penalties incurred. Forms = Ex 10.3, 10.4.
### Item 3.02 – exchange agreements (2022-12-07)
- Noteholders agreed to exchange $95,307,000 principal of 2025 Notes for cash + DKS common shares (share count VWAP-based, averaging from 2022-12-08). Shares unregistered, Sec 4(a)(2) exemption. Form = Ex 10.5.
- Exchanged notes ≈61.7% of outstanding principal; after exchange ≈$59M principal remains; annual interest payments reduced ≈$3.1M.
- Signed 2022-12-08 by John E. Hayes III, SVP – GC & Corporate Secretary.
