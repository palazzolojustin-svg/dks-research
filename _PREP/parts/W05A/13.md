### Headline
- Title: "Reports First Quarter Results; Reaffirms 2023 Outlook – Delivers 5.3% Growth in First Quarter Net Sales – Delivers Strong Double-Digit EBT Margin of 11.6%".
- Q1 comp +3.4%, driven by +2.7% transactions + higher avg ticket.
- EPS $3.40 vs $2.47 GAAP / $2.85 non-GAAP prior yr.
- Retired remaining $59M converts principal; fully settled all amounts under Convertible Senior Notes.
- Reaffirmed FY23 (53-wk) EPS $12.90–13.80 incl ≈$0.20 53rd wk; comp flat to +2.0%.
### Quotes
- "Our strong start to 2023 demonstrates the sustained strength of our business. We are very enthusiastic about our strategies and continue to invest in our future to fuel long-term growth opportunities, including a return to square footage growth." —Ed Stack, Executive Chairman
- "We are very pleased with our first quarter results. Even as consumers face macroeconomic uncertainties, our athletes have continued to prioritize sport and rely on DICK'S to meet their needs, and we continue to gain market share. Our Q1 sales grew 5.3%, driven by strong comps and healthy transaction growth, and we delivered another strong double-digit EBT margin. We remain confident in our ability to drive sales and profitability growth in 2023 and over the long term." —Lauren Hobart, President & CEO
