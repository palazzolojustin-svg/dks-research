### Headline
- Title: "Record Third Quarter Sales; Delivers 6.5% Increase in Comparable Store Sales and Raises Full Year Guidance".
- Q3 comp +6.5% on top of +12.8% Q3'21, +23.2% Q3'20, +6.0% Q3'19.
- Net sales $3.0B, +7.7% vs Q3'21, +50.8% vs Q3'19.
- EPS $2.45; non-GAAP EPS $2.60; pre-tax income 10.3% of sales = >2.5x Q3'19 GAAP rate and >3x Q3'19 non-GAAP rate.
- FY22 comp gdnc raised to -3.0% to -1.5% (prior -6.0% to -2.0%); FY22 EPS gdnc raised to $10.50–11.10 (prior $8.85–10.55); non-GAAP EPS $11.50–12.10 (prior $10.00–12.00).
### Quotes
