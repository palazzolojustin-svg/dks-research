### CEO letter (Lauren R. Hobart, President & CEO)
- 2022 "continued to build momentum and delivered another year of strong performance"; results "underscore the value of our long-term business strategies"; athletes "continue to prioritize sport", "increasingly rely on DICK'S".
- 2022 highlights (incl. 3-yr trajectory): record sales $12.4B, +41% vs 2019; "continued to gain market share at an accelerating pace, with considerable growth in our largest and most important categories"; acquired 7 million new athletes; record ScoreCard Gold (top-tier loyalty) members, Gold = >40% of total sales; EBT margin >11%, more than double 2019 rate; diluted EPS $10.78, non-GAAP diluted EPS $12.04, "more than three times higher than 2019".
- People: >50,000 teammates (stores, DCs, HQ); Teammate Relief Fund distributed ~$500 thousand to >350 teammates in 2022; recognized by national media/industry experts as great place to work; latest engagement survey: >80% of teammates rate DKS fun place to work (key engagement driver).
- 75th anniversary (2023): founded 1948 by Dick Stack (bait & tackle shop; $300 from grandmother's cookie jar); 2nd location later; 1984 son Ed Stack + siblings bought business; grew from 2 stores in upstate NY to nation's preeminent sporting goods retailer; Ed continues as Executive Chairman.
- Foundation: 2019 goal give 1 million kids chance to play by 2024 → surpassed Feb 2022 (2 yrs early); new goal impact 2 million youth athletes by 2024 ("well on our way"); 75th anniversary: 75 grants × $75,000 to youth sports orgs → >$5.6 million donated to underfunded communities in 2023.
- Sustainability (Purpose Playbook, 4 pillars): progress in 2022 toward GHG emissions -30% by 2030; inclusivity pledge $300 million annual spend with diverse suppliers by 2025.
- Capital return: since 2021 returned nearly $2.4B; 2023 more than doubled quarterly dividend = annualized $4.00/sh; "underscores our confidence in the structurally higher sales and earnings profile of our business".
- "Since 2017, we have methodically transformed virtually every aspect of our business"; positioned "not only to maintain our leadership but to extend it within our large and highly fragmented industry"; 2023: continue current strategies, build on 2022 which "set a new bar for us in both sales and profitability".
- Merchandising: industry-leading differentiated/on-trend assortment; key brand partners value DKS rooted in sport + ability to showcase complete brand portfolios; developing new/emerging brands + "powerhouse vertical brands".
- Stores: significant training investments; tech-enabled experiences (HitTrax batting cages, TrackMan golf simulators, premium full-service footwear decks); stores hub of omnichannel, enabled >90% of total 2022 sales incl. thriving in-store pickup & curbside.
- Data: "most extensive youth sports dataset in our industry" + digital/data science = key strategic advantage; personalized marketing. GameChanger = premier youth sports scoring/stats app in multibillion-dollar youth sports tech market.
- Specialty concepts: Public Lands, Golf Galaxy Performance Center; recently acquired outdoor retailer Moosejaw (multibillion-dollar outdoor category).
- House of Sport (launched 2021): 3 locations, all exceeded initial performance expectations; plan ~20 additional over next 2 yrs; by 2027 est. 75–100 HoS locations nationwide; "a significant part of our future growth story".
- Largest US sporting goods retailer; "well-positioned to extend our lead and continue gaining share in a vibrant $140 billion industry".
