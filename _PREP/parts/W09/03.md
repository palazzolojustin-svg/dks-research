### CEO letter (Lauren R. Hobart, President & CEO)
- Pillars: compelling omnichannel athlete experience, differentiated product assortment, deep engagement with brand and teammates, great place to work. 2024 = "another year of extraordinary business growth that included record financial results and steady market share gains"; "we enter 2025 positioned to build on our progress".
- 2024 highlights: record sales $13.4B, +3.5% vs 2023; diluted EPS $14.05, +15% vs 2023 GAAP and +9% vs 2023 non-GAAP; FY comp +5.2% "fueled by growth in both average ticket and number of transactions"; gained ~50 bps market share vs 2023, driven by improved performance in priority product categories; >25 million active ScoreCard members = ~75% of sales; opened 7 House of Sport and 15 Field House locations in 2024. >50,000 teammates.
- 3 key 2025 growth areas: (1) repositioning real estate/store portfolio; (2) footwear; (3) accelerating eCommerce.
- (1) House of Sport: elevated assortment + experiences (multi-sport cages for baseball, softball, soccer, lacrosse; golf simulators; rock climbing walls; fields; ice rinks) + services (baseball glove steaming, lacrosse stick restringing, bike repair); since first HoS 2021 "exceptional financial results"; community hub/events; showcases brands, strengthens partnerships, opens new ones; "commanding unprecedented landlord interest, positioning us to be an anchor in some of the most successful retail centers in the country". 2025 plan ~16 new HoS; 75–100 HoS by YE2027. Field House = HoS learnings applied to standard 50,000 sq ft DKS store (elevated assortment/service, premium experiences, bold visuals), "delivering very strong results"; 26 at YE2024, ~18 more in 2025. "this one-two punch of House of Sport and Field House is the future of our DICK'S stores."
- (2) Footwear: premium footwear decks added in ~90% of DKS locations over past decade; brands give premium product access → loyalty + robust sales growth; "significant opportunity to further grow"; strategic marketing investments, more footwear focus in-store/digital, partnering with elite brands, athletes and celebrities.
- (3) eCommerce = "multibillion-dollar" business; "aggressively pursuing" via functionality/design + brand-awareness marketing; DICK'S app enhancements (increasing interest/engagement); in-store tech to find/deliver product faster.
- Digital businesses: GameChanger (live streaming, scheduling, scorekeeping) — 2024 ~9 million unique active users, >$100 million revenue; "targeting it to deliver revenue of approximately $150 million in 2025". DICK'S Media Network — "still in the early stages of scaling and optimizing"; "strong initial interest"; potential to drive long-term sales growth and gross margin expansion.
- Tone: "We move forward with these growth initiatives in a dynamic macroenvironment, and we will continue to balance our growth with appropriate caution."; confident operational strength, brand relationships, healthy balance sheet, athlete engagement position DKS to navigate environment.
- Capital return: ~$2.2 billion returned over past 3 yrs (dividends + opportunistic buybacks); 2025 quarterly dividend +10% = 11th consecutive year of increases; new 5-yr buyback program up to $3 billion; increased rate of business reinvestment during 2024.
- Industry: sports industry "poised for incredible growth through 2030 and beyond" — women's sports popularity, US hosting Soccer World Cup matches, 2028 LA Olympics, 2031 Rugby World Cup; "convergence of sport and culture in the United States has never been stronger". DKS = largest omnichannel sports retailer in US, "commanding nearly 9% of our $140 billion industry".
