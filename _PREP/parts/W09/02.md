### CEO letter (Lauren R. Hobart, President & CEO)
- Strategic pillars: athlete experience, differentiated product, marketing storytelling/brand connection, culture enabling teammates; "during this past year, it's evident just how far we've extended our leadership position"; athletes prioritize sports/healthy active lifestyles, increasingly look to DKS.
- 2023 highlights: record sales "approximately $13 billion"; continued market share gains, considerable growth in largest/most important categories; added nearly 7 million new athletes, record highs in active athlete database; FY comp +2.4% "driven by growth in transactions"; diluted EPS $12.18, non-GAAP diluted EPS $12.91.
- 55,000 teammates (stores, DCs, corporate HQ, GameChanger).
- Omnichannel: stores = hub + >800 forward points of distribution for omnichannel fulfillment; stores enabled >90% of total 2023 sales.
- House of Sport origin: Ed Stack challenged team to create the store that, "if put across the street from a current DICK'S store could put that DICK'S store out of business"; since 1st location 2021 "very strong engagement" + "powerful financial results"; vs typical DKS store athletes travel farther, spend more time in store, visit more frequently; national brands give access to unique/expanded assortments; new/emerging brands see platform for growth.
- HoS unit economics (gdnc): "For a new House of Sport, in year one, we expect approximately $35 million in omnichannel sales and very strong profitability, with a target of approximately 20% EBITDA margins." 20 HoS stores at end of 2024; 75–100 HoS by end of 2027.
- Next-gen 50,000 sq ft prototype (inspired by HoS; "completely revolutionizes our most typical 50K sq. ft. DICK'S store format"; similar elevated assortment/service model, premium experiences, enhanced visuals): "delivering great results"; 11 opened in 2023, another 16 planned 2024. "This one-two punch of House of Sport and our next generation 50K format is the future of our DICK'S stores."
- Golf Galaxy: footprint >100 locations incl. 14 Golf Galaxy Performance Centers; more performance centers planned 2024.
- Product: brand relationships → industry-leading assortment, "go-to destination for differentiated and on-trend product"; "continue to make big bets with our partners"; seeking new/emerging brands; investing in "highly profitable portfolio of powerhouse vertical brands, including VRST, DSG and CALIA" [names garbled in source as "-*ST"/"CA%IA"], "gaining meaningful traction".
- Brand engagement: storytelling (power of sport, vertical brands, digital offering); data science on athlete database for personalized marketing/loyalty; GameChanger = premier scoring, streaming and statistics app (multibillion-dollar youth sports tech market).
- Teammates "our biggest differentiator"; culture/energy in stores "infectious".
- Youth sports: Sports Matter program + DKS Foundation helped >2 million kids gain access to sport; committed >$190 million (equipment, registration fees, league costs, playing fields); 2023 (75th anniversary) 75 grants × $75,000; 2024 Sports Matter 10-yr anniversary → another $2 million in grants.
- Capital return: returned $1 billion to shareholders during 2023; Q1 2024 declared 10% quarterly dividend increase → expected annualized $4.40/sh; "reflects our deep confidence in our business".
- Outlook 2024: "extremely optimistic"; "We're guiding to another strong year and expect to grow both our sales and earnings through positive comps, higher merchandise margin and productivity gains."
