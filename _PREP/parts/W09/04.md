### CEO letter (Lauren R. Hobart, President & CEO, "DICK'S, Inc.")
- "This past year was a defining chapter for DICK'S, Inc., marked by growth and significant transformation across our business." Foot Locker acquisition = "bold and strategic step forward", "transforming DICK'S into a new global sports retail platform". DKS and FL have distinct brands/strategies/near-term priorities but shared fundamentals (differentiated assortment, athlete/consumer experience, brand engagement, teammates).
- DICK'S Business 2025: record sales $14.11B, +5% vs 2024; FY comp +4.5%; non-GAAP diluted EPS $14.58, +3.8% y/y; >40 bps GM expansion vs 2024; gained nearly 50 bps market share (priority categories); ~30 million active ScoreCard members = >75% of sales. Athletes "willing to invest in differentiated product".
- Ecosystem: expanded HoS and DICK'S Field House (platform for brand innovation; emerging brands "test, learn and grow"; "unprecedented interest from landlords"); "tremendous growth" in eCommerce and app (launches, exclusive access, experiences like MOVE); GameChanger "nearly $150 million in sales in 2025 and close to a 40% sales CAGR since 2017" (multibillion-dollar sports tech market; youth baseball, softball, basketball, soccer); DICK'S Media Network "generated nearly 20 billion impressions in 2025", connecting brand partners with athletes.
- Sport/culture connection "at an all-time high"; "one of the most dynamic periods for sport".
- 2026 outlook DKS Business: "we expect to deliver continued comparable sales growth, strategic expansion of our square footage, and strong profitability for the DICK'S Business. While we remain mindful of macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainty, we are confident in our strategies..."
- Foot Locker Business: acquisition completed Sept 2025; "excitement – and conviction – in the long-term opportunity at Foot Locker continues to grow"; combination = more scale, deeper relationships with most important brands, access to new consumers, global footprint; FL benefits: improved key-brand relationships, stronger retail fundamentals, deeper investment in consumers/teammates. Since Sept: assembled "world-class management team", "strong alignment and renewed engagement from our brand partners", "clean out the garage" inventory clean-up completed → positions FL well for FY2026. "Fast Break" 11-store pilot (clearer storytelling, better presentation, more focused assortment): "Early results have been very encouraging"; plan to expand to ~250 stores by back-to-school. "Back to school is also when we expect an inflection point in both sales and profitability for the Foot Locker Business." Reaffirmed $100 million–$125 million cost synergies over medium term. "We are confident that Foot Locker will reemerge as a stronger, more resilient, and more dynamic business"; goal "return Foot Locker to its rightful place in the industry".
- Investment priorities: DKS — on-trend differentiated assortment, additional store growth, relocations/remodels, technology, supply chain; FL — optimize assortment, reenergize stores, compelling marketing, invest in teammates.
- Capital return: ~$2.4 billion returned over past 3 yrs; 2026 quarterly dividend +3% → expected annualized $5/sh, 12th consecutive year of dividend growth.
- ~100,000 teammates across all banners.
