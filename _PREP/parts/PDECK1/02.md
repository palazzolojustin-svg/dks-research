- Title: "Feeling Better On Hoka, But Feeling Worse On UGG; Still At Buy, But Lower PT".

### Our call
- Shares down in 10/23 aftermarket despite 2Q26 beat; "poised for another negative investor response to earnings". Issues largely UGG: major deterioration of DTC trends, overshadowing Hoka positives (accelerating DTC, solid wholesale order books). "we're getting concerned about the UGG brand, as they may be hitting a wall after several strong years."
- FY26/27 EPS to $6.36/$7.00 (from $6.28/$7.10). 2Q rev growth 9%, ~100bps above gdnc; EPS $1.82 vs gdnc $1.50-$1.55. FY26 EPS gdnc introduced $6.30-$6.39 (Street $6.33).

### Takeaways
- #1 Hoka DTC acceleration: Hoka +11% in Q2 (wholesale +13%, DTC +8%); DTC accelerated from +3% in each of prior 2 qtrs. Strength from new styles in Clifton, Bondi, Arahi with strong sell-through (up DD+ in wholesale channel). Spring '26 order book strong (up y/y, helped by new Mach franchise styles); early Fall '26 order book feedback positive. FY Hoka gdnc low-teens increase → implies 2H +LDD; "could prove conservative if DTC continues to accelerate."
- #2 UGG +10% in Q2, driven by wholesale +17% (partners took shipments earlier given inventory shortages last Holiday). UGG DTC worsened to -10% in Q2 after 2 straight qtrs of -LSD. Mgmt attributed to cannibalization by wholesale (better in-stock availability in channel siphoned DTC sales) + incrementally cautious US consumer shopping across brands (exacerbating shift to wholesale). Solid response to new styles incl Zora ballet flat (debuted late in qtr; Needham flagged initial success in preview). "It remains to be seen" if new styles drive 2H upside. UGG FY gdnc +LSD-MSD → implies 2H flat to down LSD.
- #3 2H GM planned down ~300bps: 200-250bps tariffs (net of mitigation), remainder normalizing markdowns. Beat GM past 2 qtrs (Q2 +30bps y/y vs gdnc down ~200-250bps) → hope outlook conservative.

### Thesis / cases
- Thesis: "one of the highest-quality companies in our coverage"; multi-year track record; two of strongest brands (Hoka, UGG); strong mgmt; fortress balance sheet.
- Bull: new products + wholesale channel fill → Hoka re-accelerates to 15%-20% next few years; UGG MSD-HSD (intl momentum + new domestic launches); margins sustained low-20s → CY27 EPS ~$8.50, price ~$170 (20x P/E on FY27E EPS).
- Base: FY26 EPS roughly flattish vs FY25 (slowing growth + tariffs); UGG +LSD-MSD, Hoka low-teens; ~200bps GM and EBIT margin erosion; resume +DD EPS growth FY27 on MSD-HSD rev growth, modest margin erosion (tariff wrap), buyback. PT $113 = mid-teens P/E on CY27 EPS ~$7.50.
- Bear: difficult compares + tough macro → UGG flat-lines, Hoka slows to single-digit (mainly new distribution); tariffs + normalized discounting push margins to high-teens → CY27 EPS $5.00, price $65 (low-teens P/E).
- Upside drivers: Hoka gains awareness/shelf space faster; UGG continues successful new products; full-price selling maintained; accelerated buybacks.
- Risks: Hoka in highly competitive athletic footwear market; Hoka could run out of new distribution channels; UGG stall if new products fail; margin pressure if growth slows and more discounting/markdowns needed.
