- Title: "Deja Shoe? Another Mixed Print with 2H Outlook Coming Down; Lowering Estimates, PT to $95".

### Our call
- "DECK controversy continues." 1Q mixed; this print "more one-sided" — continued DTC + domestic weakness → smallest top-line beat in years + more cautious 2H outlook.
- 2Q: smallest top-line beat in 4 years; upside entirely GM-driven (tariff timing); 2H Street EPS coming down on weaker demand (FY rev guided +7-7.5% vs Street +10%). UGG appears larger issue than Hoka: UGG DTC missed plan in 2Q; mgmt says 2H pressure continues (increased earlier distribution to partners). "The story remains a challenge."
- Hoka: rev +11%, slight beat vs plan +10%, below buyside expectations. Seq DTC improvement (+8% in 2Q from +3%) good signal but not as robust as many expected; continues to imply lack of US growth. 2H rev plan +LT driven by DTC → also illustrates wholesale channel may be full after 1H sell-in; channel concern likely becomes larger post 2Q.
- UGG: rev +9% (vs +MSD guide) — better than Hoka but complexion not ideal. UGG DTC -10%, missed plan; mgmt: due to higher inventory sell-in at wholesale earlier in 2025 affecting DTC demand (will hurt 2H too). Wholesale +17% but European shipments pulled forward → sell-in growing above sell-through.
- "Deja Shoe" on channel mix: after 1Q mgmt spoke of more balanced channel mix through FY; Hoka improved seq but UGG missed (DTC/US weakness). Mgmt cited weakening US consumer + building preference for wholesale ("US customers prefer multi-brand retail, try shoes on"); wholesale outperformed DTC internationally too (UGG Europe pulled fwd). "Appears that sell-in > sell through at both brands."
- Margins: 1) 2H26 GM pressure expected to persist through 1H27 (tariff-related = 250-325bps); 2) mgmt non-committal on normalized OM rate. 2Q had some items sold at tariff-adjusted prices despite no tariff. Larger question = go-fwd OM% as unfavorable channel mix + increasing competition pressure results. "Out-year EPS power remains in flux."

### Thesis / valuation / scenarios
- EW thesis: stock re-rated lower after 2H25 slowdown; Hoka needs meaningful re-acceleration to recapture above-average multiple of 2022-2024; competitors investing more heavily in running (where Hoka highly concentrated) and have copied Hoka's silhouette.
- Base PT $95 = 15x FY27 EPS (slowing growth, declining margins, where stock trades); in-line view: relative top-line strength in both brands + best-in-class profitability ("a rare ~20% margin brand") offset by slowing demand.
- Upside $125 = ~18x on FY26 EPS ~$6.75: Hoka sustains momentum with technically-driven launches + possibly fresh sartorial viewpoint for lifestyle customers; UGG +MSD or higher ("pull model" intact); sticky GM gains, channel/brand mix sustains margins >20%.
- Downside $75 = ~14x on FY26 EPS ~$5.50: pricing/margin normalization as both brands revert to normalized growth; Hoka slows further as competition ramps in technical running + aesthetics (silhouette); margins retrench to 18%.
- Catalysts: earnings; competitor and retail partner earnings.
- Upside risks: HOKA 20%+ growth; UGG faster; aggressive buybacks. Downside: slowing HOKA, margin reversion, choppy global macro.
- Key themes: (1) Hoka acquired 10+ yrs ago at $3-5M sales; from just over $200M pre-COVID to $2B+ FY25 (+50% CAGR); dominant in technical running; Top 3 share in specialty run channel; expanded to casual joggers + lifestyle. Hoka sees opportunity to add "billions"; but growth slowed in FY26 as US slowed on tough compares + increased competition; DTC no longer growth vehicle; more reliance on outside US. (2) Growth slowed: +30-35% → +24% in 3Q25 → +20% 1Q26 → +11% 2Q26; outlook +LDD for 2H26; primary culprit US DTC slowdown (tougher compares, crowded road/trail running); bears flag product release schedule focused on core runner vs casual customer (now much bigger part of mix); intl solid but masked by US softness. (3) Margin inflection?: Hoka margin 5%→25%+ drove DECK margin 12%→24% in FY25 and EPS ~$1.00→$6.00+; FY26 guided margin pressure (GM + SG&A); not guiding EPS up y/y for first time since Hoka launched; tariffs ~$85M (mitigated in FY26/2H26); debate whether Hoka matured vs reversible via new product + channel mix (leaned wholesale).
- Bull (buyside): Hoka recaptures magic, re-accelerates >20% as compares ease; 2H26/1H27 margin headwinds tariff-related one-time, channel mix less of headwind; UGG healthy, innovated beyond classics, manages inventory; valuation better risk/reward. Bear: Hoka plateaued, competition reverts growth; Hoka margins normalizing as brand matures + moves deeper into wholesale; UGG over-earning, hard to sustain profitability (channel mix).
