- Title: "The Art of Channel Management... More Shoes on More Feet". Aftermarket reaction: DECK -12.3% vs S&P 500 flat.

### Thesis
- 2Q26 beat on sales, GM, adj EPS $1.82 (cons $1.58); beat primarily GM from price increases + less-than-expected tariff pressure. Reinstated FY26 gdnc: sales just below cons, EPS midpoint just ahead.
- Channel argument: brands balance DTC vs wholesale; early stage both grow together; as company matures one channel outpaces other. At DECK wholesale expansion currently hurting DTC growth as new competing points of distribution enter market; "This is temporary and not structural" → reiterate OW over longer horizon. "DECK is 'putting more shoes on more feet'" and gaining global share in both UGG and HOKA.
- Wholesale + international drove majority of growth; US + DTC remained under pressure. Wholesale predominant driver in 2Q26 with some shipment timing benefiting 2Q26. DTC (esp US) to remain pressured through FY2H26; co expects sequential improvement 3Q26→4Q26.
- Near-term tariff headwinds to pressure GM; SG&A % of sales up through rest of year. Mgmt highlighted BTS strength, anticipates strong holiday; cautious on US consumer health (higher prices/inflation could pressure demand).
- Confidence reasons: 1) wholesale demand for product increasing; 2) well positioned for holiday; 3) ample opportunity domestic + international; 4) continued product innovation in multiple categories at UGG and HOKA; 5) opportunity for continued full-price selling supporting top line + earnings.
- Why OW (box): 1) runway for HOKA growth; 2) improving wholesale inventory backdrop supportive of replenishment, benefiting HOKA as traffic-driving, full-priced brand; 3) expanding margins from channel + geography mix; 4) highest ROIC in footwear, still expanding.

### Tariffs
- FY26 unmitigated tariff impact now ~$150mm (vs prior-qtr gdnc ~$185mm); mitigation offsets ~$75mm-$95mm. Delay in net headwind because inventory shipments pulled forward before higher rates → no meaningful negative impact in 2Q26; mgmt: unique to 2Q26; FY2H26 net tariff headwind expectation unchanged. Mitigation: 1) select, strategic, staggered price increases; 2) partial cost-sharing with factory partners. Sourcing: <5% footwear from China (some not routed to US); majority SE Asia, Vietnam ~80%.
- Prior commentary (1Q26): Vietnam rate increase 10%→20% raised unmitigated FY26 COGS impact to $185mn from prior $150mn; selective initial price increases + phased additional pricing through FY26 aiming to recapture up to $75mn; not expected to fully offset; margin pressure 2H26.
- Barclays Tariff QuikCalc (rates effective 8/7/25; prior assumed 30% China/10% RoW ex Mexico/Canada): DECK total avg unit cost +12.8%; price increase needed to fully offset on US = 8.4%.
