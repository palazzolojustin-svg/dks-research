### Risk factors (FY2022-specific substance)
- Long-range strategic plan execution risk; failure to meet shareholder expectations re sales, supplier diversification, cost-cutting, op margins, EPS → stock volatility.
- Top-5 suppliers 86% of 2022 purchases; Nike ~65%; individual banners bought ~50%–70% from Nike.
- N America: "only 26% of our locations are off-mall."
- COVID-19 cited; Russia invasion of Ukraine; cyberattacks expected to rise with Ukraine war.
- Riots/vandalism/social unrest risk factor (property damage, event cancellations).
- Privacy: GDPR fines up to 4% worldwide revenue (incl UK GDPR); CCPA eff 2020-01-01; CPRA eff 2023-01-01 creates CA data protection agency; Connecticut, Utah, Virginia privacy laws.
- Omnichannel technology enablement complexity risk.
- Labor: unusually low worker availability attributed to COVID-19; need functional & technology specialists.
- Minority investments $630M at 1/28/23.
- Material weakness at newly acquired WSS (no misstatements; remediation plan in development).
- Shareholder activism risk.
- Significant 2022 sales & OI outside U.S.; UK inventory purchases in euros.
- Brexit: UK exited EU 2020-01-31; trade agreement 2020-12-24; Feb 2020 engaged 3PL in England to mitigate.
- Tariffs on Chinese-made goods.
- LIBOR phase-out: Fed final rule Dec 2022 replacing LIBOR after 2023-06-30; revolver has alternative rate methods.
- Cash & equivalents $536M at 1/28/23 (all investment-grade). U.S. pension trust assets $509M at 1/28/23.
- ESG risk factor: 4 pillars — (1) Leveraging the Power of Our People and Communities, (2) Strengthening Sustainability of Supply Chain, (3) Managing and Reducing Environmental Impacts, (4) Operating Ethically and Transparently; ESG scores/investor exclusion risk.
- Legislation/litigation: regularly involved in litigation incl. wage & hour, data privacy, anti-corruption, purported class actions.
- Debt: 2021 sale of $400M 4% Senior Notes due 2029 — debt service risk. Credit facility borrowing base tied to inventory; covenant risk.
