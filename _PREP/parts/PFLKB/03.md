### Risk factors (FY2021-specific substance)
- Top-5 suppliers 87% of 2021 purchases; Nike ~68%; banners individually 50%–75% from Nike. **"Beginning with the fourth quarter of 2022, we do not expect any one supplier to represent more than 55% of merchandise purchases."**
- Athletic footwear ordered 4–6 months ahead.
- COVID-19 risk factor: 2020 store closures, stay-at-home orders; variants; mask/vaccine mandates; supplier DC/factory closures; port congestion delaying merchandise receipts; lasting economic effects, recession, inflation.
- N America: "only 21% of our locations are off-mall."
- Acquisitions: WSS (Eurostar, Inc., Q3'21) and atmos (Q4'21, Japan) integration risks.
- Russia invasion of Ukraine (recent); expected cyberattack increase.
- Privacy: GDPR fines up to 4%; CCPA (2020); CPRA eff 2023-01-01; Virginia and Colorado laws passed 2021 effective 2023.
- DCs + 3PLs in US, Canada, England, Australia, NZ, Asia; COVID transport delays.
- Key executive retention/succession risk. Unusually low worker availability in 2021 (COVID).
- Impairment risk on LL assets, ROU, goodwill tied to long-range plan.
- Minority investments $781M at 1/29/22.
- Significant 2021 sales & OI outside U.S.; suppliers manufacture abroad. UK buys inventory in euros.
- New inflation risk factor. LIBOR phase-out; 2020 Credit Agreement has alternatives.
- Cash $804M at 1/29/22 (investment-grade institutions). U.S. pension trust assets $631M at 1/29/22.
- Labor law: NLRB regulation changes; climate change physical/regulatory risk; ESG 4 pillars (=same as 02).
- SOX 404: WSS and atmos excluded from FY2021 ICFR assessment; to be included for FY ending 1/28/23 if necessary.
- Debt: Q3'21 sale of $400M 4% Senior Notes due 2029 (covenant/default risk). Credit facility borrowing base tied to inventory.
