Investing in Our Growth 
We have a long track record of investing in areas that offer the potential for significant market share growth. As we 
move into 2025, we are committed to investing in three key growth areas repositioning our real estate and store 
portfolio, driving continued growth in our footwear business, and accelerating our eCommerce business. 
The first of these opportunities centers on optimizing the value of our highly successful House of Sport and DICK’S 
Field House concepts. House of Sport, our experiential sports retail destination, combines an elevated product 
assortment with in-store experiences, including multi-sport cages for baseball, softball, soccer and lacrosse, golf 
simulators, rock climbing walls, fields, ice rinks and services like baseball glove steaming, lacrosse stick restringing 
and bike repair. 
Since we opened our first House of Sport in 2021, this concept has generated exceptional financial results while 
driving strong engagement with our athletes, brand partners and communities. House of Sport serves as a vibrant 
community hub, hosting a wide range of events and activities that generate local excitement and connect area 
residents. House of Sport also gives us the ability to showcase our brands in a unique and powerful way, 
strengthening our existing brand partnerships and opening doors to new ones. In addition, House of Sport is 
commanding unprecedented landlord interest, positioning us to be an anchor in some of the most successful retail 
centers in the country.

In 2025, we plan to open approximately 16 new locations, and by year-end 2027, we expect to have between 75 to 
100 House of Sport locations. We are also expanding our Field House concept, which takes the learnings from House 
of Sport and applies those best practices to our standard 50,000 square-foot DICK’S Sporting Goods location. Like 
House of Sport, Field House offers an elevated product assortment and service model, premium experiences, and 
bold visuals. Field House is delivering very strong results, and we are committed to expanding it across our DICK’S 
network. At year-end 2024, we had a total of 26 Field House locations, and we expect to add approximately 18 more 
in 2025. Together, this one-two punch of House of Sport and Field House is the future of our DICK’S stores.
Our second key growth area is our footwear business. Footwear is the product every athlete needs, and over the past 
decade, we have added premium footwear decks in approximately 90% of DICK'S locations, providing our brand 
partners with the ideal platform to showcase their prime footwear options for every sport, athlete and occasion. In 
return, our brands offer DICK'S access to premium product assortments, inspiring athlete loyalty and generating 
robust sales growth. We believe we have significant opportunity to further grow our footwear business. Moving 
forward, we are supporting this growth with strategic marketing investments, increasing our focus on footwear across 
our in-store and digital channels, and partnering with elite brands, athletes and celebrities who resonate with our 
athletes. 
Our third key growth area is to accelerate our multibillion-dollar eCommerce business. We believe we can significantly 
expand our online presence, and we are aggressively pursuing this by investing in functionality and design to enhance 
the omnichannel athlete experience and in marketing opportunities that drive brand awareness. We are also making 
enhancements to the DICK'S app, where we see increasing interest and engagement, as well as enhancing our in-
store technology to assist our teammates and athletes in finding and delivering the product they need as quickly and 
as efficiently as possible. 
In addition to focusing on these key growth areas, we are actively growing two exciting digital businesses that we 
believe will enrich the athlete experience and create new growth avenues. The first of these is GameChanger, our 
youth sports app, which enables live streaming, scheduling, and scorekeeping for athletes and sports teams, while 
providing an innovative platform for us to engage with athletes. In 2024, GameChanger hosted approximately nine 
million unique active users, generating more than $100 million in revenue. We believe this platform has excellent long-
term growth potential, and we are targeting it to deliver revenue of approximately $150 million in 2025. Our second 
digital opportunity is DICK'S Media Network, which we developed to bring our athletes closer to the brands and 
products they care about. We are still in the early stages of scaling and optimizing this network, but it has already 
garnered strong initial interest, and we believe it has the potential to drive long-term sales growth and gross margin 
expansion in the years ahead. 
We move forward with these growth initiatives in a dynamic macroenvironment, and we will continue to balance our 
growth with appropriate caution. We believe that these are the right long-term investments for our business and feel 
confident that our operational strength, strong brand relationships, healthy balance sheet and deep athlete 
engagement will position us well to navigate the overall environment and prioritize these growth areas even in times of 
uncertainty.

DICK’S Sporting Goods, Inc. (together with its subsidiaries, referred to as “the Company”, “we”, “us” and “our” unless

Goodwill represents the excess of acquisition cost over the fair value of the net assets of acquired entities. The Company
