Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation
Investment Thesis
DKS is executing at a high level, has strongly improved its 
relationships with vendors and is benefitting from casualization 
and healthy living trends. On the other hand, we worry about the 
footwear cycle slowing, while any potential softness in the core 
DKS business will make improving FL even more important in 
2026 from an investment perspective.  We think this process will 
take time, especially with its lower income consumer exposure 
in the US and global retail footprint.  Hence, we maintain our 
Neutral rating.  
Valuation
Our Dec 26 price target is $228 and based on ~13.5x our 2027 
EPS estimate, which is in the range of its historical FY1 
valuation, and equivalent to ~7x EV/EBITDA.

Risks to Rating and Price Target
Downside Risks. Higher unemployment, woes in the housing market, and weakening 
consumer demand could render our estimates too aggressive. In the long term, DKS has 
faced high competitive pressures, both from DTC and online, and it is in a highly 
promotional environment.  Not to mention, FL increases DKS’ exposure to malls and lower-
income consumers, as well as introducing a potentially unwieldy international presence. 
Upside Risks. If our estimates prove conservative, our EPS numbers could be too low and 
thus prove upside to our valuation numbers. If DKS is able to comp positive on difficult laps 
improving underlying fundamentals, the stock could have the ability to re-rate. On the FL 
side, potential benefits include buying and SG&A synergies, TAM expansion, unlocking 
operational efficiencies, and improved brand relationships.
