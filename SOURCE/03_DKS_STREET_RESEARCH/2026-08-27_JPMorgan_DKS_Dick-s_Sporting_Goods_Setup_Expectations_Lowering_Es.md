Below we preview DKS 2Q26 EPS, which reports tomorrow, August 25th before 
the market open (typically at 7AM ET), followed by a conference call at 8AM ET. 
Setup & Expectations: Lowering sales/margin outlook. Low bar with BTS 
timing in focus as market ponders the risk of a slower footwear cycle and 
lower-end consumer exposure (FL). As detailed below, based on our data and 
channel work, we are maintaining our DKS SSS forecasts (+5% in 2Q and +2.5% 
in 2H), with 2Q’s +5% above our +4.5% view of market expectations and our 
+2.5%e in 3Q also better than what we generally see as “the bar.” We are lowering 
our FL SSS forecasts (-2% in 2Q and +1.5%/+3% in 3Q/4Q), with these outcomes 
also likely a relief relative to our view of market expectations of down L-MSD 2Q 
comps and FL running much worse QTD. We are also reducing our margin 
assumptions for both businesses in light of the softer FL sales expectations and 
some incremental promotions, with the Street seemingly too low on DKS 2Q SG&A 
(gross margin sees a “change of trajectory” but op margin down in the 110-120 
bps zipcode vs. Street’s -70 bps) and for FL in 3Q as well given the recent brand 
re-launch spending. Our updated 2Q EPS estimate is $3.62 vs. $3.75 per 
Consensus Metrix, with 2026 and 2027 estimates now at $14.04/$17.00 vs. the 
Street’s $14.28/$16.43 with our updated Dec 2026 price target $245. See Figure 1 
detailing our fully updated estimates.  
We remain optimistic on the 2H setup, however, considering the ongoing 
supportive sporting goods/wellness backdrop, the FL re-merchandising/ad 
campaign, and gross margin drivers (clearance laps, synergies, alternative 
profit pools). Indeed, we continue to see 2H26 as the the potential “own the hope” 
stock moment. Expectations have also plummeted, starting with the post-World 
Cup lull and later BTS suppressing QTD credit card data, which we believe has 
spiraled concerns that the category could be at fault. Considering DKS’s 
geographic exposure, the LDW/BTS timing shift likely has a significant impact. 
The bull case is that the data headwind of lapping BTS weeks last year with lower 
volume weeks so far QTD shifts to a benefit that lasts into early Sept (similar to 
WMT’s messaging).
Top-Line Breakdown: Maintaining DKS Comps but Lowering FL
•
For core Dick’s, we are maintaining our +5% 2Q SSS vs. +4.9% per 
Consensus Metrix, with our work indicating that market expectations 
have generally come down over the past month to ~+4.5% (with the lower 
end of expectations at +4%). We believe ongoing momentum benefitted from 
World Cup and other key moments in 2Q (America 250, Knicks win; see 
Figures 11-12), and continued mid- to high-income consumer resilience. We 
continue to model +2.5% SSS in 2H26, in-line with consensus.
•
For Foot Locker, we are lowering our 2Q SSS forecast to -2% (vs. +1%e 
prior), which we believe would be a well-received outcome considering 
weakness in the lower-income consumer, a potential post-World Cup lull 
and disappointing laterals (e.g., JD Sports, On Running, see Figures 3-6).

Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation
Investment Thesis
Big picture, DKS is executing at a high level, with the company 
well-positioned to benefit from ongoing wellness and high 
sports engagement trends in US society. Moreover, with DKS 
being the only national sporting goods retailer and its emphasis 
on better/best products, we believe the brands need DKS and the 
FL acquisition only improves the balance of power against them. 
We are also optimistic that the FL turnaround will proceed faster 
than expected with DKS lapping high levels of clearance and 
driving better merchandising/assortments starting with BTS. 
This, plus the company’s alternative profit pools, should drive 
margin and earnings upside.  
Valuation
Our updated $245 Dec 26 price target is based on ~14x 2027E 
P/E (equivalent to ~8x EV/EBITDA) with both largely in the 
range of historical averages.

Margin Breakdown: Lowering Margin Forecasts
•
For core Dick’s, we are now modeling 2Q GM of 37.2%, up 10 bps YOY (vs. Street 
37.1%) on an expected “change in trajectory” as tariffed inventory has been largely sold 
through and alternate profit pools remain tailwinds. In addition, the back half benefits 
from buying synergies for both brands, with moderating supply chain tailwinds likely 
offset by persistently high fuel costs (DKS did not embed these when it guided on 1Q).

12.1% 2Qe op margin reflects peak World Cup ad spend in the qtr, with 2H expansion

product in 2H following the BTS assortment. On 3Q specifically, we believe the Street 
is too low on SG&A given the brand re-launch and BTS assortment, with advertising the 
key driver to watch (see Figures 9-10 for our FL promo trackers).

Dick's Sporting Goods (Overweight; Price Target: $245.00)
Investment Thesis
Big picture, DKS is executing at a high level, with the company well-positioned to benefit 
from ongoing wellness and high sports engagement trends in US society. Moreover, with 
DKS being the only national sporting goods retailer and its emphasis on better/best 
products, we believe the brands need DKS and the FL acquisition only improves the balance 
of power against them. We are also optimistic that the FL turnaround will proceed faster than 
expected with DKS lapping high levels of clearance and driving better merchandising/
assortments starting with BTS. This, plus the company’s alternative profit pools, should 
drive margin and earnings upside.

EBITDA) with both largely in the range of historical averages.
