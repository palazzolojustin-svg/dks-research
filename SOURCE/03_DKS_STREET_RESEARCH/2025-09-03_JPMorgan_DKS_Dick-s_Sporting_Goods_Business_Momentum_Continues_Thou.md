PM’s take: business momentum continues with topline guidance prudent on 
2H uncertainty and gross margin lower on tariffs and promotional 
uncertainty; share dilution from FL acquisition in focus.  DKS’s confidence in 
its business continues to rank highest among our discretionary companies that have 
reported 2Q with no hesitation around the innovation and newness in its assortment 
or consistency of the consumer (no BTS hangover expected).  We believe QTD 
trends continue in the 5% range with all parts of its assortment humming, aided by 
the company’s key brand access combined with investments in digital, advertising, 
and store experience continuing to bear fruit (HoS remodels/relos adding 2 pts to 
comp in 2025). While traffic growth was modest, it was similar to 1Q, and the 4.9% 
ticket growth was mostly driven by mix (full priced selling, expanded high heat 
product such as On and Hoka).  The wrinkles for the quarter were "as if" gross 
margin expansion was a tick below where we would have modeled on a 5% comp 
and the company backing off the 75-bp GM expansion guide for the year with 2H 
increasing more than 1H, but not to the same degree as prior (we estimate GM up 
50 bps and more in 4Q given a 30-bp product donation to storm victims in 4Q24).  
The change was a higher and more certain impact from tariffs (and how that is 
shared with the brands) as well as promotional uncertainty (although no issues 
currently).  On the FL side, the deal is expected to close on September 8th, with the 
consideration deadline tomorrow after the market close. If FL closes above the $24 
cash consideration tomorrow and all FL shareholders elect stock (the economically 
rational decision), that is ~11MM additional shares coming in representing ~11% 
share count dilution.  Mgmt noted that even if this occurs, it still expects the deal 
to be accretive in FY26, though the degree would be less.  Net-net, like last qtr, 
despite strong fundamentals and company execution, uncertainty remains high for 
the stock with clarity on FL a key unlock.  See our DKS/FL mini-deep dive.  
Key takeaways from the call and our follow-up with management: 
•
(1) FL deal to close on September 8th; synergies reiterated but EPS 
accretion the big unknown. Specifically, FL shareholders approved the 
transaction and DKS received all regulatory approvals required to close. The 
company continues to see “tremendous” opportunities with FL, reiterating the 
$100-125MM of mostly buying synergies (merchandise and non-
merchandise), although future synergies from head count and other areas are 
expected with the work there yet to be done. Importantly, although the deal is 
still expected to be EPS accretive in the first full fiscal year post deal close 
ex-transaction costs, the level of accretion now depends on the consideration 
mix between cash and stock, in addition to core business performance and 
synergy timing.

Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation
Investment Thesis
DKS is executing at a high level, has strongly improved its 
relationships with vendors, and is benefitting from the 
casualization and healthy living trends despite facing key 
hydration and footwear laps. That said, we maintain our Neutral 
rating as the stock is net down near the entire value of the 
proposed FL acquisition since the announcement, with investors 
skeptical of hurting the momentum in the core business. Given 
the uncertainty around the deal, we remain on the sidelines, 
though we do see upside to current guidance and remain 
impressed by management’s strategy and execution.  
Valuation
We are establishing our Dec 2026 price target of $235, given the 
ongoing momentum in the business combined with the Foot 
Locker acquisition overhang, and based on ~14x PE on our 2027 
forecasts (equivalent to ~8.5x EV/EBITDA; in-line with 
historical average).

DKS posted its 6th consecutive qtr of a 4+ comp at +5.0% in 2Q, driven by strength in both 
ticket (+4.1%) and traffic (+0.9%), with e-comm growth still exceeding the company 
average. Importantly, the majority of the ticket increase was driven by full priced selling 
from newness/innovation resonating with DKS’s athletes (including continued strength

metrics including number of athletes, purchase frequency, and spend per trip all increased

throughout the quarter with all months positive. Further, DKS called out continued 
market share gains from both omnichannel and online competitors. On the consumer, 
DKS once again called out growth across all income cohorts (despite continued macro 
uncertainty and “sporadic”/strategic price increases), with strength across all 4 key 
categories (apparel, footwear, team sports, golf), and saw no signs of consumer trade 
down (helped by innovation/newness). We are now modeling 3Q and FY25 comps of 
+3.5% and +3.7%, respectively, with 70% of footage growth related to HoS relocations/
remodels, supporting ~2 points of comp growth for the year (70% * 3% footage growth).

(4) GM expansion on merch margins and occupancy leverage; GM to accel 
“slightly” more in 2H than 1H. 2Q GM expanded ~30 bps YOY, primarily driven by 
merch margin expansion (+18 bps) from differentiated/high quality assortment, 
favorable mix, and early stage GameChanger and Media Network benefits (no 
incremental promo pressures YOY, per se). DKS expects GameChanger to grow 
consistently in 1H and 2H (adding similar GM tailwinds and more so in 2026 as the 
business scales) with the ad business accelerating in the back half (that and the 
merchandise donation represent big drivers of the 2H acceleration). DKS now expects 
GM expansion for the full year (and 2H slightly more than 1H) driven by the factors above 
(vs. +75 bps expansion at the midpoint prior), with the change primarily driven by 
increased clarity around tariffs, wanting to stay clean with on-trend inventory, and 
uncertain future risks on promotionality (although DKS is not currently facing any promo 
risk currently). Merch margin specifically is also expected to accelerate more in 2H vs. 
1H, with mgmt calling out GameChanger and Dick’s Media Network as some of the key 
drivers, in addition to lapping $10MM (30 bps) in wildfire and flood donations in 4Q. 
Net-net, this expansion is expected to be entirely offset by SG&A deleverage from further 
investments in digital, in-store, and marketing with op margin still up 10 bps at the high 
end. We also view this lowered GM expectation conservatively, especially as DKS starts 
facing incremental benefits from the high margin GameChanger and Dick’s Media 
Network “alternative profit pools.” We are now modeling FY25 and FY26 gross margins 
of 36.4% (up 50 bps YOY) and 36.9% (up 50 bps YOY), respectively.
