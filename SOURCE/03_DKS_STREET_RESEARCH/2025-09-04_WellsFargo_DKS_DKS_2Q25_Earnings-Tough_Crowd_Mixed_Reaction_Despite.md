Our Call
A continuation of key theme this earnings season—higher multiple names selling off on 
solid prints, but w/ guide coming in under high bar. Core Dick's executing at high level, but 
still more Qs than As on FL, remain EW; PT to $220 on higher earnings.
 
Thoughts on 2Q. Another solid beat w/ SSS upside (+5% vs St +3.2%) resulting in slightly 
less SGA deleverage than expected (-105bps vs St -135). The sales beat was broad based 
across product cats and selling channels, and w/ new store formats (FieldHouse and 
House of Sport) outperforming expectations. Customers are responding to newness/
innovation in apparel and hardgoods, w/ footwear innovation potentially on the come. 2H 
guide (see below) was conservative, likely why shares are down today.
 
Mgmt Callback Takes. 1) expect GM and MM to expand in 2H as well as FY, 2) GM update 
from 1Q based on tariffs and flexibility on 4Q promo environ, 3) HOS—getting access 
to better real estate and better product from both large and small brands, 4) better 
SSS, better GM and better GP $ by moving clearance to Going, Going Gone stores when 
they're opened in the same market as an existing DSG and 5) Game Changer consistently 
driving 30 bps of comp, growing at 50%, w/ strong margins (SaaS model).
 
Holding Their Blocks, Wrapping Up on Tackles. DKS continues to execute at a very 
high level (6 straight qts of SSS 4%+) across all facets of the business: in-store, online 
(ecomm again outpaced the overall biz), and digital customer interaction—underpinned 
by a strong product assortment. DKS is also leaning into the success of their new store 
formats (Field House from 26 to 42 and House of Sport from 19 to 35 YoY). The end 
result is con't share gains and no signs, yet, of a consumer slowdown.
 
Still More Questions Than Answers on FL. As per FL results yesterday (total sales -2.4%, 
SSS -2% & NA SSS -1.4%) DKS clearly has lots of work to do. While bulls could argue it's 
exactly the focus and attention to detail, which is driving core results, that DKS brings to 
the table, bears have a reasonable argument around the amount of work to be done. We 
think the 2Q call did little to resolve any concerns w/ perhaps now more Qs than As given 
recent FL results & no incremental detail on the call.
 
Conservative Guidance Underwhelms. Relative to a strong 1H25 (SSS +4.5% to +5%) 
DKS' updated FY25 guide (+2.5% to +3%) implies 2H SSS in the +2% to +3% range, even 
at the upper end of the range. While 2H compares are +50bps tougher than 1H, the 
implied slowdown is closer to -150 to -200bps. We also believe shares are reacting to a 
change to GM guide from "improve by approx 75bps at the midpoint" to "we expect to 
drive GM expansion for the FY." EPS guide goes up, but by $0.10, or less than 1%.

Investment Thesis
Key themes surrounding the name include:
1. Can DKS Fix Foot Locker? On May 15, DKS announced the acquisition of Foot Locker for $24/
share, or approximately $2.3B. At the time, DKS outlined 5 key tenants behind the proposed 
acquisition of FL; these include: 1) the opportunity to move from a purely domestic to global 
operator, 2) the ability to serve a broader variety of customer sets through multiple concepts,
3) the ability to strengthen its relationships with key suppliers, 4) a broader omni-channel reach 
and 5) potential synergy opportunities of $100-$125mm. Synergies are mostly in COGS with 
plans to run the businesses separately. While mgmt plans to run the biz separately, we would 
note historical synergies closer to 2%, or $160mm based on ~$8b in FL revs suggesting slight 
upside optionality. DKS, also at that time, suggested the deal would be accretive within 12 months 
of closing, with cost synergies in the medium term achieved through procurement and direct 
sourcing efficiencies (COGS). That said, DKS was a bit less direct in that regard on the 2Q25 call, 
discussing a) the timing of synergies and b) the performance of the core FL business, and said 
"there are lots of moving pieces" with "more work to be done."
2. Executing at a High Level. DKS continues to execute at a very high level (6 straight qts of 
SSS 4%+) across all facets of the business: in-store, online (ecomm continues to outpace the 
business), and digital customer interaction through Game Changer and Dicks Media Network—
all underpinned by a strong product assortment. DKS is also leaning into the success of their new 
store formats (Field House from 26 to 42 and House of Sport from 19 to 35 YoY). The newer 
concepts, mostly remodels of existing locations, bring DKS closer to hardcore athletes, provides 
a differentiated experience, unlocks better ("exciting") real estate and provides access to better 
product from both larger, global suppliers, and smaller "boutique" suppliers. At the core DSG 
stores, DKS' continued share gains help them secure access to premium, unique product, and 
the upside to SSS has allowed DKS to re-invest in the stores through training and technology. 
For DKS, it really does come down to the blocking and tackling. If the recent rebound in sport 
participation continues, we believe DKS would be the prime beneficiary.
3. Youth Sports Participation Figures Cut Both Ways. The data here for DKS cuts both ways. 
On the one hand, youth sport participation is up YoY across boys and girls, but data is mixed 
and inconsistent over the medium to longer term. While the NSGA suggests high school sport 
participation hit a record high in 2023-2024, and the SFIA reports that participation rates recently 
hit the highest level since 2015, other studies have shown a drop in participation rates, from the 
50% range to the 40% range for boys, between 2013 and 2023, and from 56% to 50% for girls 
from 2017 to 2023. The potential for further retracement in sport participation levels to those 
several years ago is a key bull case argument. We believe bears could argue that participation rates 
are fluctuating but generally biased down over the LT.
