Our Call
Momentum on core DSG continues with solid SSS, non-comp opportunities, and 
optionality on ancillary profit drivers - but the key debate remains on if solving for product 
will drive the turnaround at FL. Remain Equal Weight, PT to $200 from $225.
 
Thoughts on 4Q. The turnaround of FL remains front and center to the DKS story, and 
management doubled down on its confidence in the business with a rollout of the "Fast 
Break" remodels in 2026 with re-merchandised assortments post inventory cleanup. 
The key question that bulls have to get comfortable with is whether better product and 
merchandising alone can drive the 2H26 inflection at FL, or whether it will take longer, 
take more investment, and drag on strong core performance at DSG.
 
Mgmt Callback Takes. 1) Mgmt confident FL turnaround is gaining traction with path 
to positive (1-3%) comps and margin expansion; 2) Core DKS comps weighted to 1H vs 
2H (2Q higher than 1Q), FL improves sequentially, weighted to a positive 2H26; 3) DC 
opening in April is a GM headwind offsetting merch margin expansion; 4) $100-$125M 
synergies split w/ GM and SG&A starting in 2H, core DKS benefits as well; 5) Fast Break 
rollout driving improvement from better allocation & merchandising.
 
Driving The Core. The core DSG business momentum continued as the company drives 
positive comps and merchandise margin expansion, with non-comp opportunities 
on House of Sport, Field House, and Golf Galaxy, and additional profit drivers in 
GameChanger, DICK's Media Network (DMN) and vertical brands. Elevated retail 
experiences are further entrenching the business long-term as the partner of choice with 
vendors across categories.
 
Some +'s On Foot Locker, But More Work To Be Done. Management highlighted that 
inventory is the cleanest it has ever been after 4Q with the "clean out the garage" actions 
taken. The "Fast Break" initiative is yielding positive results and the company expects to 
roll it out to over 250 locations by back-to-school 2026 globally (~11% of total stores), 
and noted the early tests are turning around previously unprofitable locations, giving 
confidence to close fewer stores than previously expected.
 
Where We Shake Out. Confidence in the 2H inflection at FL is predicated that better 
inventory is on the way, and when combined with better retail fundamentals, it will drive 
stronger sales and profitability. Though we do believe that a rejuvenated relationship with 
key vendors and marginal improvements from Nike in NA should be a benefit to trough 
level earnings and performance at FL, we remain EW today when balancing the risk that 
the business will need more investment and take longer to turn.

Investment Thesis
Key themes surrounding the name include:
1. Can DKS Fix Foot Locker? On May 15, DKS announced the acquisition of Foot Locker for $24/
share, or approximately $2.3B. At the time, DKS outlined 5 key tenants behind the proposed 
acquisition of FL; these include: 1) the opportunity to move from a purely domestic to global 
operator, 2) the ability to serve a broader variety of customer sets through multiple concepts,
3) the ability to strengthen its relationships with key suppliers, 4) a broader omni-channel reach, 
and 5) potential synergy opportunities of $100-$125mm. Synergies are mostly in COGS with 
plans to run the businesses separately. While mgmt plans to run the biz separately, we would 
note historical synergies closer to 2%, or $150mm based on ~$7.5b in FL revs suggesting slight 
upside optionality. DKS has repeated that the deal would be accretive in FY26, with cost synergies 
in the medium term achieved through procurement and direct sourcing efficiencies (COGS). 
While DKS again repeated this on the 3Q25 call, the company was a bit less direct in that regard 
on the 2Q25 call, discussing a) the timing of synergies and b) the performance of the core FL 
business, and said "there are lots of moving pieces," with "more work to be done." As of 4Q25, 
DKS remains committed to the $100M-$125M of cost synergies, with a portion reflected in its 
2026 outlook for the business, while expecting improvement in 2H26 driven by the "Fast Break" 
initiative (essentially re-merchandising, clearing out 30% of non-performing SKUs) being rolled 
out into a larger portion of the FL store fleet.
2. Executing at a High Level. DKS continues to execute at a very high level (14 straight qts of LSD/
MSD SSS as of 4Q25) across all facets of the business: in-store, online (ecomm continues to 
outpace the business), and digital customer interaction through Game Changer and Dicks Media 
Network—all underpinned by a strong product assortment. DKS is also leaning into the success 
of their new store formats (Field House from 26 to 51 and House of Sport from 19 to 35). The 
newer concepts, mostly remodels of existing locations, bring DKS closer to hardcore athletes, 
provides a differentiated experience, unlocks better ("exciting") real estate, and provides access 
to better product from both larger, global suppliers, and smaller "boutique" suppliers. At the core 
DSG stores, DKS's continued share gains help them secure access to premium, unique product, 
and the upside to SSS has allowed DKS to re-invest in the stores through training and technology. 
For DKS, it really does come down to the blocking and tackling. If the recent rebound in sport 
participation continues, we believe DKS would be the prime beneficiary.
3. Youth Sports Participation Figures Cut Both Ways. The data here for DKS cuts both ways. 
On the one hand, youth sport participation is up YoY across boys and girls, but data is mixed 
and inconsistent over the medium to longer term. While the NSGA suggests high school sport 
participation hit a record high in 2023-2024, and the SFIA reports that participation rates recently 
hit the highest level since 2015, other studies have shown a drop in participation rates, from the 
50% range to the 40% range for boys, between 2013 and 2023, and from 56% to 50% for girls 
from 2017 to 2023. The potential for further retracement in sport participation levels to those 
several years ago is a key bull case argument. We believe bears could argue that participation rates 
are fluctuating but generally biased down over the LT.

The Bull Case… We believe bulls view (1) FL results and synergies coming in "better than feared";
(2) the potential that DKS acquired FL at almost trough levels on potentially trough profitability,
(3) excellent execution on the base business, now more consistently, and (4) potential for further 
retracement in sport participation levels to those several years ago.
The Bear Case… On the other hand, we believe bears are concerned that (1) the FL acquisition 
becomes an albatross given the myriad of issues DKS needs to address and fix, (2) the margin outlook 
becomes less visible following multiple years of growth post-COVID, (3) footwear innovation / 
newness gets pushed out, and (4) cumulative macro headwinds from tariffs pressures discretionary 
spend.
Financial Outlook
FY26: Our FY26E EPS estimate of $14.08 represents EPS +6.6%. We project core Dick's Sporting 
Goods SSS of 2.3% and Foot Locker SSS of 1.3%. We expect gross margin down 115bps to 33.0% and 
5bps of SG&A leverage, resulting in operating margin contraction of 110bps to 7.7%. All estimates are 
impacted by the 2025 mid-year (9/08/25) acquisition of Foot Locker.
FY27: Our FY27 EPS of $16.00 represents EPS 12.5%. We project total sales up 2.7% on consolidated 
comps up 1.9%. We expect gross margin +39bps to 33.4% and 27bps of SG&A leverage, resulting in 
operating margins up 67bps to 8.4%.

Investment Thesis
We are Equal Weight on shares of DKS. While core Dick's Sporting Goods performance and positioning remains strong, we believe the 
near-term visibility remains opaque as the Foot Locker is integrated.
Target Price Valuation for DKS
Our price target of $200 applies ~12.5x our FY27 EPS estimate of $16.00, roughly in-line with the historical average ~12x given continued 
momentum in the model, driven by new store formats, offset by risks on the acquisition of Foot Locker.
Risks to Our Price Target and Rating for DKS
Upside risks include: (1) comps move back to +MSD run rate; and (2) merch margins remain sticky; (3) Acquisition of FL and execution of 
synergies drives EPS upside and further expands the TAM opportunity of DKS internationally.
Downside risks (1) comps decelerate and/or turn negative; (2) EBIT margin gains reverse; (3) Acquisition of FL proves to be long-term 
dilutive or takes longer to turn.
