Our Call
Herein lies the debate - core DKS again strong w/ comps +5.7% (vs St +3.7%) and DKS-
only EPS beat St by +$0.09 (+3.5%). But total co EPS $2.07 vs DKS-only $2.78 suggests 
"duration" seems to be the name of the game here w/ lots of work to do on FL.
 
Initial Thoughts
3Q Core DKS Was Solid. Core DKS comps +5.7% vs St +3.7% and EPS $2.78 vs St $2.69 
show continued execution at the core banner. That said, core DKS GM does look like a 
miss at 36% (vs St 36.4%). Separately, pro forma FL comps (-4.7%) decelerated from 2Q 
(-2%), with Intl comps -10%.
 
But Early Feedback on FL Suggests Lots of Work to Do. DKS announced several 
strategic actions to improve FL's underlying business - incl store closures and clearing 
unproductive inventory - resulting in $500M-$750M pre-tax charges. DKS also 
announced these actions would weigh on FL 4Q25 GM by 1,000-1,500bps, with SSS 
down (HSD)-(MSD)%. DKS disclosed issuing 9.6M shares ($2.1B), plus paying $233M 
in cash and $111.6M (pre-existing ownership) for the FL acquisition ($2.5B total 
consideration).
 
Core Guidance Getting a Raise, But All Eyes on FL Commentary. DKS raising 1) revenues 
$13.95-14.0B (from $13.75-13.95B), 2) SSS +3.5-4.0% (from +2.0-3.5%) and 3) EPS 
$14.25-14.55 (from $13.90-14.50). Separately, DKS reiterating FL should be accretive to 
FY26 EPS (excl integration and restructuring costs).

Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks
Dick's Sporting Goods, Inc. (DKS)
Investment Thesis
We are Equal Weight on shares of DKS. While mgmt execution in a choppy backdrop remains impressive, valuation is rich, which we believe 
caps upside and keeps us sidelined.
Target Price Valuation for DKS: $220.00 from NC
Our price target of $220 applies ~15x our FY25 EPS estimate, a premium to the historical average of ~12x given continued momentum in 
the model, driven by new store formats and outperformance in the sporting goods space.
Risks to Our Price Target and Rating for DKS
Upside risks include: (1) comps move back to +MSD run rate; and (2) merch margins remain sticky.
Downside risks (1) comps decelerate and/or turn negative; and (2) EBIT margin gains reverse
