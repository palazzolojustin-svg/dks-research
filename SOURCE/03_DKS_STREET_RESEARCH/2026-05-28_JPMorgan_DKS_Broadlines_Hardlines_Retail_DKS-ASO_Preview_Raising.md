Below we preview DKS and ASO 1Q26 EPS. DKS reports on Wednesday, May 
27th BMO, followed by a conference call at 8AM ET. ASO reports on Tuesday, 
June 9th BMO, followed by a conference call at 10AM ET.  
•
DKS: Increasing core DKS comp to +4.8% with FL at +1%; EPS above 
Street led by FL op profit. Based on our data and channel work, we are 
increasing our core DKS comp to +4.8% from +3.4% with FL comps now at 
+1%, which compares to +3.4% and -1.4% per Consensus Metrix, respectively. 
Our updated operating profit dollar forecasts are $404MM total company (vs. 
$367MM consensus), DKS $364MM (Street ~in-line), and FL $40MM (vs. 
-$10MM consensus) with our view that promotions were rational YOY (see 
our tracker in Figures 11-14). Our 1Q26 EPS forecast is now $3.24 vs. $2.90 
prior and $2.87 per Consensus Metrix, while our estimates for the remainder 
of the year are largely unchanged. In our view, with footwear momentum 
continuing (see brand laterals in Figures 6-9) on top of ongoing sports/healthy 
living/casualization engagement, a stimulated consumer, and clean inventory 
(FL being the most noteworthy YOY), we are optimistic on the top and bottom 
lines. With a mid-to-high end consumer that remains healthy and the World 
Cup ahead, we think strong results are likely to be extrapolated, especially on 
the FL front where improved merchandising for BTS, higher heat product, 
better in-stocks, Fast Break remodels (see Figures 17-18), and easy topline and 
margin compares portend potentially much higher results than the current 
consensus going forward. Indeed, as noted from our meetings with DKS mgmt, 
including Chairman Ed Stack, at our April conference, confidence in the 
category remains high along with the ease of turnaround at FL and high 
visibility to better product allocations with vendors “fully behind fixing” FL. 
As a result, we are placing DKS on Positive Catalyst Watch.
•
ASO: Raising our 1Q26 SSS forecast to +3.2%, the high end of the 
company’s updated +2-3% range, with EPS ahead.  Based on our data and 
channel work, we believe ASO comped ~4% for most of the calendar year sans 
storm periods, but are assuming a moderation in April on the Easter shift, some 
tax stim spending pullforward, and less favorable weather than in Feb/March 
in its southern store footprint. As discussed in our note following our April

normally strong seller).  In the near term, we think investors are overly bearish and 
believe 2Q could come in better than expected, especially with the stock at the lower end 
of its range.  Beyond 2Q, however, the outlook is much more uncertain in light of its male-
dominant assortment and lower income consumer exposure.

Dick's Sporting Goods (Neutral; Price Target: $240.00)
Investment Thesis
DKS is executing at a high level, has strongly improved its relationships with vendors and 
is benefitting from casualization and healthy living trends. On the other hand, we worry 
about the footwear cycle slowing, while any potential softness in the core DKS business will 
make improving FL even more important in 2026 from an investment perspective.  This 
process may take time, especially with its lower income consumer exposure in the US and 
global retail footprint.  Hence, we maintain our Neutral rating.

Our Dec 26 price target of $240 (up from $228 prior) is based on ~15x our 2027 EPS 
estimate, which is in the range of its historical FY1 valuation, and equivalent to ~8x EV/
