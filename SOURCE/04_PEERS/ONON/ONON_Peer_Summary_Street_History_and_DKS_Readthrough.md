## 1. Why ONON Matters to DKS (facts from the notes only)

1. **On is a key growth vendor inside Dick's and Foot Locker doors, and the relationship is still early.** Barclays (pub 12 May 2026) lists "continued door growth into the key sporting goods retailers: Dick's Sporting Goods, Foot Locker (DKS), and JD Sports" as one of three pillars of its Overweight thesis. Bloomberg Intelligence (dated 06/16/25, reprinted in the 2026-02-20 file) wrote that On had "just 20% penetration among some of its key larger wholesale accounts -- Dick's, Foot Locker and JD" and planned to expand its wholesale door count ~5% to 11,300 in 2025. UBS (pub 12 May 2026 and 29 July 2026) relayed the 1Q26 call comment that On's products are "available at approximately 50% of the doors operated by its key wholesale partners, including Dick's Sporting Goods, Foot Locker, and JD Sports." BI (03/13/26) put it at 40-50% of key accounts' locations with "mid-single-digit door expansion annually."
2. **Investor Day (22 Sept 2026) made the sporting-goods channel an explicit 2029 growth lever.** JPMorgan (pub 22 Sept 2026): "The brand aims to deepen relationships with key accounts such as Dick's Sporting Goods, Foot Locker, and JD, and expand into new doors and new verticals. By 2029, On expects to be in c.75% of doors at key partners across all regions vs 50% today." UBS (pub 22 Sept 2026): key-partner door penetration went from ~20% (FY23) to ~50-60% (FY26E), with a path to ~75% long term; the ~75% ceiling "reflects its desire to remain selective, continuing to distribute only through premium distribution doors, even among its existing partners." Barclays (pub 23 Sept 2026): wholesale "should contribute more absolute incremental revenue than retail or e-commerce" through FY29. Needham (pub 23 Sept 2026): "select wholesale door expansion (focusing on specialty accounts and in select sporting goods chains i.e. Dick's, FootLocker, and JD Sports), albeit at a deliberate pace."
3. **On is a high-ASP, low-markdown vendor.** JPMorgan (pub 22 Sept 2026): "In the US, management noted that On has the highest ASP and the lowest discount levels at its wholesale partners." Needham (pub 23 Sept 2026): "the brand carries the highest ASP and lowest discount rate of any brand in the industry across many markets, including the U.S."; average footwear price rose from $145 to $170 over five years.
4. **The 2Q26 wholesale reset was concentrated in Americas wholesale (i.e., the DKS channel).** 2Q26 wholesale grew +12.7% cc (vs. +25.1% in 1Q26) and Americas +13.0% cc; management "deliberately reins in wholesale sell-in, particularly in the Americas, to protect full-price positioning" amid "heavy promotions across brands and softer sell-through in some everyday-running franchises in the Americas" (BI, 08/11/26). UBS (pub 12 Aug 2026) estimated 2Q26 Americas wholesale rose only mid-to-high single digits ex-FX while Americas DTC rose low-to-mid 20s. JPMorgan (pub 12 Aug 2026) models 3Q26 wholesale at +7% and Americas at +7% (the "low point").
5. **The Street links the US promotional backdrop directly to DKS/Foot Locker results.** Needham (pub 18 Sept 2026): "DKS reported a disappointing comp decline of almost 4% at their Foot Locker segment (vs. a slight increase in Q1) and they lowered FY Foot Locker comp guidance to a range of flat-to-down-2% vs. an original range of up-1%-to-3%. Additionally, they lowered FY EBIT margin guidance by ~100bps (including a 50bps reduction at the core Dick's banner and larger cut at Foot Locker), attributed to the highly promotional environment ... JD Sports reported Q2 like-for-like sales in North America declined almost 7%." Needham attributes the promotional pressure to Nike over-shipping (~$1.5bn incremental wholesale sell-in LTM, ~$3bn at retail).
6. **Early sign of US wholesale re-acceleration.** Barclays (pub 23 Sept 2026): management noted a "meaningful uptick" in U.S. wholesale sell-out momentum versus 2Q26; 3Q26 cc sales growth guided to ~17%. UBS (pub 12 Aug 2026): Cloudsurfer 3 rolls to run specialty in October with 3-month exclusivity before a broader January launch; all everyday-running franchises refresh within ~14 months.
7. **Run specialty is small for On in the US.** UBS (pub 29 July 2026): "only ~5% of On's running footwear product in the US is sold in run specialty"; run specialty order books were up >25% y/y (1Q26 call). This implies the large sporting-goods/mall chains, not run specialty, carry the bulk of On's US wholesale volume.
8. **The one bear-leaning voice ties its ONON doubt directly to DKS.** Williams Trading (Sept 25, 2026; PT $27 -> $31, after cutting $32 -> $27 on Sept 16 and insisting the Americas, >50% of revenue, be secured before new categories) rates DKS Hold with a $130 PT and says the risk to On's 2029 plan "comes from the plan to expand On's presence at Foot Locker (DKS-Hold-$130 PT) and JD Sports ... from 50% of their store bases to 75%, when On's performance has been inconsistent in those retailers to this point"; its Aug 6, 2026 checks found athletic-specialty sell-in up but sell-through rates not, i.e., a "push" rather than "pull" model.
9. **Channel mix is shifting toward DTC but wholesale remains the larger absolute contributor.** DTC reached 45.7% of sales in 2Q26, a Q2 record (Ameriprise, Aug 17 2026); target 50% by FY29 vs ~45% FY26 (JPM/Barclays/Needham, Sept 2026); Bloomberg consensus has wholesale at 54.5% of FY26E revenue falling to 50.4% by FY29E (Bloomberg FA, 10/04/2026).

### 7.1 2Q26 first-ever miss and guidance cut (11 Aug 2026)

### 7.2 Wholesale reset
- Mechanism: deliberate sell-in restraint in Americas wholesale "to ensure new products launch into a clean marketplace" (UBS 24 June); "very much linked to the wholesale actions" (Ameriprise 17 Aug); Q3 more impacted than Q4 (Needham 11 Aug); additional wholesale actions in 3Q (UBS 12 Aug).
- Cause: "highly promotional" US marketplace, Nike inventory clearance (UBS 11-12 Aug; Needham 18 Sept), softer sell-through of mature everyday-running franchises (Cloudsurfer, Cloudflow) ahead of refresh (Ameriprise 17 Aug). Williams Trading (6 Aug, pre-print) had flagged the same sell-in/sell-through gap in the athletic-specialty channel and trimmed estimates.
- Street model impact: Barclays FY26 wholesale growth 14.4% -> 10.2% -> 9.7%; JPM 3Q26 wholesale +7%; UBS 3Q26 wholesale +11.5% cc; Bloomberg cons 3Q26E wholesale +8.3% cc.
- Recovery markers: Cloudsurfer 3 to run specialty October (3-month exclusive), broad January; S/S27 order book strong (JPM 12 Aug); "meaningful uptick" in US wholesale sell-out vs 2Q (Barclays/Needham 23 Sept); 4Q26E wholesale +12.1% cc and 2027E mid-teens (Bloomberg cons).

Needham (18 Sept): normalizing ~200bps tariff headwind and ~100bps DTC mix, 2Q26 underlying GM expansion ~500bps.
