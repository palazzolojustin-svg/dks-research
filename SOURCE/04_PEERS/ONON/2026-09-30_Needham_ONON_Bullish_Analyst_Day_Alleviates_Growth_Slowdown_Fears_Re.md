Bullish Analyst Day Alleviates Growth-Slowdown
Fears, Reiterate Buy
OUR MAIN TAKEAWAY
Amid a challenging athleticwear environment, ONON provided a compelling multi-year
roadmap, with 3-year financial targets that exceeded Street expectations (high-teens revenue
CAGR, while we think the buyside was expecting low- or mid-teens). Heading into the event,
we were worried that even if they provided an optimistic outlook, investors would view it
skeptically, but management seems to have threaded the needle nicely, and offered an outlook
that is both bullish and believable. Now, execution will be key in order to clear the high bar
they've set for themselves. Given the new multi-year framework from which to work, we are
raising our FY27/28 EPS forecasts to CHF1.63/2.07 (from CHF1.50/1.70) and our price target
moves to $40 (from $37).
TOM'S TAKEAWAYS
Tom’s Takeaway #1: FY29 Targets Alleviate Investor Growth Concerns. For FY29, the company
expects at least 5.6 billion CHF in revenue (high-teens constant-FX CAGR) and an adjusted EBITDA
margin of at least 22% (up 200+ bps vs. FY26), driven by expense leverage and steady gross margin.
Tom's Takeaway #2: Core Categories to Drive Most Of The Growth, In Addition To New
Categories. Of the ~2 billion CHF of revenue ONON plans to add by FY29, ~75% of it will come from
continued expansion in core pillars Run, Sneakers (Lifestyle), and Apparel. In addition, ONON will be
expanding into Golf and Global Football over the medium-term, supported by the announcement late
last week that the company will be signing football superstar Kylian Mbappe.
Tom’s Takeaway #3: Solid Growth Across Regions/Channels. ONON expects 3-year CAGRs of low-
to-mid-teens in the Americas, high-teens to low-20s in EMEA, and mid-to-high 20s in APAC. The
Americas target is encouraging, as the guidance cut last month was driven by U.S. wholesale (stoking
fears that the brand was reaching saturation). ONON believes DTC will become 50% of the mix by FY29
(vs. 45% today), implying 20%+ growth vs. wholesale growth in the teens. DTC should be aided by store
growth, with plans to more-than-double the fleet in 3 years (180 stores vs. ~80 today). In wholesale,
door penetration at strategic accounts stands at ~50%, with plans to get to ~75% by FY29.
Tom’s Takeaway #4: Encouraging Commentary On Q3 Demand. ONON reiterated FY26 sales/
EBITDA guidance, and specified that they expect Q3 constant-FX sales growth of ~17% (roughly in
line with consensus). Recall, management lowered 2H/FY revenue guidance last month due to slower
sell-throughs and reduced sell-in to the highly-promotional U.S. wholesale channel. However, ONON
encouragingly called out "a meaningful uptick in sellout momentum in U.S. wholesale vs. Q2".
Tom's Takeaway #5: New Repurchase Program Allows ONON To Put Ample Cash Balance To
Work. ONON announced a $1 billion repurchase authorization over the next 3 years, which can boost
the EPS algo further. ONON ended Q2 with ~$1.5 billion of cash.

PRODUCT INNOVATION, KEY CATEGORY GROWTH EXPECTED TO SUPPORT AMBITIOUS
FY29 TARGETS
Management began the Investor Day by noting that it is on track to exceed the goals/targets laid out previously at the company's 2023
Investor Day (at least 26% constant-FX revenue CAGR, >60% gross margins, >18% adjusted EBITDA margins). For FY29, the company
expects 1) at least 5.6 billion CHF in revenue, which amounts to a high-teens growth CAGR, 2) gross margins of at least 65%, and 3) an
adjusted EBITDA margin of at least 22% (which equates to an adjusted EBITDA growth CAGR of 20% through FY29). By region, this equates
to low-to-mid-teens growth in the Americas, high-teens to low-20s growth in EMEA, and mid-to-high 20s growth in APAC (with China
responsible for 10% of total company sales).

At the core of ONON's strategy is continued product innovation in Running. Throughout the presentation, management referenced
product innovation as a key strength, noting its success in introducing new products at the leading edge of the industry over the years,
including the CloudMonster franchise, LightSpray, etc. More recently, ONON introduced the CloudSurfer 3 (which utilizes new Surreal
Superfoam technolog), which has already launched with select run specialty channels, and is expected to rollout more broadly in October.

The company has expanded its running selection over the past few years and now has products/franchises in many (if not all) running
subcategories (long distance, sprint, trail, etc.). It plans to continuously refresh/innovate within each of these subcategories over the next
few years, and continue to drive "premiumization" (noting that their average footwear price has risen from $145 to $170 over the past
5 years).
In addition to Running, ONON intends to build on its (newer) key categories of Sneaker (aka Lifestyle) and Apparel. In Sneaker,
ONON is increasing its presence through various collaborations, including the marketing campaign with actress Zendaya and a recent
collaboration with Kith for a new lifestyle sneaker. While lifestyle remains underpenetrated particularly in the U.S., management noted
that the brand ahs become a popular lifestyle staple with Gen Z in Europe. Growing apparel has been a key focus for management over the
past few years, and the company has introduced various product innovations in the category over the past few years, including Tritech,
Thermotec, and Sensortec, with Sensortec in particular helping to bring more female customers into the brand (management noted that
leading with the female consumer in apparel is a key component of their growth strategy).
Overall, ONON is expecting the Run, Sneaker, and Apparel categories will drive more than 75% of total sales growth through FY29.
MARKETING TO REMAIN A KEY INVESTMENT
The company has positioned its brand around the "Movement Class", i.e. consumers who prioritize and invest in health and fitness. It
noted recent successes in its back-to-school campaign. In addition to product innovation, elevated marketing and brand storytelling is
expected to be a key area of investment over the next few years in order to drive faster brand adoption and revenue growth.
FOOTBALL (AKA SOCCER) AND GOLF TO SUPPORT LONG-TERM GROWTH AMBITIONS
The company announced it will be entering into the large global football (soccer) and golf markets over the next few years, piggybacking
off of its announcement last week to sign soccer superstar Kylian Mbappe (which garnered a strong 8 billion earned media impressions).
However, management noted that this is more of a medium-to-longer term strategy, and that it is expected to have modest near-term
financial implications. Football is expected to build gradually, beginning with limited product drops in 2027 before broader in-line product
becomes available in 2028, with golf framed similarly. The expansion into new verticals is also expected to accelerate brand awareness,
which is currently 30%, though management intends to reach 50% brand awareness by FY29.
DTC TO BECOME A BIGGER PART OF REVENUE MIX
Underlying the company's growth assumptions is an additional 100 stores expected to open by FY29, which would bring ONON's total
store count to ~180 (from 80 currently and 22 in 2023). ONON will continue to be selective as to where it opens its stores, with its focus
primarily on "key global cities", such as New York, Tokyo, Paris, Stockholm, Copenhagen, etc. The company has continued to evolve its
store format, focusing on 1) a larger, more premium store format and 2) a greater expression of apparel (50% increase in apparel in stores
planned vs. the existing fleet). Interestingly, management noted that the larger store format is actually more productive on a per-store
basis than smaller formats. ONON's average store productivity has increased by 1.5x since 2023, and footwear productivity now sits well
above luxury and premium peers/benchmarks (as such, the company does not have additional increases in store productivity baked into
its growth plan assumptions). However, apparel productivity is currently half of that of footwear, and management believes there is ample
opportunity to grow this key metric. Management views the store rollout as a necessary investment (store payback is ~2 years, with retail-
level profitability ahead of ONON overall) to accelerate brand awareness to its 50% target.
The company expects DTC will become ~50% of total revenue by FY29 (vs. ~42% in FY25), driven by the aforementioned store expansion,
in addition to continued growth in digital.
In wholesale, ONON is expecting to both grow/deepen its existing partnerships (bring more product to its existing accounts) in addition
to select wholesale door expansion (focusing on specialty accounts and in select sporting goods chains i.e. Dick's, FootLocker, and JD
Sports), albeit at a deliberate pace, as to not overextend themselves. They have grown from 20% of doors in 2023 to ~50% today, with a goal
of ~75% in FY29. This also includes working with partners to elevate brand presentation in-stores and the overall customer experience.
Currently, the brand carries the highest ASP and lowest discount rate of any brand in the industry across many markets, including the U.S.

MARGIN OUTLOOK REMAINS STRONG DESPITE INVESTMENT PLANS
ONON's current FY26 guidance calls for gross margins of at least 65%. For FY29, they expect gross margins to remain at a similar level,
with leverage in channel mix (higher DTC penetration) and continued operational efficiencies offset by ongoing product investment and
category mix shifts, with ONON choosing to hold gross margins roughly steady and use additional COGS savings to reinvest in product
innovation efforts. Thus, the AEBITDA target of 22%+ by FY29 is driven by SG&A leverage, particularly around lower distribution and
G&A costs, partially offset by continue investment in marketing and selling costs (i.e. store buildout).
CAPITAL ALLOCATION STRATEGY REMAINS UNCHANGED
As noted in the press release yesterday, ONON's board authorized a new $1 billion share repurchase program. Overall capital allocation
priorities remain unchanged: 1) continued reinvestment into the business, 2) maintaining a strong balance sheet, and 3) returning capital
via buybacks.
Separately, the company announced that they will be moving to USD reporting as early as 2027, a change intended to better align reported
financials with the company's underlying currency mix.

VALUATION (PRICE TARGET: $40.00)
Our 12-month price target of $40 is based on 16x FY28E EPS of 2.07 CHF, or ~$2.50 when converting the EPS forecast to USD (the share
price is in USD, but the P&L is in CHF).
POTENTIAL UPSIDE DRIVERS
Potential upside drivers include:
■
The company could gain market share and wholesale shelf space faster than we expect, given the momentum at the brand.
■
The company has major growth opportunities in Asia and apparel, potentially presenting long runways for growth.
■
If ONON can continue generating strong full-price sell-throughs, margins could exceed our expectations given the possible leverage
on sales growth.
RISKS TO TARGET
ONON
Key risks to our bullish thesis on this stock include:
■
The company operates in a highly-competitive athletic footwear market, where trends can change very rapidly.
■
If the company fails to generate compelling product innovations, consumer interest could wane and growth could slow.
■
The company's margins are among the best in the industry due to their ability to sell predominantly at full price. If the promotional
retail environment starts to affect them, they may need to discount more, driving potential margin degradation.
