1. On’s Wholesale Reset Puts US Demand at Fore: Earnings Outlook
(Bloomberg Intelligence) -- Post-2Q Earnings Outlook: On's sales growth may slow in 3Q before
improving in 4Q as the company deliberately reins in wholesale sell-in, particularly in the Americas, to
protect full-price positioning and clear the channel ahead of a major running-product refresh. In 2Q,
FX-neutral sales rose 21.6%, buoyed by 34.3% DTC growth, while wholesale slowed to 12.7% amid heavy
promotions across brands and softer sell-through in some everyday-running franchises in the
Americas. With new products coming and a broader refresh of all everyday-running franchises over
the next 14 months, we'll be watching US wholesale trends closely for evidence that the softness is
transitory and underlying demand remains intact.
Gross margin could be a key offset to slower near-term sales growth after expanding 390 bps to 65.4%
in 2Q, driven by higher DTC mix, full-price execution and operating efficiencies despite higher US
tariffs. (08/11/26)
