Our Take: On just reported solid Q1’26 results and raised its FY26 profitability 
guidance, while keeping the topline outlook intact, noting geo-political 
uncertainties. Sales were up a very solid 26% ex-FX, in line with Bloomberg 
consensus (also at 26%), driven by strong performance in the DTC channel (+29% 
vs cons. +30%), while wholesale was also up 25% (vs cons. +24%). By region, 
growth was once again led by APAC +61% (vs cons. +62%), followed by EMEA, 
which came in ahead of consensus at +26% (vs. cons +20%), while the Americas 
performance missed consensus, up 17% vs cons +18.5%. Thanks to the strong top-
line momentum and a solid beat on gross margin (+430bps yoy to 64.2% vs 61.4% 
BBG consensus), supported by “full-price discipline and strong operational 
execution”, adj. EBITDA grew 45% to CHF174m, leading to an adj. EBITDA 
margin up +450bps yoy to 21.0% (vs cons. +17.8%). On the back of such a strong 
print and profitability guidance raise, we expect LSD% upgrades to estimates and 
a positive share price reaction first thing today, given the stock has been under 
pressure YTD (-27%).
•
Noteworthy Areas: 1) Q1 Group sales were +26% ex-FX/14.5% yoy 
reported to CHF832m, coming just a tad above consensus (Bloomberg cons. 
CHF825m); 2) Both distribution channels remained strong, with DTC 
+29% ex-FX (vs. cons. +30%), outperforming Wholesale +25% ex-FX (cons 
+24%); 3) By region, APAC remains the fastest growing, up 61% ex-FX (vs. 
cons. +62%), driven by “standout momentum” in China and South Korea. 
Performance in EMEA came in above consensus at +26% (vs. cons. +20%), 
while Americas was a small miss, up 17% (vs cons +18.5%); 4) Gross 
margin was very strong up 430bps yoy to 64.2%,  a solid beat to consensus of 
61.4%, driven by full-price discipline and strong operational execution, 
partially offset by “meaningful headwinds” from higher US tariffs; 5) Adj. 
EBITDA grew 45% to CHF174m and beat consensus by 22% (CHF143m), 
implying margin +450bps yoy to 21.0% (vs. cons. 17.8%); 6) Inventory was 
2% yoy, despite the solid topline growth.
•
Outlook & Guidance: For FY26, On has raised its profitability guidance, 
while re-iterating its net sales growth outlook of “at least” +23% ex-FX 
(absolute net sales of “at least” CHF3.51bn vs CHF3.44bn prior and current 
BBG cons CHF3.54bn). Management now expect Gross Margin of at least 
64.5% (vs. at least 63.0% prior; cons at 63.1%), up at least 170bps yoy, and an 
adjusted EBITDA margin of 19.5-20.0% (vs. 18.5-19.0% prior; cons. 18.9%), 
implying an adj. EBITDA at mid point of around CHF679m (BBG cons at 
CHF666m).
•
Likely changes to consensus: Given the Q1 beat and the FY26 profitability 
guidance raise, we expect LSD% upgrades to estimates.
•
Valuation: On is trading at 15.2x P/E 2y fwd, in-line with the current sector 
average (at 14.9x, average including adidas, Nike, lulu, Asics, and Deckers)
