Our Take: This morning, ahead of its Investor Day presentation in Zurich, On 
issued three-year targets (to FY29) in which it targets a high-teens constant 
currency net sales CAGR through 2029 (above the mid-teens buyside bar) and 
corresponding absolute net sales reaching at least CHF5.6bn in 2029 (2% above 
BBG cons of CHF5.5bn). It also targets a Gross Margin of 65%+ throughout the 
2026-2029 period and an adjusted EBITDA margin of 22%+ by 2029 (vs cons and 
JPMe 21%), implying an EBITDA growth CAGR of 20%+.  As widely anticipated, 
the company also announced an inaugural share repurchase of up to $1bn (c.11% 
of current market cap) through the end of 2029. In addition, On confirmed its FY26 
guidance this morning, while guiding to a Q3 cFX net sales growth of “around 
17%” (slightly lower than cons /JPMe of18%). Given the weak sentiment ahead of 
the Investor Day, the higher than expected mid-term growth CAGR, and a 
meaningful share buyback plan, we expect shares react positively upon market 
open.  More details that will be provided in the presentations later (webcast 
available from 8am ET/2pm CET) may also be key. 
•
Noteworthy Areas: Beyond the headline financial targets, On also expects 
key growth pillars, Run, Sneaker, and Apparel to drive “outsized contribution”, 
further supported by the entry into new sports categories, Football and Golf. No 
financial targets are shared with regard to these new categories and we expect 
to hear more information on how management plans to drive these categories 
in the presentations today.
•
Outlook & Guidance: By FY29, the company expects sales to increase by 
high-teens CAGR relative to FY26 (BBG: 19%/17%/13% cFX yoy for FY27/
FY28/FY29 respectively), while also exceeding a 65% gross margin (BBG 
cons 65.5%) and a 22% adj. EBITDA margin by 2029 (BBG cons. 21%). 
•
Likely changes to consensus: The newly issued FY29 targets imply c.6% 
upgrade to FY29 cons. adj EBITDA.
•
Valuation: On is trading on 12x P/E 2Y fwd, a 57% discount to its 3Y average 
of 27x.
•
Conference Call Details: A webcast of the presentations and Q&A session, 
starting at 8am ET/2pm CET, can be accessed here.

Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks
On Holding (Overweight; Price Target: $41.00)
Investment Thesis
On is a premium Swiss sports brand founded in 2010 that primarily sells footwear (>95% 
of sales). On operates in an attractive industry with substantial barriers to entry and high 
growth prospects underpinned by health and casualisation tailwinds. On has successfully 
broken into the category with a distinct premium product rooted in performance and 
technology. Importantly, we think On is striking a balance between its performance roots 
(crucial for credibility and in the sportswear space) and lifestyle (paramount for growth). 
Recent debates show the changing narrative of On from an "early stage, high growth, 
premium brand" to a "somewhat maturing running shoe brand that needs to find its next leg 
of growth". In the mid to long term, we continue to expect a large growth runway from 
1) apparel expansion, 2) international expansion, notably in Asia, and 3) brand 
building which will allow On to transform from a niche running brand to a multi-category 
lifestyle brand. We rate On Overweight.
Valuation
Our Dec-27 PT is based on a three-stage DCF (model forecasts + mid-term + fade to 
terminal) and assumes 4% terminal growth, 15.5% terminal EBIT margin and a 10.3% 
WACC. 
Risks to Rating and Price Target
•
Highly competitive landscape: On is competing with notable players in the sportswear 
category including the likes of Nike, adidas, Hoka and Brooks. 
•
Success of new launches: On has significant newness in its pipeline and is expected to 
refresh everyday running franchises with new foams and technologies. On’s growth in 
2027 and beyond hinges on whether such newness will be well received. 
•
Fashion risk: Many of On’s shoes have a similar look and feel to them, which leads to 
a risk that this style may go out of fashion.
•
Changes in discretionary spending: The premium footwear category remains somewhat 
discretionary in nature.
•
Ambassadors: Key partners, including co-entrepreneurs Roger Federer, Zendaya, and 
other athletes. If On were to lose its celebrity endorsers, in particular Federer, the brand 
and its business could be adversely impacted.
