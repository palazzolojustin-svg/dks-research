WILLIAMS TRADING
ON Holdings(ONON)
Footwear&Apparel
August 6, 2026
Pushing Too Hard with Too Much.Trimming Ests.
While we do not foresee a 2Q26 miss when earnings are released on Tuesday, August
11th, Bmo, we continue to witness a push model (rather than a pull model) continuing
as oN attempts to gain more shelf space in order to attract young influential consumers.
Such an approach is likely to lead to pressure on sale and margin in the not so distant
future. Strong sell in does not necessarily lead to strong sell-through rates. We expect
that the outlook for APAc will remain robust, but the outlook for the balance of the world
will be mostly tempered. Our checks indicate that, while sales within the athletic specialty
channel is up. the sell though rates are not. Also, there have been some small tests
within city specialty retail, which have not gone well, but oN has approached other city
specialty retailers, offering them the brand (pushing not pulling).
It has become more evident to us that there are too many styles at the same or similar
price points. Those styles appear to have been created to segment performance and
lifestyle product. For example, both the Cloud 6, a lifestyle shoe, and the Cloud Runner
point running shoe. The Cloud 6, on the other hand (foot), is a best-selling lifestyle shoe.
but does not have any of the performance attributes of the Cloud Runner. Why not put
a Cloud 6 upper on the Cloud Runner. and focus on one franchise rather than 2? At
the high end, in partnership with Loewe, ON offers the Cloudtilt in a High top at s650.
compared to the inline walking shoe that retails for s17o. Also. there is a Loewe version
of the high performance Lightspray Cloudmonster (s99o), compared to the inline shoe
that retails for s28o. The Loewe Cloudmonster has sold out on the Loewe website.
