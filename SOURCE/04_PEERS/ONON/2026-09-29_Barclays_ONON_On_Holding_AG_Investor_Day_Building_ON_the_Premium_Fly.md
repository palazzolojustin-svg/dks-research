On Holding AG
Investor Day: Building ON the
Premium Flywheel
ONON hosted its IR Day on 09/21/26 and 09/22/26 in Zurich.
Management outlined its FY26-FY29 growth algorithm, seeing
a path to at least CHF5.6bn in sales by FY29, with 65%+ GM
and 22%+ adj. EBITDA margin, fueled by high-teens CC sales
CAGR and above 20% adjusted EBITDA CAGR.
We recently attended ONON's Investor Day in Zurich, Switzerland and met with members of the
executive and senior management team, as well as experienced the breadth and depth of its
innovation platform and technologies. Please see the full presenter list below. Please contact us
to discuss any of the topics below in more detail.
Building ON the Premium Flywheel. ONON's Investor Day reinforced our view that ONON
remains one of the best global growth stories in our coverage, with an increasingly
differentiated premium playbook that combines breakthrough innovation, athlete-led
performance credibility, full-price discipline, and highly curated distribution to drive a powerful
self-reinforcing growth model – the premium flywheel. Most impressive was the breadth and
depth of the innovation pipeline from LightSpray and next-generation foams to new category
expansion into football and golf – alongside substantial white space in apparel, sneakers, retail,
and global brand awareness. Importantly, the growth algorithm is increasingly translating into
superior economics: ONON is on track to exceed each of the financial commitments laid out at
its 2023 Investor Day and now targets high-teens constant-currency sales growth through 2029,
65%+ gross margin and 22%+ adjusted EBITDA margin, implying above 20% EBITDA CAGR as the
bottom line grows faster than sales. With awareness still just 30% globally, significant runway in
premium distribution and owned retail, and continued reinvestment in product, people and
innovation, we came away incrementally positive on the durability of ONON's growth trajectory
and confident in our Overweight thesis. At current valuation levels, we believe the risk/reward is
highly attractive for investors with multi-year, long term investment horizons. In reaction to
ONON's LRP and showcase of innovation, management, and strategy, shares were up 7.5% vs
the S&P 500 flat.
Innovation is the core of profitable growth. At ONON's Investor Day, the company outlined
how it plans to drive the next evolution of its innovation journey, and its path toward driving
faster earnings growth than sales growth – in our view highlighting the company's status as a
high-quality compounder with durable earnings power. Management detailed its key growth
levers, including outsized contributions from run, sneaker, and apparel; continued expansion
across regions and channels; and new entries into football and golf. In our view, ONON remains
one of the leading innovators in global sportswear, with its deepest-ever running innovation

5 Key Takeaways
Takeaway #1: ONON entering its next phase of 'premium' growth, powered by: 1) run, 2)
sneaker, and 3) apparel. Management expects these three target areas to contribute more
than 75% of ONON's absolute sales growth through FY29, with each contributing an
approximately equal amount to revenue growth. Run remains the foundation of ONON's
performance brand image and, as such, receives the largest share of R&D investment, which
management believes drives healthy innovation that can then be applied to other categories
(most notably sneaker, which is the second pillar). Apparel represents the third pillar, with
management targeting a tripling of the business through FY29, supported by stronger female
engagement, owned retail expansion, and broader toe-to-head assortments. Encouragingly,
management noted that the growth outlook remains "multidimensional", with additional key
levers including: training, tennis, golf, outdoor, and young movers (kids) providing additional
resilience. One of the key points that we took away from the Investor Day was that ONON's high-
teens CC sales growth target is not dependent on any single product, geography, or channel –
but instead reflects a broader portfolio of established and emerging growth engines.
Takeaway #2: Innovation cycle, led by SURREAL and LightSpray, is a key growth lever.
Management noted that every major running franchise will receive a next-generation update,
with the transition expected to be substantially completed by mid-2027. Additionally,
Cloudsurfer 3 introduces SURREAL superfoam, which management quantified as 15% lighter,
20% softer, and 15% more explosive than Helion, ONON's existing foam technology, while the
broader portfolio will be organized around four distinct sensations: support, soft, bounce, and
fast. Encouragingly, management noted that early Cloudmonster 3 demand has skewed
meaningfully toward higher-priced Hyper and LightSpray versions, demonstrating consumer
willingness to pay for innovation. Longer-term, LightSpray is expected to reach approximately
10% of footwear sales, with applications planned across franchises and early pilots underway in
lifestyle. More broadly, we believe ONON's continued investment in performance innovation
should reinforce technical credibility, support full-price selling, and provide the foundation for
continued growth across both performance and lifestyle.
Takeaway #3: Growth by channel: DTC targeted to reach 50% by 2029; wholesale to remain
the largest growth contributor. Management expects DTC mix to increase from approximately
45% in FY26 to 50% by FY29, although management emphasized that the mix is an outcome
rather than a target. The store base should expand from approximately 80 stores today to 180
by FY29, with cash paybacks of approximately two years and profitability comfortably above the
company's adj. EBITDA margin. Encouragingly, management noted that larger stores generate
higher productivity, while apparel productivity is expected to more than double through FY29.
Wholesale remains central to the growth algorithm and should contribute more absolute
incremental revenue than retail or e-commerce. We note that aggregate key-account door
penetration is expected to increase from approximately 50% today to around 75% by FY29.
Existing-door growth should benefit from broader vertical representation, additional shelf
space, elevated brand presentation, and deeper collaboration with strategic partners. Notably,
shop-in-shop upgrades have generated triple-digit sell-through growth in certain doors, while
omnichannel customers generate up to 4.5x the lifetime value of single-channel customers. We
left the event impressed by the continued white space across both owned and partner channels.
Takeaway #4: International white space opportunity remains meaningful. Management
expects the Americas to grow low-to-mid teens through FY29; Europe, the Middle East, and
Africa to grow high-teens to low-20s; and Asia-Pacific to grow mid-to-high 20s. Encouragingly,
management believes Europe, the Middle East, and Africa may exceed current investor
expectations, as ONON's market share across Southern Europe, Eastern Europe, Belgium, the
Netherlands, Luxembourg, and Scandinavia remains approximately half its penetration in

Proprietary PRISM Curve: ONON in "HUNT" Mode
From a fundamental perspective, we use our proprietary PRISM cycle as a leading indicator of
potential sales inflection (i.e., either from positive to negative or negative to positive). Our
PRISM Curve attempts to look for fundamental changes in the business model of a company,
driven by proactive management decisions to stabilize margins (e.g., purge inventory, cut costs,
share best practices, invest in product and brand). Figure 1 and Figure 2 below depict our model
graphically and identify where we would theoretically expect to see sales and margin changes
along the business model curve.
The HUNT Phase (Strategically Offensive)
During the HUNT Phase, management's strategy is on the offense. They are in “HUNT” mode
and are in control of taking market share. Management is focused on driving company-specific
strategies forward, building on their own brand equity and positioning, rather than focusing
primarily on competitors or the environment around them. At this point, retailers are usually
beating sales and margin expectations and may begin to grow earnings faster than total sales.
Investors would want to hold and accumulate shares of a company in Region C in Figure 2, the
area of maximum potential stock appreciation. This phase may take place over multiple years
as the company enters its sweet spot.

Tariff Exposure
2Q26 Tariff Commentary. As of 6/30/26, ONON's total IEEPA tariffs paid were CHF55.6mn, of
which CHF52.7mn was submitted through the U.S. Customs refund processing. The company
has not recognized any of these potential refund amounts and will recognize refunds when
realization of these amounts is deemed virtually certain. As of 6/30/26, the majority of the
potential refund relates to inventory already sold. As of 8/11/26, ONON has received
approximately CHF27.9mn in IEEPA tariff refunds. This amount will be recognized in 3Q26. In
2Q26, GM expanded +385bps y/y to 65.4%, driven by strong DTC momentum, operating
efficiencies, and favorable freight/FX which more than offset higher U.S. tariffs. 2Q26 results and
updated FY26 GM guidance of at least 65% (raised from prior guidance of at least 64.5%)
exclude the potential benefit from any tariff refunds, which management anticipates are likely
to come throughout 2H26 and expects to begin recognizing in 3Q26. Likewise, the company's
reiterated adj. EBITDA margin guidance of 19.5% to 20.0% does not incorporate any benefit
from future tariff refunds.
