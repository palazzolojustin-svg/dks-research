OUR CALL
DECK posted strong fiscal 4Q26 results, and issued achievable FY27 guidance given momentum
in the two major brands. Our FY27/28 EPS estimates move marginally lower as we were slightly
above newly-issued guidance (now $7.45/$8.30 vs. $7.48/$8.36 prior), but given DECK's history
of beating-and-raising, we wouldn't be surprised if EPS ended meaningfully higher by fiscal
yearned (e.g. FY27 at $8.00+). For the quarter itself, revenue growth of 10% was well above
implied guidance of LSD-MSD and EPS of $0.96 was well above guidance $0.72-$0.77. For FY27,
the company expects revenue growth of 7%-8% and EPS of $7.30-$7.45, both of which were in
line with Street. The company also introduced a bullish multi-year framework through FY30,
calling for HSD annual revenue growth and low-double-digit EPS growth.
TOM'S TAKEAWAYS
Tom's Takeaway #1: New Multi-Year Framework Underscores Runway of Growth. DECK
provided their first multi-year algo in many years (since their last Investor Day in 2017), calling for HSD
annual revenue growth from FY27-FY30. This includes UGG +MSD annually and Hoka growing at a
LDD CAGR. We think investors have generally been more cautious on both brands, with the buyside
fretting over Hoka potentially becoming a single-digit grower and UGG "hitting a wall" after several
strong years in a row, so we're encouraged by the outlook. The outlook also assumes that margins hold
steady in the low-20's, and they generate accretion via buyback, yielding a LDD EPS CAGR FY28-FY30.
This implies FY30 EPS in the $10.00-$10.50 range.
Tom’s Takeaway #2: Hoka Growth Remains Strong. Hoka was up 15% in Q4, vs. guidance for
13%-14% growth. Growth was once again led by the DTC channel, which was up 18%, while the
wholesale channel was up 13%. Brand awareness has risen by 10 points YoY, now 60% domestically and
40% in key international markets. Interestingly, management noted that Hoka now has 6 franchises
of $100+ million revenue, and 3 more that are approaching that mark, which suggests that the brand
is more diversified than some skeptics think (many doubters believe Hoka is essentially a "two trick
pony", with the Bondi and Clifton). Management expects LDD revenue growth this year, including tests
of new distribution in the U.S. and overseas.
Tom’s Takeaway #3: Timing-Related Hoka Headwinds in Q1. For Q1, Hoka is expected to grow just
+HSD, due to 1) lapping a wholesale timing shift that favorably impacted 1Q26, 2) some APAC product
being delayed to Q2, and 3) reduced shipments of the Clifton, as they prepare for a new Clifton launch
in July.
Tom’s Takeaway #4: Solid Growth From UGG. Ugg was up 9% in Q4, which was well above guidance
for flattish growth, driven by extended selling of Fall product, though the brand also continues to gain
traction with new all-weather products and with male consumers. For FY27, they expect the brand to
grow +MSD, balanced across DTC and wholesale.

At a Glance
OUR INVESTMENT THESIS
We think DECK is one of the highest-quality companies in our coverage, with an impressive multi-year track record of performance, two
of the strongest brands in the industry (Hoka and UGG), a strong management team and a fortress balance sheet.
BULL CASE ASSUMPTIONS
With the benefit of a slew of new products,
as well as channel fill in the wholesale
channel, Hoka growth could re accelerate
to the 15%-20% range the next few
years. Additionally, given international
momentum and new product launches
domestically, UGG could generate MSD-
HSD revenue growth. If margins are
sustained in the low-20's, then FY28 EPS
could be ~$9.50 and the share price could
reach ~$190 (20x P/E on FY27E EPS ).
BASE CASE ASSUMPTIONS
We expect FY27 EPS to come in at the
high end of guidance, We expect EPS to
grow +DD in FY28, resulting in EPS power
of $8.00-$8.50 in fiscal 2028 (Mar'28). Our
$138 price target is based on a 16x-17x P/E
on FY28 EPS of ~$8.30.
BEAR CASE ASSUMPTIONS
Against difficult compares and a tough
macro environment, UGG growth could
flat-line, while Hoka could slow to
single-digit growth (mainly driven by
new distribution). Tariffs plus normalized
discounting levels could push margins
down to the high-teens. If so, EPS growth
would slow dramatically, and FY28 EPS
could be $7.00, and the share price could
fall to ~$90 (low-teens P/E on FY27E EPS).
COMPANY DESCRIPTION
DECK owns a portfolio of global footwear brands, led by the UGG and Hoka brands.

VALUATION (PRICE TARGET: $138.00)
Our $138 price target is based on a 16x-17x P/E on FY28 EPS of $8.30.
POTENTIAL UPSIDE DRIVERS
The company could do better than we expect if:
■
Hoka gains brand awareness and retailer shelf space faster than we expect.
■
UGG is able to continue rolling out successful new products.
■
Recent levels of full-price selling are maintained.
■
The company accelerates the pace of share repurchases.
RISKS TO TARGET
DECK
Potential downside risks include:
■
Hoka operates in a highly competitive athletic footwear market.
■
Hoka could run out of new channels of distribution, slowing potential growth.
■
UGG growth could stall if new product introductions fail to resonate with consumers.
■
Margins could come under pressure if growth slows and more discounting/markdowns are necessary.
