EPS of $0.94 coming in ahead vs. consensus of $0.87, driven by better GM as a result of strong
full-price selling and more favorable mix. These results highlight continued brand momentum,
healthy demand trends, and encouraging contributions from newer product franchises (Otzo

Tariff Exposure Update. FY1Q27 GM expanded +60bps y/y despite a 150bps tariff headwind,
which was entirely offset by favorable channel and product mix, better full-price selling,
favorable FX, and better management of product closeouts. Management noted that FY1Q27 is
expected to represent the largest tariff-related headwind of FY27, as prior-year inventory sold in
the quarter did not carry tariffs and the company will begin lapping tariff-impacted periods as
the year progresses. FY2Q27 guidance calls for GM to be down due to tariffs and higher freight
costs. FY27 GM guidance of slightly above 56.5% incorporates an increased tariff assumption of
12.5%, up from the prior 10% assumption. While DECK continues to pursue tariff refunds, no
potential recovery has been included in guidance. Management indicated that any eventual
refunds would likely be received over time, with a portion returned to partners and a portion
subject to taxes, while the company will also evaluate ways to drive growth in the business and
return value to shareholders.
Quarter in Review. Adj. EPS of $0.94 beat consensus of $0.87, driven by better GM. Sales of
$1.02bn matched consensus, as DTC came in above consensus at $353mn (vs. cons. of $344mn),
while wholesale came in just below consensus at $667mn (vs. cons. of $669mn). GM of 56.4%
beat consensus of 54.4% due to favorable channel and product mix, better full-price selling,
favorable FX, and better management of product closeouts, partially offset by tariff
headwinds. SG&A rate of 41.2% came in above consensus of 40.3%, driven by the annualization
of new hires, marketing investments, higher rent costs related to adding global HOKA stores,
increased technology expenses, and unfavorable FX. Management updated FY27 guidance, with
revenue expected to be in the range of $5.86bn to $5.91bn (unchanged from prior and vs. cons.
of $5.90bn), with HOKA expected to increase +LDD (unchanged from prior) and UGG expected to
increase +MSD (unchanged from prior). GM is expected to be slightly better than 56.5% (from
prior ~56.5% and vs. cons. of 56.7%), with SG&A rate of ~35% (unchanged from prior and vs.
cons. of 35.0%), OM to be slightly better than 21.5% (from prior 21.5% and vs. cons. of 21.7%),
and adj. EPS in the range of $7.35 to $7.50 (from prior $7.30 to $7.45 and vs. cons. of $7.50).
FY2Q27 guidance was also provided, with management expecting revenue to be up +5% y/y (vs.
cons. of +8.2%), including HOKA to increase +HSD and UGG to increase +MSD, GM down y/y,
SG&A dollars to continue to reflect 1H weighted investments, and adj. EPS of $1.73 to $1.78 (vs.
cons. of $1.89). 
Inventory. Based on our proprietary inventory analysis, DECK has posted two consecutive
quarters of positive sales-to-inventory growth. In FY1Q27 the Inventory Management Spread
("IM Spread") was 942 basis points and improved from 502 basis points in the prior quarter.
DECK's Gross Margin Return on Inventory ("GMROI") has improved for four consecutive
quarters, and its Operating Margin Return on Inventory ("OMROI") has improved for one
quarter.

Proprietary PRISM Curve: DECK in the "HUNT" phase
From a fundamental perspective, we use our proprietary PRISM cycle as a leading indicator of
potential sales inflection (i.e., either from positive to negative or negative to positive). Our
PRISM Curve attempts to look for fundamental changes in the business model of a company,
driven by proactive management decisions to stabilize margins (e.g., purge inventory, cut costs,
share best practices, invest in product and brand). Figure 2 and Figure 3 below depict our model
graphically and identify where we would theoretically expect to see sales and margin changes
along the business model curve.
The HUNT Phase (Strategically Offensive)
During the HUNT Phase, management’s strategy is on the offense. They are in “HUNT” mode
and are in control of taking market share. Management is focused on driving company-specific
strategies forward, building on their own brand equity and positioning, rather than focusing
primarily on competitors or the environment around them. At this point, retailers are usually
beating sales and margin expectations and may begin to grow earnings faster than total sales.
Investors would want to hold and accumulate shares of a company in Region C in Figure 3, the
area of maximum potential stock appreciation. This phase may take place over multiple years
as the company enters its sweet spot.

Tariff Exposure Update. FY1Q27 GM expanded +60bps y/y despite a 150bps tariff headwind,
which was entirely offset by favorable channel and product mix, better full-price selling,
favorable FX, and better management of product closeouts. Management noted that FY1Q27 is
expected to represent the largest tariff-related headwind of FY27, as prior-year inventory sold in
the quarter did not carry tariffs and the company will begin lapping tariff-impacted periods as
the year progresses. FY2Q27 guidance calls for GM to be down due to tariffs and higher freight
costs. FY27 GM guidance of slightly above 56.5% incorporates an increased tariff assumption of
12.5%, up from the prior 10% assumption. While DECK continues to pursue tariff refunds, no
potential recovery has been included in guidance. Management indicated that any eventual
refunds would likely be received over time, with a portion returned to partners and a portion
subject to taxes, while the company will also evaluate ways to drive growth in the business and
return value to shareholders.
Prior Tariff Commentary. FY4Q26 GM expanded +90bps y/y despite tariff headwinds, with
higher full-price selling across UGG and HOKA, favorable FX, reduced freight costs, and favorable
product and channel mix driving the increase. For FY26, GM decreased -20bps, including an
80bps headwind from tariffs partially offset by 60bps of underlying GM expansion from
favorable product mix and lower freight costs. Guidance assumes the current 10% tariff rate
remains in effect for the duration of FY27, with management citing they are pursuing tariff
refunds but have not incorporated any refund amount into guidance. The company paid around
$120mn in IEEPA tariffs, but noted that once it receives the money back, the company will factor
in cost sharing arrangements with partners. FY1Q27 GM is expected to be down y/y primarily
due to tariffs, with slight positive offsets from favorable channel mix and favorable FX.
