Top 10 Questions for Management 
We see a balanced upside/downside skew heading to DECK's 3Q26  report:
We rate DECK Buy.  We think Deckers' sales and EPS outlook justify a high valuation. We 
estimate its high-growth brand, HOKA, drives a ~13% 5-yr. consolidated top-line CAGR 
and believe this makes DECK one of Softlines' best growth stocks. Plus, the market is 
wondering if  Hoka's US DTC  sales growth rate will re-accelerate back into +MSD-HSD% 
territory. We believe Hoka will continue to deliver US DTC sales growth acceleration over 
CY26 and expect this drives  stock outperformance. At the same time, we see a balanced 
upside/downside skew heading into the company's 3Q print on January 29th (link). 
Top  Questions for DECK Management:
1. UGG brand US DTC traffic has been soft in recent quarters and in 2Q26 
DTC sales declined -10.4% y/y, highlighting continued pressure in the 
channel. How does management view the medium- and long-term growth 
outlook within the US following 2 years of very solid growth? What specific 
initiatives are being implemented that address mgmt.'s comments from its 2Q call 
that "recent consumer trends [are] indicating a heightened preference for multi-
brand shopping experiences"? When does the company expect the UGG brand to 
return to positive, sustainable growth in the channel? What does this imply for 
Ugg’s total US growth potential? 
2. What is the underlying sales growth rate within UGG's core classic 
offering? This includes key "hero" products such as the Tasman franchise, Classic 
Boot, Classic  Mini/ Ultramini, Disquette, and the Scuffette slippers. While DECK 
has commented on its innovation within the Golden Collection and the Lowmel to 
support assortment evolution into a 4-season brand, what innovation does DECK 
have planned within the core classic offering heading into Fall/Winter 2026/7? 
3. What impact does DECK mgmt. expect from pulled forward EMEA 
wholesale shipments given its third-party warehouse transition? We note 
DECK mgmt. has highlighted earlier wholesale shipments related to the transition 
for its past 3 earnings  calls. Does DECK foresee any changes in its inventory 
management capacity for its European wholesale business going into FY27? 
4. Tariffs are expected to create a $150M unmitigated headwind in FY26, 
partially offset by ~$75-95M of mitigation strategies. Does the company  
feel it will be able to offset tariff pressures within FY27? Does DECK see 
incremental mitigation opportunity beyond its $75-95M target offered on its 2Q 
earnings call?

Please find questions 5-11 below:
5.  What new innovation does Hoka have planned for CY26 within the lifestyle 
as well as the trail and road running categories? How much of the innovation 
pipeline is concentrated within max cushioning styles vs. low-profile 
silhouettes? Management  noted during its 2Q26 earnings conference call its Solimar, 
Transport in lower-profile tooling, and Speedgoat 2 styles which have lower profiles are 
resonating well with consumers. How does management think about potential trend 
evolution within trail and road running towards lower profile styles and related 
implications for its higher-profile franchises, such as the Bondi? What innovation does 
Hoka have planned for the lifestyle category? Does management believe it can add a 
third iconic style to complement its Bondi and Clifton styles?
6.  How does management view higher marketplace promotional activity within 
the athletic footwear category? Does DECK expect to utilize more promotional 
selling practices to compete against elevated promotional activity in the US from some 
large public peers within the marketplace? Does this impact the brand's full-price selling 
positioning within the US market? Given choppy Hoka and UGG US sales trends over the 
last few quarters, what gives management confidence both brands remain premium, 
full-price, relevant, high-quality brands in US consumers’ eyes?
7. What new innovation does Hoka and UGG have within the apparel 
categories? Where does apparel stand today as a percentage of total sales within the 
respective Hoka and UGG brands? 
8.  What is the underlying growth rate for Hoka's APAC business? How does DECK 
management view the long-term growth opportunity for Hoka within the 
APAC market? How much does APAC represent as a percentage of Hoka's total global 
business? What timeline does management expect for Hoka's APAC business to reach 
~10% of total global Hoka sales? How much is the Hoka APAC region expected to 
contribute within DECK's long-term  target of international sales reaching 50% of total 
global DECK sales? Separately, what is the underlying growth rate within Hoka's Europe 
business and what does DECK mgmt. view as the long-term growth opportunity in the 
region?
9. How does management view its capital allocation priorities over the medium 
term? Should investors expect DECK to continue to allocate $500-600M annually to 
share buybacks? How does management feel about the potential to issue a 
dividend? How much cash does DECK management believe it needs to hold on its 
balance sheet for working capital needs?
10.  When does DECK expect to offer new LT guidance targets? DECK has not 
offered long-term revenue or EBIT margin targets since February 2024. Does 
management continue to view a healthy long-term growth rate for the UGG brand as  
+MSD% y/y growth? What does management view as a healthy growth rate for the 
Hoka brand? Does the introduction of tariffs, higher promotions, or other factors 
change DECK management's view a healthy EBIT margin for the business as in the 
+high-teens% range? How does management feel about hosting an analyst day where 
it gives a long-term financial roadmap with sales and margin targets?
11. Is the company currently implementing any AI initiatives to enhance 
customer experience, optimize supply chain operations, improve digital 
marketing, and drive DTC traffic? What is the expected financial impact and timing 
of that impact from these initiatives?

Deckers Outdoor Corp designs, markets, and distributes mainly footwear, as well as apparel 
and accessories, for lifestyle and high-performance activities. The company distributes its

Valuation Method and Risk Statement
Our price target is derived from a multiple analysis, supported by a DCF valuation as a 
validation method. Several downside risks to consider: (1) The health of the consumer. Brands 
are reliant on the state of the macro environment and any deterioration in consumer 
spending and/ or unemployment could negatively impact sales and margins. (2) Fashion risk/ 
inability to execute on merchandise strategy. Brands are inherently exposed to fashion risk 
due to ongoing shifts in trends and customer preferences. The company's success has hinged 
upon its ability to forecast fashion trends and appropriately translate the looks for its target 
customer. If it finds itself unable to predict and/ or interpret changes in trends, sales and 
margins could suffer. (3) Inventory risk. The company's ability to manage inventory levels is 
important to be the health of margins and earnings. If sales are worse than expected and 
inventories are aggressively purchased relative to actual sales trends, the company will likely 
need to take incremental markdowns to clear remaining product, which could pressure gross 
margins.
