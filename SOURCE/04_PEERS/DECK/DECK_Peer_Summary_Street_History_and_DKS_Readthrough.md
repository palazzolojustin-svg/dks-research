## 1. Why DECK matters to DKS (facts from the notes only)

1. **DKS is named by UBS as a key domestic HOKA wholesale partner, repeatedly.** UBS (Jay Sole) wrote in the 3Q26 preview (23-Jan-26, file 2026-01-28), the 3Q26 review (29-Jan-26, file 2026-02-04), the 4Q26 preview (14-May-26, file 2026-05-19) and the 1Q27 preview (7-Jul-26, file 2026-07-13): "We anticipate expanding distribution with key domestic wholesale partners like Foot Locker and Dick's Sporting Goods as well as key international wholesale partners should further drive solid wholesale sales growth for the Hoka brand." The July 2026 version adds "and sell-in to new wholesale partnerships like Academy Sports."
2. **HOKA is still under-penetrated in US sporting goods doors.** Needham (30-Jan-26): "Hoka is only in about half the targeted sporting goods doors in the U.S., and only ~40% penetrated in Europe. In athletic-specialty, they're only in ~25% of doors in the U.S. and less than 20% of doors in Europe. And though run specialty is fairly saturated in the U.S., they're only ~80% penetrated in Europe." Barclays conference takeaways (9-Sep-26): "HOKA's U.S. specialty run category is relatively mature, but ... there remains meaningful whitespace opportunities across sporting goods, athletic specialty, and international markets. The company noted that several key wholesale partners remain underpenetrated, particularly in athletic specialty." Wells Fargo (21-May-26) lists "W/S expansion at sporting goods/specialty athletic" as a HOKA growth lever.
3. **DECK's wholesale channel is large and growing faster than DTC through FY26.** Bloomberg FA: FY26 wholesale revenue $3,208mm (+12.3% y/y) vs DTC $2,264mm (+6.3%); wholesale = 58.6% of FY26 revenue. HOKA FY26 wholesale $1,652mm (63.8% of HOKA); UGG FY26 wholesale $1,445mm (52.7% of UGG). Barclays FY27E: wholesale +7.0%, DTC +9.5% (22-May-26), i.e., the channel mix is expected to start rebalancing toward DTC in FY27.
4. **Management's stated channel philosophy is tightly controlled, selective door expansion.** UBS (7-Jul-26) quoting DECK FY27 guidance language: "fuel DTC acquisition through differentiation and expand wholesale doors in a controlled manner." Barclays (9-Sep-26): "door expansion remaining highly selective and guided by full-price sell-through metrics." UBS (10-Sep-26): DECK "is entering 15 Academy Sports + Outdoors doors with a limited assortment and color offering."
5. **Wholesale sell-in timing creates quarter-to-quarter noise that affects retail partners' receipts.** Examples: UGG wholesale +17% in FY2Q26 because "wholesale partners took shipments earlier given inventory shortages last Holiday season" (Needham 24-Oct-25); $25M of UGG wholesale deliveries pulled from FY3Q26 into 1H26 (UBS 23-Jan-26); FY4Q26 UGG wholesale orders pulled into FY3Q26 (Barclays 30-Jan-26); FY1Q27 HOKA wholesale only +3% because of lapping an EMEA warehouse transition, delayed APAC distributor shipments and reduced Clifton 10 shipments ahead of the Clifton 11 launch (UBS 21-May-26; Needham 24-Jul-26).
6. **Running category competition is a live debate.** Wells Fargo (21-May-26, 23-Jul-26): "it is more than coincidental that Nike saw significant growth in specialty run in North America throughout 2025 at the same time that HOKA began to focus on wholesale fill-in with decelerating DTC trends." UBS (23-Jan-26): "others are worried Hoka will be negatively impacted by a Nike comeback."
7. **Tariff pass-through and pricing.** DECK sources <5% from China, ~80% Vietnam (Barclays 24-Oct-25). FY26 unmitigated tariff estimate moved $185mm -> $150mm -> $110mm; net FY26 impact ended at ~$25mn (Barclays 30-Jan-26). Mitigation included "select, strategic, and staggered price increases" and "partial cost sharing with factory partners" (DECK 2Q26 call, quoted by UBS 23-Jan-26). BI (07/21/25): DECK's "scale and full-price sell-through could also provide leverage in negotiations with wholesale partners." For FY27, DECK paid ~$120mn of IEEPA tariffs and said if refunded it "will factor in cost sharing arrangements with partners" (Barclays 22-May-26).
8. **Wells Fargo lists "retail partner earnings" as an upcoming catalyst for DECK** in every note (Oct-25 through Jul-26), i.e., DKS results are treated as a read on DECK demand, and vice versa.

## 6. Key themes with evidence

### 6a. HOKA growth: deceleration then re-acceleration, by period
- Reported brand growth (Wells Fargo Jul-26; BI timelines; UBS): FY4Q24 +34%; FY1Q25 +29.5%; FY2Q25 +34.7%; FY3Q25 +23.7% ("slowing"); FY4Q25 +10%; FY1Q26 +19.8% (wholesale +30%, DTC +3%); FY2Q26 +11.1% (wholesale +13%, DTC +8%); FY3Q26 +18.5% (DTC +19%, wholesale +18%); FY4Q26 +14.5% (DTC +18%, wholesale +13%); FY1Q27 +7.7% (DTC +13% [Needham/Williams say +17%], wholesale +3%).
- Guidance path: FY26 Hoka "low-teens" (Oct-25) -> "mid-teens" (Jan-26); FY26 actual +15.9% (Bloomberg); FY27 guide "low-double digits" with 1Q27 and 2Q27 +HSD and implied 2H "teens"/12-13% (Needham, Wells Jul-26); FY28-30 framework LDD annually (BI/Needham/Barclays May-26).
- US DTC inflection: UBS observed-sales data: FY2Q26 -13%, FY3Q26 -10% (Oct -16, Nov flat, Dec -15), FY4Q26 +9% (Jan -1, Feb +26, Mar +4), FY1Q27 -5% (Apr -5, May -1, Jun -11) with promos +520bps y/y on Clifton 10 clearance. Barclays (30-Jan-26) estimated Hoka US DTC positive MSD in 3Q; BI (02/19/26) "Hoka's US DTC inflected positively."
- Brand awareness: 60% US (vs 50% prior year), 40% international (vs 30%) (Wells, Needham May-26). Six franchises >$100M revenue, three approaching (Needham May-26). Price architecture widened from $140-160 to $115-$300 (UBS Jul-26). US share 3.4% (2024) / 3.6% (2025), 7th-largest US sports footwear brand; 1.1% global share of $169-179bn market (BI).
- Bear framing: "Hoka brand has plateaued and competitive pressures will revert growth rates downward" (Wells, every note); "competition has caught up to Hoka" (UBS characterization of market view, 29-Jan-26).

### 6b. UGG
- Reported growth: FY1Q26 +18.9% (wholesale +30%, DTC -1%); FY2Q26 +10.1% (wholesale +17%, DTC -10%); FY3Q26 +4.9% (wholesale +5%, DTC +4%); FY4Q26 +9.2% vs consensus flat (DTC +13%, wholesale +4% per UBS; Wells says DTC +8%, W/S +10%); FY1Q27 +4.9% (DTC +6%, wholesale +5%).
