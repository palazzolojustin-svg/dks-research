5. Global Growth Levers, Strong Brand Momentum Aid Hoka
Hoka is well positioned to sustain midteen expansion over the next several years as it expands
internationally, broadens into adjacent categories, and expands direct-to-consumer. The brand is
underpenetrated globally, with just a 1.1% share of the $179 billion sports-footwear market, leaving
ample room for for gains. Strength in core franchises like Clifton, Bondi and Arahi, alongside
innovation in trail and racing and disciplined wholesale expansion, supports durable share growth.
A more strategic launch cadence, with less overlap of major updates and cleaner marketplace
inventory, helped reaccelerate Hoka’s sales growth in fiscal 2026, leading to raised annual guidance
from low-to-midteen gains, in line with our original expectations. Consensus moved toward 15% from
low-double digits at initiation. (02/19/26)

6. Ugg Expands Beyond Seasonal Core, Winning With Men
Under new president Anne Spangenberg, Ugg has developed a more robust year-round product
assortment, reducing reliance on its traditional cold-weather core and positioning it for market-share
gains. It's making headway in men's, and we expect that momentum to accelerate given recent
initiatives like the first male-focused campaign in over five years featuring Post Malone, who
previously had a long-standing collaboration with rival Crocs. Men's and apparel are key catalysts to
doubling Ugg sales in the long term.
Shoulder-season styles like Lowmel and Tasman are resonating globally, helping drive 1H sales up 12%,
in line with our expectations and ahead of consensus' 7%. We believe colder weather in 4Q will boost
sales ahead of guidance for flat growth and drive high-single-digit gains for the year. (02/19/26)

7. DTC Can Reaccelerate as Marketplace Normalizes
Direct to consumer has trailed wholesale, yet much of the pressure appears to be structural rather
than demand-driven. Earlier wholesale shipments and expanded door counts, particularly for Ugg,
shifted traffic toward retail partners, while Hoka's prior model transitions temporarily disrupted
digital momentum. Importantly, Hoka's US DTC inflected positively following cleaner inventory
positioning, tighter franchise launch spacing and its enhanced membership program, which can lift
revenue per consumer and multicategory purchasing.
For UGG, softer DTC trends reflect stronger wholesale in-stocks and a consumer preference for
multibrand shopping, and not brand fatigue. As product newness resonates, DTC could modestly
outperform wholesale in 4Q as management leverages owned channels to manage launches. (02/19/26)

13. Focus on Product Expansion Beyond Running
Hoka's ability to expand its product categories beyond running will be key to becoming a multibillion-
dollar brand over the next 10 years. The company's biggest near-term opportunities for expansion
are hiking, lifestyle and apparel. Through cohesive product families spanning trail, hike and lower-

investments in apparel, positions Hoka to broaden its wholesale footprint and capture additional shelf
space while maintaining a disciplined, premium marketplace strategy.

expanding its appeal beyond performance purists. (02/25/26)

14. Running Remains Core to Growth, Brand Awareness
Even as Hoka expands into lifestyle and hiking, running will remain a core growth driver. The category
dominates the athletic-footwear market and continues to become more mainstream globally,
presenting an opportunity to gain share. While legacy brands compete for elite marathon credibility,
Hoka has strengthened its position with everyday runners through consistent franchise innovation.
The brand recently gained share in the US road-running category above $140 and has returned to
the top position in run specialty, with double-digit sell-through growth and rising reorders
internationally.
A cleaner marketplace, tighter launch cadence and disciplined inventory management position Hoka
to sustain share gains in running even as competition intensifies around maximal cushioning.

17. Product Diversification, Refinement Has Ugg Year-Round
Ugg is solidly positioned to drive sustainable mid- to high-single-digit annual sales growth as efforts
to diversify its offerings with clogs, sandals and sneakers pay off, helping it become a year-round
brand. It has simplified the assortment, reducing style count and concentrating inventory behind
core franchises. This tighter focus allows Ugg to scale winning silhouettes more effectively, improve
allocation discipline and maintain strong full-price sell-through. This helps make growth less
dependent on a narrow set of seasonal products and more supported by a diversified, franchise-led
portfolio.
Ugg is also selectively leaning into emerging footwear trends, with styles such as the Quill ballet
sneaker and Ellis loafer helping it capture fashion-driven demand without straying from its core
aesthetic. (02/27/26)
