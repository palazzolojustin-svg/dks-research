OUR MAIN TAKEAWAY
1Q27 was slightly better than guidance, but given DECK's history of big beats, investors are likely
disappointed. However, it's the smallest quarter of the year, both brands are growing nicely
(even if the growth isn't as exceptional as it once was), DTC trends are solid, inventory/margin
dynamics are favorable, and valuation is compelling to us. So, we continue to be positively
inclined on the name as we head into the key Fall/Winter season, though we tweak our PT
down to $125 (from $138) on a more conservative multiple. In the quarter, revenues grew 6%
(vs. guidance +5%) and EPS of $0.94 beat guidance of $0.82-$0.87. The FY revenue outlook was
reiterated at 7-8%, with FY EPS raised by $0.05 to $7.35-$7.50. DECK did guide below consensus
for Q2, calling for 5% revenue growth and EPS of $1.73-$1.78 (vs. +8% and $1.90 respectively).
TOM'S TAKEAWAYS
Tom’s Takeaway #1: Strong DTC Trends At Hoka, Wholesale Impacted By Timing Shifts. Hoka
grew 8%, in line with guidance +HSD. Growth was once again led by the DTC channel, which was
up 17%, while the wholesale channel was up 3%. Management noted strong domestic wholesale
trends, which was offset by timing of international shipments (which contributed to unusually strong
wholesale growth of +30% globally in the year-ago quarter). Hoka is expected to be +HSD again in
Q2, with the timing shift benefits helping 2H and resulting in re-acceleration. Thus, management
continues to expect FY growth of +LDD, implying 2H growth in the teens. To support their optimism,
management noted strong new product introductions, including two additions to the popular Clifton
franchise (the elevated Clifton Pro, as well as the Clifton 11).
Tom’s Takeaway #2: Solid and Balanced Growth At UGG. UGG grew 5%, in line with guidance of
+MSD, with similar growth at DTC (+6%) and wholesale (+5%), and with growth both domestically and
abroad. Management cited strong consumer adoption of newer categories such as sneakers, sandals,
and fashion footwear, which has resulted in mix benefits to gross margin. The FY outlook of MSD
growth was reiterated, and management expects Q2 in that range as well, implying that 2H will be
+MSD also.
Tom’s Takeaway #3: Gross Margin Beats Expectations, Lean Inventories Bode Well. Gross margin
was up 60bps YoY, beating management's guidance for a YoY decline (consensus was -130bps).
Management noted tailwinds from channel mix, product mix, full-price sell-through rates, FX, and
better closeout management; these factors more than offset the 150bps headwind from tariffs. Notably,
inventory was down 5% YoY, which bodes well for continued gross margin upside during the Fall/
Winter season, amid a conservative outlook. Guidance implies that gross margin is down ~150bps
YoY the remainder of the year, which may prove conservative. Notably, guidance does not include any
impact from IEEPA tariff refunds, which could provide further upside.

At a Glance
OUR INVESTMENT THESIS
We think DECK is one of the highest-quality companies in our coverage, with an impressive multi-year track record of performance, two
of the strongest brands in the industry (Hoka and UGG), a strong management team and a fortress balance sheet.
BULL CASE ASSUMPTIONS
With the benefit of a slew of new products,
as well as channel fill in the wholesale
channel, Hoka growth could re accelerate
to the 15%-20% range the next few
years. Additionally, given international
momentum and new product launches
domestically, UGG could generate MSD-
HSD revenue growth. If margins are
sustained in the low-20's, then FY28 EPS
could be ~$9.50 and the share price could
reach ~$170 (18x P/E on FY27E EPS ).
BASE CASE ASSUMPTIONS
We expect FY27 EPS to come in at the
high end of guidance, We expect EPS to
grow +DD in FY28, resulting in EPS power
of $8.00-$8.50 in fiscal 2028 (Mar'28). Our
$125 price target is based on a 15x P/E on
FY28 EPS of ~$8.30.
BEAR CASE ASSUMPTIONS
Against difficult compares and a tough
macro environment, UGG growth could
flat-line, while Hoka could slow to
single-digit growth (mainly driven by
new distribution). Tariffs plus normalized
discounting levels could push margins
down to the high-teens. If so, EPS growth
would slow dramatically, and FY28 EPS
could be $7.00, and the share price could
fall to ~$70 (10x P/E on FY28E EPS).
COMPANY DESCRIPTION
DECK owns a portfolio of global footwear brands, led by the UGG and Hoka brands.

VALUATION (PRICE TARGET: $125.00)
Our $125 price target is based on a 15x P/E on FY28 EPS of $8.30.
POTENTIAL UPSIDE DRIVERS
The company could do better than we expect if:
■
Hoka gains brand awareness and retailer shelf space faster than we expect.
■
UGG is able to continue rolling out successful new products.
■
Recent levels of full-price selling are maintained.
■
The company accelerates the pace of share repurchases.
RISKS TO TARGET
DECK
Potential downside risks include:
■
Hoka operates in a highly competitive athletic footwear market.
■
Hoka could run out of new channels of distribution, slowing potential growth.
■
UGG growth could stall if new product introductions fail to resonate with consumers.
■
Margins could come under pressure if growth slows and more discounting/markdowns are necessary.
