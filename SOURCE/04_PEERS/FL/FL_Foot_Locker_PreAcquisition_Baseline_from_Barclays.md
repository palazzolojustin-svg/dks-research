## 6. Rating, valuation and scenarios (Note 1, p.2 and p.7)

- Rating: Equal Weight (unchanged); industry view Neutral (unchanged). PT $24 (unchanged).
- PT methodology: NTM P/E of **15x** (was 16x) applied to **CY27 adj. EPS of $1.59** (prior: 16x on CY26 EPS $1.51). "Our PT is in line with the stated acquisition price."
- Why Equal Weight: "high likelihood of closing at the proposed $24 per share, as both FL's and DKS's board of directors have given approval."
- Upside case $24: 15x on CY27 earnings power $1.59; assumes deal consummation at $24.
- Downside case $14: 10x on CY27 earnings power $1.43; assumes deal does not close "combined with sustained negative comps, building inventory, and margin contraction."
- Downside risks listed: 1) deal uncertainty; 2) tariffs; 3) inability to execute against long-term sales/earnings targets.
- Upside risks listed: 1) return to sustainable positive comps; 2) tariff mitigation; 3) improved merchandise margins; 4) improving sales-to-inventory spread.

## 8. Key takeaways for the DKS model (data only, from the notes)

- FL entered DKS ownership with: FY2024A revenue $7,988mn, adj. EBIT $200mn (2.5% margin), adj. EBITDA $402mn (5.0%), 2,410 stores, comp +1.4%.
- Last pre-close run-rate (Barclays FY2025E, FYE Jan-2026): revenue $7,794mn (-2.4%), comp -0.9%, adj. EBIT $147mn (1.9% margin), adj. EBITDA $351mn (4.5%), 2,320 stores (-90 y/y), square footage -3.4%, inventory -11.1%, net cash $261mn, capex $262mn.
- 2Q25 (last quarter reported as an independent company): sales $1.86bn, comp -2.0% (NA +1.4%, EMEA -11.4%, APAC -6.4%), GM 27.4%, OpEx 27.9% of sales, adj. LPS ($0.27).
- Barclays' longer-dated earnings power for FL: FY2026E EBIT $198mn (2.5%), FY2027E EBIT $231mn (2.8%); store base shrinking to 2,239 by FY2027E.
- Deal price $24/share; ~95.5mn diluted shares → equity value ~$2.3bn at close (Bloomberg market cap $2,294mn at $24.01 on 05-Sep-2025).
