Foot Locker, Inc.
Model Update
FL did not hold a conference call to discuss its 2Q25 results
and did not provide or update its previous guidance due to
the pending transaction, which is expected to close on
9/8/25. We update our model to include 2Q25 actual results
and lower our adj. EPS estimates. Our PT remains unchanged.
Model Update. As previously announced on 5/15/25, FL and DKS entered into a definitive
merger agreement under which DKS will acquire FL. On 8/22/25, FL received shareholder
approval for the acquisition. All required regulatory approvals to complete the transaction have
been received and FL expects the transaction will close on 9/8/25. Due to the pending
transaction, FL did not hold a conference call to discuss its 2Q25 results and did not provide or
update its previous guidance. We update our model to include 2Q25 actual results and lower
our adj. EPS estimates. Our PT remains unchanged. 
Quarter in review. Adj. LPS of ($0.27) missed consensus adj. EPS of $0.11, driven by lower sales,
a miss on GM, and OpEx deleverage. Sales of $1.86bn missed (vs. cons. $1.88bn), as same store
sales of -2.0% missed (vs. cons. +0.1%), driven by EMEA comp down -11.4% and APAC down
-6.4%, partially offset by NA +1.4%. GM missed at 27.4% (vs. cons. 28.7%) as merch margins
decreased 50bps. OpEx rate at 27.9% of sales missed (vs. cons. 27.6%), due to the lower sales.
Lowering estimates; maintaining PT of $24. We lower our adj. EPS estimates for FY25, FY26,
and FY27 to $0.76, $1.38, and $1.59 (from $1.23, $1.51, and $1.93), respectively, as we update our
model for 2Q25 actual results and lower our sales and GM assumptions. Our PT of $24 is based
on an NTM P/E multiple of 15x (was 16x) applied to our CY27 adj. EPS estimate of $1.59 (prior
CY26 adj. EPS estimate of $1.51). We roll forward our PT, which is now based on our CY27 EPS
estimate vs. our prior CY26 EPS estimate. Our PT is in line with the stated acquisition price.

Our rating is based on our view that
the proposed deal with DKS has a
high likelihood of closing at the
proposed $24 per share, as both FL's
and DKS's board of directors have
given approval. Further, we see no
fundamental anti-trust risk that
would prevent the transaction from
closing.
Upside case
Our upside case of $24 is based on
an NTM P/E multiple of 15x applied
to our CY27 earnings power of $1.59

the deal at $24/share.

applied to our CY27 earnings power

not close combined with sustained
negative comps, building inventory,
