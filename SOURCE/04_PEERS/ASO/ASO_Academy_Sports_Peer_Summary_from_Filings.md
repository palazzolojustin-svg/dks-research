# Academy Sports + Outdoors (ASO) — Peer Summary from SEC Filings

**Purpose:** Structured extraction of Academy's reported numbers and KPIs for benchmarking against DICK'S Sporting Goods (DKS). Academy is DKS's closest public full-line sporting goods peer (322 stores, 21 southern/midwestern states, FYE Saturday nearest Jan 31).

- [10-Q Q2 FY26] `ASO_10-Q_FY2026-Q2_filed-2026-09-09.md` — 13 and 26 weeks ended Aug 1, 2026.

**Fiscal calendar note:** Academy's "2025" = FYE Jan 31, 2026; "2026" = FYE ~Jan 30, 2027. This aligns with DKS's fiscal calendar (DKS FY2025 also ends Jan 31, 2026).

**Extraction notes:** Tables in the filings converted cleanly (SEC HTML-to-markdown); no garbling observed. Q4 FY2025 and Q4 FY2024 quarterly figures below are **derived** (10-K full year minus 10-Q 39-week YTD) and are flagged as such. Prior-year quarterly columns in each 10-Q were used for Q1 FY2025 and Q2 FY2025. Comparable sales for Q1 FY2025, Q2 FY2025 and Q4 FY2025 are not stated in these four filings (the Q1/Q2 FY2025 10-Qs are not in the folder; the 10-K gives only full-year comps).

**Mentions of DICK'S / competition:** None of the four filings names DICK'S Sporting Goods, Foot Locker, or any competitor by name. The 10-K's competition discussion (Item 1 and Item 1A) lists only categories: mass general merchants; department stores; large format sporting goods stores; traditional sporting goods stores; specialty outdoor retailers; specialty footwear retailers; internet retailers; suppliers selling direct; and emerging formats (digitally native brands, marketplaces). Risk factor text: "Traditional competitors have become increasingly promotional"; competitors' "innovative store formats, aggressive pricing, promotion or delivery strategies" could cause share loss. [10-K, Item 1A]

## 5. Merchandising / brand / sourcing facts — Source: [10-K, Item 1 & 1A]

- National brands ≈ 78% of FY2025 merchandise sales; private label (19 brands incl. Magellan Outdoors, BCG, Academy, O'rageous, Game Winner, Outdoor Gourmet, Freely, Redfield, H2OX, R.O.W.) ≈ 22%; ~53% of customers bought a private brand in FY2025.
- No single brand > ~12% of FY2025 sales; largest vendor ≈ 12% of inventory purchases; ~1,500 vendors. Named key brands: Nike (incl. Jordan Brand — launched Q1 FY2025, $7.5M SG&A), Under Armour, adidas, Winchester, Columbia, The North Face, Brooks, Skechers, Yeti, Stanley, Carhartt.
- Sourcing: significant portion manufactured in China, Bangladesh, Vietnam, Cambodia and Brazil (incl. much private label); Hong Kong global sourcing office. Tariff mitigation: purchase timing/quantities (pre-tariff pull-forward in FY2025), pricing/promo adjustments, country-of-origin diversification, vendor negotiations.
- Firearms ≈ 6% of FY2025 net sales (federally licensed dealer; also sells ammunition and suppressors).
- Customer: "active families"; average customer visits 2–3x per year; myAcademy Rewards loyalty launched July 2024; Academy Credit Card program.
- Seasonality: Q2 (Memorial Day/Father's Day/July 4) is the largest profit quarter; back-to-school Q2/Q3; holiday Q4; cold-weather goods Q4.
- Sixteen categories in four divisions (table in 10-K Item 1): Outdoors = camping, fishing, hunting; Sports & Rec = fitness, team sports, recreation (patio, wheeled goods, trampolines), front end; Apparel = outdoor/seasonal, youth/swim, athletic, licensed; Footwear = casual/seasonal, work, youth, athletic, team sports.
