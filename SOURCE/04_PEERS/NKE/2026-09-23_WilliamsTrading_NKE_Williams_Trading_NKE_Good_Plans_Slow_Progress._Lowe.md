Footwear&Apparel

GoodPlans-SlowProgress.LoweringEstimates&PT
We are lowering our 1Q27.FY27. and FY28 estimates and reducing our PT from $44 to
s42. Returning Nike to what it once was has been more of a slog than we had expected.
Nike's running offerings are improving and performing well. Color updates of the Air
Force One are selling well, while basic colors are not. Converse is beginning to positively
inflect. Apparel sales, especially within the city specialty channel, are picking up
momentum. On the other foot, compelling new court shoes and sportswear footwear are
in short supply, especially within its core (family footwear) offerings. We remain confident
through cleaning up its global marketplaces. Nike will report 1Q27 earnings on Thursday.
