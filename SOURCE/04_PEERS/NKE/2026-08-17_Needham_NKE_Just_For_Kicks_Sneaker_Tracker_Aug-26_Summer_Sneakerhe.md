Just For Kicks, Sneaker Tracker Aug'26; Summer Sneakerhead Slog
Welcome To Our Monthly Sneaker-Launch Tracker. We believe that one way to assess
demand for the sneaker category is to track the performance of collectible sneakers. Though
they represent just a portion of the industry, when the industry is performing well, we usually
see a large number of "high heat" sneaker launches, which we define as products that sell out
completely in primary distribution channels, and then sell at premiums on resale platforms
such as StockX and GOAT (excluding low-volume "niche" launches). In this monthly series,
we look at the performance of the prior month's launches, as well as the current month's
pipeline.
July Was A Tough Month For Sneakerhead Styles. Last month, there were only 7 sneaker
releases that sold out quickly (at meaningful volume) and are trading at premiums in the
secondary market. This is a big step down from 21 high-heat launches in July 2025. With
a lack of meaningful innovation and "buzz" in the sneakerhead market, coupled with less-
than-ideal macro conditions, it seems clear to us that the sneaker market is challenged at
the moment. The most noteworthy development in the month was the poor performance of
the Jordan cohort. Despite the reduced supply of Jordans YoY (6 launches vs. 8 in July 2025),
not a single one of July's retro Jordans is trading at a resale premium at the moment. Thus,
it still seems as though the Jordan brand is facing a difficult demand environment, despite
a significant pullback in supply in recent years.
A Lot Of Sneakers Being Released For Back-To-School, But August Seems Off To A
Tough Start. As of this writing, there are 25 potential "high heat" sneaker launches for the
month of August, which would be up from 20 high-heat launches in August 2025. However,
the month already seems to be off to a tough start, as 3 of the 5 Jordan launches that have
already been released are trading at discounts in the resale market. Although there are a
lot of launches still to come, it doesn't seem to us like there are many "must have" styles
(no "Mind" sneakers, no Dunks), which could result in a challenging back-to-school season
overall.

JULY WAS A TOUGH MONTH FOR SNEAKERHEAD STYLES
Last month, there were only 7 sneaker releases that sold out quickly (at meaningful volume) and are trading at premiums in the secondary
market. This is a big step down from 21 high-heat launches in July 2025, and it's even lower than July 2024's highly-disappointing result
(8 high-heat launches). With a lack of meaningful innovation and "buzz" in the sneakerhead market, coupled with less-than-ideal macro
conditions, it seems clear to us that the sneaker market is challenged at the moment.

NKE's Jordan Sub-Brand (6 Launches)
There were 6 retro Jordan launches in July, which was down from 8 retro launches in July 2025 (as well as the debut of the Jordan 40).
Surprisingly, despite the reduced supply of Jordans YoY, not a single one of July's retro Jordans is trading at a resale premium at the
moment. Thus, it still seems as though the Jordan brand is facing a difficult demand environment, despite a significant pullback in supply
in recent years.

NKE's Dunk Sub-Brand (0 Launches)
Surprisingly, Nike did not release any Dunks during July, as compared to 2 Dunk launches in July 2025. It seems clear to us that NKE is less
enthusiastic about the Dunk franchise than they've been in many years, resulting in a very big decline in supply.
Nike Mind (2 Launches)
A recent success for Nike has been the "Mind" franchise, shoes designed to be worn before-and-after athletic activity, with specialized
texturing in the soles of the shoes, designed to stimulate an athlete's mind (to prepare them for activity or to recover from activity). Each
launch so far has sold out quickly, and in July both Mind shoes that were released sold out quickly and are trading at resale premiums.
This includes the Mind 001 "Indigo Burst" slide (essentially in the colors of Marvel superhero Spider-Man) and the Mind 002 sneaker in
the "Black and Indigo Burst" colorway.

NKE's Dunk and Mind Franchises
As of this writing, there are no Dunk launches planned for August, and if it remains this way, it would be essentially three months without
a Dunk launch (the last one was on June 1st). Last August, there was one Dunk launch in August. In addition to the zero Dunk launches,
there aren't any launches planned for the new "Mind" franchise either. Considering the issues currently plaguing the Jordan retro business,
we don't see a ton of "heat" for sneakerheads to get excited about this month.
Jordan Sub-Brand (8 Launches)
As of this writing, there are 8 Jordan launches planned for August 2026. In August 2025, there were 9 retro Jordan launches, in addition
to two "high heat" modern Jordan styles (a strong colorway of the Jordan 40 and a women's "Trunner" that sold through quickly). August
2026's Jordan launches are front-loaded, as 5 have already been released. Unfortunately, it seems as though it's been a tough month so far. 3
of the 5 launches have been slow to sell and are trading at discounts in the resale market. While this an improvement from July (when every
single Jordan launch traded at a discount), it still is a far cry from the Jordan brand's heyday, when almost every launch was a rapid sell-out.
