NKE: Short Positioning Builds to Record Highs as 
Shares Fall 
Nike (NKE) shares remain under significant pressure, with the stock down more than 
50% from its October 2025 high. As the footwear and apparel company has 
continued its decline, short sellers have added exposure, pushing short interest to 
record levels. 
 
Short Interest Sentiment 
 
• 
Shares currently sold short stand at over 87 million, an all-time high.  Short 
exposure has increased by 55 million shares over the past 52 weeks, pushing SI % of 
float from below 3% to above 7%. 
 
• 
Importantly, short sellers have persistently added exposure as NKE shares 
declined, rather than using the weakness to unwind positions and book profits.  
Shorts are sitting on an estimated $1.4 billion in mark-to-market paper gains so 
far in 2026. 
 
• 
Record short positioning, however, leaves more exposure vulnerable to short 
covering if a positive catalyst sparks a sustained reversal. Next earnings are 
scheduled for Thurs., 10/1, with the options market pricing in an implied one-day 
move of 8%.
