Conventions used throughout:
- Every number is attributed to its source note and date. "File date" is the date in the filename; where the document itself carries an earlier publication date, both are shown (e.g., "JPM 2026-08-07 file / note dated 4 Aug 2026").

- "cc" = currency-neutral. "DTC"/"Nike Direct" = Nike-owned stores + Nike Brand Digital. "FL" = Foot Locker (now a DKS segment). "BI" = Bloomberg Intelligence.
- Extraction caveats are flagged where the PDF-to-markdown conversion garbled a table or chart. Two pairs of BI files in the folder are content-identical duplicates (see Section 2 notes); they are listed once each in the chronology with the duplicate filename noted.

## 1. Why Nike matters to DKS (facts from the notes only)

**Stated Nike share of Foot Locker / JD Sports sales**
- "NKE brand as ~60% of FL's revenue mix" — JPMorgan (Boss), 2026-09-03 file / note dated 31 Aug 2026, paraphrasing DKS management on the 25 Aug 2026 DKS 2Q (May-Jul) call. Same note: JPM "estimates pointing to NKE in aggregate as over 40% of JD's [JD Sports'] total revenues."
- No note in this folder states a Nike percentage of core Dick's Sporting Goods sales. The only DKS-level color is qualitative: "core DKS has a more diversified brand & category mix" (JPM, 2026-09-03, citing DKS management).
- Historical framing: BI's archived earnings timeline (inside the 2026-10-01 BI "Revenue Reset" package, item dated 12/21/18) called Foot Locker Nike's "largest retail partner," and a 03/21/23 BI item referenced "Nike's revamped relationship with Foot Locker" as a sales driver.

**Nike's stated channel strategy (wholesale vs. Direct)**
- Nike FY2026 10-K (filed 2026-07-15), "Marketplace Management": "Repositioning NIKE Brand Digital as a full-price platform and reinvesting in wholesale distribution. This includes liquidating inventory through increased markdowns across NIKE Direct, and higher sales returns and discounts with our wholesale partners to reduce inventory and create capacity for new product. We are also making investments to elevate the presentation of our brands in physical retail." The 10-K says these actions should be complete "by the end of December 2026," that "North America has made the most progress," and that "Greater China and Converse will take more time."
- Nike FY26 10-K: North America wholesale revenues +14% in FY26 "including expanded distribution, higher shipments to existing partners and fewer marketplace management actions taken in the current year," while NA NIKE Direct fell 6% (digital -10%, stores -2%). FY26 Nike Brand revenue mix: ~61% wholesale / ~39% Nike Direct (BI Credit, 2026-09-22, citing FY26 results; 10-K FY26 shows wholesale $27,453M vs Direct $17,720M of $45,222M Nike Brand revenue).
- BI (2026-09-15 "Turnaround Underway" package, item dated 07/16/26): "Nike is elevating and revamping its wholesale offerings... A multibrand basketball concept at Foot Locker, HomeCourt, may help spur Nike and Jordan basketball sales, while an elevated women's fitness concept at Dick's Sporting Goods could draw more female shoppers." Same item: Nike "has refreshed more than 150 owned stores with sport-led experiences and more than 15,000 wholesale spaces globally." BI catalyst list (07/23/26): "2026: Strategic Partnerships Should Push Fiscal 2030 Wholesale Revenue Target Above $31 Billion."
- BI (2026-06-08 survey package, item dated 07/23/26): "A greater focus on wholesale partnerships, especially with Foot Locker, will likely improve assortment and spur growth. Its Amazon.com partnership and NikeSKIMS brand can also fuel sales."
- UBS (2026-09-22 file / note dated 17 Sep 2026): expects Nike commentary "to highlight wholesale sales growth expansion partially offsetting DTC sales trend declines as the company benefits from expanded points of distribution, particularly in North America." Also lists "Jordan Strength" training equipment launching "physically at participating Dick's Sporting Goods stores on October 13, 2026," and "55 unified 'Jordan Brand shop concepts' across Academy Sports + Outdoors stores during the second quarter of 2026."

**Why the Nike channel reset is a live risk for the DKS/FL P&L (as the notes frame it)**
- Needham (2026-10-01 file / note dated 24 Sep 2026): Nike had "four consecutive quarters of double-digit sell-in growth in North America (+14% YoY over the LTM), and the November 2025 quarter was the largest period of domestic wholesale sell-in during the company's long history... NKE has shipped in $1.5 billion more wholesale product YoY, and when grossing that up to retail sales, it's about $3 billion of incremental product at retailers... we don't believe NKE has sufficient demand at the moment to match the increased supply, which is resulting in excess promotional activity at retailers."
- BofA via Proactive Investors (2026-09-25): "North American wholesale sales, previously a source of strength, grew 14% in the 2026 fiscal, compared with flat total sales growth. Sell-through is lagging sell-in in some cases... this puts forward order books at risk as retailers become less willing to bet on new products until they prove successful. The bank expects North American wholesale sales to begin declining in the second quarter and remain challenged through the rest of the 2027 fiscal."
- Nike FY1Q27 10-Q (filed 2026-10-02): North America wholesale +9% cc in Q1 while NA Direct -6%; management flagged (per Freedom Broker 2026-10-02 and BI 2026-10-01) ~400 bps of added Q2 revenue pressure "including last year's Cyber Week activity in EMEA and higher North America sell-in."
- Post-print, Nike guided FY27 revenue down high-single digits and adjusted EPS $1.15-$1.35 (Proactive 2026-10-01; Freedom Broker 2026-10-02; BI 2026-10-01), with Sportswear and Jordan Brand reset actions "to continue affecting revenue into FY2028" (Freedom Broker).
