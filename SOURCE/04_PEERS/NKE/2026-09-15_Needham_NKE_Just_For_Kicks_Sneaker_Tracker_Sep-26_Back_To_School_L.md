Welcome To Our Monthly Sneaker-Launch Tracker. We believe one way to assess
demand for sneakers is to track the performance of collectible sneakers. Though they
represent just a portion of the industry, when the industry is performing well, we usually see
a large number of "high heat" sneaker launches (products that sell out completely in primary
distribution, and then sell at premiums on resale platforms such as StockX and GOAT). In
this monthly series, we look at the performance of the prior month's launches, as well as the
current month's pipeline.
Lots Of Sneaker Releases In August, Partially Making Up For A Very Bad July. There
were 21 high-heat sneaker launches in August, up from 20 in August 2025. However, this
comes on the heels of a very challenged July, where the number of high-heat launches
declined to just 7 (from 21 the year before). Thus, if we combine the two key back-to-school
months of July and August, the number of high-heat launches declined to 28 (from 41 in
summer 2025). The key Jordan franchise from NKE remained under pressure, as 7 of the 10
launches were slow sellers that are trading at resale discounts.
A Lot On the Calendar For September, But Will It Sell?  As of this writing, there are
30 potentially high-heat product launches in September. This would be a big step up from
September 2025's 19 high-heat launches. Of course, some of September's 30 launches could
fall short of expectations, particularly given the challenges NKE is experiencing with the
Jordan sub-brand.
A Baby That's Been Thrown Out With The Bath Water: DECK. It seems clear that the
athletic industry is challenged right now, given several negative data points this earnings
season (lowered guidance from LULU, ONON, DKS, JD Sports, etc.) However, one stock that
we think has been overly punished is DECK. Shares are down 17% YTD (vs. SPX +13%) despite
the fact that numbers have continued moving higher (consensus FY27 EPS now stands at
$7.53, 10% higher than $6.87 entering the year). As a result, shares trade at just 11x/10x
consensus FY27/28 EPS. We recently caught up with DECK's IR team, and they remain
confident in the trajectory of the two main brands. Hoka has posted three strong quarters
of DTC growth in a row (17%-20%), and wholesale is poised to reaccelerate in fiscal 2H.
Additionally, as they relaunch the key Clifton and Bondi franchises, they've learned some key
lessons from the poorly-executed relaunches that occurred in early-2025 (better inventory
management of outgoing styles, more spacing between the launches, etc.). They also note
that UGG has been generating encouragingly-balanced growth across wholesale and DTC,
with a variety of franchises poised to do well this Fall/Winter season (Tasman, Lowmel,
Golden, ballet flats, etc.) They also note that the brand is less weather-dependent than it's
been in the past, so long-range weather forecasts calling for warmer Fall/Winter weather
(due to El Nino) aren't a major concern.

LOTS OF SNEAKER RELEASES IN AUGUST, PARTIALLY MAKING UP FOR A VERY BAD JULY
There were 21 high-heat sneaker launches in August, which was up from 20 high-heat launches in August 2025. However, we'd note that
this comes on the heels of a very challenged July, where the number of high-heat launches declined to just 7 (from 21 the year before).
Thus, if we combine the two key summer/back-to-school months of July and August, the number of high-heat launches declined to 28
(from 41 in summer 2025).

NKE's Jordan Sub-Brand (10 Launches)
There were 10 retro Jordan launches in August 2026, which was a surprisingly large number considering the challenges the sub-brand is
facing at the moment. While August is a big month for Jordan launches due to back-to-school, August 2026 was even up vs. August 2025's
9 launches. Notably, 7 of the 10 launches were slow to sell and are trading at discounts in the resale market. While this an improvement
from July (when every single Jordan launch traded at a discount), it still is a far cry from the Jordan brand's heyday, when almost every
launch was a rapid sell-out. It's also worse YoY, as only 3-of-9 Jordan launches in August 2025 were slow sellers.

NKE's Mind Franchise
One of NKE's recent success stories has been the "Mind" franchise, which includes nodes int he sole of the shoe designed to help an athlete's
mental state before and after athletic activity. In late-August, NKE released a collection of their "Mind 001" in a few different colorways
("cucumber green", black, tan/beige), and it seems as though all the colorways sold well.
Figure 6 - NKE's "Mind" Franchise Continues To Be A Bright Spot
Source: StockX.com
NKE's Dunk Franchise
There were no Dunk launches in August, which made it essentially three months without a Dunk launch (the last one was on June 1st).
Last August, there was one Dunk launch in August. Thus, NKE appears committed to reining in the supply of this once-key franchise that
seems to have lost some of its luster due to oversaturation.
A LOT ON THE CALENDAR FOR SEPTEMBER, BUT WILL IT SELL?
As of this writing, there are 30 potentially high-heat product launches in September on the various release calendars we track. This would
be a big step up from September 2025's 19 high-heat launches. Of course, some of September's 30 launches could fall short of expectations,
particularly given the challenges NKE is experiencing with the Jordan sub-brand. So while NKE (and peers) are throwing a lot of product
out there to the consumer, it is somewhat of a risk given the relatively subdued demand for sneakers these days.

NKE's Jordan Sub-Brand (7 Launches)
As of this writing, there are 7 retro Jordan launches listed on the various sneaker-release calendars, which is up from 5 retro launches in
September 2025 (and one contemporary style, a pink Jordan 40, which did well upon initial release). It's surprising that NKE is releasing
more Jordans than they did a year ago, considering the issues that the Jordan brand is facing and the fact that it seems like supply and
demand have not yet become rebalanced. There's already been 2 Jordans launched MTD, and they've both performed poorly: a women's-
exclusive Jordan 1 that launched on 9/2 and a Jordan 4 that launched on 9/5. Both are trading at mid-teens discounts to MSRP on resale
sites. The mos compelling release of the month is probably the latest one currently on the calendar. On September 19th, they are re-
releasing a colorway of the Jordan 9 tied to the movie Space Jam, which marks 30 years since the movie's 1996 release. Given the nostalgia
around the movie and popularity of white/lack/red colorway, we think this could be a bright spot in the month.

NKE's Mind Franchise (1 Launch)
The Mind franchise remains a bright spot for NKE, though we believe that release volumes have been relatively low. Regardless, each
release seems to be selling out quickly, giving the brand a much-needed "win" amid a tough environment. Already this month, NKE has
released a new colorway of the Mind 002 sneaker, named "Fossil Stone and Plum Eclipse". Like prior Mind releases, this one sold out
quickly and is trading at premiums in the resale market. Specifically, it's reselling for ~$200, vs. the original retail price of $145. It will be
interesting to see how NKE scales up this product, and if demand can support higher unit volumes.
Figure 12 - NKE Continues To Roll Out New Iterations Of Its "Mind" Product

NKE's Dunk Franchise (No Launches)
As of this writing there are no Dunk launches planned for September, which would be essentially the 4th consecutive month without
any Dunk launches (the last one was on June 1st). By comparison, there were 4 Dunk product releases in September 2025. Thus, NKE is
dramatically reducing supply of this franchise, which appears to have lost some of its relevance due to oversaturation.
