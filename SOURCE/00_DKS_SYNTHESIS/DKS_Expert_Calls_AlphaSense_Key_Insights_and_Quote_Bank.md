## 6. Bias and reliability notes

- **AI-interviewer format.** Every call uses a fixed question script (role, categories, % revenue, May–Jul sell-in, Aug–Oct forecast, CY forecast, share, pricing, cost inputs, private label, categories, assortment shifts, allocation, innovation, Fast Break, partnership, relationship trajectory). The interviewer frequently paraphrases back in leading, affirming language ("That's a strong endorsement", "That's a clear and compelling case", "Strong local execution combined with overall business momentum") and the expert then agrees with one word ("Yes."). Several experts asked for questions to be repeated or answered the wrong period (Crocs answered the Aug–Oct question with Q4 numbers; denim mixed Q1/Q2 and Aug–Oct), so period attributions in Section 2 should be read with that caveat.
- **Supplier bias.** All fourteen voices are suppliers whose growth depends on DKS; AlphaSense labels every one "Partner." Nine said the relationship strengthened, none said weakened. Four were new or near-new accounts (uniform socks since early 2026, premium apparel since 2026, fashion boots since 2H 2025, UGG "a couple of years") whose triple-digit growth rates are base effects, not DKS demand signals. Several vendors (UGG, Crocs) explicitly control their own sell-in ("their business model remains scarcity in the marketplace. They control their growth"), so their numbers measure brand allocation decisions rather than DKS open-to-buy.
- **Role label discrepancy.** The AlphaSense title for the 2026-07-06 call says "Executive at a Retail Shoe Chain; oversees forecasting and retail logistics", but the expert self-describes as a 24-year employee of "the largest athletic apparel manufacturer in the world" supporting the DKS account; the content is supplier-side. The file is now named `DKS_Expert-Call_2026-07-06_Executive_Footwear-Brand-Planner_forecasting-logistics.md` with a header note to that effect, and this synthesis attributes it as "Footwear-Brand Planner". There is therefore no competing-retailer voice in the set.
- **Second interview in one export.** The AlphaSense export that produced the VP-Wholesale-Planning call also contained a second complete interview (Director of Sales, athletic apparel brand; casual/golf/swim) with no AlphaSense headline or metadata. It is now its own file, `DKS_Expert-Call_2026-07-14_SECOND-INTERVIEW-same-export_Casual-Golf-Swim-Apparel-Director.md`, dated by the title date of the export; it is roster row 10 and is attributed as (Director of Sales, casual/golf/swim apparel, 2026-07-14).
- **Dates.** Filenames carry the AlphaSense interview date where metadata exists (the title/publish date was 2–6 days later); calls #1, #13, #14 and the un-split second interview (#10) use title dates. All calls predate the Q2 period end (Aug 1, 2026) and the print; the latest (2026-07-28 and 2026-07-29) fall in the final week of the quarter.
- **Most direct DKS exposure.** (i) Under Armour Director of Merchandising (2026-07-10): "hundreds of millions of dollars", futures-based receipts, Ed Stack visit, 40+ buyers at UA — highest-volume, first-hand. (ii) Senior Director, women's footwear (2026-07-29): "About 18%" of company revenue — highest concentration. (iv) Nike-type Planning Manager (2026-07-14) and basketball Merchandising Director (2026-07-13): direct FL+DKS exposure but each "single digits"/"less than a couple percentage points." (v) UGG Manager (2026-07-28): does not manage the DKS account directly ("I manage the other half of the business, but I work very closely with the manager on the DICK'S account").
- **Second-hand or outside-in claims.** Commodity supplier's "from sources within the industry, I have heard that they have been buying quite aggressively" (2026-06-13); Nike-type planner's "from an outsider looking in, that Foot Locker inventory must have been a pretty big hit" and the 25–30% apparel and 30%/half Nike-share figures (2026-07-14); premium-apparel director's reliance on "their earnings calls or their public information" and store walks (2026-07-08); VP Wholesale Planning's share view ("We don't sell to a ton of retailers that would be in the same specific of marketplace as DICK'S"); basketball director's "they just increased their overall guidance" (2026-07-13); Crocs manager's "the public retailer data shows that DSG is gaining share" (2026-07-06).

Apparel is over-represented (8 of 14); footwear calls are dominated by casual/brown-shoe brands (Crocs/HEYDUDE, UGG x2, fashion boots) plus one basketball and one Nike-type planner.
