# DKS (DICK'S Sporting Goods) — Earnings Calls: Results, Guidance History and Key Quotes

**Source set:** Seven FactSet corrected transcripts (Q3 FY2024 through Q2 FY2026) in `02_DKS_TRANSCRIPTS/earnings_calls/`, plus five slide-text extractions in `02_DKS_TRANSCRIPTS/slides_text/` (Q1 FY2025 earnings-slide fragment; Investor Presentations dated Nov-2025, Mar-2026, May-2026, Aug-2026). Slide text is OCR-fragmentary; figures from slides are flagged "(slides)".

**Coverage gap:** The Q4 FY2024 call (held March 11, 2025, where initial FY2025 guidance was issued) is **not** in the transcript folder. Initial FY2025 guidance is reconstructed from the "prior expectation" references on the Q1 FY2025 call (May 28, 2025) and from the Nov-2025 investor deck.

**Terminology (set on the Q3 FY2025 call, Nov 25, 2025):** "DICK'S Business" = DICK'S Sporting Goods, Golf Galaxy, Going Going Gone!, Public Lands banners plus GameChanger; EPS for the DICK'S Business excludes the 9.6 million shares issued for Foot Locker. "Foot Locker Business" = Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs Sports, WSS, atmos. Foot Locker enters the comp base in Q4 FY2026 (14th full month post-acquisition). Foot Locker closed September 8, 2025.

All quotes below are verbatim from the FactSet corrected transcripts. No opinions are added.

## 3. Reported results snapshot per quarter

### Q3 FY2024 (call 26-Nov-2024)
- **Comp:** +4.2% (calendar-shift adjusted); average ticket +4.8%, transactions –0.6%. Third straight quarter of >4% comp.
- **Net sales:** +0.5% to $3.06B; calendar shift (53rd week LY) moved a key BTS week out of Q3: ~–$105M sales, –$0.35 EPS.
- **Gross margin:** $1.09B, 35.77% of sales, +67 bps vs non-GAAP LY; merchandise margin +84 bps ("favorable sales mix and the quality of our assortment"), partly offset by occupancy deleverage from the calendar shift. (Gupta in Q&A: "the merch margin increased 83 basis points.")
- **SG&A (non-GAAP):** +7.2% to $787.1M, deleveraged 162 bps (~65 bps from calendar shift); rest from marketing, technology, talent and higher incentive comp.
- **Pre-opening:** $16.8M, down $3.6M (timing of openings).
- **EBT:** $297.1M, 9.7% of sales vs $321.1M / 10.6% non-GAAP LY; ~95 bps calendar-shift impact.
- **EPS:** $2.75 vs $2.85 non-GAAP LY (–$0.35 calendar shift).
- **YTD (39 weeks):** sales +4.8% to $9.55B; comp +4.7% (ticket +3.7%, transactions +1%); EBT $1.12B / 11.75% vs $975.3M / 10.71%; EPS $10.43 vs $9.08 (+15%).
- **Balance sheet:** cash ~$1.5B; no borrowings on $1.6B facility; inventory +13% (deliberate key-item investment; "clearance inventory is down meaningfully year-over-year").
- **Capital:** net capex ~$185M; dividends $90M; repurchased ~35,000 shares for $6.7M at $194.22; YTD buybacks $170.3M.
- **Stores:** House of Sport 19 open (3 in Q3 + 2 in Nov); Field House 26 open (5 in Q3 + 4 in Nov); 12 HoS at end of 2023, ~20 at end of 2024; only ~2% sq ft growth in 2024; 90% of stores have premium full-service footwear decks; Fort Worth, TX DC broke ground (open early 2026).
- **GameChanger:** >5.5M unique active users in Q3 (+21%); ~2M average daily active users; streamed games +50%; ~$100M 2024 revenue; 30%–40% CAGR.
- **Other:** 1.5M more athletes entered the ecosystem in the quarter; vertical brands carry 600–800 bps higher margin than national brands.

### Q1 FY2025 (call 28-May-2025)
- **Comp:** +4.5% (ticket +3.7%, transactions +0.8%); two-year stack 9.8%, three-year 13.4%; fifth straight quarter >4%.
- **Net sales:** +5.2% to $3.17B.
- **Gross margin:** $1.17B, 36.7%, +41 bps, driven by merchandise margin.
- **SG&A (non-GAAP):** +7% to $791.2M, deleveraged 42 bps (digital, in-store, marketing investments; partly offset by lower incentive comp).
- **Pre-opening:** $13.4M, down $7.7M.
- **Operating income (non-GAAP):** $360.4M, 11.35% vs $334.5M / 11.08%. Other income $13.3M (–$8.3M). **EBT:** $361.6M, 11.39% vs $342.4M / 11.34%.
- **Tax:** ~24% vs 19.6% LY (~440 bps; LY had ~$0.19 equity-award benefit).
- **EPS:** non-GAAP $3.37 (+2.1% vs $3.30); GAAP $3.24 (non-cash losses on Foot Locker stock investment).
- **Balance sheet:** cash ~$1B; no borrowings on $1.6B facility; inventory +12%.
- **Capital:** net capex $242M; dividends $100M; repurchased 1.4M shares for $298.7M at $218.65.
- **Stores:** 2 HoS in Q1 + 1 in May; 4 FH in Q1 + 2 more; ~16 HoS and ~16 FH for 2025; YE ~35 HoS, "just over 40" FH; ~20 HoS planned for 2026; GGPC 27 → 35 in 2025; ~30% of DICK'S stores in malls.
- **GameChanger:** >6.5M unique active users; ~2.2M daily active users (+~28%); >$100M 2024 revenue growing to $150M; 40%–50% top-line growth; Unrivaled Sports investment (600,000 youth athletes, 2M families; ~$40B youth-sports TAM).
- **Other:** >20M new athletes acquired over three years; ~$140B US TAM, ~8% share; no tariff impact in Q1.

### Q2 FY2025 (call 28-Aug-2025)
- **Comp:** +5% (ticket +4.1%, transactions +0.9%) on +4.5% LY and +2% in 2023; two-year stack 9.5%, three-year 11.5%; sixth straight >4%.
- **Net sales:** +5% to $3.65B.
- **Gross margin:** $1.35B, 37.06%, +33 bps (merch margin +18 bps plus occupancy leverage); "minimal impact from tariffs in Q2."
- **SG&A (non-GAAP):** +9.9% to $864M, deleveraged 105 bps.
- **Pre-opening:** $12.3M, +$3.4M.
- **Operating income (non-GAAP):** $475M, 13.02% vs $480.5M / 13.83%. **EBT:** $472.6M, 12.96% vs $482.3M / 13.89%.
- **EPS:** non-GAAP $4.38 vs $4.37; GAAP $4.71 (non-cash gains on FL stock; merger/integration/financing costs).
- **Balance sheet:** cash ~$1.2B; no borrowings on new $2B unsecured facility; inventory +7.1%.
- **Capital:** net capex $213M; dividends $96M; no repurchases cited (S-4 restrictions).
- **Stores:** 1 HoS in Q2, 13 planned Q3 (record); 4 FH in Q2, 6 in Q3; YE ~35 HoS / ~42 FH.
