**Conventions**
- Income-statement and balance-sheet figures are in **$ thousands** exactly as printed in the release financial statements. Capital-allocation summary figures are in **$ millions** where the release only prints millions. Per-share data in dollars. Shares in thousands unless stated.
- "[D]" = derived value (e.g., Q4 = FY minus 39 weeks; Q2 = 26 weeks minus Q1). Every derived value is marked. Every table cites the release date(s) it came from.
- "DSG" = DICK'S Sporting Goods banner stores; "HoS" = DICK'S House of Sport; "FH" = DICK'S Field House; "GG" = Golf Galaxy; "GGPC" = Golf Galaxy Performance Center; "GGG" = Going Going Gone!; "PL" = Public Lands; "WS" = Warehouse Sale; "FL" = Foot Locker; "M&I" = merger and integration costs; "EBIT" = income from operations / operating income; "EBT" = income before income taxes / pre-tax income.
- Fiscal years: FY2022 = 52 weeks ended Jan 28, 2023; FY2023 = 53 weeks ended Feb 3, 2024; FY2024 = 52 weeks ended Feb 1, 2025; FY2025 = 52 weeks ended Jan 31, 2026; FY2026 = 52 weeks ending Jan 30, 2027.
- Later releases reclassified some prior-period lines (grand-opening advertising moved from SG&A to pre-opening beginning FY2024; deferred-compensation plan adjustments added to the non-GAAP bridge beginning Q4 FY2023). Where a later release restates an earlier quarter, both figures are shown and labeled.

## 7. Caveats, ambiguities and cross-reference notes

### 7.1 Source-quality caveats
1. **Markdown table extraction is garbled in places.** The EDGAR-to-markdown conversion splits numbers across empty cells and sometimes drops the "%" sign; all figures above were read from the numeric cells, and percentages were checked against the "% of Sales" columns. The 2025-11-25 release header reads "Weighted average diluted shares outstanding ... 87 ... 83 ... 4 ... 5%" (rounded millions); exact share counts come from the income statement (87,115 / 82,776).
2. **Q3 FY2022 and Q4 FY2022 store counts are not in those releases** (store tables begin with the 2023-05-23 release). They are taken from the "beginning stores" / prior-year columns of later releases (2023-05-23; 2023-11-21). The Q4 FY2022 specialty breakdown (98 GG, 7 PL, 15 GGG, 5 Field & Stream) is from the 2024-03-14 footnote.
3. **Store-count basis changed twice.** Beginning FY2025, Warehouse Sale locations (29 at Feb 1, 2025; 1.3M sq ft) are included in store counts and Going Going Gone! (FY2024 YE 856 → restated beginning FY2025 885). Also one FY2024 Field House opening previously counted as a DICK'S store was reclassified (FY2024 YE 678 DICK'S / 26 FH vs FY2025 beginning 677 / 27). Q3 FY2024 release gives only total DSG (727) with HoS (17) and FH (22) counts; DICK'S-format count was not split out.
4. **Comp definitions changed three times** (relocated stores from FY2022; GameChanger from FY2024; Warehouse Sale from FY2025; DICK'S Business basis from Q3 FY2025 with Foot Locker excluded until Q4 FY2026). Restated prior-year comps are listed in Section 1 where the company published them (2024-03-14 EX-99.2 and 2025-03-11 EX-99.2). The FY2024 calendar shift (53rd week in FY2023) means FY2024 comps compare like calendar weeks while reported net sales do not (Q1 +~$45M, Q2 +~$95M, Q3 −~$105M, Q4 −~$30M; neutral for the year).
5. **SG&A / pre-opening reclassification.** Beginning FY2024, grand-opening advertising moved from SG&A to pre-opening; FY2023 quarters were restated in the FY2024 releases (total $20,578K). Both the originally reported and reclassified figures are shown. The non-GAAP SG&A figures for Q4 FY2022, FY2022, Q3 FY2023, Q4 FY2023 and FY2023 were also restated when the deferred-compensation reclass was introduced; the later figure is used for "non-GAAP SG&A" and the original is noted.
6. **Non-GAAP EBIT before FY2024 is derived** (the company did not present non-GAAP income from operations until the 2025-03-11 release); derived as non-GAAP gross profit less non-GAAP SG&A (latest presentation) less pre-opening (reclassified basis). It is marked [D] throughout.
7. **Q2 FY2026 FL capex: derived net capex ($75,241K) exceeds derived gross capex ($74,367K)** because FL construction allowances fell from $1,175K (Q1 YTD) to $301K (26-week YTD), implying a negative Q2 allowance of $(874)K. Taken as printed.
8. **Q3 FY2025 investment-gain tax effect.** The after-tax effect of the $6,376K pre-tax investment gain is $(15,702)K because of a $10.8M favorable tax item (gain on the pre-existing FL stake became non-taxable at closing). This is as printed, not an error.
9. **FL segment gross margin and segment-profit margins** are derived from the supplemental tables (segment GP or profit / segment sales). The company's consolidated non-GAAP gross margin excludes the FL inventory write-downs (Q4 FY25 $217.9M; Q1 FY26 $42.7M; Q2 FY26 $(2.3)M credit), which are reported in "corporate and other" and not in FL segment gross profit; the FL segment-level GP therefore already excludes them.
10. **"Total company store locations"** stated in the releases (3,230 / 3,195 / 3,115 / 3,104) = DICK'S Business stores + FL owned stores; FL licensed stores are excluded from those totals.
11. **FY2023 53rd week**: Q4 FY2023 was 14 weeks ($170,223K net sales; ~$0.19 EPS from the extra week); the Q4 FY2024 YoY comparison (+0.5%) is against the 14-week base.
12. **Guidance recon rounding**: FY2026 guidance reconciliations print per-share adjustments that do not always add exactly (2026-03-12 notes "Column does not add due to rounding"). Litigation settlement is printed as $(175)M in 2026-03-12 and $(174)M in 2026-05-27 / 2026-08-25 (actual recorded $174,464K).
13. **Pro forma figures (2025-09-05 and 2026-09-21)** use FL's historical statements reclassified to DKS presentation and transaction accounting adjustments; they exclude synergies and are "for illustrative purposes only." They are not comparable to the reported FL segment results.
