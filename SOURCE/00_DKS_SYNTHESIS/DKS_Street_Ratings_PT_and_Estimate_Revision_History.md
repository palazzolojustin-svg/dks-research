### E.1 Ratings and headline conclusions
- DKS upgraded to Overweight, PT $240 (14.5x FY27 EPS $16.50; up/down $285/$150). ASO Equal Weight, PT $50 (7.5x FY27 GAAP EPS $6.54; FY26 EPS $6.11; up/down $70/$30). NKE Equal Weight, PT $40 (22.5x CY27 EPS $1.75).
- Industry thesis: a $150-250B US market (definition-dependent) growing +LSD that has consolidated, shifted to higher-margin softlines/brands/omni-channel, and held Amazon's category share at ~13% for most of a decade; demand backdrop "as healthy as it has been in two decades."

### E.2 Demand drivers
- Participation: daily sports/exercise participation 23% of US population (all-time high; <20% two decades ago); annual participation ~75%; inactivity below 20% for first time after seven consecutive years of decline (SFIA: 250M participated at least once in 2025, ~+1% YoY; team sports 90M+ participants; pickleball fastest-growing five years running). Sports/exercise hours as % of leisure hours 6.6% in 2025 vs 5.7% in 2003-07; per-participant share has fallen (31% -> 27%), i.e., more casual participants. Age 15-19 cohort at 13.3% (+172bps vs long-term avg); 45-54 at 7.0% (+162bps).
- Youth: NSCH participation (ages 6-17) 58% in 2024 from 49% in 2021; 42% for households below poverty vs 75% at 400%+ of poverty; avg spend on a child's primary sport $1,016 in 2024 (+46% vs 2019, ~2x inflation), ~$1,500 incl. secondary sports; $100k+ households spend $2,361/child vs $890 for <$49k; total family youth-sports spend >$40B/yr (Aspen Institute). State range ~49-74%; growth strongest in lower-cost recreational formats (Southwest).
- Outdoor (OIA 2025 report): record 181M participants in 2024 (>60% of population 6+, from 50% in 2007; +3% YoY); seniors +7.4%, youth +5.6%, Black +12.8%, Hispanic +11.8%; $100k+ households +5% 4-yr CAGR; regional 2020-24 gains: West South Central +840bps, Middle Atlantic +680bps, Mountain +670bps; average participant age 34.7 -> 38.8; hikers participate in other activities 85% of the time.
- Wellness: US wellness economy $2.1T, +8% CAGR 2019-24 (Global Wellness Institute), $6.3K/person; record 77M gym members (23% of population >6, +20% vs 2019), ~96M incl. non-members; Google Trends for "health/wellness/nutrition/gym" at or near highs; GLP-1 and "MAHA" cited.

### E.5 Mix, brands, channel
- Softlines shift: DKS softlines 43% (FY06) -> 63% (FY24: hardlines 37% / apparel 34% / footwear 29%) -> 76% pro forma FY25 with FL (hardlines 24% / apparel 27% / footwear 49%). - Brand concentration: Nike ~25% of core DKS (12% FY06; ~20% FY15-16); FL ~55-60% Nike in 2025; combined ~36-37%. ASO Nike 12% (14% FY17). Brand access cited by experts as the primary reason ASO under-comps DKS (DKS Nike +36% in FY22 drove footwear +16% while ASO Nike -6%).
- E-commerce: DKS consolidated ~16% e-com (DSG 12% FY17, 29% FY20, ~15% FY24); 70% of sales from omni customers, 90% store-enabled, 80% of online orders store-fulfilled; e-com became profit-neutral after 2017 in-housing. ASO 12% (4% FY17; target 15% in 5 yrs); ASO 1Q26 e-com +17% vs B&M +1%.
