# DKS — Financial Statements and KPIs from 10-K / 10-Q filings (FY2020 – FY2026-Q2)

All figures in **$ thousands** except per-share data, percentages, store counts and square footage, exactly as printed in the filings. Parentheses = negative / credit. Sources are the markdown files in `01_DKS_SEC_FILINGS/10-K/` and `01_DKS_SEC_FILINGS/10-Q/` (abbreviated below as, e.g., **10-K FY2025** = `DKS_10-K_FY2025_filed-2026-03-27.md`; **10-Q FY2025-Q3** = `DKS_10-Q_FY2025-Q3_filed-2025-12-05.md`).

Fiscal calendar: fiscal year ends the Saturday closest to January 31. FY2020 = 52 wks ended Jan 30, 2021; FY2021 = 52 wks ended Jan 29, 2022; FY2022 = 52 wks ended Jan 28, 2023; **FY2023 = 53 wks ended Feb 3, 2024** (53rd week added $170.2 million of net sales and ~$0.19 of diluted EPS per 10-K FY2024); FY2024 = 52 wks ended Feb 1, 2025; FY2025 = 52 wks ended Jan 31, 2026 (includes Foot Locker from Sep 8, 2025); FY2026 in progress.

**Derived figures**: DKS does not publish Q4 financial statements in its 10-K (press-release exhibits are not in this file set), so every Q4 column is **derived = fiscal year (10-K) minus 39-week YTD (Q3 10-Q)** and is marked "derived". Quarterly cash-flow figures for Q2/Q3 are derived from YTD differences. Diluted EPS and share counts for derived quarters are **not disclosed** (quarterly averages cannot be derived from annual averages).

**Restatements**: beginning FY2024, grand-opening advertising was reclassified from SG&A to pre-opening expenses; the 10-K FY2024 and FY2024 10-Qs restate FY2023 and FY2022 on that basis (FY2023 SG&A 3,204,108 -> 3,183,530; pre-opening 47,262 -> 67,840; FY2022 SG&A 2,805,462 -> 2,799,853; pre-opening 16,077 -> 21,686). Gross profit and operating income are unaffected. Quarterly tables use the basis of the filing cited.
