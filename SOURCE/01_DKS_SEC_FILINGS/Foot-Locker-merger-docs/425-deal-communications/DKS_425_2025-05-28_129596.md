**Agreement to Acquire Foot Locker**

On May 15, 2025, the Company announced that it entered into a definitive merger agreement to acquire Foot Locker, Inc., a leading footwear and apparel
retailer. Under the terms of the merger agreement, Foot Locker shareholders will elect to receive either (i) $24.00 in cash or (ii) 0.1168 shares of DICKS Sporting Goods common stock for each share of Foot Locker common stock, for a total
equity value of approximately $2.4 billion and an enterprise value of approximately $2.5 billion. The completion of the acquisition is subject to Foot Locker shareholder approval and other customary closing conditions, including regulatory
approvals, and is expected to close in the second half of 2025. The Company intends to finance the acquisition through a combination of cash-on-hand, revolving borrowings and other new debt, to the degree Foot Locker shareholders do not elect to
receive their consideration entirely in shares of the Companys common stock.
