Retail Bonds Reward Long-End Quality and Select High Yield
(Bloomberg Intelligence) -- Retail bond performance looks to remain issuer- and maturity-specific,
with long-dated Amazon.com, Target, Home Depot and Dollar General debt benefiting from spread
compression, while Alibaba and Dick’s remain pressure points. High yield retail still offers stronger
relative carry and excess-return momentum, led by Michaels, though Guitar Center, Nordstrom and VF
Corp keep downside dispersion elevated. (09/22/26)
