Athleisure App Trends Mixed as Summer Momentum Fades: Chart Pack
(Bloomberg Intelligence) -- Athleisure app engagement could be uneven into autumn as back-to-
school product launches and marketing fail to translate into broad-based user growth. August trends
were mixed, according to Sensor Tower. Under Armour was a stand out with strong gains in downloads
and active users, while Puma's higher downloads may signal improving interest despite softer
engagement. Nike and SNKRS were pressured, while Adidas' momentum moderated following the
World Cup. Lululemon engagement was subdued despite new products and digital features aimed at
improving discovery.
Sensor Tower tracks new downloads and monthly active users on apps from iOS and Android stores.
