Earnings on Deck Week of 03/09/26: Positive ULTA (price target up to $712). As we approach
the end of the 4Q25 earnings season, the key themes as we see it are: 1) resilient consumer
demand; 2) better-than-expected tariff impact on margins; 3) lower costs from freight, cotton,
polyesters, and sourcing; and 4) clean and disciplined inventory. However, we note the potential
risks of rising oil prices and consumer slowing associated with geopolitical risk. We continue to
believe stock selection will be critical as demand uncertainty persists and consumers are
choiceful with their spend. While surging gas prices are an incremental headwind, companies
driving sales acceleration with pricing power should outperform as tariff headwinds subside in
CY2H26. We further highlight that many of our companies are issuing prudently conservative
guidance which continues to factor-in IEEPA tariff rates, suggesting there could be upside
potential in 2H26 should the current global 15% rate hold.
Reiterate Positive Industry View for 2026. We remain Positive on Specialty Retail, Apparel &
Footwear for 2026 as inventories at a sector-level seem well-positioned, which we believe will
be supportive of merchandise margins. We continue to highlight five key points which support
our Positive industry view: 1) inventory discipline is better than expected; 2) promotions remain
under control; 3) potential healthier U.S. macro backdrop in 2026, driven by Fed cuts, and the
One Big Beautiful Bill providing stimulus, offset by geopolitical tensions; 4) tariff headwinds to
ease in CY2H26 as effective tariff rates move lower, while price increases likely prove sticky; and
5) consumer spending should be supported by strong macro and higher-end HH spending.
Price inflation drives sales growth; worst of the tariff pressure is over. In the aggregate for
our sector, we continue to believe that price increases have the potential to dampen some
demand, but we also highlight an unexpected side benefit: price inflation necessarily drives
sales growth. Further, we expect our retailers to be more optimistic about the extent of the tariff
headwind going forward, as relief to effective tariff rates appears likely following the SCOTUS
ruling.

BMO: DKS; Short Interest 8.6%
• Expect 4Q25 upside driven by DICK'S, partially offset by Foot Locker; guidance is
typically conservative but we believe management could reiterate FL being accretive by
2H26. We have a positive medium- to long-term view on the core DICK'S banner, but are
cautious on Foot Locker for 4Q25 and FY26 as the business undergoes a necessary reset. It's
also important to note that 4Q25 earnings results will be the first full quarter with the Foot
Locker banner consolidated in results (3Q25 was 8-week stub quarter) and, as such, we
expect substantial incremental information on the combined DKS-FL entity. For DKS, we
expect continued healthy comp growth although in the positive LSD range versus recent MSD
trend, expanding gross margins for core DKS, and ongoing growth-focused OpEx investments;
while FL will be challenged by the margin reset it laid out last quarter. Recall, core DICK'S
results were solid in 3Q25, beating on sales and EPS driven by +5.7% comp growth fueled by
both average ticket +4.4% and transactions +1.3%. Foot Locker, meanwhile, posted comp
sales -4.7% in 3Q25, and its 4Q25 guidance calls for sharper headwinds ahead due to 1) comp
sales down mid- to high-single digits, and 2) gross margin to be down between 1,000bps to
1,500bps. While Foot Locker's headwinds impacting 4Q25 are outsized, we see the actions as
necessary pain-points which will enable the company to quickly clear unproductive inventory
via markdowns and the jobber-channel, with the ultimate aim of being clean for 2026. While
this will likely push promotions to deeper levels in the near term, we continue to have a
positive long-term view on better promotional levels across the total sporting goods
landscape post-merger, as we expect Foot Locker to be more disciplined under the DKS
umbrella.

AMC: ULTA; Short Interest 4.9%
• Expect 4Q25 beat; expect positive 2026 guidance. During 4Q25, we noted that ULTA
expanded its Health and Wellness category as well as Asian beauty (such as K Beauty and J
Beauty). Since CEO Kecia Steelman took the helm roughly one year ago, we have seen ULTA's
strategic initiatives gain traction, with successful execution of key promotional events, new
product launches, focus on optimizing points of retail distribution, and secular growth of
health and wellness trends positioning ULTA to return to its dominant position as the
omnichannel leader in the beauty segment. Furthermore, while consumer discretionary
demand in the U.S. market remains an unknown variable, we believe that beauty, and in
particular ULTA’s core customer (aka the "beauty enthusiast"), will continue to over-index
wallet share toward beauty, health, and wellness categories. In addition, ULTA remains
relatively tariff-insulated versus the rest of our coverage. We expect another quarter of
outperformance and potential for positive 2026 guidance as fundamentals continue to
improve and the company continues to execute on its near-and-long-term strategies to scale
the business. We reiterate our OW rating despite any near-term pressure as ULTA continues to
execute and deliver on its near-and-long-term objectives including: 1) controlled use of
promotions; 2) expanding its product assortment with both new and exclusives, across both
mass and prestige, such as in Health and Wellness and Asian Beauty; 3) launching high
impact marketing campaigns; and 4) elevating its go-to-market strategy.
• Raising FY25 estimate; maintaining FY26 and FY27 estimates; raising multiple and PT to
$712 (from $680). Our FY25, FY26, and FY27 adj. EPS estimates are $25.70 (from $25.50),
$28.50 (unchanged), and $32.37 (unchanged). Our new PT of $712 (from $680) is based on an
NTM P/E multiple of 22x (from 21x) applied to our CY27 adj. EPS estimate of $32.37
(unchanged). We raise our multiple one turn as we believe ULTA is a multi-year share winner
in the beauty space. Our multiple compares to the Street NTM P/E multiple of ~22.2x.
