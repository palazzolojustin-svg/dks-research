Athleisure App Trends Diverge Into Back-to-School: Chart Pack
(Bloomberg Intelligence) -- Athleisure app engagement could stay uneven into back-to-school as
product momentum and marketing lift select brands and softer demand weigh on others. Trends were
mixed in July, according to Sensor Tower. Under Armour led app growth as its turnaround progresses,
while Nike engagement fell, with recent App Store reviews and Reddit discussions pointing to
customer frustration with technical issues after an app update during the month. Adidas active users
rose 12%, likely aided by World Cup activations and product drops, and Puma also gained as autumn
footwear launches built back-to-school demand. Lululemon engagement remains soft despite app-
exclusive sales, launches and new features aimed at improving discovery.
Sensor Tower tracks new downloads and monthly active users on apps from iOS and Android stores.
