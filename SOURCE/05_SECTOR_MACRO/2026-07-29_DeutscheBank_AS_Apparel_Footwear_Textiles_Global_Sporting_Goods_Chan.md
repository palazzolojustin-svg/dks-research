Global Sporting Goods Channel Checks: 
Athletic Slows, Sandals Show 
Over the past several days, we conducted channel checks across the US, EMEA, 
and China that reinforce an increasingly bifurcated athletic and outdoor market. 
Across all regions, contacts described a persistent "winners vs. losers" 
environment, with brands offering compelling newness, innovation, and clear 
product segmentation taking share. We walked away with increased conviction in 
AS’ Arc’teryx and Salomon brands, with Salomon consistently praised by our 
contacts across all regions with a Q/Q acceleration noted by our China contact in 
2Q. We think yesterday’s -5% pullback provides a compelling entry point to own 
one of the cleanest growth stories in our coverage. Conversely, our checks raised 
incremental concerns on NKE internationally (i.e., Spring 2027 order books 
written down), whereas our US contacts noted improving momentum behind key 
running models. See pages 2-4 for 2Q trends, order book changes, inventory 
levels and promos, and key brand takeaways.   
Key Macro Themes from Our Conversations 
While higher energy costs, macro uncertainty, and weather disruptions continue 
to pressure demand, innovation remains the clearest driver of share gains. 
Specifically, we heard: 1) continued strength in sandals and seasonal footwear 
globally; 2) a meaningful World Cup-driven demand boost in Europe; 3) 
consumers remaining willing to pay for differentiated product; 4) a notable 
slowdown in US run-specialty demand since April; 5) accelerating promotional 
activity in China; and 6) increasing retailer selectivity around inventory 
commitments and shelf-space allocation.  
Key Brand Takes from Our Conversations  
Our checks increased our conviction in Salomon (AS), while raising incremental 
concerns around NKE internationally. Salomon was arguably the most 
consistently praised brand across all regions, with contacts highlighting 
continued market share gains, strong innovation, and growing relevance across 
performance, outdoor, and lifestyle categories. NKE remains most polarized in 
our checks: US wholesale partners highlighted improving momentum behind 
Pegasus, Vomero, and Structure, while EMEA and China contacts remain 
concerned by still-subdued consumer interest, elevated inventory levels, ongoing 
markdown activity, and further shelf-space loss into Spring 2027.

Inventory Levels and Promotional Activity 
▪ 
US Contact #1: Inventory remains relatively controlled across both 
brands and retailers, limiting the industry's ability to increase promotions 
materially. The contact instead highlighted reduced vendor marketing 
support and modest expense controls.  
▪ 
US Contact #2: Inventory is somewhat elevated relative to expectations, 
though revision privileges have helped manage exposure. The contact 
expects holiday discounting could increase if sales do not improve in 3Q.  
▪ 
EMEA Contact #1: Promotional intensity remains elevated across 
Europe, particularly within NKE and lifestyle footwear. The contact 
continues to view excess NKE inventory as a significant overhang on the 
market.  
▪ 
China Contact #1: Promotional activity has increased materially, with the 
retailer shifting to monthly promotions (as opposed to only for special 
periods) as consumers become increasingly discount-oriented and 
inventory risk remains a priority. 
Brand-Specific Takeaways 
▪ 
Nike (NKE; Hold, TP $45): Views were highly polarized by region, with 
both US contacts constructive while EMEA and China remained 
negative. US Contact #2 cited Nike as the standout performer in 2Q, 
driven by strong demand for Pegasus, Vomero and Structure, improved 
inventory availability, strong turns, and attractive wholesale economics. 
In contrast, EMEA Contact #1 remained concerned by persistent 
markdowns, excess marketplace inventory, and weak brand momentum, 
while China Contact #1 expects conditions to worsen before improving 
despite ongoing inventory clearance efforts. Both international contacts 
questioned execution and brand positioning, whereas the US channel is 
seeing clear share gains and improving wholesale productivity.  
▪ 
Adidas (ADS GY; covered by DB analyst Adam Cochrane; Buy, TP €210): 
Sentiment was consistently positive across regions. EMEA Contact #1 
highlighted Adidas' balanced portfolio, strong innovation pipeline, and 
continued market share gains, while comparing current running 
momentum to prior periods of major product success. China Contact #1 
reported double-digit growth, citing strong execution, World Cup 
momentum, and a successful "China-for-China" operating strategy. The 
brand was also identified as a key beneficiary of incremental shelf space 
and open-to-buy dollars at several retail partners.

channel remains constructive on Puma's running category but sees 
ongoing challenges in lifestyle and overall brand positioning.
