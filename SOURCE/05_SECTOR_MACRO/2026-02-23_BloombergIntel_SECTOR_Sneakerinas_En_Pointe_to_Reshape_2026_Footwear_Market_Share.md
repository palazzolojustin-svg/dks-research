Sneakerinas En Pointe to Reshape 2026 Footwear, Market Share
(Bloomberg Intelligence) -- Shifting footwear demand toward slimmer, fashion-forward sneaker
silhouettes in 2026 stand to benefit larger lifestyle incumbents, particularly Adidas and Puma, while
creating an opportunity for Nike to rebuild share if product innovation gains traction, based on BI's
proprietary analysis. On, Hoka and Salomon are rightfully prioritizing technical credibility over trend
participation to boost share. (02/23/26)
1. Emerging Fashion Trends Could Spur Sneaker Demand
Traditional footwear leaders such as Nike, Adidas and Puma can regain share as fashion preferences
shift back toward more aesthetically driven, mixed-use styles, supported by their scale, distribution
and established lifestyle positioning. All three already have deep consumer awareness and historical
authority in lifestyle sneakers, giving them an advantage as trends evolve toward slimmer profiles,
sneakerinas, clogs and other fashion-led forms. In a more selective consumer-spending backdrop,
these fashion shifts can help support demand and drive full-price sell through.
By contrast, players such as On and Hoka are likely to remain laser-focused on technical performance
while building more measured, less trend-dependent lifestyle franchises, selectively testing limited
releases to maintain brand heat. (02/23/26)

3. Adidas Finds Next Act; Nike, Puma Chase Right Formula
Contributing Analysts Sydney Goodman (Retail)
The lifestyle pivot in sneakers is driving share gains for Adidas and Puma, as newer fashion-forward
franchises outpace legacy retro styles. Adidas has successfully built on momentum from its Samba and
Gazelle, with its on-trend Tokyo and Taekwondo shoes, while Puma's Speedcat is resonating as
consumer preferences shift toward slimmer, low-profile silhouettes with broader lifestyle appeal.
Nike is also experimenting with ballet-inspired “sneakerina” concepts and slimmer profiles, and its
new Mind franchise, which emphasizes sensory engagement, is a bid to recapture share. Though
Nike's progress in lifestyle has been slower, leaving it without a truly scaled, on-trend lifestyle
franchise since Air Force 1, it's making inroads in the core performance sneakers category. (02/23/26)

Athletic Brands Take Fashion Silhouettes Sporty
4. Ugg, Birkenstock Face Competition; Credibility Matters
As athletic brands expand into recovery clogs, sandals and mule-style footwear, competition with
comfort-first players such as Ugg, Birkenstock and Crocs is intensifying. Nike, Adidas and Puma are
increasingly offering products that overlap directly with these categories, and while this validates
their popularity, it raises the bar for differentiation. For established comfort brands, success now
depends on maintaining clear comfort leadership rather than relying solely on brand awareness. Ugg
has showed that strong marketing and comfort positioning mixed with fashion can still resonate, but
sustained success will depend on continued differentiation.
Birkenstock maintains a stronghold in sandals, though further momentum in closed-toe styles may
require greater innovation in materials, silhouettes or its narrative. (02/23/26)

8. Product Innovation, Expansion Key to Better Sales
Deckers' robust innovation pipeline, with updates to key Hoka franchises and the expansion of Ugg
into year-round products and men's, can boost revenue by at least 10% in fiscal 2026 vs. consensus'
high-single digits, our analysis shows. Ugg's growing relevance outside of its core -- sandals, clogs and
