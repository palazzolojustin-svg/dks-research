# V1: Wayback densification (FL/Champs/Kids FL/FL Canada), new-arrival cohort analysis, fresh live census 2026-10-08
## Method
- CDX prefix queries (scripts/V1_cdx.py, V1_cdx2.py; day-collapsed, 200-only) for www.{footlocker.com, champssports.com, kidsfootlocker.com, footlocker.ca}: /category/sale*, mens|womens|kids/shoes(.html and sub-pages), shoes/clothing/mens/womens/kids.html, brands/*. Query-string variants with facets/page params were excluded (they change totals); plain and sort-only variants kept. Fetched `id_` captures, parsed embedded search JSON (totalResults, Sale Product facet, first-48 products incl. variant newArrivalDate) with W03 parser (scripts/V1_snap.py; ~2,650 captures parsed, raw/V1/snaps.jsonl; brand-page fetch was still running at write-up). Merged with W03 rows (W03 anomalies dropped) -> data/V1_timeseries.csv (1,632+ rows; cols dom,path,date,total,sale_facet,sale_share,p1 depth, p1_new_after_20250908/20260701 = share of first-48 listings whose earliest dated variant newArrivalDate is after the cut, source). Analysis: scripts/V1_analyze.py (output raw/V1/analyze_out.txt), V1_census_an.py.
- Live 2026-10-08: scripts/V1_live.py (top-level pages on all 4 sites; exact counts) and V1_census.py / V1_census_champs.py (full brand x sale census, men/women/kids shoes+apparel; raw/V1/census_*.jsonl, brand_counts csv).
- Layout check: kidsfootlocker.com and footlocker.ca use the same embedded JSON and the same "Sale Product" facet as footlocker.com (Champs uses "Sale Product" + "Eligible For Discount"/"New Arrivals"). Same parse works. Kids FL domain has /category/kids/shoes.html and /shoes.html but no men's/women's pages; footlocker.ca has Canadian-dollar prices and very sparse Wayback coverage (6 mens-shoes captures, 4 sale-page captures since 2025).
- HARD LIMIT: Wayback simply does not hold weekly captures of the category pages. After day-collapse, FL men's shoes has only 21 captures from Aug-2025 on (2 in Aug-Oct 2025: 8/26 only plus one more; none between 8/26 and ~11/24/2025), so Q3 FY25 men's-shoes share rests on 2 points. Dense (near-weekly) series exist only for the Sale pages (FL 64, Champs 41, Kids FL 30 captures since Aug-2025). Unit = listings (style-colour) on the online catalogue; sale share = Sale Product facet / totalResults. Q3 FY25 = 2025-08-03..11-01; Q3 FY26 = 2026-08-02..10-08 (data to date).

## Evidence AGAINST (X)
- [X] The drop in sale share happened between Aug-25 and Dec-25 (25.5% -> 11-14%) and has since RISEN slightly (9.0% Feb-26 -> 14.3-17.6% Jul-Sep 26 for men's); live 13.9% is only ~5 pts below the 2026 H1 level, and Champs/FL all-shoes shares fell ~4 pts Jul->Oct 2026. The step-change coincided with post-close clearance, not BTS.
- [X] Sample gaps: Q3 FY25 men's shoes and all-shoes have only 1-2 captures (8/26 plus the 6/30 all-shoes point); the y/y headline for men's shoes rests on that one 8/26 point (25.5%), which W03 showed is consistent with the 26-33% 2024-25 range, so not an outlier, but an average over Aug-Oct 2025 is NOT measurable. Wayback has no weekly category captures; densification only worked for Sale pages.
- [X] New-listing sale share is mechanically low; the older-listing on-sale share (~21% FL, ~20% Champs) is above the all-listing share (~14%), i.e. markdown tail remains. Depth when on sale (first-48 variant depth): 24-27% in Jul-Oct 2026 vs 28-45% Jan-May 2026: lower than the clearance peak but not evidence of shallower discount vs 2025 (W03: 20-29% in 2024-25).
- [X] Listings in 2026 are far below 2024 (e.g. men's 2,294 vs ~3,400): fewer SKUs may reduce sales as well as markdowns; online listing counts say nothing about sell-through.
- [X] Canada's high and sticky promo share (24-48%) shows the platform can still discount heavily when inventory is long; it is small.
