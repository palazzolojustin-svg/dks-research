Foot Locker inventory shift: what the new data shows

Bottom line

  • The "less discounting" half of the thesis has new hard support. On footlocker.com, the share of men's shoes on sale fell from 26–33% every month of 2024–Aug 2025 to 9–16% since Dec 2025 (14.8% on Oct 3, 2026). The number of men's shoe listings fell about a third (≈3,400 → ≈2,300).
  • Foot Locker now discounts far less than Nike's own site. 13.9% of FL men's shoes are on sale vs 46.9% on nike.com; on 20 matched Nike/Jordan styles the average discount is 13.4% at FL vs 31.4% at nike.com.
  • The "higher uptake" half is not proven. We found no new hard comp, traffic or sell-through data. Search, social, review and card-data avenues were stopped for cost or blocked.
  • Timing matters for the variance. The sale-share drop happened in Dec 2025, so Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 compare against periods that already had it. The y/y benefit shows up in Q3 FY26 and in the FY27 margin level.
  • Our variance (inference): FL gross margin +100–150bps vs consensus in 3Q26 (≈+$0.14–0.21 EPS) and +70–150bps in FY27 (≈+$0.42–0.90 EPS). No comp variance called.

Evidence against

  • Discount depth did not shrink. On-sale items are cut 21–45% in 2026 vs 20–29% in 2024–25. Fewer items are on sale, but those that are go deeper.
  • Checkout promotions are invisible to listing data. Late September: up to 30% off Nike/Jordan applied at checkout, 50% off NB 9060 and adidas Samba (Sep 25). Hoka up to 51% off (Sep 8). Tiered 20/25/30% BTS codes.
  • Low sale share already coexisted with a weak quarter. Q2 FY26 FL GM fell to 25.66% and comp was −3.6% while online sale share was 14–16%.
  • Newer brands are not clean. Hoka (19–24%) and New Balance (21–27%) are on sale more than Nike. Apparel is promo-heavy, with private label 88–97% on sale at 40–50% off.
  • The mix is still Nike-heavy. Nike+Jordan share of FL men's shoe listings rose from 34.3% to 38.9%; Champs is 48.6%.
  • The shift predates the DKS-bought assortment. The listing cut and sale-share drop were visible by Dec 11, 2025, before Fast Break scaled and before the BTS 2026 assortment. Some of it is clean-out, not new product.
  • FL is pricier than Nike. Being priced above nike.com on 13 of 20 styles protects margin but risks traffic.

Best avenues to keep tracking

  • Weekly catalog census (scripts/W01_census.py, ≈12 min): sale share and depth by brand through the Q3 print (~Nov 24). The holiday benchmark is Dec 2025: 2,608–2,866 sale-page items at 42–47% depth.
  • Wayback pipeline (scripts/W03_*.py): extend to new-arrival dates (share of catalog added after Sep 2025), kidsfootlocker.com and footlocker.ca.
  • Cross-retailer prices: Finish Line, JD, Hibbett and DICK'S blocked plain requests; a headless browser would be needed.
  • Not run: review counts (blocked; needs your decision), Google Trends, Reddit/social, import records, Fast Break store list. These are the ways to test the uptake half.
