# W03: Wayback time series (US) - footlocker.com / champssports.com catalog, sale, brand pages 2024-01 to 2026-10
## Method
- Wayback CDX (web.archive.org/cdx) to list captures of ~70 category URLs on www.footlocker.com and www.champssports.com (list in raw/W03/cdx_targets.json); fetched raw captures (`.../web/<ts>id_/...`) at ~1/month (2/month for FL sale.html), 2024-01 to 2026-10-03. Archived pages embed the server-side search JSON: `pagination.totalResults`, facets (Brand, Price, Miscellaneous incl. "Sale"/"Sale Product" count, "Eligible For Discount"), and the first 48 products with list/sale price (variant-level listPrice/salePrice).
- 266 parsed snapshots (raw/W03/snap_parsed.jsonl). Scripts: scripts/W03_wb.py (fetch), W03_parse.py, W03_snap.py (loop), W03_analyze.py (summary + CSV; W03_cdx/list/fetch.py were abandoned first attempts). Tidy dataset: data/W03_timeseries.csv (target, date, total listings, sale facet count, sale share, p1 discount depth, brand facet counts, n brands).
- Units: "listings" = style-colour products shown on the category page (online catalogue, not stores). Sale share = Sale facet count / totalResults. Depth = mean % off among on-sale items in the first 48 results (relevance-sorted, so indicative only). Excluded as capture anomalies: FL mens/shoes 2024-11-16 (58 results), 2025-02-09 (0), FL sale.html 2026-07-16 (62).
- Caveat: FL Nike/clothing/brand pages in 2024H1 show inflated totals (13-26k) consistent with a different site structure then; use footwear category series as the clean comparable.
