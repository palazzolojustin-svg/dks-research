# W05: Cross-retailer price / discount comparison
## Method
- Snapshot 2026-10-07. footlocker.com category/search pages parsed from server-rendered JSON (curl_cffi chrome impersonation): facet counts (men's shoes total vs sale collection, by brand) plus top-48-by-relevance product slices per brand (price, originalPrice). Pagination beyond page 0 is ignored server-side and the API is blocked (403), so the sample is 657 unique men's shoe colorways (top relevance per brand), not the full 2,303. Facet counts are complete.
- nike.com: 8 men's shoe category pages (shoes, sale, running, lifestyle, Jordan, basketball, new, Air Max), 883 unique style codes with currentPrice/initialPrice; page totals from totalResources. The Nike browse API returned 404.
- zappos.com: men's running and sneaker pages, 521 unique listings with price/originalPrice/onSale.
- Blocked or unusable: finishline.com (403), jdsports.com (404 and nothing usable), dickssportinggoods.com (403), hibbett.com (200 once, then 403; the one page gave 16 impressions, all discount 0, plus facet counts only). So the Finish Line / JD / Hibbett / DKS comparison was NOT achieved.
- Name-matched pairs (Nike/Jordan styles on both FL and nike.com): 20 styles. FL uses min price across sampled colorways, Nike likewise; rough.
- Scripts: scripts/W05_fl_brands.py, W05_others.py, W05_analyze.py, W05_match.py. Raw: raw/W05/. Data: data/W05_prices.csv (20 matched pairs).

## Evidence AGAINST (X)
- [X] Nike.com undercuts FL on 13 of 20 matched Nike/Jordan styles, often by 30-40% (AF1 '07 $67.97 vs $109.99). If consumers cross-shop, FL's higher shelf price vs the brand implies traffic/conversion risk in Nike lifestyle, FL's largest category, and any move by DKS to price-match would hit GM.
- [X] Nike is 726 of 2,303 men's shoe colorways (31.5%) and Jordan another 173 (7.5%): legacy Nike/Jordan is still ~39% of men's footwear count at FL, so no evidence of a large mix shift away from Nike in the online assortment. Nike on-sale share at FL (17.6%) exceeds the fleet figure (13.9%).
- [X] HOKA (23.8% on sale) and New Balance (21.4%) are among the more marked-down brands at FL, so new-brand does not equal low-markdown.
- [X] No time series or prior-year baseline was collected: a single snapshot cannot show less discounting than before. A Wayback comparison is the missing piece.
