## Known evidence FOR the thesis (FL inventory/SKU/assortment shift → higher uptake / less discounting)
Pre-DKS evidence that assortment and inventory discipline (not just traffic) drove FL's comps and GM. These set the baseline that the DKS assortment has to beat.
- **The last cycle where markdowns came down drove a big GM recovery.** Q3 FY24 GM was 29.6% vs 27.3% (+230bps), with merch margin +230bps "led by reduced markdowns". Q4 FY24 GM was 29.6% vs 26.6% (+300bps), again merch +300bps "led by reduced markdowns". Inventory was −6.3% y/y at Q3 FY24 and +1.1% at Q4 FY24 ("Controlled Inventory Levels"). This is precedent for GM upside when inventory is clean, even in an "elevated" promo environment. (P_FL_8K_B › 8-K/FL_8-K_2024-12-04_EX-99-1/EX-99-2; 8-K/FL_8-K_2025-03-05_EX-99-1/EX-99-2)
- **Mgmt said promo levels were lower y/y.** "less promotional this year as compared with last year": Q2 FY24 (ex-loyalty, merch margin improved) and Q3 FY24 (merch +230bps). (P_FL_10Q_B › FL_10-Q_FY2024-Q2; P_FL_10Q_C › FL_10-Q_FY2024-Q3)
- **Tighter inventory and better turns came first, then the comp turned.** Inventory was −10.0% y/y at Aug-3-24 ($1,648M vs $1,831M) and the Q2 FY24 comp turned to +2.6%. The 10-Q credits "concerted efforts to improve inventory turnover" ($51M CFO benefit). (P_FL_10Q_B › FL_10-Q_FY2024-Q2; P_FL_8K_B › 8-K/FL_8-K_2024-08-28_EX-99-2 "Healthy Inventory Levels Headed into 3Q24")
- **Product and assortment drove conversion.** Q2 FY24 comps rose on "improved conversion rates resulting from a positive customer response to product offerings ... and strategic promotions". Footwear was up "led by our array of strategic and emerging brand partners" (Q1 FY24, Q2 FY24, Q3 FY24: "exciting products from our array of strategic and emerging brand partners"). (P_FL_10Q_B, P_FL_10Q_C)
- **Assortment fixes worked by banner.**
  - Champs "Sport For Life" repositioning toward the active athlete went from comps of −25.3% (Q2 FY23) to −13.4% (Q1 FY24), −3.9% (Q2 FY24), +2.8% (Q3 FY24), +1.8% (Q4 FY24), +0.5% (Q1 FY25) and +2.0% (Q2 FY25), its 4th straight positive quarter.
  - KFL "compelling assortments": +3.4% in Q1 FY25 and +7.6% in Q2 FY25.
  - EMEA up on "improved assortments" in Q2 FY24 (+7.6%) and Q3 FY24 (+6.4%).
  (P_FL_10Q_B/C; P_FL_8K_B)
- **Store-format economics (pre-DKS Lace Up).**
  - Reimagined doors: ~$4-5M year-1 sales, ~20% year-1 EBITDA margin, ~6,500 gross sq ft, capex $1.0-1.2M, payback ~2 yrs, **net inventory ~$0.53M per door**, cash-on-cash ~50%.
  - Refresh: "+low- to mid-single-digit" annualized year-1 sales lift and "+MSD" merch/gross profit $ lift, ~$150K capex, cash-on-cash 35-45%.
  - Counts: 407 refreshes in 2024 and ~300 planned for 2025, later cut to ~240. 8 Reimagined doors at end-2024, ~80 planned for 2025, later cut to ~70.
  (P_FL_8K_B › FL_8-K_2025-03-05_EX-99-2; P_FL_10Q_C › FL_10-Q_FY2025-Q2)
- **Loyalty data asset.** ~49% of Q4 FY24 NA sales came from FLX members (+28pts y/y), and ~3.2M new NA members were added in Q4 FY24 (+295% y/y). FLX gives "priority access to sneaker launches, exclusive sales". (P_FL_8K_B › FL_8-K_2025-03-05_EX-99-2; P_FL_10Q_B › Q2 FY24)
- **Inventory heading into DKS ownership was partly fall product.** Q2 FY25 inventory was $1,709M (+3.7% y/y), "largely strategic pull-forward of fall product" plus 100bps FX (ex-FX +2.7%). Inference: this is not purely aged inventory. (P_FL_8K_B › FL_8-K_2025-08-27_EX-99-1, verified in SOURCE)
- **DKS deal rationale (proxy supplement) frames the fix as reducing brand-partner concentration.** It cites "broader product portfolio, less brand partner concentration, wider customer base, less dependent on single category". Hobart (5/15/25): "a product mix that appeals to our different customer bases". Stack: "clear path to further unlocking growth". (P_FL_8K_B › FL_8-K_2025-08-11; FL_8-K_2025-05-15_EX-99-2)
- **Inference: there is room for GM to recover.** FL standalone GM was FY23 ~27.7% [d: 1−5,895/8,154] and FY24 ~28.9% [d: 1−5,666/7,971]. Quarterly peaks were 29.6% (Q3 FY24 and Q4 FY24). FL's original FY24 guide was 29.8-30.0% and its FY25 guide was 29.3-29.7% ("improving merch margin"), withdrawn 5/15/25. Consensus FL GM is FY26E ~25.9%, FY27E ~26.8-27.0% and FY28E ~27.5%, still below FL's own FY23-FY24 levels. Caveat: DKS FL-segment GM definitions and purchase accounting may differ from FL standalone (DKS Q1 FY26 FL GM 27.90% vs FL standalone Q1 FY25 28.4%; Q2 FY26 25.66% vs Q2 FY25 27.1%).

## Known evidence AGAINST
- **FL depended on one brand and one category (the "important brand partner" is Nike, by inference).**
  - 2024-05-20 board discussion listed: "heavy reliance on single product category, merchandise from limited number of brand partners, sales highly dependent on consumer enthusiasm for brand partners' new/innovative product".
  - Investor topics were "brand partner allocations and product mix, promotional activity level".
  - The 2025-05 "additional scenario" assumed "slower recovery of an important FL brand partner and lower comp growth".
  - The board rationale cites "the underperformance and delayed turnaround of an important Foot Locker brand partner".
  (P_FL_8K_B › FL_8-K_2025-08-11)
- **The pre-DKS markdown recapture faded quickly. Lower markdowns at FL have reversed before.**
  - Q1 FY25: merch −10bps (promo to clear exited countries), GM 28.4% vs 28.8%.
  - Q2 FY25: merch −50bps, "more promotional in certain markets due to market pressures. Additionally, markdowns were taken to ensure we maintained healthy inventory levels", lower vendor allowances. GM 27.1% vs 27.6%.
  - EMEA summer sale "resulted in gross margin erosion".
  (P_FL_10Q_C › FL_10-Q_FY2025-Q1/Q2; verified in SOURCE)
- **Launch and basketball dependence.** Q2 FY25 store comps were hurt by "lower demand of key basketball footwear styles". DTC was up on "improved digital product launches" (Q1 and Q2 FY25). Home Court rollout "in collaboration with Nike and Jordan Brand" (Q3 FY24). (P_FL_10Q_C; P_FL_8K_B › FL_8-K_2024-12-04_EX-99-1)
- **Demand was concentrated in event periods, and promos stayed elevated.**
  - Q3 FY24: "promotional environment was more elevated than anticipated" and "softer consumer demand outside of key selling periods". FY24 GM guide was cut from 29.8-30.0% to 29.5-29.7% (Aug-24) to 28.7-28.8% (Dec-24) on "promo pressure in international and WSS".
  - Monthly comps swung around key periods: Q2'24 May LSD down, Jun/Jul MSD up; Q3'24 Aug HSD up, Sep/Oct LSD down; Q4'24 Nov MSD down, Dec/Jan MSD up.
  - Q4 FY24: "despite elevated promotions". Mgmt (3/5/25) expected "promotional pressures to remain uncertain into 2025, especially within the first half".
  (P_FL_8K_B)
- **Apparel was a chronic drag. This is the baseline for "apparel re-added".** Global comp by category ex-WSS/atmos:
  - Q2'24: footwear HSD up, apparel down mid-teens.
  - Q3'24: apparel down low-twenties.
  - Q4'24: apparel down mid-teens.
  - Q1 FY25: apparel down, footwear flat.
  - Q2 FY25: apparel down, footwear modestly up.
  - Apparel also fell in Q2 FY23, Q1 FY24 and Q2 FY24.
  A new apparel push therefore starts from a shrinking category with FL's customer. (P_FL_8K_B decks; P_FL_10Q_B/C)
- **Exclusives were stuck below target.** Exclusives % of sales: Q2'23 14% → Q2'24 13%; Q3'23 16% → Q3'24 15%; Q4'23 15% → Q4'24 15%; 2026 target 20%. (P_FL_8K_B Lace Up dashboards)
- **The refresh sales lift was modest.** Refreshes gave only a "+low- to mid-single-digit" sales lift. Q2 FY25 traffic was soft ("soft store traffic trends"; WSS high-single-digit traffic declines). Q1 FY25 had "softer traffic trends globally". (P_FL_8K_B; P_FL_10Q_C)
- **Inventory is a lumpy baseline (risk of DKS clearance).** Inventory grew ahead of sales in FY23: Jul-29-23 +11.4% y/y and Oct-28-23 +10.5% y/y while sales fell 9.9% and 8.6% [d]. That excess drove the −300 to −470bps merch/GM hits ("higher promotional activity, higher merchandise costs, increased shrink"). Inventory was +3.7% y/y at Aug-2-25 (sales −2.4%), handed to DKS just before close. Inference: some Q3/Q4 FY25 DKS-era markdown was inherited. (P_FL_10Q_B/C)
- **FL's own outlooks proved too optimistic.**
  - FY24: initial comp +1-3%, delivered +1.4%. Initial GM 29.8-30.0%, delivered ~28.9% [d]. EPS guide $1.50-1.70 cut to $1.20-1.30, actual $1.37.
  - FY25 guide (comp +1.0-2.5%, GM 29.3-29.7%) was withdrawn after Q1 FY25 comp of −2.6%.
  - Wall Street estimates sat below mgmt's Lace Up case (board 2024-05-20 and 2025-05-10).
  Inference: a credibility discount on turnaround promises is rational. (P_FL_8K_B)
- **Older vendor-mix drag.** In FY23, "changing vendor mix" (the Nike pullback) plus the Champs repositioning drove comps of −9.4% (Q2 FY23) and −8.0% (Q3 FY23). atmos was hurt by "lack of certain key styles from adidas". (P_FL_10Q_B)
