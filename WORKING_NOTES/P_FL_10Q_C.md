### Business/strategy (Note 1 & MD&A overview)
- 3 operating segments (North America, EMEA, Asia Pacific) aggregated into 1 reportable segment. NA = Foot Locker, Champs Sports, Kids Foot Locker, WSS (US & Canada) incl e-com; EMEA = Foot Locker & Kids Foot Locker in Europe; APAC = Foot Locker (Australia, NZ, Asia) + atmos (Japan). Brands: Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs Sports, WSS, atmos.
- Q2'24 enhanced FLX Rewards loyalty across North America (plans to expand to other geographies); introduced FLX Cash (points toward discount), priority access to sneaker launches, exclusive sales, member-only events, free returns, upgraded birthday gifts, complimentary shipping. Mgmt: "We believe that our FLX Rewards Program is key to driving customer retention and engagement."
- Annual strategic review → exit underperforming ops in South Korea, Denmark, Norway, Sweden; close all stores there by mid-2025. Agreements to sell Greece & Romania businesses + license purchaser to operate Foot Locker stores in Greece, Romania and 6 other South East Europe countries; closing expected 1H 2025; "We do not believe the sale will be significant to our financial results." Global HQ to move to St. Petersburg, FL in late 2025 (Lace Up Plan support; collaboration, cost reduction). Q3'24 impairment + reorganization charges $13m tied to these decisions.
- Store count: 2,450 at 11/2/24 vs 2,523 at 2/3/24 and 2,607 at 10/28/23. Licensed stores (Middle East & Asia, excluded from count): 214 vs 202 vs 190. Q3'24 license partners opened first Foot Locker store + digital platform in India.
- New ASU 2024-03 (expense disaggregation) effective FY beginning after 2026-12-15 / interim after 2027-12-15; evaluating.
