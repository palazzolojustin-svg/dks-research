### MD&A
- Overview: mission "Fun for All"; localized merchandising + value. Q3'25 net sales mix: outdoors 32%, sports & rec 21%, apparel 25%, footwear 22%. National brands + private label. Locally relevant items (e.g., crawfish boilers in LA, local licensed apparel, local baits/lures, beach towels coastal).
- 317 stores at 11/1/25, ~40K–130K gross sq ft, avg ~70K; 21 contiguous states mainly southern US; ~24,000 team members; 3 DCs; academy.com + app. Omnichannel investments: app, website optimization, fulfillment upgrades.
- Store activity (39w): FY25 begin 298; Q1 +5, Q2 +3, Q3 +11; 0 closed; end 317; 0 relocated. FY24: begin 282; Q1 +2, Q2 +1, Q3 +8; end 293.
- Tariffs/macro: inflation, tariffs, trade policy "have impacted consumer spending and could adversely affect our ability to grow sales and merchandise margin"; mitigation levers: inventory purchase quantity/timing, strategic pricing & promo adjustments, diversify sourcing/country of origin, vendor negotiations.
- KPI defs: comp = stores open >13 full fiscal months + all e-com; significantly remodeled/relocated stores excluded until open substantially all of compared periods; extended closures excluded; all website/app sales (incl BOPIS, curbside) = e-com. Transactions = comp-basis customer transactions (stores + e-com); avg ticket = sales/transactions. Adj EBIT used as incentive comp target.
- Inventory mgmt: 3rd-party tools for stock analysis and disciplined markdowns at every location; uses Academy Credit Card program data, customer database, surveys to forecast inventory.
- Private label priced below comparable national brands. Softgoods (apparel, footwear) higher margin than hardgoods (outdoors, sports & rec); mix shift to softgoods +GM.
- Omnichannel: investments in new customer data platform, customer segmentation; website redesigned home page, more BOPIS features, enhanced shipping notifications. YTD Q3'25 stores facilitated ~95% of total sales (ship-from-store, BOPIS, in-store).
- New stores = key future growth driver for sales and GM; off-mall power centers/stand-alone; 11 opened Q3'25; 58 stores opened since FY2021.
- COGS includes merchandise cost + procurement, warehousing, distribution (transport, payroll, DC occupancy). GM drivers incl promo, mix (private label), commodity, freight, shrink, inventory processing, e-com shipping. Prolonged elevated shrink could materially hurt GM.
- SG&A % sales 25.5% YTD'24 → 27.4% YTD'25, primarily strategic investments incl 24 new stores since end Q3'24. Pre-opening expense expected to rise w/ store growth (negative to SG&A %).
