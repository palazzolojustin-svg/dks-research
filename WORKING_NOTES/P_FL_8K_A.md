### Q4 2022

### Stores

### Headlines
- Total sales -11.4%; comp -9.1%; EPS $0.38, non-GAAP EPS $0.70; lowering 2023 sales and earnings guidance; Mike Baughn named CFO.
- "Coming off the recent launch of our Lace Up Strategy at our Investor Day in March, we are making early progress in building a strong foundation to return to sustainable growth beyond this year... However, our sales have since softened meaningfully given the tough macroeconomic backdrop, causing us to reduce our guidance for the year as we take more aggressive markdowns to both drive demand and manage inventory." —Mary Dillon, CEO. "Despite the challenging near-term trends, we remain committed to our long-term strategy, including making the necessary investments to drive our Lace Up plan, and maintain conviction in our ability to execute against our new strategic imperatives."
### Q1 2023
- Comp -9.1%: macro headwinds incl lower US income tax refunds, changing vendor mix, Champs Sports repositioning.
- Sales $1,927M vs $2,175M (-11.4%); ex-FX -10%.
- GM -400 bps: higher markdowns vs historically low LY, occupancy deleverage, increased theft-related shrink.
- SG&A rate +110 bps: cost-optimization savings more than offset by deleverage on sales decline, inflation, investments in front-line wages and technology.
- NI $36M vs $133M; non-GAAP NI $66M vs $155M. EPS $0.38 vs $1.37; non-GAAP $0.70 vs $1.60.
- Cash $313M; debt $451M. Inventory $1,758M, +25% y/y (2023-04-29).
- Q1 dividend $0.40/sh = $38M; declared $0.40 payable 2023-07-28, record 2023-07-14. No buybacks in Q1'23 (vs $89M Q1'22).
- Stores: opened 13, remodeled/relocated 18, closed 35; 2,692 stores in 29 countries at 2023-04-29; 163 franchised (Middle East, Asia).

### International updates
- Closing stores and ecommerce in South Korea; closing stores and ecommerce in Denmark, Norway, Sweden; agreements with Fourlis Holdings S.A. (Fourlis Group; SE Europe retail/licensing operator) to transfer store and ecommerce ops in Greece and store ops in Romania. Combined: closure/transfer of ~30 stores of FL's 140 APAC and 629 Europe stores; complete by mid-2025. Fourlis also licensed for future SE Europe expansion; FL + Fourlis see opportunity for >100 stores in region over next several years.
### HQ relocation / tech hub
- Global HQ to move to St. Petersburg, Florida in late 2025; only limited NYC presence going forward; intent: build on St. Petersburg presence, cross-banner collaboration, reduce costs.
