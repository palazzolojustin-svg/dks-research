### Termination rights
- Mutual written consent anytime before effective time.
- Either party: final non-appealable order permanently enjoining; or not closed by outside date May 15, 2026 — auto-extended 3 months if all conditions other than antitrust/no-injunction (antitrust-related) and closing-deliverable conditions satisfied/waived; auto-extended further additional 3 months on same test; right unavailable to party whose material breach proximately caused failure.
- FL may terminate: (1) before shareholder adoption, board change of recommendation + substantially concurrently enters definitive agreement for superior proposal, if FL complied in all material respects w/ no-shop and match-right provisions and pays DKS $59.5mm termination fee at/before termination; (2) DKS/Merger Sub breach/inaccuracy causing failure of FL closing conditions, incurable by outside date or uncured by earlier of business day before outside date and 30th calendar day after FL written notice, and FL not in material breach.

### Termination fees & expenses
- Expenses: each party bears own, except as provided.
- FL termination fee to DKS: $59.5mm if (i) DKS terminates for failed FL vote + acquisition proposal publicly disclosed after signing and not publicly withdrawn ≥3 business days before meeting + within 12 months of termination acquisition proposal consummated or definitive agreement entered; (ii) acquisition proposal made to FL board/mgmt or publicly disclosed and not publicly withdrawn ≥3 business days before termination, then termination for outside date (either party) or by DKS for FL covenant breach following acquisition proposal, + 12-month tail as above; (iii) DKS terminates for FL board change of recommendation or FL willful breach of obligations (a)–(c) above; (iv) FL terminates to accept superior proposal. For tail purposes "acquisition proposal" thresholds 15% / 85% replaced by 50%. Payable only once; upon receipt (plus any costs/expenses/interest), FL parties have no further liability except fraud/willful breach.
- DKS reverse termination fee to FL: $95.5mm if (i) either party terminates at outside date and all conditions satisfied/waived other than no-injunction (antitrust) or antitrust-approval conditions; or (ii) either terminates because final non-appealable order permanently enjoins deal arising solely under antitrust laws. Payable once; upon receipt, DKS/Merger Sub parties have no further liability except fraud/willful breach.
- Effect of termination: agreement void except survival provisions (effect of termination, fees, misc., FL–DKS confidentiality agreement); no liability except fraud or willful breach pre-termination. "Willful breach" = deliberate act/failure to act that breaching party actually knows would or would reasonably be expected to be/cause material breach.
- Specific performance: both entitled to injunctions/specific performance; waive objections (adequate remedy at law); no bond required.
- Amendment by written agreement of DKS, Merger Sub, FL; extensions/waivers only in signed writing.
- Governing law: Delaware; except certain actions against DKS's financing parties governed by New York law.

### Material U.S. federal income tax consequences (FL holders)
- Merger is fully taxable to U.S. holders: gain/loss = (cash incl. cash in lieu of fractional DKS shares + FMV of DKS stock received) − adjusted basis in FL shares; capital gain/loss; long-term if held >1 yr; compute per block. - Section 304 risk on CASH consideration (not stock consideration): if FL holders owning (incl. attribution) ≥50% of FL vote/value before merger own ≥50% of DKS vote/value after, U.S. holders who actually/constructively own DKS stock may have dividend income up to cash received unless Sec. 302 tests met (substantially disproportionate / not essentially equivalent to dividend / complete termination).
- Non-U.S. holders: withholding agent unable to determine dividend status may withhold 30% (or treaty rate) on entire cash consideration (not stock consideration).
- Backup withholding currently 24%.
