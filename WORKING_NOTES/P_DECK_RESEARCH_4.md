### Thesis / headline
- 4Q26 beat on sales, GM, OM, EPS $0.96 (vs cons $0.83); beat driven by better GM + sales. FY27 gdnc sales & EPS m/p above cons, OM below.
- 4Q26 rev $1.12bn (+10% y/y); HOKA +14.5%, UGG +9.2%; FY26 OM 23.1%; record FY EPS $7.02 "underscore the durability of the model".
- HOKA: brand awareness meaningfully expanding US + intl; deeper franchise breadth; healthy wholesale order book; DTC momentum.
- UGG: improving execution via 365 strategy, men's penetration, sneakers + sandals growth -> all-season, broader consumer base.
- Near-term macro headwinds: tariffs + Middle East-related logistics/cost impacts; Barclays sees manageable, limited structural impact (sourcing flexibility, pricing power, disciplined inv/channel mgmt).
- New multi-year framework (LRP): HSD rev CAGR FY28-FY30; HOKA LDD; UGG MSD; LDD EPS growth; supported by faster intl + DTC expansion.
- OW reasons: 1) increased wholesale demand; 2) strong innovation pipeline at UGG + HOKA across categories; 3) domestic + intl build opportunity; 4) full-price selling commitment; 5) multiple levers for global share gains.
- "Why OW" box: 1) HOKA runway; 2) improving wholesale inv backdrop supportive of replenishment, HOKA as traffic-driving full-priced brand; 3) margin expansion from channel + geography mix; 4) highest ROIC in footwear, still expanding.

### Tariffs
- 4Q26 GM +90bps y/y despite tariffs: higher full-price selling UGG+HOKA, fav FX, lower freight, fav product/channel mix.
- FY26 GM -20bps incl 80bps tariff headwind, offset by 60bps underlying expansion (product mix, lower freight).
- FY27 gdnc assumes current 10% tariff rate for full FY27; pursuing tariff refunds, none in gdnc. Paid ~$120mn IEEPA tariffs; on refund receipt will factor cost-sharing arrangements w/ partners.
- 1Q27 GM down y/y mainly tariffs; slight offsets from fav channel mix + FX.
- Prior commentary (3Q26): 3Q GM better than expected on lower tariff impact (inventory flow timing, lower-tariff inv in pipeline); mgmt said 4Q26 would have largest FY26 tariff impact, ~200bps headwind entirely tariffs as full 20% tariff burden flows; FY26 unmitigated tariff headwind lowered to $110mn (from $150mn), net tariff impact $25mn (from implied $55-75mn) on better price action + favorable inv timing. Barclays expects tariff pressure in 1Q27, 2Q27, lesser 3Q27.
