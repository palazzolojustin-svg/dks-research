### MD&A highlights / strategy
- Q2 highlights: rev $12.4B +1% reported; NIKE Brand wholesale $7.5B vs $6.9B (NA up; GC, APLA down); NIKE Direct $4.6B vs $5.0B (lower NIKE Brand Digital traffic); GM -300bps to 40.6% mainly higher NA tariffs; inventories $7.7B +3% vs May-31-25 (higher product costs incl NA tariffs); returned ≈$598M via dividends in Q2.

- Actions: Product mgmt (accelerate innovation, reduce supply of certain footwear to rebalance footwear mix); Marketplace mgmt (reposition NIKE Brand Digital as full-price platform, reinvest in wholesale; liquidate inventory via increased markdowns in NIKE Direct, higher sales returns/discounts with wholesale partners; invest to elevate physical retail presentation); Brand mgmt (higher demand creation incl brand/sports marketing for launches & sports moments).
- Segments at different stages; actions "have had, and in the future will have, a negative impact on our Revenues and overall profitability." "North America has made the most progress against these actions, while Greater China and Converse will take more time." GC: declining store traffic, elevated promotions, higher marketplace inventory hurting rev/profit; Converse mid strategic reset of brand & marketplace. "We expect negative impacts from Greater China and Converse to continue throughout fiscal 2026." Believes actions will reignite brand momentum.
