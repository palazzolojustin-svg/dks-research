- Cash+ST investments $9b > $8b funded debt at May 31 (funded-debt net cash before $3.1b operating-lease liabilities); title cites "$11 Billion of Debt" [incl leases]. Two committed facilities totaling $3b + $3b CP program, undrawn.

NKE executing 2 competing turnarounds: North America entering "HUNT" (beating sales/margin expectations; earnings > sales growth; multi-yr); Greater China early "TRAP" (stiff competition all categories, fleeting pricing power on core franchises, share erosion → operating deleverage).
