### Tariff ruling react (02/20/26)
- Footwear cos incl Deckers, On, Steve Madden could see incremental GM tailwinds after Supreme Court struck down Trump reciprocal tariffs; potential duty refunds might take years; benefit phases in as higher-cost inv clears -> more visible in 2H, not immediate step-change.
### Equity outlook thesis (11/12/25)
- Raised 2025 gdnc + updated 3-yr targets = sustained brand momentum, structural growth beyond core running. CC sales +34% FY25 vs ≥31% prior; mgmt forecasts 30% CAGR through 2026 vs 26% before. Drivers: running innovation, apparel reach, DTC mix; new stores; accelerating intl demand esp APAC; premium pricing, full-price discipline, scale efficiencies -> LT margin expansion.
### 3Q25 results (11/12/25)
- Sales +24.9% (+34.5% CC); DTC +27.6% (+37.5% CC); wholesale +23.3% (+32.5% CC). DTC investment (omnichannel, loyalty) drove DTC.
- Footwear +21.1%, apparel +86.9%, accessories +145.3%.
- CC sales by region: EMEA +33%, Americas +21%, APAC +109.2%.
- GM +510bps to 65.7% (sustained pricing power; scale efficiencies, lower logistics offset tariff costs); FY25 GM gdnc raised to 62.5% from 60.5-61%.
- Adj diluted EPS (Class A) 43 centimes, ahead of cons; FY25 sales gdnc +34% vs ≥31% prior.
### 2026 targets in reach (06/16-17/25)
- On has 2.3% share global sports-footwear mkt, less in sportswear; could grow sales ≥25% each of next 2 yrs, achieving 2023 goal of doubling sales by 2026 (to CHF 3.55bn); BI scenario analysis says could comfortably reach/exceed. Drivers: online, new stores, wholesale demand, underpenetrated mkts like China, innovation.
- Store opening pace accelerating to 20-25/yr, half earmarked for China; stores key to tripling apparel sales (stores have higher apparel penetration than online/wholesale). Mid-to-high-20% annual sales gains possible even as growth moderates w/ tech/infrastructure investment.
### Brand awareness/share
- On ~1% share global sportswear (footwear+apparel); brand awareness high-single to low-double digits in key countries, far below peers. Levers: events, wholesale to reach untapped communities, athletes.
- Rose to No. 9 sports-footwear (06/16/25), widening gap vs Deckers' Hoka (1.1% share). (03/26/25 peers section: 7th-largest sports-footwear brand globally, surpassing Brooks, Under Armour, Hoka.)
### China/regions (06/16/25)
- China biggest opportunity (middle class, sports interest); new stores, larger formats, digital platforms. China sales triple-digit growth since first store 2019; aspiration China 10% of sales beyond 2026. Asia = 11% of sales. Americas remains largest region (nearly two-thirds of sales per 01/28/26 chart); EMEA most mature but underpenetrated in France, Spain, Italy.
### DTC/stores
- Plan to at least triple store footprint by 2026 (to 90 stores); DTC could grow 33% CAGR over 3 yrs; online remains majority of DTC. Wholesale scenario 23% CAGR (moderating but solid).
- Mgmt: after new store opens, e-com traffic can be 3x higher; wholesale partners in market also see uplift. 20-25 openings/yr could push retail store sales penetration to 10% LT; driven by new retail formats beyond flagships. E-com may keep outpacing overall growth; expanding distribution capacity key.
### Wholesale (06/16/25)
- More controlled wholesale growth; focus on efficiency with existing partners; new partners less incremental (relationships with most key strategic retailers already).
- DKS-READ: only 20% penetration among key larger wholesale accounts — Dick's, Foot Locker, JD — significant room to expand presence with newest partners. FY25 plan: wholesale door count +~5% to 11,300; 20-24% wholesale growth over next 2 yrs largely from same-store sales gains.
### Sports/product expansion
- Shift footwear->sportswear brand; tennis, training, outdoor, lifestyle expand TAM; running remains core. On top-3 running brand in most main mkts and with key retail partners over past 5 yrs. Nike, Adidas, Puma racing for fastest marathon shoe but struggle w/ everyday runner loyalty. Two new speed-performance technologies launching 2025. US sports footwear share 5.2% vs 1.2% in 2020.
- Tennis: Roger Federer partnership; 2024 roster adds Iga Swiatek, Ben Shelton; multiyear partnership w/ Zendaya; custom Roger Pro editions + on-court head-to-toe collection; The Roger franchise (pro tennis shoe + sport-inspired sneaker) for off-court wear.
- Apparel: entered 2019; global sports apparel mkt $238.7bn, forecast >$310bn by 2029 (Euromonitor); apparel sales >CHF 100mn in 2024; 2025 push apparel-first categories (training) -> BI est apparel sales +>55% in 2025; stores higher clothing penetration.
### Chart book (charts not extracted; titles only)
