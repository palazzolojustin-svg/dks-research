### Hoka
- Sustain mid-teen expansion over next several yrs (intl, adjacent categories, DTC). Underpenetrated: 1.1% share of $179B sports-footwear market (p.3; elsewhere $174B, Euromonitor → $223B by 2030). Core franchises Clifton, Bondi, Arahi; trail/racing innovation; disciplined wholesale expansion.
- More strategic launch cadence (less overlap of major updates), cleaner marketplace inventory → reaccelerated FY26 growth; annual gdnc raised from low-to-mid-teen gains; cons moved toward 15% from low-double digits at initiation.
- (02/25/26) Crown jewel; DTC + wholesale share gains + diversification beyond running could boost sales mid-to-high teens annually through FY27. On track for >$2.5B brand FY26. Gains more balanced wholesale/DTC this yr; selective wholesale door additions + franchise refreshes skew near-term growth to wholesale.
- Intl: China and Europe largest opps; Europe majority of intl sales; China greatest growth opp (recently increased focus). US: top-10 sports-footwear brand 2023 (topped Asics & Brooks; tied Under Armour for 10th); 7th largest in 2025 (Euromonitor); only 3.6% US share.
- DTC leads LT, wholesale near term: wholesale growth via sell-in, store additions, reorders as sell-through outpaces shipments; DTC could reach 50% of brand sales; e-com majority of DTC; select retail stores in key markets for awareness.
- Product beyond running: hiking, lifestyle, apparel biggest near-term opps; Mafate, Speedgoat versatile formats; trail/hike/lower-profile lifestyle silhouettes; early apparel investment; selective collabs/fashion releases.
- Running core: gained share in US road-running >$140; returned to top position in run specialty, double-digit sell-through growth, rising intl reorders; cleaner marketplace, tighter launch cadence position for share gains despite competition in maximal cushioning.

### Ugg
- New president Anne Spangenberg (ex-Nike chief merchant; role since mid-2022): year-round assortment, less reliance on cold-weather core; first male-focused campaign in >5 yrs featuring Post Malone (prior long-standing Crocs collaborator). Men's + apparel key to doubling Ugg sales LT.
- Shoulder-season styles Lowmel, Tasman → 1H sales +12% (in line w/ BI, vs cons 7%). Colder 4Q weather to beat flat gdnc, drive high-single-digit FY gains.
- (02/27/26) FY26 at least high-single digits; could top cons 6.9% rev gain. FY26 growth may moderate from last yr's 13%; LT sustainable mid-to-high single-digit via "365 strategy"; sneakers, sandals, transitional styles, men's. Wholesale aided by in-stocks/allocation; DTC to reaccelerate via loyalty/CRM.
- Spangenberg: product diversification, franchise evolution, tight marketplace scarcity → high full-price sell-through; brand heat all-time high. Quote from Dave Powers (CEO, 1Q23 call 7/28/22) praising Anne: 3 yrs in China at Nike, "she is an exceptional merchant, first and foremost."
- Simplified assortment, reduced style count, inventory concentrated behind core franchises; trend styles Quill ballet sneaker, Ellis loafer.
- Men's ~1/3 of customer base; men's outpacing women's recently; all-gender Lowmel, men's weather hybrid collection; Ugg holds 1.4% of US men's footwear market, less abroad.
