## Brand list (FY25 10-K)
Owned: Alpine Design, CALIA, DSG, ETHOS, Fitness Gear, MAXFLI (also written Maxfly), Nishiki, Quest, Tommy Armour, Top-Flite, VRST, Walter Hagen. Licensed: adidas (football), Cobra (golf), Marucci (baseball), Lotto (soccer & pickleball).

## Size / mix (already known)
- Vertical brands $1.6B end FY23, goal $2B ("well on our way"); $1.7B FY24 (13% of sales); ~$1.8B FY25 (~13% of DICK'S Business sales, 10-K risk factor). Collectively #2 vendor behind Nike (31% of purchases). #1 or #2 vendor in accessories, athletic apparel, fitness, golf, team sports. Flagships incl. "2nd largest women's apparel brand (behind Nike)", "largest premium men's apparel brand" (investor deck).
- Wells Fargo Exhibit 85 private brand % of DKS sales: 10% FY06-FY16, 12% FY17, 14% FY18, 14% FY19, 15% FY20, 14% FY21, 14% FY22, 13% FY23, 13% FY24; PF consolidated FY25E ~11% (incl FL). FL historically ~50% of apparel private (not broken out recently).
- ASO private label ~22% of sales, aspires >25%; >50% of ASO customers bought owned brand.
- Field & Stream was largest vertical brand in 2019, exited; replaced by DSG/CALIA/VRST.

## Margin (already known)
- GM premium 600-800bps (Sep-Dec 2024) → 700-900bps (May-2025 onward); "Actually, the margins are even becoming better" (2026). Vertical brands most of direct imports; negligible China/Mexico/Canada tariff exposure ("diversified supply chain").
