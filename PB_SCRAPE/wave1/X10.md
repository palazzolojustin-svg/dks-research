# X10: News, press, trade media & executive statements (2026): DKS owned brands
Agent X10, wave 1. Run 2026-10-07. Apify: none used ($0). WebSearch budget exhausted at the end of the run (shared 200-call session cap).

Bottom line: the 2026 news flow shows that DKS is spending more on owned-brand MARKETING and DISTRIBUTION. There are national TV/cinema campaigns for DSG and Maxfli, a lifestyle category extension and celebrity campaign for CALIA, the first owned-brand standalone store (a pop-up), the first international wholesale deal (Maxfli/Top Flite in Australia), and a loyalty credit that can only be spent on owned brands. But no 2026 source quantifies owned-brand sales or mix. Management stopped mentioning owned brands entirely at the two post-guidance-cut events (Q2 FY26 call on 2026-08-25 and Goldman on 2026-09-14), and every 2026 SEC filing and investor deck still repeats the FY25 "~$1.8B / ~13%". The evidence supports "investment is accelerating". It does not prove "mix is accelerating".

The JPM split already in the notes (C01B l.1326) implies core merch margin ex those items ≈ −30bp. Owned-brand mix is not visible in reported GM.

Exclusive national-brand collabs (Adidas x Pokemon, Owala Halloween, On x FP Movement; nj.com Sep-2026) compete for the same "only at DICK'S" slot.
- Owned brands are being discounted: a MSN/Golf Galaxy deal story (2026-06-25) says Walter Hagen $75 polos dropped to $25. The folder promo tracker (digest l.468) already has "12/18 75% off vertical brands" and DSG promo up to 70%.
