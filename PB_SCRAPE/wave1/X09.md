## Evidence against / caveats
- CALIA, by far the largest owned brand in search volume, is flat to down: CY25 +1%, Jul-Sep 2026 -10% to -13% y/y, losing share to Vuori, Alo and Fabletics. Women's apparel is the category management calls the "2nd largest women's apparel brand", so this matters.
- Maxfli search growth has stalled: Jul-Sep 2026 is -1% y/y after +46-73% in the prior three quarters. "Accelerating over the last six months" is NOT supported by search data. The real story is a multi-year share gain that is now leveling off, though still ahead of a weak golf category.
- The Apr-Jun 2026 Google Trends data cannot be used (X09-1), so the core of the six-month window the user cares about has no reliable search read. Only Jan-mid-Mar and Jul-Sep 2026 are clean.
- Absolute volumes are tiny for DSG product terms, VRST and Walter Hagen (index 0.5-9 on most scales; Google shows "<1" or zero on many days). Changes of ±30-80% rest on a handful of index points.
- Search interest is not sales. Owned brands are bought mostly in-store and found on DKS shelves and dicks.com rather than searched for, so search understates their penetration. A rising branded-search share is a sign of brand equity, not a mix measure.
- "dicks sporting goods" search fell 68% y/y in Jul-Sep 2026 (63 vs 200 index-pts; noisy, because people mostly search "dicks"). This is not owned-brand-specific but conflicts with DKS comp strength. Treat it as a Google measurement issue (INFERENCE).
- Wikipedia (X09-5) points the other way for Maxfli.
