## Evidence against / caveats
- **VRST**: no new product filing since 2022. The 2021 VRST extensions into personal care, supplements, medical-compression and fitness equipment (SN 97163988, 97150862) were **abandoned with no Statement of Use**. VRST and CALIA digital/content ITUs (Class 9/41, filed 2022-11-29) are on their **5th (final) SOU extension**, i.e. never launched. This is evidence that some planned owned-brand extensions were dropped.
- Abandoned ITUs: CC and CHELSEA COLLECTIVE (a 2023 yoga apparel/equipment brand, never launched), SMARTHEAT (2024 outerwear tech), TRUELIGHT (2025 bottoms), OMNIDYNE (2026 footwear). ORBIT (putter) is under final refusal. Roughly 1 in 4 product ITUs since 2023 never ships.
- Product filing count is flat to down over the last 12 months (14 vs 19).
- A registration is not sales. First-use dates prove a launch, not volume. The DSG "sneakers/athletic shoes" registration covers goods, but the specimen was a pant, so actual adult sneaker sales are not shown.
- Coverage: USPTO filings take about 1-6 weeks to index. Anything filed in Sep-Oct 2026 is not yet visible; the latest indexed is 2026-08-26.
