## Evidence against / caveats
- **No surge in absolute open roles.** Only 4 "Product Development" category roles were open on 2026-10-07, and ~6 VB roles out of 54 corporate. That looks like steady backfill plus a targeted build, not a hiring spree. No JD gives scale language ("billion-dollar", growth %, new-category targets). Searched for and not found.
- **Reposts may be churn, not growth.** "Assistant Print and Textile Designer" was posted Jul-2025 (202508771), Feb-2026 (202601269 associate), Apr-2026 (202604136) and Oct-2026 (202637634). "Staff Industrial Designer (Outdoor)" was posted Apr-2026 (202605115) and again Sep-2026 (202635181). Repeated reqs for the same seat can mean turnover.
- **LinkedIn cohort biases.** (i) Survivorship: people who left are not in the sample, so recent halves are over-represented. (ii) Keyword sampling: the "VB-org" and control groups are not random. (iii) "Current-role start" mixes promotions, retitles and reorgs. The Aug-Sep 2023 cluster (VP Hardlines, VP Softlines, VP Brand, Sr Dir, Dir) looks like a reorg under the EVP rather than net adds. (iv) LinkedIn search capped results at 62 for "vertical brands", which suggests the visible VB-titled population is about 60-120 people. That is a small org, where a few hires move the series.
- **The Jan-2026 re-scope can cut both ways.** The VB chief now also runs national-brand apparel buying. That could dilute VB focus, or simply reflect a merchant reorg after the Foot Locker deal.
- **The footwear evidence is about existence, not scale.** One associate sourcing seat plus legacy cleats or boots does not show material vertical footwear. Stack's "never super high" logic (footwear-heavy mix) still largely holds.
- **The Wayback series is tiny** (VB-org n = 10/12/9 per req-year), and Wayback refused connections mid-session (rate-limited, probably by parallel agents), so archived JD text for 2024-25 VB postings was not retrieved.
- **Side observation (NEUTRAL):** company-wide Workday req numbering reached ~202640012 by 2026-10-06, about 40k reqs YTD, vs ~19.9k for all of 2025 (max seen 202519884) and ~19.4k for 2024. That doubling is almost certainly store/seasonal plus Foot Locker integration (e.g., "Foot Locker Employment Attorney (NYC)" posted in the DKS tenant). It is not a VB signal.
