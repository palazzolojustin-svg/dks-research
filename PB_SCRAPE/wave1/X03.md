# X03: Wayback Machine (+ Common Crawl) history of DKS owned-brand assortment and featuring (wave 1)
Agent X03 | run 2026-10-07 02:30-08:45 CDT | Apify $0.00 | all sources accessed 2026-10-07
STATUS: see last line

**Bottom line.**
- **Merchandising priority (new series).** dicks.com's mega-menu "Featured" call-to-action slots shifted toward owned brands at full price in Q3 2026.
  - Jul-Sep pooled: owned feature CTAs went 20 → 27 while national feature CTAs went 98 → 69, so owned share of feature slots rose 16.9% → 28.1%.
  - National-brand discount CTAs nearly doubled (24 → 44). Owned discount CTAs were flat (20 → 20).
- **Assortment depth.** On matched archived pages (Aug/Sep-2025 vs Jul-Sep-2026), owned brands added styles about 10x faster than the category total: owned +34% vs all brands +3% on 71 regular category pages. Owned share of listed products went 5.43% → 7.03%.
  - This corroborates R12's 735-page result with an independent page sample.
  - Owned share of sale/clearance pages is flat (11.0% → 11.2%).
- **Brand pages.** By brand: ETHOS +33%, DSG women's +38%, CALIA apparel +18%, Nishiki +13%, Alpine Design tops +81%, VRST bottoms +37%. Fitness Gear (−21%), Quest (−17%), Top Flite (−14%) and Maxfli balls (−24%) shrank.
- **Against.** Clearance depth grew too: CALIA's "last chance" clearance page went 242 → 430 products and DSG's brand-sale page 269 → 401.
- **Caveat.** The "Sale" facet looked like a collapse in Maxfli/Top Flite markdowns (27-31% → 4-5%). Product-card checks show this is mostly a facet-definition artifact, so do not use it.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **"Sale" facet artifact.** The 5004 "Sale" facet fell sharply in 2026:
  - Maxfli 70-79 of 256-259 (27-31%) → 11-13 of 257-262 (4-5%), Aug/Sep y/y.
  - Top Flite 29-31 of 81-83 → 11 of 69-71.
  - Quest 32/131 → 13/109.

  But first-page product cards on `/f/maxfli` show "Previous Price" markdowns on 18/53 (Aug-25) → 13/53 (Aug-26) and 17/53 → 13/49 (Sep), a modest decline. X11 found ETHOS on-sale share rising (21% → 45%) while the ETHOS facet fell (20 → 18). So in 2026 the facet probably excludes "New Lower Price" clearance items. **Do not use facet sale counts as a markdown series.** Use card-level prices (X11).
- Owned clearance assortment up sharply (X03-6); owned share of sale/clearance pages flat, not falling (X03-2).
- Contracting owned lines: Quest −17%, Fitness Gear −21%, Top Flite −14%, Maxfli balls −24% (style counts, y/y). VRST's assortment is below its Dec-2023 peak (164 → 114). CALIA view-all is below its Sep-2023 peak (617 → 482).
- Listing or slot share ≠ sales mix. CTA counts are small (about 20-30 owned slots per quarter) and partly depend on how many snapshots exist per month.
- Capture months differ within windows: base Aug-Oct 2025 vs compare Jul-Sep 2026. Seasonal mix (summer swim/resort vs fall fleece) can move counts.
- Archives have no dicks.com data Oct-2024 to Jul-2025 (Akamai blocks Wayback and CC), so there is no clean Apr-Jun 2026 vs Apr-Jun 2025 comparison.
- Common Crawl /p/ product-URL census (owned share of crawled product URLs) is unusable. Crawl coverage is alphabetical/partial per crawl: owned share swings 0.4% → 8.9% between crawls. Not reported as a finding.
