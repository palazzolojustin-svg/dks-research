## Evidence against / caveats
- **Base effect:** 2025 was a tariff-depressed year for DKS direct imports. Entity Apr-Sep kg fell 51% from 2024 to 2025 (25.48M → 12.57M), with May-Jun 2025 at 93/96 shipments. Per F1, IEEPA tariffs were struck down 2026-02-20. Y/y growth is heavily flattered. On a 2-year basis, Apr-Sep 2026 entity kg is still -25% vs 2024, and **trailing-12-month entity kg is flat-to-down** (to Sep-26 28.4M vs 29.6M to Sep-25 vs 43.5M to Sep-24). Owned non-apparel (hardlines/golf) Apr-Sep kg: 2024 12.26M, 2025 5.79M, 2026 6.03M. That is flat y/y and -51% vs 2024.
- **National brands surged too:** national-brand FOB Q3-26 was 2.42M kg vs 0.32M in Q3-25. Part of the Q3 surge is a general restock or DKS buying more national goods FOB, not purely owned-brand mix.
- **Overbuild risk:** the Q3-2022 import surge (3,115 TEU) preceded the 2022-23 industry inventory glut, and mgmt now calls the Q4-2026 environment "increasingly aggressive promotional" (known). X12 found VRST/CALIA items clearing at 75-86% off. If Q3 FY26 DKS-business inventory comes in well above sales growth, this intake may become markdowns, not mix.
- **Consolidator effect:** CDS appeared in 2026. Excluding it, owned apparel Apr-Sep 2026 is -5% vs 2024 (X04-3).
- **Coverage / method:** sea only (air excluded; air is likely used for chase replenishment). The free pages show the top ~48-50 suppliers only (80% of L12 shipments now, about 71% in Sep-2025). Survivorship is reduced but not removed by the union panel (Q3-25 coverage 63%). Classification is my judgment from BOL text: Tangshan basketball stands and DJM are "ambiguous"; CDS (owned) mixes apparel and hardlines. ImportYeti revised its TEU method between snapshots (2024-04 TEU 1,038 in the Sep-25 snapshot vs 1,472 now), so use kg and shipment counts for long comparisons. Recent months get backfilled: Jul-2025 showed 120 shipments in Sep-2025 vs 153 now. So Sep-2026 may still revise UP.
- Shipments ≠ sales. Intake leads sales by 1-4 months, and owned-brand $/kg varies hugely between apparel and treadmills.
- Stack's "never super high" comment (known) is not contradicted: the data says intake is rising from a depressed 2025 base, not that mix structurally re-rated.
