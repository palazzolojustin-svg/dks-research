## Evidence against / caveats
- **Fewer factories, vendors and countries.** The Tier-1 factory count fell 30% from Jan-23 to Jul-24, and softline factories fell 16%. There is no sign of a broader vertical supply base through mid-2024. The bull read is concentration, not expansion.
- **Sample bias in the import join (X14-1).**
  - The panel is X04's top-48 suppliers by last-12-month shipments, so it has survivorship bias. It covers ~86% of entity TEU in 2026 but only ~53% in 2024.
  - Apr-Sep 2025 was distorted by the tariff shipment pause, so y/y is overstated.
  - 765 TEU of 2026 softline comes through a new consolidator. Excluding it, softline is 1,017 TEU, about flat vs 2024's 1,078. X04 independently gets the same result.
- **Worker counts are not DKS volume.** Workers at listed factories reflect the factories' total headcount, not DKS orders, so they should not be read as a volume proxy.
- **Suppliers don't name DKS.** No listed supplier names DKS as a customer. Makalot, Hansae and Top Form revenue cannot be attributed to DKS.
- **The 2021 counts are approximate.** The Oct-2021 list is a 146-page PDF with columns split across pages, so its counts come from tokens.
