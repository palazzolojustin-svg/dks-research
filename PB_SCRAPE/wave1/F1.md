[F1-3] **IEEPA refunds as a way to size DKS's direct imports, which are mostly vertical brands (INFERENCE / lead).** Facts:
- $59.0M of refunds received in Q2 FY26: $57M DICK'S Business, $2M FL. $38.1M was "attributable to tariff costs incurred in the prior year" and excluded from non-GAAP; about $19-21M was in Q2 merch margin.
- The 10-K says "most of our vertical brand merchandise is manufactured abroad", and vertical brands are most of DKS's direct (importer-of-record) imports.
- So $38.1M ≈ the IEEPA duty DKS paid as importer in FY25, which was mostly on vertical goods.

Dividing by an effective IEEPA rate gives the dutiable value. The FY25 average was partial-year, since rates phased in from Feb to Aug 2025: China fentanyl +20%, reciprocal 10% to 20% by country. That implies FY25 direct-import customs value. Repeat with any later CBP/refund disclosure or an 8-K to get a y/y read. This also contradicts the Nov-2024 "very, very negligible" tariff-exposure framing.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Q2 FY26 10-Q: declines in golf, outdoor equipment and **accessories**, three categories where owned brands are #1 or #2 (F1-1). This is the newest hard data point and it cuts against acceleration.
- 2026 margin commentary credits DMN, GC, tariff refunds and pricing, never owned-brand mix. Licensed and trading-card mix was a GM headwind in Q3 FY25 and Q1 FY26 (F1-4).
- Hardlines strength in Q4 FY25 and Q1 FY26 is explained by trading cards, licensed and World Cup product (F1-2).
- Field House gives footwear 50% more space; new third-party athleisure brands take HoS apparel space (F1-5). The Stack "never super high" ceiling (KNOWN) is consistent with this.
- Nike's share of purchases rose 23% (FY22) → 24% → 25% (FY24), before FL. National-brand concentration rose while vertical % stayed about 13% (C01A l.76-82; BURIED). The FY25 31% is consolidated with FL, so it is not comparable.
- Expert E13: "name brands are seeing better sell-through than private label" (KNOWN). E5: PL gains "start to slow down" (KNOWN).
- Margin bridge FY19→FY25: merch +328bps (DUP, primer). INFERENCE: it came while vertical % was flat to down (~15% FY20 → 13% FY24, KNOWN), so vertical mix is an unused lever, but there is also no history of DKS pulling it.
- Caveat on F1-2: the assumption that FL hardlines are about zero is not verified. Verify by checking FL's FY24 10-K category note (P_ files) or asking IR.
