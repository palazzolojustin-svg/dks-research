# X12: Consumer & industry data on private-label / value trade-down in athletic apparel, footwear, golf (2026)
Agent X12, wave 1. Run 2026-10-07. No Apify budget, so $0 spent. Shared WebSearch budget ran out mid-run (200/200), so later work used WebFetch and python requests only.

## Bottom line
- I found no third-party 2026 survey or panel that measures private-label share in athletic apparel or sporting goods. Circana, Numerator, NIQ and PLMA publish private-label share for CPG only. Circana/Golf Datatech/OIA sporting-goods panels are paywalled and have no owned-brand cut.
- The public 2026 consumer data is mixed. Value-seeking is high but has stopped rising (Deloitte private-label intent is flat at 51% for three years). Category data show price-led growth with falling units (Circana: footwear, youth team sports, outdoor). That is a backdrop for opening-price-point owned brands, but it does not prove owned-brand acceleration.
- New time series (Slickdeals deal posts at DKS, May-2025 → Oct-2026): national-brand markdown posts at DKS, especially footwear (Nike, On, adidas, Birkenstock), roughly tripled in Q3-26 y/y. Owned-brand posts only doubled, so the visible price gap to national brands widened. Owned brands (VRST/CALIA) are routinely cleared at 75-86% off, but their share of all DKS deal posts (~12-22%) is roughly in line with their ~13% sales mix.
- Best paid avenue: YipitData already holds SKU-level POS + online transaction data AT Dick's Sporting Goods (published DKS-specific SKU analysis in Jun-2026). It is the one vendor that could directly measure DSG/CALIA/VRST share of DICK'S sales by month.

## Evidence against / caveats
- DKS management has said for two years that there is NO trade-down. It said "no trade down best→better→good" every call from Nov-24 to May-26 and dropped the line in Aug-26 (C01B). An expert said "DSG consumer is trading up" (C04). So owned-brand gains should not be modelled as a macro trade-down story, and the third-party data in X12-3 and X12-8 says trade-down intent is flat or easing in 2026.
- Owned apparel is heavily marked down: VRST/CALIA are routinely at 75-86% off (X12-2), with a clearance spike in May-Jun 2026. That cuts against "owned brands at full price while national brands discount" and suggests some owned-brand inventory needed clearing in early summer 2026.
- Slickdeals limits:
  - Posts are user-submitted and skew to online and clearance.
  - Search pagination stops at about 1,600 posts (DKS history only back to 2025-05-02), so Q2-25 is partial (May-Jun only).
  - Brand flags are regex on titles (I hand-cleaned "DSG" store-pickup mentions, Under Armour and North Face "Quest" false positives).
  - The academy.com control covers only ~20-40 posts per quarter.
- No public owned-brand or private-label share data exists for sporting goods or athletic apparel in 2026 (Circana, Numerator, NIQ and PLMA are CPG-only publicly).
- Unverified statistics are circulating on AI-content blogs (amworldgroup.com 2025-08-12 etc.), with no source cited. Do not use them: "PL buyers visit DKS 2.7x more", "89% higher ScoreCard LTV", "23% less promo correlation".
- Search-tool date errors: WebSearch summaries labelled 2023 Golf Datatech articles as "2026". I checked them by fetching and discarded them.
