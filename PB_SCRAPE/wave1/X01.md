# X01: Review velocity on dicks.com / golfgalaxy.com (unit-sales proxy)
Agent X01, wave 1. Run 2026-10-07. Apify spent: $0.00 (not needed).

## Method in one paragraph
dicks.com and golfgalaxy.com reviews are hosted by **Bazaarvoice** (client `dsg`, displayCode 13107; client `golfgalaxy`, displayCode 15899; found in a Wayback snapshot of a PDP from 2026-04-11). dicks.com itself blocks plain requests with a 403 from Akamai, but Bazaarvoice's public "front door" endpoint serves the same Conversations API the product pages call. It needs **no passkey and no bot challenge**:
`https://apps.bazaarvoice.com/bfd/v1/clients/dsg/api-products/cv2/resources/data/{products|reviews}.json?apiversion=5.4&...` with header `bv-bfd-token: 13107,main_site,en_US`.
`products.json` accepts `Filter=BrandId:eq:<id>`. `reviews.json` accepts a single `ProductId` or a single `CategoryAncestorId` plus `SubmissionTime` ranges and `CampaignId`. Pipeline:
1. Enumerated every owned-brand product: 12,731 product IDs across 16 brand IDs.
2. Pulled every review on those products since 2023-01-01: 152,296 unique reviews.
3. Pulled **all-brand** monthly review totals for 29 dicks.com categories, Jan-2023 to Oct-2026 (2,668 category-months).
4. Metric = **owned-brand share of reviews within each category-month**, using the same current BV category ancestry for numerator and denominator. Raw counts are not usable (see caveats), so share is the unit-mix proxy.
Variant "PIE" = only reviews from DSG's post-purchase incentive email (`CampaignId=ESP_PIE_INCENTIVE`). These are triggered by a purchase, so PIE is the cleanest units proxy. It excludes product-seeding (sampling) reviews.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **Raw review counts are NOT usable as a units series.** Review volume stepped up about 3x site-wide in Jun/Jul-2025, when DSG scaled the post-purchase incentive email. For example, all-brand Golf reviews were 761 in May-25 and 5,390 in Jul-25. The PIE share of owned reviews rose from ~80% to ~95%.
- **Publication lag hides the most recent months.** PIE reviews are released in batches with a lag. Every December is near-empty and every January spikes. Aug-2026 is about half-populated, and Sep-2026 is near-empty (all-brand Women's apparel: 119 reviews in Sep-26 vs 3,656 in Sep-25). Q3 FY26 QTD shares rest on Aug-2026 alone. Re-run in Nov-Dec 2026 to fill Aug-Oct.
- **Share level ≠ sales mix.** Owned brands are ~61% of women's-apparel PIE reviews and ~45% of the apparel basket, far above the known ~13% sales mix. Likely reasons, all INFERENCE: owned brands carry most of their volume through dicks.com review collection; national brands' reviews are partly syndicated in (excluded here) or bought with lower review propensity; footwear is excluded. Use **changes** in share, not levels. If DSG began targeting PIE emails toward owned brands, the share rise would be overstated. I found no evidence of this, and it cannot be tested from outside.
- Women's apparel is flat y/y and down in Q3 QTD. CALIA's style count is falling. Golf total is flat. Maxfli's y/y gain is ~0 in Q3. Owned golf-club share is falling. Team sports is flat across Apr-Aug.
- BV category ancestry is the current catalog's (messy, with seasonal promo categories). Categories overlap: golf apparel can sit in both Men's apparel and Golf. Baskets were not summed across categories.
- About 16% of review rows were duplicate review IDs across product variants. These are de-duplicated in the numerator, and the denominator is BV's own count.
- Golf Galaxy shares one review pool with dicks.com: 100% of GG owned-brand review IDs also appear in the dsg data. It adds no independent signal.
