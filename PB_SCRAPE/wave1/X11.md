# X11: Price gap & markdown depth: owned vs national brands (wave 1)
Agent: X11 | Run 2026-10-07 | Apify spent: $0.00 (budget $2; not needed)

## Method in one paragraph
Plain requests to dicks.com are blocked (Akamai 403) and Wayback was offline. The workaround is Common Crawl (CC), which has free, public, monthly crawls. It holds HTTP-200 captures of dicks.com category pages (/f/...) from Aug-2025 to Sep-2026; the 2024 to Jul-2025 captures are all 403 or absent. Each captured page embeds the site's own server-side JSON (`<script id=dcsg-ngx-plp-server-state>`, the /v2/search response). That JSON holds the category totalCount, facet counts (Brand, a "Sale" facet = number of SKUs on sale in the whole category, Price), and the first 48 products. For each product it gives brand (mfName), SKU list price and offer price, min/max list and offer across SKUs, dsgPriceIndicators.dealsPercentage, dsgProductSortDate, and an attribute **6025 = "Vertical Brand"** that DKS itself uses to tag owned-brand products. That tag covers DSG, CALIA, VRST, Maxfli, Quest, Nishiki, ETHOS, Fitness Gear, Walter Hagen, Alpine Design, "DICK'S Sporting Goods", Top Flite, P-TEX, DBX, Tommy Armour Golf and Northeast Outfitters.
The sample is 14 crawls, ~400-800 category pages each (60% priority brand/category slugs + 40% deterministic random), giving ~4k-27k unique products per crawl and ~200k product rows in total. "On sale" = the displayed SKU's offer price < its list price. "Avg disc" = mean of (1 - offer/list) over all products, with zero for full-price items.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Owned brands are discounted more, not less, than national brands (X11-4), and the gap widened y/y. CALIA (X11-5) looks like the weak spot in summer 2026: deep clearance prices ($9-18) and median realized price down ~11%.
- High-low pricing makes "% on sale" a poor margin proxy for owned brands. Owned list prices behave like reference prices: 20% of owned products are always on sale. The GM premium is about cost structure, which this scrape cannot see.
- Online-only, and only the first 48 "Featured" results per page, so merchandising choices (pinning, "isPinned") affect the sample. The displayed swatch may be a clearance colour. Store shelf prices and in-cart/loyalty promos (ScoreCard, coupons) are not captured.
- CC sampling is uncontrolled: the set of URLs differs each month, and Feb/Mar-2026 samples are small. The brand-landing-page Sale-facet series (raw\X11_brandpage_sale.csv) is too composition-sensitive to use alone. The matched-URL brand-facet panel (61 pages) was dominated by one large page and was inconclusive: owned SKU share 3.27% → 2.19%, with 18 pages up, 13 down and 30 flat.
- The rise in owned listing share (X11-2) is +1.1pp with a CI that only just excludes zero. It reflects online placement/assortment, not unit sales.
- National promo intensity in Aug-Sep 2026 rose only modestly (+3pp). Most of the swing is seasonal (Dec-Feb peaks).
