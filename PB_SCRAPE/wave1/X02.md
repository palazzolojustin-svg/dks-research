# X02: Live dicks.com catalog census (wave 1)
Agent: X02 | Run 2026-10-07 | Apify budget $5, spent $0.00
(Top-100 apparel lists could not be pulled: the Akamai challenge persisted on two retries over about 2 hours; see leads.)

Method summary (all accessed 2026-10-07):
- Listing pages `https://www.dickssportinggoods.com/f/<category>` embed `<script id="dcsg-ngx-plp-server-state">` JSON (PRODUCT_LIST_API_RESPONSE). For every product it has brand (`mfName`), **DKS's own owned-brand flag (attribute `6025` = "Vertical Brand")**, a launch/sort date (attribute `4474` = `dsgProductSortDate`), list and offer price range, and Bazaarvoice review count and rating. It also has brand facet counts. Parameters: `pageSize=144`, `pageNumber=N` (0-based), `selectedSort=1` (Top Sellers) / `6` (New Products) / default `5` (Featured), `filterFacets=X_BRAND%3A<brand>`.
- I ran the census in the built-in browser with same-origin fetches, one request at a time with a 400 ms gap. It covered 40 dicks.com categories (full pagination plus Top-Sellers top-144 plus New-Products top-144 each), giving 33,950 rows and 22,122 unique category×product listings. Golf Galaxy: 5 categories (5,566 rows) before Akamai began returning 403s.
- "Owned" = attribute 6025 flag. Flagged brands seen: DSG, CALIA, Walter Hagen, Maxfli, VRST, Top Flite, Nishiki, Quest, Alpine Design, Tommy Armour Golf, ETHOS, Fitness Gear, "DICK'S Sporting Goods", PRIMED, Tour Trek. Only 6 DSG and 3 Nishiki listings lacked the flag.
- Sitemaps: robots.txt points to `seo_sitemap.xml`, which links 7 product sitemaps (164,958 product URLs, open to plain python). The Wayback Machine has complete archived sets for 2024-05-15 and 2025-05-22, and a near-complete set for Nov-2023.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Assortment breadth share is flat 2023→2026 (X02-5). There is no SKU-count acceleration; Nike product pages grew faster than owned ones May-25→Oct-26.
- Owned products are more promotional on-site than national brands (X02-6), including 2026 launches. This contradicts a simple "owned at full price" story.
- Launch-month share is flat to down y/y (X02-8).
- Top-Sellers ranking could be influenced by DKS's own boosting; the default Featured sort clearly favours owned brands (X02-4). Top-Sellers (20%) is lower than Featured (26%) but still 2.7x the assortment share.
- No owned brand exists in running footwear (0 of 766 products), consistent with Stack's "no vertical footwear" comment.
- Owned brands are weak in team sports (2.0% of products, 0% of top-24) and in kids' boys tops (2.1%).
- Facet counts vary slightly between requests (for example CALIA in leggings 29 vs 32), likely because of store and inventory context. Use deltas of several points or more only.
- These are single-day snapshots, so there is no 2025 comparison for the best-seller metric yet. See leads.
