# R12 (wave 2): Owned-brand share of listed products, year over year, across ALL dicks.com / golfgalaxy.com census categories (extends X02)
Agent R12 | run 2026-10-07 05:50-07:25 CDT | Apify $0.00 | dicks.com was NOT hit live (Wayback + Common Crawl only, plus X02's 2026-10-07 live census files)

## Bottom line
- **Owned share of listed products has accelerated in the last 12 months.** Same-season, archive-vs-archive comparisons show it rising about **+0.8 to +0.9pt y/y (about +15% relative) into Aug/Sep-2026**, after it was **flat over the prior ~18-20 months** (early-2024 → Aug/Sep-2025).
  - On 735 matched category pages (R12-1): **5.82% → 6.76%**, bootstrap 95% CI +0.56 to +1.42pt. Owned listings rose +25% while all listings rose +8%. The prior period was −0.26pt, with a CI of −0.73 to +0.31pt.
  - On the X02 census categories (R12-2), 18 categories: **8.1% → 8.9% pooled; 8.3% → 9.5% like-for-like**. Feb-May-2024 → Aug/Sep-2025 on 29 categories was flat (7.1% → 7.1%).
- **Where it is happening.**
  - Women's and men's apparel, fitness, outdoor and accessories lead (+1 to +2.4pt by group). Women's tops, women's golf, men's pants, wedges, dumbbells and coolers are +2 to +5pt.
  - Kids apparel, team sports and golf hardgoods are flat to down. Leggings plateaued at 26-27% after their Feb-2024 → Aug-2025 step.
  - DSG (+45% listings), Walter Hagen (+62%) and ETHOS (+55%) drive breadth. CALIA breadth is flat to down (−5%).
- **Against live Oct-2026 (not same-season), the gain vanishes: 7.4% → 7.4%.**
  - Oct assortments are larger, and national brands, Nike above all, re-expanded on dicks.com. Nike listings were +53% on the census categories but only +20% on the broad panel.
  - So X02's 6-category +2.6pt is the top of the distribution, not the chain average.
- **Model implication (INFERENCE).** Listing share is a leading indicator, not sales mix. It supports M1's +0.5pt/yr penetration base case and an apparel-led +1pt/yr upside, and it marks FY26 as a change of slope versus FY24-FY25.

## Method
All sources were accessed 2026-10-07.
- Archived category pages embed DKS's own catalog API response. It contains the complete brand facet (X_BRAND = number of listed products per brand), the first page of products, and DKS's owned flag (attribute 6025 = "Vertical Brand").
- **Owned share** = Σ owned-brand facet counts ÷ Σ all brand facet counts on that category URL.
- **Owned brands** = every brand DKS flags 6025 in any parsed page: DSG, CALIA, VRST, Walter Hagen, Lady Hagen, Maxfli, Top Flite, Tommy Armour Golf, Alpine Design, ETHOS, Fitness Gear, Nishiki, Quest, PRIMED, Tour Trek, "DICK'S Sporting Goods", and Field & Stream (still flagged in Dec-2023).
- **Parser fix (an avenue finding).** X02 wrote that pre-2025 captures have "no embedded response". They do. The 2022 → Mar-2024 layout carries the same response in `window.__STATE__ … productApiData {productVOs, facetVOs}`, with the same 6025 flag and the complete brand facet. `scripts\R12_common.py` parses both layouts, which unlocks history from 2022 onward.
- **Sources:**

| Source | What was used |
|---|---|
| Common Crawl (CC) | 117 WARC records of the exact `/f/<slug>` URLs, found via the CC index files X03/R9 had already downloaded |
| Wayback | 45 pages from the R12 job, plus X02's and X03's cached pages re-parsed |
| R9's CC panel extraction | 735 to 1,318 matched category pages, re-measured with the facet metric |
| X02's live census | 07-Oct-2026 facets, default store 15108, the same store context as the archived dicks.com pages |

- Several CC captures are the same captures Wayback holds, because Wayback ingests CC.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **Against live Oct-2026, aggregate listing share is flat** (R12-2). Nike (+53%) and other national brands expanded as fast as owned brands on the census categories.
- **Declines:**

|---|---|---|

| Boys' shorts | 11.7% | 5.3% |

| Sports bras | 24.8% (Dec-23) | 19.2% |
| Sleeping bags | 14.3% (Mar-24) | 8.5% |
| Family tents | 11.7% (Apr-24) | 6.8% |
| Soccer balls | 10.1% (Feb-24) | 4.4% |
| Golf drivers | 5.4% (Feb-23) | 2.7% |

  Running shoes are 0% owned in every capture.
- **Kids apparel is the weakest owned group on both measures** (panel −0.56pt; census −1 to −2.5pt).
- **CALIA breadth fell 5%** on the panel.
- **Owned share of clearance-apparel rose** (13.5% → 16.3%).
- **Part of 2024-25 owned-share movement is denominator churn.** Women's golf apparel listings fell 1,274 → 711 as third-party brands such as "Tail" were cut (617 → 197 across matched categories). That is why like-for-like brand shares are shown.
- **Listing share ≠ sales share.**
  - Facet counts are in-stock listings for the page's store context (15108 dicks.com, 10701 GG).
  - Seasonal assortment changes the denominator, which is why same-season comparisons are preferred.
  - Panel pages overlap.
- **Single captures per category per period;** dates differ by up to about 6 weeks within a window.
- **No dicks.com archive captures exist for about Apr-2024 → Jul-2025** (Akamai 403 to crawlers; Wayback has none either). There is no Aug-2024 or Apr-2025 point, so an H1-2025 → H1-2026 y/y cannot be built.
