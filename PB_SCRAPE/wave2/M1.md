**Bottom line**
- F2 and F6 are both arithmetically right. They disagree for three reasons:
  - **(a) Denominator.** F6 counts "Other" revenue (services, shipping, GameChanger, Dick's Media Network; 1-3% of sales and rising) as non-footwear. That understates penetration by 0.3-0.7pt and flattens the trend.
  - **(b) Rounding.** Each used only one of the two rounded 10-K disclosures ($ or %), not both.
  - **(c) Window.** F6 starts at the FY20 COVID high. F2 starts at the FY23 trough.
- **Reconciled result.** Taking the tightest band consistent with both the $ and % disclosures, owned-brand penetration of DICK'S non-footwear merchandise was:
  - **17.6-17.85% in FY23** (trough);
  - **18.0-18.8% in FY24**;
  - **18.7-19.6% in FY25**.
- **What is and isn't proven.**
  - FY23 → FY25 rose by **+0.8 to +2.0pt**. That rise is real: the two bands do not overlap.
  - FY24 → FY25 cannot be told apart from rounding noise.
  - FY25 only matches the FY20/FY22 highs (18.3-19.5%). The long-run drift since FY18 is only about +0.17pt a year.
- **The 2026 picture.** Two non-owned items keep diluting the headline mix:
  - DICK'S footwear share was still rising y/y through 1H FY26.
  - Non-merchandise "Other" revenue rose a further **+0.4 to +1.0pt of DICK'S sales y/y in every quarter** (a new dilution source).
  - As a result, consensus's "flat ~12.8%" needs penetration to *rise* ~0.2pt just to hold.
- **Financial size.** Even the most bullish case (+1pt a year of penetration plus footwear share −1pt a year) is worth only **+13bps of DICK'S GM, +$20M EBIT and +$0.16 EPS (+1.2%) in FY27E**, rising to +20bps, +$32M and +$0.26 in FY28E.
  - The owned-brand mix lever is real but small. It supports a GM-floor / quality argument. It does not move EPS on its own.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **No acceleration visible in disclosed data.** FY25 penetration (18.7-19.6%) only matches the FY20 and FY22 levels (18.3-19.5 and 18.2-19.1). FY24→FY25 is within rounding.
- **The headline will keep printing "~13%".** Footwear share was up y/y in 1H FY26 at the base FL assumption, and "Other" revenue keeps rising (M1-3). The 10-K % is too coarse (±0.5pt) to confirm any variant before FY27.
- **The 1H FY26 non-footwear pool grew on non-owned drivers:** World Cup (adidas), trading cards (lower margin) and licensed goods. Golf, outdoor equipment and accessories declined in Q2 FY26 (W04 L1157). Owned brands therefore need to beat a pool inflated by national and licensed product to gain penetration.
- **The "vertical" metric includes licensed brands** (adidas football, Cobra, Marucci, Lotto; Prince until FY24; per N1). Owned-only penetration is lower and its trend is unknown. Cobra/Marucci/Lotto were added in FY23-FY24, so part of the FY23→FY25 rise could be newly licensed lines, not owned brands. **This is the biggest threat to M1-1's "real gain" reading.**
- **The FL category split is unknown after the acquisition.** FL's FY20-FY24 footwear share ranged 80-84%. Fast Break's reintroduction of national apparel would *lower* FL's footwear share, which would mean the 84% base overstates DICK'S apparel and understates DICK'S penetration. All FY25+ and quarterly levels carry this ±0.5-2pt uncertainty.
- **The P&L effect is small** (M1-5) and assumes 100% drop-through. Owned brands carry design, R&D and marketing SG&A, and 2026 owned-brand discounting rose (F6-1). The net effect could be below the gross 800bp premium.
- **The rounding convention is assumed.** If DKS truncates rather than rounds "approximately X%", the bands shift up to +0.5pt. The intersection method is still valid but the FY23 band would widen.
- **FY20-FY21 denominators are COVID-distorted** (hardlines 46% / 44% of sales from fitness and outdoor), so the FY20 "high" is partly a denominator artefact.
