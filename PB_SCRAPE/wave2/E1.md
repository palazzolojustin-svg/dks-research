# E1: DKS marketing-email archive → owned-brand promo vs feature series (wave 2)
Agent: E1 | Run 2026-10-07 | Apify spent: $0.01 (1 failed rag-web-browser call) of $2 budget

## Bottom line
- Milled.com, the email archive this assignment targeted, could not be accessed. Every page returns a Cloudflare "Just a moment..." JS challenge (HTTP 403), and I did not try to get past it. Wayback holds almost no 2024-26 milled pages. I pivoted to the **dicks.com homepage promo grid in the Wayback Machine** (144 usable daily captures, Jan-2024 → Jun-2026). The homepage tiles carry the same weekly offers as the emails.
- **Result: owned brands are being marketed far more at full price, and their discount presence has not grown.**
  - Feb-Jun 2026 vs Feb-Jun 2024 on the DKS homepage: owned-brand *feature* mentions per capture rose **0.60 → 2.10**. Captures showing any owned-brand feature rose **29% → 100%**. Owned features per 100 national-brand mentions rose **5.9 → 17.9**.
  - Owned-brand *discount* phrases per capture were flat-to-down (**0.64 → 0.53**). The owned share of all discount phrases fell **31.5% → 26.3%**.
  - The step-change starts in **Feb-2026**. Every month before that sits at 0-45% of captures with an owned feature.
- This reframes the JPM tracker count of owned-brand discount events (0 → 1 → 6). Owned-brand markdowns are now concentrated in a few very deep, labelled **"Exclusive Brands" events**: 12/18/25 up to 90% off; 4/15/26 up to 70% on both the DKS and Golf Galaxy heroes; 7/6/26 Walter Hagen up to 89%; 10/5/26 CALIA Inspire jogger $98 → $8.97. Meanwhile the everyday homepage features owned-brand launches at full price.
- The honest caveat: depth is extreme. Slickdeals owned-brand posts have a median of about 80% off in both 2025 and 2026. Clearance is real. It is not getting deeper, but it is more frequent (X12).

## Evidence against / caveats
- **Depth.** Owned-brand event days run at 70-91% off, and Slickdeals medians are about 80% off. Owned apparel, especially CALIA and Walter Hagen, clears at very low price points ($8.97-$29.98). If 2026's higher owned-brand sales come partly from clearance units, the GM premium on incremental mix is below 700-900bp.
- **Frequency.** JPM's tracker shows owned-brand-named discount events rising 0 → 1 → 6 (F5/F6). My homepage data says everyday owned-brand discount presence is flat, but I cannot see emails directly. Email frequency of owned-brand markdowns may still have risen.
- **Coverage.** Wayback homepage captures are thin or blocked for Aug-2024 → Mar-2025 and Jul-Aug 2026. So there is no clean y/y for Q3, and I cannot tell whether the Feb-Jun 2026 feature intensity persisted into BTS.
- **Wording.** "Feature" means marketing prominence, not sell-through. A launch tile can still be a slow seller. The text-based classifier also mislabels a few phrases where an adjacent tile's text falls inside the discount window. I hand-checked the 2026 phrases, but expect ±1 per month.
- **Mix of what's discounted.** Owned brands now appear in national-brand headline promos ("Up to 50% Off Select Nike, adidas, VRST" 6/15/26; "Up to 50% Off Patagonia, The North Face, VRST" 2/14-16/26). That can be read as parity positioning or as markdown pressure.
