## Evidence against / caveats
- **Advertising is not mix.** All of this shows marketing weight. None of it shows units or revenue. VRST got heavy TV weight in Aug-Sep 2026, but iSpot's spend rank (#863 overall) implies a modest budget (INFERENCE), and no sales outcome is visible yet.
- **iSpot covers national linear TV only.** Streaming/CTV, YouTube, social, cinema and local TV are not in the airing counts. DSG's main platform (Sporthood) runs largely outside that scope, and so may much of CALIA's 2026 push (the Turlington campaign, X10; the creator push, X08). CALIA had zero national airings in the window and has had no CALIA-branded TV creative since May-2025, apart from one DKS-umbrella 'Want It? Got It: Calia' :15 (2026-05-29).
- **The 30-day window is a single snapshot.** Wayback has no snapshots of the VRST/CALIA iSpot pages (CDX empty), so I can't produce a like-for-like Sep-2025 window. The VRST "relaunch" claim therefore rests on creative publish dates, not on airing history.
- **iSpot "Published" date is the cataloguing date.** It approximates first airing but is not the airing history.
- **The DICK'S master-brand creative list is a sample** (about 60 of 540 reached by crawl/search). Owned-brand executions inside the umbrella brand may be undercounted in [R4-3].
- **Spend rank ordering cuts both ways.** DKS master (#676) still out-spends VRST (#863). VRST's airings lead may reflect cheaper inventory (more :15s) as much as budget.
