[N4-2] **The exec-comp metric "External Merchandise Margin" is not a vertical-brand KPI. It is consolidated merchandise margin *including vendor margin support*. In March 2026 the board more than tripled the Executive Chairman's stake in it.**

Verbatim definitions:
- 2026 proxy (filed 2026-05-01, p.28-29): "A portion of 2025 LTIP achievement will be measured based on a new metric: External Merchandise Margin … measured over both 2025 and 2026 based on consolidated merchandise margin, which includes margin support received from vendors."
- Predecessor, 2023 LTIP (FY23-24): "Adjusted Merchandise Margin Retention", "based on consolidated merchandise margin, adjusted to include margin support received from vendors". Its rationale: "reflects the strategic focus on preserving the significant margin rate improvements realized since 2019".
  - Weight 20% (Net Sales 40%, Adjusted EBT 40%).
  - Result: "between target and maximum".
  - Aggregate payout of the 2023 LTIP: 118.1% (2025 proxy p.37).

March 2026 changes (2026 proxy p.36-37):
- Stack's 2025 LTIP target raised from **$5.0M to $17.5M**; Hobart's to **$7.0M**. The two-year performance period runs FY25-FY26, so FY26 merch margin % directly drives their payout.
- Stack "oversees the Company's merchandising and real estate functions" (2026 proxy, director bio).

Other proxy data points:
- 2025 STIP attainment 100%.
- 2025 annual PSU: EBT "just below the high range of target"; sales "between the high range of target and maximum".
- The numeric thresholds and targets are images and are not in the text.

INFERENCE on "External": it is undefined beyond the sentences above. It plausibly means merchandise sold to outside customers, excluding services such as GameChanger and DMN. Treat that reading as unverified.

- Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1089063/000108906326000015/dks-20260501.htm and https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1089063/000108906325000054/dks-20250501.htm. ## Evidence against / caveats
- Q1 FY26 DICK'S merch & services margin fell −29bps y/y (derived). MD&A blamed "sales mix" in a quarter of comp +6.0% with owned brands discussed on the call. The mix that quarter (trading cards, licensed, World Cup) swamped any owned-brand gain.
- Q2 FY26 merch margin +137bps is mostly IEEPA refunds and DMN/GameChanger leverage, per the 10-Q (W04 L1190). Management did not credit vertical brands.
- The 10-Q forward-looking plan language on vertical-brand space, marketing and categories was dropped from FY25-FY26 10-Qs (N4-3).
- The exec-comp merch-margin metric counts vendor margin support. FY26 incentives can be met through national-brand markdown funding rather than owned-brand mix (N4-2).
- FY26 guidance (2026-09-03): "full year gross margin to decline slightly … most pronounced in the third quarter", in a "more promotional marketplace" (W04 L1118). That is a headwind to owned-brand full-price sell-through (see F5/F6 promo findings).
- The Q2 FY26 10-Q says industry inventory built up in "legacy footwear silhouettes **and apparel franchises**" (W04 L1114). Promotional national-brand apparel narrows the price gap that owned apparel exploits. This is AGAINST in the near term but could set up 2H share gains (F6-3, 2017 analog).
- W08 deal communications (May-Aug 2025): no owned-brand content beyond the already-known FL apparel "toe to head" quote (C01A L920, DUP).
- W06 debt docs: only boilerplate vertical-brand risk wording in forward-looking statements.
