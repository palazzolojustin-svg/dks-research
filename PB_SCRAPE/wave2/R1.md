[R1-4] **CORRECTION to X01: there is no "review publication lag", and Aug-Oct 2026 will not backfill. DSG's post-purchase review email has been paused since 2026-08-11. That is unprecedented outside the December holiday blackout.**

- **Send calendar.** PIE emails go out **every Tuesday** (118 of 119 detected batches; a response spike on Tuesday-Wednesday, then decay). Every year there is a Dec blackout: last batch 2024-11-26 â†’ 2025-01-07, and 2025-11-25 â†’ 2026-01-06 (2,216 apparel PIE reviews on the first day back).
- **The 2026 pause.**

  - Apparel PIE reviews per week fell from 730 to 31, 10, 17, 7, 3, 2, 1.
  - The longest previous non-December gap was 21 days (Apr and May 2025).
- **The rest of the review flow is still running:** organic, MyAccount (relaunched Mar-2026) and vendor sampling (e.g. a Callaway sampling campaign in Aug-2026).
- **Program size had already shrunk.** 2026 PIE volume was about 30% below 2025 in the same weeks: Aug 1-17 apparel 2,774 vs 3,968; Jul weekly ~700-800 vs ~900-1,100.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **Organic shares fell** y/y (R1-6). **CALIA and golf clubs are losing** (R1-7, R1-8). **Q1 FY26** blended index is âˆ’1.8% y/y. In 1H FY26 the gain is small: Feb-Jul apparel PIE+ORG is +0.4pp (z 1.0). The clear acceleration is Apr-Aug 2026 only, and Aug rests on two weekly batches.
- **The PIE program is paused** (R1-4). From Aug 17 onward there is no clean read until it resumes. It had also shrunk about 30% y/y before pausing.
- **Review share â‰  sales share.** Owned shares (46% of apparel reviews) far exceed the ~13% sales mix. The Î² back-test is two points and the 10-K bands are wide. Owned buyers may have a higher review propensity. Units â‰  $ (owned ASPs are lower than Nike's), so a unit-share gain overstates $-share gain. This is another reason for Î² < 1.
- **Nike's lower share may partly reflect DKS carrying fewer Nike apparel SKUs** (assortment decision or Nike wholesale pullback), not consumer preference. Either way it is owned share of the apparel floor, but it is supply-led.
- **National numerators cover 35 named brands only and exclude footwear-named products.** Owned shares use full all-brand denominators, so they are unaffected.
- **Categories are BV's current catalog ancestry** (messy; baskets can overlap, e.g. golf apparel sits in both Men's and Golf).
- **The Q3 to-date windows are short.** The fitness n is ~120.
