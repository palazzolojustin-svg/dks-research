## Method in one paragraph
Bazaarvoice front-door `products.json` (client `dsg`, header `bv-bfd-token: 13107,main_site,en_US`, no key; see X01) returns every dicks.com style ever fed to BV, **active and discontinued** (971,783 records; 186,149 active), with DKS item id, brand, current category ancestry, active flag, **the full UPC list (= size x colour SKUs)** and first/last review date. Two pulls: (1) every owned/exclusive brand by `BrandId` (15,000 rows, 23 brand ids incl. Monarch, DICK'S-branded, Slazenger, P-TEX, Jawbone, DBX); (2) an **all-brand census of 16 category trees** (women's, men's, boys, girls apparel, golf, fitness, camping, bikes, outdoor, team sports, men's/women's/youth footwear, slides, swim; 199,856 product rows). Season = 2-digit item-id prefix. Calibration from first-review dates: "25" styles reach customers Mar-2025 to Mar-2026, so **prefix ≈ the DKS fiscal year of launch** (FY25 = Feb-25 to Jan-26). Fan/licensed team gear is removed by name filter. Because BV keeps discontinued styles, there is **no survivorship bias**, unlike the sitemap/listing counts in X02-5/X02-8/X11.

[R5-2] **"UPC clock": DKS is creating owned-brand SKUs ~25-30% faster than in 2022-24.**
- How it works: owned and DKS-sourced goods carry UPCs from DKS's own GS1 company prefixes, used in sequence: 889751/889752 (2015-19) → 194375 (2019-22) → 196358 (2021-24) → 198690 (2024-26) → 199980 (opened 2026Q3).
- Within a block, the item number rises steadily with time. I dated it by the median item number of styles first reviewed in each quarter.

| Block | Quarter → quarter (12 months) | Median item number | UPCs used in 12 months (median / p90) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 196358 | 2022Q3 → 2023Q3 | 13.4k → 48.0k | 34.6k |
| 196358 | 2023Q3 → 2024Q3 | 48.0k → 86.3k | 38.2k / 36.1k |
| 198690 | 2025Q1 → 2026Q1 | 13.8k → 56.8k | 43.0k / 45.5k |
| 198690 | 2025Q2 → 2026Q2 | 20.3k → 71.8k | 51.6k / 45.8k |
| 198690 | 2025Q3 → 2026Q3 | 31.9k → 79.6k | **47.7k / 46.9k** |

- **Block lifetimes:** 196358 took ~2.75 years to use 100k UPCs; 198690 took ~1.75 years, and 199980 started in 2026Q3 (27 reviewed styles, median item 3.2k).
- Cross-check with the 99th percentile of UPC position for owned styles live in the X02 sitemap snapshots: May-24 → May-25 = +40.1k; May-25 → Oct-26 = +80.5k over 16.5 months (= 58.5k annualised).
- **Who is in the clock:** non-owned brands using DKS UPCs in FY25-26 are negligible (see R5-8), so the 2025-26 clock is essentially owned brands. The 2022-24 base included Prince and Northeast Outfitters, so the owned-only acceleration is if anything larger.
- Raw: `raw/R5_upc_clock_block_quarter.csv`, `raw/R5_upc_clock_quarterly.csv`. Script: `scripts/R5_upc_clock.py`.
- NEW method and series. Confidence M:
  - dating relies on review lag;
  - the Jun/Jul-2025 review step-up makes post-step items look newer, which inflates the 2024→2025 step but not the clean 2025Q3→2026Q3 step;
  - UPCs measure SKU set-up (buying intent), not sell-through.
- SUPPORTS.
- Model use: owned SKU (size x colour) creation is up ~+25-30% y/y while owned style count is up only ~0-15% (R5-6). DKS is buying deeper on fewer styles, an input-side lead indicator for owned-brand units in 2H26-FY27. It matches X04/X14's +168-181% y/y owned-factory TEU in Jul-Sep 26.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **CALIA is shrinking** (DUP for the headline 128 vs 245 in X01-6). The new detail: women's new styles 76 vs 169, swim 9 vs 28, bras 1 vs 5. The women's owned new-style share fell 14.2% → 12.1%. Golf owned share is down (R5-9).
- **Review-based like-for-like (same calendar cut-off) is contaminated by review-regime changes.** It shows a big FY24 → FY25 step and FY26 flat-to-up:
  - Owned share of new-season styles first reviewed by Jun-30, ex-footwear: 3.1 / 5.6 / 7.9 / 8.8% (FY23/24/25/26).
  - By Aug-31: 4.6 / 6.0 / 10.4 / 9.6%.
  - Reasons: the Jun/Jul-2025 PIE step-up raised 2025 first reviews, and Aug/Sep-2026 reviews are not yet fully published (X01).
  - Treat FY26 vs FY25 as "holding the FY25 step", not as accelerating, on this metric. Raw: `raw/R5_lfl_share_cutoffs.csv`, `raw/R5_lfl_owned_brands.csv`.
- **Raw count of owned styles reviewed by Oct-7 of season year:** 321 / 347 / 483 / 414 (FY23-26). FY26 is below FY25, but this is affected by the same lag.
- **Style share ≠ sales mix.** Owned brands are 8% of new styles but ~13% of sales and ~18% of non-footwear sales. Use direction only.
- **FY26 YTD vs full FY25** assumes similar intra-season launch timing (verified for apparel, not for golf). Season prefix = launch season is INFERENCE from first-review dates (~88% of reviewed "25" styles had their first review in 2025Q1-2026Q1; 3% fell in 2026Q2-Q3).
- **Category ancestry is BV's current mapping.** Some old styles sit in "Orphan"/promo categories. Keyword classification (R5-6/7) has some misclassification; spot-checked.
- **UPC clock (R5-2):** it assumes DKS assigns UPCs in sequence within a block (borne out: quarterly medians rise steadily through each block's main life, with noise at block start and tail; no regression fitted). Blocks overlap briefly at transitions, so within-block deltas are used. It counts SKUs set up, including store-only and Golf Galaxy-only goods.
- The DC collab and pickleball shoes are new and unproven (few or no reviews).
