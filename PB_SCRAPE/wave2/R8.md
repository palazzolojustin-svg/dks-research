## Evidence against / caveats
- **No acceleration in the owned share of direct imports** (R8-2, R8-3, R8-6). On value, kg and BOL-count bases alike, 2026 owned share sits in the 2023-24 range. The 2025 highs were a tariff artifact: national-brand FOB stopped. The import data supports "owned apparel intake rebuilt above 2024", NOT "owned mix accelerating faster than consensus".
- **X04's headline numbers were overstated.** CDS is about 80% non-apparel by weight, and 3 suppliers were double-counted in the 2016-2025 union panel (R8-1). Owned apparel Apr-Sep 2026 is +34% vs 2024, not +89%. Ex-CDS it is +3%.
- **Owned HARDGOODS intake is down sharply**: Q1-Q3 value -43% vs 2024, with outdoor/camp and fitness weakest. Part of that is suppliers leaving the visible top-50 (survivorship) and relocating China→Vietnam/Cambodia. But the residual "unknown" did not grow (Q1-Q3 est. $47M in 2026 vs $45M in 2024), so most of the decline looks real. It cuts against a broad owned-brand acceleration; Quest/Alpine Design/ETHOS look smaller.
- **Only ~half of vertical-brand product is on DKS's own entries** (R8-4). Import data cannot see vendor-delivered DDP private label, so it is an incomplete proxy.
- **Overbuild risk**: Aug-Sep 2026 intake at 2.30x run-rate is the highest ratio since at least 2017; the 2022 analogue (2.01x) preceded the glut. With a "highly promotional" Q4 (known) and X12's 75-86% clearance on VRST/CALIA items, part of this could end up as markdowns rather than mix.
- **Method limits**: sea only; top-50 supplier panels (coverage 50-91% of entity kg by quarter, worst Q3-2025); supplier splits are quarterly, so monthly owned/national for 2023-Jun 2026 is an ALLOCATION, and only Jul-Sep 2026 has true BOL-level monthly data; $/kg are Comtrade averages (Comtrade HS6 net weights were unreliable, so R8 used HS4/chapter anchors); brand-group mapping is by supplier; ImportYeti backfills the last 1-2 months upward (Sep-2026 may still rise).
