# R3 (wave 2): DICK'S Sporting Goods Foundation 990-PF: back out DSG brand sales
Agent R3, wave 2. Run 2026-10-07. Apify: $0. WebSearch: 5 calls. No logins, no forms.

Bottom line: the 990-PF itself can't isolate the DSG 1% giveback. DKS Inc is NOT a named Schedule B contributor in FY22 or FY23, and only $0.2M in FY24, so the giveback is booked inside the pool of unnamed contributions under $5k each, mixed with register round-up donations. DKS and Foundation publications do, however, disclose two hard DSG giveback figures, and those convert to DSG sales:
- **≥$690M cumulative DSG sales from the Aug-2019 launch through FY21**, which is ≥2.6% of DKS sales.
- **≥$300M in the first 9 months of FY24**, which is ≥3.1% of DKS sales and ~$420M+ annualized, about 25% of the $1.7B vertical total.

INFERENCE: DSG grew at roughly 5-10%/yr floor-to-floor vs DKS +3.0%/yr over FY21-FY24. There is no 2025 or 2026 DSG $ figure. The Foundation stopped quoting the YTD number after Q3 2024. The FY25 990-PF (FYE 2026-01-31) is not filed yet; it is expected around Nov-Dec 2026.

## Evidence against / caveats
- Both DSG $ disclosures are lower bounds ("more than", "over"), with different scopes and a wording conflict (R3-2). The growth estimate is floor-to-floor and could be off by several points either way.
- The DSG figures say nothing about Apr-Oct 2026, the window the thesis needs. The latest datapoint is 9M FY24.
- The Foundation has gone quiet on DSG $ since 2024. That parallels X10-1 (mgmt silence on owned brands since the cut). It is not evidence of decline, but it removes a disclosure channel.
- The DSG share of vertical rising to ~25% in FY24 is consistent with DSG replacing Field & Stream (KNOWN) rather than with incremental mix. The vertical total stayed ~13% FY23-FY25 (KNOWN).
- Foundation grant budgets were cut in 2025: Good Sports, LISC and Every Kid Sports went from $2M/$2M/$2.5M (2024) to $1.25M each (2025) per the YE 2024/YE 2025 QGS. This is not linked to DSG, but it means total Foundation inflows aren't booming.
- The 990-PF residual includes POS round-up campaigns. The Q2 2025 "Sports Matter Night" alone raised ">$4 million" (QGS Q2 2025, 2025-09-04; likely FYE Jan-26 return), so FY25 residual growth will be contaminated.
