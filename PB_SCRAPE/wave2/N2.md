**Bottom line:** the 8-K/press-release set has very little direct owned-brand content. The novel items are:
- a dated wording series in the press releases' forward-looking-statement (FLS) risk lists, including a new Aug-2026 insertion of "competition, innovation" into the vertical-brand risk;

## Evidence against / caveats
- The press releases never quantify vertical brands: no $, %, growth or margin figure appears in any of the 16 earnings releases.
- Management's named growth drivers in releases are real estate, footwear, eCommerce, HoS, GameChanger and DMN (N2-2).
- The new "competition, innovation" risk wording (N2-1) and the inventory moving above sales (N2-5) are both mild negatives for 2H FY26.
- Golf door growth lags plan (N2-4).
- The "combined company's vertical brand strategy" language in the FL-deal 8-Ks (May to Sep 2025) most likely covers Foot Locker's own private label. It is not evidence of DKS brands going into FL, and it was dropped after close. This matches F3-12.
- Outdoor channel contraction: Public Lands went from 7-8 stores to 3, and FY23 "go-forward inventory assortment decisions" in outdoor specialty took a $12.0M inventory write-down (W05A l.901, l.1024). Together with F1-1, this means a shrinking channel for Alpine Design and Quest. BURIED.
