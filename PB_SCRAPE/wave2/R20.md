## Bottom line
- I checked about 25 vendors. **None publishes a number for DSG, CALIA, VRST or Maxfli sales, share or mix.** Their public outputs are teasers, banner-level card spend, or gated PDFs behind lead forms. I did not submit any forms.
- The only third-party public statements about DKS private label come from **YipitData's public blog text, based on its own transaction data**:
  - **Jul-2024 (YTD through 2Q24): SUPPORTS.** Private-label apparel at DKS and ASO grew faster than apparel overall.
  - **Nov-2024 (3Q24): AGAINST.** Strip out Hoka, Stanley, Yeti and On, and DKS growth looks "remarkably similar to ASO". On this read, DKS's edge was national hot brands, not owned brands.
- **Best avenue (confirmed): YipitData.**
  - It has covered DKS, ASO and FL since Mar-2024 (e-receipt, card and web-scrape data).
  - It publishes "brand market share at DKS" (2Q25 chartbook) and brand wallet share for DKS shoppers.
  - It has a gated Mar-2026 report on Academy shoppers' apparel purchases at DKS, by brand and SKU.
  - Since May-2026 it puts out a monthly ranking of the Top-30 athletic-wear brands.
- **Second avenue: Particl (newly identified).** It estimates product-level e-commerce sales for 42K products / 287K SKUs on dicks.com since 2023-05-16. That would allow a monthly series of owned-brand share of online units and $. Its absolute levels look inflated (see caveats).
- **Not useful for owned-brand mix:** card-panel vendors (Consumer Edge/Earnest, M Science, Facteus, Bloomberg Second Measure), foot traffic (Placer.ai) and domain traffic (Similarweb, Grips public tier). None of them can see the brand inside a DKS basket.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **No vendor has published a DKS owned-brand number.** After crawling the sitemaps of Yipit, Numerator, Circana, Placer, EDITED, DataWeave, Profitero, Trendalytics, Facteus, M Science, Earnest, Consumer Edge, CivicScience, Grips, Particl, Second Measure, Wiser, CommerceIQ and others, the only DKS private-label text is Yipit's 2024 qualitative line (R20-1).
- **Yipit's Nov-2024 read (R20-2) is the strongest third-party AGAINST point.** DKS's growth edge over ASO was explained by Hoka, On, Stanley and Yeti. The Jul-2024 post said the same about footwear.
- **Both Yipit statements are from 2024, not Apr-Oct 2026.** No 2026 public vendor text on DKS private label exists that I could find.
- **Particl's public estimates are not credible in level.** It shows $5.9B of dicks.com e-commerce over 6 months and $1.2B in Jun-2026, vs Grips' $184.4M for Aug-2026 and DKS's total online business of a few $B a year.
  - Its "top products" list is stale and partly cumulative: Public Lands' list was identical in Jul-2025 and Oct-2026, and items like Pegasus 41 and SIM2 Max are old models.
  - Use only relative brand shares, and test them against Bazaarvoice (X01/R1) before relying on them.
- **Card-panel vendors cannot see the brand inside a DKS basket.** Consumer Edge/Earnest, M Science, Facteus and Bloomberg Second Measure all have this limit. Consumer Edge's 2026 product list includes a "Receipt" dataset, which might, but it is not public.
- **Placer.ai** (19 DKS articles 2025-26) and **Similarweb** have no owned-brand content.
  - Similarweb, Sep-2026: calia.com US rank #1,356,372 with "+101.1% m/m" off a negligible base; vrst.com "no data".
  - The standalone domains only redirect to dicks.com, so they carry no useful traffic.
- **The ScoreCard+ "5, $20 coupons for DICK'S Vertical Brands like DSG and CALIA"** appears in Particl's captured influencer posts (2026-07-31). This is DUP of X10's $100 owned-brand credit. It is an incentive to buy owned brands, so some of the 2H26 owned-brand unit lift may be subsidized.
