# R7 (wave 2): How much of DKS "vertical brands" is licensed, not owned?
Agent R7 | run 2026-10-07 (~04:30-05:30 CDT) | Apify spend $0.00 | all sources accessed 2026-10-07

**Bottom line (INFERENCE, but built on hard review counts):** The licensed lines inside the 10-K "vertical brands" figure (adidas football, Cobra, Marucci, Lotto, plus Prince until FY24 and adidas baseball until FY22) are small. On a dollar-weighted review proxy they are about **5% of vertical sales (range 2-8%) in 2024-2025, roughly $40-135M (central ~$90M) of the $1.7-1.8B**. Their share has **fallen** every year: about 8% in 2023, 5.3% in 2024, 5.1% in 2025 and 3.9% in 2026 YTD (central case).

The licensed roster turned over rather than grew. adidas baseball ran off and Prince is being converted to an owned brand (Monarch). Marucci and Lotto were added, and Lotto is already fading in 2026.

**So licensed lines do NOT explain the FY23→FY25 rise in vertical dollars. They diluted it.** Owned-only vertical sales grew about +16% (central ~$1.47B → ~$1.71B), against the reported +12.5% ($1.6B → $1.8B). In 2026 YTD the licensed share is falling further, so the headline vertical number will slightly UNDER-state owned-brand growth. This removes the "licensed inflation" threat that M1/N1 flagged.

## Top findings

[R7-1] **Licensed lines are a small and shrinking share of "vertical" volume on dicks.com.**

Method:
- I pulled every product and review for Lotto, Cobra, Marucci, Prince, Slazenger, Umbro and the tail exclusive brands, plus adidas within Football, Baseball, Soccer and Racquet. That is 4,790 products and 16,785 unique reviews since 2023.

[R7-2] **Dollar-weighted estimate: licensed ≈ $90M of vertical sales in FY24-FY25 (range $40-135M), about 5% (2-8%), down from ~$130M / 8% (range $58-188M / 4-12%) in FY23. Implied owned-only vertical ≈ $1.47B (FY23) → $1.61B (FY24) → $1.71B (FY25): +16% vs the headline +12.5%.**

How the estimate is built:
- I multiplied review counts by an ASP per line:
  - owned: review-weighted dicks.com offer price $41 (list $73), from the X02 2026-10-07 census, 53% price coverage
  - Lotto $40
  - Marucci apparel/accessories $35 (census offer $34)
  - adidas football and baseball $30
  - Prince $80
  - Cobra sets $600 (census offer ~$800; junior sets $380-480 per search)
- The range cases are licensed ASP ×0.75 with owned at list price (low), and licensed ASP ×1.5 with owned at offer price (high).
- Broad-scope $ share (upper bound, including national-brand Cobra clubs and Marucci bats) is 21.6% → 16.4% → 13.8% → 13.5%. It is also falling.
- Calendar year ≈ DKS fiscal year (FY ends ~Jan 31).

[R7-4] **Licence-to-owned conversion: DKS has replaced licensed Prince table tennis with its own MONARCH brand (2025-26).**

The evidence:
- The same Bazaarvoice review families moved from "Prince" to "Monarch" product IDs:
  - "Prince Pro Table Tennis Net & Clip Set" and "Monarch Pro Table Tennis Net & Clip Set" share first review 2019-10-17.
  - The "Classic Table Tennis Net & Post Set" pair shares 2018-11-14.
  - Monarch premium paddle sets carry 2018 first-review dates but new 26MON IDs.
- New Monarch tables launched: "Monarch Navigator Table Tennis Table" (first review 2025-11-05).
- The DKS 2025 holiday press release (11/13/2025) features "Monarch Table Tennis", where 2022-2024 holiday releases featured Prince tables.
- Monarch style count: 13 ("25") → **21 ("26")**. It was 2-9 per year in 2016-2024.
- Monarch PIE reviews: 176 (2023), 144 (2024), 290 (2025), **313 (2026 YTD, already above all of 2025)**. Table tennis alone: 43 → 22 → 104 → 116 (all reviews).
- Prince table-tennis reviews: 56 → 33 → 41 → 14.
- MONARCH is owned by American Sports Licensing LLC (DKS IP subsidiary; USPTO raw\X05_uspto_dks_marks.csv). A new 1b application in May-2023 covers racquet-sport paddles.
- Monarch is NOT in X01's owned-brand list, nor in the 10-K "vertical brands" sentence. It does appear in the 10-K registered-marks list.

## Evidence against / caveats
- **The licence scope is inferred, not disclosed.** Narrow vs broad is my judgment. If Marucci's licence covers bats and gloves (DKS-exclusive models like the "M Type Acadia" youth gloves could be), the licensed share is nearer the broad 6-7% of units and 14-16% of $. Even then it is falling, not rising.
- **Review propensity varies by price and category**, so review share ≠ unit share ≠ $ share. ASPs are rough (owned offer $41 is a markdown-heavy census-day price; X02 found 45% of owned items marked down that day).
- **Calendar years vs fiscal years.** 2026 YTD is affected by the BV publication lag (Aug half-filled, Sep near-empty). The lag affects owned and licensed equally.
- **Rounding.** The 10-K $ figures are rounded to $0.1B, so the "+16% owned-only vs +12.5% headline" gap (about 3.5pts) is within the ±6pt rounding error of the headline growth itself. It is directionally robust, not precise.
- **Missing licensed lines.** "including" lists may omit licensed lines, and some licensed product may not be on Bazaarvoice (store-only).
- **Prince→Monarch moves some volume from "licensed" to "owned" inside vertical.** That is mix inside the vertical line, not new vertical sales.
- **What this does not settle.** This does not show that owned brands are accelerating. It removes one objection.
