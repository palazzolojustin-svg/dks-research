### Our Call / 4Q thoughts
- "Momentum on core DSG continues with solid SSS, non-comp opportunities, and optionality on ancillary profit drivers - but the key debate remains on if solving for product will drive the turnaround at FL."
- FL turnaround front and center; mgmt doubled down w/ 2026 rollout of "Fast Break" remodels w/ re-merchandised assortments post inventory cleanup. Key bull question: can better product/merchandising alone drive 2H26 FL inflection, or take longer, more investment, drag on strong core DSG.
- Mgmt callback takes: 1) confident FL turnaround gaining traction, path to positive (1-3%) comps + margin expansion; 2) core DKS comps weighted 1H vs 2H (2Q higher than 1Q); FL improves sequentially, weighted to positive 2H26; 3) DC opening in April = GM headwind offsetting merch margin expansion; 4) $100-125M synergies split GM and SG&A starting in 2H; core DKS benefits too; 5) Fast Break rollout driving improvement via better allocation + merchandising.
- Core: positive comps + merch margin expansion; non-comp opportunities House of Sport, Field House, Golf Galaxy; additional profit drivers GameChanger, DICK'S Media Network (DMN), vertical brands; elevated experiences entrench DKS as vendor partner of choice.
- FL: inventory "cleanest it has ever been" after 4Q ("clean out the garage"). Fast Break to >250 locations by BTS 2026 globally (~11% of total stores); early tests turning around previously unprofitable locations -> confidence to close fewer stores than previously expected.
- Where we shake out: 2H FL inflection predicated on better inventory arriving + better retail fundamentals; rejuvenated key vendor relationships + marginal Nike NA improvements should benefit trough-level FL earnings; remain EW given risk FL needs more investment/takes longer.

### Investment thesis themes
