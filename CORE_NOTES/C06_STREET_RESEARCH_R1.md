### PM take / thesis
- PM's take: momentum continues; topline gdnc prudent on 2H uncertainty; GM lowered on tariffs & promo uncertainty; FL share dilution in focus.
- DKS confidence "ranks highest among our discretionary companies that have reported 2Q"; no hesitation on innovation/newness or consumer consistency (no BTS hangover expected). JPM believes QTD trends continue in 5% range; all parts of assortment humming; key brand access + digital/advertising/store investments bearing fruit (HoS remodels/relos adding 2 pts to comp in 2025).
- Traffic growth modest but similar to 1Q; 4.9% ticket growth mostly mix (full-priced selling, expanded high-heat product e.g. On, Hoka). [note: body also says ticket +4.1%/traffic +0.9%]
- Wrinkles: "as if" GM expansion a tick below JPM model on 5% comp; co backed off 75-bp GM expansion guide for yr — 2H up more than 1H but not to same degree as prior (JPM est GM +50 bps, more in 4Q given 30-bp product donation to storm victims in 4Q24). Change = higher/more certain tariff impact (and sharing w/ brands) + promo uncertainty (no issues currently).
- FL: deal expected to close Sep 8; consideration election deadline "tomorrow" after close. If FL closes above $24 cash consideration and all FL holders elect stock (economically rational) → ~11MM addl shares, ~11% share count dilution. Mgmt: still accretive in FY26 even so, though less. Net: uncertainty remains high; clarity on FL key unlock.
- Investment thesis: DKS executing at high level, strongly improved vendor relationships, benefitting from casualization & healthy living despite key hydration & footwear laps. Neutral because stock net down near entire value of proposed FL acquisition since announcement; investors skeptical of hurting core momentum; sees upside to current gdnc; impressed by mgmt strategy/execution.

### Key takeaways (call + mgmt follow-up)
1. FL closes Sep 8; FL holders approved; all regulatory approvals received. "tremendous" opportunities; reiterated $100-125MM mostly buying synergies (merch & non-merch); future synergies from headcount/other areas, work yet to be done. Still EPS accretive in first full FY post close ex-transaction costs; level depends on cash/stock consideration mix, core performance, synergy timing.
2. Gdnc raised: FY25 comp +2-3.5%; 3Q toward high end of annual guide, 4Q lower on tough compare; EPS $13.90-14.50. Driven by 2Q strength + momentum despite macro uncertainty, tariffs, post-BTS lull, dynamic promo backdrop on back half. Gdnc excludes acquisition costs, investment losses, results; includes all tariffs currently in effect. JPM: conservative — implies LSD 2H comps vs 1H MSD despite mgmt citing momentum across all key categories.
3. 2Q comp +5.0% = 6th consecutive qtr of 4+ comp; ticket +4.1%, traffic +0.9%; e-comm growth > co avg. Majority of ticket from full-priced selling of newness/innovation (incl. expanded Hoka/On locations) rather than strategic ~LSD tariff pricing. # athletes, purchase frequency, spend per trip all up y/y. All months positive. Continued share gains vs omnichannel & online competitors. Growth across all income cohorts (despite macro & "sporadic"/strategic price increases); strength across all 4 key categories (apparel, footwear, team sports, golf); no trade-down. JPM models 3Q comp +3.5%, FY25 +3.7%; 70% of footage growth from HoS relos/remodels → ~2 pts comp for yr (70% * 3% footage growth).
4. 2Q GM +~30 bps y/y, mainly merch margin +18 bps (differentiated/high-quality assortment, favorable mix, early GameChanger & Media Network benefits; no incremental promo pressure y/y). GameChanger to grow consistently 1H & 2H (similar GM tailwinds; more in 2026 as it scales); ad business accelerating 2H (that + merch donation = big drivers of 2H accel). Now expects FY GM expansion (2H slightly > 1H) vs +75 bps at midpoint prior; change from tariff clarity, staying clean w/ on-trend inv, uncertain future promo risk (none currently). Merch margin to accelerate more in 2H vs 1H (GameChanger, Dick's Media Network, lapping $10MM (30 bps) wildfire & flood donations in 4Q). Expansion entirely offset by SG&A deleverage (digital, in-store, marketing); op margin still +10 bps at high end. JPM views lowered GM conservatively (high-margin GameChanger/DMN "alternative profit pools"). JPM models FY25 GM 36.4% (+50 bps), FY26 36.9% (+50 bps).
5. SG&A % sales deleveraged ~105 bps y/y; $ +8.5% y/y (digital, in-store, marketing). FY25 SG&A still to deleverage. Pre-opening ~2/3 in 2H, ~75-80% of that in 3Q (13 HoS + 6 Field House openings in 3Q; still 16 HoS openings in 2025; 75-100 HoS by end FY27). 2H deleverage < 1H; EPS decline y/y in 3Q, increase y/y in 4Q. Op margin 11.1% at midpoint (+10 bps at high end).
6. Inventory: 4Q +18% (pull-forward spring inv, successful), 1Q +12% (key items/categories), 2Q +7% (cleanliness/newness). "well positioned"; growth to moderate regardless of tariffs as 2H24 investments lapped.
7. Financial model: potential to leverage SG&A on LSD comp in "future years"; but investments drive topline & GM (GameChanger "SaaS margins"); flexes between GM & SG&A, focus on op margin expansion.
