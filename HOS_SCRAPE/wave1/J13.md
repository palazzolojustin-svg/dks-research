## Methodology
- WARN: not re-run. THESIS_SCRAPE/wave2/L6.md already shows ZERO DICK'S-family WARN notices nationwide 2025-26 (60K notices, 43 states). WARN is blind to a 40-55 person store (needs 50+ at a site). KNOWN; I did not repeat it.
- Store-locator diff (script scripts/J13_citypages.py; data data/J13_citypage_diff.csv; raw raw/J13/citypages.json): for each of 35 HoS events, fetched stores.dickssportinggoods.com/<state>/<city>/ today (2026-10-08) and the Wayback copy ~200 days before the opening (web.archive.org/web/<date>id_/), plus 1-2 neighbouring cities. Parsed listed DICK'S store addresses; a pre-opening address missing today = legacy store closed, moved or renamed. Caveat: listing renames/re-addressing create false positives (e.g., Wilmington, OKC), so each hit is tiered below. Cities not archived (Baton Rouge, Charlottesville, Houston, Pearland) = no result.
- News: 9 WebSearches + 6 page fetches for statements on staff transferred vs cut at the closed store. None found (see X/Leads). Outside-knowledge items are labelled.
- Bridge to QCEW: B5 county series already net out a closed legacy store IN THE SAME COUNTY. I grouped B5_event_summary.csv y1_jobs (year-1 excess jobs) by whether a legacy closure is evidenced.
