# J01: HoS MASTER LIST (every House of Sport opened or announced)
## Methodology
- Output dataset: `HOS_SCRAPE/data/J01_hos_master.csv` (82 rows: 42 open + 1 "open?" + 39 pipeline). Columns: status, store_no, center_or_note, city, state, county, fips, open_date, open_q, date_conf (H/M/L), type, replaced_store, qcew_event (flag), in_locator_HoS, source.
- Sources: (1) THESIS_SCRAPE/raw/B5_hos_events.csv (36 stores / 35 events, 2021Q2-2026Q1) and B5_event_summary.csv (to flag which are in the QCEW event study); (2) a fresh scrape of all 778 DKS locator pages on 2026-10-08 (`scripts/J01_locator_scrape.py` copied from H02, 16 threads; raw `raw/J01/store_pages.json`) keeping pages whose title/name says "House of Sport" (44 found, plus 2 pre-opening "GRAND OPENING" pages #1608 Raleigh, #1677 Frisco); (3) Google News RSS (`scripts/J01_gnews.py`) to date the 2026 openings; (4) pipeline from THESIS_SCRAPE H02/H03/H04 notes (the H02 raw census CSV is NOT in the repo, so pipeline rows are rebuilt from the H02-1/H02-2 text; FIPS codes for pipeline and the 8 new open stores are my general knowledge, not sourced). Build script: `scripts/J01_build_master.py`.
- Replaced-store numbers are only filled where earlier notes name them (Baybrook #5415, Polaris DSG+Public Lands, Kennesaw ex-Public Lands, Johnson City Vestal 47K, etc.). The locator lists only live stores, so closed legacy store numbers are not recoverable from it; leave for J13.
