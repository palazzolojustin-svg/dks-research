# M05: Foot Locker / Champs / Kids FL store sales in CMBS prospectuses (FL section feeder)

## Methodology
- EDGAR full-text search (efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index) over 2019-01-01 to 2026-10-08. Pass 1: 7 queries ("Foot Locker" + "Sales PSF" / "Occupancy Cost" / "Largest Tenants" / "In-line Sales"; "Champs Sports" + same; "Kids Foot Locker" + "Sales"), per calendar year, forms FWP/424B2/424H/424B5/424B3/424B4 and a no-form-filter variant. Pass 2: bare "Foot Locker", "Champs Sports", "Kids Foot Locker", "House of Hoops", forms FWP/424H, 2021-2026 (added only 18 new docs, zero new sales rows, so coverage looks saturated for what FTS indexes).
- Downloaded 197 CMBS/other docs (185 mention FL/Champs; many are DKS merger 424B3, Barclays structured notes, CBL etc., excluded). Regex-parsed tenant-sales tables (tenant name followed by $ sales/psf series) and rent-roll rows (SF, base rent psf, expiry); property names taken from nearest "Loan No." header. Dedupe by values.
- Only 6 properties disclose FL/Champs sales (table below). Most CMBS docs list FL only in rent rolls or prose, not in sales tables (mall sales tables show top ~10 tenants, FL often too small).
- Scripts: scripts/M05_fts.py, M05_fts2.py, M05_dl.py, M05_rows2.py. Data: data/M05_fts_hits*.csv, M05_docs.csv, M05_rows2.csv, data/M05_fl_store_panel.csv. Raw: raw/M05/.

## FL section
All above is FL-section material. Bottom line from the panel: no evidence of FL sales recovery in CMBS data. Latest datapoints (calendar 2025/TTM 12-2025): Prince George's FL -6.5% y/y (still +1.7% vs 2022); Northwoods FL -1.4% y/y (-10.9% vs 2022); Northwoods Champs flat y/y at a depressed level (-38% vs 2022). Sample is 2 properties with 2025 data; coverage is thin (borrower-reported, mall-top-tenant subset, may be biased toward better stores that make the top-10 list; FL 2024 sales grew at Prince George's +6.3% so 2025 decline is the first drop shown).
