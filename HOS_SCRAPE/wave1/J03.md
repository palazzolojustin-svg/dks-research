# J03: QCEW extensions (monthly employment, 2025Q3-2026Q1 HoS openings)

## Methodology
- Source: BLS QCEW open-data API, industry slice files `https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/<year>/<q>/industry/459110.csv` (NAICS 459110 Sporting Goods Retailers; the 451110 slice returns 404 for 2023+, i.e. the series is fully 459110 now), 2023Q1-2026Q1. Filter: own_code 5 (private), agglvl_code 78 (county x 6-digit NAICS). Fields used: month1/2/3_emplvl, qtrly_estabs, total_qtrly_wages, disclosure_code. Raw: `HOS_SCRAPE/raw/J03/` (git-ignored); scripts `scripts/J03_fetch.py`, `J03_analyze.py`, `J03_robust.py`; tidy outputs `data/J03_monthly_excess.csv`, `data/J03_robust.csv`.
- 2026Q2: NOT released (API 404 on 2026-10-08; QCEW 2026Q2 normally arrives ~Dec 2026). 2026Q1 is the latest quarter and is the first release (revisable). So B5's k=0/1 for Q3-Q4 2025 openers already used 2026Q1 month3; the added value here is the month1/month2 detail (what happens inside each quarter) and an independent baseline.
- Events: the 14 B5 events with open_q 2025Q3-2026Q1 and usable county data (Columbus/Polaris, Dallas/Galleria, Leawood, Jersey City, Durham, Live Oak/San Antonio, Glendale, Charlottesville, Strongsville, Freehold, Brandon, Kennesaw, Mobile, Amherst). Event list from THESIS_SCRAPE/raw/B5_hos_events.csv (dates as in B5; Mobile date uncertain).
- Control = 769 counties with sporting-goods employment disclosed in all 36 months 2023-2026Q1, never a B5 HoS county (national-ish seasonal/trend index, removes holiday seasonality).
- Two estimators of "excess" jobs per month (county emp minus counterfactual): (a) YOY: emp(m-12) x control growth over the same 12 months; (b) PRE6: mean county/control ratio of the 6 months before the opening quarter x control(m). Both are my own, differ from B5's quarter-based k/seasonal-quarter baseline.
- Noise (same YOY estimator applied to the 769 control counties, Feb-Mar 2026 avg excess): SD 10 jobs (base<100), 23 (100-300), 35 (300-800), 61 (800-2,000), 80 (>2,000). Treat single-county reads below ~1.5x SD as indistinguishable from zero.
- Implied sales = jobs x $215K and x $70K (INFERENCE; $70K is the Johnson City calibration, $215K the QCEW-derived sector average).

- Cohort summary (INFERENCE): all post-opening months pooled, mean YOY excess = +95 jobs across the 14 events (+113 across the 11 excluding Dallas, Charlottesville, Glendale) => ~$24M at $215K / ~$7.9M at $70K per store. Post-holiday Feb-Mar mean (13 events, excl. Glendale) = ~79 jobs (median ~61) => ~$17M / ~$5.5M; the B5 quarterly k=0/k=1 average (~110-140) is inflated by Nov-Dec seasonal staffing that does not persist.
- [B] Staffing timing signal: new stores are on payroll 4-6 weeks before opening (Columbus +84 in Jul vs 8/15 opening; Jersey City 192 jobs in the Aug pay period vs 9/18 opening; Amherst +161 in Feb vs 3/13). Quarter-of-opening k=0 therefore overstates "jobs at opening" if the opening falls late in the quarter, and a naive pre-period must exclude the quarter before.
- [B] Seasonal give-back: from the Dec/Jan peak to Mar, excess falls 15-25% for most (Jersey City 173 -> 138; Live Oak 146 -> 118; Columbus 193 -> 133; Durham 131 (Nov) -> 63). Sales per job on the Mar level, not the Dec level, is the right denominator for run-rate.
- Establishment counts / size (NAICS 459110): estab counts barely move (HoS replaces or sits among 5-330 county establishments; B5 est excess is noise: Leawood +6, Durham +4, Glendale -11 vs. 2025Q1 control-adjusted). Average establishment size jumps only in thin counties: Jersey City 3.6 (25Q2) -> 15.3 (25Q4) -> 12.5 (26Q1) jobs/estab; Durham 13.2 -> 19.9 -> 15.2; Freehold 11.6 -> 15.1 -> 13.9; Columbus 17.5 -> 31.6 -> 26.7 (19 estabs). Consistent with one large establishment added/expanded. Cross-check: Jersey City's HoS-era avg size of 12-15 sits at a 13-establishment county, so HoS ~ 150 of ~190 jobs.
- Wage cross-check (2026Q1 control-adjusted excess wages annualized / excess jobs): Jersey City $5.4M/150 = ~$36K per job; Freehold $4.1M/155 = ~$26K; Live Oak $3.6M/144 = ~$25K; Amherst $2.7M (1 month). Low pay per job confirms large part-time headcount, which weakens $215K/job conversion (sales per FTE-equivalent vs per head). INFERENCE.

## Evidence AGAINST (X)
- [X] Dallas/Galleria (opened 9/10/25): excess +150 to +175 in Aug-Sep (pre-open hire / other store), then ~0 from Oct (-15, +6, +7, +15, +28). Net post-opening effect ~0 (YOY -25 Feb-Mar, -77 all months) in a 2,300-job county; B5 also flagged it contaminated. Possible relocation of staff inside the county, not a measurable net add.
- [X] Charlottesville/Albemarle (10/31/25): 0 excess in every month (+3 -> -4 -> -1), county base 30 jobs. Either the HoS did not net-add jobs vs the legacy Fashion Square DSG, or the store is in a different QCEW county / classified elsewhere. Noise SD ~10, so a 100-job HoS would be visible: evidence of a small-footprint or non-incremental store.
- [X] Brandon FL (in-place conversion 11/7/25): +120 Q4 but collapses to +33 (Feb) and -5 (Mar). By March no net jobs vs counterfactual; Q4 was seasonal.
- [X] Kennesaw GA (reopened ex-Public Lands): +58 -> +10 -> 0; no persistent add.
- [X] Leawood and Mobile: persistent signal only 1-1.5 SD above noise.
- [X] Fade within the first 5 months: nearly every event has lower Mar excess than its Q4/Jan peak (Brandon, Kennesaw, Durham, Jersey City, Live Oak, Columbus clearly; only Leawood, Freehold-Jan, Glendale, Amherst rise). Not formally counted.
