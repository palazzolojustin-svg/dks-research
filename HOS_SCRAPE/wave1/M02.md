# M02: EDGAR FTS by HoS center names (first half, alphabetical by center)
## Methodology
- Store list: J01 master not ready (data/ empty), so used THESIS_SCRAPE/raw/B5_hos_events.csv (36 rows). It has no mall names for most stores, so I mapped store/city to center from general knowledge (INFERENCE) and added pipeline centers known from B8/B9/H04 (Empire Mall, Washington Square). 39 centers sorted alphabetically; M02 = first 20 (Arrowhead Towne Center, Baybrook Mall, Bel Air Mall, Boulevard Mall, Brandon Exchange, Brandywine Town Center, Colonie Center, Cross Creek Mall, Crossgates Mall, Dadeland Mall, Eastview Mall, Empire Mall, Fairfield Commons, Fashion Square (Charlottesville), Freehold Raceway Mall, Galleria Dallas, Greenbrier Mall, International Plaza, Katy Mills, Kennesaw Town Center). Second half (Leawood ... Woodland Hills) is M03's; the full 39-name list is in raw/M02/centers.json.
- Query: `"<center>" "Dick's"` on efts.sec.gov search-index, 2019-01-01 to 2026-10-08, run twice (with a CMBS/8-K forms filter and without). 3 queries returned errors (Katy Mills, Champaign, Newport Centre with filter); rerun without the filter returned hits. "New" = accession not in the 2,068 accessions cached in THESIS_SCRAPE B8/B9/B10 hit files.
- 110 CMBS-form docs (FWP/424H/424B2/424B3/424B5) for the 20 centers were downloaded and scanned for the center name within 600 chars of "Dick's"/"House of Sport". Also ran 8 generic probes ("DHOS", "House of Sport" + gross sales/first three months/TTM/annualized/tenant sales; 117 hits): 100% already cached.
- Fetched and read in full for HoS content: BANK5 2025-5YR18 ts (n5416_x3), BBCMS 2025-5C38 ts (n5388_x3), BANK5 2025-5YR19 424B2 (n5484_x16), WFCM 2026-5C8 ts (n5644_x5) and annexes.
- Scripts: scripts/M02_fts.py, M02_fetch.py, M02_scan.py. Data: data/M02_fts_hits.csv, M02_center_dick_ctx.csv, M02_dhos_hits.csv. Raw: raw/M02/.
