# J14: Census County Business Patterns / ZIP Business Patterns establishment-size test
## Methodology
- Census API (api.census.gov/data/<yr>/cbp) returns an HTML "Missing Key" page without an API key. Per rules I did not register for a key. I used the identical public bulk files on www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/datasets/<yr>/: `zbp<yy>detail.zip` (ZIP x NAICS x employment-size class, 2018-2023) and `cbp<yy>co.zip` (county x NAICS, 2018-2023). Raw files are in HOS_SCRAPE/raw/J14/.
- Latest vintage is CBP 2023 (employment reference week = 12 March 2023). The 2024 vintage is not published (`cbp24co.zip` returns 404; the 2023 folder is the newest in the directory listing). So no CBP observation exists after March 2023, and only 3 of the 35 HoS events (Victor Apr-2021, Knoxville May-2021, Ridgedale Jun-2022) have a March snapshot taken after opening.
- Sample: all 35 events in THESIS_SCRAPE/raw/B5_hos_events.csv. ZIPs were matched by city name and state using the ZBP city field (same-name-city ZIPs only; Minnetonka, Beavercreek and Amherst had no sporting-goods ZIP rows). Counties were matched by FIPS.
- Tests: (1) does a 100-249 or 250-499 employee establishment appear in NAICS 451110/4511 in the HoS ZIP or county after opening? (2) Substitute test: difference-in-differences on county annual payroll (`ap`, $K) and March employment for NAICS 451110. Controls are the ~630 counties with at least 60 employees in every year 2019-2023, using the median growth rate and the percentile rank of the event county.
- Scripts: scripts/J14_zbp.py, scripts/J14_did.py (the 451110 run was inline, same logic). Data: data/J14_zbp_sportinggoods_all.csv, data/J14_cbp_county_4511.csv, data/J14_cbp_county_451110.csv, data/J14_did_payroll.csv.

## NEW findings (strongest first)
No A-grade HoS data. The avenue is mostly a methodological negative.
- [C/method] The size-class test cannot work. Census suppresses size-class cells with a count below 3. The minimum non-blank value in the county files is 3 (n50_99 and n100_249). In ZBP, ZIP-level sporting-goods rows show zero cells at 100-249 or above in any year, nationwide: 20K establishments in 4.6K ZIPs, 2018-2023. A single new 100-249 employee HoS is invisible by design. At county level, 100-249 counts are visible only where at least 3 such sporting-goods establishments exist (44 counties in 2023), and no HoS county gained such a cell. Source: Census CBP/ZBP bulk files, 2018-2023.
- [C] 4511 counties already showing 100-249 class counts include Hennepin MN (3 in 2018-2020, 6 in 2021, 5 in 2022, 6 in 2023), Harris TX (8-14 over the period) and Maricopa AZ (3-5 in 2020-2023). The Hennepin step from 3 to 6 happened in 2021, before Ridgedale HoS opened (Jun 2022). It cannot be attributed to HoS.
- [C] Victor NY (Ontario County, relocation Apr-2021), NAICS 451110. Annual payroll $2.45M (2020), $3.19M (2021), $4.01M (2022), $3.52M (2023). Control median growth was +16.7% in 2021 and +10.6% in 2022. County excess payroll was about +$0.34M in 2021 (86th percentile) and about +$0.85M in 2022. At about $22-27K payroll per March job that is roughly 30-40 jobs, versus 128 y1 jobs in QCEW (B5). Implied sales for 36 jobs: $7.7M at $215K/job, $2.5M at $70K/job. This is far below the Street's $20M increment. Caveats: county NAICS 451110 payroll nets out other store closures, and CBP applies noise infusion (flag G/H). Grade C. Exposed to X-type interpretation, see below.

## Evidence AGAINST (X)
- [X/C] 2023 cohort (Davenport, Latham, Fayetteville, Johnson City, Chesapeake, Champaign, Scranton, Baybrook/Katy; opened Jul-Aug 2023). County 451110 payroll for calendar 2023 versus 2022 (control median -3.7%). Excess payroll in $K: Latham -579, Chesapeake -184, Champaign -76, Davenport -39, Fayetteville +25, Johnson City +29, Scranton +230, Harris +1,755 (a very large county, so noise). Pooled excluding Harris is -594. Percentiles are mostly 34-65, so no detectable payroll bump. A year-1 HoS of +110 jobs at ~$22K/job opened in Aug should add about $0.8-1.0M in 2023 (about 20-25% of a ~$4M county base), which would be visible. It is not. QCEW (B5) shows +42 to +124 y1 jobs for these stores. Caveats: low power, county employment noise, legacy-store closures offsetting (relocation/in-place), partial-year effect. Treat as non-confirmation, not refutation.
- [X/C] Knoxville (West Town Mall, May-2021): CBP 451110 March employment 392 (2020), 424 (2021), 426 (2022), 434 (2023). Payroll $9.4M (2020), $11.3M (2021), $11.6M (2022), $11.3M (2023); control payroll rose about +29% over 2020-22 vs Knox +23%. This shows essentially no sustained net sporting-goods employment gain, versus the B5 QCEW y1 of +228 (the QCEW base of 439 matches CBP, so the definitions line up). Possible offsets: legacy DSG store and other sporting-goods closures in the county. CBP March snapshots and noise infusion also apply. Implied sales at $215K/$70K from CBP net jobs (approx. 0-10 jobs): about $0-2M / $0-0.7M. Conflicts with the QCEW result and should be flagged to the synthesizer.
- [X/C] Victor and Knoxville March employment: Victor 147 (Mar-2021, pre) to 150 (Mar-2022) to 163 (Mar-2023), i.e. +3 to +16 jobs in 451110 versus QCEW +128. The broader 4511 total (which includes hobby and music retail) rose 287 to 324 (+37) in Victor.

## Leads for next wave
- Census Business Dynamics Statistics and Nonemployer data are not useful: BDS county data is only 2-digit sector and Nonemployer excludes employer stores.
- When CBP 2024 (March 2024 reference) is released, re-run scripts/J14_did.py for the 2023Q3 cohort. Check https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/datasets/2024/ . It would cover 11 events (through 2024Q1 openers).
- QWI (J02) is the better employment source: county x NAICS 4511 x quarter, with new-hire and separation flows.
- Reconcile the Knoxville and Victor CBP-vs-QCEW gap (net of legacy closures) in a QCEW-by-establishment check (J03/J13).

## Avenue verdict: DEAD
Size-class cells under 3 are suppressed, so a lone 100-249 employee HoS never appears. The 2024 vintage is unpublished, and the county payroll/employment fallback is too noisy, with the few usable cases (Knoxville, 2023 cohort) failing to confirm the QCEW year-1 gain.
