## Methodology
- Stores covered (from THESIS_SCRAPE/raw/B5_hos_events.csv + H02/H06 pipeline): Miami (Kendall/Dadeland), Minnetonka (Ridgedale), Mobile (McGowin Park), Nashville (CoolSprings, pipeline), Oklahoma City, Pittsburgh (Ross Park), Raleigh (Crabtree), Sacramento, Salem NH, San Antonio (Live Oak), San Jose, Sarasota, Scranton (Viewmont), Sioux Falls, Springfield MO, Strongsville, Tampa (Intl Plaza), Tulsa, Victor NY, Wilmington (Brandywine), plus Frisco/Mesa/Tomball as pipeline checks.
- Step 1: Google News RSS sweep, 37 city queries ("House of Sport" Dick's <city> hiring/jobs/employees/teammates) -> scripts/J10_gnews.py, raw/J10/gnews.json (~170 unique House of Sport headlines; only ~8 hiring-keyword headlines, none M-Z HoS-specific with a headcount).
- Step 2: ~20 WebSearch queries by store + curl_cffi fetch of ~20 article/press pages, regex for job/employee/hire counts -> scripts/J10_fetch.py, raw/J10/*.txt. (WebFetch tool was non-functional: DNS errors; Wayback not used.)
- Novelty check: grep of THESIS_SCRAPE, EVIDENCE_BOOK, HOS_SCRAPE/wave* for key numbers.
- Result: local news almost never states HoS headcount. The only company-stated HoS headcounts are in pre-opening press releases (Minnetonka 2022, Fayetteville 2023) and are not in M-Z coverage for 2024-2026 openings. Post-opening TV/paper coverage (Tulsa, OKC, Tampa, Wilmington, Strongsville, Pittsburgh, Scranton) covers celebrities/features only.
