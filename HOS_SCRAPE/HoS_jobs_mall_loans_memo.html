House of Sport: new jobs and mall-loan evidence

Bottom line

  • New job sources confirm the House of Sport (HoS) jobs bump, but they do not make it bigger. Census quarterly jobs data (QWI) and monthly government jobs data (QCEW) show +94 to +245 jobs at Kennesaw, Freehold, Leawood, Live Oak, Jersey City and Polaris. Once holiday staff roll off, the cohort averages about +79 jobs (median +61).
  • Relocations add about half the jobs of brand-new stores. Net-new HoS: +190 jobs in year 1 (n=5). Relocations that closed an old store: +103 (n=9).
  • Sales per employee is better pinned now: $155–210K per head (gross). That puts a relocation's sales gain at roughly $7–30M, with a central case of about $16–22M (103 jobs × $155–210K). The Street's ~$20M per relocation sits inside that range, near the upper middle. This is inference, not reported data.
  • Mall-loan filings hold no new HoS sales. We checked SEC EDGAR exhaustively, and then rating agencies, mall REITs and real-estate press. Every HoS sales figure that exists publicly was already in the folder.
  • Foot Locker store sales in mall loans do not show recovery yet. The only stores with 2025 data are flat to down (−1% to −7% y/y). That is before the first DKS-bought assortment arrived at back-to-school 2026.

Sales per employee (calibration)

  • Range: $155–210K of gross sales per HoS employee. Minnetonka gives $156K per posting. A DICK'S Novi staffing model gives $211K; the corporate average is $234K. Scheels Chandler (a comparable big box) gives $300–375K on 400–500 staff and >$150M sales. Tulsa and Littleton projections are about $245–250K.
  • The $70K and $215K figures are marginal ratios. They depend on how productive the old store was, so neither is a rule.
  • Bottom-up: 166–200 staff × $156–211K = $26–42M per HoS, less $12–19M for the old store = +$7–30M per relocation.

Gross vs net jobs

  • Net-new HoS (Boston, Pittsburgh, Jersey City, Glendale, Live Oak): +190 QCEW year-1 jobs (≈$41M / $13M). Relocations with an evidenced closure (9): +103 (≈$22M / $7M). The Street's ~$20M matches relocations only at $215K per job.
  • New closure dates: Binghamton Court St (Store #1) closed 2025-02-07; Vestal closed 2023-08-08, the day before Johnson City opened. Both pull down Broome County's series.
  • The old store's address is gone from the store locator for Minnetonka, Davenport, Amherst, Cedar Rapids, Freehold, Brandon and Dallas. A second old store also closed at Champaign, Scranton (Dickson City), Strongsville and Latham (Field & Stream).
  • Against: Census county payroll data (CBP, March snapshots) shows only about 0–10 net jobs for Knoxville and 30–40 for Victor, vs 228 and 128 in QCEW. Treat that as a caution flag: CBP adds deliberate noise and only covers one month.

Stores the government jobs data cannot see yet

Seven HoS opened after Q1 2026: Cedar Rapids #1661 (Jun 3), Gaithersburg #1634 (Jun 5, in-place conversion), Arlington TX #1612 (Jun 17), Schaumburg #1660 (Jul 24), Greensburg #1617 (Aug 7, 200K sf), Annapolis #1648 (Aug 14, ex-JCPenney box), and Thornton CO #1610 (~Aug 14). Methodology

  • Census QWI (agent J02): LEHD bulk files, county × NAICS 4591 (sporting goods), 10 states, latest quarter 2025 Q4. Difference-in-differences of each HoS county's y/y jobs and hires vs a same-state control. The free API needs a key, so bulk files were used. A large-firm cut (K1) was not possible: firm size is not available at county × industry level.
  • Monthly QCEW (J03): BLS open data, NAICS 459110, county monthly employment, 769-county control, two baselines (y/y and 6-month pre-period). 2026 Q2 data is not released until Dec 2, 2026.
  • Headcount statements (J09–J12, J15, K2): WebSearch, direct article fetches and Wayback copies of local news, DICK'S press releases and city documents; each number checked against the folder for novelty.
  • Calibration (J12): stores where both headcount and sales are public (Minnetonka, Johnson City), a DICK'S staffing model, and big-box analogs (Scheels, Bass Pro).
  • Net vs gross (J13): store-locator pages compared with Wayback snapshots ~200 days before each opening to find closed old stores; WARN notices (none found).
  • Census CBP (J14): County Business Patterns bulk files 2018–2023, county payroll vs ~630 control counties. The size-class test failed because Census suppresses cells with fewer than 3 stores.
  • Incentive filings (J11): Good Jobs First Subsidy Tracker (93 DKS awards, none for HoS) and local IDA/PILOT records.
  • Mall loans (M01–M06): SEC EDGAR full-text search, ~1,500 queries over 6 HoS phrase variants and every HoS mall name, 2019–2026, with and without the form filter; 246+ documents parsed and reconciled by accession with the earlier 517-document cache. Then public rating-agency pages, mall REIT reports and real-estate press. Paywalled Trepp, CRED iQ and KBRA pre-sale reports were not reachable.
  • Cost controls: Sonnet agents capped at ~25–40 actions each; 6 lower-yield agents (careers-site postings, job boards, local news A–L) stopped early at your request.
